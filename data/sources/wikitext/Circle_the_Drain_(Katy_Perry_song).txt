"'''Circle the Drain'''" is a song by American singer [[Katy Perry]], from her third studio album, ''[[Teenage Dream (Katy Perry album)|Teenage Dream]]'' (2010). It was written by Perry, [[Tricky Stewart|Christopher "Tricky" Stewart]] and Monte Neuble. Stewart would also produce the song. Lyrically, "Circle the Drain" candidly focuses on a former lover's drug-addiction and the strains it put on both of them. Following its release, several media outlets reported it to have been inspired by her former relationship with [[Travie McCoy]]. [[Capitol Records]] released the track as a [[promotional single]] two weeks before the album's release on August 10. 

Critics were divided on the track, with some finding Perry to be in a hypocritical position, given the album's other material. Other reviewers found it nice to see a different side to the singer. The song achieved modest charting, with its highest peak position being number 30 on the [[Canadian Hot 100]]. It charted similarly in New Zealand and managed to enter the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] at number 58. "Circle the Drain" has been performed by Perry on her 2011 world tour, [[California Dreams Tour]]. On the tour, she was dressed in a catsuit and the stage was decorated in [[cartoon]]ish meat. Concert reviewers gave her performances mixed reviews.

"Circle the Drain" is a rant song where, lyrically, Perry is telling off a [[self-destructive]] drug-addicted ex-boyfriend. Its candid lyrics<ref name="spin"/><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1649141/travie-mccoy-finally-addresses-katy-perrys-circle-drain.jhtml |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110310081407/http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1649141/travie-mccoy-finally-addresses-katy-perrys-circle-drain.jhtml |url-status=dead |archive-date=March 10, 2011 |first=James |last=Montgomery |title=Travie McCoy Finally Addresses Katy Perry's 'Circle The Drain' |publisher=[[MTV]] |date=October 1, 2010 |access-date=August 24, 2011}}</ref> also discuss the strains his addiction put on both of them. In the track's [[Refrain|chorus]], she sings about how she wants to be his lover, not someone who has to take care of him, such as a maternal figure. She also sings about how he had ultimately lost large opportunities.<ref name="spin"/> Styled in the genres of [[disco]]-[[rock music|rock]],<ref name="toronto">{{cite news |url=http://www.torontosun.com/entertainment/music/2010/08/22/15105231.html|title=Perry album covers the bases|first=Darryl |last=Sturdan|newspaper=[[Toronto Sun]] |publisher= Canoe Sun Media|date=August 22, 2010 |access-date=April 21, 2012}}</ref> [[gothic rock]],<ref name="chicago tribune">{{cite news |url=http://leisureblogs.chicagotribune.com/turn_it_up/2010/08/album-review-katy-perry-teenage-dream.html |title=Album review: Katy Perry, 'Teenage Dream' |first=Greg |last=Kot |work=[[Chicago Tribune]]  |date=August 24, 2010 |access-date=December 10, 2023|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20100825001400/http://leisureblogs.chicagotribune.com/turn_it_up/2010/08/album-review-katy-perry-teenage-dream.html|archivedate=August 25, 2010}}</ref> and [[hard rock]] tones,<ref name="pitchfork">{{cite web|last=Gaca|first=Anna|title=Katy Perry: Teenage Dream Album Review|url=https://pitchfork.com/reviews/albums/katy-perry-teenage-dream/|website=[[Pitchfork (website)|Pitchfork]]|date=February 21, 2021|access-date=July 22, 2022}}</ref> it is an [[up-tempo]] song that lasts for four&nbsp;minutes and 32 seconds, and also has influences of [[techno]].<ref name="spin">{{cite news |last=Wood |first=Mikael |url=http://www.spin.com/reviews/katy-perry-teenage-dream-capitol |title=Katy Perry, 'Teenage Dream' (Capitol) |work=[[Spin (magazine)|Spin]]|year=2010 |access-date=August 24, 2011}}</ref> The track is composed in the [[Key (music)|key]] of E{{music|b}} minor and is set in [[time signature]] of [[common time]], with a [[tempo]] of 135 [[beats per minute]]. Perry's [[vocal range]] spans over an [[octave]], from A{{music|b}}<sub>3</sub> to D{{music|b}}<sub>5</sub>.<ref name="toronto"/><ref name="sheet">{{cite web |url=http://www.musicnotes.com/sheetmusic/mtdVPE.asp?ppn=MN0088650#ProductDetails|title=Katy Perry Peacock – Digital Sheet Music|date=22 November 2010 |publisher=MusicNotes.com |access-date=December 23, 2010}}</ref>

''[[Entertainment Weekly]]'' writer Leah Greenblatt stated that Perry's lyrical talent is not her strength, adding: "neither, in fact, is rhyming 'forte' with 'foreplay,' as she does painfully on the druggie-boyfriend takedown 'Circle the Drain'."<ref name="ew">{{cite magazine |url=https://ew.com/article/2010/08/11/teenage-dream/ |magazine=Entertainment Weekly |title=Teenage Dream (2010) |first=Leah |last=Greenblatt |date=August 11, 2010 |access-date=August 22, 2011 |archive-date=August 15, 2010 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100815025228/http://www.ew.com/ew/article/0,,20409522,00.html |url-status=live }}</ref> ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' journalist Monica Herrera found the song to have been inspired by [[Pat Benatar]]'s material, while Perry herself said: "It's kind of like my '[[You Oughta Know]]' [[Alanis Morissette]] moment."<ref name="billboardcoverstory">{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/articles/news/957236/katy-perry-the-billboard-cover-story |magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]] |title=Katy Perry: The Billboard Cover Story |page=2 |first=Monica |last=Herrera |date=July 23, 2010 |access-date=August 24, 2011}}</ref> Al Fox of [[BBC News]] wrote that the song has "menacing undertones" which unveils a different side to Perry, unlike her current "unsolicited residency in many a showbiz column."<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/music/reviews/zmcg/ |title=Katy Perry Teenage Dream Review |first=Al |last=Fox |publisher=[[BBC News]] |date=August 23, 2010 |access-date=August 24, 2011}}</ref>

== Release and reception ==
[[File:CircleTheDrain-CaliforniaDreams.jpg|Perry performing the song on her California Dreams Tour in March 2011|thumb|205px|alt=|right]]

[[Capitol Records]] released "Circle the Drain" as a [[Promotional recording|promotional single]] from ''Teenage Dream'' two weeks before the album's release on August 10. The song charted modestly and only remained on music charts for its debut week. The track's highest peak position was on the [[Canadian Hot 100]], where it debuted at number 30; "Circle the Drain" became the biggest jump on that chart (28 places, from number 58) for the week ending on August 28.<ref name="canada100"/> On the [[Official New Zealand Music Chart|New Zealand Singles Chart]], the song performed similarly, peaking at number 36 on August 23.<ref name="nzchart"/> Additionally, "Circle the Drain" managed to enter the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] at number 58.<ref name="hotdance"/>

Critical reaction to "Circle the Drain" was mixed. Keri Mason from ''Billboard'' believed that tracks like "Circle the Drain" made the ''Teenage Dream'' album "get heavier sonically and lyrically, with a boom-boom-pow electro punch".<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/articles/review/1068749/katy-perry-teenage-dream |title=Katy Perry, "Teenage Dream" |magazine=Billboard |first=Keri |last=Mason |date=September 3, 2010 |access-date=August 24, 2011}}</ref> ''[[Slant Magazine]]''{{'}}s Matthew Cole found the singer to be virtually less sympathetic than her former lover. This being because her "put-downs are alternately pedantic" and hypocritical even, as she finds offense to his substance use before [[foreplay]], but was singing about blacking out and hooking up on another of the album's tracks – "[[Last Friday Night (T.G.I.F.)]]".<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.slantmagazine.com/music/review/katy-perry-teenage-dream/2219 |title=Katy Perry: Teenage Dream |work=[[Slant Magazine]] |first=Matthew |last=Cole |date=August 22, 2010 |access-date=August 23, 2011}}</ref> Chris Richards of ''[[The Washington Post]]'' had a similar reaction, finding it impossible to take any of the cautionary advice "Circle the Drain" offered, as Perry had just bragged about being very drunk on the very same record.<ref>{{cite news |url=https://www.washingtonpost.com/wp-dyn/content/article/2010/08/23/AR2010082304256.html |title=Album review of 'Teenage Dream' by Katy Perry |first=Chris |last=Richards |newspaper=[[The Washington Post]]  |date=August 24, 2010 |access-date=August 23, 2011}}</ref>

[[MTV News]] reporter James Montgomery, said when discussing several tracks on ''Teenage Dream'', one of which included "Circle the Drain", that "they are all really pretty great" and are "destined to be smash hits."<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1645930/katy-perrys-teenage-dream-noconcept-concept-album.jhtml |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110212153815/http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1645930/katy-perrys-teenage-dream-noconcept-concept-album.jhtml |url-status=dead |archive-date=February 12, 2011 |title=Katy Perry's Teenage Dream: The No-Concept Concept Album|first=James |last=Montgomery |publisher=MTV |date=August 18, 2010 |access-date=August 24, 2011}}</ref> [[Yahoo! Music]] journalist Chris Parkin found the song to be the only track on ''Teenage Dream'' to offer something deep and dark, citing the lyric "You fall asleep during foreplay because the pills you take are more your forté".<ref>{{cite web |url=http://new.uk.music.yahoo.com/blogs/albumreviewsuk/987/katy-perryteenage-dream/ |title=Katy Perry - 'Teenage Dream' |first=Chris |last=Parkin |publisher=[[Yahoo! Music]] |date=September 3, 2010 |access-date=August 24, 2011}}</ref> In his review of the record for ''[[PopMatters]]'', Steve Leftridge stated that tracks such as "Circle the Drain" are not strong, edgy or clever enough "to lend any genuine credibility to Perry as a serious artist with anything to actually say."<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.popmatters.com/pm/review/130887-katy-perry-teenage-dream/ |title=Katy Perry: Teenage Dream |work=[[PopMatters]] |publisher=PopMatters Media, Inc |first=Steve |last=Leftridge |date=October 4, 2010 |access-date=August 24, 2011}}</ref>

== Live performances ==
[[File:Katy Perry 13 - Zenith Paris - 2011 (5507284113).jpg|Perry performing the song on her California Dreams Tour in March 2011|thumb|205px|alt=|right]]

Perry included the song on the [[setlist]] for her 2011 world [[concert tour]] entitled [[California Dreams Tour]]. For performances of the track, the stage was decorated with [[cartoon]]ish link sausages and rib-eye steak that dangled from the [[rafter]]s,<ref name="thr">{{cite web|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/news/katy-perrys-california-dreams-tour-203209 |title=Katy Perry's 'California Dreams' Tour: What the Critics Are Saying |work=[[The Hollywood Reporter]] |date=June 20, 2011 |access-date=August 24, 2011}}</ref> while Perry was dressed in a catsuit.<ref>{{cite news |url=https://www.azcentral.com/thingstodo/music/articles/2011/08/04/20110804katy-perry-brings-her-candy-coated-california-dreams-phoenix.html |first=Ed |last=Masley |title=Katy Perry brings candy-coated California Dreams to Phoenix |work=[[The Arizona Republic]] |date=August 4, 2011 |access-date=August 24, 2011 |archive-date=January 5, 2012 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120105005659/http://www.azcentral.com/thingstodo/music/articles/2011/08/04/20110804katy-perry-brings-her-candy-coated-california-dreams-phoenix.html |url-status=dead }}</ref> Along with Perry's performances of "Teenage Dream" and "[[E.T. (song)|E.T.]]", [[music blog]] [[Idolator (website)|Idolator]] staff writer Erika Brooks Adickman cited "Circle the Drain" as being a highlight of the tour.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://idolator.com/5813902/katy-perry-rocks-newcastle-with-her-california-dreams-tour |archive-url=https://archive.today/20120717015517/http://idolator.com/5813902/katy-perry-rocks-newcastle-with-her-california-dreams-tour |url-status=dead |archive-date=July 17, 2012 |first=Erika Brooks |last=Adickman |title=Katy Perry Rocks Newcastle With Her California Dreams Tour |publisher=Idolator |date=April 4, 2011 |access-date=August 24, 2011 }}</ref> ''[[The Patriot Ledger]]''{{'}}s Jay N. Miller found "Circle the Drain" to be "the most rocking" out of the setlist's line-up at [[TD Garden]].<ref>{{cite news |url=http://www.patriotledger.com/entertainment/x536836943/Katy-Perry-s-California-dreams-rock-TD-Garden |first=Jay N. |last=Miller |title=Katy Perry's California dreams rock TD Garden |work=[[The Patriot Ledger]] |date=June 20, 2011 |access-date=August 24, 2011}}</ref>

In contrast, ''[[The Hollywood Reporter]]'' quoted ''Rolling Stone''{{'}}s Meredith Blake in her concert review saying she found it pointless to have those items onstage given the song's theme.<ref name="thr"/> Writing for ''[[NME]]'', Emily MacKay did a live review of the singer's tour at the [[Hammersmith Apollo]] in London during March 2011. She said that the spiritless "Circle the Drain", "Hummingbird Heartbeat" and "Who Am I Living For?" were enough to make you feel somewhat like that of a "fractious toddler having a series of expensive toys waved in its mottled face by a fretful aunt to keep it from deciding it wants to cry."<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.nme.com/reviews/katy-perry/11946 |title=Live Review: Katy Perry |work=[[NME]] |first=Emily |last=MacKay |date=April 4, 2011 |access-date=August 24, 2011}}</ref>
