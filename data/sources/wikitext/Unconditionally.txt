"'''Unconditionally'''" is a song by American singer [[Katy Perry]]. It was released as the second single from her fourth studio album ''[[Prism (Katy Perry album)|Prism]]'' (2013) on October 16, 2013, two days before the album was released. Inspiration for the song came primarily from a humanitarian trip to [[Madagascar]] that Perry made in partnership with [[UNICEF]]. Having been surrounded by what she described as [[unconditional love]], she co-wrote it with the song's producers [[Dr. Luke]], [[Max Martin]] and [[Cirkut (record producer)|Cirkut]] to reflect that feeling. The song was recorded at Luke's in the Boo, based in [[Malibu, California]], [[Conway Recording Studios]], based in [[Hollywood, Los Angeles]], [[MXM Studios]], based in [[Stockholm, Sweden]], and Secret Garden Studios, based in [[Montecito, California]]. "Unconditionally" is a [[Pop music|pop]] [[power ballad]] styled in [[Rock music|rock]], backed against a [[bassline]] and [[percussion]]. 

Commercially, the song peaked within the top ten in Bulgaria, the Czech Republic, Italy, Lebanon, Slovenia, and South Africa as well as the top twenty in Australia, Austria, Canada, Hungary, Poland, Slovakia, and the United States. It also was certified diamond in Brazil and received multi-platinum certifications in Australia, Canada, the United States. The single release was promoted with an [[Instagram]]-based campaign, a lyric video directed by [[Aya Tanimura]] and live performances. Her performance of "Unconditionally" at the [[American Music Awards of 2013]] was the center of controversy due to accusations of [[cultural appropriation]]. Brent Bonacorso served as the director for the track's main music video, released on November 19, 2013, which adopts the theme of love and uses a series of metaphors to enrich its concept.

==Writing and production==
"Unconditionally" was written by Perry alongside its producers [[Dr. Luke]], [[Max Martin]], and [[Cirkut (record producer)|Cirkut]]. Perry called the song her favorite within the record. She also mentioned that she wrote the song bearing in mind the sentiments she had felt during her [[UNICEF]] humanitarian trip to [[Madagascar]] in April 2013. Such sentiments inspired her to delve into the theme of [[unconditional love]]. She stated that, while on that voyage, Perry and her team would "go in cruisers up the mountain" to see newly constructed schools. After observing a group of children, she realized that even without social networking, they felt "pure" [[unconditional love]] towards each other.<ref name=Goosebump>{{cite news|last=Montgomery|first=James|title=Katy Perry Reveals Secret Formula For Single Success: 'The Goose Bump Test'|url= https://www.mtv.com/news/clj6h7/katy-perry-unconditionally-goosebumps|archive-url= https://web.archive.org/web/20230323171611/https://www.mtv.com/news/clj6h7/katy-perry-unconditionally-goosebumps|url-status= dead|archive-date= March 23, 2023|publisher=MTV News|date=November 20, 2013|access-date=March 23, 2013}}</ref>

Inspired by that situation, the song was described by Perry as approaching all forms of [[love]]. "I love this song because not only can it be a romantic, intimate song", she expounded. "But it can be about the type of love that a mother has when she sees her first child, or best friends [...] It is a transcendent love, not just relationship status."<ref name=Goosebump /> "Unconditionally" was also inspired by the acceptance of one's flaws in a relationship, which Perry believed was relatable to listeners.<ref>{{cite web|publisher=MTV UK. Viacom Media Networks.|access-date=March 9, 2014|date=October 24, 2013|title=Katy Perry 'Was Scared To Be Accepted in Relationships'|url=https://www.mtv.co.uk/news/xsdyrh/katy-perry-was-scared-to-be-accepted-in-relationships|archive-date=December 4, 2023|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20231204102245/http://www.mtv.co.uk/news/xsdyrh/katy-perry-was-scared-to-be-accepted-in-relationships|url-status=live}}</ref> It was engineered by Peter Carlsson, Clint Gibbs, Sam Holland, and Michael Illbert, with assistance by Eric Eylands, Rachael Findlen, Justin Fox, and Cory Bice, at the MXM Studios in [[Stockholm]], [[Sweden]] and Conway Recording Studios in [[Hollywood, Los Angeles|Hollywood]], [[California]]. Perry also handled vocal production. Engineering for mix was carried out by John Hanes and final mixing was helmed by Serban Ghenea at the MixStar Studios in [[Virginia Beach, Virginia]].<ref name="Notes">{{cite AV media notes |title=Prism |title-link=Prism (Katy Perry album) |others=Katy Perry |year=2013 |type=liner notes |publisher=[[Capitol Records]] |location=United States}}</ref>

==Composition==
At a length of 3 minutes 48 seconds, "Unconditionally" is a [[pop music|pop]]<ref name="digitalspyrc">{{cite web|last=Copsey|first=Robert|url=http://www.digitalspy.com/music/feature/a525129/playlist-10-tracks-you-need-to-hear/|title=Playlist: 10 tracks you need to hear|work=[[Digital Spy]]|date=October 21, 2013|access-date=February 21, 2019}}</ref> [[power ballad]], which is styled in the genre of [[rock music|rock]].<ref name="iTunes">{{cite web |url=https://music.apple.com/ca/album/prism-deluxe-version/690928033 |title=Prism (Deluxe) – Katy Perry|publisher=[[iTunes Store]]. [[Apple Inc.|Apple]]|access-date=March 8, 2014 |date=October 22, 2013}}</ref><ref name="Fuse">{{cite web |url=http://www.fuse.tv/2013/10/katy-perry-unconditionally |title=Listen: Katy Perry Wails on New Single 'Unconditionally' |first=Jeff |last=Benjamin |publisher=[[Fuse (TV channel)|Fuse]] |access-date=March 8, 2014 |date=October 16, 2013 |archive-date=July 21, 2015 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150721193758/http://www.fuse.tv/2013/10/katy-perry-unconditionally |url-status=live }}</ref> According to the sheet music published by Kobalt Music Publishing America, Inc. at Musicnotes.com, it is written in the key of [[E minor]]. It paces at a moderately fast [[metronome]], at approximately 126-132 beats per minute, while it is set at a [[Common time|{{music|time|4|4}} time signature]], following a chord progression of Em–C–G–D. Perry's vocals in the track span from G{{sub|3}} to C{{sub|5}}.<ref>{{cite web|title=Unconditionally – By Katy Perry – Digital Sheet Music|url=http://www.musicnotes.com/sheetmusic/mtdFPE.asp?ppn=MN0126609|publisher=Musicnotes|access-date=March 8, 2014|date=October 30, 2013|archive-date=November 9, 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131109234400/http://www.musicnotes.com/sheetmusic/mtdFPE.asp?ppn=MN0126609|url-status=live}}</ref> The track's "uncluttered" instrumentation features woodblock percussion and a "dramatic" bass line. Lyrically, Jason Lipshutz from ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' deemed it the "most mature offering" on ''Prism'', claiming it served as a "predicate" to Perry's 2010 single "[[Teenage Dream (Katy Perry song)|Teenage Dream]]".<ref name="BB1">{{cite magazine|last=Lipshutz|first=Jason|title=Katy Perry's 'Prism' Album Preview: 10 Things You Need To Know|url=https://www.billboard.com/music/music-news/katy-perrys-prism-album-preview-10-things-you-need-to-know-5687099/|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|date=September 6, 2013|access-date=October 27, 2013|archive-date=March 4, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160304072407/http://www.billboard.com/articles/columns/pop-shop/5687099/katy-perrys-prism-album-preview-10-things-you-need-to-know|url-status=live}}</ref>

==Music video==
===Production and release===
The music video for "Unconditionally", directed by Brent Bonacorso, was filmed in London during October 2013.<ref name="slant" /><ref name=MusicVideo>{{cite web|title=Katy Perry Confirms She Filmed 'Unconditionally' Music Video in London Last Week|url=http://www.capitalfm.com/artists/katy-perry/news/unconditionally-music-video-london/|publisher=Capital FM|access-date=October 23, 2013|date=October 23, 2013|archive-date=January 18, 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210118230708/https://www.capitalfm.com/artists/katy-perry/news/unconditionally-music-video-london/|url-status=live}}</ref> It was partly inspired by the [[historical drama|costume drama]] films ''[[Dangerous Liaisons]]'' and ''[[Anna Karenina (2012 film)|Anna Karenina]]''.<ref>{{cite news|last=Vena|first=Jocelyn|title=Was Katy Perry Tempted To Write Any 'Screw You' Anthems on Prism?|url=https://www.mtv.com/news/du9ogl/katy-perry-prism-songwriting|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230531005341/https://www.mtv.com/news/du9ogl/katy-perry-prism-songwriting|url-status=dead|archive-date=May 31, 2023|publisher=MTV News|date=November 19, 2013|access-date=May 30, 2023}}</ref> During an interview with [[MTV News]], Bonacorso talked about the concept for the video. He stressed that he wanted to match the song's tone with its visual, explaining that he wanted to depict a "more mysterious, elegant and sophisticated world to live in. ... less about a time period and more about creating an impression and a feeling." As the song essentially discusses the feeling of love, the video itself sees Perry amidst scenarios which refer to the power of it.<ref name="mtv">{{cite news|title=Katy Perry's Bed Is on Fire: Her 'Unconditionally' Director Explains Why|url=https://www.mtv.com/news/556582/katy-perry-unconditionally-video-director-explains|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20221024222301/https://www.mtv.com/news/556582/katy-perry-unconditionally-video-director-explains|url-status=dead|archive-date=October 24, 2022|access-date=May 30, 2023|date=November 20, 2013|last=Vena|first=Jocelyn|publisher=MTV News}}</ref> The video is set in two different spaces, as Perry is found both in a snowy background and in a ballroom surrounded by dancers, which are mostly dressed in black apparel.<ref name="uncon">{{cite video|title=Unconditionally|date=2013|people=Bonacorso, Bren|medium=music video|publisher=Capitol Records}}</ref> Those settings were used to represent "different part[s] of the subconscious".<ref name="mtv" /> The video premiered through MTV on November 19, 2013.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.mtv.com/news/qzsd9s/katy-perry-unconditionally-video-premiere|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230531005343/https://www.mtv.com/news/qzsd9s/katy-perry-unconditionally-video-premiere|url-status=dead|archive-date=May 31, 2023|last=Montgomery|first=James|title=Katy Perry's 'Unconditionally' Video: Watch It Now|publisher=MTV News|date=November 19, 2013|access-date=May 30, 2023}}</ref>

===Synopsis===
It begins with Perry, sitting on the ground while mouthing the lyrics of the song and staring at the viewer. As she finishes the first verse, the camera shifts to a ballroom, in which elegantly-dressed dancers start to dance. The song's chorus kicks in, and Perry is now found in the ballroom, in the middle of all the dancers. As the second verse begins, she has an [[owl]] on her arm, which she lets go and flies. The bird represents "something strong, free [and] powerful", according to Bonacorso, who added: "She lets it go into the world[,] and that literally [means] letting this force within her go wild. That's an important part of love[,] is that you have to let it go." The video also shows Perry, with a golden dress and cross-shaped earrings, with flames on her body. By the second repetition of the chorus, a bed gets lit on fire, which the director included to refer to love that "can burn you like an inferno". As the song reaches its climax, Perry gets hit by a car, with tiny broken debris from it shattering behind her, while she remains stationary and unharmed. This scene symbolizes the experience of true love, that changes "your world" and "hits you out of the blue". This scene cuts back to the ballroom, where Perry is surrounded by a similar explosion of "colorful" flowers, which is used to contrast the more violent nature of falling in love and the softer part of it, the "joyful and wonderful" feelings.
