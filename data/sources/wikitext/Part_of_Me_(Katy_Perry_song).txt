"'''Part of Me'''" is a song by American singer-songwriter [[Katy Perry]] from ''[[Teenage Dream: The Complete Confection]]'' (2012)'','' the reissue of her third studio album, [[Teenage Dream|''Teenage Dream'']] (2010). It was written by Perry, [[Dr. Luke]], [[Max Martin]], and [[Bonnie McKee]], and produced by Dr. Luke, Martin, and [[Cirkut]]. The song was not included on the original edition of ''Teenage Dream'' because Perry felt that it did not fit the composition of the album. A demo of the song leaked online in late 2010, amid speculation that the lyrics were directed to the singer's ex-boyfriend [[Travie McCoy]]. "Part of Me" was re-worked and officially released as the lead single from ''The Complete Confection'' on February 13, 2012, through [[Capitol Records]]. 

A [[dance-pop]] and [[power pop]] record with [[electropop]] elements and a distinctive [[house music|house]] beat, it has been compared to Perry's 2010 singles "[[Firework (song)|Firework]]" and "[[California Gurls]]". Its lyrics describe a female protagonist who declares herself as unbreakable and strong following a break-up. Many critics theorized that the lyrics addressed Perry's divorce from British comedian, media personality and actor [[Russell Brand]], although she stated it was not about him, since it was written in early 2010. The single's artwork was produced by art director Gavin Taylor and photographer [[Mary Ellen Matthews]].

"Part of Me" was a commercial success. It was the twentieth song to debut at number one on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]], Perry's seventh number-one single on the chart, and her ninth consecutive number-one single on the [[Hot Dance Club Songs|''Billboard '' Hot Dance Club Songs]] chart. Outside of the United States, "Part of Me" topped the charts in Bulgaria, Canada, New Zealand, and the United Kingdom, and has been certified diamond in Brazil while receiving multi-platinum certifications in Australia, Canada, New Zealand, and the United States. Marketing campaigns for [[Adidas]] and ''[[The Sims 3: Showtime]]'' have featured it.

An accompanying music video was filmed at the [[United States Marine Corps]]' [[Marine Corps Base Camp Pendleton|Camp Pendleton]] in [[Oceanside, California]]. The video depicts Perry enlisting in the Marines following a break-up. It garnered generally positive reviews for Perry's "[[Girl power]]" message but drew criticism from feminist author [[Naomi Wolf]], who denounced it as military [[propaganda]]. Perry debuted the first televised performance of the song at the [[54th Annual Grammy Awards|54th Grammy Awards]] and has also performed the song at the [[ECHO Awards]], the [[2012 Kids' Choice Awards]], and ''[[American Idol]]''.

[[Katy Perry]] wrote "Part of Me" with [[Dr. Luke]], [[Bonnie McKee]], and [[Max Martin]] during the 2010 ''[[Teenage Dream (Katy Perry album)|Teenage Dream]]'' writing sessions, which also produced the Hot 100 number-one singles "[[California Gurls]]", "[[Teenage Dream (Katy Perry song)|Teenage Dream]]"  and "[[Last Friday Night (T.G.I.F.)]]."<ref>{{cite web|title=Teenage Dream|url=http://www.allmusic.com/album/teenage-dream-r1834720|publisher=[[AllMusic]]|first=Stephen Thomas|last=Erlewine|author-link=Stephen Thomas Erlewine|access-date=February 10, 2012|archive-date=November 8, 2011|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20111108172456/http://allmusic.com/album/teenage-dream-r1834720|url-status=live}}</ref> [[Cirkut]] also helped produce the track with Luke and Martin.<ref name=CompleteConfectionNotes>{{cite AV media notes|title=Teenage Dream: The Complete Confection|others=[[Katy Perry]]|year=2012|publisher=Capitol Records}}</ref> Perry and McKee had been casual friends prior to Perry's request for her help with a song that Perry had been writing called "Part of Me". According to ''[[Rolling Stone]]'', McKee stayed up all night writing the lyrics. When Perry was presented the song, she shouted "We're buying you a car!" by the time she heard the third line.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=http://www.rollingstoneme.com/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=253|title=Bonnie McKee|magazine=Rolling Stone Middle East|first=Gavin|last=Edwards|access-date=February 12, 2012|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130921061414/http://www.rollingstoneme.com/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=253|archive-date=September 21, 2013|url-status=dead|df=mdy-all}}</ref>

Perry recorded a demo of the track that [[Music leak|leaked]] in full on December 30, 2010. The song was rumored to be a leftover cut from her ''Teenage Dream'' recording sessions, and fans speculated that the track might be included in an as-yet-unconfirmed re-release of the LP.<ref name=leak>{{cite news|title=Katy Perry's 'Part Of Me' Leaks Online|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1655103/katy-perrys-part-me-leaks-online.jhtml|publisher=[[MTV News]]|last=Dinh|first=James|access-date=February 10, 2012|archive-date=14 October 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131014195630/http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1655103/katy-perrys-part-me-leaks-online.jhtml|url-status=dead}}</ref> This re-release was confirmed in January 2012, when Perry announced through her official website that the album would contain all twelve songs from the original album, plus three new songs and a remix, and would be released on March 26, 2012.<ref>{{cite web|title=Katy Announces 'Teenage Dream: The Complete Confection'|url=http://www.katyperry.com/katy-announces-release-of-teenage-dream-the-complete-confection/|publisher=Katyperry.com|access-date=June 24, 2012|archive-date=May 26, 2012|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120526164214/http://www.katyperry.com/katy-announces-release-of-teenage-dream-the-complete-confection/|url-status=live}}</ref>

==Critical reception==

===Synopsis===
[[File:Snapshot of Part of Me video.jpg|thumb|right|200px|Perry featured in [[military camouflage]], signaling her final transformation into a Marine]]
Perry sits in her car outside boyfriend Jason's (Lucas Kerr) workplace and sees him flirting with a woman. Perry confronts him in his office, slams down her heart pendant on the desk, and ends their relationship before Jason can talk to her. Perry storms out the door. The title track begins as we see Perry driving to a gas station, where she buys a can of tea. After paying for her items, she sees a moto-sticker on a notice board which reads, "All women are created equal, then some become Marines". The increasingly emotional Perry gathers her possessions from her car trunk, enters a nearby restroom, and begins her change of identity. She tearfully cuts her hair short, removes her bracelets, flattens her breasts, and changes into a hoodie and jeans.

Perry enlists in the [[United States Marine Corps]], and after a brief scene of recruit training, reports to the School of Infantry for the Marine Corps' rigorous basic combat instruction course. She reminisces over her experiences with her ex-boyfriend, yet remains spiteful towards him. She then burns a love note from him (as opposed to a [[Dear John letter]]) and vents her frustration through the [[Marine Corps Martial Arts Program]]. The remainder of the video depicts Perry's training, and shows her dancing beneath a large [[Star-Spangled Banner (flag)|garrison]] flag. Perry ultimately transforms into a trained warrior, clad in camouflage [[MARPAT|marine pattern]], body armor and face paint.

This was similar to her previous performances and the video, but was pre-taped due to illness. After a short, introductory military-style video, Perry made a simulated airdrop onto a military base peopled by female background dancers, all clad in military clothing. The complexity and originality of the performance were praised. [[Brian Mansfield]], of ''[[USA Today]]'', called it a "pretty impressive production", but criticized Perry's vocal abilities.<ref>{{cite news|title=Katy Perry: 'Part of Me'|url=http://content.usatoday.com/communities/idolchatter/post/2012/04/katy-perry-part-of-me/1|first=Brian|last=Mansfield|newspaper=[[USA Today]]|access-date=April 27, 2012|date=April 26, 2012|archive-date=April 30, 2012|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120430092037/http://content.usatoday.com/communities/idolchatter/post/2012/04/katy-perry-part-of-me/1|url-status=live}}</ref>
