"'''Wide Awake'''" is a song by American singer-songwriter [[Katy Perry]] from ''[[Teenage Dream: The Complete Confection]]'' (2012)'','' the reissue of her third studio album, [[Teenage Dream|''Teenage Dream'']] (2010). She co-wrote the song with [[Max Martin]] and [[Bonnie McKee]], alongside its producers [[Dr. Luke]] and [[Cirkut]]. The song was recorded at NightBird Studios, based in [[West Hollywood, California]]. It was specifically written for Perry's autobiographical film ''[[Katy Perry: Part of Me]]'' (2012). [[Capitol Records]] released the song onto radio stations on May 22, 2012. The track is a [[power ballad]] styled in [[dance-pop]], and features lyrics about the reality of a break up and moving forward.

"Wide Awake" became another commercial success for Perry. In the United States, it peaked at number two on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] and topped the charts in Canada and New Zealand while reaching the top 10 in Australia, Hungary, Ireland, Lebanon, and the United Kingdom, as well as the top 20 in Brazil, Italy, and Slovakia. The single's music video was directed by Lance Drake, and featured a fairy tale-like story, starring Perry and a child version of her. The video also makes references from Perry's ''Teenage Dream'' singles and the previous ''Complete Confection'' single "[[Part of Me (Katy Perry song)|Part of Me]]".

Perry co-wrote "Wide Awake" with [[Bonnie McKee]], [[Max Martin]], and its producers [[Cirkut]] and [[Dr. Luke]]. The production coordination was helmed by Irene Richter and Katie Mitzell. Recorded at NightBird Recording Studios in [[West Hollywood, California]], it was engineered by Clint Gibbs, who was assisted by Angelo Caputo, and John Hanes, and was mixed by Serban Ghenea.<ref name="Notes">{{cite AV media notes|others=Katy Perry|title=[[Teenage Dream: The Complete Confection]]|year=2012|type= inlay cover|publisher= [[Capitol Records]]|page=13}}</ref>

According to Perry, "Wide Awake" was inspired by "major life changes", including her divorce from actor and comedian [[Russell Brand]]. In an interview with [[MTV News]], Perry revealed, "Well, this song, I have a lot of different emotions and feelings in all of my songs. This song in particular is a dose of reality. It's kind of like coming down from a high. You’ve been on cloud nine for so long, and it can't always be so sweet and sometimes you need to realize that, and you have to pick yourself up and move forward and face the facts of life and know that this is just a lesson you learn and you're stronger because of it."<ref name="MTVWrite">{{cite web|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/1687697/katy-perry-wide-awake-reality/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140715124442/http://www.mtv.com/news/1687697/katy-perry-wide-awake-reality/|url-status=dead|archive-date=July 15, 2014|title=Katy Perry Calls 'Wide Awake' A 'Dose Of Reality'|first=Amy|last=Wilkinson|publisher= MTV News|date=June 18, 2012|access-date=July 12, 2014}}</ref> Initially Max Martin had produced a demo with the chorus being "No Matter What"; however due to his accent, McKee misheard the lyrics as "I'm Wide Awake/I'm Born Again" and wrote the song around that hook.<ref>{{cite web |last1=Wiser |first1=Carl |title=Songwriter Interviews Bonnie McKee |url=https://www.songfacts.com/blog/interviews/bonnie-mckee |website=Songfacts |access-date=July 23, 2019}}</ref> McKee opined that the song represented Perry's departure from her previous releases.<ref name="Bonnie">{{cite web|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1681387/katy-perry-wide-awake-single.jhtml|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120322210937/http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1681387/katy-perry-wide-awake-single.jhtml|url-status=dead|archive-date=March 22, 2012|title=Katy Perry Rebounds From Breakup On 'Wide Awake' Single|first=Joceyln |last=Vena|publisher=MTV News|date=March 20, 2012|access-date=July 12, 2014}}</ref>

"Wide Awake" impacted US [[contemporary hit radio]] on May 22, 2012, through [[Capitol Records]],<ref name=release>{{cite web|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120507205726/http://www.allaccess.com/top40-mainstream/future-releases|url=http://www.allaccess.com/top40-mainstream/future-releases|title=Top 40/M Future Releases|publisher=All Access|archive-date=May 7, 2012|access-date=July 12, 2014|url-status=live}}</ref> in support of Perry's autobiographical film ''[[Katy Perry: Part of Me]]''.<ref name="DigSpy" /> The single's cover artwork was released on the same day via Perry's Twitter account,<ref name="goth">{{cite web|author=Mahlmeister, Chrissy|title=Katy Perry Ditches Goth Makeup For 'Wide Awake' Single Cover Art|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/2516519/katy-perry-wide-awake-2/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161104075553/http://www.mtv.com/news/2516519/katy-perry-wide-awake-2/|url-status=dead|archive-date=November 4, 2016|publisher=MTV News|access-date=April 22, 2018|date=May 22, 2012}}</ref> featuring Perry with deep purple hair and multicolored lettering.<ref name="DigSpy">{{cite web|title=Katy Perry unveils new single 'Wide Awake' artwork|url=http://www.digitalspy.co.uk/music/news/a382872/katy-perry-unveils-new-single-wide-awake-artwork-picture.html#~oJN1qg0HjxxZxV|first=Lewis|last=Corner|publisher=[[Digital Spy]]|date=May 22, 2012|access-date=July 12, 2014}}</ref>

==Composition==
"Wide Awake" is a midtempo [[dance-pop]] ballad.<ref name="rolling">{{cite magazine |url=https://www.rollingstone.com/music/songreviews/wide-awake-20120327|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130314031729/http://www.rollingstone.com/music/songreviews/wide-awake-20120327|url-status=dead|archive-date=March 14, 2013|title=Katy Perry: Wide Awake |first= Jody |last=Rosen |magazine=[[Rolling Stone]] |date=March 27, 2012 |access-date=July 12, 2014}}</ref> Sheet music for "Wide Awake" is set in the key of [[F major]] with a moderately fast tempo. The vocals span from [[C (musical note)|C<sub>4</sub>]] to [[D (musical note)|D<sub>5</sub>]].<ref name="sheet music">{{cite web|url=http://www.musicnotes.com/sheetmusic/mtdFPE.asp?ppn=MN0104119&|title=Katy Perry– Wide Awake Sheet Music (Digital Download) |publisher=[[Sony/ATV Music Publishing]] |website=musicnotes.com |date=May 25, 2012 |access-date=July 9, 2012}}</ref>

"Wide Awake" is lyrically about the reality of a break up and moving on, which many have believed to have been written about Perry's ex-husband, Russell Brand, since it was written and recorded after the divorce.<ref>{{cite web|author=Abend, Carrie|title=Listen to Katy Perry's New Break-Up Song 'Wide Awake' [AUDIO]|url=http://www.ryanseacrest.com/2012/03/19/listen-to-katy-perrys-new-break-up-song-wide-awake-audio/#sthash.YUT6QkFR.dpuf|work=RyanSeacrest.com|access-date=December 26, 2013|date=March 19, 2012}}</ref> Byron Flitsch from [[MTV]] stated that the [[downtempo]] melody of the song "highlights the song's introspective lyrics".<ref name="brian">{{cite web|last=Brian|first=Filtcsh|title=New Song: Katy Perry, 'Wide Awake'|url=https://www.mtv.com/news/gynreb/katy-perry-wide-awake|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220927024320/https://www.mtv.com/news/gynreb/katy-perry-wide-awake|url-status=dead|archive-date=September 27, 2022|publisher=MTV}}</ref> Perry's Christian upbringing is also prominent on "Wide Awake", which references the concept of being [[born-again]].<ref>{{cite web|author=Holz, Adam|title=Katy Perry – "Wide Awake"|url=http://www.pluggedin.com/music/tracks/2012/katy-perry-wide-awake.aspx|work=[[Plugged In (publication)]]|access-date=December 26, 2013}}</ref>

==Commercial performance==
[[File:Katy Perry 12, 2012.jpg|thumb|right|190px|Perry (pictured) performing "Wide Awake"]]

In the United States, "Wide Awake" made its first appearance on the US ''Billboard'' [[Bubbling Under Hot 100]] chart at number 19 based on digital sales alone following the release of ''Teenage Dream: The Complete Confection''. After Perry's performance on the [[2012 Billboard Music Awards|2012 ''Billboard'' Music Awards]], it debuted on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] at number 35 as the second highest debut of the week, behind [[Phillip Phillips]]' "[[Home (Phillip Phillips song)|Home]]".<ref>{{cite web|last=Grein |first=Paul |url=http://music.yahoo.com/blogs/chart-watch/week-ending-may-27-2012-songs-bieber-transitional-054029489.html |title=Week Ending May 27, 2012. Songs: Bieber's Transitional Hit &#124; Chart Watch (NEW) – Yahoo! Music |publisher=Music.yahoo.com |date=May 31, 2012 |access-date=June 22, 2012}}</ref> The same week, the song debuted on multiple ''Billboard'' component charts – at number 29 on the [[Billboard Pop Singles|Pop Songs]] and 35 on the [[Adult Pop Songs]] charts.<ref>[{{BillboardURLbyName|artist=katy perry|chart=all}} Wide Awake – Katy Perry | Billboard.com]</ref> For the chart issue dated June 4, 2012, "Wide Awake" debuted at number 50 on the [[Dance/Club Play Songs]] chart as well as moving 5 spots on the Pop songs chart to number 20. The song reached the summit on the week of August 4, 2012.<ref>{{cite web|title=Chart Highlights: Carrie Underwood Crowns Country Songs With 'Good Girl'|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/chartbeat/484791/chart-highlights-carrie-underwood-crowns-country-songs-with-good-girl|publisher=Billboard|access-date=June 5, 2012}}</ref> On June 13, 2012, the single moved ten positions to nine on the ''Billboard'' Hot 100, and has sold 402,000 downloads.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/483096/katy-perrys-wide-awake-hits-big-before-3d-film |title=Katy Perry's 'Wide Awake' Hits Big Before 3D Film |publisher=Billboard |access-date=April 22, 2013}}</ref> The following week, the single reached number four, and on the chart dated August 11, 2012, it peaked at number two on the ''Billboard'' Hot 100, making the song Perry's eighth consecutive single to reach the top three of the chart since "[[California Gurls]]", as well as her first song to peak in the runner-up position. It was kept off the top spot by [[Carly Rae Jepsen]]'s "[[Call Me Maybe]]". On the week August 4, 2012, the song topped the [[Dance Club Songs]] chart, making her tenth consecutive number-one on the chart since 2009's "[[Waking Up in Vegas]]", extending her own record.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.billboard.com/column/chartbeat/chart-highlights-madonna-s-turn-up-the-radio-1007648152.story|title=Chart Highlights: Madonna's 'Turn Up the Radio' Debuts on Dance/Club Play Songs|access-date=July 23, 2012|publisher=Billboard.com}}</ref> The song has sold 3.5&nbsp;million copies in the nation as of August 2020<ref name="USsales">{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/articles/business/chart-beat/9441173/katy-perry-career-bests-american-idol|title=Ask Billboard: Katy Perry's Career Bests, From 'One of the Boys' to 'Smile,' & Every Hot 100 'Idol' Top 10|last=Trust|first=Gary|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|date=August 28, 2020|access-date=August 31, 2020}}</ref> and received a quintuple platinum certification from the [[Recording Industry Association of America]] (RIAA).<ref name=RIAA/>

In Australia, the song debuted at number 26 on the [[ARIA Charts|Australian Singles Chart]], and peaked at number four, position it stayed in for three weeks.<ref>{{cite web|title=Katy Perry – Wide Awake (song)|url=http://www.australian-charts.com/showitem.asp?interpret=Katy+Perry&titel=Wide+Awake&cat=s|website=Australiancharts.com|access-date=December 26, 2013}}</ref> In New Zealand, the song debuted at number 32, and peaked at number one on July 1, 2012, making it Perry's seventh single from the album ''Teenage Dream'' to peak inside the top 10, and her sixth song from the ''Teenage Dream'' era to take out the number one spot.<ref>{{cite web|author=Steffen Hung |url=https://charts.nz/showitem.asp?interpret=Katy+Perry&titel=Wide+Awake&cat=s |title=Katy Perry – Wide Awake |publisher=charts.nz |access-date=June 22, 2012}}</ref> The song was then certified [[Music recording sales certification|Gold]] by the [[Recording Industry Association of New Zealand]] (RIANZ). The song is the eighth song from ''Teenage Dream'' to enter the top ten, and the ninth consecutive top ten single since "[[Waking Up in Vegas]]" and its eleventh top ten overall. The song makes Perry the female artist with the most number ones on New Zealand (eight), passing the previous record of [[Mariah Carey]] with seven number one singles. In Canada, the song peaked at number one, becoming her eighth single to do so. It also peaked at number one on the Canadian Adult Contemporary (AC), CHR/Top 40, and Hot AC airplay charts monitored by [[Nielsen Broadcast Data Systems]]. The song became the 21st best selling single in New Zealand of 2012.<ref name="nztop40.co.nz">{{cite web|url=https://aotearoamusiccharts.co.nz/archive/annual-singles/2012-12-31 |title=Top Selling Singles of 2012 &#124; The Official New Zealand Music Chart |publisher=Nztop40.co.nz |date=December 31, 2012 |access-date=April 22, 2013}}</ref> In the United Kingdom, the song debuted at number 69 on the [[UK Singles Chart]], due to strong downloads in April 2012. The song then re-entered at number 74 in May 2012. It has peaked at number nine, selling 24,545 copies in that country.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.musicweek.com/story.asp?sectioncode=1&storycode=1050081 |title=Official Charts Analysis: Bieber beats Cheryl to No.1 by 3,181 sales |publisher=Music Week |access-date=April 22, 2013}}</ref> In Ireland, the song launched at number 30 and is currently peaking at number six.<ref>{{cite web|title=Katy Perry Wide Awake Chart |website=αCharts |url=http://acharts.us/song/69765|access-date=June 30, 2012}}</ref>

===Synopsis===
[[File:Katy-Perry-Wide-Awake-music-video-400x300.jpg|thumb|left|220px|Perry as she explores a [[crepuscular rays|crepuscular]] [[labyrinth]] with a lantern in the music video for "Wide Awake"]]
The video starts with Perry finishing shooting the topless cloud scene for the "[[California Gurls]]" video and going to her dressing room while talking with her assistant Johnny Wujek. She then takes off her wig, looks into the mirror, and the setting turns to a dark [[labyrinth]], with Perry holding a lantern. The camera pans up to show the entire labyrinth, while also revealing a hill with light shining through the clouds onto it. Perry sees a strawberry, picks it off and eats it, and the walls close in on her. Perry pushes the walls, as a firework shoots from her chest into the dark sky, a reference to her "[[Firework (Katy Perry song)|Firework]]" video. A door opens to reveal a younger version of Perry (Zoey Diaz), and their hands touch. The two reach a mirror in which the younger Perry does not have a reflection, and [[paparazzi]] taking pictures (one of whom is dressed as [[Freddy Krueger]]) are seen behind the mirror. The floor begins to crumble as Perry finally shatters the mirror and the shards of the glass become butterflies.

The little girl is then seen pushing a wheelchair in which Perry sits in a hospital gown, trembling with the poisoned strawberry in one hand. In the hallway, they are met by two [[minotaur]] guards who block their way. The little girl furiously goes up to them and stomps her foot, sending the minotaurs flying. Perry gets up from the wheelchair, grabbing the little girl's hand as they run down the hallway to the exit. The doors open to reveal a garden. A man dressed as [[Prince Charming]] (Jef Groff) appears riding a [[unicorn]], and walks to Perry with [[Crossed fingers|fingers crossed]] behind his back. Perry punches him in the face, sending him through the fence. Perry and the girl find a heart-shaped exit, and the two girls [[high-five]]. They hug each other before the little girl places something in Perry's closed fist. The little girl rides away into a suburban neighborhood on a bike with a [[license plate]] reading "Katheryn", which is Perry's birth name. Perry returns to her dressing room and opens her fist, revealing a butterfly. The camera pans to a concert stage where Perry stands on a rising platform, and sings the beginning bars of "[[Teenage Dream (Katy Perry song)|Teenage Dream]]" to a full audience as the butterfly flies into the arena.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/1687787/katy-perry-wide-awake-scenes/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140821034009/http://www.mtv.com/news/1687787/katy-perry-wide-awake-scenes/|url-status=dead|archive-date=August 21, 2014|title=Katy Perry's 'Wide Awake' Video: Five Key Scenes|first=James|last=Montgomery|website=MTV News}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|title=Katy Perry Uses New Video For 'Wide Awake' To Slam Russell Brand|url=http://www.hollywoodlife.com/2012/06/19/katy-perry-wide-awake-music-video-russell-brand/|date=June 19, 2012|author=Bonnie Fuller|access-date=July 25, 2012|archive-date=November 6, 2012|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121106135445/http://hollywoodlife.com/2012/06/19/katy-perry-wide-awake-music-video-russell-brand/|url-status=dead}}</ref>

===Release and reception===
The video was supposed to have its world premiere on June 19, 2012. However, the video was unexpectedly uploaded by [[EMI]] to the Czech website mixer.cz.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.mixer.cz/1/52260-wide-awake |title=Katy Perry: Wide Awake &#124; Mixér.cz |publisher=Mixer.cz |access-date=June 22, 2012 |archive-date=June 23, 2012 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120623223721/http://www.mixer.cz/1/52260-wide-awake |url-status=dead }}</ref> one day earlier. Due to this, it was announced that the June 19 premiere was being scrapped and that the release was pushed up to June 18, 2012, on [[MTV]] as part of ''MTV First: Katy Perry''.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1687675/katy-perry-wide-awake-video-tonight.jhtml |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120620174117/http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1687675/katy-perry-wide-awake-video-tonight.jhtml |url-status=dead |archive-date=June 20, 2012 |title=Katy Perry To Premiere 'Wide Awake' Video Tonight On MTV! – Music, Celebrity, Artist News |publisher=MTV.com |date=June 18, 2012 |access-date=June 22, 2012}}</ref> After the premiere, an exclusive 30-minute interview with Perry took place on MTV.com.<ref name="mtv.com" /> The music video was also shown on MTV's sibling networks [[VH1]], Logo and [[TeenNick]]. In addition, the video went into rotation immediately on [[mtvU]], MTV Hits and AMTV the following morning.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://mtvpress.com/press/release/katy_perrys_new_music_video_wide_awake_premiere_on_mtv |title=Releases |publisher=MTV Press |date=June 13, 2012 |access-date=June 22, 2012 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120623062009/http://mtvpress.com/press/release/katy_perrys_new_music_video_wide_awake_premiere_on_mtv |archive-date=June 23, 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref> Perry stated in an interview that the music video is symbolic of her life, where various scenes in the video are redolent of her rise to stardom, personal struggles, love and marriage.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.mtv.com/video-clips/srvess/katy-perry-feels-like-a-superhero-in-wide-awake-video|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170730023508/http://www.mtv.com/video-clips/srvess/katy-perry-feels-like-a-superhero-in-wide-awake-video|url-status=dead|archive-date=July 30, 2017|title=Katy Perry Feels 'Like A Superhero' In 'Wide Awake' Video (Video Clip)|website=MTV}}</ref>

The music video was met with acclaim from most critics, with a number of them deeming it the best video to date from Perry.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://buzzworthy.mtv.com/2012/06/18/katy-perry-wide-awake-video/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120620031549/http://buzzworthy.mtv.com/2012/06/18/katy-perry-wide-awake-video/|url-status=dead|archive-date=June 20, 2012|author= Jenna Hally Rubenstein|date=June 18, 2012|title=Video Premiere: Katy Perry, 'Wide Awake'}}</ref> E! Online opined in a Twitter status that they love the video and compared it to ''[[Alice's Adventures in Wonderland]]''.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://twitter.com/eonline/status/215132847114162176|title=E! Online Twitter|date=June 19, 2012}}</ref> ''[[Rolling Stone]]'' gave the clip a positive review, praising its introspective nature, saying that "though the clip recalls familiar looks, the video puts a new spin on it all, recasting every colorful look with a melancholy tone and a darker palette."<ref name="rollingstone1">{{cite magazine|url=https://www.rollingstone.com/music/videos/katy-perry-looks-back-in-wide-awake-20120619#ixzz1yLg5vpB8|date=June 19, 2012|title=Katy Perry Looks Back in 'Wide Awake'|magazine=[[Rolling Stone]]|access-date=September 18, 2017|archive-date=June 22, 2012|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120622044328/http://www.rollingstone.com/music/videos/katy-perry-looks-back-in-wide-awake-20120619#ixzz1yLg5vpB8|url-status=dead}}</ref> ''Hollywoodlife'' felt that the video was inspiring, while calling Perry a symbol of strength.<ref>{{cite web |author= Chloe Melas |title= Katy Perry Admits: 'Wide Awake' Is About Russell Brand |publisher= Hollywoodlife |date= June 19, 2012 |url= http://www.hollywoodlife.com/2012/06/19/katy-perry-wide-awake-video-russell-brand/ |access-date= June 20, 2012 |archive-date= June 20, 2012 |archive-url= https://web.archive.org/web/20120620213643/http://www.hollywoodlife.com/2012/06/19/katy-perry-wide-awake-video-russell-brand/ |url-status= dead }}</ref> ''[[Entertainment Weekly]]'' also credited the fantastical, whimsical vibe of the visual as Perry teams up with her younger self.<ref name="rollingstone1" /> Perry received three nominations for the video at the [[2012 MTV Video Music Awards]], winning the prize for Best Art Direction.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.mtv.com/vma/2012|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160713073507/http://www.mtv.com/vma/2012|url-status=dead|archive-date=July 13, 2016|title=VMA 2012 – MTV Video Music Awards|website=MTV}}</ref> On April 23, 2023, the video surpassed 1&nbsp;billion views.

===Lyric video===
A [[lyric video]] of the track was released on May 20, 2012, when Perry was set to premiere the song at the [[2012 Billboard Music Awards|2012 ''Billboard'' Music Awards]]. The video implemented the use of the Facebook timeline and displayed a range of posts about the release of the ''Teenage Dream'' album, the seven singles (from "California Gurls" to "[[Part of Me (Katy Perry song)|Part of Me]]") and the various achievements for the singles and the album.<ref>{{cite web|title=Katy Perry uses Facebook Timeline for new 'Wide Awake' video |url=http://www.digitalspy.co.uk/music/news/a382767/katy-perry-uses-facebook-timeline-for-new-wide-awake-video-watch.html|publisher=Digital Spy. Hachette Filipacchi Médias|first=Lewis|last=Corner|date=May 21, 2012|access-date=June 28, 2012}}</ref> The lyric video received positive reviews from critics with many praising Perry's different approach to lyric videos on YouTube. Contessa Gayles from [[AOL|AOL Music]] commented that the video "is practically a music video in itself".<ref>{{cite web|last=Contessea|first=Gayle|title=Katy Perry 'Wide Awake': Billboard Music Awards Performer Debuts Lyric Video|url=http://blog.music.aol.com/2012/05/21/katy-perry-wide-awake-billboard-music-awards-performance-lyric-video/|date=May 21, 2012|publisher=[[AOL]]|access-date=June 5, 2012|archive-date=January 14, 2019|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190114210501/http://blog.music.aol.com/2012/05/21/katy-perry-wide-awake-billboard-music-awards-performance-lyric-video/|url-status=dead}}</ref> Jenna Rubenstein from MTV Buzzworthy stated that "it's a clever and emotional way to recall her past successes and offer fans some closure on this chapter of the Katy Perry journey."<ref>{{cite web|last=Rubenstein|first=Jenna|date=May 22, 2012|title=Watch Katy Perry's 'Wide Awake' Lyric Video!|url=http://buzzworthy.mtv.com/2012/05/22/katy-perry-wide-awake-lyric-video/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120605213338/http://buzzworthy.mtv.com/2012/05/22/katy-perry-wide-awake-lyric-video/|archive-date=June 5, 2012|access-date=June 5, 2012|publisher=MTV Buzzworthy. ==Live performances==
[[File:Katy Perry live in Berlin (19403745663).jpg|thumb|Perry performing "Wide Awake" in Berlin in 2015]]
Perry debuted the song in an acrobatic fashion for the first time during the [[2012 Billboard Music Awards|2012 ''Billboard'' Music Awards]] on May 20, 2012, where she floated over the stage in a white ribbon swing keeping her suspended in the air while several background dancers performed with [[aerial silk]].<ref name="Wide Awake Debut">{{cite web|last=Vena|first=Jocelyn|title=Katy Perry Flies High At Billboard Music Awards|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1685463/katy-perry-part-of-me-wide-awake-billboard-music-awards.jhtml|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120524010005/http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1685463/katy-perry-part-of-me-wide-awake-billboard-music-awards.jhtml|url-status=dead|archive-date=May 24, 2012|publisher=MTV|access-date=May 20, 2012}}</ref> On June 17, 2012, Perry performed "Wide Awake" at [[2012 MuchMusic Video Awards]], in which she stood on a raised platform, beginning the song as a cocoon and turned into a butterfly when she sang the last chorus.<ref>{{cite web|title=2012 MuchMusic Video Awards: Justin Bieber & Katy Perry Bring Music Madness To MuchMusic Stage (VIDEOS)|url=http://globalgrind.com/music/muchmusic-video-awards-2012-mmvas-performances-justin-bieber-katy-perry-videos|author= Brittany Lewis|date= June 18, 2012|publisher= Global Grind}}</ref> On June 26, Perry performed "Wide Awake" on ''[[Jimmy Kimmel Live!]]''. For [[Barack Obama presidential campaign, 2012|President Barack Obama's 2012 presidential candidacy]], Perry performed several of her songs dressed as a [[ballot]], including "[[Teenage Dream (Katy Perry song)|Teenage Dream]]", "[[Firework (song)|Firework]]", and "Wide Awake".<ref name=holly1>{{cite web|last=Daunt|first=Tina|title=Katy Perry Performs for Obama at Las Vegas Rally|url=http://www.hollywoodreporter.com/news/obama-rally-katy-perry-performs-383053|work=[[The Hollywood Reporter]]|date=October 24, 2012|publisher=[[Prometheus Global Media]]|access-date=November 4, 2012}}</ref><ref name=mtvobama>{{cite web|last=Vena|first=Jocelyn|title=Katy Perry, Dressed As Human Ballot, Rallies For Obama In Vegas|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1696210/katy-perry-ballot-dress-obama-fundraiser.jhtml|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121027191727/http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1696210/katy-perry-ballot-dress-obama-fundraiser.jhtml|url-status=dead|archive-date=October 27, 2012|work=MTV|publisher=Viacom|access-date=November 4, 2012}}</ref> Expressing solidarity for his campaign, the box next to Obama's name was shaded.<ref name=holly1 /><ref name=mtvobama />
