"'''Sippin' on Fire'''" is a song written by Cole Taylor, Matt Dragstrem and [[Rodney Clawson]], and recorded by American [[country music]] duo [[Florida Georgia Line]].  It is the third single from their second studio album, ''[[Anything Goes (Florida Georgia Line album)|Anything Goes]]''.<ref name="tasteofcountry" />

==Critical reception==
Website ''[[Taste of Country]]'' reviewed the single favorably, saying that "After pushing the edges of their sound with “Dirt” and “Sun Daze,” Hubbard, [[Brian Kelley]] and Moi return home with “Sippin’ on Fire,” the third single from Anything Goes. It’s a song that could have found its way on to Here’s to the Good Times."<ref>{{cite web|url=http://tasteofcountry.com/florida-georgia-line-sippin-on-fire/|title=Florida Georgia Line, 'Sippin' on Fire' [Listen]|date=18 February 2015|work=[[Taste of Country]]|accessdate=23 July 2015}}</ref>

==Commercial performance==
The song has sold 513,000 copies in the US as of July 2015.<ref name="us sales">{{cite news |url=http://roughstock.com/news/2015/07/36496-top-30-digital-country-singles-july-22-2015 |title=The Top 30 Digital Country Singles: July 22, 2015 |author=Matt Bjorke |work=Roughstock |date=July 22, 2015}} Sales figure given [https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1hXiKW7vx_pGwqvoW5NIuHk2CzeWksRGyPJDytH0eWyI/edit?pli=1#gid=0 here]</ref> It was certified Platinum by the RIAA on January 29, 2016.<ref name=RIAA />

==Music video==
The music video was directed by [[Marc Klasfeld]] and premiered in March 2015. It features the duo and a full band performing the song from within a ring of fire in the [[Mojave Desert]], and scenes of a man and woman walking towards each other from afar, eventually meeting at night and caressing around the ring of fire. Their images "melt away" in the fire as the video goes on.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cmt.com/videos/florida-georgia-line/1183632/sippin-on-fire.jhtml|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150328193257/http://www.cmt.com/videos/florida-georgia-line/1183632/sippin-on-fire.jhtml|url-status=dead|archive-date=March 28, 2015|title=CMT : Videos : Florida Georgina Line : Sippin' on Fire|publisher=[[Country Music Television]]|accessdate=March 26, 2015}}</ref>
