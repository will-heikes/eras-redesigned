"'''Fall Down'''" is a song by American recording artist [[will.i.am]] featuring [[Miley Cyrus]] from his fourth [[studio album]], ''[[willpower (will.i.am album)|#willpower]]'' (2013). It was released on April 16, 2013, by [[Interscope Records]] as the fifth single from the album. The song was written and produced by will.i.am, [[Dr. Luke]], [[Benny Blanco]], and [[Cirkut (record producer)|Cirkut]]. "Fall Down" is an [[electropop]] and [[Hip hop music|hip hop]] song; it strays from the [[Electronic dance music|electronic dance]] elements displayed in will.i.am's earlier singles "[[Scream & Shout]]" and "[[thatPower|#thatPower]]", and instead leans towards an [[urban contemporary]] style.

"Fall Down" received generally negative reviews from [[Music journalism|music critics]], who were disappointed with its overall production and drew comparisons to the work of American recording artist [[Kesha]]. It peaked at number 58 on the U.S. [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]], and charted in the lower ends of several [[record chart]]s worldwide. However, the song reached numbers 14 and 15 on the Australian [[ARIA Charts]] and the [[Recorded Music NZ|Official New Zealand Music Chart]], respectively, being certified gold in both countries. In the United States when it was planned to be a single, the track was promoted with live performances on ''[[Jimmy Kimmel Live!]]'' and ''[[Good Morning America]]'', weeks later it was confirmed the single release was cancelled.

"Fall Down" marks the first of three collaborations between will.i.am and Cyrus in 2013. In October, he was credited as a songwriter and producer on the track "Do My Thang" from her fourth studio album ''[[Bangerz]]'',<ref>{{cite AV media notes|title=Bangerz|others=[[Miley Cyrus]]|year=2013|publisher=[[RCA Records]]}}</ref> while Cyrus was featured on his track "[[Feelin' Myself (will.i.am song)|Feelin' Myself]]" from the [[reissue]] of his fourth studio album ''[[willpower (will.i.am album)|#willpower]]'' in November.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://mashable.com/archive/miley-cyrus-william-feelin-myself|title=Miley Cyrus Stars in Will.i.am's 'Feelin' Myself' Video|author=Neha Prakash|publisher=[[Mashable]]|date=November 26, 2013|access-date=January 3, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140104205629/http://mashable.com/2013/11/26/miley-cyrus-william-feelin-myself/|archive-date=January 4, 2014|url-status=live|df=mdy-all}}</ref> will.i.am first became interested in collaborating with Cyrus after hearing an earlier version of her single "[[Wrecking Ball (Miley Cyrus song)|Wrecking Ball]]", and came in contact with her through producer [[Mike Will Made It]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://live.wsj.com/video/william-on-working-with-pop-star-miley-cyrus/11957D89-0B81-423A-AE1C-648B404388AA.html#!11957D89-0B81-423A-AE1C-648B404388AA|title=will.i.am on Working with Pop Star Miley Cyrus|newspaper=[[The Wall Street Journal]]|publisher=[[News Corp (2013–present)|News Corp]]|date=September 30, 2013|access-date=January 3, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131123013152/http://live.wsj.com/video/william-on-working-with-pop-star-miley-cyrus/11957D89-0B81-423A-AE1C-648B404388AA.html#!11957D89-0B81-423A-AE1C-648B404388AA|archive-date=November 23, 2013|url-status=live|df=mdy-all}}</ref>

In contrast from the [[electronic dance music]] elements presented in "Scream & Shout" and "#thatPower", "Fall Down" leans towards an [[urban contemporary]] musical style.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.take40.com/news/39192/listen:-miley-cyrus-new-song-with-will.i.am-%27fall-down%27---listen-here! |title=Miley Cyrus New Song With Will.I.Am 'Fall Down' - Listen Here! |publisher=Take 40 Australia |date=April 16, 2013 |access-date=January 3, 2014 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131103221028/http://www.take40.com/news/39192/listen%3A-miley-cyrus-new-song-with-will.i.am-%27fall-down%27---listen-here%21 |archive-date=November 3, 2013 }}</ref> The chorus incorporates [[electropop]] elements, while the verses tend towards a [[Hip hop music|hip hop]] format, while the track itself culminates with an orchestral bridge.<ref name="Idolator">{{cite web|url=http://www.idolator.com/7452783/miley-cyrus-fall-down-will-i-am|title=Miley Cyrus & Will.i.am Team Up For "Fall Down": Listen|author=Sam Lansky|publisher=Idolator. ==Critical reception==
[[File:Ke$ha Today Show2 2012.jpg|thumb|upright|Critics compared "Fall Down" to Dr. Luke's collaborations with Kesha (''pictured'').]]
"Fall Down" received generally negative reviews from [[Music journalism|music critics]], who were disappointed with its overall production. Writing for [[AllMusic]], Fred Thomas was displeased that featuring "big gun" Cyrus still resulted in a "manufactured disposable pop moment", and further elaborated that the song felt like one of ''#willpower''{{'}}s "interminable 15 tracks [that] were written in the studio moments before they were recorded."<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.allmusic.com/album/willpower-mw0002320063|title=#willpower - will.i.am|author=Fred Thomas|publisher=[[AllMusic]]. [[Rovi Corporation]]|access-date=January 4, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131226112408/http://www.allmusic.com/album/willpower-mw0002320063|archive-date=December 26, 2013|url-status=live|df=mdy-all}}</ref> Sam Lansky from [[Idolator (website)|Idolator]] shared a similar sentiment, opining that Cyrus felt "mostly phoned-in" by comparison with the "emotional punch" Cyrus delivered on her collaboration with American rapper [[Snoop Dogg|Snoop Lion]], "[[Ashtrays and Heartbreaks]]".<ref name="Idolator"/> Andy Peterson of Contactmusic.com criticized the production of the track, stating that listeners are "treated to the sort of identikit trance-plus-autotune sound that's been annexing American teen culture in the last couple of years".<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.contactmusic.com/album-review/william-willpower|title=Will.i.am - #Willpower Album Review|author=Andy Peterson|date=22 May 2013|publisher=[[Contactmusic.com]]|access-date=January 4, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131103013839/http://www.contactmusic.com/album-review/william-willpower|archive-date=November 3, 2013|url-status=live|df=mdy-all}}</ref>

Writing for [[entertainment.ie]], Karen Lawler criticized lyrics like "you could be my Coca Cola, let me sip it up" for acting as a substitute for the "articulate rhymes on which [will.i.am] built his reputation",<ref>{{cite web|url=http://entertainment.ie/music/news/Album-Review-william-Willpower/178657.htm|title=Album Review: will.i.am - 'Willpower'|author=Karen Lawler|publisher=[[entertainment.ie]]. ==Commercial performance==
In the United States, "Fall Down" peaked at number 58 on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]]. It also charted at number four on the [[Dance/Electronic Songs]] component chart.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/artist/431547/william/chart?f=379|title=will.i.am - Chart history|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|publisher=[[Prometheus Global Media]]|access-date=January 4, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140601071141/http://www.billboard.com/artist/431547/william/chart?f=379|archive-date=June 1, 2014|url-status=live|df=mdy-all}}</ref> The track reached number 15 on the [[Canadian Hot 100]], which is also organized by ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]''.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/artist/431547/william/chart?f=793|title=will.i.am - Chart history|magazine=Billboard|publisher=Prometheus Global Media|access-date=January 4, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140601125211/http://www.billboard.com/artist/431547/william/chart?f=793|archive-date=June 1, 2014|url-status=live|df=mdy-all}}</ref> In Europe, the track experienced varying commercial success.
