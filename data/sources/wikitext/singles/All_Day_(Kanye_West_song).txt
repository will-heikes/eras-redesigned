"'''All Day'''" is a song by American rapper [[Kanye West]]. It features Trinidadian-American rapper [[Theophilus London]], Canadian rapper [[Allan Kingdom]], and English musician [[Paul McCartney]]. The song was [[Hip hop production|produced]] by West and ten others, including [[Sean Combs|Puff Daddy]], [[French Montana]] and [[Travis Scott]]. Having initially leaked in August 2014 after being announced for release as a [[Single (music)|single]] by West, he premiered the song on March 2, 2015 via [[KPWR|Power 106]] and [[WQHT|Hot 97]]. The following day, it was released for [[Music download|digital download]] and [[Streaming media|streaming]] as a standalone single, through [[GOOD Music]] and [[Def Jam Recordings|Def Jam]]. The song contains a re-recorded sample of "[[Wild Life (Wings album)|When the Wind Is Blowing]]" by [[Paul McCartney and Wings]] (known colloquially as "Two Fingers") and an [[Interpolation (popular music)|interpolation]] from "Dance with Me" by Jamaican singer Noel Ellis. It includes elements of [[Industrial music|industrial]] and [[drill music]], alongside [[synthesizer]]s. Rapping in a fast flow, West's lyrics center around his success in life and the music industry.

The accompanying [[Steve McQueen (director)|Steve McQueen]]-directed [[music video]] was announced in March 2015 and ultimately released in February 2016. The video sees West in a warehouse room running around, though he becomes tired and a song entitled "I Feel Like That" is played. "All Day" received widely positive reviews from music critics, who mostly praised West's vocals. The focus around his vocals was often placed on the flow and lyrical content, while a few critics complimented the catchiness of the song. It was named to year-end lists for 2015 by multiple publications, including ''[[NME]]'' and ''[[Pitchfork (website)|Pitchfork]]''. At the [[58th Annual Grammy Awards|58th Grammy Awards]], the song was nominated for both [[Grammy Award for Best Rap Song|Best Rap Song]] and [[Grammy Award for Best Rap Performance|Best Rap Performance]].

"All Day" peaked within the top 20 of music charts in the United States, the United Kingdom, and Scotland. It has since received platinum certification by the [[Recording Industry Association of America]] and silver by the [[British Phonographic Industry]] (BPI) in the US and UK, respectively. The song was first performed by West at the [[2015 Brit Awards]], with the performance including flamethrowers and accompanied by numerous other rappers. West later performed it live on multiple occasions in 2015, including at the [[2015 NBA Playoffs|NBA Playoffs]] and [[Glastonbury Festival 2015|Glastonbury Festival]].

==Background and recording==
The song was one of three collaborations between West and McCartney to be released, with the other two being the [[Single (music)|singles]] "[[Only One (Kanye West song)|Only One]]" (2014) and "[[FourFiveSeconds]]" (2015), the latter of which includes Barbadian singer [[Rihanna]] alongside the two.<ref name="paul">{{cite magazine|last1=Holmes|first1=Charles|url=https://www.rollingstone.com/music/music-news/kanye-west-paul-mccartney-album-722785/|title=Kanye West Offered to Produce a Paul McCartney Album|magazine=[[Rolling Stone]]|url-access=limited|date=September 11, 2018|access-date=June 5, 2019|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190605193631/https://www.rollingstone.com/music/music-news/kanye-west-paul-mccartney-album-722785/|archive-date=June 5, 2019|url-status=live}}</ref> In response to criticism of West using the n-word within the song, McCartney stated: "It's a great record, sonically it's brilliant, but quite a few people said, 'You can't be connected with this, there's, like, 40 N-words!"<ref name="word"/> McCartney also said in reference to the usage of the word that he "was actually pleased with it."<ref name="word"/> Allan Kingdom became involved with West through [[Plain Pat]], who was close to West.<ref name="allan">{{Cite web|last=Nostro|first=Lauren|url=https://www.complex.com/music/2015/03/allan-kingdom-all-day-interview/|title=Interview: Allan Kingdom Speaks on His 'All Day' Feature and Working With Kanye West|work=[[Complex (magazine)|Complex]]|date=March 13, 2015|access-date=July 4, 2019|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190218141708/https://www.complex.com/music/2015/03/allan-kingdom-all-day-interview|archive-date=February 18, 2019|url-status=live}}</ref> A phone call was made for Allan Kingdom to join West and Plain Pat's session; he showed West his music during the session and called doing so "nerve-racking."<ref name="allan"/> One month after West announced the release of the song, a snippet of it leaked online.<ref name="gq"/><ref name="time">{{Cite magazine|last=Locker|first=Melissa|url=https://time.com/3103849/kanye-west-all-day-leak-new-single/|title=Kanye West's New Single 'All Day' Springs a Leak: Listen|magazine=[[Time (magazine)|Time]]|date=August 12, 2014|access-date=May 1, 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180716231525/http://time.com/3103849/kanye-west-all-day-leak-new-single/|archive-date=July 16, 2018|url-status=live}}</ref> Melissa Locker of ''[[Time (magazine)|Time]]'' called the snippet "a promising hint" of what will be released by West.<ref name="time"/> "All Day" was premiered live by West at the [[2015 Brit Awards]] on February 25.<ref name="complex"/> On March 2, 2015, ''[[Rolling Stone]]'' provided the first official stream of the track.<ref name="rollingstone1">{{cite magazine |last=Grow |first=Kory |url=https://www.rollingstone.com/music/news/hear-kanye-wests-triumphant-new-all-day-20150302 |title=Hear Kanye West's Triumphant New 'All Day' |magazine=Rolling Stone |url-access=limited |date=March 2, 2015 |access-date=March 3, 2015 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20171021000604/http://www.rollingstone.com/music/news/hear-kanye-wests-triumphant-new-all-day-20150302 |archive-date=October 21, 2017 |url-status=live }}</ref>

"All Day" includes [[Industrial music|industrial]] elements, reminiscent of West's sixth studio album ''[[Yeezus]]'' (2013).<ref>{{cite web|author=Zo|url=https://www.okayplayer.com/news/kanye-west-all-day-cdq-mp3.html|title=Kanye West - 'All Day' (CDQ)|publisher=[[Okayplayer]]|date=2015|url-access=registration|access-date=June 24, 2019|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190624120503/https://www.okayplayer.com/news/kanye-west-all-day-cdq-mp3.html|archive-date=June 24, 2019|url-status=live}}</ref> The song also includes elements of [[drill music]], as well as [[synthesizer]]s in its composition.<ref name="rollingstone1"/><ref name="pitchfork">{{cite web|last1=Greene|first1=Jayson|last2=Avissar|first2=Erez|url=https://pitchfork.com/reviews/tracks/17354-kanye-west-all-day-ft-allan-kingdom-theophilus-london-and-paul-mccartney/|title='All Day' [ft. ==Release and artwork==
"All Day" was announced for release as a single by West in July 2014.<ref name="gq">{{cite news |last=Baron |first=Zach |url=https://www.gq.com/story/kanye-west |title=Kanye West's GQ Profile: A Brand-New Ye |work=[[GQ]] |date=July 21, 2014 |access-date=June 9, 2019 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190601150358/https://www.gq.com/story/kanye-west |archive-date=June 1, 2019 |url-status=live }}</ref> On March 2, 2015, West premiered the studio version of the song on [[KPWR|Power 106]] and [[WQHT|Hot 97]].<ref name="Credits"/> The next day, the song was released to [[iTunes Store]]s in various countries by [[GOOD Music]] and [[Def Jam Recordings|Def Jam]], and made available for streaming on [[Spotify]].<ref name="Credits"/><ref name="download"/> On the same day, it was also serviced to US [[rhythmic contemporary]] radio stations through the labels.<ref name="radio"/> The song was ultimately scrapped from the track list of West's seventh studio album ''[[The Life of Pablo]]'' (2016).<ref>{{cite magazine |last=Spanos |first=Brittany |url=https://www.rollingstone.com/music/music-news/kanye-west-announces-albums-final-title-the-life-of-pablo-track-list-191137/ |title=Kanye West Reveals Album's Final Title, 'TLOP,' Track List |magazine=Rolling Stone |url-access=limited |date=February 11, 2016 |access-date=June 8, 2019 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190608172550/https://www.rollingstone.com/music/music-news/kanye-west-announces-albums-final-title-the-life-of-pablo-track-list-191137/ |archive-date=June 8, 2019 |url-status=live }}</ref>

The artwork for "All Day" was initially thought to be the album cover for West's then-upcoming album ''So Help Me God,'' eventually released with the title [[The Life of Pablo]], when he posted it before the song's release.<ref name="xxl">{{cite news |last=C.M. |first=Emmanuel |url=https://www.xxlmag.com/news/2015/03/kanye-wests-13th-century-virgin-mary-artwork-single-day-help-god-album/ |title=Kim Says Kanye West's 13th Century Virgin Mary Artwork is for His Single 'All Day' not 'So Help Me God' Album |work=[[XXL (magazine)|XXL]] |date=March 3, 2015 |access-date=June 29, 2019 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180925123734/http://www.xxlmag.com/news/2015/03/kanye-wests-13th-century-virgin-mary-artwork-single-day-help-god-album/ |archive-date=September 25, 2018 |url-status=live }}</ref> When West released the song, the information became known that the artwork was intended for the single instead.<ref name="xxl"/> West's wife [[Kim Kardashian]] revealed via [[Instagram]] that the artwork is a 13th Century monastic symbol for the [[Mary, mother of Jesus|Virgin Mary]].<ref name="xxl"/>

==Critical reception==
The song was met with widely positive reviews from music critics, with West's vocals gathering praise from the majority of them. [[Jayson Greene]] of ''[[Pitchfork (website)|Pitchfork]]'' dubbed it as "an odd hybrid" and elaborated, writing: "Kanye's rapping harder, faster, sharper than ever: Years and years into his evolving style, he's fashioned his voice into something dull and jagged at once, a hacked-off piece of [[Jet airliner|jetliner]] assaulting the beat."<ref name="pitchfork"/> Greene viewed West's rapping speed as sounding like American singer [[James Brown]] "imitating a [[Thompson submachine gun|tommy gun]]" at parts and also praised his lyrical content.<ref name="pitchfork"/> Kory Grow from ''Rolling Stone'' wrote that "the song shows off its triumphant synth line and bouncy beat, as well as West's one-liners like, 'You a fake Denzel like the [[Allstate]] nigga/If you run in to me, better have Allstate with you' and guest Allan Kingdom's soulful vocals."<ref name="rollingstone1"/>

Writing for ''[[Bustle (magazine)|Bustle]]'', Kenya Foy stated that "West's flow is absolutely crazy" and complimented the song's catchiness, describing it as having the ability to get "the most shiftless couch potato to abandon those comfy cushions" for dancing.<ref name="bustle"/> Foy elaborated, writing that it "also has the potential to put folks in a sulky mood" due to their daily activities paling in comparison to those undertaken by West.<ref name="bustle"/> ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]''{{'}}s Alex Gale commented that the song shows West "at his most unapologetically brash" and observed that he performs "over [[Machine gun|machine-gun]] [[Snare drum|snares]]" and synths reminiscent of ''Yeezus'', while Gale described West's lyricism as "nimble and quotable."<ref name="Gale">{{cite magazine|last1=Gale|first1=Alex|url=https://www.billboard.com/articles/business/6494591/single-review-kanye-west-all-day|title=Single Review: Kanye West, 'All in Paris]]" (2011), though criticized the composition of the later points within the song.<ref name="Hasty"/> Andrew Barber, Brendan Klinkenberg and Ross Scarano of ''[[Complex Networks ==Commercial performance==
Additionally, the song became West's second top-20 single in the United States in 2015.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/artist/kanye-west/chart-history/hsi/|title=Kanye West – Chart History|magazine=Billboard|access-date=June 28, 2019|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190617045449/https://www.billboard.com/music/kanye-west/chart-history/hot-100|archive-date=June 17, 2019|url-status=live}}</ref> "All Day" debuted at number 15 on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]], selling 140,000 downloads in its first week and this led to it entering atop of the US Rap Digital Songs chart.<ref name="billboard">{{cite magazine |last1=Mendizabal |first1=Amaya |url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/kanye-west-22nd-top-10-rb-hip-hop-hit-all-day/ |title=Kanye West Scores 22nd R&B/Hip-Hop Top 10 Hit With 'All Day' |magazine=Billboard |date=March 12, 2015 |access-date=January 27, 2019 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190127152811/https://www.billboard.com/articles/columns/chart-beat/6501979/kanye-west-22nd-top-10-rb-hip-hop-hit-all-day |archive-date=January 27, 2019 |url-status=live }}</ref> It is West's first single, as a lead artist, to debut at number one on the chart and followed "[[Clique (Kanye West, Jay-Z and Big Sean song)|Clique]]" (2012) as his second to reach the position.<ref name="billboard"/> It became the rapper's 22nd top 10 single on the US ''Billboard'' [[Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs]] chart, reaching number 6 on the chart.<ref name="billboard"/> It entered at number 14 on the US Rap Airplay chart with 11.8 million audience impressions logged, becoming West's 3rd highest debut on the chart and was also streamed 1.9 million times in the US that week.<ref name="billboard"/> The song was certified platinum in the United States by the [[Recording Industry Association of America]] on September 23, 2020.<ref name="riaa"/>

"All Day" debuted at number 18 on the [[UK Singles Chart]] for the issue date of March 8, 2015, while "FourFiveSeconds" peaked at number 3 on the same date.<ref>{{cite
