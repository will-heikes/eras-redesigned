"'''I Luh Ya Papi'''" is a song recorded by American singer [[Jennifer Lopez]] for her eighth studio album, ''[[A.K.A. (album)|A.K.A.]]'' (2014). Featuring guest vocals from American rapper [[French Montana]], the song was released on March 11, 2014 as the [[lead single]] from the album by [[Capitol Records]]. The song was written and produced by [[Detail (record producer)|Noel "Detail" Fisher]], with additional writing from [[Dre Moon|Andre Proctor]], [[French Montana|Karim Kharbouch]], and Lopez. [[Cory Rooney]] and Fisher provided vocal production for the song. Musically, "I Luh Ya Papi" is a [[hip-hop|hip hop]] song, backed with synths and metallic beats, that lyrically contains [[innuendoes]] and references to Lopez's body.

"I Luh Ya Papi" received generally positive reviews from music critics, who praised the song's catchy chorus, as well as its playfulness, but criticized its title. The song's accompanying music video was shot by director [[Jessy Terrero]], a previous collaborator of Lopez's. The video was filmed in [[Miami]], [[Florida]] and depicts her in fantasy, following a concept of Lopez objectifying males. The video was met with positive reviews from critics for confronting music industry [[sexism]]. The single was promoted with a performance on [[American Idol season 13|season thirteen]] of ''[[American Idol]]'', where Lopez also served as a judge, as well as on Lopez's concerts throughout 2014, including [[KPWR|Powerhouse 2014]] and her free concert in [[the Bronx]].

== Recording and writing ==
[[File:TinasheSoBs.jpg|right|thumb|upright|[[Tinashe]] recorded an early version of the song, originally titled "I Luh You Nigga".<ref name=thefader/>]]

"I Luh Ya Papi" originated as a song called "I Luh You Nigga" which was written by American singer-songwriter [[Tinashe]] for her debut studio album, ''[[Aquarius (Tinashe album)|Aquarius]]''.<ref name=thefader>{{cite news|last1=Trammell|first1=Matthew|title=Tinashe Wrote J. Lo's "I Luh Ya Papi"|url=http://www.thefader.com/2014/10/08/tinashe-wrote-j-los-i-luh-ya-papi|access-date=November 28, 2014|work=The Fader|date=October 8, 2014}}</ref><ref name=hotnewhiphop>{{cite news|last1=Lyons|first1=Patrick|title=Tinashe Reveals That She Wrote Jennifer Lopez's "I Luh Ya Papi"|url=https://www.hotnewhiphop.com/25228-tinashe-reveals-that-she-wrote-jennifer-lopez-s-i-luh-ya-papi-news|access-date=November 28, 2014|work=Hot New Hip-Hop|date=October 8, 2014}}</ref> According to Tinashe, she went on to an early session with [[record producer]] [[Detail (record producer)|Detail]] and they recorded the track, which was planned to be the singer's first single.<ref name=thefader/> Later, Detail was in a session with Jennifer Lopez and he played the track to her, prompting the singer to like it and cut her version. As claimed by Tinashe, "they switched the swag up a little bit," adding: "One day Detail calls me and is like 'B-T-dubs, J. Lo is singing that song.' I didn't believe it at first, I thought he was just not trying to give it to me for some reason. I was like 'J. Lo does not want that song, she's not gonna sing that.' And then it came out. I guess she wanted it."<ref name=thefader/><ref name=hotnewhiphop/>

"I Luh Ya Papi" was re-written by [[Detail (record producer)|Noel Fisher]], [[Dre Moon|Andre Proctor]], [[French Montana|Karim Kharbouch]] and [[Jennifer Lopez]], with production being handled by Noel "Detail" Fisher, who was also responsible for vocal production, along with [[Cory Rooney]]. It features a rap verse by [[French Montana]], whose vocals were recorded by Max Unruh at [[Doppler Studios]] in [[Atlanta]], while the song was mixed by Jayeen Joshua at Larrabee Sound Studios in [[North Hollywood, Los Angeles|North Hollywood, California]].<ref name="credits">{{cite AV media notes|others=[[Jennifer Lopez]]|title=[[A.K.A. (album)|A.K.A.]]|year=2014|type=booklet|publisher=[[Capitol Records]]|id=060253767098}}</ref>

== Critical response ==
"I Luh Ya Papi" was met with favorable reception from [[music journalism|music critics]], although there was criticism directed towards its title. A music video for "I Luh Ya Papi" was directed by [[Jessy Terrero]] in [[Miami]], [[Florida]].<ref name="Rap-Up.com: New Music">{{cite web|title=New Music: Jennifer Lopez f/ French Montana&nbsp;– 'I Luh Ya Papi'|url=http://www.rap-up.com/2014/03/05/new-music-jennifer-lopez-f-french-montana-i-luh-ya-papi/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140306131241/http://www.rap-up.com/2014/03/05/new-music-jennifer-lopez-f-french-montana-i-luh-ya-papi/|url-status=dead|archive-date=March 6, 2014|work=[[Rap-Up.com]]|publisher=Rap-Up, LLC|access-date=March 6, 2014|date=March 5, 2014}}</ref><ref name="JLo Premieres (MTV UK)">{{cite web|title=Jennifer Lopez Premieres New Single 'I Luh Ya Papi'|url=https://www.mtv.co.uk/news/vhvsmp/jennifer-lopez-premieres-new-single-i-luh-ya-papi|work=[[MTV News|MTV News UK]]|publisher=[[Viacom International Media Networks]]|access-date=March 6, 2014|date=March 6, 2004}}</ref> On February 12, 2014, Lopez was photographed on a large luxury yacht in Miami filming a music video.<ref>{{cite web|title=Jennifer Lopez Looks Sexy in White Hot Pants While Filming a Music Video|url=http://www.celebuzz.com/photos/jennifer-lopez-looks-sexy-in-white-hot-pants-while-filming-a-music-video-in-miami/jennifer-lopez-video-shoot-02122014-17/|work=[[Celebuzz]]|publisher=[[SpinMedia]]|access-date=March 7, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141227130824/http://www.celebuzz.com/photos/jennifer-lopez-looks-sexy-in-white-hot-pants-while-filming-a-music-video-in-miami/jennifer-lopez-video-shoot-02122014-17/|archive-date=December 27, 2014|url-status=dead}}</ref> Lopez was clothed in a "skin-tight long-sleeve cut-out top" and "white hot pants", while wearing gold sunglasses and sporting a "sleek sectioned ponytail". She was surrounded by shirtless men.<ref>{{cite web|last=Webber|first=Stephanie|title=Jennifer Lopez Wears Hot Pants, Films With Speedo-Clad Men in Miami for New Music Video|url=http://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-style/news/jennifer-lopez-wears-hot-pants-films-with-speedo-clad-men-in-miami-for-new-music-video-pictures-2014132|work=[[Us Weekly]]|publisher=Wenner Media|access-date=March 7, 2014|date=February 13, 2014}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|last=Mercedes Lara|first=Marla|title=Jennifer Lopez Filming With Shirtless Men in Miami|url=http://www.popsugar.com/Jennifer-Lopez-Filming-Shirtless-Men-Miami-34021806#opening-slide|publisher=PopSugar|access-date=March 7, 2014|date=February 13, 2014}}</ref> The clip for "I Luh Ya Papi" is a comic concept video that "speaks out about men objectifying women in 'every single video'".<ref name="TG">{{cite web|last=Buchanan|first=Daisy|title=J Lo 'sexism' video: Are we finally entering the post-twerk era?|url=https://www.telegraph.co.uk/women/womens-life/10704765/J-Lo-I-Luh-Ya-Papi-sexism-video-Are-we-finally-entering-the-post-twerk-era.html|work=[[Telegraph Media Group|The Telegraph]]|publisher=[[Telegraph Media Group]]|access-date=March 22, 2014|date=March 18, 2014}}</ref> Speaking of the music video, Lopez stated: "The director came up with the concept to kind of flip the rap video&nbsp;— to make me the rapper and, you know, just turn the tables in that way...instead of having girls in bathing suits, have guys in bathing suits. Instead of having me be the soft girl in the video, be the rapper who's in the mansion and the yacht. It was all meant to be in good fun, but you know, I knew people would get the joke."<ref>{{cite web|last=Iasimone|first=Ashley|title=Jennifer Lopez Interview: Star Opens Up About a 'Turning Point' in Her Life|url=http://popcrush.com/jennifer-lopez-interview-2014-album-i-luh-ya-papi-video/|work=PopCrush|publisher=PopCrush Network|access-date=March 27, 2014|date=March 21, 2014}}</ref>

=== Synopsis ===
[[File:jloiluhyapapivid.png|thumb|250px|Lopez surrounded by semi-naked men during the music video, while also wearing a jumpsuit inspired by her famous [[Green Versace dress of Jennifer Lopez|Green Versace jungle dress]].]]
The video opens with Lopez and two of her female friends consulting with a record label executive on treatment ideas for her new music video, who suggests that it be filmed in a water park or zoo. Her friends complain about females being objectified in music videos and begin fantasizing a concept video where Lopez objectifies males. Their fantasy video then begins with Lopez in a "morning-after scene", where she walks around a mansion filled with partly naked men ("for no reason"),<ref>{{cite web|last=Johnson|first=Zach|title=Jennifer Lopez Objectifies Men in "I Luh Ya Papi" Music Video, Rocks Familiar Looks—Watch Now!|url=http://au.eonline.com/news/521127/jennifer-lopez-objectifies-men-in-i-luh-ya-papi-music-video-rocks-familiar-looks-watch-now|publisher=E! Online Australia|access-date=March 22, 2014|date=March 14, 2014}}</ref> wearing a tracksuit inspired by her famed 2000 [[Green Versace dress of Jennifer Lopez|Green Versace jungle dress]].<ref>{{cite web|last=Chan|first=Stephanie|title=Jennifer Lopez's 'I Luh Ya Papi' Music Video|url=http://www.hollywoodreporter.com/news/jennifer-lopezs-i-luh-ya-688667|work=[[The Hollywood Reporter]]|access-date=March 22, 2014|date=March 17, 2014}}</ref> Then, Lopez and her friends (clothed in denim shorts) are seen dancing in a driveway, where they later view topless men cleaning cars. In a poolside scene, she and her friends are taking pictures of the men, and are fed by them. Later, Lopez is clothed in an all-white outfit on a yacht, where she and her friends continue objectifying the males. Towards the end of the clip, Lopez, French Montana and her friends all appear singing, rapping and dancing in a studio-lit setting.<ref name="GGG" />

=== Reception ===
Rosie Swash of ''[[The Guardian]]'' stated that "gender politics are confused but the outfits are fabulous".<ref name="GGG">{{cite web|last=Swash|first=Rosei|title=The best outfits from Jennifer Lopez's I Luh Ya Papi video|url=https://www.theguardian.com/fashion/fashion-blog/2014/mar/17/jennifer-lopez-i-luh-ya-papi-video-best-outfits|work=[[The Guardian]]|access-date=March 22, 2014|date=March 17, 2014}}</ref> Lily Rothman of ''[[Time (magazine)|Time]]'' magazine similarly asked, "If she and her girlfriends are upset enough about women being objectified in music videos to make a whole video skewering that tradition, why respond by objectifying other people?." Rothman also noted the two female back-up dancers who act as "decorative objects" for French Montana's appearance in the clip, and wrote: "They don't play characters, they don't really show off any particular dance skills, you can barely see their faces&nbsp;— it's pretty much a textbook case of the "[[video vixen]]" objectification that's derided in the video's intro."<ref>{{cite magazine|last=Rothman|first=Lily|title=Jennifer Lopez's Music Video for "I Luh Ya PaPi" Objectifies Men&nbsp;— And Maybe Women, Too|url=https://time.com/25000/jennifer-lopez-i-luh-ya-papi-video/|magazine=[[Time (magazine)|Time]]|access-date=March 22, 2014|date=March 14, 2014}}</ref> ''[[The Huffington Post]]''{{'}}s Emma Gray commented that, "There is something simply wonderful about seeing a mainstream pop star&nbsp;– one who has been working within the confines of music industry sexism for decades&nbsp;– explicitly call out a gender-based double standard". However, Gray noted the video's "empowering undertones" to fall apart in French Montana's scene: "With the entrance of a powerful male character, the scripts are immediately flipped back to normal."<ref>{{cite web|last=Gray|first=Emma|title=J.Lo's 'I Luh Ya Papi' Video Attempts To Tear Down Sexism|url=http://www.huffingtonpost.com/2014/03/17/j-lo-i-luh-ya-papi-music-video_n_4979363.html|work=[[The Huffington Post]]|access-date=March 22, 2014|date=March 17, 2014}}</ref> Speaking of the video's theme, Daisy Buchanann of ''[[Telegraph Media Group|The Telegraph]]'' wrote: "I suspect she's savvy enough to know that full frontal feminism would lead to a commercial disaster, and she knows she can't fix sexism within the industry with one video, but she can start a conversation about it."<ref name="TG" /> Nadeska Alexis of [[MTV News]] praised the video's role reversal, praising Lopez for "actually waving a flag for women in the accompanying music video, as she should, since March is Women's History Month and all." Alexis called the clip "Basically '[[Blurred Lines]]' For Women".<ref>{{cite web|last=Alexis|first=Nadeska|title=J.Lo's 'I Luh Ya Papi' Video Is Basically 'Blurred Lines' For Women|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1724166/jennifer-lopez-i-luh-ya-papi-video.jhtml|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140315014032/http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1724166/jennifer-lopez-i-luh-ya-papi-video.jhtml|url-status=dead|archive-date=March 15, 2014|work=[[MTV News]]|publisher=[[Viacom (2005–present)|Viacom]]|access-date=March 22, 2014|date=March 14, 2014}}</ref> The video racked up 9.5&nbsp;million views on Vevo during its first week of release.<ref>{{cite magazine|last1=Gruger|first1=William|title='I Luh Ya Papi' Pushes Jennifer Lopez to Top 10 on Social 50 Chart|url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/i-luh-ya-papi-pushes-jennifer-lopez-to-top-10-on-social-50-chart/|access-date=December 6, 2014|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|date=March 20, 2014}}</ref>

== Live performances ==
Lopez performed the song live on the March 20, 2014 episode of ''[[American Idol]]'', where she is a part of the judging panel. The performance opened with an a cappella introduction that featured Lopez singing with former contestants of the show, [[Jessica Sanchez]], [[Allison Iraheta]] and [[Pia Toscano]]. [[French Montana]] joined Lopez on stage towards the end of the performance. She wore a "skimpy" pair of denim cut-offs "paired with glittery tights and a furry parka", while being accessorized with "big earrings, white wedge sneakers and a tight bun".<ref>{{cite web|last=Maharana|first=Kabita|title=American Idol Season 13 Result Show: Jennifer Lopez Performs 'I Luh Ya Papi' [Video]|url=http://www.ibtimes.co.uk/american-idol-season-13-result-show-jennifer-lopez-performs-i-luh-ya-papi-video-1441233|work=International Business Times|publisher=IBT Media Inc.|access-date=March 22, 2014|date=March 21, 2014}}</ref> The stage, according to [[MRC (company)|Idolator]]'s Lisa Timmons, was a "throwback to the gritty fabulous Bronx in the '80s with Jennifer and a gang of fly girls".<ref>{{cite web|last=Timmons|first=Lisa|title='American Idol' Recap: Jennifer Lopez Debuts 'I Luh Ya Papi' Live & MK Nobilette Leaves|url=http://www.idolator.com/7510876/american-idol-recap-jennifer-lopez-i-luh-ya-papi-mk-nobilette|archive-url=https://archive.today/20140322053744/http://www.idolator.com/7510876/american-idol-recap-jennifer-lopez-i-luh-ya-papi-mk-nobilette|url-status=dead|archive-date=March 22, 2014|work=Idolator|publisher=SpinMedia|access-date=March 22, 2014|date=March 21, 2014}}</ref> However, not all of the reception was positive; some fans accused Lopez of [[Lip sync|lip syncing]] the performance.<ref>{{cite news|last1=Rainbird|first1=Ashleigh|title=American Idol judge Jennifer Lopez blasted by viewers for appearing to mime on the show|url=https://www.mirror.co.uk/3am/celebrity-news/american-idol-jennifer-lopez-appeared-3270082|access-date=December 6, 2014|work=[[Daily Mirror|Mirror]]|date=March 22, 2014}}</ref> The song was also on the [[setlist]] of Lopez's concert at [[KPWR|Power 106]]'s "Powerhouse 2014", on May 17, 2014,<ref name=rapup>{{cite news|title=J.LO, Wiz Khalifa, YG, Trey Songz, & Schoolboy Q Perform at Powerhouse 2014|url=http://www.rap-up.com/2014/05/18/j-lo-wiz-khalifa-yg-trey-songz-schoolboy-q-perform-at-powerhouse-2014/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140529165107/http://www.rap-up.com/2014/05/18/j-lo-wiz-khalifa-yg-trey-songz-schoolboy-q-perform-at-powerhouse-2014/|url-status=dead|archive-date=May 29, 2014|access-date=July 6, 2014|work=[[Rap-Up]]|date=May 18, 2014}}</ref> and at her [[Orchard Beach (Bronx)|Orchard Beach]] free concert, in [[the Bronx]], on June 4, 2014.<ref name=billboardbronx>{{cite magazine|last1=Hampp|first1=Andrew|title=Jennifer Lopez Brings 15 Years of Hits Home to The Bronx (Review)|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/events/live/6113697/jennifer-lopez-bronx-concert-review-setlist-video-pelham-bay-park|access-date=July 6, 2014|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|date=June 5, 2014}}</ref> During the latter, she performed it in three-part harmony while sitting on a neighborhood stoop set with her backup singers, including, once again, Toscano.<ref name=billboardbronx/>
