"'''Demons'''" is a song by American [[pop rock]] band [[Imagine Dragons]]. It was written by [[Alex da Kid]], [[Ben McKee]], [[Dan Reynolds]], [[Wayne Sermon]], Josh Mosser and produced by Alex da Kid. The song appears on their major-label debut [[extended play]], ''[[Continued Silence]]'' (2012), and also makes an appearance on their debut studio album, ''[[Night Visions]]'' (2012), as the fourth track. "Demons" was solicited to American [[adult album alternative|triple-A]] radio stations on January 28, 2013, and to [[modern rock]] stations two months later, on April 1, 2013. It serves as the album's overall fifth single and was released as the third single from ''Night Visions'' in the United States,<ref>{{cite magazine | url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/1572960/imagine-dragons-the-billboard-cover-story | title=Imagine Dragons: The Billboard Cover Story | magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]] | publisher=[[Prometheus Global Media]] | date=July 12, 2013 | access-date=August 24, 2013 | author=Waddell, Ray}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://playiga.com/imagine-dragons-top/ |title=Imagine Dragons – 'On Top Of The World' |publisher=[[Interscope Records]] |access-date=September 1, 2013 |archive-url=https://archive.today/20130901145416/http://playiga.com/imagine-dragons-top/ |archive-date=September 1, 2013 |url-status=usurped }}</ref><ref name="Kaufman"/> later released to US [[contemporary hit radio]] stations on September 17, 2013, as an official single.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.allaccess.com/top40-mainstream/future-releases|title=Top 40/M Future Releases – Mainstream Hit Songs Being Released and Their Release Dates ...|work=All Access}}{{Dead link|date=February 2026 |bot=InternetArchiveBot }}</ref> The lyrics portray the protagonist warning the significant other of his or her flaws.

The song was a commercial success, becoming their second top ten single after "[[Radioactive (Imagine Dragons song)|Radioactive]]".  It spent twelve weeks in the top ten on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]], four of which were spent at its number six peak. It is Imagine Dragons' second song to stay more than one year on the [[Billboard Hot 100|Hot 100]] (61 weeks).  Within two years of its release, more than 4.1 million copies were sold in the United States, making it the eighth most downloaded song in rock history at the time.<ref name="USSales">{{cite web|url=https://music.yahoo.com/photos/top-selling-rock-songs-slideshow/ |title=The 15 Most Downloaded Songs in Rock History |author=Christopher Polk |publisher=[[Yahoo! Music]] |date=January 31, 2015 |access-date=February 5, 2015 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150204045408/https://music.yahoo.com/photos/top-selling-rock-songs-slideshow/ |archive-date=February 4, 2015 }}</ref> It has also been a moderate commercial success worldwide, charting in several countries.  The song won a [[MuchMusic Video Award]] for [[MuchMusic Video Award for Best International Group Video|International Video of the Year – Group]] (2014) and an [[iHeartRadio|iHeartRadio Music Award]] for Alternative Rock Song of the Year (2014).

==Composition==
The song is written in the key of [[E-flat major|E{{music|flat}} major]] with a slow-moderate tempo of 90 beats per minute.<ref>[http://www.musicnotes.com/sheetmusic/mtd.asp?ppn=MN0136411 Imagine Dragons "Demons Sheet Music] musicnotes</ref> The chord progression is [[I–V–vi–IV progression|E{{music|flat}}-B{{music|flat}}-Cm-A{{music|flat}}]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Demons by Imagine Dragons Chords, Melody, and Music Theory Analysis - Hooktheory |url=https://www.hooktheory.com/theorytab/view/imagine-dragons/demons |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250812192529/https://www.hooktheory.com/theorytab/view/imagine-dragons/demons |archive-date=August 12, 2025 |access-date=December 18, 2025 |website=www.hooktheory.com |language=en |url-status=live }}</ref>

==Music video==
An [[MTV]] report on the "Demons" music video stated that it would "fit nicely with the artful imagery of '[[It's Time (song)|It's Time]]' and the thoroughly out-there puppet grappling of '[[Radioactive (Imagine Dragons song)|Radioactive]]'."<ref>{{cite web | url=http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1702434/imagine-dragons-demons-music-video-details.jhtml | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130225013118/http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1702434/imagine-dragons-demons-music-video-details.jhtml | url-status=dead | archive-date=February 25, 2013 | title=Imagine Dragons Reveal Details Of New 'Demons' Music Video | work=[[MTV]] | publisher=[[Viacom Media Networks]] | date=February 22, 2013 | access-date=August 24, 2013 | author=Montgomery, James}}</ref> Released on May 7, 2013, and shot in [[Las Vegas]], Nevada at the band's performance at [[The Theater at Virgin Hotels|The Joint]] on February 9, 2013, the video features a mix of live footage of the band and a companion narrative.<ref name="Kaufman">{{cite web | url=http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1711777/imagine-dragons-demons-video.jhtml | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130807224753/http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1711777/imagine-dragons-demons-video.jhtml | url-status=dead | archive-date=August 7, 2013 | title=Imagine Dragons Exorcise 'Demons' On New Single | work=[[MTV]] | publisher=[[Viacom Media Networks]] | date=August 4, 2013 | access-date=August 24, 2013 | author=Kaufman, Gil}}</ref> Various characters with personal hardships are depicted, including a grieving daughter, a man with [[Marfan syndrome]] who stares at himself in a mirror, a victim of parental abuse and a military veteran carrying his wounded comrade.<ref name="Kaufman"/><ref>{{cite AV media|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mWRsgZuwf_8|title=Imagine Dragons – Demons (Official)|date=May 7, 2013|work=YouTube}}</ref> The meaning imposed is that most do not see beyond the face that everyone puts on for the public. The clip ends with a dedication to Tyler Robinson (1995–2013), a fan of the band who died at the age of 17 in March 2013 following a battle with cancer.<ref name="Kaufman"/> The music reached the one billion view milestone on July 18, 2022.
