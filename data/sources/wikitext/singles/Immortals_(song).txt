"'''Immortals'''" is a song written and recorded by American [[Rock music|rock]] band [[Fall Out Boy]] for the 2014 [[Walt Disney Animation Studios]] film ''[[Big Hero 6 (film)|Big Hero 6]]''. An alternative version appears on the band's sixth studio album ''[[American Beauty/American Psycho]]'' (2015).<ref>[https://twitter.com/falloutboy/status/547823090496192512 Fall Out Boy Tweets Immortals] twitter.com/falloutboy. Retrieved December 26, 2014.</ref>

The "End Credits" single version was released by [[Walt Disney Records]] on October 14, 2014, as a [[music download|digital download]].<ref>{{cite magazine |last=Dornbush |first=Jonathon |title=Hear 'Immortals,' Fall Out Boy's song from the 'Big Hero 6' soundtrack |url=http://music-mix.ew.com/2014/10/14/big-hero-6-fall-out-boy-immortals/ |magazine=[[Entertainment Weekly]] |date=October 14, 2014 |access-date=October 20, 2014 |archive-date=October 23, 2014 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141023072351/http://music-mix.ew.com/2014/10/14/big-hero-6-fall-out-boy-immortals/ |url-status=dead }}</ref> A [[Gramophone record|7" vinyl]] bundled with a poster was released on December 16, 2014.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.disneymusicemporium.com/rgshop/fall-out-boy-immortals-from-big-hero-6/ |title=Big Hero 6 Bundle – Fall Out Boy 'Immortals' 7" Vinyl |publisher=[[Disney Music Group|Disney Music Emporium]] |access-date=October 18, 2014 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141018231540/http://www.disneymusicemporium.com/rgshop/fall-out-boy-immortals-from-big-hero-6/ |archive-date=October 18, 2014 |url-status=dead }}</ref> As of November 6, 2023, "Immortals" was certified [[RIAA certification|4× Platinum]] by the [[Recording Industry Association of America]] (RIAA) for one million units sold.

==Background and composition==
"Immortals" is composed in the key of [[F-sharp minor]].<ref>{{cite web |date=May 10, 2022 |title=Key & BPM of "Immortals" from Disney's Big Hero 6 |url=https://tunebat.com/Info/Immortals-DCappella/4OnLqETfycuXRG2wlRUzxo |access-date=May 10, 2022 |website=Tunebat.com}}</ref> Disney asked the band to write and perform the song for the film's sequence in which the ''[[Big Hero 6 (comics)|Big Hero 6]]'' team is transformed from a group of super smart individuals to a band of high-tech heroes. "We went over to Disney and met with the directors," bassist [[Pete Wentz]] recalled. "They explained the story to us. It was insane how much it lined up with the way our band saw the world."<ref name="Wentz">{{cite web |url=http://petewentz.com/post/99972018630/ill-be-the-guard-dog-of-all-your-fever-dreams |title=ill be the guard dog of all your fever dreams |publisher=PeteWentz.com |date=October 14, 2014 |access-date=October 25, 2014 |author=Wentz, Pete |author-link=Pete Wentz |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161014052832/http://petewentz.com/post/99972018630/ill-be-the-guard-dog-of-all-your-fever-dreams |archive-date=October 14, 2016 |url-status=dead }}</ref> Wentz later stated, "Disney asked the band to come in and talk to the directors so we did that.... and we discussed the scene and what our song would attempt to do. Then we went home and wrote and sent it to them- it was a back and forth from there. It was interesting to be a part of because our song was just a small part of a bigger thing and the challenge to just be a part of the fabric. It was a really cool experience."<ref>{{cite web|last1=Wentz|first1=Pete|title=Fall Out Boy on Tumblr - "little-starl0rd asked: Did Disney ask you to write a song for Big Hero 6 or did you write it first and they ask to use it? Was the inclusion of your song everything you hoped it would be? Anyone have a favorite character in the movie? disney asked us to come in a talk to the directors so we did that…. and we discussed the scene and what our song would attempt to do. then we went home and wrote and sent it to them- it was a back and forth from there. it was interesting to be a part of because our song was just a small part of a bigger thing and the challenge to just be a part of the fabric. it was a really cool experience. and i think hiro would be my favorite character- the rage and empathy and love he feels is so relatable to me. pw"|url=http://falloutboy.com/post/121042712497/did-disney-ask-you-to-write-a-song-for-big-hero-6|website=FallOutBoy.com|publisher=Fall Out Boy|access-date=8 June 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150608225523/http://falloutboy.com/post/121042712497/did-disney-ask-you-to-write-a-song-for-big-hero-6|archive-date=8 June 2015|date=8 June 2015|quote=disney asked us to come in a talk to the directors so we did that…. and we discussed the scene and what our song would attempt to do. then we went home and wrote and sent it to them- it was a back and forth from there. it was interesting to be a part of because our song was just a small part of a bigger thing and the challenge to just be a part of the fabric. it was a really cool experience.}}</ref>

Vocalist [[Patrick Stump]] said that the song's title was inspired by "the idea of this kid stepping up on behalf of his brother. Your victories aren't exclusively yours, so the fact that all of these people are coming together to help him get to the finish line is really exciting."<ref name="prnewsire">{{cite press release |url=http://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/walt-disney-records-big-hero-6-soundtrack-features-original-song-immortals-from-fall-out-boy-and-score-by-henry-jackman-279194941.html |title=Walt Disney Records' Big Hero 6 Soundtrack Features Original Song 'Immortals' From Fall Out Boy And Score By Henry Jackman |publisher=[[PR Newswire]] |access-date=October 18, 2014}}</ref> Wentz further elaborated: "They're testing out their new super suits for the first time in the scene. Some of it works—some of it doesn't. You get the sense that they're stepping into a bigger role. The story's in the DNA of Fall Out Boy. It is an authentic story and it's who our band is—we've always identified with the underdog."<ref name="prnewsire"/>

On December 15, when the band's upcoming ''[[American Beauty/American Psycho]]'' (2015) album was made available for preorder, "Immortals" was revealed to also be included as track 10. On January 12, 2015, the six yet-unreleased songs from the album, including "Immortals" were made available for streaming on [[YouTube]]. The band quipped, "according to [[Baymax]] this is his favorite song on the record".<ref>[http://falloutboy.com/post/108016850302/according-to-baymax-this-is-his-favorite-song-on according to baymax this is his favorite song on the record] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150115095627/http://falloutboy.com/post/108016850302/according-to-baymax-this-is-his-favorite-song-on |date=2015-01-15 }} falloutboy.com. Retrieved January 15, 2015.</ref> The album version and film versions slightly differ: on the film version the percussion "is all marching-band strident, but on the album version much of that power is stripped away."<ref>[http://www.popmatters.com/review/189892-fall-out-boy-american-beauty-american-psycho/ American Beauty / American Psycho]. [[Popmatters]]. Retrieved June 7, 2015.</ref>

A remix featuring [[Black Thought]] of [[The Roots]] appeared on the remixed album [[Make America Psycho Again|''Make America Psycho Again'']].

== International versions ==
The Italian version was performed by Patrick Stump, even though he did not speak the language. Rick Dempsey, senior vice president of [[Disney Character Voices International]], said, "he’d sing every phrase again and again and just change the pronunciation just a little bit until the person listening—who spoke fluent Italian—said, ‘That’s it!’".<ref>{{Cite web|date=2016-12-16|title=Making Movie Magic in Any Language|url=https://d23.com/making-movie-magic-in-any-language/|access-date=2020-06-03|website=D23|language=en-US}}</ref>
The Spanish version was performed by Spanish girl group [[Sweet California]], titled "Inmortales".

==Music video==
A music video was released with the song, depicting the four members of the band walking up to a [[jukebox]] and choosing to play the single. The vinyl record playing the song is white and depicts the face of [[Baymax]] from ''Big Hero 6''. The concept of the music video is similar to that of [[Green Day]]'s "[[X-Kid]]".

A second music video for the band's album version of "Immortals" was released on December 24, 2014, composed of scenes taken from ''Big Hero 6'' intertwined with Fall Out Boy performing live. It was released on the 10th day of "12 Days of FOB", an event during which the band posted exclusive videos, pictures or lyrics.<ref>[http://www.panarmenian.net/eng/news/186647/ Fall Out Boy debuts music vid for "Big Hero 6" OST “Immortals”] www.panarmenian.net. Retrieved December 26, 2014.</ref>

==Appearances==
A snippet of the song first appeared unannounced in a teaser trailer for ''Big Hero 6''.<ref name=frmx>[http://fdrmx.com/fall-out-boy-immortals-single-review/ Fall Out Boy: ‘Immortals’ Single Review] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141021140604/http://fdrmx.com/fall-out-boy-immortals-single-review/ |date=2014-10-21 }} fdrmx.com. Retrieved October 18, 2014.</ref>
 
Fall Out Boy performed "Immortals" live for the first time on ''[[Jimmy Kimmel Live!]]''.<ref>[http://www.kerrang.com/24729/fall-out-boy-debut-immortals-on-jimmy-kimmel/ FALL OUT BOY DEBUT IMMORTALS ON JIMMY KIMMEL] ''[[Kerrang!]]''. Retrieved November 9, 2014.</ref><ref>[http://www.rocksound.tv/news/read/fall-out-boy-take-to-kimmel-to-perform-immortals FALL OUT BOY TAKE TO KIMMEL TO PERFORM ‘IMMORTALS’] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141107021747/http://www.rocksound.tv/news/read/fall-out-boy-take-to-kimmel-to-perform-immortals |date=2014-11-07 }} ''[[Rocksound]]''. Retrieved November 9, 2014.</ref> Stump accidentally sang a different version of the song on that performance.<ref>[https://twitter.com/petewentz/status/536240227270918144 Pete Wentz Tweet on Immortals version] twitter.com/petewentz. Retrieved December 24, 2014.</ref> It was also performed on Japanese TV.

It was being used as a promo for the 2015&ndash;16 NFL Thursday Night Games on CBS and NFL Network.

[[NHL on ESPN|ESPN]] used "Immortals" for their coverage of the [[2023 NHL All-Star Game]], beginning with the Skills Competition on February 3, followed by the game the next day on [[NHL on ABC|ABC]]. ESPN later integrated the instrumental parts into their coverage for the rest of the season.

==Commercial reception==
During the week ending November 29, 2014, "Immortals" sold 25,000 downloads and peaked at No. 9 on the US ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' [[Hot Rock Songs]] and No. 1 on the US [[Bubbling Under Hot 100]].<ref>[https://www.billboard.com/pro/foo-fighters-sonic-highways-top-rock-albums/ Foo Fighters Take 'Highways' To No. 1 on Top Rock Albums Chart]. ''Billboard''. Retrieved December 3, 2014.</ref><ref name="BillboardBIZ">{{cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/biz/search/charts?f%5b0%5d0=ts_chart_artistname%3AFall%20Out%20Boy&f%5b1%5d=ss_chart_search_title%3A%2AImmortals%2A&f%5b2%5d=ss_bb_type%3Achart_item&type=1&artist=Fall%20Out%20Boy&title=Immortals |title=''Billboard'' Chart Search: Artist=All Time Low / Chart=Bubbling Under Hot 100 | magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]] |access-date=January 26, 2015}}</ref> In ''American Beauty/American Psycho'''s release week, "Immortals" debuted on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] at No. 96. In the week ending March 14, the song re-entered the chart at No. 78 for its second chart week, following ''Big Hero 6''{{'}}s [[87th Academy Awards|Academy Award]] win for [[Academy Award for Best Animated Feature|Best Animated Feature]].<ref>[https://www.theverge.com/2015/2/22/8088715/academy-awards-2015-big-hero-6-best-animated-feature Big Hero 6 wins Oscar for Best Animated Feature]. ''[[The Verge]]''. Retrieved March 7, 2015.</ref> It peaked at No. 72 the week after. To date, the song has been certified Platinum denoting one million units.

The song debuted at No. 122 on the [[UK Singles Chart]] during the week of October 25, 2014.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.zobbel.de/cluk/141025cluk.txt |title=CHART: CLUK Update 25.10.2014 (wk42) |publisher=Zobbel.de |access-date=October 25, 2014 |author=Zywietz, Tobias}}</ref> In ''American Beauty/American Psycho'''s third chart week, "Immortals" peaked at No. 84, its fourth chart week (week of February 14, 2015).<ref name=UK84>{{cite web|url=http://www.officialcharts.com/singles-chart/ |title=Official Singles Chart UK Top 100 - 14th February 2015 |publisher=[[Official Charts Company]] |access-date=February 9, 2015 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150209033807/http://www.officialcharts.com/singles-chart/ |archive-date=February 9, 2015 |url-status=dead }}</ref> It notched one week in Australia at No. 82 in mid-January.
