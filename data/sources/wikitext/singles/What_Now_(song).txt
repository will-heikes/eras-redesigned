"'''What Now'''" is a song recorded by the Barbadian singer [[Rihanna]] for her seventh album, ''[[Unapologetic]]'' (2012). It was written by Olivia Waithe, [[Parker Ighile]], Maestro The Baker and Nathan Cassells with production handled by Maestro The Baker, Ighile and Cassells. A [[remix]] collection was released exclusively to [[Beatport]] on August 29, 2013<ref name="Beatport">{{cite web |url=http://www.beatport.com/release/what-now-remixes/1151628 |title=What Now [Remixes] |publisher=[[Beatport]] |date=August 29, 2013 |access-date=September 1, 2013 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140330195029/http://www.beatport.com/release/what-now-remixes/1151628 |archive-date=March 30, 2014 |url-status=dead }}</ref> and later via [[iTunes]], [[Amazon.com|Amazon]], and [[Google Play]] on September 17, 2013.<ref name="itunes">{{cite web|title=What Now (Remixes) by Rihanna|date=January 2013 |url=https://music.apple.com/us/album/what-now-remixes/700808855|publisher=[[iTunes Store]]|access-date=September 27, 2013| archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20131014175600/https://itunes.apple.com/us/album/what-now-remixes/id700808855 | archive-date = October 14, 2013| url-status=live}}</ref><ref name="amazon">{{cite web|title=Amazon: What Now (Remixes) by Rihanna|website=Amazon |url=https://www.amazon.com/What-Now-Remixes/dp/B00F0VO37U|access-date=September 27, 2013| archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20131003225414/http://www.amazon.com/What-Now-Remixes/dp/B00F0VO37U | archive-date = October 3, 2013| url-status=live}}</ref><ref name="googleplay">{{cite web|title=Rihanna: What Now on Google Play|url=https://play.google.com/store/music/album/Rihanna_What_Now?id=B5dx5ggfkgy6l3duno73lqi43ja|work=[[Google Play]]|access-date=September 27, 2013}}</ref> The single was serviced to US rhythmic radio on September 24, 2013, before impacting mainstream radio on October 1, 2013, as the fifth single from ''Unapologetic''. Another remix collection was released to Beatport on October 29, 2013.<ref name="Beatport2" /> The song is a mid-tempo piano ballad which incorporates sounds which resemble "sonic bombs" during the chorus and "crashing" drums.

"What Now" received generally positive reviews from music critics, with many calling it an emotional, stand-out song on the album and praising Rihanna's vocals. Following the release of ''Unapologetic'', "What Now" made appearances on various charts around the world. Following its release as a single, the song re-peaked in multiple territories, having it also reach new peaks at number 25 on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] and number 25 on the [[UK Singles Chart]], while topping the US [[Dance Club Songs]] chart and attaining a top three peak on the [[UK R&B Singles and Albums Charts|UK Hip Hop and R&B Singles Chart]].

The song's accompanying music video was directed by Jeff Nicholas, Jonathan Craven, and Darren Craig from Uprising Creativity. It portrays Rihanna having an "emotional breakdown" and performing exorcism-type dance moves. Critics complimented the video and compared it to Rihanna's 2008 video for the song "[[Disturbia (song)|Disturbia]]". Rihanna promoted "What Now" on ''[[Alan Carr: Chatty Man]]'' and the song is included on the set list of her [[Diamonds World Tour]] (2013).<ref name="DWT">{{cite web|url=http://www.mercurynews.com/music/ci_22974451/setlist-rihanna-san-jose|title=Setlist: Rihanna in San Jose|publisher=[[Mercury News]]|last=Harrington|first=Jim|date=April 6, 2013|access-date=May 2, 2013| archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20131029201842/http://www.mercurynews.com/music/ci_22974451/setlist-rihanna-san-jose | archive-date = October 29, 2013| url-status=live}}</ref> British television network [[ITV (TV network)|ITV]] used the song for their "Where Drama Lives" 2014 advertisement.<ref name="ITV">{{cite web|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=28RO4lTyVIY|title=ITV, Where Drama Lives 2014|publisher=ITV, YouTube|date=February 4, 2014|access-date=February 4, 2014| archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20140503093911/https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=28RO4lTyVIY | archive-date = May 3, 2014| url-status=live}}</ref>

==Production and release==
[[File:Livvi-Franc 091212 Photoby-Adam-Bielawski.jpg|130px|thumbnail|left|[[Livvi Franc]] co-wrote "What Now".]]
Rihanna began "working on the new sound" for her seventh studio album in March 2012, even though she had not yet begun recording.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.rap-up.com/2012/03/15/rihanna-on-katy-perry-collaboration-its-gonna-happen|title=Rihanna on Katy Perry Collaboration: 'It's Gonna Happen'|work=[[Rap-Up]]|publisher=Devine Lazerine|access-date=September 29, 2012| archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20121215050000/http://www.rap-up.com/2012/03/15/rihanna-on-katy-perry-collaboration-its-gonna-happen/ | archive-date = December 15, 2012| url-status=dead}}</ref> On September 12, 2012, [[Def Jam Recordings|Def Jam]] France announced via [[Twitter]] that Rihanna would release a new single the upcoming week while her seventh studio album was scheduled to be released in November 2012. On October 11, 2012, in one of her tweets revealed that the title of her new album is ''[[Unapologetic]]'' along with its cover.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.capitalfm.com/artists/rihanna/news/new-album-unapologetic-artwork/|title=Rihanna Reveals New Album Title 'Unapologetic' And Raunchy Cover Artwork |publisher=Capital FM|date=October 11, 2012|access-date=November 23, 2012| archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20131212204418/http://www.capitalfm.com/artists/rihanna/news/new-album-unapologetic-artwork/ | archive-date = December 12, 2013| url-status=live}}</ref>

"What Now" was written by British singer-songwriter [[Livvi Franc]] together with Rihanna, Parker Ighile, Maestro The Baker and Nathan Cassells.<ref name="Notes" /> Ighile, Maestro and Cassells recorded the music for the track in [[Metropolis Studios]] located in [[London]], United Kingdom, and provided all of the [[instrumentation]] and [[Programming (music)|programming]].<ref name="Notes" /> [[Kuk Harrell]] provided the vocal production of the song and also recorded Rihanna's vocals together with Marcos Tovar at [[Westlake Recording Studios]] in [[Los Angeles]], California. Blake Mares and Robert Cohen served as assistant vocal engineer of "What Now".<ref name="Notes" /> It was [[Mixing (music production)|mixed]] by Phil Tan at Ninja Club Studios in [[Atlanta]], Georgia with Daniela Rivera serving as assistant mixing engineer.<ref name="Notes" />

"What Now" was released as the sixth single from the album ''Unapologetic''. Ten remixes of the song were digitally released via [[Beatport]] on August 28, 2013, in the United States.<ref name="Beatport"/> The same remixes, were also added on the [[iTunes Store]] on September 17.<ref name=itunes/> "What Now" was serviced to [[rhythmic contemporary]] radio in the United States on September 24. It was later sent to [[contemporary hit radio]] on October 1.<ref name="FMQB">{{Cite magazine|year=2013|title=FMQB: Radio Industry News, Music Industry Updates, Arbitron Ratings, Music News and more!|url=https://www.fmqb.com/Article.asp?id=69239|magazine=[[FMQB]]|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131016081246/https://www.fmqb.com/Article.asp?id=69239|archive-date=October 16, 2013|access-date=August 5, 2022}}</ref> Via her [[Instagram]] account, Rihanna revealed the official artwork for the song on October 16. It features the singer dressed in black and "stares out with piercing eyes" while her name is written on chalkboard behind her.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.rap-up.com/2013/10/16/single-cover-rihanna-what-now/|title=Single Cover: Rihanna – 'What Now'|work=Rap-Up|access-date=October 16, 2013| archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20131017061906/http://www.rap-up.com/2013/10/16/single-cover-rihanna-what-now/ | archive-date = October 17, 2013| url-status=dead}}</ref> Jocelyn Vena of [[MTV News]] described Rihanna's style on the artwork as [[goth subculture|goth]] and noted that it is reminiscent of the behind-the-scenes shot she posted during filming the song's video.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1715716/rihanna-what-now-goth.jhtml|title=Is Rihanna Going Goth? Her 'What Now' Artwork Makes Us Think So|publisher=[[MTV News]]|last=Vena|first=Jocelyn|date=October 16, 2013|access-date=October 17, 2013| archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20131019010224/http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1715716/rihanna-what-now-goth.jhtml | archive-date = October 19, 2013| url-status=dead}}</ref>

"What Now" follows a chord progression of G{{music|sharp}}m–B<small>2</small>–F{{music|sharp}}–F{{music|sharp}}<small>sus4</small>–F{{music|sharp}}.<ref name="musicnotes">{{cite web|url=http://www.musicnotes.com/sheetmusic/mtdFPE.asp?ppn=MN0117396&|title=Rihanna&nbsp;– What Now Sheet Music (Digital Download)|date=May 10, 2013|publisher=Musicnotes.com. On November 13, Rihanna unveiled a behind-the-scenes video via her official [[Vevo]] account.<ref name="behind the scenes"/> During it she explained the concept of the video, "It's gonna be kind of eerie, very creepy because 'What Now' is one of those songs that you can get really boring with the visual. You can get really almost expected. Everybody's probably expecting narrative type of video, a love story of some sorts or something really soft and pretty" and then says, "It is pretty and kind of soft, but it's really a little demented."<ref>{{cite web|title=Rihanna's "What Now" Music Video "Is a Little Demented"—Watch Now!|date=November 15, 2013|url=http://www.eonline.com/news/481543/rihanna-s-what-now-music-video-is-a-little-demented-watch-now|publisher=[[E!]]|access-date=November 15, 2013| archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20131215013537/http://www.eonline.com/news/481543/rihanna-s-what-now-music-video-is-a-little-demented-watch-now | archive-date = December 15, 2013| url-status=live}}</ref> Prior to the video's release, Rihanna posted a sneak peek of the video on [[YouTube]].<ref>{{cite web|title=What Now Music Video Sneak Peek|url=http://www.rihannanow.com/news-article/what-now-music-video-sneak-peek/|publisher=Rihanna Official Website|access-date=November 15, 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131212050215/http://www.rihannanow.com/news-article/what-now-music-video-sneak-peek/|archive-date=December 12, 2013|url-status=dead}}</ref> The official music video was released to [[VEVO]] on November 15, 2013.<ref>{{cite magazine|title=Rihanna Embraces The Darkness In 'What Now' Music Video: Watch|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/5793165/rihanna-embraces-the-darkness-in-what-now-music-video-watch|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|access-date=November 15, 2013}}</ref>

Rob Newman is the producer of the video, while Craven, Nicholas and Thananath Songchaikul executively produced the visual. Sing Howe Yam performed the direction of the photography while Clark Eddy is the editor.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b-3BI9AspYc|title=Rihanna - What Now (Official)|date=November 15, 2013 |publisher=Vevo. YouTube|access-date=November 17, 2013| archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20140507003514/https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b-3BI9AspYc&feature=autoshare | archive-date = May 7, 2014| url-status=live}}</ref> The video opens to Rihanna appearing on a static television screen in a simple nude gown and [[crucifix]] necklace. She then begins to sing whilst being in a darkened room with a black gown on and later appearing in a brightened room with a white gown on. The video switches between the creepy and the pretty Rihanna as she mopes in a sparse warehouse space, possessed by her loneliness.<ref name="mtv">{{cite web|last=Vena|first=Jocelyn|title=Rihanna 'What Now' Video: Looks Familiar To Us...|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1717529/rihanna-what-now-video-scenes.jhtml|publisher=MTV|access-date=November 15, 2013| archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20140313070923/http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1717529/rihanna-what-now-video-scenes.jhtml | archive-date = March 13, 2014| url-status=dead}}</ref>

===Reception===
Jocelyn Vena of [[MTV]] compared the video to four of Rihanna's past videos, with "[[Disturbia (song)|Disturbia]]" being one of the four, she says "both 'What Now' and 'Disturbia' have a dark, supernatural vibe about them. And in addition to sharing that similarity, the visuals' shots are layered, giving off a sort of 3-D effect." Other comparisons included were "Diamonds" and "Stay", which is also included on her 2012 ''[[Unapologetic]]'', and "[[We Found Love]]". Even Rachel Brodsky of [[MTV]]'s ''Buzzworthy Blog'' used seven comparisons of how the video relates to the 1996 film ''[[The Craft (film)|The Craft]]''.<ref>{{cite web|last=Brodsky|first=Rachel|title=7 Ways Rihanna's 'What Now' Video Is A Remake Of 'The Craft'|url=http://buzzworthy.mtv.com/2013/11/15/rihanna-what-now-video-the-craft-remake/|publisher=MTV|access-date=November 15, 2013| archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20131205142538/http://buzzworthy.mtv.com/2013/11/15/rihanna-what-now-video-the-craft-remake/ | archive-date = December 5, 2013| url-status=dead}}</ref> Jason Lipshutz of ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' says that Rihanna "dials it down" for the video after the "twerking, pole-dancing and general skin-showing" video she released for "Pour It Up" the month before.<ref>{{cite magazine|last=Lipshutz|first=Jason|title=Rihanna Embraces The Darkness In 'What Now' Music Video: Watch|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/5793165/rihanna-embraces-the-darkness-in-what-now-music-video-watch|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|access-date=November 15, 2013}}</ref> Other reviewers gave similar opinions, as an anonymous reviewer of ''[[The Huffington Post]]'' says "The clip shows the 25-year-old singer in a spooky place, writhing in a sparse room shot in a fashion similar to many horror movies."<ref>{{cite news|title='What Now' Video Takes Rihanna To A Dark Place|url=http://www.huffingtonpost.com/2013/11/15/what-now-video-rihanna_n_4281109.html|work=[[The Huffington Post]]|access-date=November 15, 2013|first=Madeline|last=Boardman|date=November 15, 2013}}</ref>
