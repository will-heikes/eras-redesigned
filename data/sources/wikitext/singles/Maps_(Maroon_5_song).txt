"'''Maps'''" is a song by the American rock band [[Maroon 5]]. The song was released on June 16, 2014,<ref name="E">{{cite web|url=http://uk.eonline.com/news/551341/maroon-5-drops-new-song-maps-listen-now |title=Maroon 5 Drops New Song "Maps"—Listen Now! |publisher=[[E!]] |first=Zach |last=Johnson |date=June 16, 2014 |access-date=June 16, 2014}}</ref><ref name="Radio.com"/> as the lead single from their fifth studio album ''[[V (Maroon 5 album)|V]]'' (2014). The song was written by [[Adam Levine]], [[Ammar Malik]], [[Benny Blanco|Benjamin Levin]], [[Noel Zancanella]] and [[Ryan Tedder]], and produced by the latter three. It was also their first single after the return of keyboardist and rhythm guitarist [[Jesse Carmichael]]. "Maps" received mainly positive reviews from music critics, with praise going to the song's melody and chilled-out vibe. However, some criticized the song for being similar to their previous lead single "[[Payphone (song)|Payphone]]", on their last album ''[[Overexposed (album)|Overexposed]]'' (2012).

The song was a commercial success, reaching number-one in Canada and peaking at number six on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] and number two on the [[UK Singles Chart]]. The song also reached the top 20 in over 25 countries, including [[French Singles Chart|France]], [[German Singles Chart|Germany]] and [[Tracklisten|Denmark]]. On July 1, 2014, a music video for the song premiered on [[MTV]] and [[VH1]]. It was inspired by the film ''[[Irréversible]]'' and shows the events that occur in the video in reverse. In August 2014, "Maps" was named ''Battle of the Summer Jams'' for 2014 by VH1.<ref>{{cite web|title=Battle Of The Summer Jams: The Winner REVEALED!|url=https://www.vh1.com/news/kxi9r0/maroon-5-summer-jams-winner|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230406060817/https://www.vh1.com/news/kxi9r0/maroon-5-summer-jams-winner|url-status=dead|archive-date=April 6, 2023|work=[[VH1]]|date=August 29, 2014|access-date=May 10, 2019}}</ref>

The remix versions of the song are performed by featured artists, American rapper [[Big Sean]] and Colombian singer [[J Balvin]]. Another remix version was made by Circuit Jerks and was included on their [[extended play|EP]] titled ''EP1'' (2016).<ref name="Circuit Jerks Remix"/> The band has performed the song multiple times including the [[Maroon V Tour]] (2015–2018), as well as the [[2014 MTV Video Music Awards]] and ''[[Today (American TV program)|The Today Show]]''. The song was used in the advertisement for the 2013 film ''[[Begin Again (film)|Begin Again]]'', which stars Levine.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.indiewire.com/2014/07/watch-lively-new-begin-again-spot-features-newest-single-from-star-adam-levines-maroon-5-maps-24475/|title=Watch: Lively New 'Begin Again' Spot Features Newest Single from Star Adam Levine's Maroon 5, 'Maps'|work=Indie Wire|date=July 9, 2014}}</ref> It was featured on the television shows ''[[Shameless (American TV series)|Shameless]]'' and ''[[State of Affairs (TV series)|State of Affairs]]'' and the video games ''[[Just Dance 2015]]'', ''[[Rock Band VR]]'' and ''[[Fuser (video game)|Fuser]]''.

The song was written by [[Adam Levine]], [[Ammar Malik]], [[Ryan Tedder]], [[Benny Blanco]] and [[Noel Zancanella]], and produced by the latter three.<ref name="Idolator"/> Blanco and Malik have previously teamed with Maroon 5 on the songs "[[Moves like Jagger]]" and "[[Payphone (song)|Payphone]]", while Tedder and Zancanella have worked with the group on the songs "[[Love Somebody (Maroon 5 song)|Love Somebody]]" and "[[Lucky Strike (Maroon 5 song)|Lucky Strike]]". "Maps" is an up-tempo [[pop rock]] and [[the Police]]-esque [[reggae]] track,<ref name="Fuse"/><ref>{{Cite news |last=Sullivan |first=Caroline |date=September 12, 2014 |title=Maroon 5 review – undeniable pop melodies but no rough edges |url=https://www.theguardian.com/music/2014/sep/12/maroon-5-review-roundhouse-london-adam-levine |access-date=April 13, 2026 |work=[[The Guardian]] |issn=0261-3077}}</ref> that contains a "breezy guitar lick". It marks Maroon 5's further departure from the funkier sound for which the band was originally known.<ref name="Radio.com">{{cite web|url=http://radio.com/2014/06/16/listen-to-maps-new-single-from-maroon-5/|title=Listen to 'Maps,' the New Single from Maroon 5|publisher=[[Radio.com]]|date=June 16, 2014|access-date=June 16, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140724075535/http://radio.com/2014/06/16/listen-to-maps-new-single-from-maroon-5/|archive-date=July 24, 2014}}</ref>

Lyrically, the song is about "the search for love, particularly a love that has been lost and needs to be found".<ref name="Radio.com"/> In the chorus, Levine sings "All the roads you took came back to me / So I'm following the map that leads to you."<ref name="Radio.com"/><ref name="Seacrest">{{cite web|url=http://www.ryanseacrest.com/2014/06/16/listen-to-maroon-5s-brand-new-song-maps/|title=Listen to Maroon 5's Brand New Song, 'Maps'|publisher=[[RyanSeacrest.com]]|first=Kathleen|last=Perricone|date=June 16, 2014|access-date=June 16, 2014}}</ref>

== Critical reception ==
''[[Idolator (website)|Idolator]]''{{'s}} Robbie Daw wrote that "similar to [[Bruno Mars]]' '[[Locked Out of Heaven|Locked Out Of Heaven]]' ", "Maps" has a "[[Sting (musician)|Sting]]/[[The Police]] vibe, particularly with the guitar licks and Levine's multi-tracked vocal harmonies on the chorus." He also added that the influence of [[Ryan Tedder]] (and [[OneRepublic]]'s pop-rock anthems, such as "[[Counting Stars]]" and "[[Love Runs Out]]") is evident.<ref name="Idolator">{{cite web |url=http://www.idolator.com/7522831/maroon-5-maps-listen-new-single |archive-url=https://archive.today/20140616163202/http://www.idolator.com/7522831/maroon-5-maps-listen-new-single |url-status=dead |archive-date=June 16, 2014 |title=Maroon 5's "Maps": Listen To The New 'V' Single |publisher=[[Idolator (website)|Idolator]] |first=Robbie |last=Daw |date=June 16, 2014 |access-date=June 16, 2014 }}</ref>
Jeff Benjamin of [[Fuse (TV channel)|Fuse]] compared the opening guitar licks to the "California-cool sound" of [[Red Hot Chili Peppers]].<ref name="Fuse">{{cite web|url=http://www.fuse.tv/2014/06/maroon-5-maps |title=Maroon 5 Return With Lovelorn New Single "Maps" |publisher=[[Fuse (TV channel)|Fuse]] |first=Jeff |last=Benjamin |date=June 16, 2014 |access-date=June 16, 2014}}</ref>

Music Times commented, "With his [Levine's] singsong melody over the relaxed, plucked guitars and subtle drums, Maroon 5 create a chill vibe before building the song into something a bit more powerful. Though Levine's vocals remain constant, the song soon crescendos into a fuller sound, with a chorus that isn't so much explosive as it is, well, danceable," and compared the track to "[[Payphone (song)|Payphone]]", the lead single from the band's fourth studio album ''[[Overexposed (album)|Overexposed]]'' (2012): "The more you think about the two lead singles versus each other, the more they feel exactly the same."<ref name="Music Times">{{cite journal|url=http://www.musictimes.com/articles/6826/20140616/review-maroon-5-new-single-maps-v-echoes-payphone-wrong.htm |title=REVIEW: Maroon 5 New Single 'Maps' From 'V' Echoes 'Payphone' In All The Wrong Ways |journal=Music Times |date=June 16, 2014 |access-date=June 16, 2014}}</ref>

== Commercial performance ==
Shortly after its release, "Maps" quickly reached number one on the [[Billboard Twitter Real-Time|''Billboard'' Twitter Real Time Trending 140]] chart, eventually staying within the upper ranks for several hours. The song also logged close to 1,800 first-day spins on radio which, according to the [[Nielsen BDS]], would amount to a one-day audience impression of seventeen million.<ref name=BillboardTwitter>{{cite magazine|last1=Gruger|first1=William|last2=Trust|first2=Gary |title=Maroon 5 Finds Way To Billboard/Twitter Charts With 'Maps' |url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/maroon-5-maps-billboard-twitter-chart-trending-140-v/ |magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]] |access-date=June 18, 2014 |date=June 17, 2014}}</ref> It has since peaked at number six on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]]. It reached its million sales mark in August 2014<ref>{{cite web |url=https://music.yahoo.com/blogs/chart-watch/chart-watch-nicki-minaj-zooms-to-2-221739201.html |title= Chart Watch: Nicki Minaj Zooms To #2 |author=Paul Grein |date=August 27, 2014 |work=Yahoo! Music }}</ref> and had sold 1.6 million copies by the year's end.<ref name=us_sales>{{cite web|url=http://www.hitsdailydouble.com/news/newsPage.cgi?news10225m01 |archive-url=https://archive.today/20141211001027/http://www.hitsdailydouble.com/news/newsPage.cgi?news10225m01 |archive-date=December 11, 2014 |title=The Big 9 in 2014: Interscope |website=HITS Daily Double |publisher=HITS Digital Ventures |date=December 10, 2014 |access-date=December 10, 2014 }}</ref>

The single debuted at number two on the [[UK Singles Chart]] in late August 2014.

As "Maps" topped the eight biggest music charts in [[South Korea]], Maroon 5 became the first foreign artist to top several charts at the same time in Korean music history.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://news.naver.com/main/read.nhn?mode=LSD&mid=sec&sid1=106&oid=312&aid=0000035488&viewType=pc|title=5, 8 Ʈ ...˾ƼƮ}}</ref>

=== Official music video ===
The music video for "Maps" was directed by [[Peter Berg]] and cinematographed by [[Tobias A. Schliessler]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.instagram.com/p/C8SwP74ydfN/|title=10 years of “Maps”!|work=[[Instagram]]|author=[[Maroon 5]]|date=June 16, 2024|access-date=March 4, 2026}}</ref> It premiered on [[MTV]] and [[VH1]] on July 1, 2014,<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/1858609/exclusive-watch-maroon-5s-dramatic-video-for-maps/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140706161846/http://www.mtv.com/news/1858609/exclusive-watch-maroon-5s-dramatic-video-for-maps/|url-status=dead|archive-date=July 6, 2014|title=Exclusive: Adam Levine Gets Dark In Maroon 5's Grisly 'Maps' Video|work=[[MTV]]|last=Blake|first=Emily|date=July 1, 2014|access-date=July 1, 2014}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.vh1.com/news/cw8mm3/video-premiere-maroon-5-maps|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230406202148/https://www.vh1.com/news/cw8mm3/video-premiere-maroon-5-maps|url-status=live|archive-date=April 6, 2023|title=VIDEO PREMIERE: Maroon 5 Is Back On The Map With Their New Single, "Maps"|work=VH1|date=July 1, 2014|access-date=July 1, 2014}}</ref> and on [[Vevo]], the day after.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NmugSMBh_iI|title=Maroon 5 – Maps (Explicit) (Official Music Video)|publisher=[[Vevo]]. [[YouTube]]|date=July 2, 2014}}</ref> According to the band's bassist [[Mickey Madden]], the story from the video is inspired by the 2002 film ''[[Irréversible]]'', so the events shown in the video are [[Reverse chronology|reversed]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://mashable.com/archive/maroon-5-v-album-interview|title=Maroon 5, a Band You Love to Hate, 'Blown Away' by Its Own Longevity|work=[[Mashable]]|last=Anthony Hernandez|first=Brian|date=September 7, 2014|access-date=July 27, 2019}}</ref> "Maps" also marks the first music video appearance in two years of keyboardist and rhythm guitarist [[Jesse Carmichael]], who returned after taking a temporary break from performing with the band. He plays one of the hospital paramedics in some of the first scenes of the video. The other members of the group: the aforementioned Mickey Madden, guitarist [[James Valentine (musician)|James Valentine]], keyboardist [[PJ Morton]] and drummer [[Matt Flynn (musician)|Matt Flynn]] are portraying some of the guests seen at a house party and the band's assistant manager Shawn Tellez, who plays a partygoer.

==== Synopsis ====
The video begins when a worried [[Adam Levine]] enters a hospital and searches the emergency room until he finds a young woman, revealed to be his girlfriend (Tereza Kacerova), as the paramedics attempt to [[Cardiopulmonary resuscitation|resuscitate]] her; but he is ushered out. The video follows while the woman walks into a street in tears heartbreakingly as a man tries to get her attention and she pushes him away, before she is hit by a truck. In the next scene, Levine and his girlfriend arrive at the house in a party and are greeted by guests. While the crowd was having fun, Levine has drinks with his friends, becomes intoxicated and meets a woman (Meghan Wiggins) and they share a kiss. However, his girlfriend notices them and she leaves, despite Levine's attempts to stop her. In another scene, Levine is helping his girlfriend choose which dress to wear and they watch a [[Association football|football (soccer)]] game on television together and then the camera fastly turn back into the beginning. During the final scene at the hospital, the girl is shown [[flatline|flatlining]], depicting that she died from the accident.

== Live performances ==
Maroon 5 performed "Maps" for the first time on June 20, 2014, during their concert at the [[Bowery Ballroom]] in [[New York City]], as part of the [[American Express]]' EveryDay Live.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://globalgrind.com/3982310/maroon-5-bowery-ballroom-nyc-review/|title=CONCERT CHAOS: Maroon 5 Lights Up Bowery Ballroom In NYC (REVIEW)|work=Global Grind|last=Tropnas|first=Melissa|date=June 23, 2014|access-date=June 24, 2014}}</ref> The following day, they also performed the single at another concert in [[Laval, Quebec|Laval]], [[Canada]]. The band played the song for ''[[Today (American TV program)|The Today Show]]'' on June 25, at [[Rockefeller Center#Rockefeller Plaza|Rockefeller Plaza]] in New York,<ref>{{cite web|url=https://heavy.com/entertainment/2014/06/maroon-5-today-show-adam-levine-begin-again-performance/|title=WATCH: Maroon 5 Performs on the 'Today Show'|work=Heavy|date=June 25, 2014|access-date=October 14, 2018}}</ref> and again on September 1, 2014, the day before the world premiere of their album ''[[V (Maroon 5 album)|V]]''.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://directlyrics.com/video-maroon-5-perform-v-songs-on-the-today-show-news.html|title=Video: Maroon 5 Perform "V" Songs On "The Today Show"|work=Direct Lyrics|last=Apaza|first=Kevin|date=September 1, 2014|access-date=September 8, 2018}}</ref> On August 10, 2014, Maroon 5 performed the song at the Hyundai Card City Break concert in [[Seoul]], South Korea.
