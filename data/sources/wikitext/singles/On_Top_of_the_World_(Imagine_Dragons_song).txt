"'''On Top of the World'''" is a song by the American [[Rock music|rock]] band [[Imagine Dragons]] first appearing on their major-label debut [[extended play]] ''[[Continued Silence]]'' (2012). The song also appears on their first full-length album ''[[Night Visions]]'' (2012). "On Top of the World" was released digitally as a single on March 18, 2013.

"On Top of the World" primarily features Magne [[guitar]] and [[piano]] instrumentation, with vocals performed by lead singer [[Dan Reynolds (musician)|Dan Reynolds]].<ref name="Music Notes">{{cite web | url=http://www.musicnotes.com/sheetmusic/mtdFPE.asp?ppn=MN0112551 | title=Imagine Dragons 'On Top of the World' Sheet Music | date=7 January 2013 | publisher=[[Universal Music Publishing Group]] | access-date=23 June 2013 | id=MN0112551}}</ref> Originally published in the key of [[C major]],<ref>{{Cite web|last1=Daniel|first1=Reynolds|last2=Benjamin|first2=McKee|last3=Daniel|first3=Sermon|last4=Alexander|first4=Grant|last5=Dragons|first5=Imagine|date=2013-01-07|title=On Top of the World|url=https://www.musicnotes.com/sheetmusic/mtd.asp?ppn=MN0112551|access-date=2021-06-02|website=Musicnotes.com}}</ref><ref name="Music Notes"/> the song itself expresses a celebration of accomplishment for the band after striving for years to become successful.<ref>{{cite web | url=http://www.absolutepunk.net/showthread.php?t=2831002 | title=Imagine Dragons – Continued Silence EP | publisher=[[AbsolutePunk]] | date=25 July 2012 | access-date=23 June 2013}}</ref> The song incorporates the chord progression of C-F-C-Dm in the verses, and F-C-G-Dm in the chorus and bridge, with the pre-chorus using Am and G to create a different break in the song.<ref>{{cite web|title=On Top of the World Chords|url=http://tabs.ultimate-guitar.com/i/imagine_dragons/on_top_of_the_world_crd.htm|work=ultimate-guitar.com|access-date=3 April 2013}}</ref> "On Top of the World" is found to be a positive, upbeat track, unlike other songs found on ''Night Visions'', including "[[Bleeding Out (song)|Bleeding Out]]" and "[[Hear Me (song)|Hear Me]]".{{citation needed|date=June 2013}}

==In media ==
Imagine Dragons performed the song live on the Australian version of  ''[[The X Factor (Australian TV series)|The X Factor]]'' in 2013.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://au.tv.yahoo.com/x-factor/video/watch/19488913/imagine-dragons-on-top-of-the-world/|title=- Watch The X-Factor Clips – Official site}}</ref>

"On Top of the World" was licensed for inclusion on the soundtrack of [[EA Sports]] games ''[[FIFA 13]]'' and [[FIFA 23|''FIFA 23'']]. The band, longtime fans of the [[FIFA (video game series)|''FIFA'' video game series]], reacted positively to its inclusion in the game and stated that it would be "a lot of fun hearing the track play while we are doing bicycle kicks and screaming at the screen."<ref name="FIFA">{{cite web | url=http://www.imaginedragonsmusic.com/news/default.aspx?nid=39178 | title='On Top of the World' to be featured in FIFA 2013! | work=[[Imagine Dragons|Imaginedragonsmusic.com]] | publisher=[[Interscope]] | access-date=26 January 2013 | archive-url=https://archive.today/20130811220726/http://www.imaginedragonsmusic.com/news/default.aspx?nid=39178 | archive-date=11 August 2013}}</ref> It also served as the opening theme of the short-lived [[CBS]] comedy series ''[[Partners (2012 TV series)|Partners]]''.<ref>{{cite web | url=http://blogs.shawconnect.ca/music/debut-spotlight-imagine-dragons/ | title=Debut Spotlight: Imagine Dragons | publisher=[[Shaw Media]] | date=4 October 2012 | access-date=23 June 2013 | author=Kyte, Kathryn | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130921053433/http://blogs.shawconnect.ca/music/debut-spotlight-imagine-dragons/ | archive-date=21 September 2013 | url-status=dead }}</ref> "On Top of the World" was performed by the [[PS22 Chorus]] during [[Barack Obama]]'s [[Second inauguration of Barack Obama|second United States presidential inauguration]].<ref>{{cite news | url=https://www.chicagotribune.com/2013/03/10/interview-imagine-dragons-singer-savors-flight-on-the-wings-of-success/ | title=Interview: Imagine Dragons singer savors flight on the wings of success | work=[[Chicago Tribune]] | date=10 March 2013 | access-date=23 June 2013 | author=Gomez, Luis}}</ref> It also featured in [[Konami]] video game ''[[Pro Evolution Soccer 2013]]'' as well as being used in a [[Samsung Galaxy Note 8.0]] advert.

The song was featured prominently in the film ''[[The Incredible Burt Wonderstone]]'' several times.

In 2013, a Philippine commercial of the [[Toyota Vios]] was used in this song.

The song was featured in the British kids film ''[[All Stars (2013 film)|All Stars]]''.

The song is featured in the ''[[Liv and Maddie]]'' episode "Twin-a-Rooney", performed by [[Dove Cameron]] in the title role of Liv Rooney.  Dove Cameron's version appears on the compilation album ''[[Disney Channel Play It Loud]]''.

[[Hunter Hayes]] covered the song live on his US tour.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://savannahnow.com/do/2013-10-14/review-hunter-hayes-rocks-savannah#.UmcQZZTEodY|title=REVIEW: Hunter Hayes rocks Savannah}}</ref>

The song was used by [[ESPN]] for their coverage of the [[NBA on ESPN|NBA]] for the [[2013–14 NBA season]].

The song was used during [[Cartoon Network]]'s 2013 [[Hall of Game Awards]].

The song was used in trailers for the films ''[[The Croods]]'' (2013) and ''[[Island of Lemurs: Madagascar]]'' (2014).

[[WPVI-TV]] in [[Philadelphia, Pennsylvania]] uses the song to promote their morning news, ''Action News Mornings''.

The song is used in the [[Coca-Cola]] promotional video "Moments of Happiness", shown exclusively at the [[World of Coca-Cola]] in [[Atlanta, Georgia]].

On November 9, 2014, Australian [[Furry fandom|Furry]] YouTuber and singer, thatdancingdog, released a cover of the song featuring other guests from the Furry Down Under!{{efn|Also known as FurDU.}} convention in [[Surfers Paradise, Queensland, Australia]].<ref>{{cite web |title=FurDU On Top of the World |url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7qbmkv03obw |website=youtube.com| date=9 November 2014 }}</ref>

The song was featured in the soundtracks of ''[[Alpha and Omega 4: The Legend of the Saw Tooth Cave]]'' and ''[[The Angry Birds Movie]]''.

From 2013 to 2015, [[Vodafone Portugal]] and [[Vodafone Greece]] used the song in their ads for the Red phone plan.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://aquelamusicanuncio.blogspot.pt/2013/03/anuncio-e-musica-vodafone-red-top-of.html|title=Anuncio e Música Vodafone RED (Top of the World) 2013|website=aquelamusicanuncio|access-date=2017-05-15}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|title=TVsongs – Vodafone|url=https://www.tvsongs.gr/advert/v/item/1311-vodafone|access-date=2022-01-26|website=tvsongs.gr|language=el-gr}}</ref>

==Music video==
[[File:Stanley Kubrick in Dr. Strangelove Trailer (4) Cropped.jpg|thumb|left|The official music video satirizes a popular conspiracy theory that the Apollo Moon landings were faked with the help of director [[Stanley Kubrick]] (pictured)]]
As a part of the [[Palladia]] documentary ''Imagine Dragons: The Making of Night Visions'', which originally aired on November 7, 2012, the band filmed a video of themselves performing "On Top of the World" and subsequently uploaded it to [[YouTube]].

On November 13, 2013, the band released an official music video for the song.<ref name="mtv-video">{{cite web|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1717376/imagine-dragons-on-top-of-the-world-video-kubrick.jhtml|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131113212555/http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1717376/imagine-dragons-on-top-of-the-world-video-kubrick.jhtml|url-status=dead|archive-date=13 November 2013|title=Imagine Dragons Reveal 'Subliminal' Secrets of New 'On Top of the World' Video|publisher=[[MTV]]}}</ref> The video satirizes a [[Moon landing conspiracy theories#Alleged Stanley Kubrick involvement|popular conspiracy theory]] that the 1969 [[Apollo 11]] moon landings were faked, the footage having been directed by [[Stanley Kubrick]], and that Kubrick then made references to the fakery in various of his subsequent films. In the video, a rock band similar to [[the Beatles]] (played by the members of Imagine Dragons) pretend to be astronauts for the faked Moon landing. The taping goes awry as fans of the band stream into the sound stage and turn the event into a rock concert, much to the chagrin of Kubrick and President [[Richard Nixon]], who is also on hand; though no one else watching, either in the studio or at home, seems concerned about the revelation of fakery, instead enjoying the music.<ref name="mtv-video" /> The video also briefly references another popular conspiracy theory from around the same time, that Beatles member [[Paul McCartney]] [[Paul is dead|died in 1966]] and was replaced by a [[look-alike]].<ref name="mtv-video" />

The video was filmed in [[Provo, Utah]], Imagine Dragons' original hometown, and includes cameos from various celebrities associated with Provo or [[Brigham Young University]], including [[Jon Heder]], [[Whit Hertford]], the cast of ''[[Studio C]]'', [[Fictionist|Robbie Connolly]], and [[The New Electric Sound]]. [[Aja Volkman]], Imagine Dragons frontman Dan Reynolds' wife, appears as Reynolds' character's wife.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.sltrib.com/sltrib/blogsinternet/57129048-71/video-band-com-provo.html.csp|title=Imagine Dragons' new Utah video secrets revealed, mapped out|first=Jim Dalrymple Ii The Salt Lake|last=Tribune}}</ref> The video was co-directed by [[Matt Eastin]] and Corey Fox.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://imvdb.com/video/imagine-dragons/on-top-of-the-world|title = Imagine Dragons - on Top of the World (2013)|date = 13 November 2013}}</ref>

==Commercial performance==
The song peaked at 79 on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] and 10 on the [[Hot Rock Songs]] charts.  It has also charted in the top ten in Australia (No. 10), Austria (No. 6), Netherlands (No. 10), New Zealand (No. 10), Poland (No. 7), and Portugal (No. 1). Despite having not yet been released to mainstream radio, it has received airplay on contemporary hit stations, such as 97.1 ZHT in Salt Lake City.

The song reached a million copies sold in the U.S. in April 2014.  It is the fourth million seller from the ''Night Visions'' album.<ref name="yahoo sales">{{cite news |url=https://music.yahoo.com/blogs/chart-watch/chart-watch-sell-14k-albums-top-10-172855366.html |title=Chart Watch: Pharrell Steps Up | author=Paul Grein |date=30 April 2014 |work=Yahoo Music }}</ref>

==#MakeTheFuture Version==
In November 2017, petroleum company [[Royal Dutch Shell|Shell]] created a music video of "On Top of the World" as part of their "Make The Future" campaign to promote environment-friendly energy. The featured artists were [[Jennifer Hudson]], [[Pixie Lott]], [[Luan Santana]], [[Yemi Alade]], and [[Monali Thakur]]. Another #MakeTheFuture music video also featured [[American Authors]]' 2014 hit single [[Best Day of My Life]].
