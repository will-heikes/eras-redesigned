"'''True Love'''" is a song by American singer [[Pink (singer)|Pink]] featuring British singer [[Lily Allen]] from the former's sixth studio album, ''[[The Truth About Love (Pink album)|The Truth About Love]]'' (2012). It was written by the artists, and producer [[Greg Kurstin]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.idolator.com/6903301/pink-lily-allen-true-love-lyric-video|title=Pink & Lily Allen Find "True Love" Together: Watch The Lyric Video|date=13 September 2012|website=idolator}}{{dead link|date=August 2025|bot=medic}}{{cbignore|bot=medic}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.nme.com/news/music/lily-allen-123-1263405|title=Pink and Lily Allen's collaboration 'True Love' surfaces online - listen|last=NME|date=13 September 2012|website=NME}}</ref>

The song was released as the album's second promotional single, following "Are We All We Are", in early 2013. It was released exclusively to Hungarian and Dutch radio. On April 29, 2013, it was announced that the song would be released as the fourth single.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.idolator.com/7455257/pink-true-love-lily-allen-new-single|title=Pink Announces "True Love" With Lily Allen As New Single|date=29 April 2013|website=idolator}}{{dead link|date=August 2025|bot=medic}}{{cbignore|bot=medic}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.entertainmentwise.com/Pink-Announces-New-Single-True-Love-Featuring-Lily-Rose-Cooper/|title=Pink Announces New Single 'True Love' Featuring Lily Rose Cooper » Entertainmentwise|date=2 June 2017|access-date=21 March 2019|archive-date=8 April 2023|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230408113755/https://www.entertainmentwise.com/pink-announces-new-single-true-love-featuring-lily-rose-cooper/|url-status=dead}}</ref><ref>[http://www.clickmusic.com/features/article/pink-set-to-release-lily-rose-cooper-allen-collaboration-true-love Pink set to release Lily Allen collaboration ‘True Love’ as next single | Features & Reviews | Clickmusic | Online Music News, Downloads, Videos & More<!-- Bot generated title -->] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130618122336/http://www.clickmusic.com/features/article/pink-set-to-release-lily-rose-cooper-allen-collaboration-true-love |date=2013-06-18 }}</ref> It officially impacted Italian radio stations on June 28, 2013. In the US, it impacted Hot/Modern/AC radio on July 15, 2013, before impacting Top 40/Mainstream radio one week later on July 23, 2013. The song was released in the UK on July 22, 2013.<ref name="digitalspy.co.uk">{{cite web|url=http://www.digitalspy.co.uk/music/news/a494564/pinks-family-lily-allen-star-in-true-love-music-video-watch.html|title=Pink's family, Lily Allen star in 'True Love' music video - watch|work=Digital Spy|date=July 2013}}</ref> The song reached the top 10 in Australia, the Czech Republic, Lebanon, Slovakia, Slovenia, and South Africa.

==Background==
In late February 2012, Pink tweeted that she was currently in the studio, working on an upcoming studio album. In early June, she released a video saying that her first single from the album was to be called "[[Blow Me (One Last Kiss)]]". It was released in July. Her new album was named ''[[The Truth About Love (Pink album)|The Truth About Love]]'' and was released in September. Since then, Pink has released two other singles from the album: "[[Try (Pink song)|Try]]", and "[[Just Give Me a Reason]]". Pink says that the song is about her relationship with [[Carey Hart]] and she finds the song funny lyrically.<ref>[http://www.pinkspage.com/us/news/pnk-breaks-down-truth-about-love-part-2 P!nk Breaks Down The Truth About Love – Part 2 | The Official P!nk Site<!-- Bot generated title -->] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130119194740/http://www.pinkspage.com/us/news/pnk-breaks-down-truth-about-love-part-2 |date=2013-01-19 }}</ref>

==Critical reception==
The song received mostly positive reviews from critics and was compared to the album's title track due to both songs revealing the positive and negative aspects of love. In its review of ''The Truth About Love'', ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' called the song the album's "brightest moment" and added that it "deserves to be one of Pink's signature songs".<ref>{{Cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/articles/review/1066850/pink-the-truth-about-love-track-by-track-review|title = Pink, 'The Truth About Love': Track-By-Track Review|magazine = [[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|date = 14 September 2012}}</ref> Amy Sciarretto of PopCrush also gave a positive review of the song, saying, "The upbeat, hell yeah, high-five love song features Pink calling her lover an “a–hole” and saying she wants to slap his whole face. Again, a dysfunctional relationship, which is sung about over a resonant rhythm, makes the misery of love sound like a delight in Pink’s capable hands, er, voice."<ref>{{cite web|url=http://popcrush.com/pink-the-truth-about-love-album-review/|title=Pink, 'The Truth About Love' – Album Review|work=PopCrush|date=18 September 2012 }}</ref> MSN Music also reacted positively to the song, calling it "feisty".<ref>{{cite web|url=http://social.entertainment.msn.com/music/blogs/expert-witness-blogpost.aspx?post=c07b3062-b49d-4ea5-823a-0aa34c3a6324|title=msn|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121015071915/http://social.entertainment.msn.com/music/blogs/expert-witness-blogpost.aspx?post=c07b3062-b49d-4ea5-823a-0aa34c3a6324|archive-date=2012-10-15}}</ref>

''[[The New York Times]]'' complimented the song, as well as the album's title track, for recognizing "some nonstorybook sides of romance", and further praised "True Love" for being "bouncy" and upbeat.<ref>{{cite news| url=https://www.nytimes.com/2012/09/18/arts/music/albums-from-pink-dwight-yoakam-and-aimee-mann.html?_r=0 | work=The New York Times | first1=Jon | last1=Pareles | first2=Ben | last2=Ratliff | first3=Nate | last3=Chinen | title=Albums From Pink, Dwight Yoakam and Aimee Mann | date=September 17, 2012}}</ref> ''The [[Chicago Tribune]]'' gave a more critical review of the song, calling Allen's vocals "strangely subdued" and added that Allen was "tamping down Pink's strident, comical posturing". They also wrote that the collaboration "should've been a dream pairing, but falls flat".<ref>{{cite news| url=https://www.chicagotribune.com/2012/09/18/album-review-pink-the-truth-about-love/ | work=Chicago Tribune | title=Album review: Pink, 'The Truth About Love' | date=September 18, 2012}}</ref> [[PopMatters]] also gave the song a mixed review, sarcastically complimenting the song, as well as the title track, for "daring to reveal the truth about love", and describing the album as "Brave, honest stuff—that’s already been covered in millions of other songs.".<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.popmatters.com/pm/review/163290-pnk-the-truth-about-love/|title=P!nk: The Truth About Love|work=PopMatters|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130529165624/https://www.popmatters.com/pm/review/163290-pnk-the-truth-about-love/|archive-date=2013-05-29}}</ref>

==Music video==
[[File:CareyHartMay10.jpg|thumbnail|right|Pink's husband, [[Carey Hart]] (''pictured'') makes a cameo appearance in the music video|120px]]
According to Pink's official [[Facebook]] page, the music video for "True Love" was planned to premiere on July 1, 2013.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.facebook.com/pink?hc_location=stream|title=P!nk|work=Facebook}}</ref> The video was leaked online on June 30, on the website [[Direct Lyrics]]; however, a few hours later the video was released to her official VEVO account, a day earlier than planned.

The video directed by [[Sophie Muller]] (who also directed videos for "[[Trouble (Pink song)|Trouble]]" and "[[I Don't Believe You]]") features Pink, her husband [[Carey Hart]] and her 2-year-old daughter Willow. It is the fourth music video that Hart appears alongside P!nk. The video is a melody of flashing and swirling colors. Pink stated that she had no time for a dramatic music video, (like her previous videos for "[[Just Give Me a Reason]]" and "[[Try (Pink song)|Try]]", which show Pink's emotional and dramatic side) due to her being on her sold-out worldwide tour, backstage footage of which is featured in the video. Lily Allen was filmed separately, and during her short part in the song she is shown cutting vegetables and throwing them into a blender.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.directlyrics.com/pink-true-love-news.html|title=Pink - 'True Love' Music Video|work=Directlyrics}}</ref>
