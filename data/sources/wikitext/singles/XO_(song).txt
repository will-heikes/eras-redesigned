"'''XO'''" is a song by the American singer [[Beyoncé]] from her fifth studio album, [[Beyoncé (album)|''Beyoncé'']] (2013). [[Columbia Records]] released it to US [[pop radio]] on December 16, 2013. Beyoncé, [[Ryan Tedder]], and [[The-Dream|Terius "The-Dream" Nash]] wrote and produced "XO", a [[mid-tempo]] [[Pop music|pop]] and [[electro-rock]] [[Sentimental ballad|ballad]] that is instrumented by [[synthesizer]]s and [[Drum machine|drums]]. It incorporates elements of [[hip-hop]] and [[dancehall]] and has a [[call and response (music)|call and response]] chorus. A [[love song]], its lyrics detail a narrator's plea for a lasting and stable romance amidst the ups and downs of life.

Music critics generally acclaimed the radio- and stadium-friendly production and the intimate lyricism of "XO". The track's beginning contains an audio sample from the [[Space Shuttle Challenger disaster|Space Shuttle ''Challenger'' disaster]], which was criticized by the families of the lost crew and [[NASA]]. Beyoncé released a statement saying that the sample was meant to be a tribute to the ''Challenger'' crew. "XO" charted and received platinum [[List of music recording certifications|certifications]] in Australia, Canada, New Zealand, and the UK. In the US, the single peaked at number 45 on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] and has been [[RIAA certification|certified]] double platinum.

[[Terry Richardson]] directed the [[music video]] for "XO", filmed in [[Coney Island]] in late August 2013. It was made available on [[iTunes]] on December 13, 2013 and released online three days later. On [[the Mrs. Carter Show World Tour]], Beyoncé performed the song during the last stops of the North American leg in December 2013 and the second European leg in early 2014. She also performed the song at the [[Brit Awards 2014]] and the [[2014 MTV Video Music Awards]]. In 2014, [[Haim (band)|Haim]] covered the song during their several concerts, and [[John Mayer]] released his cover version as a single.

"XO" was written by [[Beyoncé]], [[The-Dream|Terius "The-Dream" Nash]] and [[Ryan Tedder]] while the production was helmed by the aforementioned group as well as [[Hit-Boy|Chauncey "Hit-Boy" Hollis]] and HazeBanga. Ramon Rivas [[audio engineer|engineered]] the song, with assistance from Justin Hergett. Beyoncé's vocals were recorded by Stuart White and Bart Schoudel while all instrumentation and [[programming (music)|programming]] was carried out by Tedder. The song was finally [[Audio mixing (recorded music)|mixed]] by Andrew Scheps. "XO" was recorded in four studios: Jungle City Studios and Oven Studios, both in [[New York City]], Trackdown Studios in [[Sydney]] and Tritonus Studios in [[Berlin]].<ref name="liner"/>

Beyoncé's vocals in the song span from the note of [[A (musical note)|A<sub>3</sub>]] to the note of [[C (musical note)|C<sub>5</sub>]].<ref name="musicnotes" /> When asked about her raw vocals in the song previously unheard on her material and the song's less polished sound, Beyoncé revealed that "XO" was recorded when she had a bad [[sinus infection]] and that it was recorded as a [[demo (music)|demo]] in several minutes. The original demo vocals were kept for a year without being re-recorded as the singer "really loved the imperfections" and wanted to focus on the album's music instead of the vocals.<ref>{{cite news|url=http://www.out.com/entertainment/music/2014/04/08/beyonc%C3%A9-liberated?page=0,1|title=Beyoncé Liberated|work=[[Out (magazine)|Out]]|page=2|first=Aaron|last=Hicklin|date=April 8, 2014|access-date=June 18, 2014|archive-date=July 18, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140718023012/http://www.out.com/entertainment/music/2014/04/08/beyonc%C3%A9-liberated?page=0,1|url-status=live}}</ref>

The lyrics of "XO" cheerfully celebrate love and life as Beyoncé attempts "to create light from darkness".<ref name="nypost" /> It has been described as a universal love song which describes different types of relationships.<ref name="thefour" /> The singer emphasizes how important living in the present with a person's loved one is as life is unpredictable,<ref name="slate"/> as well as how both tragic and magnificent life can be.<ref name="thefour" /> Throughout the song, the singer adopts a hopeful attitude and seems to have gone in trance with love,<ref name="hampp"/> a state further augmented by her usage of a generally low register.<ref name="pop" /> Caitlin White from the website The 405 interpreted the song's lyrics in depth: 
<blockquote>
In the darkest night of hate and intolerance we see impossible love stories conquering what our governments and societies declare is legally 'allowed' to be love... It is for love that transcends the romantic conception of man and woman and stretches out into the impossibilities of the cosmos. It leaves room for failure but hopes for success.<ref name="thefour" /></blockquote>

The opening lines of "XO" are echo-laden<ref name="guardian"/> and unlike other songs on ''Beyoncé'', the ballad contains several [[Hook (music)|hooks]].<ref name="pop"/> Beyoncé starts the song by asking a loved one to kiss her.<ref name="latimes" /> Some of the ascending [[Refrain|chorus lines]] are [[Call and response (music)|call and response]];<ref name="pop"/><ref name="vib"/> [[Backing vocalist|backed]] by a [[sing-along]] crowd, Beyoncé sings about how her lover's face enlightens her "darkest nights": "In the darkest night hour / I search through the crowd / Your face is all that I see / I give you everything".<ref name="Idolator" /><ref name="fusetv" /><ref name="hanan"/> The chorus ends with her adding, "Baby love me, lights out", with a croak in her voice.<ref name="finney" /><ref name="hanan">{{cite web|url=http://www.underthegunreview.net/2013/12/16/beyonce-releases-videos-for-drunk-in-love-and-xo/|title=Beyoncé Releases Videos for "Drunk In Love" and "XO"|publisher=Under the Gun Review|first=Tyler|last=Hanan|date=December 16, 2013|access-date=January 1, 2014|archive-date=January 2, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140102192024/http://www.underthegunreview.net/2013/12/16/beyonce-releases-videos-for-drunk-in-love-and-xo/|url-status=live}}</ref>

==Sampling controversy==
[[File:Challenger flight 51-l crew.jpg|thumb|right|215px|[[STS-51-L]] crew who died during the [[Space Shuttle Challenger disaster|Space Shuttle ''Challenger'' disaster]].]]
The song begins with a six second-long sample of former [[NASA]] public affairs officer Steve Nesbitt, recorded moments after [[Space Shuttle Challenger disaster|Space Shuttle ''Challenger'' disaster]] on January 28, 1986: "Flight controllers here looking very carefully at the situation. Obviously a major malfunction."<ref name=guardian>{{cite news |title=Nasa criticises Beyoncé over Challenger sample |url=https://www.theguardian.com/music/2014/jan/01/nasa-criticises-beyonce-challenger-sample-xo |first=Josh |last=Halliday |newspaper=[[The Guardian]] |date=January 1, 2014 |access-date=January 1, 2014 |archive-date=January 1, 2014 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140101221826/http://www.theguardian.com/music/2014/jan/01/nasa-criticises-beyonce-challenger-sample-xo |url-status=live }}</ref> The use of the sample has been criticized in the media, including Hardeep Phull of the ''[[New York Post]]'', who described its presence in the song as "tasteless".<ref name="nypost">{{cite news |url=https://nypost.com/2013/12/13/preview-beyonces-entire-new-album-now/ |title=Beyonce's sexy new album: Track by track |first=Hardeep |last=Phull |work=[[New York Post]] |publisher=[[News Corp (2013–present)|News Corp]] |date=December 13, 2013 |access-date=December 29, 2013 |archive-date=December 28, 2013 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131228184845/http://nypost.com/2013/12/13/preview-beyonces-entire-new-album-now/ |url-status=live }}</ref> Former [[NASA]] astronauts and families were also dismayed and labeled the use of the sample as "insensitive".<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.digitalspy.co.uk/music/news/a540889/beyonce-criticised-over-challenger-disaster-sample-on-xo.html |title=Beyoncé criticised over Challenger disaster sample on 'XO' |publisher=Digital Spy. Hearst Corporation |first=Tom |last=Eames |date=December 30, 2013 |access-date=December 31, 2013 |archive-date=December 30, 2013 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131230234416/http://www.digitalspy.co.uk/music/news/a540889/beyonce-criticised-over-challenger-disaster-sample-on-xo.html |url-status=live }}</ref> [[Keith Cowing]] of NASA Watch suggested that the use of the clip ranged from negligence to repugnance.<ref name="NASA Watch">{{cite web|url=http://nasawatch.com/archives/2013/12/beyonce-samplin.html |title=Beyoncé: Sampling The Sounds of Tragedy For Pop Music |publisher=NASA Watch |first=Keith |last=Cowing |date=December 29, 2013 |author-link=Keith Cowing |access-date=December 29, 2013}}</ref>

On December 30, 2013, NASA criticized the use of the sample, stating: "The ''Challenger'' accident is an important part of our history; a tragic reminder that space exploration is risky and should never be trivialized. NASA works every day to honor the legacy of our fallen astronauts as we carry out our mission to reach for new heights and explore the universe."<ref name="guardian"/><ref name="NASA Watch"/> Earlier on the same date, Beyoncé had issued a statement to [[ABC News (United States)|ABC News]] in response to the families, explaining why she and her co-songwriters decided to include the audio sample in "XO":

<blockquote>
My heart goes out to the families of those lost in the ''Challenger'' disaster. The song 'XO' was recorded with the sincerest intention to help heal those who have lost loved ones and to remind us that unexpected things happen, so love and appreciate every minute that you have with those who mean the most to you. The songwriters included the audio in tribute to the unselfish work of the ''Challenger'' crew with hope that they will never be forgotten.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://abcnews.go.com/Entertainment/beyonce-slammed-sampling-shuttle-tragedy-album/story?id=21365376 |title=Beyoncé Slammed for Sampling Shuttle Tragedy on New Album |first1=Clayton |last1=Sandell |first2=Gina |last2=Sunseri |work=[[ABC News (United States)|ABC News]] |date=December 30, 2013 |access-date=December 31, 2013 |archive-date=February 18, 2021 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210218202806/https://abcnews.go.com/Entertainment/beyonce-slammed-sampling-shuttle-tragedy-album/story?id=21365376 |url-status=live }}</ref>
</blockquote>

Forrest Wickman of ''[[Slate (magazine)|Slate]]'' magazine defended Beyoncé's choice to use the sample, and criticized the media reports for their "dismissive" interpretation of the song's "vague" lyrics as being about "a girl in a relationship".<ref name="slate"/> According to Wickman, the lyrics of the song refer to mortality and "the urgency of spending time with the ones you love before you lose them, because you never know when that could be"; this is shown in the hook lines, "Baby kiss me / Before they turn the lights out / You better kiss me / Before our time has run out."<ref name="slate"/> A similar interpretation was voiced by Douglas Wolk of ''[[Wired (website)|Wired]]'', who challenged the notion of the sample being simply a "trivialization", instead describing it as "a swift, understated and brutal reminder that everything can go horribly wrong before anyone understands what's happening, and that the light could be extinguished at any moment".<ref name="wired">{{cite magazine|url=https://www.wired.com/2014/01/beyonce-challenger-sample-xo/|title=In Defense of Beyoncé: Why the Challenger Disaster Belongs in a Love Song|magazine=Wired|last=Wolk|first=Douglas|date=January 4, 2014|publisher=[[Wired (website)|Wired]]. [[Condé Nast]]|access-date=January 4, 2014|archive-date=January 4, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140104183754/http://www.wired.com/underwire/2014/01/beyonce-challenger-sample-xo/|url-status=live}}</ref> Wickman concluded, "If there's anything anyone should know by now about Beyoncé Knowles, it's that there's nothing in her self-presentation that isn't carefully chosen."<ref name="slate">{{cite news |url=http://www.slate.com/blogs/browbeat/2013/12/30/beyonc_s_xo_samples_challenger_explosion_draws_misguided_criticism.html |title=Beyoncé's Sample of the ''Challenger'' Explosion Isn't As Random As You Think |work=[[Slate (magazine)|Slate]] |publisher=[[Graham Holdings Company]] |first=Forrest |last=Wickman |date=December 30, 2013 |access-date=January 1, 2014 |archive-date=January 1, 2014 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140101034020/http://www.slate.com/blogs/browbeat/2013/12/30/beyonc_s_xo_samples_challenger_explosion_draws_misguided_criticism.html |url-status=live }}</ref>

Caitlin White from the website The 405 noted that there was a reason for the song being "couched in warnings" of malfunction and tragedy elaborating, "As far controversy, denying Beyonce the ability to embrace the intertextuality of incorporating a historical recording like the Challenger explosion is an attempt to relegate her art to a lower form of [[political discourse]], one that's not 'worthy' of interacting with an event of cultural significance like this. But this is not just a 'pop album,' it's a woman holding forth on the role of women in our society, how they should approach the nuances of their life and most importantly, how they can choose to value relationships... That isn't just the work of a diva, that is the work of a political figure."<ref name="thefour" />

===Synopsis===
The video opens with scenes of Beyoncé standing in front of a wall made of flashing multi-coloured light bulbs. Several scenes of people riding [[bumper cars]] follow as Beyoncé also goes riding one. She then continues playing carnival games and rides the Cyclone roller coaster with her stylist Ty Hunter while wearing a baseball cap emblazoned with [[Notorious B.I.G.]]'s face and white tank top with jeans. Several scenes show Beyoncé walking along the boardwalk, while paparazzi take pictures of her; during one scene a [[TMZ]] cameraman Adam Glyn makes a cameo appearance.<ref name="rapvid" /> Several other scenes show the singer dancing with fans and street performers while sporting a light bow on her head. Beyoncé also dances a choreographed dance with several female background dancers on a [[skee ball]] game. Scenes of love couples and instances of various iconic establishments are also featured throughout. Footage of Beyoncé leading a crowd for the choreography at a concert is also featured as well as the singer appearing with a bat made of money.{{citation needed|date=April 2020}}

On April 15, 2014, [[John Mayer]] covered "XO" during a concert in Adelaide, Australia.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.rap-up.com/2014/04/15/video-john-mayer-covers-beyonce-xo/|title=John Mayer Covers Beyoncé's 'XO'|work=Rap-Up|date=April 15, 2014|access-date=June 25, 2014|archive-date=August 6, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140806105034/http://www.rap-up.com/2014/04/15/video-john-mayer-covers-beyonce-xo/|url-status=dead}}</ref> Three days prior to the performance, he used his [[Twitter]] account to praise the song, writing, "The best thing about Beyoncé? The break in her voice on the first 'baby, love me lights out' in XO. Real and raw."<ref name="shipley" /> One month later, on May 22, he released a studio version of the song on his [[SoundCloud]] account.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.digitalspy.co.uk/music/news/a573141/john-mayer-unveils-acoustic-cover-of-beyonces-xo.html|title=John Mayer unveils acoustic cover of Beyoncé's 'XO'|publisher=Digital Spy. Hearst Corporation|first=Tom|last=Eames|date=May 23, 2014|access-date=May 23, 2014|archive-date=May 23, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140523212707/http://www.digitalspy.co.uk/music/news/a573141/john-mayer-unveils-acoustic-cover-of-beyonces-xo.html|url-status=live}}</ref> It was made available for digital download by Columbia Records on May 27, 2014 through the iTunes Store.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://music.apple.com/us/album/xo-single/881555962|title=XO – Single by John Mayer|date=May 27, 2014|publisher=iTunes Store. Apple Inc|location=United States|access-date=May 27, 2014|archive-date=August 13, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140813072417/https://itunes.apple.com/us/album/xo-single/id881555962|url-status=live}}</ref>

Mayer's version is an acoustic stripped-down rendition complete with guitar and accentuated by piano and [[harmonica]].<ref name="killian">{{cite magazine|url=https://www.rollingstone.com/music/news/hear-john-mayers-stripped-down-take-on-beyonces-xo-20140523|title=Hear John Mayer's Stripped-Down Take on Beyoncé's 'XO'|magazine=Rolling Stone|first=Killian|last=Young|date=May 23, 2014|access-date=May 23, 2014|archive-date=May 24, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140524022459/http://www.rollingstone.com/music/news/hear-john-mayers-stripped-down-take-on-beyonces-xo-20140523|url-status=live}}</ref> Kelcey Shipley from MTV News praised his version for its "simplicity"<ref name="shipley">{{cite web|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/1831625/john-mayer-beyonce-xo-cover/|title=John Mayer Stripped Down Beyoncé's 'XO' For A Heart-Achingly Raw Cover: Listen|publisher=MTV News. MTV Networks|first=Kelcey|last=Shipley|date=May 24, 2014|access-date=May 28, 2014|archive-date=May 28, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140528002340/http://www.mtv.com/news/1831625/john-mayer-beyonce-xo-cover/|url-status=dead}}</ref> while Killian Young of ''Rolling Stone'' shortly described it as "sweet".<ref name="killian" /> ''Spin''{{'}}s Marc Hogan noted that "XO" was a "perfect song for him" and described it as "a person-to-person romantic exchange, transferred over to acoustic instruments but no less stadium-sized – or pop – for it."<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.spin.com/2014/05/john-mayer-beyonce-xo-cover-studio-version-stream/|title=John Mayer Turns Beyonce's 'XO' Into Perfect John Mayer Song|work=Spin|publisher=Spin Media LLC|first=Marc|last=Hogan|date=May 23, 2014|access-date=May 28, 2014|archive-date=July 3, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150703012804/http://www.spin.com/2014/05/john-mayer-beyonce-xo-cover-studio-version-stream/|url-status=live}}</ref> Lauren Nostro writing for ''Complex'' deemed Mayer's live rendition of the song "impressive" before adding that the harmonica, "will tug at your heart strings almost as much as the original song".<ref>{{cite news|url=http://www.complex.com/music/2014/04/john-mayer-beyonce-xo|title=John Mayer Performs Acoustic Cover of Beyoncé's "XO"|work=Complex|first=Lauren|last=Nostro|date=April 16, 2014|access-date=July 21, 2014|archive-date=July 29, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140729030946/http://www.complex.com/music/2014/04/john-mayer-beyonce-xo|url-status=live}}</ref>

For the week ending June 1, 2014, Mayer's version debuted at number 90 on the US ''Billboard'' Hot 100 chart and number 13 on the US [[Hot Rock Songs]] chart, selling 46,000 copies.<ref name="John Mayer XO Charts"/><ref>{{cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/charts/hot-100?page=8|title=June 14, 2014: The Hot 100|magazine=Billboard|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140704070039/http://www.billboard.com/charts/hot-100?page=8|page=9|url-status=live|archive-date=July 4, 2014}}</ref> On the Canadian Hot 100, "XO" peaked at a position of 76.<ref name="mayercan" /> The single also debuted and peaked at number 81 on the Australian Singles Chart on June 7, 2014.<ref name="mayeraus" /> On June 28, 2014, "XO" peaked at number 95 on the [[Single Top 100|Dutch Singles Chart]] and it fell off the following week.<ref name="mayerdutch" /> In the UK, it peaked at 115 on the UK Singles Chart on June 7, 2014.<ref name="zobbel" />

On April 8, 2014, remix versions of "XO" and "Blow" by French musician Monsieur Adi premiered on ''Out''{{'}}s official website along Beyoncé's appearance on the cover of the magazine.<ref name="remixcomplex">{{cite web|url=http://www.complex.com/music/2014/04/beyonce-official-blow-xo-remixes|title=Listen to the Official Remixes of Beyoncé's "Blow" & "XO" by Monsieur Adi|work=Complex|first=Lauren|last=Nostro|date=April 8, 2014|access-date=June 18, 2014|archive-date=July 20, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140720151614/http://www.complex.com/music/2014/04/beyonce-official-blow-xo-remixes|url-status=live}}</ref> The remix for "XO" was influenced by [[electro house]] music and it was noted for being transformed as a "club-friendly power pop ballad" by the editors of the magazine.<ref>{{cite news|url=http://www.out.com/entertainment/music/2014/04/08/exclusive-beyonc%C3%A9-debuts-free-remixes-blow-xo|title=EXCLUSIVE: Beyoncé Debuts Free Remixes|work=[[Out (magazine)|Out]]|date=April 8, 2014|access-date=June 18, 2014|archive-date=April 29, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140429031727/http://www.out.com/entertainment/music/2014/04/08/exclusive-beyonc%C3%A9-debuts-free-remixes-blow-xo|url-status=live}}</ref> ''Complex'' magazine writer Lauren Nostro described the version as "fantastic".<ref name="remixcomplex" />
