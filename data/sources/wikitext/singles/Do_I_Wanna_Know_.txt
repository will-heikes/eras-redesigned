"'''Do I Wanna Know?'''" is a song by English [[Rock music|rock]] band [[Arctic Monkeys]], with lyrics written by frontman [[Alex Turner]]. It was released on 19 June 2013 by [[Domino Recording Company]] as the second single from their fifth studio album, ''[[AM (Arctic Monkeys album)|AM]]'' (2013). It received a [[Music download|digital download]] release through [[iTunes]] as well as an accompanying music video. Before its release as a single, the song was premiered by the band live in May 2013 on the [[AM Tour]], where it was often played as the opening number. It is an [[indie rock]], [[psychedelic rock]], [[stoner rock]], [[blues rock]] and [[alternative rock]] song, and is built around a stomping guitar riff.

"Do I Wanna Know?" peaked at number 11 in the [[UK Singles Chart]], charted in several other countries and was the first Arctic Monkeys song to have ever entered the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] chart in the United States, peaking at number 70 in March 2014 and also serving as their "big breakthrough moment" in the US. The song was nominated for [[Grammy Award for Best Rock Performance|Best Rock Performance]] at the [[57th Annual Grammy Awards]] in 2015. In December 2019, the song was ranked number three on ''[[Guitar World]]''{{'}}s list of the 20 best guitar riffs of the decade. The song has accumulated over 3 billion streams on Spotify, and ranks #62 in the [[List of Spotify streaming records|100 most-streamed songs in the streaming platform's history]], while its music video has earned 1.8 billion views on YouTube.

== Composition ==
"Do I Wanna Know?" is performed in the key of [[G minor]].<ref name= "song key finder">{{cite web|url=http://www.songkeyfinder.com/songs/2831/Arctic+Monkeys+Do+I+Wanna+Know?|title=Do I Wanna Know by Arctic Monkeys @ Song Key Finder|access-date=12 December 2014|archive-date=31 December 2020|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201231205042/http://www.songkeyfinder.com/songs/2831/Arctic+Monkeys+Do+I+Wanna+Know|url-status=live}}</ref> Musically, it has been described as an [[indie rock]],<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.radiox.co.uk/x-lists/top-100-best-rock-love-songs-all-time/arctic-monkeys-do-wanna-know/|title=The Top 100 Indie Love Songs of All Time|website=[[Radio X (United Kingdom)|Radio X]]|publisher=[[Global Radio]]|access-date=12 August 2016|archive-date=31 December 2020|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201231205049/https://www.radiox.co.uk/features/x-lists/best-indie-rock-love-songs-of-all-time/|url-status=live}}</ref> [[psychedelic rock]],<ref>{{cite web|last=Cameron|first=Keith|date=9 September 2013|title=Arctic Monkeys – AM|url=https://www.mojo4music.com/articles/6412/arctic-monkeys-am|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180727181022/https://www.mojo4music.com/articles/6412/arctic-monkeys-am|archive-date=27 July 2018|access-date=24 March 2021|website=[[Mojo (magazine)|Mojo]]|quote=Superficially, Do I Wanna Know? harks back to the spectral psych-rock the Monkeys essayed so successfully on Humbug}}</ref> [[stoner rock]],<ref>{{cite web|last=Lenser|first=Barry|date=9 October 2013|title=The Top Ten Arctic Monkeys Songs|url=https://www.popmatters.com/best-arctic-monkeys-songs-2495718568.html|access-date=9 October 2015|website=[[PopMatters]]|quote=A moody stoner-rock cocktail of midnight lust and slow-burning sonics.}}</ref> [[blues rock]]<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.stereogum.com/1705240/hozier-do-i-wanna-know-arctic-monkeys-cover/mp3s/|title= Hozier – "Do I Wanna Know?" (Arctic Monkeys Cover)|first=Chris|last=DeVille|date=15 September 2014|website=[[Stereogum]]|publisher= [[SpinMedia]]|access-date= 9 October 2015|quote=but Hozier drains it of its blues-rock swagger and converts it into an emotional folk-pop ballad}}</ref> and [[alternative rock]] song.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.iheart.com/content/2019-11-22-30-alternative-rock-songs-that-defined-the-2010s/ |title=30 Alternative Rock Songs That Defined the 2010s|publisher=[[iHeartMedia]] |last=Nattress |first=Katrina|date=November 22, 2019|access-date=May 10, 2022}}</ref> The song has a similar aesthetic style and lyrical content to their 2012 song "[[R U Mine?]]"; it is more downtempo while having similar guitar riffs.<ref name="gigwise" /> ''[[PopMatters]]'' describes it as "a cleaner, slower-burning 'R U Mine?'", while also finding its "stomping" style to be a "steadier take" on the music found on ''[[Humbug (album)|Humbug]]'' (2009).<ref name="Lenser PopMatters">{{cite web |last=Lenser |first=Barry |title=Arctic Monkeys – "Do I Wanna Know?" (video) |url=https://www.popmatters.com/173019-do-i-wanna-know-the-arctic-monkeys-2495745082.html |website=PopMatters |access-date=31 December 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201231223945/https://www.popmatters.com/173019-do-i-wanna-know-the-arctic-monkeys-2495745082.html |archive-date=31 December 2020 |date=28 June 2013}}</ref> The song also contains the edgier sound found on their previous album ''[[Suck It and See]]'' (2011).<ref name="gigwise">{{cite web | title=Arctic Monkeys' new single 'Do I Wanna Know' gets (mostly) good reaction | url=http://www.gigwise.com/news/82351/arctic-monkeys-new-single-do-i-wanna-know-gets-mostly-good-reaction | date=19 June 2013 | access-date=19 June 2013 | website=[[Gigwise]] | archive-date=31 December 2020 | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201231205047/https://www.gigwise.com/news/82351/arctic-monkeys-new-single-do-i-wanna-know-gets-mostly-good-reaction | url-status=live }}</ref>

Structurally, the song follows a common [[pop music]] "verse-pre-chorus-chorus" form up until its second chorus; from that point on, it follows what Hit Songs Deconstructed calls "a more unorthodox flow" ending in a "pre-chorus/chorus hybrid section". [[Falsetto]] backing vocals are also blended with those of Turner's throughout the song. Moreover, the title encapsulates the entire premise of the story, the narrator wondering whether he wants to remain in doubt or know if his feelings are unrequited or mutual.<ref name= "hit songs deconstructed">{{cite web|url=http://reports.hitsongsdeconstructed.com/hsdwire/wanna-know/|title= Do I Wanna Know? == Commercial performance ==
Despite a midweek [[on air on sale]] release, the song entered the [[UK Singles Chart]] at number 11,<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.officialcharts.com/charts/singles-chart/20130623/7501/|title=2013 Top 40 Official UK Singles Archive|website=[[Official Charts Company|Official Charts]] |date=29 June 2013|access-date=14 July 2013|archive-date=9 August 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180809073442/http://www.officialcharts.com/charts/singles-chart/20130623/7501/|url-status=live}}</ref> making it the band's highest-charting single since "[[Fluorescent Adolescent]]" in 2007. It was outperformed by the next single "[[Why'd You Only Call Me When You're High?]]", which debuted at number eight. "Do I Wanna Know?" has spent 65 weeks in the UK top 100, this being the longest run of any Arctic Monkeys single, to date.<ref>{{Cite web |url=https://www.officialcharts.com/artist/17120/arctic-monkeys/ |title=Arctic Monkeys &#124; full Official Chart History &#124; Official Charts Company |website=[[Official Charts Company|Official Charts]] |access-date=20 February 2020 |archive-date=31 December 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201231205047/https://www.officialcharts.com/artist/17120/arctic-monkeys/ |url-status=live }}</ref> The single was awarded a platinum certification by the [[British Phonographic Industry]] (BPI) on 7 February 2015, indicating shipments in excess of 600,000 units; it is the first Arctic Monkeys single to do so. As of {{YEAR}}, it is certified septuple-platinum in the UK.<ref name="ukcert"/>

The song had moderate success worldwide, charting in countries such as [[Australian Recording Industry Association|Australia]], [[Syndicat National de l'Édition Phonographique|France]], Belgium, Ireland and Israel. In January 2014, the song reached number one on the [[Alternative Songs|''Billboard'' Alternative Songs]] chart,<ref name="billalt"/> the group's first number one single in the United States and their first appearance on that chart since "[[I Bet You Look Good on the Dancefloor]]" peaked at number seven in 2006.<ref>{{cite web|title=Arctic Monkeys: Charts and Awards|url=http://www.allmusic.com/artist/arctic-monkeys-mn0000325357/awards|website=[[AllMusic]]|access-date=11 September 2013|archive-date=31 December 2020|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201231205050/https://www.allmusic.com/search/artists/arctic+monkeys|url-status=live}}</ref> On 28 March, the track became the 30th song to have occupied the ''Billboard'' Alternative Songs chart top spot for 10 weeks or more. As of the 25 October 2014 issue of ''Billboard'', it logged 58 weeks on the Alternative Songs list, making it the second-longest running song on the chart at the time. It also became the band's first single to appear on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] chart, peaking at number 70 in March 2014.<ref name="USchart"/> On 26 January 2014, the song reached number four on [[Triple J]]'s Hottest 100 of 2013, and, on 14 March 2020, reached number three on Triple J's Hottest 100 of 2010s.

In late 2023, for the 35th anniversary of Alternative Songs (which by then had been renamed to Alternative Airplay), ''Billboard'' ranked "Do I Wanna Know?" as the 18th-most successful song in the chart's history.<ref name="Alt-35">{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/music/chart-beat/alternative-airplay-chart-35th-anniversary-foo-fighters-all-time-low-rule-1235408126/|title=Alternative Airplay Chart's 35th Anniversary: Foo Fighters Remain No. 1 Act, 'Monsters' New Top Song|last=Rutherford|first=Kevin|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|date=September 7, 2023|access-date=November 10, 2023}}</ref><ref name="Greatest-Alt-2023">{{cite magazine|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20231006001750/https://www.billboard.com/charts/greatest-alternative-songs/|url=https://www.billboard.com/charts/greatest-alternative-songs/|title=Greatest of All Time Alternative Songs|archive-date=October 6, 2023|access-date=November 10, 2023|url-status=live|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]}}</ref>

== Music video ==
The music video for "Do I Wanna Know?", directed by [[David Wilson (director)|David Wilson]] with animation agency [[Blink (company)#Blinkink|Blinkink]],<ref name="director">{{cite web |url=http://www.itsnicethat.com/articles/arctic-monkeys-1 |title=Music Video: The fantastic animated video for the new single by Arctic Monkeys! |website=It's Nice That |date=19 June 2013 |access-date=19 June 2013 |archive-date=24 June 2013 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130624024929/http://www.itsnicethat.com/articles/arctic-monkeys-1 |url-status=live }}</ref> was first released onto [[YouTube]] on 18 June 2013.<!-- In Europe it's 18 June and not 19 June, so don't change the date. See: http://i41.tinypic.com/14upv5.jpg --> As of June 2020, it has been viewed over one billion times,<ref>{{cite web |last=Skinner |first=Tom |title=Arctic Monkeys reach one billion YouTube hits for 'Do I Wanna Know?' video |url=https://www.nme.com/news/music/arctic-monkeys-reach-one-billion-youtube-hits-for-do-i-wanna-know-video-2690222 |website=NME |access-date=31 December 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201026162153/https://www.nme.com/news/music/arctic-monkeys-reach-one-billion-youtube-hits-for-do-i-wanna-know-video-2690222 |archive-date=26 October 2020 |date=17 June 2020}}</ref> becoming one of only 12 rock videos to achieve this feat.<ref>{{cite web |last=Schatz |first=Lake |title=Arctic Monkeys' "Do I Wanna Know?" Video Surpasses One Billion Views on YouTube |url=https://consequence.net/2020/06/arctic-monkeys-do-i-wanna-know-one-billion-views-video/ |website=[[Consequence of Sound]] |access-date=31 December 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201030083104/https://consequenceofsound.net/2020/06/arctic-monkeys-do-i-wanna-know-one-billion-views-video/ |archive-date=30 October 2020 |url-status=live |date=17 June 2020}}</ref> The video begins with a black background and simple visuals of white sound waves (similar to the ''AM'' cover art) that vibrate in synchronisation, first with the percussion and lead guitar, then with the lead singer, Alex Turner. As the band enters with the chorus, coloured sound waves illustrate new voices. Simple sound waves then give way to fast-moving, representational line-drawing animations that morph between a variety of female, race car, race car engine, and road racing images. At one point, the undulating white line becomes the "trucker's [[Mudflap girl]]", seen in the single's cover art. The line drawings are interrupted several times with flashes of full-color animation, several that recall the surrealistic style of [[Robert Crumb]]. The increasingly complex video creates, by turns, a somewhat jarring and psychedelic experience, in a style not unlike the Gary Gutierrez animations that were featured in ''[[The Grateful Dead Movie]]'' (1977). The video ends with the familiar white line becoming two crossed checkered flags, which join together in a single line with the "AM" initials.
