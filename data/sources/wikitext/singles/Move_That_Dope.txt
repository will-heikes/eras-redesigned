"'''Move That Dope'''" (edited version titled as "'''Move That Doh'''") is a song by American rapper [[Future (rapper)|Future]] featuring fellow American rappers [[Pharrell Williams]], [[Pusha T]] and [[Freebandz#Artists|Casino]]. It was released on February 6, 2014 as the fourth single from Future's second studio album, ''[[Honest (Future album)|Honest]]''.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.frequencynews.com/musiclibrary_detail.cfm?LabelID=141&ArtistID=482&AlbumID=1164&SingleID=3928&Gospel=0 |title=Frequency News |access-date=June 27, 2014 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140408213351/http://www.frequencynews.com/musiclibrary_detail.cfm?LabelID=141&ArtistID=482&AlbumID=1164&SingleID=3928&Gospel=0 |archive-date=April 8, 2014 |url-status=dead }}</ref> Production was handled by the album's [[executive producer]], [[Mike Will Made It]], with co-production by P-Nasty. Casino's verse was omitted from the radio version of the song.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.hotnewhiphop.com/554031-future-move-that-dope-feat-pusha-t-pharrell-radio-version-prod-by-mike-will-made-it-new-song|title=Move That Dope (Radio Version) (Prod. By Mike WiLL Made It) Future feat. Pusha T & Pharrell|publisher=HNHH|author=Cheek, Phillip|date=February 21, 2014|access-date=September 30, 2014}}</ref>

"Move That Dope" peaked at number 46 on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]].

==Music video==
On March 6, 2014, the music video was released. Both Casino and his verse were not featured in the video. However, the extended version does feature Casino. The video includes cameo appearances by [[Tyler, the Creator]], [[Schoolboy Q]], [[Mike Will Made It]], and [[Wiz Khalifa]].

==Chart performance==
"Move That Dope" peaked at number 46 on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]]. It spent a total of 18 weeks on the chart. The song was certified [[RIAA certification|gold]] by the [[Recording Industry Association of America]] (RIAA) for sales of 500,000 digital copies in the United States.
