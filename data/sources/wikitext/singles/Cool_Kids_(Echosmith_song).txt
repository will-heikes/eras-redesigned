"'''Cool Kids'''" is the debut single by American [[indie pop]] band [[Echosmith]] from their debut studio album, ''[[Talking Dreams]]'' (2013). The song was written by Echosmith, Jeffery David, and Jesiah Dzwonek. It was produced by [[Mike Elizondo]], with additional production on the radio edit by [[Rob Cavallo]]. "Cool Kids" was originally released on May 31, 2013, as the [[iTunes Store]] Single of the Week and officially impacted United States radio the following year. The song subsequently became a [[sleeper hit]], peaking at number 13 on the ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' [[Billboard Hot 100|Hot 100]] and receiving airplay on US [[modern rock]], [[hot adult contemporary]], and [[contemporary hit radio]] stations. It also reached the top 10 in seven countries.

==Background==
The song describes a boy and a girl who both desire to be noticed.<ref name="Radio">{{cite web |url=http://radio.com/2014/01/10/new-music-to-know-echosmith-aspires-toward-role-model-status/ |title=New Music To Know: Echosmith Aspires Toward Role Model Status |publisher=[[Radio.com]] |date=January 10, 2014 |accessdate=February 3, 2015}}</ref> In an interview, guitarist Jamie Sierota discussed the song's background, saying: "This cry to be like the cool kids… it's something that everyone kind of goes through whether you want to act like it or not... There's always somebody out there that you kind of wish, 'If only I could do this, or do that.' I think that's why it connects with people so well."<ref name="Radio"/>

==Composition==

"Cool Kids" is written in the key of [[A-flat major|A♭ major]], and follows a chord progression of Fm–D♭–A♭–E♭ (vi–IV–I–V). The tempo of the song is 130 beats per minute.{{citation needed|date=March 2019}}

==Music video==
The song's official music video was filmed in [[Los Angeles]], [[California]] and directed by [[Gus Black]].<ref name=Officialvideo>{{cite web |url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UXTlczyWJ-Y |title=Echosmith – Cool Kids [Official Music Video] |publisher=[[YouTube]] |accessdate=July 19, 2014 |author=Echosmith |date=June 21, 2013 |author-link=Echosmith}}</ref> It was released on June 21, 2013, on Echosmith's official [[YouTube]] channel.<ref name=Officialvideo/> After the original video passed one million views, Echosmith released an acoustic version of the song.<ref name=Officialvideo/> The band also released a new music video for "Cool Kids" on September 12, 2014, directed by [[Mark Pellington]].<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.altpress.com/news/entry/echosmith_release_new_music_video_for_cool_kids |title=Echosmith release new music video for 'Cool Kids' |work=[[Alternative Press (music magazine)|Alternative Press]] |date=September 11, 2014 |accessdate=November 4, 2014 |author=Crane, Matt}}</ref>

==Commercial performance==
"Cool Kids" debuted at number 87 on the ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' [[Hot 100]] for the issue dated July 26, 2014 and peaked at number 13.<ref name="Hot100"/> The song also reached the top ten of the ''Billboard'' [[Mainstream Top 40]] and [[Adult Top 40]] charts, peaking at numbers 9 and 5 respectively.<ref name="AdultTop40"/><ref name="Pop"/> It was certified gold by the [[Recording Industry Association of America]] on October 4, 2014,{{Certification Cite Ref|region=United States|type=single|title=Cool Kids|artist=Echosmith}} and passed the one million sales mark later on in the same month.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://music.yahoo.com/blogs/chart-watch/chart-watch--maroon-5-busts-up-all-female-top-five-214218531.html |title=Chart Watch: Maroon 5 Busts Up All-Female Top Five |work=[[Yahoo! Music]] |publisher=[[Yahoo!]] |date=October 29, 2014 |accessdate=October 29, 2014 |author=Grein, Paul}}</ref> As of December 2014, it has sold over 1.1 million copies in the United States.<ref name=us_sales>{{cite web |url=http://www.hitsdailydouble.com/news/newsPage.cgi?news10229m01 |archive-url=https://archive.today/20141212184355/http://www.hitsdailydouble.com/news/newsPage.cgi?news10229m01 |url-status=dead |archive-date=December 12, 2014 |title=The Big 9 in 2014: Warner Bros. |work=HITS Daily Double |publisher=HITS Digital Ventures |date=December 12, 2014 |accessdate=December 12, 2014 }}</ref> "Cool Kids" also peaked at number 6 on the Australian singles chart and was certified double platinum in the country.<ref name="AUS"/><ref name="ARIA"/>

==Other versions==
In September 2022, the group released a new version of the song, labelled as "our version".<ref>{{cite web|url=https://vmagazine.com/article/echosmith-releases-new-empowered-rendition-of-hit-cool-kids/|title=Echosmith Releases New, Empowered Rendition of Hit "Cool Kids"|work=[[V (American magazine)|V]]|date=September 9, 2022|accessdate=October 4, 2023|first=Henry|last=Chandonnet}}</ref> The version was nominated for Self-Released Record of the Year at the [[2023 Libera Awards]].<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/music/awards/libera-awards-2023-nominations-list-1235291362/|title=Wet Leg Leads Nominations for 2023 Libera Awards|first=Rania|last=Aniftos|date=March 22, 2023|accessdate=October 4, 2023|magazine=Billboard}}</ref>
