"'''Pretty Girls'''" is a song recorded by American singer [[Britney Spears]] and Australian rapper [[Iggy Azalea]]. Azalea co-wrote the song with [[Maegan Cottone]], British girl group [[Little Mix]], and the song's producers, [[The Invisible Men]]. The single was released on May 4, 2015, by [[RCA Records]].

The music video for "Pretty Girls" premiered on May 13, 2015, and was directed by Azalea and Cameron Duddy. The visuals were inspired by the 1988 film ''[[Earth Girls Are Easy]]'', starring [[Geena Davis]]. The song was performed live by Spears and Azalea for the first time on May 17 at the [[2015 Billboard Music Awards|2015 ''Billboard'' Music Awards]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.baltimoresun.com/83572927-132.html|title=Britney Spears and Iggy Azalea work the Billboard Music Awards 2015|access-date=2019-12-09|archive-date=2019-12-09|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20191209115317/https://www.baltimoresun.com/83572927-132.html|url-status=dead}}</ref> Later that year, it was added to the setlist for Spears' [[Las Vegas]] residency show, [[Britney: Piece of Me]].

The collaboration peaked within the top 20 in the United Kingdom, Canada, Hungary, Scotland, and South Korea as well as the top 30 in the United States, France, and Australia. The track also received two nominations at the [[2015 Teen Choice Awards]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.usmagazine.com/entertainment/news/teen-choice-awards-2015-one-direction-vs-zayn-malik-in-nominations-201596|title=Teen Choice Awards 2015: One Direction Up Against Zayn Malik in First Round of Nominations|publisher=Us Weekly|access-date=June 10, 2015|date=June 9, 2015}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/6620382/teen-choice-awards-2015-nominations-taylor-swift-one-direction|title=Teen Choice Awards 2015 Nominations: Taylor Swift, One Direction & More Lead Second Wave of Noms|publisher=Billboard|access-date=July 9, 2015}}</ref>

==Background and release==
On September 9, 2014, during an interview with ''[[Extra (U.S. TV program)|Extra]]'' at [[Britney Spears products#The Intimate Britney Spears Collection|The Intimate Britney Spears Collection]] launch in New York City, Spears revealed she had slowly but steadily started working on a new album. When asked who she would like to collaborate with, Spears said she would like to do something with [[Katy Perry]] or [[Iggy Azalea]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.digitalspy.co.uk/music/news/a595741/britney-spears-working-on-new-album-wants-katy-perry-duet.html|title=Britney Spears working on new album, wants Katy Perry duet|work=[[Digital Spy]]|date=September 10, 2014|access-date=May 3, 2015}}</ref> Rumors began to circulate that a collaboration between the two artists was already in the works, with Azalea saying she had done a "secret collaboration" with someone she had been interested in.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://eonline.com/news/581700/iggy-azalea-says-working-with-jennifer-lopez-was-surreal-teases-new-secret-collaboration-watch-now|title=Iggy Azalea Says Working With Jennifer Lopez Was "Surreal," Teases New Secret Collaboration – Watch Now!|publisher=[[E!]] Online|date=September 22, 2014|access-date=May 3, 2015}}</ref>

In December 2014, Azalea told [[WXKS-FM|Kiss 108]] backstage at their [[Jingle Ball Tour 2014]] that their collaborative song would premiere in 2015, "We have a song, which is going to be her first single, coming out next year." Asked how the collaboration came about, Azalea added, "She said she would like to work with me in an interview and they reached out. We recorded a few different things and then one of those just ended up being, I think, undeniably great. Hopefully I think everybody will get to hear it at the beginning of next year."<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.digitalspy.co.uk/music/news/a616543/britney-spears-records-undeniably-great-new-single-with-iggy-azalea.html|title=Britney Spears records 'undeniably great' new single with Iggy Azalea|work=Digital Spy|date=December 15, 2014|access-date=May 3, 2015}}</ref> In February 2015, Azalea tweeted there were "epic" ongoing plans for the song.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.mtv.co.uk/news/q75n5h/britney-spears-has-some-epic-plans-for-her-new-single-iggy-azalea-says-so|title=Britney Spears Has Some 'Epic Plans' For Her New Single... Iggy Azalea Says So!|publisher=[[MTV News]]|date=February 4, 2015|access-date=May 3, 2015}}</ref> For Spears's [[Billboard (magazine)|''Billboard'']] magazine cover story, published online on March 12, 2015, it was reported that despite Spears being back in the recording studio as back as in September 2014 working on a duet with Azalea, among other tracks, a proper full-length album would not be coming soon. Spears further explained, "I'm gonna do [a new album] slowly but surely [...] I'm gonna try to do my best to do an amazing album, but it's not my full priority right now".<ref>{{cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/magazine/6501934/britney-spears-working-on-next-album-exclusive|title=Britney Spears: I'm 'Slowly But Surely' Working On a New Album (Exclusive)|magazine=Billboard|date=March 12, 2015|access-date=May 3, 2015}}</ref> Azalea confirmed the title of the track the following week.<ref>{{cite web|last1=Peterson|first1=Jacques|title=Britney's New Single With Iggy Azalea Is Called 'Pretty Girls' And Little Mix Wrote It|url=http://popdust.com/2015/03/15/britney-iggy-little-mix-pretty-girls/|publisher=Popdust|access-date=May 3, 2015|date=March 15, 2015|archive-date=May 20, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150520085855/http://popdust.com/2015/03/15/britney-iggy-little-mix-pretty-girls/|url-status=dead}}</ref> Afterwards, in Spears's cover story with [[People (magazine)|''People'']] magazine, it was announced that "Pretty Girls" would be released on May 5.<ref name="peoplecover">{{cite web|url=http://celebritybabies.people.com/2015/03/25/britney-spears-sons-home-cover-story/|title=At Home with Britney Spears and Her Boys|work=[[People (magazine)|People]]|date=March 25, 2015|access-date=May 3, 2015|archive-date=May 25, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150525162302/http://celebritybabies.people.com/2015/03/25/britney-spears-sons-home-cover-story/|url-status=dead}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.fuse.tv/2014/11/iggy-azalea-britney-spears-collaboration-song|title=Britney Spears Reveals Iggy Azalea Collab 'Pretty Girls' Release Date|publisher=[[Fuse (TV channel)|Fuse]]|date=March 25, 2015|access-date=May 3, 2015}}</ref>

On March 29, 2015, on the red carpet of the [[2nd iHeartRadio Music Awards]] in Los Angeles, Azalea confirmed "Pretty Girls" would be featured on Spears' upcoming album during an interview with [[MTV News]] and explained the song's evolution mentioning its final version being a real [[duet]] with her also joining Spears on the [[Refrain|chorus]] instead of an actual featured rapping.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/2119377/iggy-azalea-britney-spears-album-ready-pretty-girls/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150331024245/http://www.mtv.com/news/2119377/iggy-azalea-britney-spears-album-ready-pretty-girls/|url-status=dead|archive-date=March 31, 2015|title=Iggy Azalea Reveals Britney Spears' Album Is 'Ready' And Will Feature Their Collaboration|publisher=MTV|date=March 30, 2015|access-date=May 3, 2015}}</ref> When describing the song in an interview with ''[[USA Today]]'', Azalea said, "It's just a lot of fun. It's a classic, girly (song) you want to scream to each other when you're getting ready". Although the collaboration was finished in fall 2014, Azalea did not meet Spears in person until January 2015, when she invited Spears to her house for lunch.<ref name="usatoday">{{cite web|url=https://www.usatoday.com/story/life/music/2015/04/26/iggy-azalea-britney-spears-pretty-girls-single-video/26246349/|title= Britney, Iggy team up for 'Pretty' duet|work=[[USA Today]]|date=April 26, 2015}}</ref>

On April 30, 2015, the single artwork, an illustration of the artists in an [[outer space]] background referring to the '80s film ''[[Earth Girls Are Easy]]'', was unveiled online.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.instyle.com/news/britney-spears-and-iggy-azalea-debut-space-age-cover-art-pretty-girls-0|title=Britney Spears and Iggy Azalea Debut Space Age Cover Art for 'Pretty Girls'|publisher=Instyle.com|date=May 1, 2015|access-date=May 3, 2015}}</ref> Portions of the song began surfacing on the internet later that day, and the song [[music leak|leaked]] in its entirety on May 2, 2015, two days prior to its official release.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://time.com/3844501/britney-spears-pretty-girls/|title=Hear Britney Spears and Iggy Azalea's New Song 'Pretty Girls'|magazine=[[Time (magazine)|Time]]|date=May 2, 2015}}</ref> On May 3, 2015, Spears announced she had teamed up with [[Uber]] to give fans an early listen of the song. For six hours between 3pm-9pm PDT on that day, requesters in Los Angeles were able to ride select Britney-themed vehicles called "Bees" while listening to the song and have a chance to win surprise gifts and tickets to her show, [[Britney: Piece of Me]] in Las Vegas.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.people.com/article/britney-spears-pretty-girls-iggy-azalea-first-listen|title=The Britneymobile? See the Crazy Way Fans Can Hear Her New Single 'Pretty Girls'|date=May 3, 2015|website=people.com}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://blog.uber.com/Britney-Pretty-Girls|title=Uber LA Riders: Britney Spears Wants You To Hear 'Pretty Girls' First|date=May 3, 2015|website=uber.com}}</ref>
On May 4, 2015, "Pretty Girls" was released for digital download, with anticipating fans trending the hashtag #PrettyGirls on Twitter.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.billboard.com/pro/britney-spears-iggy-azaleas-pretty-girls-top-40-debut/|title=Britney Spears & Iggy Azalea's 'Pretty Girls' Headed for Top 40 Debut|date=May 8, 2015|website=billboard.com}}</ref>

==Critical reception==
"Pretty Girls" received mixed to positive reviews from contemporary music critics and fans. Jason Lipshutz of ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' gave the track a positive review, saying "pop and [[Pop rap|pop-rap]] fans are likely to swarm this slick slice of [[electro-hop]], which pulls off the [[Blonde bombshell (stereotype)|blonde-bombshell]] pairing by playing to each of its artists' strengths," adding that Spears "sounds more animated in her two minutes of action here than on the entirety of 2013's ''[[Britney Jean]]'' album," and praising The Invisible Men's production claiming it differs just enough from Azalea's previous singles having "bestowed her with another stylish banger."<ref name="bbreview">{{cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/review/6553860/britney-spears-iggy-azalea-pretty-girls-review|title=Britney Spears & Iggy Azalea Stay Fancy on 'Pretty Girls': Single Review|first=Jason|last=Lipshutz|date=May 4, 2015|access-date=May 4, 2015|magazine=Billboard}}</ref> Jim Farber of the ''[[Daily News (New York)|New York Daily News]]'' claimed the track "is a breezy, summer song that will get stuck in your head" but lacking "the snap, and the freshness" of "[[Fancy (Iggy Azalea song)|Fancy]]".<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.nydailynews.com/entertainment/music/review-britney-spears-iggy-azalea-pretty-girls-article-1.2209364|title=Britney Spears and Iggy Azalea's 'Pretty Girls' is a breezy, summer song that will get stuck in your head: single review|first=Jason|last=Lipshutz|date=May 4, 2015|access-date=May 4, 2015|work=Daily News|location=New York}}</ref> Jesse Spero of ''[[Yahoo! Music]]'' called it "the song of the summer."<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.yahoo.com/music/brit-iggy-drop-pretty-girls-your-song-of-the-118119212391.html|title= Brit & Iggy Drop 'Pretty Girls'|first=Jesse|last=Spero|date=May 4, 2015|access-date=May 4, 2015|publisher=Yahoo!}}</ref> Hardeep Phull of the ''[[New York Post]]'' criticized the song's similar production to "Fancy" and described it as "a woefully formulaic rehash."<ref>{{cite web|url=https://nypost.com/2015/05/04/iggy-and-britney-release-tragically-bad-collab-and-other-music-reviews/|title=Iggy and Britney release tragically bad collab and other music reviews|first=Hardeep|last=Phull|date=May 4, 2015|access-date=May 4, 2015|work=New York Post}}</ref> August Brown of the ''[[Los Angeles Times]]'' mentioned the "[[Bubblegum music|bubbly]] [[Electro (music)|electro]]-[[Trap music|trap]] beats, a lot of teen-sassy rapping and the two sharing lead singing vocals on the chorus," also commenting "your summer song sweepstakes bracket just got an early contender."<ref name="latimes">{{cite web|url=http://www.latimes.com/entertainment/music/posts/la-et-ms-britney-spears-iggy-azalea-join-for-pretty-girls-20150504-story.html|title=Listen: Britney Spears, Iggy Azalea join forces for 'Pretty Girls'|work=Los Angeles Times|access-date=May 4, 2015|date=May 4, 2015}}</ref>

Lucas Villa of ''[[AXS (ticket merchant)|AXS]]'' remarked the "anthemic chorus about [[girl power]] in the party scene" and the "plucky production kicks" with a sound marrying [[1980s in music|'80s]] [[dance music]] like [[Debbie Deb]]'s "Lookout Weekend" and recent hits "Fancy" and "[[Hollaback Girl]]," also complimenting Spears for finally giving fans "something more urban in the same vein of 2007's ''[[Blackout (Britney Spears album)|Blackout]]''" and that both artists "make good on their promise to "bring the noise" on this attitude-packed club banger."<ref>{{cite web|url=http://axs.com/news/review-britney-spears-and-iggy-azalea-call-out-pretty-girls-on-club-ba-51919|title=Review: Britney Spears and Iggy Azalea call out 'Pretty Girls' on club banger|publisher=axs.com|access-date=May 4, 2015}}</ref> Hugh McIntyre of ''[[Forbes]]'' referenced a polarizing reception of the track that was "already being heralded by some as a fantastic new direction for the singer, while others aren’t so sure."<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.forbes.com/sites/hughmcintyre/2015/05/04/britney-spears-returns-with-iggy-azalea-assisted-new-single-pretty-girls/|title=Britney Spears Returns With Iggy Azalea-Assisted New Single "Pretty Girls"|work=Forbes|access-date=May 4, 2015}}</ref> Abby Schreiber of ''[[Paper (magazine)|Paper]]'' also observed the similar sound to "Fancy" interjecting with "No matter. It’s been almost two long years since the world had a new Britney track, so we’ll take it."<ref>{{cite web|url=http://vmagazine.com/site/content/4189/britneys-back|title=Britney's Back|publisher=vmagazine.com|access-date=May 4, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150505021045/http://vmagazine.com/site/content/4189/britneys-back|archive-date=May 5, 2015|url-status=dead}}</ref> Sydney Gore of ''[[Nylon (magazine)|Nylon]]'' also heralded it as a "Fancy"-follow up as well as an "empowering song in some regard."<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.nylon.com/articles/britney-spears-iggy-azalea-pretty-girls|title=Listen to Britney Spears and Iggy Azalea's "Pretty Girls"|publisher=nylon.com|access-date=May 4, 2015|date=May 4, 2015}}</ref> Bradley Stern of ''[[PopCrush]]'' also gave the song a positive review, commenting "those trap-lite [[hi-hat]]s and twinkly club beats are pure Hip-Hopney heaven" and "it’ll be a surefire contender for song of the Summer 2015".<ref>{{cite web|url=http://popcrush.com/britney-spears-iggy-azalea-pretty-girls-single-review/|title=Britney Spears & Iggy Azalea Bring The Noise With 'Pretty Girls': Single Review|publisher=popcrush.com|date=May 2, 2015}}</ref> ''[[Time (magazine)|Time]]'' named "Pretty Girls" the third-worst song of 2015.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://time.com/4133060/top-10-worst-songs-2/|title=Worst Songs of 2015|publisher=Time|date=December 1, 2015|access-date=January 14, 2016}}</ref> ''[[Entertainment Weekly]]''{{'}}s Andrew Hampp named it the worst female collaboration of the time span 1988–2018.<ref>{{Cite magazine |url=http://ew.com/music/2018/05/17/best-worst-diva-duets/ |title=Here are the best (and worst) diva duets of the last 20 years |last=Hammp |first=Andrew |date=May 17, 2018 |magazine=Entertainment Weekly |access-date=July 29, 2018}}</ref>

==Commercial performance==
"Pretty Girls" reached the top 20 in the United Kingdom, Canada, and Finland and the top 40 in the United States, France and Australia.
In North America, the song debuted and peaked at number 29 in the United States on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]]<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/music/britney-spears|title=Britney Spears|magazine=Billboard}}</ref> with digital sales of 96,000, marking Spears' 22nd solo top 40 (24th top 40 overally) and 33rd career entry on the chart. It dropped out of the top 40 of the Hot 100 three weeks after its release and spent eight weeks on the chart. For the issue dated July 18, 2015, the single peaked at the apex of the [[Hot Dance Club Songs|Billboard Hot Dance Club Songs Chart]], becoming her ninth No. 1 and her first since 2011's "[[I Wanna Go]]".<ref>{{cite magazine|last1=Trust|first1=Gary|title=Chart Highlights: Britney Spears & Iggy Azalea's 'Pretty Girls' Tops Dance Club Songs|url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/chart-highlights-britney-spears-iggy-azaleas-pretty-girls-tops/|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|access-date=July 19, 2015|date=July 6, 2015}}</ref> The single peaked at number 16 on the [[Canadian Hot 100]] chart.<ref name=":0" />

In Europe, "Pretty Girls"'s strongest entry was seen in the United Kingdom, where it debuted at number 16 on the [[UK Singles Chart|Official Singles Chart]], becoming Spears' 30th top 20 entry in the country, but fell out of the chart only three weeks after entry.<ref>{{Cite web|title = Official Singles Chart Top 100 {{!}} June 21, 2015 – June 27, 2015 {{!}} Official Charts Company|url = http://www.officialcharts.com/charts/singles-chart/|website = www.officialcharts.com|access-date = June 25, 2015}}</ref> The track also debuted at number 64 on the [[Irish Singles Chart]] for May 7, 2015<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.chart-track.co.uk/index.jsp?c=p%2Fmusicvideo%2Fmusic%2Farchive%2Findex_test.jsp&ct=240001&arch=t&lyr=2015&year=2015&week=19|title=Irish Chart Track > 7 May 2015|publisher=Irish Recorded Music Association|access-date=May 8, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150518104213/http://www.chart-track.co.uk/index.jsp?c=p%2Fmusicvideo%2Fmusic%2Farchive%2Findex_test.jsp&ct=240001&arch=t&lyr=2015&year=2015&week=19|archive-date=May 18, 2015|url-status=dead}}</ref> although debuting at number 19 on the downloads chart,<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.chart-track.co.uk/index.jsp?c=p/musicvideo/music/latest/index_test.jsp&ct=240006|title=GFK Chart-Track – Top 20 Downloads, Week Ending 7 May 2015|publisher=Irish Recorded Music Association|archive-date=May 4, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150504221826/https://www.chart-track.co.uk/index.jsp?c=p/musicvideo/music/latest/index_test.jsp&ct=240006|access-date=May 23, 2020|url-status=dead}}</ref> subsequently peaking at number 60 on the former.

In Oceania, "Pretty Girls" debuted at number 40 on the [[ARIA Charts|Australian Singles Chart]] issued for May 11, 2015,<ref name="ariacharts">{{cite web|url=http://www.ariacharts.com.au/chart/singles/78296|title=ARIA Australian Top 50 Singles Chart|publisher=[[Australian Recording Industry Association]]|date=May 11, 2015|access-date=May 9, 2015}}</ref> becoming Spears' 31st top 50 and 32nd top 100 entry, and later peaked at number 27.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.australian-charts.com/showitem.asp?interpret=Britney+Spears+%26+Iggy+Azalea&titel=Pretty+Girls&cat=s|title=ARIA Singles: Britney Spears & Iggy Azalea – Pretty Girls}}</ref> In a Twitter Q&A with fans in the following month, Azalea expressed her desire to have done additional promotion and television performances to support the song commercially.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/6613030/iggy-azalea-blames-britney-spears-pretty-girls|title=Iggy Azalea Blames Britney Spears for 'Pretty Girls' Not Being a Hit|last=Strecker|first=Erin|date=June 29, 2015|publisher=Billboard|access-date=July 4, 2015}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.billboard.com/music/pop/iggy-azalea-britney-spears-pretty-girls-no-shade-beef-6613170/|title=Iggy Azalea Responds to Britney Spears Beef Reports: 'Women in Music Are Not the Same as a Teen Drama'|last=Stutz|first=Colin|date=June 30, 2015|publisher=Billboard|access-date=July 4, 2015}}</ref>

was wearing matching denim jacket and shorts with crimped hair, while posing together in a bright red [[convertible]] [[Fox body mustang|Fox-Body Mustang]] and with Spears also driving around in a During an interview with ''[[USA Today]]'', Azalea talked about the concept of the music video, mentioning Spears' signature dance scenes, "I think Britney's identity is really embedded in being a dancer, and that's something she really wanted to do as well. They brainstormed the concept for the video, deciding on a send-up of 1988 [[sci-fi comedy]] ''[[Earth Girls Are Easy]]'', which starred Davis. Spears said, "I do a makeover for her and we go to a club. The best part about it was just us coming together with the clothes and doing the '80s haircuts. We were all about being characters, so it was kind of funny for us. We weren't taking ourselves so seriously".<ref name="usatoday"/>

===Release and synopsis===

The music video was directed by Azalea and Cameron Duddy and was released on May 13.<ref name=mtvuk>{{cite web|url=https://www.mtv.co.uk/news/mqjvqr/so-britney-spears-and-iggy-azaleas-pretty-girls-video-is-like-totally-out-of-this-world|title=So, Britney Spears And Iggy Azalea's 'Pretty Girls' Video Is Like, Totally Out Of This World|date=May 13, 2015|work=mtv.com.uk|access-date=June 1, 2015}}</ref> It continues a cult movie related focus for Azalea's music videos, her last year's single "[[Fancy (Iggy Azalea song)|Fancy]]" paid homage to the [[1990s in film|'90s]] [[teen film]] ''Clueless'' and "[[Black Widow (Iggy Azalea song)|Black Widow]]" to the ''[[Kill Bill]]'' films. The clip is a homage to the 1988 film, ''Earth Girls Are Easy'', directed by [[Julien Temple]] and starring Geena Davis. It therefore is heavily based on [[1980s in fashion|'80s fashion]] and technology as well as the loose plot of the film.<ref name=mtv>{{cite web|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/2159734/earth-girls-are-easy/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150516005428/http://www.mtv.com/news/2159734/earth-girls-are-easy/|url-status=dead|archive-date=May 16, 2015|title=Here's The Origin Of Iggy Azalea's New 'Pretty Girls' Video|date=May 13, 2015|work=mtv.com|access-date=June 1, 2015}}</ref> The video begins with Spears reclining poolside filing her nails with the health and wellness supplement ''MateFit.'' visible. Suddenly, an alien Iggy Azalea crashes into the pool. Spears then proceeds to give Azalea a new makeover.<ref name=rs>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.rollingstone.com/music/videos/britney-spears-iggy-azalea-have-campy-fun-in-pretty-girls-video-20150513|title=Britney Spears, Iggy Azalea Have Campy Fun in 'Pretty Girls' Video|date=May 13, 2015|magazine=Rolling Stone|access-date=June 1, 2015}}</ref>

Two women dress up in '80s fashion and drive in a bright yellow jeep to a car wash, where Spears and backup dancers break into dance. Meanwhile, Azalea's alien powers cause a television to explode, water to turn pink and an ATM to begin shooting cash, which Spears dances around. Soon, two women hop into a convertible with Spears' friends, where Azalea turns Spears' retro handheld telephone into a [[Samsung Galaxy S6]]. Nightfall arrives and two women head to a club where Azalea raps and Spears and dancers choreograph a synchronized dance break. Suddenly, the nightclub begins to shake and an unseen force proceeds to abduct Azalea and Spears, to the latter's delight. The screen fades black before two women appear laughing and doing arm movements in the yellow jeep and the credits roll.<ref name="4music">{{cite episode|title=This Week's Fresh Music Top 20|network=[[4Music]]|airdate=June 21, 2015}}</ref>

===Reception===
Josh Duboff from ''[[Vanity Fair (magazine)|Vanity Fair]]'' described the video as "entertaining and kind of weird. There’s a lot of big hair; Spears does some endearing waving (and generally seems to be having a good time)". He concluded, "Our favorite part is actually probably the tag at the end, where Spears and Azalea are just goofing around in the car; you can imagine Spears turning to Azalea afterwards and squealing, "That was really cool, I had so much fun," and meaning it".<ref>{{cite web|title=Britney Spears and Iggy Azalea Go Full 80s in "Pretty Girls" Music Video|date=May 13, 2015 |url=http://www.vanityfair.com/hollywood/2015/05/britney-spears-iggy-azalea-pretty-girls-music-video|publisher=[[Vanity Fair (magazine)|Vanity Fair]]|access-date=June 1, 2015}}</ref> Kirthana Ramisetti for ''[[New York Daily News]]'' similarly noted, "The two music stars seemed to have a lot of fun filming their video".<ref>{{cite web|title=Britney Spears, Iggy Azalea have '80s flashback fun in 'Pretty Girls' music video|url=http://www.nydailynews.com/entertainment/music/britney-spears-iggy-azalea-star-pretty-girls-video-article-1.2220557|work=[[New York Daily News]]|date=May 13, 2015 |access-date=June 1, 2015}}</ref>

Francesca Bacardi commented for [[E! Online News]], "all of the neons, '80s hair and dance moves you could ever ask. In addition to Spears looking phenomenal". While noting product placement, Bacardi added "but who cares because it's ''Britney''".<ref>{{cite web|title=Britney Spears and Iggy Azalea Release Music Video for "Pretty Girls"|date=May 13, 2015 |url=http://au.eonline.com/news/656221/britney-spears-and-iggy-azalea-release-music-video-for-pretty-girls-watch-now|publisher=[[E! Online]]|access-date=June 1, 2015}}</ref> Jason Lipshutz for ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' noted "Spears nods to her classic music video choreography with a few synchronized moves", adding "there's a lot of Britney Spears dancing in this video, which should absolutely thrill her diehard fans".<ref>{{cite web|title=Britney Spears & Iggy Azalea's 'Pretty Girls' Video: Watch The Awe-Inspiring Alien Invasion|url=https://www.billboard.com/music/pop/britney-spears-iggy-azalea-pretty-girls-music-video-6561423/|publisher=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|access-date=June 1, 2015}}</ref> Nolan Feeney for ''[[Time (magazine)|Time]]'' remarked, "Iggy crash-lands in her pool (in a clear nod to the '[[My Prerogative (Britney Spears song)|My Prerogative]]' video)" before concluding "she [Spears] hasn’t looked this happy to be dancing in a video in some time".<ref>{{cite web|title=Watch Britney Spears and Iggy Azalea's Video for 'Pretty Girls'|date=May 13, 2015 |url=https://time.com/3857117/britney-spears-iggy-azalea-pretty-girls-video/|publisher=[[Time (magazine)|Time]]|access-date=June 10, 2015}}</ref> Writing for ''[[Entertainment Weekly]]'', Christopher Rosen opined "Spears and Azalea don their best Valley girl clothes for the video, which also includes a short comedy interlude about Azalea’s 'radical alien powers.' It's not as funny as that part in the '[[Oops!... I Did It Again (song)|Oops!… I Did It Again]]' video when Spears gets the necklace the old lady dropped in the ocean at the end of ''[[Titanic (1997 film)|Titanic]]'', but it'll do".<ref>{{cite web|title=Britney Spears & Iggy Azalea's video for 'Pretty Girls' is totally here (totally)|url=http://www.ew.com/article/2015/05/13/britney-spears-iggy-azalea-pretty-girls-video|publisher=[[Entertainment Weekly]]|access-date=June 1, 2015}}</ref> The music video for "Pretty Girls" was also viewed over 100&nbsp;million times on [[Vevo]], achieving "Vevo Certified" status on July 24, 2015, becoming the fastest video by Spears to reach 100&nbsp;million views on her channel. It won the fan-voted competition of music videos of 2015 by [[Fuse (TV channel)|Fuse]].<ref>{{cite web|title=Britney Spears & Iggy Azalea's 'Pretty Girls' wins Fuse's Top 40, Named No. 1 Video of 2015|url=http://www.fuse.tv/2015/12/top-40-2015-poll-winner-britney-spears-iggy-azalea-pretty-girls|publisher=[[Fuse (TV channel)|Fuse]]|access-date=December 20, 2015}}</ref>

==Live performances==
On April 22, 2015, ''Billboard'' and [[Dick Clark Productions]] confirmed Spears and Azalea would be performing "Pretty Girls" for the first time on television at the [[2015 Billboard Music Awards]] from the [[MGM Grand Garden Arena]] in [[Las Vegas]] on May 17.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/bbma/6538917/britney-spears-and-iggy-azalea-to-perform-pretty-girls-at-2015-billboard|title=Britney Spears and Iggy Azalea To Perform 'Pretty Girls' at 2015 Billboard Music Awards|magazine=Billboard|date=April 22, 2015|access-date=May 3, 2015}}</ref> Azalea described the performance as a kind of a continuation from the music video, with both Spears and Azalea going to Azalea character's planet, along with other creatures.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q3KyynFRlbI|title=MC at BBMAs: Iggy + Britney Beaming to Outer Space|last=Music Choice|date=May 16, 2015|via=YouTube}}</ref> The performance received mostly positive reviews from viewers and critics. ''[[People (magazine)|People]]'' said that Spears and Azalea proved they are more than just "pretty girls". Stating "From the moment they touched down on stage, the duo kept the crowd cheering with a fun, colorful performance."<ref>{{Cite web|url = http://www.people.com/article/britney-spears-iggy-azalea-perform-pretty-girls-billboard-music-awards|title = Britney Spears and Iggy Azalea Perform 'Pretty Girls' for the First Time at Billboard Music Awards|date = May 17, 2015|access-date = May 18, 2015|publisher = People|last = Mcafee|first = Tierney}}</ref> ''[[Entertainment Weekly]]'' also said that "Spears gave one of her most energetic televised performances in years."<ref>{{Cite web|url = http://www.ew.com/article/2015/05/17/britney-spears-iggy-azalea-pretty-girls-billboard-music-awards|title = Britney Spears & Iggy Azalea perform 'Pretty Girls' live for the first time at Billboard Music Awards|date = May 17, 2015|access-date = May 18, 2015|publisher = Entertainment Weekly|last = O'Donnell|first = Kevin|archive-date = May 20, 2015|archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20150520215934/http://www.ew.com/article/2015/05/17/britney-spears-iggy-azalea-pretty-girls-billboard-music-awards|url-status = dead}}</ref> On May 13, Spears confirmed that she would be adding the song to her Las Vegas residency show [[Britney: Piece of Me]].<ref>{{Cite web|url = https://twitter.com/britneyspears/status/598654324764475392|title = Britney Spears on Twitter: "Been so busy today rehearsing..." |date = May 14, 2015|website = twitter.com|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150901143603/https://mobile.twitter.com/britneyspears/status/598654324764475392|url-status = live|archive-date=September 1, 2015}}</ref> The song was officially integrated into the set list on August 5, 2015. It was later replaced by "[[Make Me (Britney Spears song)|Make Me...]]" and "[[Do You Wanna Come Over?]]" on August 17, 2016, following the show's revamp.<ref>{{Cite web|url = http://www.idolator.com/7603579/britney-spears-iggy-azalea-pretty-girls-las-vegas-piece-of-me-residency|archive-url = https://archive.today/20160128073735/http://www.idolator.com/7603579/britney-spears-iggy-azalea-pretty-girls-las-vegas-piece-of-me-residency|url-status = dead|archive-date = January 28, 2016|title = Britney Spears Performs "Pretty Girls" In Las Vegas For The First Time: Watch|date = August 7, 2015|website = idolator.com}}</ref>
