"'''Post to Be'''" is a song by American singer [[Omarion]] featuring fellow American singers [[Chris Brown]] and [[Jhené Aiko]] from the former's fourth studio album, ''[[Sex Playlist]]'' (2014). Each of the artists featured co-wrote the song alongside [[Ty Dolla $ign]] and producer [[DJ Mustard]]. The track was released as the album's second single on November 11, 2014.<ref name="Release">{{cite web |url=https://us.7digital.com/artist/omarion/release/post-to-be-feat-chris-brown-and-jhene-aiko |title=Post To Be (feat. Chris Brown & Jhene Aiko) |publisher=[[7digital]] |location=United States |access-date=February 28, 2015 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150402172933/https://us.7digital.com/artist/omarion/release/post-to-be-feat-chris-brown-and-jhene-aiko |archive-date=April 2, 2015 |url-status=dead}}</ref> Musically, the song is a [[hip hop]] club track that is built over a clap-heavy West Coast production.<ref name="hotnewhiphop.com">{{cite web |url=https://www.hotnewhiphop.com/564590-omarion-post-to-be-feat-chris-brown-jhene-aiko-prod-by-dj-mustard-new-song |title=Omarion - Post To Be Feat. Chris Brown & Jhene Aiko (Prod. By DJ Mustard) |last=Goddard |first=Kevin |publisher=HotNewHipHop |date=November 11, 2014 |access-date=January 27, 2015}}</ref> The song contains a "bouncy" production and samples [[Chaka Demus & Pliers]]' 1993 song "[[Murder She Wrote (song)|Murder She Wrote]]".<ref>{{cite web |url=http://radio.com/2014/11/11/omarion-post-to-be-jhene-aiko-chris-brown-maybach/ |title=Omarion Gets Sultry with Jhene Aiko, Chris Brown for 'Post to Be' |last=Marcus |first=Ezra |work=[[Radio.com]] |publisher=[[CBS Local Media]] |date=November 11, 2014 |access-date=February 23, 2015}}</ref>  The word "post" is a [[sensational spelling]] of the word "supposed".

Commercially, the song peaked at number thirteen on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] and number five on the US ''Billboard'' [[Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs]] chart. The track also made appearances on charts in Canada, Belgium and the UK. The song was later certified platinum by the [[Recording Industry Association of America]] (RIAA).

An accompanying music video was filmed in Los Angeles and was co-directed by Omarion along with Jay Ahn and Taz. The video premiered on February 17, 2015, and depicts the three musicians dancing against an all-white backdrop.

==Background==
Omarion sent the song to Aiko asking if she would make an appearance on the track; Aiko agreed due to her friendship with Omarion and began "playing with the rhyme scheme". While writing with longtime collaborator Micah Powell,  the lyric "Post To Be" reminded Aiko of rapper [[Kevin Gates]],  and she told  Powell; 'Whatever we say, I really want to say something about eating the booty.' He keeps saying in the [[Vine (service)|Vines]]—'You 'posed to eat the booty.'"<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.hiphopdx.com/index/news/id.32472/title.jhene-aiko-says-kevin-gates-inspired-eat-the-booty-lyric-from-post-to-be |title=Jhene Aiko Says Kevin Gates Inspired "Eat The Booty" Lyric From "Post To Be" |last=Harris |first=Christopher |work=[[HipHopDX]] |publisher=Cheri Media Group |date=February 9, 2015 |access-date=February 12, 2015}}</ref> Prior to the release of the song, Omarion released snippets. The song was later leaked on November 11, 2014, by the LA Leakers.<ref name="hotnewhiphop.com"/> The song was released as the album's second single on November 11, 2014, through [[Maybach Music Group]] and [[Atlantic Records]].<ref name="Release"/>

==Composition==
"Post to Be" is composed in the key of [[D-flat major|D{{flat}} major]] with a [[tempo]] of 97 beats per minute.<ref>[https://www.chromatik.com/post-to-be-feat-chris-brown-and-jhene-aiko-by-omarion-for-piano-easy Post To Be Feat. Chris Brown and Jhene Aiko by Omarion Sheet Music] chromatik.com</ref> The song follows a [[chord progression]] of B{{flat}}m–A{{flat}}–D{{flat}}–G{{flat}}.{{citation needed|date=September 2016}}

==Music video==
On January 22, 2015, Omarion revealed via his official [[Instagram]] account that he was filming the song's accompanying video in Los Angeles. Via the Instagram account Omarion posted photos of himself, Brown, and Aiko alongside a white Ferrari and standing onset by an all-white backdrop.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.hotnewhiphop.com/29229-bts-photos-omarion-chris-brown-and-jhene-aiko-s-post-to-be-video-news |title=BTS Photos: Omarion, Chris Brown and Jhene Aiko's "Post To Be" Video |last=Goddard |first=Kevin |publisher=HotNewHipHop |date=January 22, 2015 |access-date=February 12, 2015}}</ref> Later on February 16, Omarion posted two clips of the video on Instagram along with a caption saying "It's coming.... #Posttobevideo", the clips depicting the three singers performing synchronized moves on an all-white set.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.rap-up.com/2015/02/16/video-omarion-chris-brown-jhene-aiko-post-to-be-teaser/ |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150217182320/http://www.rap-up.com/2015/02/16/video-omarion-chris-brown-jhene-aiko-post-to-be-teaser/ |url-status=dead |archive-date=February 17, 2015 |title=Sneak Peek: Omarion feat. Chris Brown & Jhené Aiko - 'Post to Be' |work=[[Rap-Up]] |publisher=Devin Lazerine |date=February 16, 2015 |access-date=February 16, 2015}}</ref>

The video was co-directed by Omarion along with Jay Ahn and Taz,<ref name="thefader.com">{{cite web |url=https://www.thefader.com/2015/02/17/omarion-post-to-be-video |title=Omarion, Jhené Aiko, And Chris Brown Premiere "Post To Be" Video |last=Zeichner |first=Naomi |work=[[The Fader]] |publisher=Andy Cohn |date=February 17, 2015 |access-date=February 17, 2015}}</ref> and was released the following day on February 17. The video featured Omarion moonwalking in front of a white Ferrari, accompanied by Brown dancing.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.rap-up.com/2015/02/17/video-omarion-chris-brown-jhene-aiko-post-to-be/ |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150217224328/http://www.rap-up.com/2015/02/17/video-omarion-chris-brown-jhene-aiko-post-to-be/ |url-status=dead |archive-date=February 17, 2015 |title=Video: Omarion feat. Chris Brown & Jhené Aiko - 'Post to Be' |work=Rap-Up |publisher=Devin Lazerine |date=February 17, 2015 |access-date=February 17, 2015}}</ref>
Naomi Zeichner of ''[[The Fader]]'' praised the video comparing it to a home abs workout stating it "is four pure minutes of perfectly crisp mirror-dancing that doubles as a home abs workout."<ref name="thefader.com" /> As of July 2025, the video has 903 million views.

==Remix==
On May 8, 2015, an official remix of the song was released featuring new verses from [[DeJ Loaf]], [[Trey Songz]], [[Ty Dolla Sign]] and [[Rick Ross]].<ref>{{cite web |last1=Diep |first1=Eric |date=May 8, 2015 |title=Hear Omarion's Star-Studded Remix to 'Post to Be' |url=https://www.bet.com/article/725g30/hear-omarion-s-star-studded-remix-to-post-to-be |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250424010742/https://www.bet.com/article/725g30/hear-omarion-s-star-studded-remix-to-post-to-be |url-status=dead |archive-date=April 24, 2025 |website=BET}}</ref> Verses performed by Omarion, Brown, and Aiko were still present, though Aiko only had one line sampled into the final version.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Lyons |first=Patrick |date=May 8, 2015 |title=Post To Be (Remix) |url=https://www.hotnewhiphop.com/570662-omarion-post-to-be-remix-feat-dej-loaf-trey-songz-ty-dolla-sign-and-rick-ross-prod-by-dj-mustard-new-song |access-date=May 19, 2025 |website=HotNewHipHop |language=en}}</ref>
