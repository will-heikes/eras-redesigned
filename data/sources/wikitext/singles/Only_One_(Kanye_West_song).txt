"'''Only One'''" is a song by the American rapper [[Kanye West]], featuring English musician [[Paul McCartney]]. The song was released on December 31, 2014, and was the first song released by West after the release of his sixth studio album ''[[Yeezus]]''.<ref>{{cite web|last1=Nostro|first1=Lauren|title=Happy New Year: Kanye West Just Dropped "Only One" f/ Paul McCartney|url=http://www.complex.com/music/2014/12/kanye-west-only-one|publisher=Complex|access-date=1 January 2015|archive-date=10 October 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151010215808/http://www.complex.com/music/2014/12/kanye-west-only-one|url-status=live}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|last1=Markman|first1=Rob|title=Kanye West Drops New Song For The New Year: Listen To 'Only One'|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/2038419/kanye-west-only-one-new-year/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150103205002/http://www.mtv.com/news/2038419/kanye-west-only-one-new-year/|url-status=dead|archive-date=January 3, 2015|publisher=MTV|access-date=1 January 2015}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|last1=Nelson|first1=Michael|title=Kanye West – "Only One" (Feat. Paul McCartney)|date=January 2015|url=https://www.stereogum.com/1726996/kanye-west-only-one-feat-paul-mccartney/mp3s/|publisher=Stereogum|access-date=1 January 2015|archive-date=1 January 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150101081900/http://www.stereogum.com/1726996/kanye-west-only-one-feat-paul-mccartney/mp3s/|url-status=live}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|title=Kanye West Season Returns With The Release Of 'Only One' Ft. The song was written by West, McCartney, [[Noah Goldstein]], [[Mike Dean (record producer)|Mike Dean]], and [[Kirby Lauryen]]. Produced by the former four, the song is a tribute to West's daughter, [[North West (musician)|North]], and is sung as a message to West from the perspective of his late mother, [[Donda West]].<ref name="p4k"/> McCartney is featured as a musician, playing [[electric piano]] throughout the track. [[Ty Dolla Sign]] contributes backing vocals at several points in the song.

==Background==
The song was recorded over a two-day period in [[Mexico]] in September 2014 in which West and McCartney, alongside other artists, recorded upwards of nine songs.<ref name="p4k"/>

The track was released on December 31, 2014, to the [[iTunes Store]]. The track features English musician and former member of [[the Beatles]] and [[Wings (band)|Wings]], [[Paul McCartney]]. "Only One" was the first of three McCartney–West collaborations to be released commercially, as the two would later appear on "[[FourFiveSeconds]]" alongside singer [[Rihanna]], released just twenty-four days after "Only One", as well as on West's "[[All Day (Kanye West song)|All Day]]".<ref>{{cite magazine|last=Holmes|first=Charles|url=https://www.rollingstone.com/music/music-news/kanye-west-paul-mccartney-album-722785/|title=Kanye West Offered to Produce a Paul McCartney Album|magazine=[[Rolling Stone]]|date=September 11, 2018|access-date=May 30, 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190605193631/https://www.rollingstone.com/music/music-news/kanye-west-paul-mccartney-album-722785/|archive-date=June 5, 2019|url-status=live}}</ref>

Kanye West is described in a press release as not being able to remember singing the words to "Only One" when he and McCartney were later reviewing the early 2014 sessions that generated the song. Later, West "realized that perhaps the words had never really come from him … he understood in that moment that his late mother, Dr. Donda West, who was also his mentor, confidante, and best friend, had spoken through him that day ... A message had been passed down through generations."<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/kanye-west-releases-only-one-first-single-from-forthcoming-solo-album--first-reveal-of-several-musical-collaborations-with-paul-mccartney-to-come-300014984.html|title=Kanye West Releases "Only One," First Single From Forthcoming Solo Album & First Reveal Of Several Musical Collaborations With Paul McCartney To Come|work=PR Newswire|date=January 1, 2015|access-date=May 25, 2026|archive-date=July 25, 2026|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260725181548/http://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/kanye-west-releases-only-one-first-single-from-forthcoming-solo-album--first-reveal-of-several-musical-collaborations-with-paul-mccartney-to-come-300014984.html|url-status=live}}</ref>

West's then-wife [[Kim Kardashian]] tweeted upon the release of the track that it is her favorite track by West, saying, "People always ask me what my favorite Kanye song is and it's 'Only One'. Kanye feels like his mom sang thru him to our daughter."<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://twitter.com/kimkardashian/status/550558632639606785?lang=en|title=People always ask me what my favorite Kanye song is and it's Only One.|last=West|first=Kim Kardashian|date=2014-12-31|website=@kimkardashian|language=en|access-date=2017-09-13}}</ref>

==Chart performance==
"Only One" debuted at number 35 on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] on the chart dated January 17, 2015. Its debut was mainly on the strength of digital download sales; it sold 125,000 copies during its first week, making it the week's tenth-best selling single. It also marked McCartney's first top 40 hit in the United States in over 25 years, when 1989's "[[My Brave Face]]" had reached number 25.<ref name="billboard_debut" /> Following three weeks on sale in the United States, "Only One" had sold 230,000 total copies.<ref>{{cite magazine |url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/rihanna-kanye-west-paul-mccartney-fourfiveseconds-hot-100/ |title=Rihanna, Kanye West & Paul McCartney Debut on Hot 100 With 'FourFiveSeconds' |last=Trust |first=Gary |magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]] |publisher=[[Prometheus Global Media]] |date=January 28, 2015 |access-date=January 29, 2015 |archive-date=January 19, 2022 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220119200528/https://www.billboard.com/pro/rihanna-kanye-west-paul-mccartney-fourfiveseconds-hot-100/ |url-status=live }}</ref> On 28 July 2015, the song was certified Gold by the [[Recording Industry Association of America]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.riaa.com/goldandplatinumdata.php?artist=%22West%2C+Kanye%22 |title=Gold & Platinum: West, Kanye |last=Peake |first=Kyle |website=[[Recording Industry Association of America]] |date=July 28, 2015 |access-date=August 19, 2015 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151227181058/http://www.riaa.com/goldandplatinumdata.php?artist=%22West,+Kanye%22 |archive-date=December 27, 2015 }}</ref>

The song also debuted at number 35 in the [[UK Singles Chart]] on the chart dated January 10, 2015.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.officialcharts.com/charts/singles-chart/20150104/7501/|title=2015-01-10 Top 40 Official Singles Chart UK Archive - Official Charts|website=[[Official Charts Company|Official Charts]]|access-date=2020-05-06|archive-date=2019-09-20|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190920163533/https://www.officialcharts.com/charts/singles-chart/20150104/7501/|url-status=live}}</ref> On its second week on the chart, the song rose to number 28.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.officialcharts.com/charts/singles-chart/20150111/7501/|title=2015-01-17 Top 40 Official Singles Chart UK Archive - Official Charts|website=[[Official Charts Company|Official Charts]]|access-date=2020-05-06|archive-date=2019-06-07|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190607124921/https://www.officialcharts.com/charts/singles-chart/20150111/7501/|url-status=live}}</ref>

In New Zealand, the song debuted at number 8 on the [[New Zealand Singles Chart]], marking West's first top ten single there since [[Jay-Z]]'s 2009 single "[[Run This Town]]", and first top ten as a lead artist since 2008's "[[Heartless (Kanye West song)|Heartless]]". It also marks [[Paul McCartney]]'s first charting single there since his 1993 single "[[Hope of Deliverance]]" and first top ten single since 1983's "[[Say Say Say]]" with [[Michael Jackson]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://aotearoamusiccharts.co.nz/archive/singles/2015-01-09|title=NZ Top 40 Singles Chart - The Official New Zealand Music Chart}}</ref><ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/artist/paul-mccartney/chart-history/hsi/|title=Paul McCartney Chart History|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|access-date=February 5, 2019|archive-date=November 17, 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20211117165342/https://www.billboard.com/artist/paul-mccartney/chart-history/hsi/|url-status=live}}</ref>
