"'''I Don't Like It, I Love It'''" is a song by American rapper [[Flo Rida]] from his fifth studio album ''[[My House (EP)|My House]]''. The song features American singer [[Robin Thicke]] and [[Earth, Wind & Fire]] bassist [[Verdine White]]. It was released as the album's third [[promotional single]] on March 31, 2015. The song was released as the album's second official single on June 19, 2015, in the Republic of Ireland and on June 21 in the United Kingdom. In the latter country, [[BBC Radio 1]]'s [[Nick Grimshaw]] began playing an unofficial version of the song in which soundbites by [[Audrey Roberts]] (portrayed by [[Sue Nicholls]]) from the long-running British soap opera ''[[Coronation Street]]'' were played over it.<ref>{{cite web | url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7Pmky6nqNZs | title=- YouTube | website=[[YouTube]] }}</ref> The song was heard in the climax of the 2016 comedy film [[Ride Along 2]].

==Music video==
The music video features Flo Rida and Robin Thicke but not Verdine White. The video, directed by [[Director X]], is set on row of [[Brownstone#Use in urban private residences|brownstones]] in [[Jersey City]] during a [[block party]] on a Summer day. In the video, Flo Rida and Thicke casually flirt with women passing by; ice cream is distributed to the neighborhood and children play in the spraying water of an open fire hydrant.<ref>{{cite web|last1=Dinh|first1=James|title=Go Behind-The-Scenes Of Flo Rida's Block Party Themed New Video|url=http://news.iheart.com/articles/trending-104650/go-behindthescenes-of-flo-ridas-block-13696014/|website=iheart.com|access-date=25 August 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151018082001/http://news.iheart.com/articles/trending-104650/go-behindthescenes-of-flo-ridas-block-13696014/|archive-date=18 October 2015|url-status=dead}}</ref><ref name=MusicTimes/> The video ends with Flo-Rida, Thicke and party-goers dancing together in the street into the night.<ref name=MusicTimes>{{cite web|last1=Menyes|first1=Carolyn|title=Flo Rida, Robin Thicke Party on Summery Afternoon in 'I Don't Like It, I Love It' Music Video|url=https://www.youtube.com/articles/41426/20150623/flo-rida-robin-thicke-i-dont-like-it-i-love-it-music-video-watch.htm|website=MusicTimes.com|access-date=29 July 2015}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|title=Video: Flo Rida feat.
