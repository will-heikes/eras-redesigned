"'''It Won't Stop'''" is a song recorded by American singer [[Sevyn Streeter]]. It was released on May 22, 2013, as the second single from her debut [[extended play]] (EP) ''[[Call Me Crazy, But...]]'', by [[Atlantic Records]]. The song later received a remix featuring [[Chris Brown]], had well-received reviews from critics, and achieved a platinum certification from [[Recording Industry Association of America]].

== Reception ==
According to the ''[[Vibe (magazine)|Vibe]]'' magazine, the single "dominated the Mainstream R&B/Hip-Hop Airplay category for nine weeks straight" upon its release.<ref>Diamond Hillyer (2015): [http://www.vibe.com/2015/10/preview-sevyn-streeter-on-the-verge/ Preview: Sevyn Streeter Marries Her Pen Game To Situationships On Debut LP ‘On The Verge’], ''[[Vibe (magazine)|Vibe]]''</ref>

[[NPR]] has described the song as having "sunk into our collective consciousness through commercial radio play and a music video viewed more than 35 million times [as of 2014] and on the recommendation of a growing group of critics and fans", with lyrics that are "vernacular, warm, and unpretentious".<ref>Frannie Kelly (2014) [https://www.npr.org/event/music/338276926/sevyn-streeter-knocks-us-out Sevyn Streeter Knocks Us Out], [[NPR]]</ref>

== Background and composition ==
The song was premiered on May 22, 2013.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.rap-up.com/2013/05/23/new-music-sevyn-streeter-it-wont-stop/ |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131017064235/http://www.rap-up.com/2013/05/23/new-music-sevyn-streeter-it-wont-stop/ |url-status=dead |archive-date=October 17, 2013 |title=New Music: Sevyn Streeter – 'It Won't Stop' |publisher=Rap-Up.com |date=May 23, 2013 |accessdate=October 31, 2013}}</ref> On June 24, 2013, Streeter released an acoustic performance video for the single.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://singersroom.com/content/2013-06-25/Sevyn-Streeter-It-Wont-Stop-Acoustic-Performance/ |title=Sevyn Streeter – It Won't Stop (Acoustic Performance) |publisher=singersroom.com |date=June 25, 2013 |accessdate=October 31, 2013}}</ref> "It Won't Stop" is described as a [[contemporary R&B]] song.<ref>{{cite magazine|last1=Sheffield|first1=Rob|title=Rob Sheffield's Top 25 Songs of 2014 So Far|url=https://www.rollingstone.com/music/news/rob-sheffields-top-25-songs-of-2014-so-far-20140619|magazine=[[Rolling Stone]]|publisher=[[Wenner Media LLC]]|accessdate=October 15, 2016|date=June 19, 2014}}</ref>

Streeter released the remix to the song on August 28, 2013.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://thatgrapejuice.net/2013/08/song-sevyn-streeter-it-stop-ft-chris-brown/|title=New Song: Sevyn Streeter – 'It Won't Stop (ft. Chris Brown)'|date=28 August 2013}}</ref> It features [[Chris Brown]]. Streeter released an [[extended play]] of a collection of remixes featuring Brown on February 18, 2014.<ref>{{cite magazine|last1=Ramirez|first1=Erika|title=Sevyn Streeter, 'It Won't Stop (Remixes)': Exclusive EP Stream|url=https://www.billboard.com/music/rb-hip-hop/sevyn-streeter-it-wont-stop-remixes-exclusive-ep-stream-5908234/|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|publisher=[[Prometheus Global Media]]|access-date=October 15, 2016|date=February 13, 2014}}</ref><ref name="RemixEP">{{cite web|title=It Won't Stop (Remixes) [feat. Chris Brown] – EP by Sevyn Streeter on Apple Music|url=https://music.apple.com/us/album/it-wont-stop-remixes-feat-chris-brown-ep/815487738|website=[[iTunes]]|date=18 February 2014 |publisher=[[Apple Music]]|access-date=October 15, 2016}}</ref>

== Music video ==
On October 10, 2013, Streeter uploaded the music video for the remix on her [[YouTube]] account.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XgFiWGIVP6s|title=Sevyn Streeter – It Won't Stop ft. Chris Brown [Official Video]|publisher=[[YouTube]]|accessdate=September 4, 2016}}</ref> The video for the remix also premiered on [[BET]]'s ''[[106 & Park]]'' and was directed by Brown who also appears in the video along with NBA player [[Dorell Wright]], playing  Sevyn's love interest.<ref>{{cite web|last1=Walker|first1=John|title=Sevyn Streeter + Chris Brown Channel Everyone From Nelly + Kelly Rowland To Aaliyah + Britney Spears In Their Sensual 'It Won't Stop' Video|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/2305662/sevyn-streeter-chris-brown-it-wont-stop-video/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161018211024/http://www.mtv.com/news/2305662/sevyn-streeter-chris-brown-it-wont-stop-video/|url-status=dead|archive-date=October 18, 2016|website=[[MTV News]]|publisher=[[MTV]]|accessdate=October 11, 2013}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://singersroom.com/content/2013-10-09/Behind-the-Video-Sevyn-Streeters-It-Wont-Stop-ft-Chris-Brown/ |title=Behind the Video: It Won't Stop ft.
