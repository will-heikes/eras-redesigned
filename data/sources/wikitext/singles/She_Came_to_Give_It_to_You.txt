"'''She Came to Give It to You'''" (also titled "'''She Came II Give It II U'''") is a song by American singer [[Usher (musician)|Usher]], featuring [[Nicki Minaj]] and uncredited vocals from [[Pharrell Williams]].<ref name="4music">{{cite episode|title=This Week's Fresh Music Top 20|network=[[4Music]]|airdate=1 October 2014}}</ref> It was released on July 8, 2014, through [[RCA Records]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://music.apple.com/us/album/she-came-to-give-it-to-you/897275837|title=iTunes - Music - She Came to Give It to You (feat. Nicki Minaj) - Single by Usher|work=iTunes|date=8 July 2014 |access-date=28 September 2014}}</ref> [[RCA Records]] sent the song to US [[rhythmic contemporary]] and [[contemporary hit radio]] on July 14, 2014.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://gfa.radioandrecords.com/publishGFA/GFANextPage.asp?sDate=07/14/2014&Format=9 |title= ®R&R :: Going for Adds™ :: Rhythmic|website=gfa.radioandrecords.com |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140811010940/http://gfa.radioandrecords.com/publishGFA/GFANextPage.asp?sDate=07%2F14%2F2014&Format=9 |archive-date=2014-08-11}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=http://gfa.radioandrecords.com/publishGFA/GFANextPage.asp?sDate=07/14/2014&Format=1 |title= ®R&R :: Going for Adds™ :: CHR/Top 40|website=gfa.radioandrecords.com |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140811005750/http://gfa.radioandrecords.com/publishGFA/GFANextPage.asp?sDate=07%2F14%2F2014&Format=1 |archive-date=2014-08-11}}</ref> Originally intended to be the second single from his eighth [[studio album]] ''[[Hard II Love]]'', the song was scrapped from the standard edition track listing, with the track only being included on the Japanese edition of the album.

Minaj first mentioned the video in an interview with [[MTV]], and was licensed to [[General Mills]] for its use in a new [[Honey Nut Cheerios]] commercial featuring Usher himself, debuting on August 25, 2014.{{citation needed|date=September 2014}} The song quoted part of the lyrical melody of "[[Just Be Good to Me]]", a 1983 single by [[The S.O.S. Band]], written and produced by [[Jimmy Jam & Terry Lewis]] and "[[Got to Give It Up]]" from [[Marvin Gaye]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.gigwise.com/newmusic/92433/usher-teams-up-with-pharrell-nicki-minaj-for-new-single|title=Usher teams up with Pharrell, Nicki Minaj for new single|work=GIGWISE.com|access-date=28 September 2014}}</ref>

==Music video==
On August 26, 2014, a music video for the track was released.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/music/rb-hip-hop/usher-nicki-minaj-she-came-to-give-it-to-you-video-6229406/ |title=Watch Usher & Nicki Minaj's Underground Disco in 'She Came to Give It to You' Video |magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]] |date=2014-08-26 |access-date=2014-08-29}}</ref> Influenced by the rhythm beats of Haitian Rara bass horn. The music video on [[YouTube]] has received over 10 million views as of May 2024.<ref>[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cQY00k3wK1Y Usher - She Came II Give It II U ft. Nicki Minaj].[[YouTube]]</ref>

==Live performances==
On August 24, 2014, Usher performed the song with Nicki Minaj at [[2014 MTV Video Music Awards]].<ref>{{cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/events/vma/6229190/usher-nicki-minaj-VMA-she-came-to-give-it-to-you-video-2014 |title=Usher Performs 'She Came to Give It to You' at the MTV VMAs With Nicki Minaj |magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]] |date=2014-08-24 |access-date=2014-08-25}}</ref> On September 5, 2014, he performed the song live on ''[[Today (U.S. TV program)|Today]]'' as the closing act of its Summer Concert Series. On September 9, 2014, Usher performed the song live at the 2014 [[Fashion Rocks]]. On September 19, 2014, Usher performed the song as part of his headlining act at the 2014 [[iHeartRadio Music Festival]]. He also performed the song live as a part of his [[UR Experience Tour]].On October 13, 2014, YouTube personality [[Bethany Mota]] and her switch-up partner [[Mark Ballas]] performed a [[Hip hop music|hip hop]] routine to the song on ''[[Dancing with the Stars (U.S. TV series)|Dancing with the Stars]]''. They received a composite score of 32.
