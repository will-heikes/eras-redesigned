"'''Uma Thurman'''" is a song by American [[Rock music|rock]] band [[Fall Out Boy]], released digitally on January 12, 2015. The song prominently features sampled theme music from the television series ''[[The Munsters]]'' (1964–66) and lyrics celebrating the actress [[Uma Thurman]], famous for films such as  ''[[Pulp Fiction]]'' and ''[[Kill Bill]]''.

The song was the fifth<ref>[http://www.newsforshoppers.com/fall-out-boy-mixes-uma-thurman-the-munsters-on-new-release/36734731/ Fall Out Boy Mixes Uma Thurman & The Munsters On New Release] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160304033634/http://www.newsforshoppers.com/fall-out-boy-mixes-uma-thurman-the-munsters-on-new-release/36734731/ |date=March 4, 2016 }} newsforshoppers.com. Retrieved January 15, 2015.</ref> digital track released in build up to the band's 2015 album, ''[[American Beauty/American Psycho]]''. The song was released to [[modern rock]] radio as the album's third single (second in the U.S.) in early February.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.allaccess.com/alternative/future-releases |title=Alternative > Future Releases |publisher=All Access Music Group |access-date=January 26, 2015 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150121121522/http://www.allaccess.com/alternative/future-releases |url-status=live |archive-date=January 21, 2015}}</ref> On March 31, 2015, "Uma Thurman" was released as the album's second U.S. [[Contemporary hit radio|Top 40]] single.<ref>[http://headlineplanet.com/home/2015/03/02/uma-thurman-set-as-fall-out-boys-new-pop-single/ "UMA THURMAN" SET AS FALL OUT BOY'S NEW POP SINGLE; IMPACTS ON 3/31]. headlineplanet.com. Retrieved March 19, 2015.</ref> The remix entitled "The Boys of Zummer Remix" was released in promotion of the Boys of Zummer tour featuring American rapper and tourmate [[Wiz Khalifa]].

The song debuted at No. 1 on the U.S. iTunes chart on its digital release,<ref>[http://falloutboy.com/post/107896371762/1-song-8-song-4-album-uma-thurman-up-now  #1 song #8 song #4 album] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150113140151/http://falloutboy.com/post/107896371762/1-song-8-song-4-album-uma-thurman-up-now |date=January 13, 2015 }} falloutboy.com. Retrieved January 13, 2015.</ref> reached No. 22 on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] and was certified 2× Platinum by the [[RIAA]] in December 2015. The music video won the [[2015 MTV Video Music Awards|2015 MTV Video Music Award for Best Rock Video]]. Fall Out Boy performed "Uma Thurman" in a television ad for [[Pepsi]] &mdash; which premiered during the [[57th Grammy Awards]]. Fall Out Boy has also performed the song on TV shows including ''[[Late Night with Seth Meyers]]''<ref>[http://www.broadwayworld.com/bwwtv/article/VIDEO-Fall-Out-Boy-Performs-Uma-Thurman-on-LATE-NIGHT-20150609 VIDEO: Fall Out Boy Performs 'Uma Thurman' on LATE NIGHT. broadwayworld.com. Retrieved June 10, 2015.]</ref> and at the 2015 [[Much Music Video Awards]].<ref>[http://www.business-standard.com/article/pti-stories/ed-sheeran-the-weeknd-win-big-at-much-music-video-awards-115062200138_1.html Ed Sheeran, The Weeknd win big at Much Music Video Awards]. ''Business Standard''. Retrieved June 22, 2015.</ref>

==Composition and response from Uma Thurman==
[[File:Uma Thurman Cannes 2011.jpg|thumb|200px|[[Uma Thurman]], the inspiration for the song]]
The song prominently samples the theme music from ''[[The Munsters]]'', taking an electric guitar riff and baritone sax line.<ref name="Billboard Uma">[http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/6436532/fall-out-boy-uma-thurman-american-beauty-american-psycho New Fall Out Boy Song 'Uma Thurman' Is Best Yet From New Album] ''Billboard''. Retrieved January 15, 2014.</ref> Its title is a reference to American actress [[Uma Thurman]]'s character [[Mia Wallace]] dancing with [[John Travolta]]'s character Vincent Vega in an iconic scene of the film ''[[Pulp Fiction]]''.<ref name="Billboard Uma"/><ref name="MTV Uma">[https://web.archive.org/web/20150115062430/http://www.mtv.com/news/2046574/fall-out-boy-uma-thurman/ "Fall Out Boy's 'Uma Thurman' Samples 'The Munsters' Theme Song, And It's Rad"] [[MTV]]. Retrieved January 15, 2015.</ref> Thurman, a fan of the band, gave permission for them to use her name. "I'm so flattered, it's so nice," Thurman said. "It's incredibly chivalrous; no one ever asks permission for anything anymore, you don't even expect it."<ref>[https://www.rollingstone.com/music/features/fall-out-boy-on-the-road-14-things-we-learned-20150109 14 Things We Learned on the Road With Fall Out Boy] ''Rolling Stone''. Retrieved January 15, 2015</ref><ref>[https://twitter.com/petewentz/status/545106473052033024 Pete Wentz tweet on Uma Thurman] https://twitter.com/petewentz. Retrieved January 15, 2015.</ref> The track incorporates "jittery piano chords"<ref name="MTV Uma"/> and "clapping drums", along with bursts of verbiage like "I can move mountains, I can work a miracle".<ref>{{cite web |url-status=dead |url=http://kroq.cbslocal.com/2015/01/12/fall-out-boy-share-pulp-fiction-nodding-uma-thurman-listen/ |title=Fall Out Boy Share 'Pulp Fiction'-Nodding 'Uma Thurman': Listen |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150115105202/http://kroq.cbslocal.com/2015/01/12/fall-out-boy-share-pulp-fiction-nodding-uma-thurman-listen/ |archive-date=January 15, 2015 |website= [[KROQ]] |access-date=January 15, 2015 |date=January 12, 2015 |first1=Marissa G. |last1=Muller }}</ref> Bassist and lyricist [[Pete Wentz]] has stated:

<blockquote>This was a fun song to write. originally, when we came up with the idea, and there was this sample in it, which is a sample from The Munsters TV show, people kept saying "oh cool, like [[Quentin Tarantino]], cool" when we played it. We decided why don't we kind of create this world around that? To me, Uma Thurman and [[Winona Ryder]], they were these women in pop culture who were quirky, but that made me only crush on them harder. and rather than going with the traditional Uma Thurman role, we thought a lot about [[Kill Bill]] and who [[The Bride (Kill Bill)|her character]] was in that, and this kind of resilience and this violence, but there's something that's authentic about it (like a woman taking revenge or being empowered). so that's what the chorus of the song's about, and the verses are what you would do to try and capture this woman's affection.<ref>{{cite web |url-status=dead |url=http://falloutboy.com/post/107865716042/i-do-believe-marcellus-wallace-my-husband-your |website=Fall Out Boy |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160926062345/http://falloutboy.com/post/107865716042/i-do-believe-marcellus-wallace-my-husband-your |archive-date=September 26, 2016 |access-date=January 15, 2015 |title="I do believe Marcellus Wallace, my husband, your boss, told you to take me out do whatever I wanted. Now I wanna dance, I wanna win. I want that trophy, so dance good." pulp fiction |date=January 12, 2015 }}</ref></blockquote>

The song is in [[E minor]], at a fast tempo of 144–152 beats per minute.<ref>"[http://www.musicnotes.com/sheetmusic/mtd.asp?ppn=MN0147043 Fall Out Boy "Uma Thurman" Sheet Music in E Minor (transposable) - Download & Print - SKU: MN0147043]", musicnotes.com</ref> The song has been described by critics as being [[pop rock]]<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.xxlmag.com/rap-music/new-music/2015/01/fall-boy-featuring-wiz-khalifa-uma-thurman-remix/ |title=Fall Out Boy Featuring Wiz Khalifa "Uma Thurman (Remix)" |website=[[XXL (magazine)|XXL]] |access-date= March 7, 2015 |first1=Paul |last1=Thompson |date=January 23, 2015 }}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.complex.com/music/2015/01/fall-out-boy-uma-thurman-f-wiz-khalifa |title=Listen to Fall Out Boy's "Uma Thurman (Remix)" f/ Wiz Khalifa |website=[[Complex (magazine)|Complex]] |access-date=March 7, 2015 |first1= Zach |last1=Frydenlund |date=Jan 23, 2015 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150402123318/http://www.complex.com/music/2015/01/fall-out-boy-uma-thurman-f-wiz-khalifa |archive-date= 2015-04-02}}</ref> and [[surf rock]].<ref name="Idolator"/>

==Critical reception==
The song has received critical acclaim, with praise for the sample of the theme music from ''[[The Munsters]]'' and [[Patrick Stump]]'s vocal performance. ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' called it the "best yet" from the new album.<ref name="Billboard Uma"/>'' Music Times'' wrote positively about the song, stating that the rhythm and bassline are "what really shine" and that the "steady and funky bassline carries the melody seamlessly through the track."<ref name="MusicTimes Uma">[http://www.musictimes.com/articles/24187/20150112/fall-out-boy-uma-thurman-review-the-munsters-american-beauty-american-psycho-listen.htm Fall Out Boy "Uma Thurman" Review: New Song from 'American Beauty/American Psycho' Gets Funky with Help from 'The Munsters'] ''Music Times''. Retrieved January 15, 2015.</ref> It also felt that vocalist Stump's vocal melody played off the music well.<ref name="MusicTimes Uma"/> [[Idolator (website)|Idolator]] wrote that ''The Munsters'' sample added a 1960s surf-rock vibe to the "impossibly catchy" song.<ref name="Idolator">{{cite web |url=http://www.idolator.com/7576582/fall-out-boy-uma-thurman-the-munsters-theme-listen |title=Fall Out Boy's New Song "Uma Thurman" Blends Handclaps, The 'Munsters' Theme & An Undeniable Chorus: Listen |website=[[Idolator (website)|Idolator]] |access-date=January 15, 2015 |first1= Robbie |last1=Daw |date=Jan 12, 2015 |url-status=dead |archive-url= https://archive.today/20150118165211/http://www.idolator.com/7576582/fall-out-boy-uma-thurman-the-munsters-theme-listen |archive-date= 18 January 2015 }}</ref>

==Commercial performance==
As a promotional digital release, "Uma Thurman" debuted at No. 73 on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] and No. 6 on the [[List of number-one Billboard Rock Songs|Rock Songs]] chart on the strength of 46,000 first week downloads as reported by ''Billboard''.<ref>[https://www.billboard.com/pro/fall-out-boy-uma-thurman-rock-chart/ Fall Out Boy's 'Uma Thurman' Dances Onto Hot Rock Songs Chart] ''Billboard''. Retrieved January 23, 2015.</ref> It became the band's ninth top 15 Hot Rock Songs charting in the US since the chart began in 2009.<ref>[http://www.billboard.com/artist/276170/Fall+Out+Boy/chart?f=902 Fall Out Boy Chart History - Rock Songs] ''Billboard''. Retrieved January 23, 2015.</ref> It peaked at No. 22 as an official single (and ninth top 30 single), with a top 10 [[Pop Songs]] and top 30 [[Alternative Songs]] position. "Uma Thurman" charted in the UK reaching No. 71 and in Canada, reaching No. 67.<ref name="UK">[http://www.officialcharts.com/artist/_/fall%20out%20boy/ Official Charts Company - FALL OUT BOY] [[Official Charts Company]]. Retrieved January 23, 2015.</ref> On August 10, 2015, "Uma Thurman" was certified Platinum by the [[RIAA]]. As of September 2015, the song has sold 1,040,000 downloads in the United States.<ref name="US sales">{{cite magazine |url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/fall-out-boy-first-radio-songs-top-10-uma-thurman/ |title=Fall Out Boy Notches First Radio Songs Top 10 With 'Uma Thurman' |last=Caulfield |first=Keith |magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]] |date=September 16, 2015 |access-date=September 18, 2015}}</ref> It also became Fall Out Boy's first top 10 [[Radio Songs (chart)|Radio Songs]] hit. As of November 2023, the song has been certified 3× Platinum by the [[RIAA]].

==Music video==
The video premiered on [[VEVO]] on April 17, 2015. Its premise is contest winner "Sarah" (played by Sarah Murphree<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.odtseries.com/blog/2016/6/20/women-in-film-spotlight-sarah-murphree |title=Women in Film Spotlight: Sarah Murphree |publisher=Or Die Trying |date=June 20, 2016 |access-date=May 3, 2020}}</ref>) spending the day doing chores for the separate band members as well as pranking them by joyriding and speeding in Trohman's [[McLaren Automotive|McLaren]] given the keys by Wentz. Sarah mostly spends the day with Wentz doing some shopping, wearing bandanas in a gang-esque stickup, riding a dune buggy, playing paintball, flying in a windtunnel and crushing a pickup truck with a [[M1 Abrams]] tank with "UMA" painted twice.  The truck has printed on the door "Article 1 Section 36.03".  This likely refers to  Alabama's Supreme Court blocking same-sex marriage.<ref>{{cite web|last1=Tardio|first1=Andres|title=Fall Out Boy Uses A Tank To Fight For Same-Sex Marriage In Their New Video|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/2136886/fall-out-boy-uma-thurman-alabama-same-sex-marriage-ban/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150420171745/http://www.mtv.com/news/2136886/fall-out-boy-uma-thurman-alabama-same-sex-marriage-ban/|url-status=dead|archive-date=April 20, 2015|publisher=MTV|access-date=February 1, 2017}}</ref> Sarah also does karaoke with Stump and does a workout with Hurley.  The video, like "[[Irresistible (Fall Out Boy song)|Irresistible]]", references Stump's hands being chopped off once again. The video includes cameos by [[Big Sean]], [[Action Bronson]], [[Brendon Urie]], [[Big Data (band)|Big Data]] and [[Max Schneider|MAX]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2VTxYQL2SbA |title=Fall Out Boy - Uma Thurman |via=YouTube |date=April 17, 2015 |access-date=May 3, 2020}}</ref>
