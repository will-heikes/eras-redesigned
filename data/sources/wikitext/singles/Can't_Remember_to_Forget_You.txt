"'''Can't Remember to Forget You'''" is a song by Colombian singer [[Shakira]] featuring Barbadian singer [[Rihanna]] from the former's eponymous tenth studio album, ''[[Shakira (album)|Shakira]]''. It was written by Shakira, [[John Hill (record producer)|John Hill]], [[Kid Harpoon|Tom "Kid Harpoon" Hull]], Daniel Alexander, [[Erik Hassle]], and Rihanna, while production was handled by the former three.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.rcarecords.com/news/shakira-unveils-brand-new-single-%E2%80%9Ccan%E2%80%99t-remember-forget-you%E2%80%9D-featuring-rihanna |title=Shakira Unveils Brand New Single "Can'T Remember To Forget You" Featuring Rihanna &#124; The Official Rca Records Site |publisher=Rcarecords.com |date=13 January 2014 |access-date=1 February 2014 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140116085541/http://www.rcarecords.com/news/shakira-unveils-brand-new-single-%E2%80%9Ccan%E2%80%99t-remember-forget-you%E2%80%9D-featuring-rihanna |archive-date=16 January 2014 |url-status=dead |df=dmy-all }}</ref> The song was released as the lead single of the album on 13 January 2014 through [[RCA Records]]. A solo Spanish-language version of the song, entitled "'''Nunca Me Acuerdo de Olvidarte'''", was released on 24 January 2014 via Sony Music Latin.

The song is an [[uptempo]] track that fuses [[reggae]], [[Rock music|rock]], and [[New wave music|new wave]] [[Pop music|pop]].<ref name="theguardian1" /> Lyrically the song revolves around forgetting someone who is bad even though you love the person. Upon its release, "Can't Remember to Forget You" was met with mixed reviews from critics; while critics praised the [[ska]] influences, they felt that the song overall was not memorable. Other critics noted similarities between the song and the music of [[the Police]] and [[Bruno Mars]].

Commercially, the single fared very well, reaching number one in [[Greece]] and [[Lebanon]] and the top ten in twenty-seven additional countries. In the US, the song debuted on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] at twenty-eight and later went on to peak at fifteen on the chart, becoming Shakira's highest-debuting track in the region until "[[Shakira: Bzrp Music Sessions, Vol. 53]]" in 2023. The track is certified Double Diamond in Brazil, five times Platinum in Norway, and Gold or Platinum in nine additional countries. The music video, which was shot in a [[Los Angeles]] mansion, was directed by [[Joseph Kahn (director)|Joseph Kahn]] and was premiered and uploaded to [[YouTube]] on 30 January 2014. The accompanying video featured both Shakira and Rihanna in revealing outfits, dancing, smoking cigars, and a "naked embrace". The video has more than one billion views on YouTube. The song was performed live for the first time on [[iHeartRadio]].

==Background==
[[File:Rihanna_Singing_Unfaithful.jpg|150px|thumbnail|The song features guest vocals from [[Barbadian people|Barbadian]] singer [[Rihanna]].]]
In October 2010, Shakira released her ninth studio and Spanish-language album, ''[[Sale el Sol]]''. The record marked a return to her musical roots after her experimentation with [[electronic music]] and was primarily a [[Latin pop]] album with influences of [[Rock music|rock]] and [[merengue music]].<ref name=saleelsolcbsbayarea>{{cite web|title=Shakira Returns To Her Roots On 'Sale el Sol'|url=http://sanfrancisco.cbslocal.com/2010/10/18/shakira-returns-to-her-roots-on-sale-el-sol/|publisher=[[KPIX-TV]]. [[CBS Corporation]]|access-date=13 January 2014|date=18 October 2010}}</ref><ref name=developmentprnewswire>{{cite web|title=Shakira Sale El Sol / The Sun Comes Out Today, October 19th|url=http://www.prnewswire.com/news-/shakira--sale-el-sol--the-sun-comes-out-today-october-19th-105259393.html|publisher=[[PR Newswire]]. [[UBM plc]]|access-date=13 January 2014|date=19 October 2010}}</ref> It garnered [[Music critics|critical]] acclaim<ref name=metacriticscore>{{cite web|title=Sale el Sol - Shakira|url=http://www.metacritic.com/music/sale-el-sol/shakira|publisher=[[Metacritic]]. [[CBS Interactive]]|access-date=13 January 2014}}</ref> and was a commercial success internationally.<ref name=shakibioallmusic>{{cite web|last=Huey|first=Steve|title=Shakira - Biography|url=http://www.allmusic.com/artist/shakira-mn0000790797/biography|publisher=[[Allmusic]]. [[Rovi Corporation]]|access-date=13 January 2014}}</ref> A year later in November, Shakira revealed in an interview with ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' magazine that she had begun writing new material and working with various producers for a new record.<ref name=shakiinterviewbillboard2011>{{cite magazine|last=Cobo|first=Leila|title=Shakira Needs to Record: 'My Body Is Asking for It'|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/465307/shakira-needs-to-record-my-body-is-asking-for-it|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|access-date=13 January 2014|date=4 November 2011}}</ref> Shakira began planning to make new music in 2011: work continued into 2012, at which time she left her previous label [[Epic Records]] and joined [[RCA Records]]. Initially in 2012, it was expected that the first single from the singer's album would be a song titled "Truth or Dare".<ref name=truthordareheraldo>{{cite web|title=Escuche 30 segundos de la nueva canción de Shakira que está grabando en Lisboa|url=http://www.elheraldo.co/tendencias/escuche-30-segundos-de-la-nueva-cancion-de-shakira-que-esta-grabando-en-lisboa-73085|work=[[El Heraldo (Colombia)|El Heraldo]]|publisher=Editorial El Heraldo S.A.|access-date=13 January 2014|date=30 June 2012}}</ref> In June, Colombian newspaper ''[[El Heraldo (Colombia)|El Heraldo]]'' reported that Shakira had been filming the music video for the song in [[Lisbon]], Portugal, and speculated that it would be in late 2012.<ref name=truthordareheraldo /> "Truth or Dare", however, was not released.

On 6 December 2013, a [[Twitter]] page named @ShakiraSpace announced the duet between Shakira and Rihanna. Then, on 8 December 2013, Shakira's [[body double]] Alexita Ortiz wrote on social networking site Twitter that she had been working with her on the set for a music video, and mentioned that Rihanna was also involved.<ref name=firstrumortweet>{{cite web|last=Wass|first=Mike|title=The Rumored Shakira/Rihanna Duet Might Actually Happen|url=http://www.idolator.com/7497339/shakira-rihanna-duet|archive-url=https://archive.today/20140113172147/http://www.idolator.com/7497339/shakira-rihanna-duet|url-status=dead|archive-date=13 January 2014|publisher=[[Idolator (website)|Idolator]]. [[Spin Media]]|access-date=13 January 2014|date=9 December 2013}}</ref> The tweet was subsequently removed.<ref name=firstrumortweet /> The speculation regarding the collaboration of the two singers increased after [[Cuban Americans|Cuban-American]] rapper [[Pitbull (rapper)|Pitbull]], in a radio interview on ''[[Kidd Kraddick in the Morning]]'', commented that Rihanna wasn't able to appear as the featured artist in his song "[[Timber (Pitbull song)|Timber]]" as she had "something she had to do with Shakira."<ref name=pitbullcommentidolator>{{cite web|last=Wass|first=Mike|title=Pitbull Reveals "Timber" Was Intended For Rihanna, Inadvertently Confirms RiRi/Shakira Duet|url=http://www.idolator.com/7497939/pitbull-reveals-timber-was-intended-for-rihanna-inadvertently-confirms-ririshakira-duet|archive-url=https://archive.today/20140111140735/http://www.idolator.com/7497939/pitbull-reveals-timber-was-intended-for-rihanna-inadvertently-confirms-ririshakira-duet|url-status=dead|archive-date=11 January 2014|publisher=Idolator. [[Hearst Magazines UK]]|access-date=13 January 2014|date=7 January 2014}}</ref> "Nunca Me Acuerdo de Olvidarte", the Spanish version, was  on 21 January 2014, which does not feature Rihanna.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/music/music-news/shakira-spanish-version-nunca-me-acuerdo-de-olvidarte-listen-audio-5877089/|title=Shakira Unveils Spanish Version of New Single: Listen to 'Nunca Me Acuerdo de Olvidarte'|last=Mendizabal|first= Amaya|date=21 January 2014|access-date=23 January 2014|magazine=Billboard}}</ref> A [[CD single|physical single]] in [[Germany]] is set to be out on 14 February 2014.<ref name="CD single">{{Cite web|url=https://www.amazon.de/Remember-Forget-Shakira-Feat-Rihanna/dp/B00HWJ2GAE|title=Can't Remember to Forget You: Amazon.de: Musik|author=Shakira|author-link=Shakira|date=14 February 2014|website=[[Amazon (company)|amazon.de]]|language=de|access-date=5 August 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140130053934/http://www.amazon.de/Remember-Forget-Shakira-Feat-Rihanna/dp/B00HWJ2GAE|archive-date=January 30, 2014}}</ref>

==Critical reception==
The song received mixed reviews from critics.<ref name="idolator1">{{cite web|author=Sam Lansky |url=http://www.idolator.com/7501118/shakira-rihanna-cant-remember-to-forget-you-review |archive-url=https://archive.today/20140113184530/http://www.idolator.com/7501118/shakira-rihanna-cant-remember-to-forget-you-review |url-status=dead |archive-date=13 January 2014 |title=Shakira & Rihanna's "Can't Remember To Forget You": Review Revue &#124; Music News, Reviews, and Gossip on |date=13 January 2014 |publisher=Idolator.com |access-date=13 January 2014}}</ref> [[MTV]] praised the song and complimented its "[[Latin music|Latin]] and [[Caribbean music|Island]] flourishes", continuing to compare the song to "[[Locked Out of Heaven]]" by [[Bruno Mars]] and Shakira's previous collaboration with [[Beyoncé]] "[[Beautiful Liar]]".<ref name=developmentconceptionmtv /> ''The Guardian'' found its melodies catchy, and praised the song for keeping away from [[electronic dance music]], noting Shakira's return to "guitar-tinged new wave pop".<ref name="theguardian1"/> [[Vibe (magazine)|''Vibe'']] praised the duet calling it a "powerhouse collaboration", concluding, "The [[She Wolf (Shakira song)|she-wolf]] and [[Bajan people|Bajan]] bombshell's chemistry seeps through the bass line and their accented vocals, sure to make both stanbases happy."<ref name="vibe">{{cite web|url=http://www.vibe.com/article/new-music-shakira-and-rihanna-cant-remember-forget-you|title=New Music: Shakira Feat. Rihanna 'Can't Remember To Forget You'|date=13 January 2014|access-date=14 January 2014|work=[[Vibe (magazine)|VIBE]]}}</ref> Jason Lipshutz of ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' noted the song as being "enjoyably grandiose", praising the "ska-[[Punk music|punk]] guitar [[riff]]" and its "chunky rock arrangement."<ref name="billboard">{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/music/music-news/shakira-rihanna-team-up-on-cant-remember-to-forget-you-listen-5869585/|title=Shakira, Rihanna Team Up On 'Can't Remember To Forget You': Listen|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|last=Lipshutz|first=Jason|date=13 January 2014|access-date=14 January 2014}}</ref>'[[Popjustice]]Son gave the song a positive review saying, "You cannot go wrong with a bit of Shakira. You cannot go wrong with a bit of Rihanna. Two facts for you there ladies and gents."<ref name="idolator1"/>

Bradley Stern of ''MuuMuse'' felt the song was not consequential, despite praising the song as something different from contemporary radio.<ref name="muumuse">{{cite web|url=http://www.muumuse.com/2014/01/shakira-cant-remember-to-forget-you-feat-rihanna.html/|title=SHAKIRA, "CAN'T REMEMBER TO FORGET YOU (FEAT. ==Commercial performance==
On its first day of  the song had 1,172 spins on 143 stations, equating to an audience of 11 million.<ref>{{cite web|author=Mike Wass |url=http://www.idolator.com/7501403/shakira-and-rihannas-cant-remember-to-forget-you-explodes-at-radio#more-7501403 |archive-url=https://archive.today/20140115114234/http://www.idolator.com/7501403/shakira-and-rihannas-cant-remember-to-forget-you-explodes-at-radio%23more-7501403 |url-status=dead |archive-date=15 January 2014 |title=Shakira And Rihanna's "Can't Remember To Forget You" Explodes At Radio &#124; Music News, Reviews, and Gossip on |date=14 January 2014 |publisher=Idolator.com |access-date=1 February 2014}}</ref> "Can't Remember To Forget You" debuted at number 28 on the ''Billboard'' Hot 100 chart and number 25 on Latin Pop Songs chart. The Spanish version debuted at number 11 on the Hot Latin Songs chart.<ref>{{cite magazine |url=http://www.billboard.com/artist/278867/shakira/chart |title=Shakira - Chart history |magazine=Billboard |access-date=1 February 2014 |archive-date=24 March 2016 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160324102454/http://www.billboard.com/artist/278867/shakira/chart |url-status=dead }}</ref> With the song debuting at number 28 in on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] it became Shakira's highest-debuting track in the United States.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/5877859/pitbull-kehas-timber-still-tops-hot-100-bastille-lorde-reach-top-10|title=Pitbull & Ke$ha's 'Timber' Still Tops Hot 100; Bastille, Lorde Reach Top 10|author=Gary Trust|magazine=Billboard|date=22 January 2014|access-date=25 January 2014}}</ref> It also became Shakira 10th song to hit the top 40 in the US. Its highest ''Billboard'' Hot 100 chart position so far is at number fifteen. As of March 2014, the song has sold 376,000 digital copies in the US.<ref name="US sales March 7">{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/ask-billboard-shakiras-biggest-hot-100-hits/|title=Ask Billboard: Shakira's Biggest Hot 100 Hits|last=Trust|first=Gary|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|date=7 March 2014|access-date=7 March 2014}}</ref>

The song made its chart debut in Ireland where it debuted at number seventeen on the [[Irish Singles Chart]].<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.chart-track.co.uk/index.jsp?c=p%2Fmusicvideo%2Fmusic%2Farchive%2Findex_test.jsp&ct=240001&arch=t&lyr=2014&year=2014&week=03 |title=GFK Chart-Track |publisher=Chart-track.co.uk |access-date=1 February 2014 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140203043552/http://www.chart-track.co.uk/index.jsp?c=p%2Fmusicvideo%2Fmusic%2Farchive%2Findex_test.jsp&ct=240001&arch=t&lyr=2014&year=2014&week=03 |archive-date=3 February 2014 |url-status=dead }}</ref> In the UK the single debuted at number eleven on the [[UK Singles Chart]], giving Shakira her eleventh UK top forty and Rihanna her thirty-ninth top forty.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.officialcharts.com/charts/singles-chart/20140119/7501/ |title=2014-01-25 Top 40 Official Singles Chart UK Archive |publisher=[[Official Charts Company]] |date=25 January 2014 |access-date=1 February 2014}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/pharrells-happy-on-course-to-hit-half-a-million-uk-sales-2733/ |title=Pharrell Williams' Happy on course to hit half-a-million UK sales |publisher=Officialcharts.com |date=15 January 2014 |access-date=1 February 2014}}</ref> In France, the song entered the singles chart at number six and peaked at number 5 three week later, becoming Shakira's eleventh top ten and Rihanna's seventeenth.<ref name="France"/> The single debuted at number 20 in Germany, and climbed to number nine a few weeks later then peaked at number eight.<ref name="Germany"/> Elsewhere, in Europe, the song was commercially successful, reaching the top 10 in more than 20 countries.
In Latin American, the single fared pretty well reaching the top 10 in many territories.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.viva.tv/charts/16-viva-top-100 |title=VIVA TOP 100 - Alle Musikvideos - Chart |publisher=VIVA.tv |access-date=1 February 2014 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140125034010/http://www.viva.tv/charts/16-viva-top-100 |archive-date=25 January 2014 |url-status=dead |df=dmy-all }}</ref> The song went number one in Lebanon, Greece Digital Songs, Mexican Airplay, Polish Dance Top 50, and US Dance Club Songs charts.<ref name="Greece">{{cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/biz/charts/2014-02-22/greece|title=Greece Digital Songs: Feb 22, 2014|access-date=22 February 2014|magazine=Billboard|archive-date=28 November 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151128071005/http://www.billboard.com/biz/charts/2014-02-22/greece|url-status=dead}}</ref><ref name="Lebanon">{{cite web|url=http://www.olt20.com/Archive#&panel1-3|title=The official lebanese Top 20 - Archive|website=The official lebanese Top 20}}</ref><ref name="Mexican">{{cite magazine |url={{BillboardURLbyName|artist=shakira|chart=mex/}} |title=Shakira - Chart history |magazine=Billboard |access-date=5 March 2014}}</ref><ref name="Dance Top 50"/><ref name="US Dance Club Songs"/> The song peaked at 85 on ''Billboard'' Hot 100 year end chart. It was Shakira's only song in the 2010s to do so.<ref name="US Billboard Hot 100">{{Cite magazine|year=2014|title=Hot 100 Songs : Page 1 {{!}} Billboard|url=https://www.billboard.com/charts/year-end/2014/hot-100-songs/|url-status=live|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141210021449/https://www.billboard.com/charts/year-end/2014/hot-100-songs/|archive-date=10 December 2014|access-date=5 August 2022}}</ref> The Spanish version of the song ended up being number one in Venezuela.<ref name="moreversions">{{cite web|url=http://www.recordreport.com.ve/noticias/2014/2014-12-15.asp|title=Resumen Anual 2014|work=Record Report|language=es|date=16 December 2014|access-date=18 December 2014}}</ref>
