"'''23'''" is a song by American record producer [[Mike Will Made It]], featuring American singer [[Miley Cyrus]] and American rappers [[Wiz Khalifa]] and [[Juicy J]], released as the former's debut single on September 10, 2013, by [[Interscope Records]]. Written by the artists alongside [[R. City]] and fellow producer Pierre "P-Nasty" Slaughter, it is set to a midtempo beat and lyrically references [[Air Jordan]] sneakers and [[recreational drug use]].

"23" received generally mixed reviews from [[Music journalism|music critics]], who were ambivalent towards its production and Cyrus' rapping ability. It peaked at number 11 on the U.S. [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]], and performed moderately on international charts. An accompanying music video was filmed in [[Brooklyn]] on August 26, 2013, and premiered through [[Vevo]] on September 24, 2013. Cyrus performed the song during the first and second leg of her [[Bangerz Tour]].

During the production of "23", Mike Will Made It prepared a verse for himself to rap, which he originally planned to include alongside verses by Juicy J and Wiz Khalifa. However, after the latter two finished their respective recordings, Mike Will Made It decided that his contribution should be replaced by a woman. Mike Will Made It had recently finished producing Miley Cyrus' track "[[We Can't Stop]]" for her fourth studio album ''[[Bangerz]]'' (2013) when she first heard "23" and expressed interest in being included on the track. Mike Will Made It was initially hesitant of her suggestion, having met her earlier that day, although he "left her on there" after "she laid down the verse [and] killed it."<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/1711958/miley-cyrus-mike-will-made-it-23/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160809033419/http://www.mtv.com/news/1711958/miley-cyrus-mike-will-made-it-23/|url-status=dead|archive-date=August 9, 2016|title=Miley Cyrus Put 'Fresh' Twist On '23,' Says Mike Will Made It|author=Jocelyn Vena|publisher=[[MTV News]]. [[Viacom (2005–present)|Viacom]]|date=August 7, 2013|access-date=December 29, 2013}}</ref>

"23" is a synth-driven hip hop song set to a [[Tempo|midtempo]] beat and a "bass-heavy"<ref>{{cite web|url=http://m.axs.com/news/miley-cyrus-raps-with-wiz-khalifa-and-juicy-j-on-mike-will-made-it-s-2-26609|title=Miley Cyrus raps with Wiz Khalifa and Juicy J on Mike Will Made It's '23'|author=Lucas Villa|work=AXS|access-date=1 November 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151222144957/http://m.axs.com/news/miley-cyrus-raps-with-wiz-khalifa-and-juicy-j-on-mike-will-made-it-s-2-26609|archive-date=22 December 2015|url-status=dead}}</ref> arrangement. It is distinguished in Cyrus' catalog as the first track in which she raps.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/music/rb-hip-hop/miley-cyrus-tests-her-rapping-skills-on-mike-will-made-its-23-5687336/|title=Miley Cyrus Tests Her Rapping Skills On Mike Will Made It's '23': Listen|author=Chris Payne|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|date=September 10, 2013|access-date=December 30, 2013}}</ref> In the chorus, Cyrus mentions recreational drug use, particularly in the line "I'm in the club high off purp with some shades on".<ref name="SpinReview">{{cite web|url=https://www.spin.com/2013/09/miley-cyrus-drug-rap-23-mike-will-made-it-juicy-j-wiz-khalifa/|title=Miley Cyrus Raps Explicitly About Drugs on Mike Will Made It's Middling '23'|author=Jordan Sargent|work=[[Spin (magazine)|Spin]]|date=September 10, 2013|access-date=December 29, 2013}}</ref> The track later transitions into the repeated hook "J's on my feet" delivered by Juicy J, which references Air Jordan sneakers.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://theboombox.com/mike-will-made-it-23-feat-miley-cyrus-wiz-khalifa-juicy-j/|title=Mike WiLL Made It – '23′ (Feat. Miley Cyrus, Wiz Khalifa, & Juicy J)|author=Dharmic X|publisher=The Boom Box|date=September 10, 2013|access-date=December 30, 2013}}</ref>

== Critical reception ==
In a favorable review, Mike Wass of [[Idolator (website)|Idolator]] was complimentary of Mike Will Made It's "slick, multi-layered production" and was indifferent towards Cyrus' contributions.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.idolator.com/7483404/miley-cyrus-makes-her-rap-debut-on-mike-will-made-its-23-listen-to-the-full-song|archive-url=https://archive.today/20131229221634/http://www.idolator.com/7483404/miley-cyrus-makes-her-rap-debut-on-mike-will-made-its-23-listen-to-the-full-song|url-status=dead|archive-date=December 29, 2013|title=Miley Cyrus Makes Her Rap Debut On Mike WiLL Made It's "23": Listen To The Full Song|author=Mike Wass|publisher=[[Idolator (website)|Idolator]]. Buzz Media|date=September 9, 2013|access-date=December 29, 2013}}</ref> Sharing a similar sentiment, a writer for That Grape Juice stated that the artists "[gave] fans just what they want", adding that they "[went] along to make the jam the smash that it is."<ref>{{cite web|url=http://thatgrapejuice.net/2013/09/song-mike-23-ft-miley-cyrus-juicy-wiz-khalifa/#ixzz2p4MKycAE|title=New Song: Mike Will Made It – '23 (Ft Miley Cyrus, Juicy J & Wiz Khalifa)'|publisher=That Grape Juice|date=September 10, 2013|access-date=December 31, 2013}}</ref> ''[[Complex (magazine)|Complex]]'' ranked "23" at number 23 on their list of the 50 best songs of 2013, saying that Mike Will Made It knew that Miley would make this collaboration even better.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.complex.com/music/2013/12/best-songs-of-2013/mike-will-made-it-miley-cyrus-juicy-j-wiz-khalifa |title=The Best 50 Songs of 2013 |author=Ahmed, Insanul |publisher=Complex Music |date=2013-12-02 |access-date=2013-02-16|display-authors=etal}}</ref>
However, in a more mixed review, Danielle Cheesman of [[MSN]] opined that "the song sounds like everything else Mike WiLL's touched and turned to gold so it will be a smash but, much to Miley's chagrin, it won't be because of her", and criticized Cyrus' verses for "[stealing [[2 Chainz]]'s] whole flow."<ref>{{cite web|url=http://social.entertainment.msn.com/music/blogs/post--miley-makes-her-rapping-debut-on-mike-will-made-its-23|title=Miley Makes Her Rapping Debut on Mike WiLL Made It's "23"|author=Danielle Cheesman|publisher=[[MSN]]. [[Microsoft]]|date=September 10, 2013|access-date=December 31, 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140101103820/http://social.entertainment.msn.com/music/blogs/post--miley-makes-her-rapping-debut-on-mike-will-made-its-23|archive-date=2014-01-01|url-status=dead}}</ref> Tony Maglio from The Wrap also questioned her lyrical content, specifically criticizing the lyrics "I back it up, cause I don’t give a fuck" and "I’m [[MC Hammer]] fly."<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.thewrap.com/miley-cyrus-rap-song-touts-drug-use-michael-jordan-mc-hammer-audio/|title=Miley Cyrus Rap Song Touts Drug Use, Michael Jordan, MC Hammer (Audio)|author=Tony Maglio|publisher=The Wrap|date=September 10, 2013|access-date=December 31, 2013}}</ref> Jordan Sargent from ''[[Spin (magazine)|Spin]]'' called the track "far from Mike Will's best work" and negatively compared Cyrus' delivery to that of [[Lil Debbie]].<ref name="SpinReview"/>

==Commercial performance==
In the United States, "23" entered the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] at number 70,<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/charts/hot-100?page=6 |title=The Hot 100 |magazine=Billboard  |date=September 28, 2013 |access-date=December 30, 2013 |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130922133930/http://www.billboard.com/charts/hot-100?page=6 |archive-date=September 22, 2013 }}</ref> and later peaked at number 11.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/artist/5747924/mike-will-made-it/chart|title=Mike Will Made-It - Chart history|magazine=Billboard|access-date=December 30, 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140319075607/http://www.billboard.com/artist/5747924/mike-will-made-it/chart|archive-date=March 19, 2014|url-status=dead}}</ref> It additionally peaked at number 2 on the ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' [[Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs]] component chart.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/artist/305353/Juicy+J/chart?f=367|title=Juicy J - Chart history|magazine=Billboard|access-date=December 31, 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151024121505/http://www.billboard.com/artist/305353/Juicy+J/chart?f=367|archive-date=October 24, 2015|url-status=dead}}</ref>

Elsewhere in North America, the track reached number 26 on the [[Canadian Hot 100]].<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/artist/305353/Juicy+J/chart?f=793|title=Juicy J - Chart history|magazine=Billboard|access-date=December 31, 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151024121738/http://www.billboard.com/artist/305353/Juicy+J/chart?f=793|archive-date=October 24, 2015|url-status=dead}}</ref> In Europe, "23" respectively peaked at numbers 19 and 85 on the [[UK Singles Chart]] and the [[UK R&B Chart]], both of which are organized by the [[Official Charts Company]].<ref name="UKSinglesChart">{{cite web|url=https://www.officialcharts.com/charts/singles-chart/20131020/7501/|title=2013-10-26 Top 40 Official Singles Chart UK Archive|publisher=[[Official Charts Company]]|date=October 26, 2013|access-date=December 31, 2013}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.officialcharts.com/charts/r-and-b-singles-chart/20131013/114/|title=2013-10-19 Top 40 R&B Singles Archive|publisher=Official Charts Company|date=October 19, 2013|access-date=December 31, 2013}}</ref> The track respectively peaked at numbers 27 and 1 on the Ultratip component charts in Flanders and Wallonia, representing the fifty songs that failed to reach the flagship [[Ultratop]] chart in each Belgian region.<ref name="Charts">{{cite web|url=http://www.ultratop.be/fr/song/123d75/Mike-Will-Made-It-feat.-Miley-Cyrus,-Wiz-Khalifa-&-Juicy-J-23|title=Mike Will Made It feat. Miley Cyrus, Wiz Khalifa, & Juicy J|publisher=[[Ultratop]]. Hung Medien|access-date=December 31, 2013}}</ref> It also peaked at number 30 on the French [[Syndicat National de l'Édition Phonographique]], and number 44 on the Spanish [[Productores de Música de España]].<ref name="Charts"/> In Oceania, "23" reached number 39 on the Australian [[ARIA Charts]] and number 22 on the [[Recorded Music NZ|Official New Zealand Music Chart]].<ref name="Charts"/>

==Music video==
An accompanying music video for "23" was filmed at [[Bishop Ford Central Catholic High School]] in [[Brooklyn]] on August 26, 2013;<ref name="CastingCall">{{cite web|url=http://www.complex.com/music/2013/08/mike-will-made-it-miley-cyrus-juicy-j-wiz-khalifa-23-video-shoot-casting|title=Here's a Casting Call For Mike WiLL Made It's "23" Video Shoot Possibly Featuring Miley Cyrus, Wiz Khalifa, and Juicy J|author=Dharmic X|work=Complex|publisher=Complex Media|date=August 25, 2013|access-date=December 30, 2013}}</ref> it was directed by [[Hannah Lux Davis]] and styled by [[Haley Wollens]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.rap-up.com/2013/09/20/mike-will-made-it-goes-back-to-high-school-with-miley-cyrus-in-23-video/|title=Mike Will Made-It Goes Back to High School with Miley Cyrus in '23' Video|work=[[Rap-Up]]|date=September 20, 2013|access-date=December 30, 2013|archive-date=October 30, 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131030192820/http://www.rap-up.com/2013/09/20/mike-will-made-it-goes-back-to-high-school-with-miley-cyrus-in-23-video/|url-status=dead}}</ref><ref>{{cite magazine |last=Keltner de Valle |first=Jane |title=Fashion at Work: Haley Wollens |magazine=[[Teen Vogue]] |date=May 2014 |volume=14 |issue=4 |page=66}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |last=Freeman |first=Nathan |title=Getting Mileage from Miley Cyrus |newspaper=[[The New York Times]] |date=October 10, 2013 |page=E6 |url=https://www.nytimes.com/2013/10/10/fashion/up-next-haley-wollens-stylist-for-pop-stars.html}}</ref> An advertisement for a casting call posted to Juicy J's [[Instagram]] account requested "edgy, rebellious, sexy, [and] hood types" to appear as extras in the clip, and further stated that individuals "must wear [their] favorite pair of Jordans".<ref name="CastingCall"/>

The music video was premiered through [[Vevo]] on September 24.<ref name="DigitalSpyVideo">{{cite web|url=http://www.digitalspy.com/music/news/a518198/miley-cyrus-resurrects-vmas-foam-finger-tongue-in-23-video-watch.html|title=Miley Cyrus resurrects VMAs foam finger, tongue in '23' video - watch|author=Tom Eames|publisher=[[Digital Spy]]. [[Hearst Corporation]]|date=September 24, 2013|access-date=December 30, 2013}}</ref> The music video takes place in a high school; it begins with the school principal leaving his office, which Mike Will Made It enters and begins playing "23" on a [[Beats Electronics|Beats Pill]] over the intercom. Interspersed throughout are scenes of Cyrus wearing a bikini similar to [[Michael Jordan]]'s [[Chicago Bulls]] basketball jersey; she is frequently shown smoking in the bathroom and writing on its mirrors with red lipstick. Khalifa raps his verse in a chemistry lab, while Juicy J's verse is rapped in a trophy room.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bbEoRnaOIbs|title=23 (Explicit)|publisher=[[YouTube]]. [[Google]]|date=September 24, 2013|access-date=December 30, 2013}}</ref> A school-wide [[pep rally]] later takes place, where Cyrus is gyrating against Mike Will Made It and notably wearing the [[foam hand|foam finger]] used in her controversial performance at the [[2013 MTV Video Music Awards]] in August.<ref name="DigitalSpyVideo"/> Cash Money rappers [[Birdman (rapper)|Birdman]] and [[Mack Maine]] make cameos.

Critics were divided in their opinions for the music video. ==Live performances==
Cyrus performed the song during the first and second legs of her [[Bangerz Tour]]. During the third leg of the tour, "23" was replaced with [[The Beatles]]' "[[Lucy in the Sky with Diamonds]]". During the performance, Cyrus wore black-and-red Jordan 1 sneakers, a striped fishnet bikini and crotchless leather chaps with "Miley" scrawled down the side, through which she paid homage to [[Christina Aguilera]]'s "[[Dirrty]]" music video.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://style.mtv.com/2014/02/18/miley-cyrus-bangerz-tour-performance-outfits/|title=Miley Cyrus Kicks Off Bangerz Tour With Flurry Of Performance Outfits|author=Wilson, Gaby|date=February 18, 2014|publisher=[[MTV]]|access-date=August 8, 2014|archive-date=August 20, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140820091733/http://style.mtv.com/2014/02/18/miley-cyrus-bangerz-tour-performance-outfits/|url-status=dead}}</ref> Aguilera herself approved of the outfit and named Cyrus her 'dirrty girl' successor, writing, "Cheers from one dirrty girl to the next @MileyCyrus...wear em' loud & proud, girl- yes!!" on her Twitter account.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.mirror.co.uk/3am/celebrity-news/christina-aguilera-crowns-miley-cyrus-3176757|title=Christina Aguilera crowns Miley Cyrus as her dirrty girl successor|author=Greenwood, Carl|date=February 23, 2014|newspaper=[[Daily Mirror]]|access-date=August 8, 2014}}</ref> Jane Stevenson of the ''[[Toronto Sun]]'' praised the performance and wrote that it saw Cyrus finally twerk it out with her dancers.<ref>{{cite news|url=http://www.torontosun.com/2014/03/31/miley-cyrus-bangerz-tour-arrives-in-toronto|title=Miley Cyrus brings Bangerz fun to Toronto|author=Stevenson, Jane|date=March 31, 2014|newspaper=[[Toronto Sun]]|access-date=August 8, 2014}}</ref>
