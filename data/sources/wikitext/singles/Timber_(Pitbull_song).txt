"'''Timber'''" is a song by American rapper [[Pitbull (rapper)|Pitbull]] featuring American singer [[Kesha]]. The song was released on October 7, 2013, as the [[lead single]] from Pitbull's [[extended play]] (EP) ''[[Meltdown (EP)|Meltdown]]''. The song was produced by [[Dr. Luke]], [[Cirkut (record producer)|Cirkut]], and [[Sermstyle]], with additional production by Nick Seeley.<ref>{{cite web|first=Steffen |last=Hung |url=http://www.australian-charts.com/showitem.asp?interpret=Pitbull+feat.+Ke%24ha&titel=Timber&cat=s |title=Pitbull feat. Ke$ha&nbsp;— Timber |publisher=australian-charts.com |access-date=December 2, 2013}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://ryan.kiisfm.com/pages/ryansrecap.html?feed=152739&article=11715578|title=Ryan Seacrest|work=KIIS FM|access-date=August 16, 2015|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131121094919/http://ryan.kiisfm.com/pages/ryansrecap.html?feed=152739&article=11715578|archive-date=November 21, 2013}}</ref> The song interpolates [[Lee Oskar]]'s 1978 single "San Francisco Bay" and features harmonica player Paul Harrington, who plays through the entire song and was told to emulate Oskar.<ref>{{Citation|title=Famous Uncle Paul Harrington| date=February 19, 2014 |url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N_EXYVhNYrY|language=en|access-date=August 10, 2019}}</ref>

The song was the 90th-most popular song of the decade according to [[Billboard (magazine)|''Billboard'']]. It peaked at number one on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] for three consecutive weeks. It also topped the [[music charts|charts]] in over fifteen additional countries. According to the IFPI, the song sold 9.6 million units worldwide in 2014, including single-track downloads and track-equivalent streams, becoming the sixth best-selling song of that year.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.bbc.com/news/newsbeat-32321455|title=Frozen soundtrack was the biggest-selling album of 2014|work=[[BBC News]]|date=April 15, 2015|access-date=March 1, 2020}}</ref> It is currently [[Music recording certification|certified]] fifteen-times Platinum in Norway, twelve-times Platinum in the US, diamond in Germany, seven-times Platinum in Australia and Canada, and Gold or higher in fourteen additional countries.

==Background and composition==
Kesha had previously featured Pitbull on [[remix]]es of her songs "[[Tik Tok (song)|Tik Tok]]" and "[[Crazy Kids]]", and Pitbull featured Kesha on a 2009 song, "Girls". In an interview in December 2013, Pitbull said that [[Rihanna]] was originally meant to be the featured artist on "Timber", but she had already been asked to be the featured artist on [[Shakira]]'s "[[Can't Remember to Forget You]]" and did not have time to record "Timber".<ref>{{YouTube|eCoMybXx_4s|EXCLUSIVE Pitpull backstage iHeartRadio Jingle Ball 2013 interview}}</ref>

"Timber" is a [[dance-pop]], [[electronic dance music|EDM]] and [[folktronica]] song with elements of [[Country music|country]].<ref>{{cite news|url=http://top40.about.com/od/singles/fl/Pitbull-Timber-featuring-Keha.htm|title=Pitbull – "Timber" featuring Ke$ha|access-date=January 20, 2014|last=Lamb|first=Bill|publisher=[[IAC/InterActiveCorp]]|work=[[About.com]]|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131113065232/http://top40.about.com/od/singles/fl/Pitbull-Timber-featuring-Keha.htm|archive-date=November 13, 2013}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.vulture.com/2013/10/hear-pitbulls-new-single-a-hoedown-with-keha.html|title=Hear Pitbull's New Single: A Hoedown, With Ke$ha – Vulture|work=Vulture|date=October 7, 2013 |access-date=November 19, 2015}}</ref><ref>{{Cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/articles/news/5800854/lorde-royals-hot-100-chart-nine-weeks-eminem|magazine=Billboard|title=Lorde's 'Royals' Rules Hot 100 For Ninth Week|first=Gary|last=Trust|date=November 27, 2013|access-date=November 27, 2013}}</ref> According to the [[sheet music]] published at Musicnotes.com by [[Kobalt Music Group|Kobalt Music Publishing]], the song is set in [[common time]] with a moderately fast tempo of 130 [[beats per minute]]. The song is written in the key of [[G-sharp minor|G♯ minor]] and follows a [[Chord progression|chord sequence]] of [[G♯ (musical note)|G♯]]m–[[B (musical note)|B]]–[[F♯ (musical note)|F♯]]–[[E (musical note)|E]]. Kesha's [[Vocal range|vocals span]] from G♯<sub>3</sub> to D♯<sub>5</sub>.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.musicnotes.com/sheetmusic/mtd.asp?ppn=MN0126812&ref=google|title=Musicnotes.com: Unsupported Browser or Operating System|work=musicnotes.com|date=November 5, 2013 }}</ref>

Paul Harrington, who plays the harmonica throughout the track, was initially given a flat fee of $1,000 for his services. Three years later, however, after consulting an expert on music law, Harrington was paid a further $50,000.<ref>{{Citation|title=Paul Harrington gets his due for Pitbull's "Timber" thanks to Eric Zukoski| date=August 11, 2016 |url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Vr5uVi_Wo2Q|language=en|access-date=August 10, 2019}}</ref>

==Commercial performance==
"Timber" made its first chart appearance on October 10, 2013, in the Republic of Ireland, where it debuted at number 67 on the [[Irish Singles Chart]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.chart-track.co.uk/index.jsp?c=p/musicvideo/music/archive/index_test.jsp&ct=240001&arch=t&lyr=2013&year=2013&week=41 |title=GFK Chart-Track – TOP 100 SINGLES, WEEK ENDING 10 October 2013 |publisher=Chart-track.co.uk |access-date=May 22, 2019 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170821045017/http://www.chart-track.co.uk/index.jsp?c=p%2Fmusicvideo%2Fmusic%2Farchive%2Findex_test.jsp&ct=240001&arch=t&lyr=2013&year=2013&week=41 |archive-date=August 21, 2017 |url-status=dead }}</ref> The following week, the song climbed to number 19. In Austria, "Timber" debuted at number 69 on the [[Ö3 Austria Top 40|Austrian Singles Chart]] on October 18, 2013, and eventually crowned the chart at number one. In Germany, the song debuted at number 90 on October 18, 2013, on the [[GfK Entertainment charts|German Singles Chart]] and later peaked at number one.

Timber debuted at number 49 on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] on October 26, 2013. On January 18, 2014, the single reached number one on the chart, giving Kesha her third number-one single and her tenth top ten hit, as well as earning Pitbull his second number-one single and ninth top ten hit.<ref>{{cite news|last=Trust|first=Gary|title=Pitbull, Ke$ha Take 'Timber' to Top of Hot 100|url=https://www.billboard.com/articles/news/5862620/pitbull-kesha-take-timber-to-top-of-hot-100|access-date=May 15, 2014|newspaper=Billboard|date=January 8, 2014}}</ref> Before topping the Hot 100, it had been stuck at number two for four weeks, behind "[[The Monster (song)|The Monster]]" by [[Eminem]] featuring [[Rihanna]]. It also peaked at number one on the US [[Digital Songs]] chart, selling 442,000 copies in its peak week, constituting the fourth largest single week sum of 2013.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Chart Watch: "Radioactive" Sets A Rock Record |url=http://yahoo.com/entertainment/blogs/music-news/chart-watch-radioactive-sets-rock-record-001439867.html |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20211027132559/https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/blogs/music-news/chart-watch-radioactive-sets-rock-record-001439867.html |archive-date=October 27, 2021 |access-date=January 6, 2025 |website=yahoo.com |language=en-US}}</ref> It is also Pitbull's first song to do so. "Timber" also peaked at number one on the US [[Hot Rap Songs]] chart. It was the first number one single of 2014 in the US,<ref>{{cite news |url=https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/blogs/music-news/chart-watch-pitbull-kesha-eminem-riri-230734884.html|title=Chart Watch: Pitbull & Kesha & Eminem & RiRi |first=Paul|last=Grein |date=January 9, 2014|work=[[Yahoo!]]|access-date=May 22, 2019}}</ref> and stayed at number one for three consecutive weeks. In May 2014, the single reached its 4 million sales mark.<ref>{{cite web|last=Grein|first=Paul|title=Chart Watch: Iggy Azalea's Amazing Feat|url=https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/blogs/chart-watch/chart-watch-iggy-azalea-amazing-feat-215813622.html|work=Yahoo!|date=May 15, 2014|access-date=May 22, 2019}}</ref> As of March 2016, the single has sold 4.7 million copies in the US.<ref name="2016update">{{cite magazine | url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/ask-billboard-rihannas-record-streak-of-seven-studio-albums-with/ | title=Ask Billboard: Rihanna's Record Streak of Seven Studio Albums With Hot 100 No. 1s | magazine=Billboard | date=March 6, 2016 | access-date=March 6, 2016 | author=Trust, Gary}}</ref> As of 2025, the song has since been certified [[RIAA certification|12× Platinum]] by the [[Recording Industry Association of America]] (RIAA) for combined sales and streaming equivalent units of 12 million in the United States.<ref name=RIAA/>

In Canada, the song debuted at number seventeen on the [[Canadian Hot 100]] as the week's highest debut and climbed steadily to number one, where it stood for eight consecutive weeks.

In the United Kingdom, "Timber" debuted at the top of the [[UK Singles Chart]] on January 5, 2014 ― for the week ending date January 11, 2014 ― shifting 139,000 copies in its first week.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/timber-pitbull-and-keha-topple-pharrell-from-number-1-2714/|first=Daniel|last=Lane|title=Timber! Pitbull and Ke$ha topple Pharrell from Number 1|publisher=[[Official Charts Company]]|date=January 5, 2014|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140106040430/http://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/timber-pitbull-and-keha-topple-pharrell-from-number-1-2714/|archive-date=January 6, 2014|access-date=May 21, 2019}}</ref> This gave both artists their third chart-topping songs in Britain. By the end of 2014, the song had sold 744,000 copies in the UK.

In South Korea, "Timber" debuted at number 16 on [[Gaon charts|Gaon]] International Chart, but it debuted at number 13 on Gaon Download International Chart with 6,522 downloads.<ref name=skcert>{{cite web|url=http://gaonchart.co.kr/digital_chart/download.php?nationGbn=E&current_week=43&current_year=2013&chart_Time=week|title=Gaon Download Chart|publisher=Gaonchart.co.kr|access-date=May 21, 2019|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131103073434/http://gaonchart.co.kr/digital_chart/download.php?nationGbn=E&current_week=43&current_year=2013&chart_Time=week|archive-date=November 3, 2013|language=ko}}</ref> In its second week it reached number two on the International Chart. ==Controversies==
On June 25, 2014, it was reported that songwriters [[Lee Oskar]], Keri Oskar, and [[Greg Errico]] had filed a [[copyright infringement]] lawsuit against the makers of "Timber", which features a harmonica melody that Oskar claims is "identical" to the melody used in his 1978 song "San Francisco Bay". The songwriters were seeking $3&nbsp;million [[United States dollars|USD]] in damages.

The lawsuit alleged that while Sony Music may have obtained permission to use the sample, which is credited in the album notes for ''Meltdown'', from a license holder, the label failed to obtain permission from the songwriters themselves.<ref name="lawsuit">{{cite web|url=https://www.independent.co.uk/arts-entertainment/music/news/pitbull-and-keha-in-multimillion-dollar-lawsuit-over-identical-harmonica-riff-from-the-70s-9562792.html |archive-url=https://ghostarchive.org/archive/20220507/https://www.independent.co.uk/arts-entertainment/music/news/pitbull-and-keha-in-multimillion-dollar-lawsuit-over-identical-harmonica-riff-from-the-70s-9562792.html |archive-date=May 7, 2022 |url-access=subscription |url-status=live|title=Pitbull and Ke$ha in multi-million dollar Timber lawsuit over 'identical' harmonica riff from the 70s|work=The Independent|date=June 25, 2014 }}{{cbignore}}</ref>
