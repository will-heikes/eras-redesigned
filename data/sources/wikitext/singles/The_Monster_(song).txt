"'''The Monster'''" is a song by American rapper [[Eminem]] from his album ''[[The Marshall Mathers LP 2]]'' (2013) featuring a guest appearance from Barbadian singer [[Rihanna]]. The song was written by [[Bebe Rexha]], [[Eminem]], [[Rihanna]], [[Aalias]], [[Jon Bellion]], Maki Athanasiou, and [[Frequency (record producer)|Frequency]], with the latter also handling production. Released on October 29, 2013, as the fourth single from the album, "The Monster" marks the fourth collaboration between Eminem and Rihanna, following "[[Love the Way You Lie]]" (2010), its sequel "[[Love the Way You Lie (Part II)]]" (2010), and "[[Numb (Rihanna song)|Numb]]" (2012), and is a hip-hop and pop song, with lyrics that describe Eminem pondering the negative effects of his [[Celebrity|fame]] while Rihanna comes to grips with her inner demons.

Upon release, the song was met with positive reviews from [[music critics]], who compared the song to "Love the Way You Lie". "The Monster" has successfully charted worldwide, topping the charts in twelve countries including Australia, Canada, France, Ireland, New Zealand, Russia, Switzerland, the United Kingdom and the United States' [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]]. It also marks Eminem's first number one on the US [[Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs]] chart and has reached the top ten in Austria, Belgium, Italy and Spain.

==Background and production==
[[File:Bebe Rexha by rufusowliebat.jpg|thumbnail|upright|left|The song, "Monster Under My Bed," written by [[Bebe Rexha]], was originally intended to be used on her own debut album. The chorus was later used for "The Monster"]]

In 2010, Eminem and Rihanna released their first official collaboration titled "[[Love the Way You Lie]]". The song received both critical and commercial success and topped the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] chart for seven consecutive weeks.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/951892/rihanna-and-eminems-love-the-way-you-lie-part-ii-sequel-leaks |first=Monica |last=Herrera |title=Rihanna and Eminem's 'Love the Way You Lie (Part II)' Sequel Leaks |magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]] |date=November 3, 2010 |access-date=February 1, 2013 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130705063941/http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/951892/rihanna-and-eminems-love-the-way-you-lie-part-ii-sequel-leaks |archive-date=July 5, 2013 |url-status=live }}</ref> By the end of the year, 854,000&nbsp;copies of the song were sold in the UK, making "Love the Way You Lie" the [[List of best-selling singles by year in the United Kingdom|country's biggest-selling song of 2010]].<ref name="official charts end of 2010">{{cite web|title=Eminem/Rihanna & Take That scoop 2010 crowns |url=http://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/cardle-at-no-1-again-eminemrihanna-take-that-scoop-2010-crowns/ |publisher=[[Official Charts Company]] |access-date=January 29, 2011 |date=January 2, 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20111026060230/http://www.theofficialcharts.com/chart-news/cardle-at-no-1-again-eminemrihanna-take-that-scoop-2010-crowns/ |archive-date=October 26, 2011 }}</ref> The same year, a sequel to the single, titled "[[Love the Way You Lie (Part II)]]" was released as part of Rihanna's fifth studio album ''[[Loud (Rihanna album)|Loud]]''; it mainly views matters from the female protagonist perspective.<ref name="latimesmag">{{cite news|first=Gerrick D. |last=Kennedy |title=Hooked |url=http://www.latimesmagazine.com/2011/06/hooked.html |access-date=December 4, 2011 |work=[[Los Angeles Times]] |date=June 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130704171751/http://www.latimesmagazine.com/2011/06/hooked.html |archive-date=July 4, 2013 |url-status=live }}</ref> In November 2012, Eminem and Rihanna collaborated again on "[[Numb (Rihanna song)|Numb]]", which was included on Rihanna's seventh album ''[[Unapologetic]]''.<ref name="Notes">{{cite AV media notes|others=Rihanna|title=[[Unapologetic]]|year=2012|type= inlay cover|publisher= [[Def Jam Recordings]], SRP|page=19}}</ref>

In November 2012, singer Bebe Rexha was at Stadium Red studio in [[Harlem|Harlem, New York]] working on material to be included in her debut album. During the sessions, Rexha stated that she "was in a really dark place, a dark head space. Figuring out where you are in life and trying to make shit work for yourself—it gets to you. You get super down on yourself. I was just trying to get out of a depressed phase." At that moment, she wrote the hook for "The Monster"; in the moment she wrote it she knew that it was going to be a song performed by Eminem.<ref name="beberexhawritting">{{cite web|url=http://www.vibe.com/article/singer-bebe-rexha-talks-co-writing-eminem-and-rihannas-mmlp2-collaboration-monster|title=Singer Bebe Rexha Talks Co-Writing Eminem And Rihanna's 'MMLP2' Collaboration 'The Monster'|work=[[Vibe (magazine)|Vibe]]|last=Kennedy|first=John|date=October 24, 2013|access-date=October 29, 2013|archive-date=October 2, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151002182327/http://www.vibe.com/article/singer-bebe-rexha-talks-co-writing-eminem-and-rihannas-mmlp2-collaboration-monster|url-status=live}}</ref> The singer further stated that she got the inspiration from the quote "about the monsters that are around us and that live inside of us"; additionally, Rexha was disappointed at being dropped from the [[Island Def Jam]] record label.<ref name="beberexhawritting"/>

The Senior Director of A&R for [[Shady Records]], [[Riggs Morales]] started looking for potential songs to be included on Eminem's eighth studio album. When the producer of "The Monster", [[Frequency (record producer)|Frequency]], played the song for Morales he "freaked out" and asked for the verses to be stripped and [[ProTools]] sessions to be sent to Eminem.<ref name="beberexhawritting"/> When Eminem heard the demo he then added his own verses and adjusted the instrumental portion(s) of the track. However, he left the background vocals sung by Rexha intact. On September 11, 2013, Rihanna wrote on her [[Twitter]] account that she had recorded a "#monster" hook for one of her favorite artists.<ref name="beberexhawritting"/> On October 22, Eminem revealed the track listing for his eighth studio album, ''[[The Marshall Mathers LP 2]]'', including "The Monster" on which Rihanna was credited as a featured artist.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.hotnewhiphop.com/16398-eminem-reveals-mmlpii-cover-art-news |title=Eminem Reveals "MMLP2" Cover Art [Update: Deluxe Edition Tracklist Revealed&#93; |date=October 22, 2013 |publisher=Hotnewhiphop.com |access-date=October 30, 2013 |archive-date=August 10, 2023 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230810224847/https://www.hotnewhiphop.com/16398-eminem-reveals-mmlpii-cover-art-news |url-status=live }}</ref> Shortly after premiering the song, Eminem spoke of working with Rihanna again to [[MTV News]]: "The perception of the record, what it's saying, I thought it would be a good idea to have her on it because I think people look at us like we're both a little nuts. That's one of the things that I was telling her in making the record: I think that people look at us a little crazy."<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/1716821/eminem-rihanna-monster-collaboration-perfect/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140812220530/http://www.mtv.com/news/1716821/eminem-rihanna-monster-collaboration-perfect/|url-status=dead|archive-date=August 12, 2014|title=Eminem On 'Monster' Rihanna Collabo: 'People Look At Us Like We're Both A Little Nuts'|first=Rob|last=Markman|website=MTV News}}</ref>

==Composition==
The song runs for 4 minutes and 10 seconds and is set in the key of [[C-sharp minor]] in common time, with a [[chord progression]] of C#m-B-A, and a [[tempo]] of 110 beats per minute. Vocals span from C#4-D#6.<ref>{{Cite web |url=https://www.musicnotes.com/sheetmusic/mtd.asp?ppn=MN0126810 |title=The Monster Sheet Music |access-date=April 12, 2024 |archive-date=February 22, 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250222044823/https://www.musicnotes.com/sheetmusic/mtd.asp?ppn=MN0126810 |url-status=live }}</ref>

==Commercial performance==
[[File:Rihanna Cologne 2013 03.jpg|thumbnail|upright|160px|right|With "The Monster" becoming Rihanna's 13th number one on the ''Billboard'' Hot 100, she tied [[Michael Jackson]] for the fourth most number ones in the chart's history, as well as becoming the fastest solo artist to accumulate thirteen number ones, surpassing [[Mariah Carey]]'s record.]]
"The Monster" first entered the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] at number three, on November 6, 2013, marking Eminem's third best entry following "[[Not Afraid]]" (number one, 2010) and "[[Love the Way You Lie]]" also featuring Rihanna (number two, 2010). This ties "[[Berzerk (song)|Berzerk]]", which also debuted at number three on the Hot 100. The entry gave Rihanna her 25th top ten on the chart, equaling [[Elvis Presley]] for the eighth-most top tens in the chart's history. This achievement also earned the singer the honor of being the fastest woman to accumulate the top ten tally (with eight years and four months since her first single), besting [[Madonna]] by three months. Furthermore, the debut marked Rihanna's 45th overall chart entry, tying [[Mariah Carey]] for the eighth most chart appearances amongst women.<ref>{{Cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/5785501/rihanna-scores-25th-hot-100-top-10|title=Rihanna Scores 25th Hot 100 Top 10|date=November 7, 2013|magazine=Billboard|access-date=November 7, 2013|archive-date=March 22, 2017|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170322101101/http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/5785501/rihanna-scores-25th-hot-100-top-10|url-status=live}}</ref>

The song concurrently opened at number one on the US [[Digital Songs]] chart, with sales of 373,000. The feat marked Eminem's ninth chart topper, giving him the third-most leaders in the tally's history, behind [[Katy Perry]] (9), and Rihanna, who increased her total to 13. The track also entered the US [[Radio Songs (chart)|Radio Songs]] chart at number 31, marking Eminem's second-best entry position following "[[Just Lose It]]" (number 17, 2004). In the same issue, "The Monster" debuted atop the [[Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs]] chart, marking Eminem's first number one on the chart of his career, and Rihanna's third. Furthermore, the song entered at number one on the US [[Rap Songs]] chart, marking the sixth and fourth leaders for Eminem and Rihanna, respectively.<ref>{{Cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/5778259/lordes-royals-rules-hot-100-for-sixth-week|title=Lorde's 'Royals' Rules Hot 100 for Sixth Week|date=November 6, 2013|magazine=Billboard|access-date=November 6, 2013|archive-date=March 30, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140330211023/http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/5778259/lordes-royals-rules-hot-100-for-sixth-week|url-status=live}}</ref>

The song topped the Hot 100 in its sixth week on the chart, after previously spending four consecutive weeks at number two. The song's ascent to number one marked Rihanna's 13th US number one, tying her with Michael Jackson for the fourth most number ones in the chart's 55-year history. Additionally, Rihanna became the fastest solo artist to achieve 13 chart-toppers, surpassing the previous record held by Mariah Carey (seven-years, eight-month and 19 days), with only [[the Beatles]] reaching 13 number ones more quickly. The song also rose to number one on the ''Billboard'' Radio Songs chart, marking Rihanna's twelfth and Eminem's third number one on the chart. Rihanna also passes Mariah Carey for the most number ones on the Radio Songs chart with the single's number one peak. For Eminem, he ties [[Sean Combs]] and [[Ludacris]] for the most Hot 100 number ones among rappers, with five each.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/5820081/eminem-rihanna-rule-hot-100-with-the-monster|title=Eminem, Rihanna Rule Hot 100 With 'The Monster'|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|publisher=Nielson Soundscan, Inc|first=Gary|last=Trust|date=December 11, 2013|access-date=December 11, 2013|archive-date=December 22, 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131222014903/http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/5820081/eminem-rihanna-rule-hot-100-with-the-monster|url-status=live}}</ref><ref>{{cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/5819991/chart-highlights-rihanna-breaks-record-for-most-pop-songs-no-1s|title=Chart Highlights: Rihanna Breaks Record For Most Pop Songs No. 1s|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|access-date=December 9, 2013|archive-date=April 2, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160402152327/http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/5819991/chart-highlights-rihanna-breaks-record-for-most-pop-songs-no-1s|url-status=live}}</ref> The song has sold 3,844,000 digital copies in the US as of June 2015.<ref name=us_sales>{{cite magazine |url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/ask-billboard-rihannas-best-selling-songs-albums/ |title=Ask Billboard: Rihanna's Best-Selling Songs & Albums |last=Trust |first=Gary |magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]] |publisher=[[Prometheus Global Media]] |date=June 23, 2015 |access-date=June 23, 2015 |archive-date=June 29, 2016 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160629182257/http://www.billboard.com/articles/columns/chart-beat/6605708/ask-billboard-rihannas-best-selling-songs-albums |url-status=live }}</ref>
In Canada, the song debuted at number one on the [[Canadian Hot 100]], selling 54,000 downloads, making it Eminem's highest selling debut week in Canada for a single since his song "[[Berzerk (song)|Berzerk]]," which sold 50,000 downloads in its debut week.<ref name="CANsales">{{cite web|url=https://dl.dropboxusercontent.com/u/67270545/Canadian%20Newsletters/nielsen_music_canadian_update_Nov6_2013.pdf |title=ARCADE FIRE SCORES THIRD CHART-TOPPING ALBUM |last=Tuch |first=Paul |date=November 6, 2013 |access-date=November 13, 2013 }}{{dead link|date=May 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

The song however, sold 96,059 copies the following week it dropped to number two, selling more copies than it did on the week it reached number one. The song marked Eminem and Rihanna's eighth number one single in that country of their careers respectively. It became Eminem's first single to top the chart as a lead artist since "[[Like Toy Soldiers]]" in 2005. Eminem however was featured on [[Akon]]'s chart topping single "[[Smack That]]" in 2006.<ref name="occ chart history em">{{cite web|title=Eminem|url=http://www.officialcharts.com/artist/_/eminem/|publisher=[[Official Charts Company]]|access-date=November 12, 2013|archive-date=June 8, 2012|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120608071515/http://www.officialcharts.com/artist/_/eminem/|url-status=live}}</ref> As for Rihanna, the chart topper placed her with [[Elvis Presley]] and [[The Beatles]] in the chart's history, as just one of three acts to have scored seven number ones in Britain over seven consecutive years. The song was commercially successful around Europe, reaching the top of the charts in eight countries and entered the top 10 in every territory it charted in.
Thus, in France, the song debuted atop the Singles Chart, becoming Rihanna's fifth number one and Eminem's first and only one. It was also Eminem's highest-charting debut in the country since "Love The Way You Lie" with Rihanna in 2010.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://lescharts.com/showitem.asp?interpret=Eminem+feat%2E+Rihanna&titel=The+Monster&cat=s|title=Les charts - Eminem feat.
