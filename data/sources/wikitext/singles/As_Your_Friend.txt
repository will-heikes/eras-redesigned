"'''As Your Friend'''" is a song by Dutch DJ [[Afrojack]], featuring vocals by American singer [[Chris Brown]]. The song was released as a single via [[iTunes Store|iTunes]] on 13 February 2013.{{citation needed|date=May 2014}} It was written by Afrojack, Chris Brown, Nadir Sakir, [[DJ Buddha]], and [[Polow da Don]], and was produced by Afrojack with co-production by Leroy Styles, DJ Buddha and Polow da Don.<ref>{{cite web|last1=Polonsky|first1=Sarah|title=Afrojacks Signs With Island Def Jam and Universal Music Group|url=http://www.vibe.com/article/afrojacks-signs-island-def-jam-and-universal-music-group|work=[[Vibe (magazine)|Vibe]]|date=March 14, 2013}}</ref> "As Your Friend" is featured as a bonus track on the deluxe version of Afrojack's 2014 debut studio album ''[[Forget the World (Afrojack album)|Forget the World]]''.

==Music video==
On 22 March 2013, Afrojack uploaded a lyric video onto Vevo, while the official music video was released on 29 March. Chris Brown does not appear in the video.

The video features a lot of special effects, and features Afrojack with an eagle and many women dancing around him. Since its release, the video has gained over 32,600,000 views.
