"'''Bright'''" is a song by American [[indie pop]] band [[Echosmith]]. It was released to American radio on February 2, 2015 by [[Warner Bros. Records]] as the second single from their debut studio album, ''[[Talking Dreams]]'' (2013). The song became the group's second top 40 hit on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] and their second top-ten hit on the [[Adult Top 40]] chart.

==Composition==

"Bright" is written in the key of G♭ Major, and follows a chord progression of Gb-E♭m-Cb-Db (I-vi-IV-V). The tempo of the song is 90 beats per minute.

==Music video==
A music video to accompany the release of "Bright" was first released onto YouTube on February 12, 2015 at a total length of three minutes and 37 seconds.<ref>{{Cite video|url = https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMAzstG5O7E|title = Echosmith - Bright [OFFICIAL MUSIC VIDEO]|last = |first = |date = Feb 12, 2015|language = English|type = Video|publisher = Echosmith}}</ref> Sydney, Jamie, Noah and Graham are shown packing up for a camping trip. They arrived to see their friends and hiked up to their campsite. The group is excited to see the night sky. Nearing the end, at nightfall, they hiked up to see a meteor shower. The video ends with the word "bright" being shown as a [[constellation]].
