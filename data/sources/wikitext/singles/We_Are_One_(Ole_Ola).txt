"'''We Are One (Ole Ola)'''" is a song recorded by Cuban-American rapper [[Pitbull (rapper)|Pitbull]] for ''[[One Love, One Rhythm – The 2014 FIFA World Cup Official Album]]'' (2014). It is the [[List of FIFA World Cup songs and anthems|official song]] of the [[2014 FIFA World Cup]] held in [[Brazil]] and features guest vocals from American singer [[Jennifer Lopez]] and Brazilian singer [[Claudia Leitte]]. The artists co-wrote the song with [[Sia]], [[RedOne]], [[Danny Mercer]], [[Dr. Luke]], [[Cirkut (record producer)|Cirkut]], and [[Thomas Troelsen]]; the latter three are also the producers.<ref>{{cite web|title=We Are One (Ole, Ola) – Jennifer Lopez & Cláudia Leitte|url=http://www.dr.dk/musik/titel/we%20are%20one%20(ole%2c%20ola)/9009898-1-1|publisher=[[DR (broadcaster)|DR]]|access-date=April 11, 2014|language=da}}</ref>

While receiving positive reviews from critics, initially the song received some negative reactions from Brazilians over its lack of Brazilian feeling. To change that, another version was also released and the music changed slightly to fit Afro Brazilian group [[Olodum]]'s style of drumming – that version being the one that was used in the music video for the song. "We Are One" has experienced commercial success, reaching the top spot in Belgium and Hungary, top 10 in several countries including Austria, France, Germany, Italy, Spain, and World Cup's host country, Brazil and top twenty in Denmark and Norway. The song peaked at number one in three countries.

== Background and release ==
According to one of the co-writers of "We Are One (Ole Ola), [[Thomas Troelsen]], he pitched a demo version "that had a sort of whistle thing in it" to Pitbull.<ref>{{cite web |last1=Greeves |first1=David |title=Thomas Troelsen |url=https://www.soundonsound.com/people/thomas-troelsen |publisher=SOS Publications Group |access-date=31 July 2018 |date=August 2014}}</ref> On January 22, 2014, [[FIFA]] and [[Sony Music Entertainment]] announced that "We Are One (Ole Ola)" would serve as the [[FIFA World Cup official songs|official song]] of the [[2014 FIFA World Cup]].<ref>{{cite magazine|last=Cantor-Navas|first=Judy|title=Pitbull's 'We Are One (Ole, Ola)' Featuring Jennifer Lopez, Claudia Leitte Named FIFA'S Official World Cup Song|url=http://www.billboard.com/biz/articles/news/global/5884960/pitbulls-we-are-one-ole-ola-featuring-jennifer-lopez-claudia-leitte|magazine=Billboard|publisher=[[Prometheus Global Media]]|access-date=April 6, 2014|date=January 23, 2014}}</ref> At the occasion, Pitbull said, "I'm honored to join Jennifer Lopez and Claudia Leitte at the FIFA World Cup to bring the world together. I truly believe that this great game and the power of music will help unify us because we are best when we are one."<ref>{{cite magazine|last=Grow|first=Kory|title=Pitbull's 'We Are One (Ole Ola)' Selected as Official World Cup Song|url=https://www.rollingstone.com/music/news/pitbulls-we-are-one-ole-ola-selected-as-official-world-cup-song-20140124|magazine=[[Rolling Stone]]|publisher=[[Wenner Media]]|access-date=April 6, 2014|date=January 23, 2014}}</ref> Lopez said Pitbull asked her to join the song: "I can't take credit for this. This was one of Pitbull's call-ins. He had this record, and he's like, 'I think this record could be great for the World Cup. He's like, 'Will you do it with me?' And I go, 'Yeah, of course.'"<ref>{{cite web|last1=Fekadu|first1=Mesfin|title=From Bronx to Brazil: J.Lo to sing at World Cup|url=http://www.newsday.com/news/region-state/from-bronx-to-brazil-j-lo-to-sing-at-world-cup-1.8404750|website=[[Newsday]]|access-date=June 11, 2014|date=June 10, 2014}}{{dead link|date=May 2026|bot=medic}}{{cbignore|bot=medic}}</ref> A solo version of the song featuring only Pitbull leaked online on February 2.<ref>{{cite web|last=Rigby|first=Sam|title=Pitbull's World Cup anthem 'We Are One (Ole Ola) surfaces|url=http://www.digitalspy.ca/music/news/a548363/pitbulls-world-cup-anthem-we-are-one-ole-ola-surfaces-listen.html#~oACXWYc8zFiWNY|work=[[Digital Spy]]|publisher=[[Hearst Magazines UK]]|access-date=April 6, 2014|date=February 2, 2014}}</ref> On April 8, Pitbull tweeted an [[iTunes Store]] link to the track, debuting the song.<ref name="huffpost">{{cite web|last=Moreno|first=Carolina|title=Pitbull Debuts 'We Are One (Ole Ola)' Ft. J.Lo For Brazil World Cup (AUDIO)|url=http://www.huffingtonpost.com/2014/04/08/pitbull-we-are-one-ole-ola_n_5110542.html|work=[[Huffington Post]]|access-date=April 12, 2014|date=April 8, 2014}}</ref>

== Critical response ==
The song received generally lukewarm reviews. Carl Williott of [[Idolator (website)|Idolator]] opined that there is too much of Pitbull in the song, and not enough Lopez or Leitte. He, however, stated that the song is an upgrade from [[Shakira]]'s "[[Waka Waka (This Time for Africa)]]", the official song of the [[2010 FIFA World Cup]].<ref>{{cite web|last=Williott|first=Carl|title=Pitbull & Jennifer Lopez's "We Are One (Ole Ola)": Hear The Official 2014 World Cup Song|url=http://www.idolator.com/7513264/pitbull-jennifer-lopez-world-cup-song-we-are-one|archive-url=https://archive.today/20140411033726/http://www.idolator.com/7513264/pitbull-jennifer-lopez-world-cup-song-we-are-one|url-status=dead|archive-date=April 11, 2014|work=Idolator|access-date=April 12, 2014|date=April 8, 2014}}</ref> [[Fuse.tv]] wrote that, similarly to "Waka Waka", the song "blends the feel of the tournament's hosting country with an accessible, exciting pop sound that has instant repeat appeal".<ref>{{cite web|title=J.Lo & Pitbull Unleash FIFA World Cup Song "We Are One (Ole Ola)"|url=http://www.fuse.tv/2014/04/jennifer-lopez-pitbull-we-are-one-ole-world-cup-claudia-leitte|work=Fuse|publisher=Fuse Networks|access-date=April 11, 2014|date=April 8, 2014}}</ref> Judy Cantor-Navas of ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' described the song as "surprisingly breezy",<ref>{{cite magazine|last=Cantor-Navas|first=Judy|title=Pitbull Debuts Official World Cup Song: Listen to 'We Are One' Feat. Jennifer Lopez|url=https://www.billboard.com/music/latin/listen-pitbull-official-world-cup-song-we-are-one-fifa-6042197/|magazine=Billboard|publisher=Prometheus Global Media|access-date=April 11, 2014|date=April 8, 2014}}</ref> with ''[[Spin (magazine)|Spin]]'' noting it to contain the "rah-rah international unity you'd expect for a Cup song".<ref>{{cite web|title=Get Numb to Pitbull and Jennifer Lopez's World Cup Anthem|url=http://www.spin.com/articles/pitbull-jennifer-lopez-world-cup-anthem-we-are-one-ola-ola/|work=[[Spin (magazine)|Spin]]|publisher=Spin Music|access-date=April 13, 2014|date=April 8, 2014}}</ref> Luís Antônio Giron of ''[[Época (Brazilian magazine)|Época]]'' criticized the album saying "maybe I'm wrong and Brazil only crave to be the same as what FIFA wants it to be. Brazil is ''fifalized''". And praised music produced in Miami, "bomb on any track."<ref>{{cite web|last=Antênio Giron|first=Luís|title=O Brasil se "fifaliza"|url=http://epoca.globo.com/colunas-e-blogs/luis-antonio-giron/noticia/2014/04/o-brasil-se-bfifafizab.html|work=[[Época (Brazilian magazine)|Época]]|publisher=[[Globo.com]]|access-date=May 10, 2014}}</ref>

==Music video==
In February 2014, Pitbull, Leitte and Lopez shot the music video for "We Are One (Ole Ola)"  with [[Olodum]] in [[Fort Lauderdale, Florida|Fort Lauderdale]], Florida, United States. Its concept was to simulate [[Carnival]] in its home country Brazil with the Olodum mix of the song being used as the soundtrack. It was released on May 16, 2014.<ref name="huffpost" /> As of November 2025, the video has received over 1 billion views.

==Live performances==
[[File:The opening ceremony of the FIFA World Cup 2014 13.jpg|200px|thumbnail|right| Pitbull, Leitte and Lopez performing "We Are One" at the [[2014 FIFA World Cup opening ceremony]]]]
Pitbull, Lopez and Leitte performed "We Are One (Ole Ola)" at the [[2014 Billboard Music Awards]] on May 18, 2014.<ref>[http://www.billboard.com/articles/events/bbma-2014/6091960/pitbull-jennifer-lopez-claudia-leitte-billboard-music-awards-2014-ole-ola Pitbull, Jennifer Lopez Perform World Cup Anthem at Billboard Music Awards | Billboard<!-- Bot generated title -->]</ref> They also performed the song at the [[2014 FIFA World Cup opening ceremony]] on June 12 in [[Arena Corinthians]], São Paulo, prior to the [[2014 FIFA World Cup Group A#Brazil vs Croatia|Brazil v. Croatia]] match, preceded by Leitte's solo rendition of [[Ary Barroso]]'s classic "[[Aquarela do Brasil]]".<ref name="huffpost" /> However, due to the amount of noise made by dancers and poor quality stadium acoustics, some spectators had difficulty hearing the vocals.<ref name="4music">{{cite episode|title=This Week's Fresh Music Top 20|air-date=June 15, 2014|network=[[4Music]]}}</ref>

In 2023, Pitbull, Lopez and Leitte performed "We Are One (Ole Ola)" at the [[Miss Universe 2023]] in [[El Salvador]] during the opening number of the contestants with the floral [[Swimsuits]] on November 18, 2023 and [[Miss Earth 2023]] for the opening number of the contestants with the [[Swimsuits]] on December 22, 2023 in [[Vietnam]] performances [[Bamboo Mañalac]] (instead Pitbull), [[Natalia Jimenez|Jimenez]] (instead Lopez) and [[Anahi]] (instead Leitte).
