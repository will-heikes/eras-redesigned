"'''Good Thing'''" is a song by the American rapper [[Sage the Gemini]] featuring [[Nick Jonas]]. It was released on May 18, 2015, by [[Republic Records]] as the intended lead single from the former's unreleased album ''Bachelor Party''.<ref>{{cite web|author= |url=https://hiphop-n-more.com/2015/05/new-music-sage-the-gemini-good-thing-feat-nick-jonas/ |title=New Video: Sage The Gemini – 'Good Thing' (Feat. Nick Jonas) |publisher=HipHop-N-More.com |date=2015-05-21 |access-date=2015-05-28}}</ref> The song peaked at number 75 on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]].

==Background==
An early version of the song [[Music leak|leaked]] online on April 4, 2015.<ref name="idolator1">{{cite web|last=Lee |first=Christina |url=http://www.idolator.com/7587338/nick-jonas-sage-the-gemini-good-thing |archive-url=https://archive.today/20150809020959/http://www.idolator.com/7587338/nick-jonas-sage-the-gemini-good-thing |url-status=dead |archive-date=August 9, 2015 |title=Nick Jonas Joins Sage The Gemini On "Good Thing": Listen |publisher=Idolator.com |date= 5 April 2015|access-date=2015-05-28}}</ref> Talking about the collaboration, Jonas said that it was a "great next step for his career", hoping for the song to become a successful summer hit.<ref>{{cite web|author= |url=http://www.ryanseacrest.com/2015/05/18/nick-jonas-talks-fall-tour-with-bebe-rexha-new-single-good-thing-with-sage-the-gemini/ |title=Nick Jonas Talks Fall Tour With Bebe Rexha, 'Good Thing' With Sage the Gemini |publisher=Ryanseacrest.com |date=2015-05-18 |access-date=2015-05-28}}</ref> The song was eventually released in explicit and clean versions on May 18, 2015.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/articles/news/6568694/nick-jonas-announces-tour-dates |title=Nick Jonas Announces 'Live In Concert' Tour: See the Dates! |magazine=Billboard |date= 18 May 2015|access-date=2015-05-28}}</ref> The song appeared on the re-release of Nick Jonas' 2014 self-titled album, ''[[Nick Jonas (album)|Nick Jonas X2]]''.<ref>{{cite web | url=https://music.apple.com/gb/album/nick-jonas-x2/1440845540 | title=Nick Jonas X2 by Nick Jonas | publisher=[[Apple Inc.|Apple]] | work=[[iTunes Store]] | date=20 November 2015 | access-date=November 20, 2015}}</ref>

==Music video==
The music video for the song was shot in early May 2015 in [[Los Angeles]]. It premiered on [[Complex (magazine)|''Complex'' magazine's]] website on May 21, 2015, and was published on Sage's [[Vevo]] channel on [[YouTube]] the same day.<ref>{{cite web|last=Ortiz |first=Edwin |url=https://www.complex.com/music/2015/05/sage-the-gemini-new-video-premiere-good-thing-feat-nick-jonas |title=Premiere: Watch Sage the Gemini's "Good Thing" Video f/ Nick Jonas |publisher=Complex.com |date=2015-05-21 |access-date=2015-05-28}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://hit-zound.nl/index.php/2-hitzound-blog/3774-sage-the-gemini-good-thing-ft-nick-jonas |title=Sage the Gemini - Good Thing (ft. Nick Jonas) |publisher=Hit-zound.nl |date=2013-08-15 |access-date=2015-05-28}}</ref> Directed by [[Hannah Lux Davis]], the video takes an exotic approach, in which Sage the Gemini and Jonas are seen featured in a lush, tropical backdrop, surrounded by models.<ref>{{cite web |author= |url=http://imusicbuzz.com/sage-the-gemini-good-thing-featuring-nick-jonas-official-single-cover/ |title=Sage the Gemini "Good Thing" (featuring Nick Jonas) &#91;Official Single Cover&#93; |publisher=iMusicBuzz.com |date=2015-05-11 |access-date=2015-05-28 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150520002439/http://imusicbuzz.com/sage-the-gemini-good-thing-featuring-nick-jonas-official-single-cover/ |archive-date=2015-05-20 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

==Reception==
Christina Lee of Idolator wrote that "the song proves that Sage the Gemini can write a smash hit if he wants".<ref name="idolator1"/> Rapup.com called the song "seductive", adding that the rappers rhymes are heartfelt. while Nick delivers a soulful hook.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.rap-up.com/2015/05/18/new-music-sage-the-gemini-nick-jonas-good-thing/ |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150519225334/http://www.rap-up.com/2015/05/18/new-music-sage-the-gemini-nick-jonas-good-thing/ |url-status=dead |archive-date=May 19, 2015 |title=New Music: Sage the Gemini feat. Nick Jonas – 'Good Thing' |publisher=Rap-Up.com |date=2015-05-18 |access-date=2015-05-28}}</ref> Brendan V van DJbooth said that "even though the rapper is the headliner of the collaboration, the song is mostly commanded by the Jonas brother, as you’ll hear a lot of his crooning over the roaring synthesizers".<ref>{{cite news|url=http://www.djbooth.net/index/tracks/review/sage-the-gemini-good-thing-nick-jonas |title=Sage The Gemini - Good Thing |newspaper=Djbooth |publisher=Djbooth.net |date=2015-05-19 |access-date=2015-05-28}}</ref> Vibe's Mia V. praised Nick's strong R&B vocals while the rapper switched it up with a silky smooth sixteen.<ref>{{cite web |last=Golding |first=Shenequa |url=http://www.vibe.com/2015/05/sage-the-gemini-and-nick-jonas-team-up-for-good-thing/ |title=Sage The Gemini And Nick Jonas 'Good Thing' |publisher=Vibe.com |date=2015-05-18 |access-date=2015-05-28 |archive-date=2016-12-20 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161220103205/http://www.vibe.com/2015/05/sage-the-gemini-and-nick-jonas-team-up-for-good-thing/ |url-status=dead }}</ref> MTV's Madeline Roth called the song "the perfect summer song with plenty of spellbinding rhymes and soulful refrain".<ref>{{cite web|author=v |url=http://www.mtv.com/news/2164852/nick-jonas-new-song-and-tour/ |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150522014359/http://www.mtv.com/news/2164852/nick-jonas-new-song-and-tour/ |url-status=dead |archive-date=May 22, 2015 |title=Nick Jonas Just Dropped A Sexy New Song AND Announced A Headlining Tour |publisher=Mtv.com |date=2015-05-19 |access-date=2015-05-28}}</ref>

==Live performance==
Sage the Gemini and Nick Jonas performed the song during the July 23 episode of ''[[The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon]]''.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://headlineplanet.com/home/2015/07/12/sage-the-gemini-nick-jonas-performing-on-the-tonight-show-starring-jimmy-fallon/|title = Sage the Gemini, Nick Jonas Performing on "The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon"|website=Headlineplanet.com|date = 12 July 2015}}</ref> Jonas performed the song live during his [[Nick Jonas: Live in Concert|Live in Concert]] tour.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://theearlyregistration.com/2015/09/15/photos-nick-jonas-house-of-blues-chicago/|title=Photos: Nick Jonas and Bebe Rexha Perform at the House of Blues in Chicago|website=Theearlyregistration.com|date=15 September 2015}}</ref> He also performed the song during We Can Survive 2015.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.stagerightsecrets.com/we-can-survive-2/#|title=We Can Survive Review!|website=Stagerightsecrets.com|date=25 October 2015}}</ref> The song is part of the setlist of the [[Future Now Tour]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://music.blog.ajc.com/2016/06/30/concert-review-and-photos-demi-lovato-and-nick-jonas-launch-tour-in-atlanta-with-heart-and-soul/|title=Concert review: Demi Lovato and Nick Jonas launch tour in Atlanta &#124; Atlanta Music Scene with Melissa Ruggieri|date=29 July 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160729070225/http://music.blog.ajc.com/2016/06/30/concert-review-and-photos-demi-lovato-and-nick-jonas-launch-tour-in-atlanta-with-heart-and-soul/ |access-date=22 May 2023|archive-date=2016-07-29 }}</ref>
