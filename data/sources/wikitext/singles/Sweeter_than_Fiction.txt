"'''Sweeter than Fiction'''" is a song by the American singer-songwriter [[Taylor Swift]] from the soundtrack to the 2013 film ''[[One Chance (film)|One Chance]]''. Written and produced by Swift and [[Jack Antonoff]], the track was released by [[Big Machine Records]] for download via the [[iTunes Store]] on October 21, 2013. "Sweeter than Fiction" is a 1980s-inspired [[electropop]], [[synth-pop]], [[Bubblegum music|bubblegum]], [[Rock music|rock]], and [[new wave music|new wave]] song with elements of [[Britpop]]. Lyrically, it details the narrator's belief in her friend that he will succeed one day. "Sweeter than Fiction" was nominated for [[Golden Globe Award for Best Original Song|Best Original Song]] at the [[2014 Golden Globe Awards]].

The track peaked in the top 40 on the singles charts of Canada, Ireland, New Zealand, Scotland, and the United States. Critical reception of the song was generally positive, although retrospective opinions have regarded it as a lesser entry in Swift's discography. Some critics opined that "Sweeter than Fiction" was pivotal in shaping the synth-pop sound of Swift's album ''[[1989 (album)|1989]]'', as well as the long-lasting collaboration between Swift and Antonoff. Following the [[Taylor Swift masters dispute|2019 dispute over the ownership of Swift's back catalog]], she [[re-recorded]] the song as "'''Sweeter than Fiction (Taylor's Version)'''", which was released exclusively on the ''Tangerine Edition'' of her fourth re-recorded album, ''[[1989 (Taylor's Version)]]'' (2023).

==Commercial performance==
"Sweeter than Fiction" debuted and peaked at number 34 on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] chart dated October 27, 2013.<ref name="Hot100" /><ref name="Chart-Moves">{{cite magazine |last=Trust |first=Gary |date=November 1, 2013 |title=Chart Moves: Taylor Swift Makes 'Sweet' Start, Tyga Teams With Justin Bieber on Hot 100 |url=http://www.billboard.com/biz/articles/news/5778124/chart-moves-taylor-swift-makes-sweet-start-tyga-teams-with-justin-bieber |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131107025127/http://www.billboard.com/biz/articles/news/5778124/chart-moves-taylor-swift-makes-sweet-start-tyga-teams-with-justin-bieber |archive-date=November 7, 2013 |access-date=November 1, 2013 |magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]}}</ref> That same week, the track debuted at number six on the [[Hot Digital Songs]] chart, with first-week downloads of 116,000.<ref>{{cite web |last=Grein |first=Paul |date=October 30, 2013 |title=Week Ending Oct. 27, 2013. Songs: Women Roar, Twerk & Crush The Competition |url=http://music.yahoo.com/blogs/chart-watch/week-ending-oct-27-2013-songs-women-roar-214157434.html |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131102235312/http://music.yahoo.com/blogs/chart-watch/week-ending-oct-27-2013-songs-women-roar-214157434.html |archive-date=November 2, 2013 |access-date=January 22, 2014 |publisher=[[Yahoo! Music]]}}</ref> Elsewhere, the song peaked in the top 40 in Canada (17),<ref name="Canada" /> New Zealand (26),<ref name="NZ" /> Ireland (38),<ref name="Ireland" /> and Scotland (39);<ref name="Scotland" /> while charting at number 44 in Australia and number 45 in the United Kingdom.<ref name="Australia" /><ref name="UK" /> It also charted at number 83 in Italy<ref name="Italy" /> and number three on South Korea's international singles chart.<ref name="Gaon" />
