"'''Berzerk'''" is a song by American rapper [[Eminem]]. The song, released on August 27, 2013, is the first [[Single (music)|single]] from Eminem's eighth studio album ''[[The Marshall Mathers LP 2]]''. The song was produced by [[Rick Rubin]] and [[Sampling (music)|samples]] [[Billy Squier]]'s "[[The Stroke]]", as well as the [[Beastie Boys]]' "[[(You Gotta) Fight for Your Right (To Party!)|Fight for Your Right]]", taken from their 1986 debut album ''[[Licensed to Ill]]'', which Rubin had also produced, and [[Naughty by Nature]]'s "[[Feel Me Flow]]". The song was heavily downloaded in its first week of release, resulting in the song debuting at number three on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]].

==Composition==
[[File:RickRubinSept09.jpg|thumb|upright|150px|[[Rick Rubin]] produced "Berzerk".]]
The song, produced by Rick Rubin, [[Sampling (music)|samples]] [[Billy Squier]]'s "[[The Stroke]]", the [[Beastie Boys]]' "[[(You Gotta) Fight for Your Right (To Party!)|Fight for Your Right]]", taken from their 1986 debut album ''[[Licensed to Ill]]'' and also produced by Rubin, and [[Naughty by Nature]]'s "[[Feel Me Flow]]".<ref name="single">{{cite AV media notes |title= Berzerk |others= Eminem |year= 2013 |url= https://www.discogs.com/release/5008520-Berzerk/images |access-date= April 17, 2016 |type= liner notes |publisher= [[Aftermath Entertainment|Aftermath]] |archive-date= October 12, 2020 |archive-url= https://web.archive.org/web/20201012033357/https://www.discogs.com/release/5008520-Berzerk/images |url-status= live }}</ref> Writing credits are given to Eminem, Rubin, Squier, [[Adam Yauch]] and [[Ad-Rock|Adam Horovitz]] of the Beastie Boys, and [[Treach|Anthony Criss]], [[Vin Rock|Vincent Brown]], and [[KayGee|Kier Gist]] of [[Naughty by Nature]], [[Ziggy Modeliste]], [[Art Neville]], [[Cyril Neville]], [[Leo Nocentelli]] and [[George Porter Jr.]] for the "Feel Me Flow" segment.<ref>{{cite web |title=ACE Repertory – Berzerk |url=https://www.ascap.com/repertory#/ace/search/workID/885810048 |publisher=[[ASCAP]] |access-date=28 October 2021 |archive-date=January 2, 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240102011031/https://www.ascap.com/repertory#/ace/search/workID/885810048 |url-status=live }}</ref>

The song starts with a "blast of static, hard guitar and Em roaring out of the gate sounding like the long-lost, fourth Beastie Boy", according to [[Yahoo! Music]] critic Craig Rosen.<ref name=SB>{{cite web|url=http://music.yahoo.com/blogs/stop-the-presses/shady-back-eminem-goes-berzerk-sampling-beasties-billy-175057646.html|title=Shady's Back — Eminem Goes 'Berzerk' Sampling the Beasties and Billy Squier|access-date=August 29, 2013|date=August 27, 2013|work=[[Yahoo! Music]]|author=Rosen, Craig|archive-date=October 24, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141024025529/https://music.yahoo.com/blogs/stop-the-presses/shady-back-eminem-goes-berzerk-sampling-beasties-billy-175057646.html|url-status=live}}</ref> The song samples the "Kick it!" part of "[[(You Gotta) Fight for Your Right (To Party!)]]", performed by [[Ad-Rock]].<ref name="single"/><ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.rollingstone.com/music/videos/eminem-goes-old-school-in-new-song-berzerk-20130827|title=Eminem Goes Old-School in New Song 'Berzerk': Rapper's single samples Billy Squier, Beastie Boys|access-date=August 29, 2013|date=August 27, 2013|author=Danton, Eric R.|magazine=[[Rolling Stone]]|archive-date=November 8, 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131108023017/http://www.rollingstone.com/music/videos/eminem-goes-old-school-in-new-song-berzerk-20130827|url-status=live}}</ref> According to ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]''{{'s}} Lars Brandle, the song is "a punchy, guitar-and-beats driven song which channels [[Joan Jett & the Blackhearts]]' ''[[I Love Rock 'n Roll (album)|I Love Rock 'n' Roll]]'' and ''Licensed to Ill''-era Beastie Boys."<ref name=EGBFNS>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/music/music-news/eminem-goes-berzerk-for-new-single-listen-5672691/|title=Eminem Goes 'Berzerk' For New Single: Listen|access-date=August 29, 2013|date=August 26, 2013|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|author=Brandle, Lars|archive-date=April 20, 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220420081428/https://www.billboard.com/music/music-news/eminem-goes-berzerk-for-new-single-listen-5672691/|url-status=live}}</ref>

== Music video ==
During the halftime at the September 7, 2013 Michigan–Notre Dame football game, Eminem premiered a preview of the [[music video]] for "Berzerk". Then the video was premiered two days later on September 9, via [[VEVO]]. The video was filmed in [[Brooklyn, New York]] and directed by Syndrome.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.complex.com/music/2013/10/eminem-explains-berzerk-video |title=Eminem, Explains Berzerk {{!}} Complex<!-- Bot generated title --> |access-date=October 6, 2013 |archive-date=May 4, 2014 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140504065550/http://www.complex.com/music/2013/10/eminem-explains-berzerk-video |url-status=live }}</ref> It features [[cameo appearance]]s from fellow American rappers [[Kendrick Lamar]] (Em's Aftermath labelmate) and [[Kid Rock]]<ref>{{cite magazine |url=http://www.complex.com/music/2013/09/eminem-bezerk-music-video-preview |title=Eminem "Berzerk" Music Video Preview |magazine=Complex |access-date=September 8, 2013 |archive-date=May 26, 2021 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210526204030/https://www.complex.com/music/2013/09/eminem-bezerk-music-video-preview |url-status=live }}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |url=https://www.rollingstone.com/music/videos/eminem-goes-vintage-in-berzerk-video-20130909 |title=Eminem Takes It Back with 'Berzerk' – Video {{!}} Rolling Stone<!-- Bot generated title --> |access-date=August 24, 2017 |archive-date=September 21, 2013 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130921192603/http://www.rollingstone.com/music/videos/eminem-goes-vintage-in-berzerk-video-20130909 |url-status=live }}</ref> (both of whom Eminem mentions in the song's lyrics) in addition to [[Shady Records]] labelmates [[Slaughterhouse (group)|Slaughterhouse]] (most prominently [[Royce da 5'9"]]), [[Mr. Porter]], [[Yelawolf]] and [[The Alchemist (musician)|The Alchemist]], the song's producer Rick Rubin and Eminem's manager [[Paul Rosenberg (music manager)|Paul Rosenberg]]. The video also features short clips of [[backyard wrestling]], and public fights as well as short clips of the official music video of Billy Squier's "The Stroke", which is heavily sampled in the song.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1713729/eminem-berzerk-video.jhtml|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130913012904/http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1713729/eminem-berzerk-video.jhtml|url-status=dead|archive-date=September 13, 2013|title=Eminem Ropes In Detroit Homey Kid Rock For 'Berzerk' Video|author=Gil Kaufman|website=[[MTV]] |date=September 9, 2013|access-date=September 10, 2013}}</ref> The video received a nomination at [[2014 MTV Video Music Awards|MTV Video Music Awards 2014]] in the category "[[MTV Video Music Award for Best Hip-Hop Video|Best Hip-Hop Video]]".<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/1870248/2014-video-music-awards-nominations-full-list/ |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140717142334/http://www.mtv.com/news/1870248/2014-video-music-awards-nominations-full-list/ |url-status=dead |archive-date=July 17, 2014 |title=2014 MTV Video Music Awards Nominations: Get The Full List |publisher=MTV |access-date=July 18, 2014}}</ref>

==Release and promotion==
"Berzerk" originally premiered on August 26, on [[SiriusXM]] channel, [[Shade 45]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.complex.com/music/2013/08/eminem-berzerk|title=Listen: Eminem "Berzerk" (Prod. By Rick Rubin)|access-date=August 27, 2013|date=August 26, 2013|work=[[Complex (magazine)|Complex]]|author=Gleckman, Alexander|archive-date=November 5, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151105113419/http://www.complex.com/music/2013/08/eminem-berzerk|url-status=live}}</ref> ''[[The Marshall Mathers LP 2]]'', a sequel to Eminem's acclaimed studio effort ''[[The Marshall Mathers LP]]'', was revealed during [[2013 MTV Video Music Awards|MTV Video Music Awards]] on August 25, 2013, with two previews of "Berzerk" via [[Beats By Dr. Dre]] commercials.<ref>{{cite magazine | url=https://www.rollingstone.com/music/news/eminem-announces-new-album-mmlp2-20130825 | title=Eminem Announces New Album 'MMLP2' | publisher=[[Wenner Media LLC]] | magazine=[[Rolling Stone]] | date=August 25, 2013 | access-date=August 26, 2013 | author=Coulehan, Erin | archive-date=November 20, 2013 | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131120184635/http://www.rollingstone.com/music/news/eminem-announces-new-album-mmlp2-20130825 | url-status=live }}</ref> Some critics describe Eminem's return in these commercials as the highlight of the show.<ref name=SB/> The video that accompanied the song featured a [[boombox]] prominently.<ref name=SB/> The song was the featured song for the 2013 season of ''[[Saturday Night Football]]'', debuting with the September 7 [[Michigan–Notre Dame football rivalry]] game. On 10 November 2013 in [[Amsterdam]] for the [[MTV Europe Music Awards 2013]], he performed "Berzerk" and also "[[Rap God]]".<ref>{{cite web|url=http://espnmediazone.com/us/press-releases/2013/09/saturday-night-football-goes-berzerk-with-eminem/|title=Saturday Night Football Goes "Berzerk" with Eminem|access-date=September 6, 2013|date=September 5, 2013|publisher=[[ESPN]]|author=Humes, Michael|archive-date=March 26, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160326190858/http://espnmediazone.com/us/press-releases/2013/09/saturday-night-football-goes-berzerk-with-eminem/|url-status=dead}}</ref>

==Commercial performance==
"Berzerk" sold 362,000 downloads in its first week in the US, debuting at number three on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] (behind [[Katy Perry]]'s [[Roar (Katy Perry song)|Roar]] and [[Robin Thicke]]'s [[Blurred Lines]]) becoming Eminem's 15th Top 10 hit on the Hot 100.<ref name="Berzerk US Billboard debut">{{cite magazine |url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/5680131/katy-perry-dethrones-robin-thicke-atop-hot-100 |title=Katy Perry Dethrones Robin Thicke Atop Hot 100 |magazine=Billboard |date=September 4, 2013 number two on the [[Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs]] chart.<ref>{{Cite magazine |url=http://www.billboard.com/artist/301722/Eminem/chart?f=367 |title=Eminem – Chart history {{!}} Billboard<!-- Bot generated debuted at number one on the [[Rap Songs]] chart.<ref name="Berzerk US Billboard debut"/> "Berzerk"'s debut is just below the debut of the lead single for ''[[Recovery (Eminem album)|Recovery]]'', In Canada, the song debuted at number two with 50,000 downloads sold, which more than doubled the 24,000 downloads sold in the debut week of "Not Afraid".<ref>{{cite web|url=https://dl.dropboxusercontent.com/u/67270545/Canadian%20Newsletters/nielsen_music_canadian_update_Sep4_2013.pdf|title=AVENGED SEVENFOLD SCORES FIRST NUMBER ONE ALBUM|access-date=September 15, 2013}}{{Dead link|date=November 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref> It was Eminem's best one week download total ever in the country, until it was surpassed by the 54,000 downloads sold for Eminem's later single, "[[The Monster (song)|The Monster]]".<ref>{{cite web|url=https://dl.dropboxusercontent.com/u/67270545/Canadian%20Newsletters/nielsen_music_canadian_update_Nov6_2013.pdf|title=ARCADE FIRE SCORES THIRD CHART-TOPPING ALBUM Arcade|access-date=November 8, 2013}}{{dead link|date=July 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref> The song was certified Double Platinum in Digital downloads in Canada on November 1, 2013.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.musiccanada.com/GPSearchResult.aspx?st=Berzerk&ica=False&sa=Eminem&sl=&smt=0&sat=-1&ssb=Artist|title=Gold Platinum Database: Eminem – Berzerk|publisher=[[Music Canada]]|access-date=November 1, 2013|archive-date=October 12, 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131012022016/http://www.musiccanada.com/GPSearchResult.aspx?st=Berzerk&ica=False&sa=Eminem&sl=&smt=0&sat=-1&ssb=Artist|url-status=dead}}</ref>

The song debuted at number one on the [[UK R&B Chart]]<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.officialcharts.com/charts/r-and-b-singles-chart/20131013/114/ |title=2013-10-19 Top 40 R&B Singles Archive |publisher=[[Official Charts Company]] |date=October 19, 2013 |access-date=January 10, 2014 |archive-date=October 30, 2015 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151030065536/http://www.officialcharts.com/charts/r-and-b-singles-chart/20131013/114/ |url-status=live }}</ref> and was replaced on the chart by his single "[[Rap God]]".<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.officialcharts.com/charts/r-and-b-singles-chart/20131020/114/ |title=2013-10-26 Top 40 R&B Singles Archive |publisher=[[Official Charts Company]] |date=October 26, 2013 |access-date=January 10, 2014 |archive-date=February 3, 2017 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170203012802/http://www.officialcharts.com/charts/r-and-b-singles-chart/20131020/114/ |url-status=live }}</ref>

==Critical reception==
Will Hermes of ''[[Rolling Stone]]'' gave the song 4 out of 5 stars, highlighting its goal to "celebrate old-school hip-hop".<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.rollingstone.com/music/songreviews/berzerk-20130910 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130918065739/http://www.rollingstone.com/music/songreviews/berzerk-20130910 |url-status=dead |archive-date=September 18, 2013 |title= Eminem's "Berzerk" review on ''Rolling Stone'' |magazine=[[Rolling Stone]] |date=September 10, 2013}}</ref> Robert Copsey of ''Digital Spy'' gave the song 4 out of 5 stars, stating the song "harks back" to Eminem's "Slim Shady days". Copsey compared the song to [[Beastie Boys]] circa 1986, although the song as "inimitable" and "unmistakably Eminem".<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.digitalspy.ca/music/singlesreviews/a511786/eminem-berzerk-single-review.html |title=Eminem: 'Berzerk' – Single review – Music Singles Review – Digital Spy |publisher=Digitalspy.ca |date=September 2, 2013 |access-date=September 8, 2013 |archive-date=September 21, 2013 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130921171727/http://www.digitalspy.ca/music/review/a511786/eminem-berzerk-single-review.html |url-status=live }}</ref> [[VH1]] India gave the song 8/10 concluding, "Whether you shudder under 'Berzerk's' cacophonous assault or wear its raps like an emblem — it's 2013, and Eminem still got your attention."<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.vh1.in/music/reviews/berzerk |title=Berzerk {{!}} Vh1 India<!-- Bot generated title --> |access-date=September 12, 2013 |archive-date=November 18, 2013 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131118150926/http://www.vh1.in/music/reviews/berzerk |url-status=live }}</ref>
