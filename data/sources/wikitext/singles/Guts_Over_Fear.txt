"'''Guts Over Fear'''" is a song by American rapper [[Eminem]] featuring Australian singer-songwriter [[Sia]], from Eminem's compilation album ''[[Shady XV]]'', released on August 25, 2014. This song is included in the closing credits of the film, ''[[The Equalizer (film)|The Equalizer]]''.

==Background==
Previews of the song premiered in trailers and during the end credits for the film, ''[[The Equalizer (film)|The Equalizer]]'' starring [[Denzel Washington]]. The song was officially released to [[iTunes]] on August 25, 2014, the same day Eminem announced his plans to release an album later that year.

==Content==
The song's subject matter concerns Eminem and his struggles as an artist, and touches on moments of his career. Australian singer [[Sia]] provides the chorus, making this the second time the artists have collaborated, the first being "[[Beautiful Pain]]" from the deluxe version of ''[[The Marshall Mathers LP 2]]''.<ref name="Rolling stone">{{cite magazine | title=Hear Eminem and Sia's Triumphant New Duet 'Guts Over Fear' | url=https://www.rollingstone.com/music/news/hear-eminem-and-sias-triumphant-new-duet-guts-over-fear-20140825 | magazine=Rolling stone | access-date=June 26, 2014 | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20171213131100/https://www.rollingstone.com/music/news/hear-eminem-and-sias-triumphant-new-duet-guts-over-fear-20140825 | archive-date=December 13, 2017 | url-status=live}}</ref>

==Music video==
Eminem posted a picture on [[Instagram]] to serve as a sneak peek for the upcoming music video.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/1987432/eminem-guts-over-fear-teaser/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141108022228/http://www.mtv.com/news/1987432/eminem-guts-over-fear-teaser/|url-status=dead|archive-date=November 8, 2014|title=Eminem's 'Guts Over Fear' Video Might Knock You Out: See The Teaser|work=MTV News}}</ref> The video premiered on November 24, via [[VEVO]], was filmed in [[Detroit]], [[Michigan]], directed by Syndrome and starred Alexander Wraith and [[America's Next Top Model (cycle 21)|''America's Next Top Model'' cycle 21 contestant]], [[Winnie Harlow]].

==Chart performance==
The song debuted at number 22 on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]], selling 134,328 copies in its first week of release.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.hiphopdx.com/index/news/id.30530/title.hip-hop-singles-sales-this-week-eminem-iggy-azalea-nicki-minaj|title=Hip Hop Singles Sales This Week: Eminem, Iggy Azalea, Nicki Minaj|website=Hiphopdx.com|access-date=28 December 2017|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141020180917/http://www.hiphopdx.com/index/news/id.30530/title.hip-hop-singles-sales-this-week-eminem-iggy-azalea-nicki-minaj|archive-date=20 October 2014|url-status=dead}}</ref>
