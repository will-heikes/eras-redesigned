"'''You Changed Me'''" is a song by American singer and actor [[Jamie Foxx]] featuring [[Chris Brown]]. It was released on March 12, 2015 as the second single from his fifth studio album ''[[Hollywood: A Story of a Dozen Roses]]'' (2015).

==Music video==

CHRIS BROWN – 'YOU CHANGED ME'|date=May 29, 2015 |work=[[Rap-Up]] |access-date=July 22, 2015}}</ref> An official [[Making-of|behind-the-scenes]] video was released to Foxx's [[Vevo|VEVO]] channel on June 3, 2015.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g8Z4hilILpI|title=Jamie Foxx - You Changed Me (Feat. Chris Brown) BTS|website=[[YouTube]]|date=June 3, 2015 |access-date=July 22, 2015}}</ref> ''[[Empire (2015 TV series)|Empire]]'' actress [[Grace Gealey]] made a [[cameo appearance]] as Foxx's love interest.<ref>[https://web.archive.org/web/20150602094341/http://hitshowstowatch.com/empires-star-grace-gealey-stuns-in-jamie-foxxs-you-changed-me-ft-chris-brown-new-video/] </ref> Rapper [[Alexandra Reid]] from the South Korean Girl-Group [[BP Rania]] also makes a cameo as Chris Brown's love interest.<ref>{{Cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/music/music-news/rania-new-african-american-member-alexandra-demonstrate-6753071/|title = K-Pop Girl Group Rania Adds African-American Member|magazine = [[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|date = 6 November 2015}}</ref>
