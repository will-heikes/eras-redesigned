"'''Little More (Royalty)'''" is a song by American singer [[Chris Brown]] from his seventh studio album ''[[Royalty (Chris Brown album)|''Royalty'']]''.<ref name="iTunes">{{cite web|title=iTunes – Music – Royalty by Chris Brown|url=https://music.apple.com/us/album/royalty/1059790943|publisher=[[iTunes Store]] (US). [[Apple Inc.]]|access-date=November 30, 2015}}</ref> It was [[Hip hop production|produced]] by [[The Audibles]] and [[Poo Bear]].<ref>{{cite web|title=HNHH Premiere! Listen to a new leak off Chris Brown's "Royalty," "Little More."|date=16 December 2015|url=https://www.hotnewhiphop.com/578625-chris-brown-little-more-royalty-new-song|publisher=hotnewhiphop.com}}</ref> The song peaking at number 91 on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]], and number 32 on the US [[Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs]] chart.

==Music video==
===Background and synopsis===
On December 18, 2015, Brown uploaded the music video for "Little More (Royalty)" on his [[YouTube]] and [[Vevo]] account.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IajZTuxdv3Q|title=Chris Brown - Little More (Royalty) [Explicit Version]|publisher=[[YouTube]]|date=December 18, 2015|accessdate=December 21, 2015}}</ref> In the video, Brown wakes up after a night of partying to find a little girl looking around his bedroom.  He winds up acting very confused, but that confusion turns to love. The video shows them playing together, eating, and doing all sorts of fun things.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://hollywoodlife.com/2015/12/18/chris-brown-daughter-royalty-music-video-debut-little-more/ |title=[WATCH] Chris Brown's Daughter Royalty: See Music Video Debut In 'Little More' - Hollywood Life |website=hollywoodlife.com |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151220014541/http://hollywoodlife.com/2015/12/18/chris-brown-daughter-royalty-music-video-debut-little-more/ |archive-date=2015-12-20}} </ref>

===Reception===
Many critics have praised the video saying that Brown shows a more playful side in the video, playing with his little girl, feeding her breakfast, dancing, blowing bubbles and just generally goofing off together.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.celebuzz.com/2015-12-19/chris-brown-daughter-royalty-stars-little-more-music-video/|title = Chris Brown's Daughter Royalty Makes Cameo in His 'Little More' Music Video &#124; Celebuzz|date = 19 December 2015}}</ref>
