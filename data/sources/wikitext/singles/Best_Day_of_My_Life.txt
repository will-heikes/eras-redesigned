"'''Best Day of My Life'''" is a song by American [[pop rock]] band [[American Authors]], released on March 19, 2013, by [[Mercury Records]] and [[Island Records]] as the [[lead single]] from their third [[extended play]], ''American Authors'' (2013), and the second single from their debut studio album, ''[[Oh, What a Life (album)|Oh, What a Life]]'' (2014). The song was written by band members [[Zac Barnett]], Dave Rublin, Matt Sanchez, and James Adam Shelley, along with producers [[Aaron Accetta]] and [[Shep Goodman]].

== Composition ==
The song is written in [[D major]] with a tempo of 96 beats per minute.<ref>{{Cite web |last1=Zachary |first1=Barnett |last2=Adam |first2=Shelley, James |last3=Matthew |first3=Sanchez |last4=David |first4=Rublin |last5=Shep |first5=Goodman |last6=Aaron |first6=Accetta |last7=Authors |first7=American |date=2013-10-07 |title=Best Day of My Life |url=https://www.musicnotes.com/sheetmusic/mtd.asp?ppn=MN0125669 |access-date=2025-04-12 |website=Musicnotes.com |language=en}}</ref>

== Music and lyrics ==
Dave Rublin spoke in a podcast of ''[[Songfacts]]'' where he disclosed that the song was being written when the [[Sandy Hook Elementary School shooting]] happened in 2012. They were inspired to write the song to "make people happy and make people feel positive because that was missing from rock and songwriting, just something so simplistic that can be an earworm, that can carry people."<ref>{{cite web |last1=O'Flanagan |first1=Corey |title=Dave Rublin of American Authors: Songfacts Podcast |url=https://www.songfacts.com/blog/podcast/dave-rublin-of-american-authors |website=[[Songfacts]] |accessdate=19 November 2020}}</ref>

Zac Barnett told an interviewer for ''Blueprint Magazine'', "Recently we have been experimenting with a lot of different instruments including banjo, mandolin, and various percussion toys. Africa-influenced rhythms have been taking more of a prominent role in our songs which has then opened up our ideas on melodies. We're always open to trying new things and we get very excited to learn new instruments and experiment with new sounds. ... "Best Day of My Life" was an idea that James and I had been messing around with. We had the pre-chorus and chorus but once we showed the idea to the rest of the guys they came in and restructured the verses and added the stuttered "life" chorus. Looking back on it, the song changed a lot from the initial idea but that's what I love so much about it. I'll always remember that song being started on acoustic guitars in my tiny Bushwick bedroom, but all of us together as a band really took the song to a completely different place."<ref>{{cite web |url= http://blueprintonline.blogspot.com/2013/02/american-authors.html |author= Dersch, Alexandra |date= 2013 |title= American Authors |publisher=Blueprint Magazine |accessdate= September 12, 2014}}</ref>

Barnett mentions "the stuttered 'life' chorus"—as in "the best day of my li-i-i-i-ife"; the "stuttering" also occurs in a recurrent "whoa-oh-oh-oh-oh-oh-oh"—by which he refers to a lyrical tradition of [[Stuttering in popular culture|stuttering]] in rock music, popularized by [[Buddy Holly]],<ref>{{cite encyclopedia |encyclopedia= Encyclopedia of Music in the 20th Century |author= Valdez, Stephen |editor= Stacy, Lee |year=2013 |publisher= Routledge |location= Abingdon |page= 296 |isbn= 978-1-57958-079-7 |title= Buddy Holly |quote= The Buddy Holly sound was unmistakable. His hiccuping, stuttering vocals...}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url= https://www.bbc.co.uk/music/reviews/f2xm/ |author= Jones, Chris |date= 2009 |title= Buddy Holly, ''The Very Best of Buddy Holly and the Crickets'': Review |publisher= [[BBC]] |access-date= September 12, 2014 |quote= ...topped off with Holly's amazing nasal stutter. Quite literally, nothing had ever sounded like this before.}}</ref> [[David Bowie]] ("[[Changes (David Bowie song)|Changes]]"),<ref>{{cite web |url= http://www.songfacts.com/detail.php?id=3414 |author= |date= |title= Changes by David Bowie |publisher= Song Facts |accessdate= September 12, 2014 |quote= Bowie's stuttered vocals in this song ("Ch-Ch-Changes") are some of the most famous stutters in rock.}}</ref> [[Elton John]] ("[[Bennie and the Jets]]"),<ref>{{cite web |url= http://www.songfacts.com/detail.php?id=1646 |author= |date= |title= Bennie and the Jets by Elton John |publisher= Song Facts |accessdate= September 12, 2014 |quote= It was Elton's idea to stutter the vocal: "B-B-B-Bennie..." Bernie Taupin thought this worked very well with the futuristic, robotic theme of his lyrics.}}</ref> and others.

Bill Lamb, writing for [[Dotdash|About.com]], praised the song's "opening banjo lick" as "a perfect first hook," its "irrepressibly upbeat lyrics," and "a blend of key elements from across a wide range of current successful pop music." He added, "The bass drum heavy backing percussion sounds a little like [[Imagine Dragons]], and backed by a pop-punk style "Whoa Oh" chorus, "Best Day of My Life" takes some of the most distinctive elements of a variety of current pop hit genres and blends them into something new."<ref>{{cite web |url= http://top40.about.com/od/reviews/fl/Review-American-Authors-Best-Day-Of-My-Life.htm |author= Lamb, Bill |date= 2013 |title= Review: American Authors "Best Day Of My Life" |publisher= [[About.com]] |accessdate= September 12, 2014 |archive-url= https://web.archive.org/web/20140325211711/http://top40.about.com/od/reviews/fl/Review-American-Authors-Best-Day-Of-My-Life.htm |archive-date= March 25, 2014 |url-status= dead }}</ref>

The song also uses the [[millennial whoop]].{{citation needed|date=January 2020}}

==Packaging==
The [[album cover|single art]] for the "Best Day of My Life" single features a photograph of the skyline of [[Midtown Manhattan]], with the [[Empire State Building]] in the center of the shot, from [[Brooklyn]] looking north. The photograph is overwritten by the American Authors logo on the majority of the photograph and the words "[http://refinereachltd.com American Authors]", with "Best Day of My Life" written underneath it.

==Commercial performance==
The song received significant airplay on the radio for it to debut at No. 49 in the [[Hot Rock Songs]] and No. 40 in the [[Adult Top 40]] for charts dated October 5, 2013. It began climbing the various rock charts and became a [[Crossover (music)|crossover hit]] on both [[alternative rock]] and top 40 stations, becoming big hits on those formats and peaking at No. 2, No. 18 and No. 4 on the Hot Rock Songs, [[Alternative Songs]], and [[Mainstream Top 40]] charts, and was a sleeper hit.<ref>{{cite web |url= https://music.yahoo.com/blogs/the-new-now/hear-it-first--american-authors-premiere-track-from-debut-studio-album-173528970.html |title=Hear It First! American Authors Premiere Track From Debut Studio Album |author= Geller, Wendy |date=February 26, 2014 |work=Yahoo Music }}</ref> 

It debuted on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] at No. 93 for the chart dated November 30, 2013, and eventually peaked at No. 11 on the charts dated April 19 and May 10, 2014. By March, 2014, it had reached No. 1 on the Adult Top 40. The song reached its first million sales mark in the United States in March 2014,<ref name="USSales">{{cite web|first= Paul |last= Grein |date= March 19, 2014 |title=Chart Watch: "#Selfie" Is A #Smash |url= https://music.yahoo.com/blogs/music-news/chart-watch-selfie-smash-003624707.html |work= Yahoo Music |accessdate= March 30, 2014}}</ref> and its second million in August 2014.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://music.yahoo.com/blogs/chart-watch/chart-watch-nicki-minaj-zooms-to-2-221739201.html |title= Chart Watch: Nicki Minaj Zooms To #2 |author= Grein, Paul |date=August 27, 2014 |work=Yahoo! Music }}</ref> The song was [[RIAA certification|certified 2× Platinum]] on July 16, 2014, by the [[Recording Industry Association of America]] (RIAA).<ref name="RIAA"/> The song was ranked number one on ''Billboard''{{'}}s chart of Adult Pop Songs for 2014.<ref>{{cite web|title=Adult Pop Songs|website=billboard.com |url=http://www.billboard.com/charts/year-end/2014/adult-pop-songs|accessdate=15 December 2014}}</ref> To date, it remains the [[One-hit wonder|only single]] by the band to [[List of one-hit wonders in the United States|break the top 40 in the United States]].{{cn|date=March 2022}}

===Commercials===
In media, the song has been featured in television advertisements for: retail [[home improvement]] store [[Lowe's]] in the United States, which were aired during the [[Super Bowl XLVIII|Super Bowl]],<ref name="ad">{{cite web |url=http://radio.com/2014/02/20/new-music-to-know-american-authors/ |title=New Music to Know: American Authors Are Having the Best Days of Their Lives |date=February 20, 2014 |work=radio.com |access-date=April 5, 2014 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140426084956/http://radio.com/2014/02/20/new-music-to-know-american-authors/ |archive-date=April 26, 2014 |url-status=dead }}</ref> [[automotive industry|automotive]] manufacturer [[Hyundai Motor Company|Hyundai]] in the United Kingdom and France, an alcohol retailer in Australia and [[communications service provider]] [[Spark New Zealand|Telecom New Zealand]] in New Zealand.<ref>{{YouTube|Fyw-q1_lh8A|2013 Telecom NZ Advertisement}}</ref> The song is used in advertisements for [[Petron]], [[Datsun Go]], [[Lay's]], [[Center Parcs UK and Ireland|Center Parcs]] and Graton Casinos. The song was used in commercials for [[Best Western]] hotels. The song was used in a Ford sales event commercial and is used by [[Alaska Communications]] in adverts. In the Philippines, back in 2017, this song is featured in the [[Petron]] commercial as their new jingle.

===Television===
The song has also been used in the ''[[The Vampire Diaries|Vampire Diaries]]'' episode "[[Total Eclipse of the Heart (The Vampire Diaries)|Total Eclipse of the Heart]]".<ref>{{cite web |url= http://vampirediaries.alloyentertainment.com/season-5-episode-13-music-songs-206141/ |author= Theo |date= February 10, 2014 |title= ''The Vampire Diaries'' Music Playlist For Season 5 Episode 13: "Total Eclipse of the Heart" |work= The Vampire Diaries |publisher= [[Alloy Entertainment]] |accessdate= September 12, 2014 |quote= ''Best Day of My Life'' by American Authors: Plays while Katherine (pretending to be Elena) is writing in her doppelganger's diary and rejoicing over how wonderful the world is and how she finally has everything she's ever wanted.}}</ref> The song was covered by [[Blaine Anderson]] ([[Darren Criss]]) and [[Sam Evans]] ([[Chord Overstreet]]) in the 2014 ''[[Glee (TV series)|Glee]]'' episode "[[New New York (Glee)|New New York]]". [[American Authors]] performed the song while in [[The Human Centipede (First Sequence)|Human Centipede]] position on season four premiere of ''[[The Eric Andre Show]]''. The [[BBC]] used the track during their closing montage for their [[2016 United States presidential election]] coverage. An instrumental version of the song is used as the news theme for [[Tegna Inc.|Tegna]]-owned stations.

===Sports===
The song was heard in the opening sequence for [[ESPN]]'s coverage of the 2013 and 2014 [[World Series of Poker]] as well as during their coverage of the [[2013 Little League World Series]]. ''[[NHL on NBC]]'' used this song as a theme song for the [[2014 Stanley Cup Playoffs]]. The Oklahoma City Thunder (NBA) used the song in the 2014–15 pregame activities.

===Video games===
"Best Day of My Life" was part of the ''[[FIFA 14]]'' soundtrack before being removed shortly after the game's release. "Best Day of My Life" also appeared as a playable track for the video game ''[[Guitar Hero Live]]''. "Best Day of My Life" also appeared as a soundtrack in [[Konami]] video game ''[[Pro Evolution Soccer 2015]]'' as the opening track.

===Live===
"Best Day of My Life", was the official song of the 2017 Maltese Labour Party's General Election Campaign, entitled "L-aqwa żmien ta' pajjiżna." (The Best time for our country). "Best Day of My Life" was played at the conclusion of the [[Miss America 2015|2015 Miss America pageant]], during [[Kira Kazantsev]]'s crowning moment. The song was used as a [[fanfare]] to welcome the [[Singapore Armed Forces Parachute Team]] (better known as the ''Red Lions'') during the National Day Celebrations of 2014 in Singapore.

==Music video==
The music video was released on October 18, 2013, via YouTube.<ref>{{YouTube|Y66j_BUCBMY|American Authors – Best Day of my Life (Official Music Video)}}</ref> The video was filmed in [[Brooklyn]], New York. It depicts a man engaging in various activities with a large furry monster, such as drinking in a bar called Sweet & Vicious in [[Nolita]],<ref name= "Fresh">{{cite web |url= http://fresh1027.cbslocal.com/2014/03/12/american-authors-take-us-behind-the-scenes-of-best-day-of-my-life/ |author= Steagall, Cameron |date= March 12, 2014 |title= American Authors Take Us Behind The Scenes Of ''Best Day Of My Life'' |publisher= [[WNEW-FM|Fresh 102.7 FM]] |accessdate= September 12, 2014 |archive-url= https://web.archive.org/web/20171019004509/http://fresh1027.cbslocal.com/2014/03/12/american-authors-take-us-behind-the-scenes-of-best-day-of-my-life/ |archive-date= October 19, 2017 |url-status= dead }}</ref> playing basketball, looking at the [[Brooklyn Bridge]], getting matching tattoos, visiting a strip club, and going to a playground. At the end of the video, it is implied that the monster is his imaginary friend, but then the puzzled bartender finds a photo of the two.

Zac Barnett, in an interview with the Jim & Kim Morning Show ([[WNEW-FM|Fresh 102.7 FM]], New York City), explained that the video starts in Nolita and ends up in Brooklyn, and the journey of the man and his monster pal is the focus of the video. That journey is interweaved with shots of the band playing and singing as they also stroll through New York. "We started in Manhattan and walked across the bridge to the party which was down in [[Dumbo, Brooklyn|Dumbo]]. What it was, we were kind of trying to do a reverse ''[[Where the Wild Things Are]]'' type thing. You know, instead of a human going into a monster dream world, what would it be like if a monster came into our world and experienced New York City and Brooklyn and all of these things. And playing off of that, it was kind of like an alter ego of a dude and these two, together, just showing each other the best days of their lives."<ref name= "Fresh" />

[[Common Sense Media]] reviewed the video for parents: "There's nothing iffy to worry about in the song's upbeat, clean lyrics about having a positive outlook on life and never giving up, but the official music video shows a man and his furry monster friend drinking in a bar, going to a strip club, and getting tattoos. Kids will be more entertained by the adorable dog-version music video for "Best Day of My Life", which shows one lucky bulldog's adoption day in an effort to promote pet adoption."<ref>{{cite web |url= https://www.commonsensemedia.org/music-reviews/best-day-of-my-life-cd-single |author= Nojadera, Amanda |date= 2014 |title= ''Best Day of My Life'' (CD Single) |publisher= [[Common Sense Media]] |accessdate= September 12, 2014 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200224115549/https://www.commonsensemedia.org/music-reviews/best-day-of-my-life-cd-single |archive-date=2020-02-24 |url-status=dead}}</ref>
