"'''Heartbeat Song'''" is a song by [[United States|American]] singer [[Kelly Clarkson]] from her seventh studio album, ''[[Piece by Piece (Kelly Clarkson album)|Piece by Piece]]'' (2015). Written by [[Mitch Allan]], [[Audra Mae]], [[Kara DioGuardi]], and [[Jason Evigan]], the song is an uptempo [[synth-pop]] track produced by [[Greg Kurstin]]. Lyrically, it sings of meeting a person who restores someone's faith in love. Inspired by [[1980s music|1980s]] [[synth-pop]] music, "Heartbeat Song" was released by [[RCA Records]] as the album's lead single on January 12, 2015.

"Heartbeat Song" was released to a positive response from music critics, who complimented the song's production and praised Clarkson's decision to return to mainstream pop music. Other critics, however, noted the strong resemblance the song's melody bore to that of Jimmy Eat World's single "[[The Middle (Jimmy Eat World song)|The Middle]]" (2001), which later{{when|date=October 2017}} allowed the band to be credited as additional songwriters.<ref name="ASCAP 1">{{cite web|title=2016 ASCAP Pop Music Awards|url=http://www.ascap.com/eventsawards/awards/popawards/2016/awards-home|publisher=[[ASCAP]]|date=July 24, 2016}}</ref> Commercially, the single debuted on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] chart at number 37 and has peaked at number 21, marking Clarkson's sixteenth [[top 40]] hit on the chart. It also became her fifth song to top the ''Billboard'' [[Hot Dance Club Songs]] chart and marked as her highest debut among her twenty career entries on the ''Billboard'' [[Adult Top 40]] chart. Internationally, it became a top ten hit single in Austria, Iceland, Poland, Slovenia, South Africa, and the United Kingdom while also becoming a top 20 hit in the national charts of the Czech Republic, Germany, Mexico, Slovakia and Sweden.

Directed by [[Marc Klasfeld]], its accompanying music video features scenes of various downtrodden people and their process of finding love again. Clarkson premiered "Heartbeat Song" in a live televised performance on ''[[The Graham Norton Show]]'' in February 2015 and has performed it on ''[[The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon]]'' and the [[American Idol (season 14)|fourteenth season]] of ''[[American Idol]]''. The song was nominated for [[Grammy Award for Best Pop Solo Performance|Best Pop Solo Performance]] at the [[58th Annual Grammy Awards]].<ref name="Grammy nods">{{cite web|last1=Gage|first1=Jeff|title=DALLAS GETS GRAMMY NODS WITH LEON BRIDGES, KELLY CLARKSON AND MORE|url=http://www.dallasobserver.com/music/dallas-gets-grammy-nods-with-leon-bridges-kelly-clarkson-and-more-7835700|website=Dallas Observer|date=December 7, 2015}}</ref>

==Background and composition==
{{listen|pos=right|filename=Kelly Clarkson - Heartbeat Song.ogg|title="Heartbeat Song" (2015)|description=According to [[Audra Mae]], [[Kara DioGuardi]] came up with the lyric "This is my heartbeat song and I'm gonna play it!", as sung by Clarkson in this 27-second sample of "Heartbeat Song".<ref name="Entertainment Tonight 1" />|format=[[Ogg]]|}}
Shortly after the release of her first Christmas album, ''[[Wrapped in Red]]'' (2013), Clarkson, despite her pregnancy, began to record material for her seventh studio album, with a target release date in 2014.<ref name="WWGP 1" /> One of these tracks, "Heartbeat Song", is an uptempo<ref name="Spin 1" /> [[synthpop]]<ref name="Idolator 2" /> track produced by [[Greg Kurstin]].,<ref name="Billboard 1" /> Lyrically, the song sings of meeting a person who restores someone's faith in love.<ref name="Rolling Stone 1" /> Sonically, ''[[USA Today]]''{{'}}s Brian Mansfeld remarked that the record serves as sonic [[defibrillator]], starting with a jolt of melody, its rhythms shifting a couple times before finally settling into its steady pulse;<ref name="USA Today 1" /> while [[Idolator (website)|Idolator]]'s Bradley Stern described its production as [[Top 40]] radio-friendly and remarked that the track sees Clarkson's pulse raising while falling in love above kicky [[drum]]s, [[guitar]]s and hints of [[synthesizer|synths]].

"Heartbeat Song" was written by [[Mitch Allan]], [[Audra Mae]], [[Kara DioGuardi]], and [[Jason Evigan]] in a two-day session writing camp.<ref name="Entertainment Tonight 1" /> In an interview on ''[[Entertainment Tonight]]'', Mae revealed that DioGuardi came up with the main idea and used it as the song's chorus, with everybody else contributing in.<ref name="Entertainment Tonight 1" /> After pitching the song to different artists, including [[Demi Lovato]],<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.apra.com.au/cms/worksearch/worksearch.srvlt?action=workSearch#|title=Heartbeat Song – APRA ID: GW45867329|work=[[Australasian Performing Right Association|APRA]]|access-date=March 25, 2016|archive-date=November 17, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151117020448/https://www.apra.com.au/cms/worksearch/worksearch.srvlt?action=workSearch|url-status=dead}}</ref> she later found out during the Christmas season that Clarkson had recorded it and planned to release the song as the album's [[lead single]].<ref name="Entertainment Tonight 1" />

Musically, the song is written in the key of [[G-flat major]] with a fast [[tempo]] of 149 [[beats per minute]].  It follows the chord progression G{{flat}}–B{{flat}}<small>m7</small>–E{{flat}}m–C{{flat}}, and Clarkson's vocals span from G{{flat}}<sub>3</sub> to E{{flat}}<sub>5</sub>.<ref>{{Cite web|title = Kelly Clarkson "Heartbeat Song" Sheet Music - Download & Print|url = http://www.musicnotes.com/sheetmusic/mtd.asp?ppn=MN0145479|website = www.musicnotes.com|access-date = 2015-08-03|first = Clarkson|last = Kelly|date = 2015-01-16}}</ref>

==Release and reception==
In January 2015, Clarkson revealed a set of clues for the single, posting the words "Proof of life and [[Dr. Dre]] #[[The Next Episode|TheNextEpisode]] (''[[sic]]'')".<ref name="Daily Mirror 1"/> On January 7, 2015, she announced January 12, 2015, as its release date as a [[Music download|digital download]] by [[RCA Records]],<ref name="Amazon.com 1" /><ref name="Idolator 1"/><ref name="MTV 1" /> followed by a release to [[Contemporary hit radio|Mainstream Top 40]] radio stations in the United States on January 13, 2015.<ref name="FMQB 1" /> A [[CD single]] was also issued by RCA's parent company [[Sony Music Entertainment]] on March 6, 2015.<ref name="Amazon.com 4" />

===Critical response===
"Heartbeat Song" garnered positive reviews from music critics, with Clarkson's return to mainstream pop music lauded, along with her vocals.<ref name="Billboard 1" /><ref name="Spin 2" /> ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'''s associate editor Jason Lipshutz gave the song 4/5 stars, writing the song's production and vocals sounded invigorated.<ref name="Billboard 1" /> Reviewing for ''[[Rolling Stone]]'', Jon Dylan gave the song a three-and-a-half star rating, describing it as a "warm, fuzzy snuggle-fest."<ref name="Rolling Stone 2" /> In his midweek review for ''[[Spin (magazine)|Spin]]'', assistant editor Brennan Carley wrote that the song is "a Kurstin production that sparkles with new-found passion" and said Clarkson sings a lyric of the chorus with a bubblegum snap.<ref name="Spin 2" /> In his review for music website Popdust.com, Jason Scott gave the song an "A−" grade, saying that song would fit quite nicely to Clarkson's previous studio album ''[[All I Ever Wanted (album)|All I Ever Wanted]]'' (2009).<ref name="Popdust.com 1" /> He also added that despite the song's the chorus isn't quite as "tasty" as what she's capable of, it's just as momentous as her previous singles such as "[[Mr. Know It All (Kelly Clarkson song)|Mr. Know It All]]" (2011) and "[[Breakaway (Kelly Clarkson song)|Breakaway]]" (2004).<ref name="Popdust.com 1" /> Reviewing for ''[[Time (magazine)|Time]]'', Nolan Feeney wrote that "Heartbeat Song" as more of a celebration of that than a hungry comeback, reminding listeners of what Clarkson has so reliably provided over the years, with just a few souped-up adjustments.<ref name="Time 1" /> He also praised Kurstin's production and Clarkson's "arena-ready pipes", describing the song as "slick, sugary and incredibly catchy — everything you want in a pop song, really".<ref name="Time 1" /> The song has been nominated for the Choice Music Single: Female Artist category for the [[2015 Teen Choice Awards]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.teenchoice.com/article/wave-1-nominees|title=Wave 1 Nominees!|work=Teen Choice on FOX|access-date=2015-06-09|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160414154711/http://www.teenchoice.com/article/wave-1-nominees|archive-date=2016-04-14|url-status=dead}}</ref>

Critics also compared "Heartbeat Song" to [[Jimmy Eat World]]'s single "[[The Middle (Jimmy Eat World song)|The Middle]]" (2001) due to a notable similarity between the melodies of the songs' verses, as well as their choruses.<ref name="Time 2" /> In his review for Idolator, Stern observed both songs' similitude.<ref name="Idolator 3" /> Hunter Hauk of ''[[The Dallas Morning News]]'' also remarked of the two songs' similarities, but was ambivalent of "Heartbeat Song"'s lack of innovation as compared to Clarkson's previous lead singles.<ref name="Dallas Morning News 1" /> Digital Spy rated this song with 2 stars out of 5 and concluded by describing it: "ruthless in its pursuit of earworm status, and like it or not it'll find itself lodged in the inner vestibules of your brain.<ref>[http://www.digitalspy.co.uk/music/feature/a630459/this-weeks-new-singles-reviewed-madonna-george-ezra-nick-jonas.html Review by Digital Spy]. Retrieved 22 February 2015</ref>

===Chart performance===
On the chart week ending January 31, 2015, "Heartbeat Song" debuted on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] chart at number 37 with 70,000 copies of digital downloads, becoming her 16th top 40 hit on the chart.<ref name="Billboard 2" /> It also marked as her third highest debut among her 25 career entries, following "[[Never Again (Kelly Clarkson song)|Never Again]]", and "Mr. Know It All", which debuted at number 8 and number 18 respectively.<ref name="Billboard 2" /> On the chart week ending February 21, 2015, it moved to a peak of number 21.<ref name="Billboard 4" /> The song also debuted on other ''Billboard'' charts, including the ''Billboard'' [[Mainstream Top 40]] chart, the ''Billboard'' [[Adult Contemporary (chart)|Adult Contemporary]] chart, the Billboard [[Billboard Twitter Real-Time|Trending 140]] and the ''Billboard'' [[Adult Top 40]] chart, where it entered at number 20, marking a career-high for Clarkson.<ref name="Billboard 2" /> On July 23, 2015, "Heartbeat Song" was certified Platinum by RIAA for selling a million copies.<ref>https://www.riaa.com/goldandplatinumdata.php?artist=%22Heartbeat+Song%22 {{Dead link|date=February 2022}}</ref> In Canada, "Heartbeat Song" debuted on the ''Billboard'' [[Canadian Hot 100]] chart at number 37 on the chart week ending January 31, 2015.<ref name="Nielsen 1" /> In Australia, it entered the [[ARIA Charts|ARIA Singles Chart]] at number 51 on the chart week ending January 26, 2015.<ref name="Chart – AU 1" /> In the United Kingdom, the song debuted at number 7 on the [[UK Singles Chart|Official UK Singles Chart]], becoming Clarkson's ninth song to reach the chart's top 10.<ref name="Chart – UK 1" /> As of April 24, 2016 is sold over 362,000 copies in the United Kingdom.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Myers |first=Justin |date=October 24, 2017 |title=Kelly Clarkson's Top 10 biggest hits on the Official Chart |url=http://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/kelly-clarksons-official-top-10-biggest-songs-revealed__14576/ |website=[[Official Charts Company|Official Charts]]}}</ref> In addition, "Heartbeat Song" had attained a chart position in the national charts of Austria, Germany, Spain, and Sweden; and has also charted in the airplay charts of Czech Republic, Denmark, the Netherlands, Slovakia.<ref name="Chart – NL 1" />

As of September 2017, the song has sold over 909,000 copies in the US.<ref name="us sales">{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/ask-billboard-kelly-clarksons-career-sales-wild-thoughts-about/ |title=Ask Billboard: Kelly Clarkson's Career Sales & 'Wild Thoughts' About Rihanna |first=Gary |last=Trust |magazine=Billboard |publisher=Prometheus Global Media |date=September 10, 2017 |access-date=September 10, 2017}}</ref>

===Awards and nominations===
At the [[2015 Teen Choice Awards]], "Heartbeat Song" was nominated for Choice Music Single: Female Artist. For the [[58th Annual Grammy Awards]] the song is nominated for [[Best Pop Solo Performance]].<ref name="Grammy nods" /> In 2016 Audra Mae won ASCAP Pop Music Award for co-writing the song.<ref>{{Cite web | url=http://newsok.com/article/5494932 | title=Audra Mae wins ASCAP Pop Music Award for Kelly Clarkson's 'Heartbeat Song'| date=2016-04-29}}</ref> In May 2016, the song won at the [[BMI Awards|64th Annual BMI Pop Awards]].<ref>{{Cite web |date=May 11, 2016 |title=BMI Honors Taylor Swift and Legendary Songwriting Duo Mann & Weil at the 64th Annual BMI Pop Awards |url=http://www.bmi.com/news/entry/2016_bmi_pop_awards |website=[[Broadcast Music, Inc.]]}}</ref>

==Music video==
On January 26, 2015, RCA released an animated lyric video for "Heartbeat Song", featuring heartbeat graphs of Clarkson and three other radio guests in Nashville using an [[electrocardiography|ECG]] monitor.<ref name="NRJ Germany 1" /><ref name="E Online 1" /> Directed by [[Marc Klasfeld]], its accompanying music video premiered on February 5, 2015, on [[Vevo]].<ref name="Billboard 3" />  It was filmed in [[Nashville, Tennessee]].

===Synopsis===
The video depicts scenes of various heartbroken people unexpectedly finding a chance to love again.<ref name="Time 2" /><ref name="Yahoo 1" /> These scenes are inter-cut with footage of Clarkson performing the track in a colorful background.<ref name="MTV 2" /><ref name="Spin 3" /> Upon its premiere, the video had received a generally positive response, with mild criticism targeting a scene depicting a couple kissing through the opposite seating sides of a bench.<ref name="Time 2" /><ref name="MTV 2" /> In his coverage for ''Spin'', Carley remarked that there are some surprisingly deep moments tracing the journey from loss to happiness in the video.<ref name="Spin 3" /> Reviewing for [[Yahoo! Music]], senior editor Wendy Geller wrote that despite the video's concept may sound hokey, but the idea that life can change from dark to light in an instant (couple with Clarkson's buoyant vocals) is irresistible.<ref name="Yahoo 1" />

==Live performances==
Clarkson premiered "Heartbeat Song" in a live televised performance on the British television programs ''[[The Graham Norton Show]]'' on February 20, 2015, and ''[[Loose Women]]'' on March 2, 2015.<ref name="British Comedy Guide 1" /> She also performed the song on ''[[The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon]]'' on March 2, 2015, ''[[Good Morning America]]'' on March 3, 2015, [[2nd iHeartRadio Music Awards|iHeartRadio Music Awards]] on March 29, 2015, and on ''[[The Ellen DeGeneres Show]]'', on April 3, 2015. On April 2, 2015, Clarkson performed the song on the [[American Idol (season 14)|fourteenth season]] of ''[[American Idol]]'' where she was also the guest mentor for the top 8 as part of the "Evening with Kelly Clarkson" week. Clarkson performed the song in a medley with "[[Since U Been Gone]]" and "[[Stronger (What Doesn't Kill You)]]" on the [[La Voix (season 3)|season three]] finale of ''[[La Voix]]'' in Montreal on April 12, 2015. Additionally, Clarkson performed the song at the Summertime Ball hosted by Capital FM on June 6, 2015. In July 2015, Clarkson performed the song on ''[[Macy's 4th of July Fireworks|Macy's 4th of July Spectacular]]'' on [[NBC]].
