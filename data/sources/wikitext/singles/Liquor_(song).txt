"'''Liquor'''" is a song by American singer [[Chris Brown]], from his seventh studio album ''[[Royalty (Chris Brown album)|Royalty]]''. It was released as the album's [[lead single]] on June 26, 2015, by [[RCA Records]].<ref name="iTunes">{{cite web|url=https://itunes.apple.com/us/album/liquor-single/id1012629075|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150725104253/https://itunes.apple.com/us/album/liquor-single/id1012629075|url-status=dead|archive-date=July 25, 2015|title=Liquor - Single by Chris Brown|publisher=[[iTunes Store]] (US)|access-date=July 24, 2015}}</ref>

The song was written by Chris Brown along with [[Tone Stith]], Jordan Evan, [[Matthew Samuels]] and Omega Shelton, while its production was handled by Stith and The Aquarius. "Liquor" is an [[alternative R&B]] [[slow jam]], with lyrics about Brown being secretly drugged by a woman, with the effects of the drug increasing his sexual desires towards her. The single was certified Platinum by the [[Recording Industry Association of America]] (RIAA).

Its music video was released on September 22, 2015, and is the first part of the [[Royalty (Chris Brown album)#Music video series|''Royalty'' music videos]] storyline, being connected in a two part music video with the following single from the album, "[[Zero (Chris Brown song)|Zero]]". The videoclip follows the lyrical content of the song, with Brown being sneakily drugged by a woman, and later taken to her house. The video garnered positive reception from critics, for its psychedelic and cinematic visuals. Brown performed "Liquor" at the [[BET Awards 2015|2015 BET Awards]], [[2016 iHeartRadio Music Awards]], and included it on all the setlists of his tours following its release.

==Background and composition==
On June 25, 2015, ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' revealed that Brown's seventh studio album would be released in the fall of that year.<ref name="Billboard">{{cite web|url=https://www.billboard.com/music/rb-hip-hop/chris-brown-new-album-fall-2015-release-exclusive-6612587/|title=Chris Brown to Release New Album in Fall: Exclusive|publisher=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|date=June 25, 2015|access-date=August 7, 2015}}</ref> In the same interview with ''Billboard'', Brown stated that "Liquor" would be included in the album's track list.<ref name="Billboard" /> The song was premiered on June 25,<ref name="Billboard" /> and was released for [[Music download|digital download]] the next day.<ref name="iTunes" />

The song was written by [[Chris Brown]], [[Tone Stith]], Jordan Evans, [[Boi-1da|Matthew Samuels]] and Omega Shelton, and was produced by Tone Stith and The Aquarius.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.chartsinfrance.net/Chris-Brown/news-98963.html|title=Clip de "Zero" : Chris Brown danse pour panser ses blessures|website=Chartsinfrance.net|access-date=24 May 2023}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.hotnewhiphop.com/505885-chris-brown-liquor-zero-video-new-video|title=Chris Brown "Liquor/Zero" Video|website=Hothewhiphop.com|date=22 September 2015 }}</ref> "Liquor" is an [[alternative R&B]] [[slow jam]],<ref>{{cite magazine | url=https://www.billboard.com/music/rb-hip-hop/chris-brown-slow-jams-7587936/ | title=Chris Brown's 12 Sexiest Slow Jams | magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]] }}</ref> containing elements of [[psychedelic music|psychedelic]] and [[soul music]]. ''[[PopCrush]]'' described it as a "synthy organ-driven track".<ref>{{cite web | url=https://popcrush.com/chris-brown-gets-buzzed-and-freaky-on-new-song-liquor/ | title=Chris Brown Gets Buzzed + Freaky on New Song 'Liquor' | date=27 June 2015 }}</ref> In the song's lyrics, Brown starts to feel very intoxicated, noticing that a girl has secretly drugged his drink, with the effects of the drug increasing his sexual desires towards her, as he sings: “There's something in this liquor / The air is getting thicker / All I want is you”.<ref>{{Cite web | title=Liquor by Chris Brown - Songfacts | url=https://www.songfacts.com/facts/chris-brown/liquor | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201202152814/https://www.songfacts.com/facts/chris-brown/liquor | access-date=2025-09-07 | archive-date=2020-12-02}}</ref><ref>{{cite web | url=https://www.vibe.com/music/music-news/chris-brown-liquor-357601/ | title=Chris Brown Has a Little Too Much to Drink on New Single 'Liquor' | date=26 June 2015 }}</ref>

==Live performances==
"Liquor" was performed live by Chris Brown at the [[BET Awards 2015]].<ref name="liqlive">{{cite web | url=https://www.nydailynews.com/2015/06/28/chris-brown-tyga-take-stage-at-bet-awards-rihanna-reacts-to-performance/ | title=Chris Brown, Tyga take stage at BET Awards, Rihanna reacts to performance | website=[[New York Daily News]] | date=28 June 2015 }}</ref> The performance started with the singer sitting at a piano, singing "Liquor" as a woman dressed in all-white danced behind him.<ref>{{cite web | url=https://m.imdb.com/news/ni58744446/ | title=Watch: Chris Brown Performs "Liquor" at Bet Awards (Video) | website=[[IMDb]] }}</ref><ref>{{cite web | url=https://theboombox.com/chris-brown-tyga-omarion-jhene-aiko-perform-2015-bet-awards/ | title=Chris Brown Performs 'Liquor,' 'Ayo' with Tyga and 'Posed to Be' with Omarion at 2015 BET Awards &#91;VIDEO&#93; | date=29 June 2015 }}</ref> Brown also performed the song at the [[2016 iHeartRadio Music Awards]].<ref>{{cite web | url=https://www.xxlmag.com/chris-brown-liquor-back-to-sleep-2016-iheart-radio-music-awards/ | title=Chris Brown Performs "Liquor," "Back to Sleep" and More at 2016 iHeart Radio Music Awards - XXL | date=4 April 2016 }}</ref> The song was included on all the setlists of Brown's tours following its release.

===Synopsis===
The “Liquor” part of the video begins with Brown, alone at a [[dive bar]] named "Golden Gopher", drinking to get away from his sorrows. He's then offered a drink from a lady in a black dress, that he accepts, not knowing that she had sneakily put a drug in the glass. Brown drinks the drugged liquor, then 
he immediately feels dizzy and his vision gets hazy, as "Liquor" starts to play. The girl then takes a taxi with Brown that brings them to her house, and the singer, being under the influence of the drug, sees everything around him in a hallucinated way. The "Liquor" part of the video ends with Brown being dropped off by a taxi to another woman’s apartment.<ref>{{Cite web | title=Chris Brown Drops Epic Nine-Minute Video for ‘Liquor’ and ‘Zero’ « Radio.com | url=http://radio.com/2015/09/22/chris-brown-video-liquor-zero/ | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150923085258/http://radio.com/2015/09/22/chris-brown-video-liquor-zero/ | archive-date=2015-09-23 | access-date=2025-09-07}}</ref><ref>{{cite magazine | url=https://www.billboard.com/music/rb-hip-hop/chris-brown-drugged-dumped-liquor-zero-music-videos-6706875/ | title=Chris Brown Gets Drugged & Dumped in 'Liquor/Zero' Double Video | magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]] }}</ref><ref>{{cite web | url=https://m.independent.ie/entertainment/music/music-news/watch-chris-browns-drink-is-spiked-in-new-video-for-liquorzero/31552433.html | title=WATCH: Chris Brown's drink is spiked in new video for Liquor/Zero | date=23 September 2015 }}</ref>

===Reception===
According to ''[[HotNewHipHop]]'', the "Liquor" video is "filled with dancing, drinking, psychedelia, and intrigue".<ref>{{cite web | url=https://www.hotnewhiphop.com/505885-chris-brown-liquor-zero-video-new-video | title=Chris Brown "Liquor/Zero" Video | website=HNHH | date=22 September 2015 }}</ref> ''[[Irish Independent|The Irish Independent]]'' defined it as "psychedelic".<ref>{{cite web | url=https://m.independent.ie/entertainment/music/music-news/watch-chris-browns-drink-is-spiked-in-new-video-for-liquorzero/31552433.html | title=WATCH: Chris Brown's drink is spiked in new video for Liquor/Zero | date=23 September 2015 }}</ref> ''[[Hypebeast (company)|HypeBeast]]'' praised it as "a vibrant, psychedelic and cinematic outing", noting that it "displays a Chris Brown we’re not too accustomed to seeing".<ref>{{cite web | url=https://hypebeast.com/2018/9/chris-brown-releases-video-for-singles-liquor-zero | title=Chris Brown Releases Video for Singles "Liquor" & "Zero" | date=22 September 2015 }}</ref> ''Soul Bounce'' praised its visuals and Brown's "dynamic" and "discombobulated dance moves".<ref>{{cite web | url=https://soulbounce.com/2015/09/chris-brown-debuts-dual-video-for-liquor-and-zero/ | title=Chris Brown Debuts Dual Video for 'Liquor' & 'Zero' | date=22 September 2015 }}</ref>

==Chart performance==
"Liquor" peaked at number 60 on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]], spending a total of 17 weeks on the chart. The song was certified Platinum by the [[Recording Industry Association of America]] (RIAA), denoting sales of 1,000,000 copies. In the United Kingdom, "Liquor" peaked at number 82 on the [[UK Singles Chart]].
