"'''Wonderland'''" is a song by the American singer-songwriter [[Taylor Swift]] from the deluxe version of her fifth studio album, ''[[1989 (album)|1989]]'' (2014). Swift wrote it with the Swedish record producers [[Max Martin]] and [[Shellback (record producer)|Shellback]], who both produced the track. It was released for [[music download|download]] via the [[iTunes Store]] on February 17, 2015, by [[Big Machine Records]]. An [[electropop]] song, "Wonderland" takes inspiration from the children's novel ''[[Alice in Wonderland]]'' (1865) to describe an intense relationship, referencing characters from the book. Music critics praised the songwriting as witty, and several picked it as an underrated track from Swift's discography.

"Wonderland" charted in Australia, Canada, and the United States. Following a [[Taylor Swift masters dispute|2019 dispute over the masters of her first six albums]], Swift re-recorded the song as "'''Wonderland (Taylor's Version)'''", and released it as part of her fourth re-recorded album, ''[[1989 (Taylor's Version)]]'' (2023). The re-recorded song reached number 32 on the [[Billboard Global 200|''Billboard'' Global 200]] and charted in Australia, the United States, New Zealand, and Canada. Swift occasionally performed "Wonderland" live on her concert tours.

== Music and lyrics ==
Both the original and re-recorded versions of "Wonderland" are four minutes and five seconds long.<ref name="LinerNotes" /><ref name="TaylorsVersion" /> An [[electropop]] song, "Wonderland" is instrumented by [[synthesizers]], heavy [[Programming (music)|programmed]] [[drum machines]], [[syncopated]] percussion, and a distorted and filtered [[synth bass]].{{Sfn|Somville|Benoit|2025|p=230}} Lyrically, the song takes inspiration from the children's novel ''[[Alice in Wonderland]]'' (1865). Swift uses "falling down the rabbit hole" as a metaphor for falling in love and going insane in the process, while the line "haven't you heard what becomes of curious minds?" references Alice's observation that "curiosity often leads to trouble".<ref>{{Cite web |last=Ahlgrim |first=Callie |date=April 17, 2024 |title=20 Taylor Swift songs with literary references you may have missed |url=https://www.businessinsider.com/taylor-swift-lyrics-literary-references-easter-eggs-details-2023-5 |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240920043241/https://www.businessinsider.com/taylor-swift-lyrics-literary-references-easter-eggs-details-2023-5 |archive-date=September 20, 2024 |access-date=2024-09-25 |website=[[Business Insider]] |language=en-US}}</ref> The line "flash your green eyes" is also a reference to a character in the book, the [[Cheshire Cat]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Keaney |first=Quinn |date=2014-10-27 |title=Is "Wonderland" About Harry Styles, Too? Taylor Swift's Lyrics Describe Someone With Green Eyes ... |url=https://www.bustle.com/articles/46056-is-wonderland-about-harry-styles-too-taylor-swifts-lyrics-describe-someone-with-green-eyes |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240925042304/https://www.bustle.com/articles/46056-is-wonderland-about-harry-styles-too-taylor-swifts-lyrics-describe-someone-with-green-eyes |archive-date=2024-09-25 |access-date=2024-09-25 |website=[[Bustle (magazine)|Bustle]] |language=en}}</ref> The song is about a woman, mad in love, who follows a lover into what is supposedly a safe place, referred to in-song as "Wonderland". Both lovers rush into the relationship without thinking twice, consumed by erotic desire.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Rusciano |first=Aly |date=2021-09-02 |title=When Taylor Swift Fell Down Lewis Carroll's Rabbit Hole |url=https://www.popmatters.com/taylor-swift-lewis-carroll |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240925041644/https://www.popmatters.com/taylor-swift-lewis-carroll |archive-date=2024-09-25 |access-date=2024-09-25 |website=[[PopMatters]] |language=en-US}}</ref> The first draft for the song contained more references to ''Alice in Wonderland'', with the male lover proclaiming them king and queen.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Bellusci |first=Alexandra |date=2023-10-27 |title=Taylor Swift Reveals Original Lyrics for ''1989''{{'s}} 'New Romantics' and 'Wonderland' |url=https://www.eonline.com/news/1388829/taylor-swift-reveals-original-lyrics-for-1989s-new-romantics-and-wonderland |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20231112232421/https://www.eonline.com/news/1388829/taylor-swift-reveals-original-lyrics-for-1989s-new-romantics-and-wonderland |archive-date=2023-11-12 |access-date=2025-01-22 |website=[[E! == Commercial performance ==
"Wonderland" peaked at number 51 on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] chart on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]],<ref name="Billboardhot100" /> number 59 on the [[Canadian Hot 100]],<ref name="Canada" /> and number 171 on the [[UK singles chart]]. The track received a platinum [[Music recording certification|certification]] in Australia and a silver certification in the United Kingdom.<ref name="aria"/><ref name="bpi"/> The re-recorded song, "Wonderland (Taylor's Version)", charted in the United States (33),<ref name="Billboardhot100" /> Canada (37),<ref name="Canada2023"/> New Zealand (36),<ref name="NZ"/> and on the ''Billboard'' Global 200 (32).<ref name="Billboardglobal200"/> The re-recorded version received a gold certification in Brazil.<ref name="brazilTV"/>
