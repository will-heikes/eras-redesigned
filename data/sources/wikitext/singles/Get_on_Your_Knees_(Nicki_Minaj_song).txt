"'''Get on Your Knees'''" is a song by  Trinidadian rapper [[Nicki Minaj]] featuring American singer and longtime friend [[Ariana Grande]] from Minaj's third album ''[[The Pinkprint]]'' (2014). The song managed to enter single charts in multiple countries due to strong digital sales following the album's release.

== Background and release ==
"Get on Your Knees" is a [[Trap music|trap]]-[[Pop music|pop]] song with Nicki Minaj rapping the verses and Ariana Grande singing the chorus and bridge. Lyrically the song depict themes of asserting sexual dominance and embracing empowerment.<ref name="time.com">{{Cite magazine |date=2014-12-17 |title=Review: Nicki Minaj Reinvents Herself on Personal The Pinkprint |url=https://time.com/3637599/nicki-minaj-the-pinkprint-album-review/ |access-date=2024-02-03 |magazine=TIME |language=en}}</ref> [[Katy Perry]] being one of the writers of the song fueled rumors about her being featured on the song, but the final track featured Ariana Grande instead.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2014-12-01 |title=Nicki Minaj nega parceria com Katy Perry: "Get On Your Knees" terá vocais de Ariana Grande |url=https://www.vagalume.com.br/news/2014/12/01/nicki-minaj-nega-parceria-com-katy-perry-get-on-your-knees-tera-vocais-de-ariana-grande.html |access-date=2024-02-03 |website=Vagalume |language=pt-br}}</ref> In an interview by ''Fashion & Style'', Nicki Minaj herself has described Ariana Grande's delivery on the song as "very, very, sexy".<ref>{{Cite web |last=Ishler |first=Julianne |date=2014-12-23 |title=Nicki Minaj Calls Ariana Grande A 'Freak' After Their Pinkprint Duet |url=https://hollywoodlife.com/2014/12/23/nicki-minaj-ariana-grande-inner-freak-get-on-your-knees-song/ |access-date=2024-02-03 |website=Hollywood Life |language=en-US}}</ref>

"Get on Your Knees" leaked on 11 December 2014, just a few days prior to being released as a part of Nicki Minaj's third studio album ''The Pinkprint'' on 15 December 2014.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2014-12-11 |title=Nicki Minaj's New Songs With Beyoncé and Ariana Grande Leak! Ouça nova música de Ariana Grande e Nicki Minaj, "Get on your knees" – Jovem Pan |language=pt-BR}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Crews |date=2014-12-12 |title=New Music: Nicki Minaj 'Get On Your Knees' [feat. The song was performed live by Nicki Minaj and Ariana Grande on 7 April 2016 at T-Mobile Arena's grand opening concert in [[Las Vegas]], where they also performed their first collaboration "[[Bang Bang (Jessie J, Ariana Grande and Nicki Minaj song)|Bang Bang]]".<ref>{{Cite web |last=Grebey |first=James |date=8 April 2016 |title=Ariana Grande Premieres New Song and Performs 'Get On Your Knees' Live With Nicki Minaj |url=https://www.spin.com/2016/04/ariana-grande-nicki-minaj-new-song-get-on-your-knees-live-watch/ |access-date=3 February 2024 |website=SPIN}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |date=2016-04-08 |title=Nicki Minaj & Ariana Grande Perform "Get On Your Knees" & "Bang Bang" in Vegas |url=https://news.yahoo.com/nicki-minaj-ariana-grande-perform-230427885.html |access-date=2024-02-03 |website=Yahoo News |language=zh-Hant-TW}}</ref>

== Reception ==
"Get on Your Knees" was received positively by critics, with many applauding the song's production and its explicit and empowering lyrics. In an album review, Muhammad Tariq from ''[[The California Aggie]]'' describes the song as "the empowering, boost of personal-confidence and gym anthem", noting the explicit lyrics with messages of "confidence, self-love and recognizing our self-worth".<ref>{{Cite web |last=theaggie |date=2021-06-01 |title=An analysis of Nicki Minaj's iconic "The Pinkprint" |url=https://theaggie.org/2021/06/01/an-analysis-of-nicki-minajs-iconic-the-pinkprint/ |access-date=2024-02-03 |website=The Aggie |language=en-US}}</ref> Kim Osorio from [[NPR Music|NPR]] commented that Nicki's verses on the song "can be a bit too much to stomach" but that the "beats and the catchy choruses will keep you sucking on that crackpipe".<ref>{{Cite web |last=Osorio |first=Kim |date=18 December 2014 |title=On 'The Pinkprint,' Nicki Minaj Speaks The Language Of Love [[Time (magazine)|''Time'']] calls the song's production "disarmingly pretty", observing how Minaj and Grande explore their prowess in both expressing their sexuality and showcasing their exceptional vocal abilities in the song.<ref name="time.com"/> Randall Roberts from the ''[[Los Angeles Times]]'' delineates Minaj as a "hardened lover uninterested in emotion" in the song.<ref>{{Cite web highlighted how the song exemplifies the producer [[Dr. Luke]]'s "formulaic pop magic".<ref>{{Cite news |last1=Westhoff |first1=Ben |last2=Holpuch |first2=Amanda |last3=Mokoena |first3=Tshepo
