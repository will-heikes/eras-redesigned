"'''Chains'''" is a song by American singer [[Nick Jonas]], taken from [[Nick Jonas (album)|his eponymous second album]]. It was released on July 30, 2014 by [[Island Records|Island]] and [[Republic Records]] as the [[lead single]] from the album. The song was written and produced by [[Jason Evigan]], with additional songwriting provided by [[Ammar Malik]], and [[Danny Parker (songwriter)|Danny Parker]]. It is an [[Contemporary R&B|R&B]] song, using kick drum-driven beats, a splash of bass-dropping trap at the intro and bridge as the song's instrumentation. Lyrically, the song talks about feeling trapped in a binding and hopeless love.

Considered a departure from his music with the [[Jonas Brothers]] and [[Nick Jonas & the Administration]], the song was praised by music critics for being an adult song, with his vocals and its production being praised. American singers [[Justin Timberlake]] and [[Miguel (singer)|Miguel]] were influences for the song, as noted by some critics. The song's accompanying music video was directed by Ryan Pallotta and was released on July 30, 2014.

After the success of "Jealous" at radio stations in the US, Island and Republic re-released "Chains" as Jonas' next single. ==Reception==
The song received generally favorable reviews from music critics, who praised Jonas' new musical style and the song's production. Jason Lipshut of ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' was positive, declaring that the singer was "desperately trying to position himself as an adult artist, and soundly succeeding."<ref name=billboardreview/> Mike Wass of ''[[Idolator (website)|Idolator]]'' compared Jonas to [[Justin Timberlake]], writing that "it's a step in the right direction but no '[[Like I Love You]] [Timberlake's debut single]."<ref name=idolatorreview/> Emily Blake wrote for ''[[MTV News]]'' that "Chains" is "drenched in [[Sadomasochism|S&M]] and rather adult themes straight out of '[[Fifty Shades of Grey]]'."<ref name="mtv2">{{cite web|last=Blake|first=Emily|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/1878234/nick-jonas-new-song-chains-premiere/|title=Nick Jonas Sounds Like He's Been Reading 'Fifty Shades Of Grey' On New Song 'Chains' - MTV|publisher=MTV News|date=July 24, 2014|access-date=2014-07-25|archive-date=2014-09-15|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140915072633/http://www.mtv.com/news/1878234/nick-jonas-new-song-chains-premiere/|url-status=dead}}</ref> Joey DeGroot of ''Music Times'' was ambivalent toward its lyrical content, calling it "the song's weakest point", but praised "the production and Jonas' vocal performance", claiming that they were "more than make up for it, [...] earn[ing] Jonas some artistic credibility after toiling in the Disney-pop world for most of his career."<ref name=musictimes>{{cite news|last1=DeGroot|first1=Joey|title=Nick Jonas Shares Solo Single 'Chains' [LISTEN]|url=http://www.musictimes.com/articles/7958/20140724/nick-jonas-shares-solo-single-chains-listen.htm|accessdate=November 9, 2014|work=Music Times|date=July 24, 2014|archive-date=October 8, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141008023435/http://www.musictimes.com/articles/7958/20140724/nick-jonas-shares-solo-single-chains-listen.htm|url-status=live}}</ref> Dylan Mial of ''[[Blogcritics]]'' was complimentary with the song, calling it "by far the greatest, most inspired and powerful track of 2014 – it is pure musical perfection."<ref name=blogcritics/>

==Jhené Aiko Remix==

On February 28, 2015, a remix of the song surfaced online featuring the vocals of [[Jhené Aiko]].<ref name="idolator.com"/> The remix was released on March 2, 2015.<ref name="rap-up.com"/> He teams up with Jhené Aiko for the remix to "Chains". She sings the second verse on the track.<ref>{{Cite web|title = Listen to Nick Jonas' "Chains (Remix)" f/ Jhene Aiko|url = http://www.complex.com/music/2015/03/nick-jonas-chains-remix-feat-jhene-aiko-premiere|website = Complex|accessdate = 2015-10-16|archive-date = 2015-10-10|archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20151010215804/http://www.complex.com/music/2015/03/nick-jonas-chains-remix-feat-jhene-aiko-premiere|url-status = live}}</ref> Though the first verse and chorus remain the same for "Chains," the second verse swaps out Jonas for Aiko, includes new synthesizer effects.<ref name="musictimes.com">{{Cite web|title = Nick Jonas, Jhene Aiko Give 'Chains' A Sexy New Remix [LISTEN]|url = http://www.musictimes.com/articles/30161/20150302/nick-jonas-jhene-aiko-chains-remix-listen-review.htm|website = Music Times|date = 2015-03-02|accessdate = 2015-10-16|archive-date = 2015-10-13|archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20151013062413/http://www.musictimes.com/articles/30161/20150302/nick-jonas-jhene-aiko-chains-remix-listen-review.htm|url-status = live}}</ref>

===Reception===
Some critics have praised this remix, Carolyn Menyes from the site ''Music Times'' stated "Jhene Aiko sings with a sultry note in her voice, before going all Fifty Shades of Grey with her 'Chains.' Her verse may take a more literal approach to the "Chains" Jonas sings about, lyrically feeling like a rapper's take than an R&B songstress, but there's no denying that she fits in these "Chains" perfectly, balancing out Jonas' single and making it all her own along the way."<ref name="musictimes.com"/>
