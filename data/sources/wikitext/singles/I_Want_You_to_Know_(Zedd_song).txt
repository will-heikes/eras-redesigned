"'''I Want You to Know'''" is a song by German [[Record producer|music producer]] [[Zedd]], featuring vocals from American singer [[Selena Gomez]] from the former's second studio album, ''[[True Colors (Zedd album)|True Colors]]'' (2015). It was written by Zedd, [[OneRepublic]]'s frontman [[Ryan Tedder]], and [[KDrew]]. "I Want You to Know" was released on 23 February 2015 as the album's lead single and debuted on American [[contemporary hit radio]] on 3 March 2015, receiving generally positive reviews from [[music critics]]. It charted within the top 10 in Finland, as well as the top 40 in fifteen additional countries. It has been certified platinum in the United States and Sweden. It won a [[Latin American Music Awards|Latin American Music Award]] for Favorite Dance Song in 2015. It is also featured in the video games ''[[Guitar Hero Live]]'', ''[[Dance Dance Revolution A]]'' and ''[[NBA 2K16]]''.

==Composition==
Musically, according to the sheet music published at Musicnotes.com, "I Want You to Know" is written in the key of [[A Minor]] with a tempo of 130 beats per minute. It follows a chord progression of Am-Em-F-Cadd9-C.<ref>[http://www.musicnotes.com/sheetmusic/mtd.asp?ppn=MN0147704 Zedd "I Want You to Know" Sheet Music]. musicnotes.com</ref>

==Critical reception==
The song has received generally positive reviews. Nolan Feeney of ''[[Time (magazine)|Time]]'' applauded the collaboration between Zedd and Gomez saying they "make beautiful music together". He explained: "Zedd's futuristic synthesizers recall '[[Break Free (Ariana Grande song)|Break Free]],' his literally-out-of-this-world collaboration with [[Ariana Grande]], but Gomez [...] keeps the track from becoming a lifeless carbon copy."<ref>{{cite magazine|last1=Feeney|first1=Nolan|title=Selena Gomez and Zedd Make a Great Team on 'I Want You to Know'|url=https://time.com/3719148/selena-gomez-zedd-i-want-you-to-know/|magazine=[[Time (magazine)|Time]]|access-date=February 25, 2015|date=February 23, 2015}}</ref>  Jason Lipshutz of ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' wrote that Gomez "finally hit her mark" with the song. "Gomez has never been known as a vocal powerhouse, but she deftly handles patently overdramatic lines [...] with the proper amount of yearning and unflappability. When that first drop does arrive about 80 seconds in, Zedd provides a swiveling party scene with a whiff of [[Daft Punk]]'s maximalist disco thrown in for good measure."<ref>{{cite magazine|last1=Lipshutz|first1=Jason|title=Zedd Gives Selena Gomez The Perfect Muse on 'I Want You To Know': Listen|url=https://www.billboard.com/music/pop/zedd-selena-gomez-i-want-you-to-know-single-stream-6480293/|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|access-date=February 23, 2015|date=February 25, 2015}}</ref> Writing for the same publication, Dan Hyman awarded the song two stars out of a possible five, stating that it "never finds its footing, and never rises above the clichéd EDM formula it follows to a T".<ref>{{cite magazine |url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/6494734/best-and-worst-singles-of-the-week-kanye-west-kacey-musgraves-carly-rae-jepsen-zedd-selena-gomez |title=Best and Worst Singles of the Week: From Kanye West to Kacey Musgraves |magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]] |publisher=[[Prometheus Global Media]] |date=March 6, 2015 |access-date=March 20, 2015}}</ref>

''Music Times'' praised the song: "The duo's real life chemistry is present in "I Want You to Know," with Gomez's voice weaving perfectly through the beat, and it's a wonder why she hasn't ventured much into the world of EDM before."<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.musictimes.com/articles/29385/20150223/zedd-selena-gomez-release-i-want-you-to-know.htm|title=Zedd, Selena Gomez 'I Want You to Know' Review: Couple's Chemistry Shines Through on Glitzy Dance Track [LISTEN]
|first=Carolyn|last=Menyes|date=February 23, 2015|access-date=May 24, 2019}}</ref> ''Inquisitr'' called this song "underwhelming given all the hype."<ref name="inquis"/> [[Idolator (website)|Idolator]] also wrote a mixed opinion "Zedd borrows his tried-and-true EDM formula that doesn’t bring anything new to the genre, while Gomez’s vocals churn around the rhythms like clothes on auto-spin. It is not the most exciting track, but it will have people bopping at the nightclubs."<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.idolator.com/7582205/selena-gomez-zedd-i-want-you-to-know-listen|archive-url=https://archive.today/20150223190845/http://www.idolator.com/7582205/selena-gomez-zedd-i-want-you-to-know-listen|url-status=dead|archive-date=February 23, 2015|title=Selena Gomez & Zedd Release "I Want You To Know": Listen|first=Bianca|last=Gracie|date=February 23, 2015|access-date=May 24, 2019}}</ref>

==Chart performance==
Two days after its release, the song debuted at No. 6 on the US [[Dance/Electronic Digital Songs]] chart and No. 9 on the full US [[Dance/Electronic Songs]] chart.<ref>{{cite magazine|last1=Murray|first1=Gordon|title=Zedd, Selena Gomez Launch in Hot Dance/Electronic Songs Chart's Top 10|url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/zedd-selena-gomez-launch-in-hot-danceelectronic-songs-charts-top/|access-date=March 3, 2015|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|publisher=[[Prometheus Global Media]]|date=February 27, 2015}}</ref> On the chart dated 14 March 2015, "I Want You to Know" debuted at number 24 on the US [[Mainstream Top 40]] chart (the highest debut on the chart that week) and at number 22 on the US [[Dance/Mix Show Airplay]] chart. On the week of 14 March, the song debuted at its peak position of number 17 on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]].<ref>{{cite magazine|last1=Trust|first1=Gary|title=Chart Highlights: Mark Ronson, Bruno Mars Top Adult Pop Songs, Nate Ruess Debuts Solo|url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/chart-highlights-mark-ronson-bruno-mars-top-adult-pop-songs-nate/|access-date=March 3, 2015|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|publisher=[[Prometheus Global Media]]|date=March 2, 2015}}</ref> It also peaked at number 14 in UK Singles Chart. As of July 2015, "I Want You To Know" has sold 511,000 copies in the US.<ref>{{cite magazine |url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/ask-billboard-is-there-an-unusually-high-number-of-new-acts-in/|title=Ask Billboard: Is There an Unusually High Number of New Acts in the Hot 100's Top 10? |last=Trust |first=Gary |magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]] |publisher=[[Prometheus Global Media]] |date=July 19, 2015|url-status=live|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180916225929/https://www.billboard.com/articles/columns/chart-beat/6634614/ask-billboard-is-there-an-unusually-high-number-of-new-acts-in|archive-date=September 16, 2018|access-date=May 23, 2019}}</ref> It has been certified 2x Platinum in the United States.

In France, although the song failed to enter into the [[French Singles Chart]]'s top forty, it peaked at no. 12 at the French Airplay Chart.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://snepmusique.com/classement-radio/?semaine=18?&annee=2015|title=Top de la semaine|work=snepmusique.com|publisher=[[Syndicat National de l'Edition Phonographique]]|date=May 18, 2015|access-date=September 21, 2025}}</ref>

It has been certified Platinum in Sweden. It has also been certified Gold in Australia, Denmark and Italy.{{citation needed|date=September 2025}}

It was directed by Brent Bonacorso<ref>Gottlieb, Steven, "[http://www.videostatic.com/watch-it/2015/03/11/zedd-i-want-you-know-brent-bonacorso-dir Zedd 'I Want You to Know' (brent bonacorso, dir.)]", ''Video Static'', March 11, 2015.</ref><ref>"[http://collaborationfactory.com/news.php Collaboration Factory News: Brent Bonacorso directs Zedd, ft. Lyca Gairanod] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150402102014/http://collaborationfactory.com/news.php|date=2015-04-02}}", Collaboration Factory, February 10, 2015.</ref> and edited by Anthony Chirco.<ref>[http://anthonychirco.com/ Anthony Chirco] official web site.</ref> Zedd said, "It's gonna be a sexy 70s video with a lot of very interesting effects.
