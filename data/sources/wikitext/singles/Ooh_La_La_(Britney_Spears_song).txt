"'''Ooh La La'''" is a song recorded by American singer [[Britney Spears]] for the soundtrack of the 2013 family film ''[[The Smurfs 2]]''. It was written and produced by [[Dr. Luke|Lukasz "Dr. Luke" Gottwald]], [[Ammo (musician)|Joshua "Ammo" Coleman]], [[Cirkut (record producer)|Henry "Cirkut" Walter]], with additional writing from [[Bonnie McKee]], [[Jacob Kasher Hindlin]], [[Lola Blanc]], and [[Fransisca Hall]].

== Background and production ==
"Ooh La La" was written by [[Ammo (musician)|Ammo]], Fransisca Hall, and [[Lola Blanc]], and was intended to become Blanc's debut single as it was a play on her name. However, upon the request of producer [[Dr. Luke]], a deal was made in which Spears was allowed to record the track. He collaborated with additional writers [[Cirkut (record producer)|Cirkut]], Jacob Kasher, and [[Bonnie McKee]] to rewrite some lyrics into a more child-friendly nature.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://idolator.com/7461858/lola-blanc-bad-tattoo-single-premiere |title=Lola Blanc's "Bad Tattoo": Idolator Premier |date=6 June 2013 |publisher=Idolator |access-date=June 6, 2013 |archive-date=18 April 2019 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190418113654/http://www.idolator.com/7461858/lola-blanc-bad-tattoo-single-premiere |url-status=dead }}</ref> Production was handled by [[Dr. Luke]], Cirkut, and Ammo. Spears decided to record this song to contribute to the soundtrack of ''The Smurfs 2'' because, in her own words, she "always loved [[the Smurfs]] as a kid and now my boys are the biggest Smurfs fans EVER. I wanted to surprise them with a song in the movie. I know they'll think it's Smurftastic!".<ref>{{cite magazine |url=https://www.billboard.com/music/music-news/britney-spears-records-new-song-for-smurfs-2-soundtrack-1558421/ |title=Britney Spears Records New Song For 'Smurfs 2' Soundtrack |magazine=Billboard |access-date=June 12, 2013 |archive-date=December 21, 2021 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20211221210444/https://www.billboard.com/music/music-news/britney-spears-records-new-song-for-smurfs-2-soundtrack-1558421/ |url-status=live }}</ref>

== Composition ==
[[File:Katy Perry - The Prismatic (Sunrise) 01 (cropped).jpg|thumb|left|[[Katy Perry]] (pictured) and [[Bonnie McKee]] provided some background vocals on the song.]]

"Ooh La La" is a [[synth-pop]] song.<ref name="Billboard hits the web">{{cite magazine |url=https://www.billboard.com/articles/news/1562823/britney-spears-smurfs-2-single-ooh-la-la-hits-the-web |title=Britney Spears' 'Smurfs 2' Single 'Ooh La La' Hits The Web |magazine=Billboard |access-date=June 12, 2013 |archive-date=July 16, 2018 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180716074503/https://www.billboard.com/articles/news/1562823/britney-spears-smurfs-2-single-ooh-la-la-hits-the-web |url-status=live }}</ref> Writing for [[E! Online]], Alexis L. Loinaz described the song as "vintage Britney", with an "oddly [[Folk music|folksy]] [[pop-tart]] twist."<ref name="Eonlinereview">{{cite web|url=http://ca.eonline.com/news/420025/britney-spears-debuts-new-song-ooh-la-la-from-smurfs-2-listen-now |title=Britney Spears Debuts New Song Ooh La La From Smurfs 2–Listen Now! &#124; E! Online UK |publisher=Ca.eonline.com |date=May 17, 2013 |access-date=June 12, 2013}}</ref> Melinda Newman of [[HitFix]] deemed it a "sweet, slight, little love song" which is about "loving someone for their true blue self."<ref name="HitFox">{{cite web |last=Newman |first=Melinda |url=http://www.hitfix.com/news/britney-spears-new-song-ooh-la-la-from-the-smurfs-2-listen |title=Britney Spears new song Ooh La La from The Smurfs 2: Listen |work=[[HitFix]] |date=May 17, 2013 |access-date=June 12, 2013 |archive-date=June 13, 2013 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130613155358/http://www.hitfix.com/news/britney-spears-new-song-ooh-la-la-from-the-smurfs-2-listen |url-status=dead }}</ref> It begins as a "pure [[pop music|pop]]" song mixed with [[electronica]].<ref name="HitFox"/> The song then transitions, with Spears "speak-singing, [[Kesha]] style", over an [[electroclash]] beat.<ref name="HitFox"/> This is followed by an [[Acoustic guitar|acoustic-guitar]]-driven section, which, according to Newman, is "fluffy, retro pop", similar to [[Madonna]] in the early 1980s.<ref name="HitFox"/> "Ooh La La" contains a "compelling breakdown", which is "full of scrambled vocals" and "dense beats", that lasts for a duration of 30 seconds.<ref name="Billboard hits the web"/> Spears' "whimsical vocals" contain "catchy ditty layers."<ref name="Eonlinereview"/> Singer [[Katy Perry]] revealed during the film's premiere that she, along with co-writer Bonnie McKee, provided some background vocals on the song.<ref name="Exclusive: Britney Spears & Katy Perry's Secret Collaboration Revealed!">{{cite web |url=http://www.eonline.com/news/443357/exclusive-britney-spears--katy-perrys-secret-collaboration-revealed |title=Exclusive: Britney Spears & Katy Perry's Secret Collaboration Revealed! |date=28 July 2013 |publisher=E! Online |access-date=July 28, 2013 |archive-date=31 July 2013 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130731225146/http://www.eonline.com/news/443357/exclusive-britney-spears--katy-perrys-secret-collaboration-revealed? |url-status=live }}</ref> The song has been composed in the key of [[F-sharp major|F{{music|#}} major]]. It has a [[Tempo|BPM]] of 128 crotchet beats per minute. It follows a chord progression of B-C{{music|#}}-[[F♯ (musical note)|F{{music|#}}]]-D{{music|#}}m in the chorus.

== Critical reception ==
"Ooh La La" received generally positive response from critics. ''[[Rolling Stone]]''{{'}}s Dan Reilly highlighted the song's catchiness, writing "now that you've heard it, try and get it out of your head".<ref>{{cite magazine |author=Dan Reilly |url=https://www.rollingstone.com/music/news/britney-spears-debuts-ooh-la-la-from-smurfs-2-soundtrack-20130517 |title=Listen: Britney Spears' New Song 'Ooh La La' &#124; Music News |magazine=Rolling Stone |date=2013-05-17 |access-date=2013-08-20 |archive-date=2017-12-14 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20171214124433/https://www.rollingstone.com/music/news/britney-spears-debuts-ooh-la-la-from-smurfs-2-soundtrack-20130517 |url-status=dead }}</ref> An editor from ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' magazine described the track as a "sweet spot of sugary synth-pop", adding that the song is "highlighted by a compelling breakdown, full of scrambled vocals and dense beats."<ref>{{cite magazine |url=https://www.billboard.com/articles/news/1567111/britney-spears-releases-ooh-la-la-talks-new-approach-to-next-album |title=Britney Spears Releases 'Ooh La La,' Talks 'New Approach' to Next Album |magazine=Billboard |date=2013-06-17 |access-date=2013-08-20 |archive-date=2013-08-14 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130814022239/http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/1567111/britney-spears-releases-ooh-la-la-talks-new-approach-to-next-album |url-status=live }}</ref> [[Brian Mansfield]] of ''[[USA Today]]'' stated that "Momma Spears' kid-friendly track is energetic to the point of hyperactivity, caroming from a rap section to an acoustic-guitar-driven chorus to a buzzy [[Electronic music|electronic]] breakdown. Smurftastic? Maybe."<ref>{{cite web |author=Brian Mansfield and Elysa Gardner, USA TODAY |url=https://www.usatoday.com/story/life/music/2013/06/17/britney-spears-playlist/2432699/ |title=Playlist: Britney Spears, plus 10 more |publisher=Usatoday.com |date=2013-06-18 |access-date=2013-08-20 |archive-date=2013-06-25 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130625065207/http://www.usatoday.com/story/life/music/2013/06/17/britney-spears-playlist/2432699/ |url-status=live }}</ref> Jenna Hally Rubenstein from [[MTV News]] was enthusiastic about the song, describing it as a "bright, sunshiny pop jam that perfectly caters to the ''Smurfs 2'' demographic."<ref>{{cite web|url=http://buzzworthy.mtv.com/2013/06/17/britney-spears-ooh-la-la/ |archive-url=https://archive.today/20130624230940/http://buzzworthy.mtv.com/2013/06/17/britney-spears-ooh-la-la/ |url-status=dead |archive-date=June 24, 2013 |title=New Song: Britney Spears, 'Ooh La La' |publisher=Buzzworthy.mtv.com |date=2013-06-17 |access-date=2013-08-20}}</ref>

In 2014, "Ooh La La" was nominated for two [[Radio Disney Music Awards]] for Best Song That Makes You Smile and Favorite Song from a Movie or a TV Show. The award's ceremony took place on April 26, 2014, with the song winning the award for Best Song That Makes You Smile.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://twitter.com/radiodisney/status/460218726319538177|title=@britneyspears #OohLaLa just won the #SoHappy #ARDY brought to you by #ToothTunes at the #RDMAs|publisher=Twitter|access-date=2014-04-27|archive-date=2014-04-28|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140428102623/https://twitter.com/radiodisney/status/460218726319538177|url-status=live}}</ref>

==Music video==
The [[music video]] was filmed in June and was directed by [[Marc Klasfeld]]. The video features appearances by Spears' children, Sean Preston and Jayden James Federline, as well as a brief appearance by Spears' niece, Maddie Aldridge. The video premiered on Spears' [[Vevo]] channel at 12PM ET on July 11, 2013.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.facebook.com/photo.php?fbid=10151741564728234&set=a.79096253233.80143.39677118233&type=1&relevant_count=1 |title=Tijdlijnfoto's |publisher=Facebook |access-date=2013-08-20}}</ref> [[MSN Music]] described the opening scene from the video as an homage to the video for [[Janet Jackson]]'s 1986 song "[[Nasty (Janet Jackson song)|Nasty]]", stating that "the clip plays out like a more kid-friendly version of Janet Jackson's 'Nasty' video, with Spears and her kids taking in a movie when mom is suddenly transported into the on-screen action."<ref>{{cite web|url=http://blogs.music.msn.ca/2013/07/britney-spears-debuts-ooh-la-la-music-video.html|title=Britney Spears debuts "Ooh La La" music video|publisher=MSN Music|date=July 11, 2013|access-date=July 15, 2013|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130716035839/http://blogs.music.msn.ca/2013/07/britney-spears-debuts-ooh-la-la-music-video.html|archive-date=July 16, 2013}}</ref>

The video features Spears and her sons watching ''The Smurfs 2'' in a theater and as [[Gargamel]] zaps Spears, she finds herself in Smurf village singing and dancing with the smurfs. The music video heavily samples clips of the movie.
