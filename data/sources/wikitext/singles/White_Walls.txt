"'''White Walls'''" is a hip-hop song by an American [[Hip hop music|hip-hop]] duo [[Macklemore & Ryan Lewis]], taken from their collaborative debut studio album ''[[The Heist (album)|The Heist]]'' (2012). The song's [[Hook (music)|hook]] is performed by singer [[Hollis (singer)|Hollis]], while American rapper [[Schoolboy Q]] performs a guest [[Verse (popular music)|verse]]. "White Walls" was released to American [[contemporary hit radio]] on October 8, 2013, as the fifth and final [[single (music)|single]] from ''The Heist''. The song debuted at number 100 and has since peaked at number 15 on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] and has sold 1,000,000 copies as of January 2014.<ref name=sales>{{cite web |url=http://music.yahoo.com/blogs/music-news/chart-watch-pitbull-kesha-eminem-riri-230734884.html |title=Chart Watch: Pitbull & Kesha & Eminem & RiRi |author=Paul Grein |date=January 8, 2013 |work=Yahoo Music }}</ref>

==Release and promotion==
The song charted at number one on the US [[Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs#Bubbling Under R&B/Hip-Hop Singles|Bubbling Under R&B/Hip-Hop Singles]] prior to its release as a single, due to high [[music download|digital downloads]] subsequently after the album's release. Macklemore, Ryan Lewis, Schoolboy Q and Hollis performed "White Walls" on ''[[The Tonight Show with Jay Leno]]'' on August 27, 2013.<ref>{{cite journal | url=http://www.rap-up.com/2013/08/28/macklemore-ryan-lewis-perform-with-schoolboy-q-on-leno/ | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131029190805/http://www.rap-up.com/2013/08/28/macklemore-ryan-lewis-perform-with-schoolboy-q-on-leno/ | url-status=dead | archive-date=October 29, 2013 | title=Macklemore & Ryan Lewis Perform with ScHoolboy Q on 'Leno' | journal=[[Rap-Up]] | location=New York | date=August 28, 2013 | access-date=October 31, 2013}}</ref> The song impacted American [[contemporary hit radio]] on October 8, 2013.<ref>{{cite journal | url=http://www.fmqb.com/article.asp?id=16691 | title=CHR – Available for Airplay | journal=[[FMQB]] | location=Cherry Hill | access-date=August 31, 2013 | archive-url=https://archive.today/20130828114720/http://fmqb.com/article.asp?id=16691 | archive-date=August 28, 2013}}</ref>

In November 2020, "White Walls" went viral on [[TikTok]], being used in many videos.

== Music video ==
The [[music video]], directed by Macklemore, Ryan Lewis and Jason Koenig, was released September 9, 2013. The video features [[cameo appearance]]s from several rappers including [[A$AP Rocky]], [[Trinidad Jame$]], [[Wiz Khalifa]], [[Big Boi]] and [[Sir Mix-a-Lot]], as well as [[DJ Drama]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://music-mix.ew.com/2013/09/09/macklemore-white-walls-video/|title=Macklemore and Ryan Lewis party with A$AP Rocky in 'White Walls' video|work=Entertainment Weekly's EW.com|access-date=February 2, 2016}}</ref><ref>{{cite web | last=Ortiz | first=Edwin | url=http://www.complex.com/music/2013/09/video-macklemore-ryan-lewis-f-schoolboy-q-hollis | title=Video: Macklemore & Ryan Lewis f/ Schoolboy Q & Hollis "White Walls" | work= [[Complex (magazine)|Complex]] | publisher= Complex Media | date=September 9, 2013 | access-date=September 10, 2013}}</ref> Parts of the music video are shot inside a [[Vogue Tyre]] warehouse, and the eponymous [[whitewall tire]]s are explicitly mentioned by Macklemore to be Vogues.<ref name="TB-Mackle-Vogue">{{cite web | url=http://www.tirebusiness.com/article/20131001/NEWS/131009990/hip-hop-video-shot-in-vogue-tyre-warehouse | title=Hip-hop video shot in Vogue Tyre warehouse | publisher=Tire Business | date=October 1, 2013 | access-date=March 20, 2014 | author=Tire Business Staff Report}}</ref><ref name="Ridesmag-Mackle">{{cite web | url=http://www.rides-mag.com/videos/2013/09/macklemore-ryan-lewis-white-walls-official-video-featuring-schoolboy-q-hollis/ | title=Macklemore & Ryan Lewis – 'White Walls' Official Video Featuring Vogue Tyres | publisher=Rides mag. | date=September 30, 2013 | access-date=March 20, 2014 | author=Evo}}</ref> The video also features several Cadillac models, including an [[Cadillac SRX|SRX]], [[Cadillac Escalade|Escalade]], two [[Cadillac XLR|XLRs]], several [[Cadillac Eldorado|Eldorados]] and a [[Cadillac de Ville series|Coupe de Ville]].
