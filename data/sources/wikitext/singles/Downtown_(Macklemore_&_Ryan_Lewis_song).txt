"'''Downtown'''" is a song by American [[hip-hop]] duo [[Macklemore & Ryan Lewis]] featuring fellow American musicians Eric Nally of [[Foxy Shazam]], [[Melle Mel]], [[Kool Moe Dee]], and [[Grandmaster Caz]]. The song was officially released on August 27, 2015, as the lead single from the duo's second studio album ''[[This Unruly Mess I've Made]]'' (2016). A music video for the song was uploaded to [[Ryan Lewis]]' [[YouTube]] channel on the day of the song's release.

==Background==

On the song Macklemore said, "Ryan [Lewis] made a beat on the road called 'moping around,' I got the beat, I thought it said [[moped]]." "Coincidentally we had both purchased mopeds for being on the road so we could go around, leave the venues that we were performing at, and I wrote a song about mopeds, which now a year and a half later is out in the world."<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.mtv.com/news/2260941/macklemore-ryan-lewis-downtown-moped-anthem/ |title=Macklemore and Ryan Lewis purchases mopeds to get around |last=Garibaldi |first=Christina |date=4 September 2015 |website=[[MTV]] |access-date=10 July 2016 |archive-date=11 July 2016 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160711045619/http://www.mtv.com/news/2260941/macklemore-ryan-lewis-downtown-moped-anthem/ |url-status=dead }}</ref>

==Critical reception==
William Pankey of ''[[AXS (ticket merchant)|AXS]]'' described the song as a sequel to the duo's breakout single "[[Thrift Shop]]",<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.axs.com/news/watch-macklemore-attempt-to-re-create-thrift-shop-with-new-video-downt-64771 |title=Watch Macklemore attempt to re-create 'Thrift Shop' with new video 'Downtown' |last=Pankey |first=William |website=AXS |publisher=AEG Worldwide |date=August 27, 2015 |access-date=October 11, 2015 |archive-date=September 6, 2015 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150906081728/http://www.axs.com/news/watch-macklemore-attempt-to-re-create-thrift-shop-with-new-video-downt-64771 |url-status=live }}</ref> while Larry Miezel Jr. of [[The Stranger (newspaper)|''The Stranger'']] felt it was another "kid-friendly [[Pop rap|pop-rap]] stunt-fantasy."<ref>{{Cite web |last=Mizell Jr. |first=Larry |date=August 28, 2015 |title=I Can't Deal With "Downtown" Either, but Can We Talk About Macklemore with a Bit of Nuance? |url=https://www.thestranger.com/music/2015/08/28/22775678/i-cant-deal-with-downtown-either-but-can-we-talk-about-macklemore-with-a-bit-of-nuance |access-date=March 23, 2026 |website=[[The Stranger (newspaper)|The Stranger]]}}</ref> Maeve McDermott of ''[[USA Today]]'' called it "a [[Hip-hop|hip hop]] recreation of [[Bruno Mars]] and [[Mark Ronson]]'s '[[Uptown Funk]]'."<ref name="USAtoday">{{cite web |url=http://entertainthis.usatoday.com/2015/08/27/macklemore-new-music-downtown/ |title=What exactly is going on in 'Downtown', Macklemore's bizarre new song? |last=McDermott |first=Maeve |website=ENTERTAiNTHiS! |publisher=USA Today Life |date=August 27, 2015 |access-date=October 11, 2015 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160514060432/http://www.usatoday.com/story/life/entertainthis/2015/08/27/macklemore-new-music-downtown/77541706/ |archive-date=May 14, 2016 }}</ref> Amaya Mendizabal of [[Billboard (magazine)|''Billboard'']] labeled the track as [[old-school hip-hop]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Mendizabal |first=Amaya |date=September 1, 2015 |title=Macklemore & Ryan Lewis' 'Downtown' Debuts in Top 30 on Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs |url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/macklemore-ryan-lewis-downtown-top-30-debut-hot-rb-hip-hop/ |access-date=March 23, 2026 |website=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]}}</ref>

==Music video==
The official music video for the song was uploaded on August 27, 2015, to [[Ryan Lewis]]' [[YouTube]] channel. It was directed by Macklemore, Lewis, and Jason Koenig, and was filmed in [[Spokane, Washington]].<ref>{{cite web | url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JGhoLcsr8GA | title=Macklemore & Ryan Lewis - Downtown (Official Music Video) | publisher=[[YouTube]] | date=August 27, 2015 | access-date=September 9, 2015 | archive-date=December 8, 2020 | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201208063813/https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JGhoLcsr8GA | url-status=live }}</ref> [[Ken Griffey Jr.]] is featured in the music video. As of June 2026, it has over 253,000,000 views on [[YouTube]].

==In popular culture==
===Media===
"Downtown" was used in the movie trailers for ''[[Dirty Grandpa]]'' and ''[[The Secret Life of Pets]]''.

In July 2018, the [[Seattle Police Department]] created a music video set to "Downtown" as part of a lip-sync video competition between police departments across the United States that was popular at the time.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.seattletimes.com/seattle-news/watch-seattle-police-lip-sync-macklemores-downtown-as-part-of-viral-video-challenge/|title=Watch: Seattle police lip-sync Macklemore's 'Downtown' as part of viral video challenge|newspaper=[[The Seattle Times]]|access-date=July 25, 2018|archive-date=July 25, 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180725155144/https://www.seattletimes.com/seattle-news/watch-seattle-police-lip-sync-macklemores-downtown-as-part-of-viral-video-challenge/|url-status=live}}</ref>

This was used in the 2018 film ''[[Hotel Transylvania 3: Summer Vacation]]'', the song was sung by a group of Fish Men, all voiced by [[Chris Parnell]].
