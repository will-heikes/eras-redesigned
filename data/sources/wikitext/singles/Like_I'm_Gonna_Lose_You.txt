"'''Like I'm Gonna Lose You'''" is a song by the American singer-songwriter [[Meghan Trainor]] from her debut major-label studio album ''[[Title (album)|Title]]'' (2015), featuring guest vocals from [[John Legend]]. Trainor wrote the song with Justin Weaver and [[Caitlyn Smith]], and produced it with [[Chris Gelbuda]]. [[Epic Records]] released it as the album's fourth single on June 23, 2015. A [[Soul music|soul]] love [[ballad]], "Like I'm Gonna Lose You" is about savoring moments spent with loved ones and not taking them for granted.

Critics praised Trainor's vocals and the song's composition, but some thought its mellow style did not suit her. In the United States, "Like I'm Gonna Lose You" reached number eight on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] and was certified [[RIAA certification|4× Platinum]] by the [[Recording Industry Association of America]]. It peaked at number one in Australia, New Zealand, and Poland, in the top ten in Canada and South Africa, and attained an [[Music recording certification|8× Platinum]] certification in Australia.

Constellation Jones directed the music video for "Like I'm Gonna Lose You", featuring Trainor singing in a candlelit room on a rainy night while people engaged in a diverse variety of relationships are shown interacting with their loved ones. Trainor performed the song on television shows such as the [[Billboard Music Awards|''Billboard'' Music Awards]], ''[[Jimmy Kimmel Live!]]'', and the [[American Music Awards]], and included it on the [[set list]]s of her concert tours [[That Bass Tour]] (2015), [[MTrain Tour]] (2015), [[the Untouchable Tour]] (2016), and [[the Timeless Tour]] (2024).

Trainor initially decided not to include "Like I'm Gonna Lose You" on her debut major-label studio album, ''[[Title (album)|Title]]'' (2015), because it did not keep with the album's doo-wop sound. After her uncle, Burton Toney, forced her manager to listen to it, the latter was reduced to tears and convinced her to put it on the track list. Trainor's management, which also represents [[John Legend]], sent "Like I'm Gonna Lose You" to him. He said "I love this. I want to be a part of it".<ref name="MTV News">{{cite web|author=Garibaldi, Christina|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/1940460/meghan-trainor-john-legend-duet/|title=Meghan Trainor Is Full On 'Freaking Out' Because She Has John Legend On Her Album|publisher=[[MTV News]]|date=September 24, 2014|access-date=June 11, 2015|archive-date=July 28, 2020|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200728113452/http://www.mtv.com/news/1940460/meghan-trainor-john-legend-duet/|url-status=dead}}</ref> Trainor produced the song with [[Chris Gelbuda]], with whom she had co-written the song "3am" and [[Sabrina Carpenter]]'s 2014 single "Can't Blame a Girl for Trying".<ref name="liner" /><ref>{{cite AV media notes |title=Eyes Wide Open |title-link=Eyes Wide Open (Sabrina Carpenter album) |others=[[Sabrina Carpenter]] |year=2015 |type=Liner notes |author=[[Hollywood Records]] }}</ref> It was released as the sixth track on ''Title'' on January 9, 2015, featuring guest vocals from Legend.<ref name="Apple Music" /> Epic Records promoted "Like I'm Gonna Lose You" to [[contemporary hit radio]] stations in the United States on June 23, 2015, as the fourth single from the album.<ref name="AllAccess" /> The label sent the song to [[rhythmic contemporary]] radio stations in the country on October 6, 2015, and radio airplay in Italy on October 30.<ref name="FMQB" /><ref name="CHR" />

==Commercial performance==
"Like I'm Gonna Lose You" debuted at number 95 on the US ''Billboard'' Hot 100 issued for July&nbsp;25, 2015.<ref>{{cite magazine |title=The Hot 100 Chart |url=https://www.billboard.com/charts/hot-100/2014-07-26 |magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]] |access-date=March 21, 2021 |archive-date=November 12, 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201112025720/https://www.billboard.com/charts/hot-100/2014-07-26 |url-status=live }}</ref> On November&nbsp;21, 2015, the song ascended into the top 10, becoming Trainor's third and Legend's second to achieve the milestone. With this, she became the first female artist since [[Kesha]] in 2010 to attain at least three top-10 singles on the chart from a debut album.<ref>{{cite magazine |last=Trust |first=Gary |title=Adele's 'Hello' Tops Hot 100 for Second Week; Ariana Grande, Meghan Trainor Hit Top 10 |url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/adele-hello-hot-100-second-week/ |magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]] |access-date=April 25, 2022 |date=November 9, 2015 |archive-date=February 2, 2022 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220202055535/https://www.billboard.com/pro/adele-hello-hot-100-second-week/ |url-status=live |url-access=subscription }}</ref> It peaked at number eight on the ''Billboard'' Hot 100 issued for December&nbsp;12, 2015, and received a [[RIAA certification|4× Platinum]] certification from the [[Recording Industry Association of America]].<ref>{{Cite magazine |url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/adele-hello-no-1-hot-100-fifth-week/ |magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]] |title=Adele's 'Hello' Leads Hot 100 for Fifth Week |first=Gary |last=Trust |date=November 30, 2015 |access-date=November 30, 2015 |archive-date=December 2, 2015 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151202002735/http://www.billboard.com/articles/columns/chart-beat/6777992/adele-hello-no-1-hot-100-fifth-week |url-status=live |url-access=subscription }}</ref><ref name="RIAA" /> On the [[Canadian Hot 100]], "Like I'm Gonna Lose You" charted at number eight and [[Music Canada]] certified it [[Music recording certification|7× Platinum]].<ref name="CA" /><ref name="MC" /> The song reached number 99 in the United Kingdom and earned a Platinum certification from the [[British Phonographic Industry]].<ref name="UK" /><ref name="BPI" />

"Like I'm Gonna Lose You" was commercially successful in Oceania. In Australia, the song spent four consecutive weeks at number one, becoming both Trainor and Legend's second to reach the summit.<ref name="AU" /> The [[Australian Recording Industry Association]] certified it 8× Platinum in 2023.<ref name="ARIA" /> "Like I'm Gonna Lose You" peaked atop the New Zealand chart for three consecutive weeks, giving Trainor her third and Legend his first chart-topper.<ref name="NZ" /> The song received a 6× Platinum certification from [[Recorded Music NZ]].<ref name="RMNZ" /> It charted within the top 40 of national record charts, at number one in Poland,<ref name="PO" /> number six in South Africa,<ref name="SA" /> number 24 in the Czech Republic,<ref name="CR" /> number 34 in the Netherlands,<ref name="NE1" /> number 36 in Belgium,<ref name="BE" /> number 37 in Portugal,<ref name="POR" /> number 38 in Slovakia,<ref name="SLA" /> and number 39 in Slovenia.<ref name="SLE" /> "Like I'm Gonna Lose You" earned a 2× Platinum+Gold certification in Mexico,<ref name="AMPROFON" /> 2× Platinum in Poland,<ref name="ZPAV" /> Platinum in Denmark,<ref name="IFPI" /> Sweden,<ref name="GLF" /> and Gold in Italy.<ref name="FIMI" />
