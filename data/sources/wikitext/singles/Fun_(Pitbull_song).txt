"'''Fun'''" is a song by American rapper [[Pitbull (rapper)|Pitbull]], featuring American singer [[Chris Brown]]. The song was released on April 21, 2015 as the fourth single off of Pitbull's eighth studio album ''[[Globalization (album)|Globalization]]''.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.allaccess.com/top40-mainstream/future-releases|title=Top 40/M Future Releases - Mainstream Hit Songs Being Released and Their Release Dates ...|work=All Access}}</ref> The song peaked at number 40 on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]].

==Music video==
A colourful music video inspired with the influences from ''[[Miami Vice]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto: Vice City|GTA Vice City]]'' was released on June 19, 2015. It features Pitbull and Brown.<ref name="4music">{{cite episode|title=This Week's Fresh Music Top 20|network=[[4Music]]|airdate=1 July 2015}}</ref> It was directed by [[Gil Green (director)|Gil Green]]. [[Nuestra Belleza Latina 2013]] winner [[Marisela de Montecristo]] made a cameo appearance in the video.

''[[Penske Media Corporation|HollywoodLife]]'''s Christopher Rogers called the video "amazing" and "masterpiece", and also added that it has everything the audience would want in a music video released in the summer.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://hollywoodlife.com/2015/06/19/chris-brown-pitbull-fun-video-miami-vice-watch/|title=Chris Brown & Pitbull Spoof 'Miami Vice' In New Music Video For 'Fun' — Watch|work=[[Penske Media Corporation|HollywoodLife]]|access-date=July 6, 2015|archive-date=July 7, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150707082616/http://hollywoodlife.com/2015/06/19/chris-brown-pitbull-fun-video-miami-vice-watch/|url-status=dead}}</ref> Zach Frydenlund of ''[[Complex (magazine)|Complex]]'' gave a negative review, saying the video "doesn't actually look fun at all".<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.complex.com/music/2015/06/pitbull-chris-brown-new-video-fun|title=Pitbull and Chris Brown's "Fun" Video Doesn't Actually Look Fun At All|work=[[Complex (magazine)|Complex]]|access-date=July 6, 2015}}</ref>

== Live performances ==
Pitbull and Chris Brown performed the song on the finale of the singing competition series ''[[American Idol]]'' on May 13, 2015. That season's ''American Idol'' eighth-place finisher [[Qaasim Middleton]] also joined them on the stage for the final verse.

On May 17, Pitbull and Brown performed "Fun" at the [[2015 Billboard Music Awards|2015 ''Billboard'' Music Awards]].

On May 20, Pitbull performed "Fun" and "[[Time of Our Lives (Pitbull and Ne-Yo song)|Time of Our Lives]]" on ''[[The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon]]''.

==Chart performance==
The single debuted at number 77 on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] chart, on the week of May 30, 2015.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/charts/hot-100/2015-05-30|title=Hot 100 - 2015-05-30|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|access-date=March 1, 2020}}</ref> After climbing the chart for six more weeks, the single reached its peak at number 40 on the chart.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/charts/hot-100/2015-07-11|title=Hot 100 - 2015-07-11|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|access-date=March 1, 2020}}</ref>
