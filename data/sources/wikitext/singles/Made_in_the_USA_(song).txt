"'''Made in the USA'''" is a song by American singer [[Demi Lovato]]. It was released on July 16, 2013, as the second single from her fourth studio album, ''[[Demi (album)|Demi]]'' (2013). Released around [[Independence Day (United States)|Independence Day]],<ref name="EW" /> the song was written by Lovato, [[Jason Evigan]], Corey Chorus, Blair Perkins, and [[Jonas Jeberg]] and produced by Jeberg. "Made in the USA" is a midtempo [[country pop]] song that celebrates an everlasting American romantic relationship. The song peaked at number 80 on the ''Billboard'' Hot 100 and peaked within the top 20 in Lebanon. It has sold 312,000 copies and received generally positive reviews from music critics.

==Background and composition==
"Made in the USA" is an midtempo [[country pop]] song<ref name="Evigan MTV" /><ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/music/music-news/demi-lovato-preps-made-in-the-usa-single-1568070/|title=Demi Lovato Preps 'Made In The USA' Single|first=Jason|last=Lipshutz|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|date=June 24, 2013|access-date=June 28, 2013}}</ref> with a running length of three minutes and sixteen seconds.<ref name="Allmusic">{{cite web|title=Made in the USA - Demi Lovato|publisher=[[AllMusic]] |url=http://www.allmusic.com/song/made-in-the-usa-mt0047053865|accessdate=July 6, 2013}}</ref> The [[patriotism|patriotic]] love song<ref name="EW">{{cite web|last=Ugen|first=Rume|title=Demi Lovato Releases 'Made In The USA' Just In Time For Independence Day|url=http://www.entertainmentwise.com/news/119790/Demi-Lovato-Releases-Made-In-The-USA-Just-In-Time-For-Independence-Day-|publisher=Entertainmentwise|accessdate=July 6, 2013|date=July 4, 2013|url-status=dead|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20130706191332/http://www.entertainmentwise.com/news/119790/Demi-Lovato-Releases-Made-In-The-USA-Just-In-Time-For-Independence-Day-|archivedate=July 6, 2013}}</ref> was composed by Corey Chorus, Lovato, [[Jason Evigan]], [[Jonas Jeberg]] and Blair Perkins.<ref name="Allmusic" /> It was written in 2011, originally intended for [[Contemporary R&B|R&B]] artist Blair Perkins, though after producer Mio Vukovic heard it, he knew it was "meant" for Lovato. Songwriter and producer Jason Evigan told [[MTV News]] that the song was "Demi-fied" in response, though still contains an "R&B feel", explaining: "[Now] it's got like a country vibe mixed with this R&B feel, which makes it kind of pop country thing." Lovato reacted to the track instantly, and made a few changes to it. Lyrically, "Made in the USA" is inspired by "American romantic love stories" in the 1930s.<ref name="Evigan MTV">{{cite web|last=Vena|first=Jocelyn|title=Demi Lovato's Fourth Of July Single 'Made In The USA' Is About 'Real Love'|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1710005/demi-lovato-fourth-of-july-made-usa.jhtml|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130706132605/http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1710005/demi-lovato-fourth-of-july-made-usa.jhtml|url-status=dead|archive-date=July 6, 2013|publisher=[[MTV News]]|accessdate=July 6, 2013|date=July 4, 2013}}</ref>

Upon the album's release, ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' stated the song was "the right comparison of bubblegum fun and Lovato's overpowering pipes" and compared the track to the song "[[Party in the U.S.A.]]" by fellow former [[Disney Channel]] star [[Miley Cyrus]]. ''Billboard'' also described the song as "an up-tempo, red-white-and-blue-tinged celebration of everlasting love" and stated that the song contained a "wistful guitar lick and some fierce romanting statements".<ref name="BillboardReview">{{cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/review/1561233/demi-lovato-demi-track-by-track-review|title=Demi Lovato, 'Demi': Track-By-Track Review|first=Jason|last=Lipshutz|magazine=Billboard|date=May 9, 2013|accessdate=June 28, 2013}}</ref> Lovato's vocals in the song span two octaves, two notes, and a semitone, from the low note of E{{flat}}<sub>3</sub> to the high note of A{{flat}}<sub>5</sub>.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.musicnotes.com/sheetmusic/mtdFPE.asp?ppn=MN0118314 |title=Unsupported Browser or Operating System |publisher=Musicnotes.com |date= May 31, 2013|accessdate=2014-02-13}}</ref>

==Critical reception==
"Made in the USA" received generally positive reviews from music critics. ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' reacted positively to the song, praising it as a "grown-up version" of "[[Party in the U.S.A.]]" by Miley Cyrus, noting its opening line which contains a reference to [[Chevrolet]], describing it as "unusual" and "coy".<ref name="BillboardReview"/> Female First gave a generally positive review, saying that the song was not the best on the album but that it was "fun" and "upbeat" and praised its reference to Chevrolet as well as its "positive shout-outs" to America's coasts.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.femalefirst.co.uk/music/reviews/album/music-295578.html|title=Demi - Demi Lovato Album Review|publisher=Female First|date=June 5, 2013|accessdate=June 28, 2013}}</ref> Pop Spoken described the song as "slightly lackluster".<ref>{{cite web|url=http://popspoken.com/2013/06/01/demi-lovato-demi-album-review-giveaway/|title=Demi Lovato "Demi" Album Review & Giveaway!|publisher=Popspoken|date=June 1, 2013|accessdate=June 28, 2013|archive-date=June 18, 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130618155658/http://popspoken.com/2013/06/01/demi-lovato-demi-album-review-giveaway/|url-status=dead}}</ref> iMedia Monkey also offered a mixed reaction, complimenting the song's "perfect balance of radio friendly choruses and interesting instrumentation"; however, they stated that the song was not "ground-breaking" and was generally "more of the same".<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.imediamonkey.com/2013/06/10/demi-lovato-demi-lp/|title=Demi Lovato – 'Demi' (LP)|author=James Fyfe|publisher=iMediaMonkey|date=June 10, 2013|accessdate=June 28, 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130617031853/http://www.imediamonkey.com/2013/06/10/demi-lovato-demi-lp/|archive-date=June 17, 2013|url-status=dead}}</ref> [[Stephen Thomas Erlewine]] of [[AllMusic]] criticized the song for "copying" Cyrus's song.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.allmusic.com/album/demi-mw0002529176|title=Demi - Demi Lovato|author=Stephen Thomas Erlewine|publisher=[[Allmusic]]|date=May 14, 2013|accessdate=June 28, 2013}}</ref>

Regarding the appearance of the song's video, [[MTV News|MTV Style]]'s Chrissy Mahlmeister noted that Lovato is getting in the "patriotic spirit", sporting an "all American" look which included a "crocheted white dress with a brown belt, denim jacket". Mahlmeister stated that Lovato "ditched her typically edgy, all-black ensembles" seen in Lovato's previous music video for "[[Heart Attack (Demi Lovato song)|Heart Attack]]".<ref>{{cite web|last=Vena|first=Jocelyn|title=Demi Lovato's 'Made In The USA' Video: What We Know So Far!|url=http://hollywoodcrush.mtv.com/2013/07/11/demi-lovato-made-in-the-usa-preview/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130714051531/http://hollywoodcrush.mtv.com/2013/07/11/demi-lovato-made-in-the-usa-preview/|url-status=dead|archive-date=July 14, 2013|work=MTV|accessdate=July 18, 2013|date=July 11, 2013}}</ref>

==Music video==
The music video for "Made in the USA" released on July 17, 2013, on [[Vevo]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=z3zdIHDTbg0|title=Demi Lovato - Made in the USA (Official Video)|author=DemiLovatoVEVO|work=[[YouTube]]|accessdate=March 9, 2016|date=July 17, 2013}}</ref> It was directed by Ryan Pallotta and Lovato. The video is about a love story of a young American couple (portrayed by [[Aimee Teegarden]] and [[Dustin Milligan]]). At one night, the girl (Teegarden) goes with her friends to the carnival where she sees the boy (Milligan) for the first time, as the boy begins to dating with her along the way. One day, the boy leaves the girl behind, and it is revealed he is a soldier being deployed in the middle of the war. He promises to return, and meanwhile, the girl is left alone at home. The two attempt to stay in contact by writing letters to one another, and the girl anxiously awaits his correspondence. The climax of the story portrays a chaotic scene with the boy in a battlefield, shots ringing all around him. Simultaneously, the girl begins to shut herself in her room, worried about her boyfriend, and it seems as if he will die. In the end, the boy returns home and reunites with the girl. After the release of the video, Lovato dedicated to the troops of [[US Army]], as well as the people who lost their lives and for the country.<ref>{{cite web|author=Demi Lovato|author-link=Demi Lovato|url=https://twitter.com/ddlovato/status/357550120218279936|title=I dedicate my video to all of the troops, anyone who has passed away over seas and to this INCREDIBLE country that I'm so thankful 4.|work=[[Twitter]]|date=July 17, 2013|accessdate=July 26, 2020}}</ref>

==Live performances==
On May 11, 2013, Lovato performed "Made in the USA" for the first time at the [[Wango Tango#2013 lineup|2013 edition]] of [[Wango Tango]].<ref>{{cite news |url=https://www.huffingtonpost.com/2013/05/13/demi-lovato-covers-titani_n_3267955.html|title=Demi Lovato Performs New Song, 'Made In The USA', and Covers 'Titanium' (VIDEO)|work=[[Huffington Post]]|accessdate=November 15, 2018|date=May 13, 2013}}</ref> On June 28, 2013, Lovato performed the song during her appearance at the ''[[Good Morning America]]'' concert series, sporting a similar outfit to what the singer was seen wearing in the single's music video behind-the-scenes sneak peek.<ref name="MTV Style">{{cite web|last=Mahlmeister|first=Chrissy|title=Demi Lovato Sports Denim Jacket And White Dress In 'Made In The USA' 'GMA' Performance And Behind-The-Scenes Video|url=http://style.mtv.com/2013/06/28/demi-lovato-made-in-the-usa/|publisher=MTV Style|accessdate=June 29, 2013|date=June 28, 2013|archive-date=September 5, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150905120014/http://style.mtv.com/2013/06/28/demi-lovato-made-in-the-usa/|url-status=dead}}</ref> On August 11, 2013, Lovato performed at the [[2013 Teen Choice Awards]] with [[Nick Jonas]] as drummer.<ref>{{cite web|last=Lindner|first=Emilee|title=Demi Lovato Rocks 'Made In The USA' With Nick Jonas: Watch!|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/1712165/demi-lovato-teen-choice-awards-made-in-usa/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140723065228/http://www.mtv.com/news/1712165/demi-lovato-teen-choice-awards-made-in-usa/|url-status=dead|archive-date=July 23, 2014|work=[[MTV]]|accessdate=May 8, 2015|date=August 11, 2013}}</ref> The song was also a part of Lovato's setlist for [[The Neon Lights Tour]].

==Commercial performance==
"Made in the USA" made its peak number 80 on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]],<ref name="Hot100" /> selling 312,000 copies overall.<ref name="ussales">{{cite magazine|title=Ask Billboard: Demi Lovato's Career Album & Song Sales|url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/demi-lovato-21-savage-tom-petty-ask-billboard/|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|date=October 15, 2017|access-date=October 17, 2017}}</ref>
