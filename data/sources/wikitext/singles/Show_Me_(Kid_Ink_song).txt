"'''Show Me'''" is a song by American rapper [[Kid Ink]]. The song was released on September 17, 2013, by Tha Alumni Music Group, 88 Classic and [[RCA Records]] as the [[lead single]] from his second [[studio album]], ''[[My Own Lane]]'' (2014). The song, [[Hip hop production|produced]] by [[Mustard (record producer)|DJ Mustard]] and [[Mike Free]], features vocals by American singer [[Chris Brown]]. The song peaked at number 13 on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] and number 23 on the [[UK Singles Chart]].

== Background and composition ==
On September 4, 2013, Kid Ink appeared on [[Los Angeles]]' [[KPWR|Power 106]] to announce his major label debut studio album would be titled, ''[[My Own Lane]]'', and premiered the first single titled "Show Me".<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.hotnewhiphop.com/kid-ink-premieres-new-single-show-me-feat-chris-brown-on-power-106-video.18127.html |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130915055345/http://www.hotnewhiphop.com/kid-ink-premieres-new-single-show-me-feat-chris-brown-on-power-106-video.18127.html |url-status=dead |archive-date=September 15, 2013 |title=Kid Ink "Premieres New Single "Show Me" feat Chris Brown on Power 106" Video |publisher=Hotnewhiphop.com |access-date=September 30, 2013}}</ref> It was also revealed the song would be released for [[Music download|digital download]] on September 17, 2013.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.missinfo.tv/index.php/kid-ink-show-me-feat-chris-brown/ |title=New Music: Kid Ink Feat. Chris Brown "Show Me" |publisher=MissInfo.tv |date=September 4, 2013 |access-date=September 30, 2013}}</ref>

The song features a [[Hook (music)|hook]] and [[Bridge (music)|bridge]] recorded by [[RCA Records|RCA]] label-mate [[Chris Brown]], [[Record production|production]] by [[DJ Mustard]], along with containing an interpolation of [[Robin S.]]'s 1993 hit "[[Show Me Love (Robin S. song)|Show Me Love]]".<ref>{{cite web|url=http://rapradar.com/2013/09/04/new-music-kid-ink-x-chris-brown-show-me/ |title=New Music: Kid Ink x Chris Brown "Show Me" |publisher=Rap Radar |date=September 4, 2013 |access-date=September 30, 2013}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.rap-up.com/2013/09/04/new-music-kid_ink-f-chrisbrown-show-me/ |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131029193415/http://www.rap-up.com/2013/09/04/new-music-kid_ink-f-chrisbrown-show-me/ |url-status=dead |archive-date=October 29, 2013 |title=New Music: Kid Ink f/ Chris Brown – 'Show Me' |publisher=Rap-Up.com |access-date=September 30, 2013}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.xxlmag.com/rap-music/bangers/2013/09/kid-ink-featuring-chris-brown-show/ |title=Kid Ink Featuring Chris Brown "Show Me" - XXL |publisher=Xxlmag.com |date=September 5, 2013 |access-date=September 30, 2013}}</ref> The song's production features a bubbling trap instrumental with a slight [[Rhythm and blues|R&B]] vibe, along with a booming beat and clapping snares that was described as being tailor-made for the clubs and radio alike.<ref>{{cite news|author=Kid Ink |url=http://www.djbooth.net/index/tracks/review/kid-ink-show-me/ |title=Kid Ink ft. Chris Brown - Show Me &#124; Listen |newspaper=Djbooth |publisher=Djbooth.net |date=September 9, 2013 |access-date=September 30, 2013}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://thedrop.fm/kid-ink-show-me-chris-brown/ |title=Kid Ink Partners with Chris Brown on 'Show Me' |publisher=Thedrop.fm |date=September 5, 2013 |access-date=September 30, 2013}}</ref>

The song is written in a key of [[G major]].

== Recording ==
Kid Ink wrote and recorded his lyrics over the DJ Mustard production, prior to it being sent to Chris Brown. The song's original [[refrain]] was performed by West Coast singer [[TeeFlii]], who also wrote the song's chorus.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.xxlmag.com/news/2014/01/kid-ink-was-surprised-how-big-show-me-was/ |title=Kid Ink Says He Smokes An Ounce A Day - XXL |publisher=Xxlmag.com |date=January 14, 2014 |access-date=January 20, 2014}}</ref> Ink said, "I know before he had the record that it had a lot of single potential, but it just wasn't where it needed to be. When we linked up with Chris Brown, we played him the record and he kind of understood that. He put that extra... what the record needed to come to life."<ref name="PopCrush"/>

The recording session took place from 8PM to 9AM the next morning, and Brown and Kid Ink then recorded four other songs, one of which was included on Brown's ''X Files'' mixtape. However, Ink said that none of the other songs would be featured on his debut album.<ref name="PopCrush">{{cite web|url=http://popcrush.com/kid-ink-show-me-collabo-chris-brown-story/ |title=Kid Ink Reveals How His 'Show Me' Collabo With Chris Brown Came About |publisher=Popcrush.com |date=October 23, 2013 |access-date=January 14, 2014}}</ref> Nonetheless, their collaboration "[[Main Chick]]" appeared on ''My Own Lane''.<ref>{{cite web|last=Homie |first=Big |url=http://rapradar.com/2013/12/12/kid-ink-my-own-lane-tracklist/ |title=Kid Ink 'My Own Lane' Tracklist |publisher=Rap Radar |date=2013-12-12 |access-date=2016-06-16}}</ref>

== Critical reception ==
"Show Me" was met with generally positive reviews from [[Music journalism|music critic]]s. David Jeffries of [[AllMusic]] called it a light, airy and attractive track.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.allmusic.com/album/my-own-lane-mw0002599704|title=My Own Lane - Kid Ink &#124; Songs, Reviews, Credits &#124; AllMusic|website=[[AllMusic]] }}</ref> Sheldon Pearce writing for ''[[XXL (magazine)|XXL]]'' called it a clubby infectious hit.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.xxlmag.com/rap-music/reviews/2014/01/kid-ink-my-own-lane-album-review/ |title=Kid Ink Is Unpredictable And Entertaining On 'My Own Lane' - XXL |publisher=Xxlmag.com |date=January 7, 2014 |access-date=January 14, 2014}}</ref> Edwin Ortiz of ''[[HipHopDX]]'' said, "His two collaborations with Chris Brown, "Show Me" and "Main Chick," display early and often Ink's penchant for tailoring melodies for the masses."<ref>{{cite web|last=Ortiz |first=Edwin |url=http://www.hiphopdx.com/index/album-reviews/id.2212/title.kid-ink-my-own-lane |title=Kid Ink - My Own Lane &#124; Read Hip Hop Reviews, Rap Reviews & Hip Hop Album Reviews |date=January 14, 2014 |publisher=HipHop DX |access-date=January 14, 2014}}</ref> Brent Faulkner of Starpulse praised Brown's contributions to the song and called it one of the album's best songs.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.starpulse.com/news/Brent_Faulkner/2014/01/10/review_kid_ink_my_own_lane |title=Review: Kid Ink, My Own Lane |publisher=Starpulse.com |access-date=January 14, 2014}}</ref> AJ of [[Uproxx]]'s SmokingSection called it one of the album's standout songs.<ref>{{cite web|author=AJ |url=http://smokingsection.uproxx.com/TSS/2014/01/kid-ink-my-own-lane-review |title=100-Word Review: Kid Ink's 'My Own Lane' |publisher=Smokingsection.uproxx.com |date=February 4, 2013 |access-date=January 20, 2014}}</ref> Grant Jones of RapReviews praised the song's production and called it one of the album's best songs.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.rapreviews.com/archive/2014_01_myownlane.html |title=Kid Ink :: My Own Lane :: RCA Records |publisher=Rapreviews.com |date=January 21, 2014 |access-date=March 6, 2014}}</ref> In February 2014, ''[[Complex (magazine)|Complex]]'' named it the third best song that DJ Mustard had produced.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.complex.com/music/2014/02/the-20-best-dj-mustard-tracks/kid-ink-f-chris-brown-show-me |title=Kid Ink f/ Chris Brown "Show Me" — The 20 Best DJ Mustard Tracks |publisher=Complex |date=February 6, 2014 |access-date=March 6, 2014}}</ref>

In 2023, [[Robin S.]] told [[TMZ]] that "Show Me" is her personal favorite sampling of "[[Show Me Love (Robin S. song)|Show Me Love]]".<ref>{{Cite web |title=Beyoncé Showed Me Love ... BUT, CHRIS BROWN ROCKED MY SAMPLE THE BEST!!!|url=https://www.tmz.com/2023/07/01/robin-s-show-me-love-sample-beyonce-break-my-soul-chris-brown/ |access-date=2024-01-19 |website=TMZ |language=en }}</ref>

== Commercial performance ==
On the week ending October 5, 2013, the song debuted at number 82 on the US ''Billboard'' Hot 100.<ref>{{cite web|title=The Hot 100: October 5, 2013|url=http://www.billboard.com/charts/hot-100/2013-10-05|website=Billboard.com|publisher=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|access-date=December 25, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141212182514/http://www.billboard.com/charts/hot-100/2013-10-05|url-status=live|archive-date=December 12, 2014}}</ref> In its tenth week on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]], "Show Me" jumped from number 31 to number 21.<ref>{{cite web|last=Grein |first=Paul |url=http://music.yahoo.com/blogs/music-news/chart-watch-where-beyonce-top-10-hit-010753435.html |title=Chart Watch: Where's Beyonce's Top 10 Hit? &#124; Yahoo Music - Yahoo Music |publisher=Music.yahoo.com |date=January 15, 2014 |access-date=January 20, 2014}}</ref> By its twelfth week on the chart, the song had reached number 19.<ref>{{cite web|last=Grein |first=Paul |url=http://music.yahoo.com/blogs/music-news/chart-watch-two-oscar-song-nominees-soar-015837780.html |title=Chart Watch: Two Oscar Song Nominees Soar &#124; Yahoo Music - Yahoo Music |publisher=Music.yahoo.com |date=January 29, 2014 |access-date=March 6, 2014}}</ref> That same week, the song peaked at number one of the US ''Billboard'' [[Rhythmic (chart)|Rhythmic]] chart.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.billboard.com/artist/305824/Kid+Ink/chart?f=378 |title=Kid Ink - Chart history |publisher=Billboard |access-date=March 6, 2014}}</ref> By its twentieth week the song reached a peak of number 13 on the ''Billboard'' Hot 100.<ref>{{cite web|last=Grein |first=Paul |url=https://music.yahoo.com/blogs/music-news/chart-watch-frozen-comes-summer-002850198.html |title=Chart Watch: After "Frozen," Here Comes "Summer" &#124; Chart Watch - Yahoo Music |date=March 27, 2014 |publisher=Music.yahoo.com |access-date=March 29, 2014}}</ref> As of December 2014, the song has sold 1,160,000 copies in the United States.<ref name=us_sales>{{cite web |url=http://www.hitsdailydouble.com/news/newsPage.cgi?news10227m01 |title=The Big 9 in 2014: RCA |work=HITS Daily Double |publisher=HITS Digital Ventures |date=December 11, 2014 |access-date=December 11, 2014 |archive-url=https://archive.today/20141211200721/http://www.hitsdailydouble.com/news/newsPage.cgi?news10227m01 |archive-date=December 11, 2014 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

== Live performances ==
On January 7, 2014, Kid Ink made his late-night television debut performing "Show Me" on ''[[Conan (talk show)|Conan]]'' with the song's producer DJ Mustard and singer Eric Bellinger replacing Chris Brown.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.musicnewsnashville.com/kid-ink-kicks-lane-performances-conan-106-park-revolt-tv/ |title=Kid Ink Kicks Off "My Own Lane" With Performances On Conan, 106 & Park & Revolt TV |publisher=Music News Nashville |date=January 6, 2014 |access-date=January 14, 2014}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.xxlmag.com/news/2014/01/kid-ink-performs-show-conan/ |title=Kid Ink Performs "Show Me" On 'Conan' - XXL |publisher=Xxlmag.com |date=January 8, 2014 |access-date=January 14, 2014}}</ref> On January 14, 2014, Kid Ink performed "Show Me" again with Bellinger on [[DJ Skee]]'s SKEE Live.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://twitter.com/Kid_Ink/status/423257060889735168 |title=Twitter / Kid_Ink: Tonight #SkeeLive Tune in!!! |publisher=Twitter.com |date=January 14, 2014 |access-date=January 20, 2014}}</ref> Ink performed the song solo at [[Power 106]]'s Yesi Ortiz's birthday event at The Belasco Theatre in Los Angeles, California.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.hiphopdx.com/index/videos/id.15376/title.kid-ink-show-me-live-in-los-angeles- |title=Kid Ink - "Show Me" (Live In Los Angeles) &#124; Watch Hip Hop Music Videos & New Rap Videos |publisher=HipHop DX |date=January 25, 2014 |access-date=March 6, 2014}}</ref> Then in early April 2014, Ink performed the song, along with "[[Main Chick]]" during [[MtvU]]'s Spring Break 2014.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.xxlmag.com/news/2014/04/trey-songz-kid-ink-perform-mtvu-spring-break-2014/ |title=Trey Songz & Kid Ink Perform At MTVU Spring Break 2014 |date=April 7, 2014 |publisher=Xxlmag.com |access-date=2016-06-16}}</ref>
