"'''Back to Sleep'''", also known by its censored title "'''Sex You Back to Sleep'''",<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.hotnewhiphop.com/577208-chris-brown-sex-you-back-to-sleep-new-song|title=Chris Brown drops a single from his upcoming album "Royalty."|website=Hotnewhiphop.com|date=November 5, 2015|access-date=November 17, 2015}}</ref> is a song by American singer [[Chris Brown]] from his seventh studio album ''[[Royalty (Chris Brown album)|Royalty]]''. The song was released as the album's third single and its first urban single on November 5, 2015. Three official remixes of the song were released in early 2016: The first featuring [[Usher (musician)|Usher]] and [[Zayn Malik|Zayn]], the second featuring [[Miguel (singer)|Miguel]], [[August Alsina]], and [[Trey Songz]], and the final featuring [[Tank (American singer)|Tank]], [[R. Kelly]] and [[Anthony Hamilton (musician)|Anthony Hamilton]].

The song received universal acclaim from contemporary music critics, noting it as one of the album's highlights, celebrating its production and Brown's vocal performance. Some critics called it the best [[Contemporary R&B|R&B]] song of its decade. The song received comparisons to Brown's previous singles "[[Take You Down (Chris Brown song)|Take You Down]]" (2008) and "[[No Bullshit]]" (2011), and also to [[Marvin Gaye]]'s 1982 song "[[Sexual Healing]]", due to its similar musical direction. The song became the most successful one from the ''Royalty'' album, peaking at number 20 on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] and number 5 on the US [[Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs]] chart.

Its music video was released on December 14, 2015, and is part of [[Royalty (Chris Brown album)#Music video series|''Royalty'' music videos]] storyline, following the conclusion of Brown's "[[Fine by Me (Chris Brown song)|Fine by Me]]" music video. The videoclip depicts a sexually intimate scene between Brown and his love interest.

== Background ==
The song was written by [[Chris Brown]] and August Rigo, and was produced by [[Vinylz]] and [[Boi-1da]]. The song was recorded in 2015 at [[Record Plant]] in Los Angeles, California. "Back to Sleep" was premiered via [[SoundCloud]] on November 5, 2015, and was sent to radio four days later as the album's third single, also receiving its commercial release for digital download on November 9, 2015.<ref>{{cite web|url= https://twitter.com/chrisbrown/status/662379552904052739 | title = Urban single coming today! "FUCK YOU BACK TO 2 SLEEP" | publisher= Twitter | access-date= November 5, 2015}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url= https://twitter.com/chrisbrown/status/662415152998801408 | title = Listen to @Power1051 NY right now for my new urban single #SexYouBackToSleep!  | publisher= Twitter | access-date= November 5, 2015}}</ref><ref name="iTunes">{{cite web|url=https://itunes.apple.com/us/album/back-to-sleep-single/id1057316555 |title=Back To Sleep – Single by Chris Brown |publisher=[[iTunes Store]] (US) |access-date=November 17, 2015 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151118065550/https://itunes.apple.com/us/album/back-to-sleep-single/id1057316555 |archive-date=November 18, 2015 }}</ref>

== Composition ==
[[File:Marvin Gaye (1973 publicity photo).jpg|150px|thumb|Brown said that "Back to Sleep" was inspired by [[Marvin Gaye]]'s 1982 song "[[Sexual Healing]]".]]
"Back to Sleep" is a [[mid-tempo]] [[Contemporary R&B|R&B]] [[slow jam]], about late night sex, that features minor influences of [[funk]], and [[roland TR-808]] beats reminiscent of [[Marvin Gaye]]'s 1982 song "[[Sexual Healing]]".<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.hotnewhiphop.com/577208-chris-brown-sex-you-back-to-sleep-new-song|title = Chris Brown – Sex You Back to Sleep|website = HNHH|date = November 5, 2015}}</ref><ref name="billboard.com">{{cite web|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/review/6812828/chris-brown-royalty-album-review|title=Chris Brown Falls Short of King on 'Royalty': Album Review|website=Billboard.com|date=December 18, 2015|access-date=July 29, 2018}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.hotnewhiphop.com/507692-chris-brown-and-rick-ross-perform-sorry-and-back-to-sleep-on-kimmel-new-video|title=Chris Brown & Rick Ross Perform "Sorry" & "Back To Sleep" On Kimmel|website=Hotnewhiphop.com|date=December 8, 2015|access-date=July 29, 2018}}</ref> ''[[Vibe (magazine)|Vibe]]'' stated that the song infuses elements of "new school R&B" with "a light dusting of 1980s pop".<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.vibe.com/2015/11/chris-brown-sex-you-back-to-sleep-royalty-2015/|title=Chris Brown Doesn't Leave Anything To The Imagination On "Sex You Back To Sleep"|date=November 6, 2015|website=Vibe.com|access-date=July 29, 2018}}</ref><ref>http://addictedtoradio.com/chris-brown-falls-short-of-king-on-royalty/ {{dead link|date=July 2018}}</ref> According to ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'', the lyrical content of "Back to Sleep" features "Brown fresh off a late night flight into town", detailing how he plans to go to his girlfriend's house, wake her up at 3:30 a.m. to have sex with her, then tuck her in for actual sleep.<ref name="billboard.com"/><ref>{{cite magazine | url=https://www.billboard.com/music/rb-hip-hop/chris-brown-slow-jams-7587936/ | title=Chris Brown's 12 Sexiest Slow Jams | magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]] }}</ref>

The music video for "Back to Sleep" was directed by Brown,<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.directlyrics.com/chris-brown-premieres-racy-music-video-for-back-to-sleep-watch-news.html|title=Chris Brown Premieres Racy Music Video For "Back To Sleep": Watching

===Synopsis===
The video follows the conclusion of Brown's "[[Fine by Me (Chris Brown song)|Fine by Me]]" music video, and starts with the singer making a phone call to his girlfriend, asking her if he could "come over" to her house even though it's late at night, because of "weird" things happening to him throughout the night. As soon as Brown arrives to her house, they get intimate, with scenes showing them kissing and touching in the shower and bedroom, intercut with scenes of Brown performing the song in a club, with a female band.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.rap-up.com/2015/12/14/video-chris-brown-back-to-sleep/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151215123817/http://www.rap-up.com/2015/12/14/video-chris-brown-back-to-sleep/|url-status=dead|archive-date=December 15, 2015|title=Video: Chris Brown – 'Back to Sleep'|website=Rap-up.com|access-date=July 29, 2018}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.billboard.com/music/rb-hip-hop/chris-brown-back-to-sleep-video-watch-6806097/|title=Watch Chris Brown Get Frisky in 'Back To Sleep' Video|website=Billboard.com|date=December 14, 2015|access-date=July 29, 2018}}</ref>

== Critical reception ==
"Back to Sleep" received widespread acclaim from music critics. In 2018, the co-host of ''[[The Breakfast Club (radio show)|The Breakfast Club]]'', [[Charlamagne tha God]], defined the song as "the best R&B record in the past 15 years" along with "[[Adorn (song)|Adorn]]" by [[Miguel (singer)|Miguel]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.hotnewhiphop.com/158839-charlamagne-tha-god-picks-his-kings-of-randb-and-best-rap-album-of-the-year-news|title=Charlamagne Tha God Picks His "Kings of R&B" & Best Rap Album of the Year|website=HNHH|date=December 15, 2018}}</ref> [[AllMusic]]'s editor Andy Kellman stated that the track is the best one on the ''Royalty'' album.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.allmusic.com/album/royalty-mw0002889076|title=Royalty – Chris Brown – Songs, Reviews, Credits – AllMusic|website=AllMusic|access-date=July 29, 2018}}</ref> Brad Wete of ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' said that it "excels as a quality addition to his catalog’s stellar collection of panty-dropping and baby-making songs", comparing the song to Brown's previous singles "[[Take You Down (Chris Brown song)|Take You Down]]" (2008) and "[[No Bullshit]]" (2011), and also to [[Marvin Gaye]]'s 1982 song "[[Sexual Healing]]".<ref name="billboard.com"/> 

Mikael Wood of ''[[The Courier-Journal]]'' praised Brown's "great vocal performance" on the track, describing it as "a smooth depiction of genuine sexuality".<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.courier-journal.com/story/entertainment/arts/music/2015/12/29/chris-brown-royalty-review/78015688/|title=Chris Brown has no filter on 'Royalty'|website=Courier-journal.com|access-date=July 29, 2018}}</ref><ref>{{cite web | url=http://www.slantmagazine.com/music/review/chris-brown-royalty | title=Review: Chris Brown, Royalty | website=[[Slant Magazine]] | date=December 21, 2015 }}</ref> ''Soul Bounce'' called it "The highlight of ''Royalty''" and "a polished, well-produced song that [spotlights] Brown's talent".<ref>{{cite web | url=https://soulbounce.com/2016/04/chris-brown-recruits-every-damn-body-for-his-back-to-sleep-remixes/ | title=Chris Brown Recruits Every Damn Body for His 'Back to Sleep' Remixes | date=April 11, 2016 }}</ref> ''[[Renowned for Sound]]'' complimented the song's "impressive vocals" and its production, saying that "the synth R&B arrangement is as smooth as ever", stating that the chorus has a "risqué" sex appeal.<ref>{{cite web | url=https://renownedforsound.com/single-review-chris-brown-back-to-sleep-feat-usher-zayn/ | title=Single Review: Chris Brown – 'Back to Sleep' (Feat. Usher & Zayn) – Renowned for Sound }}</ref> ''[[Vibe (magazine)|Vibe]]'' said that "Back to Sleep" is "a super sexy song", finding  [[shock value]] in its sexually explicit lyrics.<ref>{{cite web | url=https://www.vibe.com/music/music-news/chris-brown-usher-zayn-back-to-sleep-remix-406402/ | title=Chris Brown Gets an Assist from Usher and Zayn for A "Back to Sleep" Remix | date=February 20, 2016 }}</ref>
