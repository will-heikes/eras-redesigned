"'''Love Me Harder'''" is a song by the American singer-songwriter [[Ariana Grande]] and the Canadian singer-songwriter [[the Weeknd]]. It was released by [[Republic Records]] on September 30, 2014, as the fourth single from Grande's second studio album, ''[[My Everything (Ariana Grande album)|My Everything]]'' (2014). It was written by the Weeknd, [[Max Martin]], [[Savan Kotecha]], [[Peter Svensson]], [[Ali Payami]] and [[Belly (rapper)|Belly]]. The song was produced by Payami and [[Carolina Liar|Peter Carlsson]], with Svensson serving as a vocal producer. Sonically, "Love Me Harder" is a [[synthwave]] tinged [[Pop music|pop]], [[synth-pop]] and [[Contemporary R&B|R&B]] ballad with a "throbbing, [[Electro (music)|electro]]-heavy chorus", a [[guitar riff]] and "big vacuum-esque synths". Lyrically, the song features [[double entendre]]s about rough sex.

In the United States, the song peaked at number seven on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]], becoming Grande's fourth consecutive top-ten single off the album and making her the artist with the most top-ten singles in 2014. The song also became the Weeknd's first top ten entry in the US. The song also reached the top ten in Canada, Colombia, and South Africa. The [[Hannah Lux Davis]]-directed video finds Grande writhing on a sandy floor and the Weeknd walking through a rainstorm. As of 2020, the song had sold 1.3 million copies in the United States, and has since been certified quintuple platinum by the [[RIAA]]. The song has over 1.5 billion streams on [[Spotify]].

In an interview with ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'', Republic Records executive vice president [[Charlie Walk]] stated, "The Weeknd is positioned to be best new artist of 2014. He is that guy."<ref>{{cite magazine|last1=Gallo|first1=Phil|title=Q&A: Charlie Walk, Republic's New EVP, on Returning to a Major, Long-Term Strategy|url=http://www.billboard.com/biz/articles/news/branding/1537709/qa-charlie-walk-republics-new-evp-on-returning-to-a-major-long|magazine=Billboard|date=February 4, 2013|access-date=February 4, 2013|archive-date=February 7, 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130207045800/http://www.billboard.com/biz/articles/news/branding/1537709/qa-charlie-walk-republics-new-evp-on-returning-to-a-major-long|url-status=live}}</ref> Producer [[Max Martin]] sent the Weeknd an unfinished version of the track. According to the Weeknd, "It was a great song, but it was a little generic. I couldn't hear myself on it. So I changed it and made it dark." Martin liked the Weeknd's revised lyrics. The Weeknd later recalled that "It was kind of like the label giving me an alley-oop. I think that's where the stars aligned for me. When I see an opening I penetrate it."<ref name="RSinterview">{{cite magazine|url=https://www.rollingstone.com/music/features/sex-drugs-and-r-b-inside-the-weeknds-dark-twisted-fantasy-20151021?page=3|title=Sex, Drugs and R&B: Inside The Weeknd's Dark Twisted Fantasy|first=Josh|last=Eeels|magazine=[[Rolling Stone]]|date=October 21, 2015|access-date=August 30, 2017|archive-date=September 25, 2017|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170925120448/http://www.rollingstone.com/music/features/sex-drugs-and-r-b-inside-the-weeknds-dark-twisted-fantasy-20151021?page=3|url-status=dead}}</ref> In June 2014, Walk posted a photo on his [[Instagram]] with the caption, "When you talk about artist development and pure talent, the Weeknd comes to mind. == Commercial performance ==
In the United States, after spending a couple of weeks on the [[Bubbling Under Hot 100 Singles]] chart, "Love Me Harder" made its official debut on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] at number 79.<ref>{{cite magazine|last1=Trust|first1=Gary|title=Hot 100 Chart Moves: Ed Sheeran, Ariana Grande, Fergie Debut|url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/hot-100-ed-sheeran-ariana-grande-fergie/|magazine=Billboard|date=October 17, 2014|access-date=October 18, 2014|archive-date=October 15, 2019|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20191015182510/https://www.billboard.com/articles/columns/chart-beat/6289067/hot-100-ed-sheeran-ariana-grande-fergie|url-status=live}}</ref> The following week, it jumped to number 57 before ascending to number 37 in its third week, becoming the Weeknd's first top-40 single on the chart.<ref>{{cite web|last1=Cantor|first1=Brian|title=Hot 100: Ariana Grande, Big Sean Both Reach The Top 40|url=http://headlineplanet.com/home/2014/10/30/hot-100-ariana-grande-big-sean-reach-top-40/|website=Headline Planet|date=October 30, 2014|access-date=November 2, 2014|archive-date=October 31, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141031081030/http://headlineplanet.com/home/2014/10/30/hot-100-ariana-grande-big-sean-reach-top-40/|url-status=live}}</ref> In its fifth week, after the music video's official release, "Love Me Harder" soared to its peak of number seven, becoming Grande's fourth consecutive top-ten single from ''My Everything'', and fifth overall.<ref name="billboardtop10">{{cite magazine|last1=Trust|first1=Gary|title=Taylor Swift Still No. 1 on Hot 100, Ariana Grande & The Weeknd Hit Top 10|url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/hot-100-taylor-swift-ariana-grande-the-weeknd-top-10/|access-date=March 29, 2016|magazine=Billboard|date=November 12, 2016|archive-date=January 31, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160131164807/http://www.billboard.com/articles/columns/chart-beat/6312408/hot-100-taylor-swift-ariana-grande-the-weeknd-top-10|url-status=live}}</ref> By becoming her fourth top ten single, Grande became the singer with most top ten entries of 2014, along with her past top ten singles "[[Problem (Ariana Grande song)|Problem]]", "[[Break Free (song)|Break Free]]", and "[[Bang Bang (Jessie J, Ariana Grande and Nicki Minaj song)|Bang Bang]]". In addition, "Love Me Harder" became the Weeknd's first top ten entry on the ''Billboard'' Hot 100.<ref name="billboardtop10" /> As of June 2020, the song had sold 1.3 million copies in the United States and has been certified triple platinum by the [[Recording Industry Association of America]] (RIAA).<ref name="us_sales">{{cite magazine |last1=Trust |first1=Gary |title=Ariana Grande's Career Streams & Sales, From 'The Way' to 'Rain on Me': Ask Billboard |url=https://www.billboard.com/articles/business/chart-beat/9409079/ariana-grande-career-streams-sales-2020-ask-billboard |magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]] |access-date=June 26, 2020 |date=June 26, 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200626154254/https://www.billboard.com/articles/business/chart-beat/9409079/ariana-grande-career-streams-sales-2020-ask-billboard |archive-date=June 26, 2020 |url-status=live}}</ref>

In Canada, the single reached the top ten,<ref name="can" /> and entered the top twenty in many other countries such as Australia, Finland, Italy and the Netherlands.<ref name="aria" /><ref name="fin" /><ref name="ita" /><ref name="dutch" /> In many countries, the song became the Weeknd's first chart appearance, including France, where it debuted at number 132, before reaching the top thirty, at number 27, becoming Grande's third top thirty and the Weeknd's first.<ref name="fra" /> The song reached top ten in French airplay, becoming Grande's fourth top ten in radio after "Problem", "Break Free" and "Bang Bang" and the Weeknd's first.
In Australia, the song debuted at number 46 then jumping at number 19, becoming Grande's fourth top twenty and the Weeknd's first, as well as in Denmark and Italy.<ref name="aria" /><ref name="ita" /><ref name="aut" /><ref name="spa" />

=== Synopsis and reception ===
The video opens with "dramatic lightning in a sky boiling over with orange clouds." Throughout the music video, Ariana and the Weeknd "withstand earth, wind and water, symbolizing what one would do for love," as [[MTV]]'s Emilee Lindner claimed.<ref name="mtvnews" /> Lindner continued: "Grande writhes around on a sandy floor, the Weeknd walks nonchalantly through an indoor rain storm, and toward the end, we find Ariana in a pool of water with an air of confidence. At one point, they sing back-to-back, ultimately illustrating what the song is about — being unable to face the wants and needs of a relationship."<ref name="mtvnews" /> Lidner called it "her most serious video to date."<ref name="mtvnews" /> Gabby Bess of ''[[Paper (magazine)|Paper]]'' called its visuals "a departure from her previous videos and mark a new, sultry era for the mini diva."<ref>{{cite news|last1=Bess|first1=Gabby|title=Watch Ariana Grande's Intimate Video "Love Me Harder"|url=http://www.papermag.com/watch-ariana-grandes-intimate-video-love-me-harder-feat-the-weeknd-1427440295.html|access-date=March 30, 2016|work=[[Paper (magazine)|Paper]]|date=November 3, 2014|archive-date=April 12, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160412235054/http://www.papermag.com/watch-ariana-grandes-intimate-video-love-me-harder-feat-the-weeknd-1427440295.html|url-status=live}}</ref> Ariana Bacle of ''[[Entertainment Weekly]]'' agreed, snorting that "The video's vibe, sensual and mysterious, is an obvious departure from Grande's earlier work [...] but fits the song, a stripped-down ballad that's meant to showcase the two singers' voices."<ref name="ballad" />
