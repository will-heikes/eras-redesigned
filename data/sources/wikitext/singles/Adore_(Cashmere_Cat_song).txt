"'''Adore'''" is a song recorded by Norwegian DJ and record producer [[Cashmere Cat]], featuring vocals by American singer-songwriter [[Ariana Grande]]. It was written and produced by Cashmere along with [[Benny Blanco]] and [[Lido (musician)|Lido]], and co-written by [[Ammar Malik]], [[Jeremih Felton]], [[Babyface (musician)|Kenneth "Babyface" Edmonds]] and Darryl Simmons. "Adore" was [[Music download|digitally]] released on March 3, 2015 by Friends Keep Secrets and [[Interscope Records]].<ref name="itunes us"/>

==Background and composition==
"Adore" is the second collaboration between Cashmere Cat and Grande, the first being the song "Be My Baby", from Grande's second studio album, ''[[My Everything (Ariana Grande album)|My Everything]]'' (2014).<ref>{{Cite web|url = http://www.mtv.com/news/2091420/cashmere-cat-new-ariana-grande-song/|archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20150228101231/http://www.mtv.com/news/2091420/cashmere-cat-new-ariana-grande-song/|url-status = dead|archive-date = February 28, 2015|title = Cashmere Cat Debuts New Ariana Grande Song During Honeymoon Tour Kickoff|date = February 26, 2015|accessdate = March 28, 2015|website = [[MTV]]|last = Roth|first = Madeline}}</ref><ref name=MTV>{{Cite web|url = http://www.mtv.com/news/2094803/ariana-grande-cashmere-cat-adore-song/|archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20150305004339/http://www.mtv.com/news/2094803/ariana-grande-cashmere-cat-adore-song/|url-status = dead|archive-date = March 5, 2015|title = Ariana Grande's New Song With Cashmere Cat Will Have You Screaming 'My My Myyyyy My My My My!'|date = March 3, 2015|accessdate = March 28, 2015|website = MTV|last = Lindner|first = Emilee}}</ref> The song features a heavy dose of breathy, falsetto vocals, atmospheric elements, subtle dance beats and punches of clattering percussion, and a vocal interpolation of [[Johnny Gill]]'s classic R&B hit "[[My, My, My (Johnny Gill song)|My, My, My]]".<ref name=MTV />

==Live performances==
The song was first presented on Grande's [[The Honeymoon Tour]] where [[Cashmere Cat]] performed the song with Grande during his opening act.<ref>{{cite magazine|last=Vena|first=Jocelyn|title=Watch Ariana Grande & Cashmere Cat Perform 'Adore'|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/6487745/watch-ariana-grande-cashmere-cat-perform-adore|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|date=March 4, 2015|accessdate=March 5, 2015}}</ref> It was also performed as an interlude for the [[Sweetener World Tour]]  <ref>https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cKuuUNl_JGA</ref> for leg 2 and 3

==Critical reception==
''[[USA Today]]'' ranked the song as one of the 50 best in 2015, writing, "Grande’s towering vocals have never sounded better than on this offbeat R&B track."<ref>McDermott, Maeve and Patrick Ryan. [https://www.usatoday.com/story/life/music/2015/12/22/songs-of-the-year-2015/77689258/ "The 50 best songs of 2015"], ''USA Today'', December 22, 2015</ref>

==Commercial performance==
Commercially, the song peaked at number 93 on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]], becoming Cashmere Cat's first appearance on that chart.<ref name="US"/>
