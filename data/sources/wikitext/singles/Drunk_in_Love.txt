"'''Drunk in Love'''" is a song by American singer [[Beyoncé]] featuring American rapper [[Jay-Z]]. The duo wrote the song with the help of its producers [[Detail (musician)|Detail]], [[Dre Moon|Andre Eric Proctor]], [[Rasool Diaz]], [[Brian Soko]], [[Timbaland]], [[J-Roc]] and [[Boots (musician)|Boots]]. The song was released as a single from Beyoncé's fifth studio album, ''[[Beyoncé (album)|Beyoncé]]'', on December 16, 2013. The song features prominent influences of [[Trap music (hip hop)|trap music]]. Its lyrics, which depict [[female sexuality]], are performed in a confident, salacious manner.

Many music critics called the song a follow up to Beyoncé's and Jay-Z's song "[[Crazy in Love]]" (2003). "Drunk in Love" peaked at number two on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] chart, spending eight weeks in the top ten. It peaked atop the airplay chart of South Africa and reached the top ten in France, Ireland, Lebanon, New Zealand, Portugal and the United Kingdom. At the [[57th Annual Grammy Awards]], "Drunk in Love" won [[Grammy Award for Best R&B Song|Best R&B Song]] and [[Grammy Award for Best R&B Performance|Best R&B Performance]].

The song's [[music video]] was directed by [[Hype Williams]] and shot in [[black-and-white]] in [[Golden Beach, Florida]] at a beach front [[manse]]. The visual features scenes of the pair singing the song together. "Drunk in Love" was performed by Beyoncé and Jay-Z at the [[56th Annual Grammy Awards|56th Grammy Awards]]. It was later added to the set lists of the second European leg of Beyoncé's [[The Mrs. Carter Show World Tour]] in 2013 and the pair's joint [[On the Run Tour (Beyoncé and Jay-Z)|On the Run Tour]] in 2014. Numerous remixes and cover versions of the song were made, including an official remix with American rapper and collaborator of both artists, [[Kanye West]]. The music video won the [[MTV Video Music Award for Best Collaboration|Best Collaboration]] and was nominated for the [[MTV Video Music Award for Video of the Year|Video of the Year]] at the [[2014 MTV Video Music Awards]].

==Conception and release==
[[File:Jay-Z @ Shawn 'Jay-Z' Carter Foundation Carnival (crop 2).jpg|thumb|left|upright|[[Jay-Z]] is credited as a co-writer and featured artist.]]

"Drunk in Love" was written by [[Beyoncé]], [[Detail (musician)|Detail]] and [[Jay-Z]] at the end of 2012. Jay-Z also appears as the featured artist on the track. Production of the song was handled by [[Boots (musician)|Boots]] and Beyoncé as well as Detail. "Drunk in Love" was recorded at [[Jungle City Studios]] and at [[Oven Studios]] in [[New York City]].<ref name="credits"/> Detail approached Beyoncé with a [[Beat (music)|beat]] he had created called "Drunk" inspired by different situations from being affected by alcohol.<ref name="nbc" /><ref name="details" /> Beyoncé further helped in the production of the song, adding elements that Detail had missed.<ref name="details" /> She and Jay-Z later [[Freestyle rap|free-styled]] their verses for the song and [[Timbaland]] contributed keyboards. [[J-Roc (record producer)|J-Roc]], [[Dre Moon]], [[Brian Soko]] and [[Rasool Diaz|Rasool Ricardo Diaz]] contributed to the production of the song. Elaborating on the conception of the song, Beyoncé said: "We just kinda had a party. It was so great because it wasn't about any ego... we weren't trying to make a hit record, we were just having fun, and I think you can hear that in the record."<ref name="nbc">{{cite web|last=Fowler|first=Brandi|url=http://www.nbcwashington.com/entertainment/entertainment-news/Beyonce_Talks_Surprise_Self-Titled_Album___I_Recorded_About_80_Songs_for_It_-237912371.html|title=Beyonce Talks Surprise Self-Titled Album: "I Recorded About 80 Songs for It"|publisher=[[WRC-TV|NBC Washington]]. In 2014, rapper [[Future (rapper)|Future]] claimed that he had previously recorded a [[Demo (music)|demo version]] of the song. He worked together with Detail who persuaded him to record vocals for the track as it would convince Beyoncé more easily to record it. Several days later, Detail informed Future that Beyoncé was in the studio recording "Drunk in Love". However, when the rapper was working on his second studio album ''[[Honest (Future album)|Honest]]'', he used the same melody of the demo version on a song called "Good Morning" as Beyoncé still hadn't released her version seven months after he was told that she worked on it. The video was filmed on August 15, 2013, in [[Golden Beach, Florida]] at a beach front [[manse]] that Beyoncé rented out for the shoot.<ref name="miami herald">{{cite news |url=http://blogs.herald.com/scene_in_the_tropics/2013/08/keeping-up-with-the-carters-beyonce-jay-zs-golden-beach-meet-greet.html |title=Keeping up with The Carters: Beyonce & Jay-Z's Golden Beach meet & greet |work=[[The Miami Herald]] |date=August 16, 2013 |access-date=December 15, 2013 |archive-date=August 18, 2013 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130818232209/http://blogs.herald.com/scene_in_the_tropics/2013/08/keeping-up-with-the-carters-beyonce-jay-zs-golden-beach-meet-greet.html |url-status=live }}</ref><ref name="black"/> It was directed by [[Hype Williams]] and shot in [[black-and-white]]; the clip depicts Beyoncé and Jay-Z "madly in love".<ref name="black">{{cite web|url=http://www.rap-up.com/2013/12/16/video-beyonce-f-jay-z-drunk-in-love/#more-175237|title=Video: Beyoncé f/ Jay Z&nbsp;– 'Drunk in Love'|work=[[Rap-Up]]|date=December 16, 2013|access-date=December 22, 2013|archive-date=December 17, 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131217015147/http://www.rap-up.com/2013/12/16/video-beyonce-f-jay-z-drunk-in-love/#more-175237|url-status=dead}}</ref> The black-and-white shoot was meant to reference the work of fashion photographer [[Herb Ritts]].<ref>{{cite news|last=Kornhaber|first=Spencer|title=Beyoncé's 'Flawless': The Full Story|url=https://www.theatlantic.com/entertainment/archive/2014/06/the-full-story-of-beyonces-flawless/373480/|access-date=June 30, 2014|work=[[The Atlantic]]|date=July 4, 2014|archive-date=July 3, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140703090817/http://www.theatlantic.com/entertainment/archive/2014/06/the-full-story-of-beyonces-flawless/373480/|url-status=live}}</ref> Talking about the video in an iTunes Radio session, Beyoncé said, "The shoot was extremely effortless and spontaneous and we completely captured the energy of the song."<ref name="black"/> She also went on to say that it was the most organic shoot she had ever done and further added: "I wanted to carry this idea of being in the moment and embracing mistakes and effortlessness into the video. I wish every video was like 'Drunk [in Love]'."<ref name="nbc"/> The clip was first released on the [[iTunes Store]] on the visual album itself on December 13, 2013, which contained a previously filmed music video for every song and was later uploaded to Beyoncé's [[Vevo]] channel three days later, on December 16, 2013.<ref name="black"/><ref name="vevo">{{cite web|url=http://www.latimes.com/entertainment/music/posts/la-et-ms-beyonce-shatters-itunes-sales-record-20131216-story.html|title=Beyonce shatters iTunes sales record|work=Los Angeles Times|first=Gerrick|last=D. Kennedy|date=December 16, 2013|access-date=July 4, 2014|archive-date=July 15, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140715045257/http://www.latimes.com/entertainment/music/posts/la-et-ms-beyonce-shatters-itunes-sales-record-20131216-story.html|url-status=live}}</ref>

The visual is almost six and a half minutes long. As the video begins, waves are seen crashing on a night time beach. Nearly a minute later, Beyoncé appears; she is strolling on a beach at night and she seemingly carries a massive trophy while an eerie music is playing in the background. Sporting a sheer negligee that reveals her bra and panties beneath, she twists her body in the sand. The singer also dances seductively against a backdrop of smoke and mouths the song's lyrics, staring at the camera. Beyoncé is later joined by husband Jay-Z, who is wearing a T-shirt and a backwards baseball cap. He raps with his eyes down holding a glass with alcohol in his hand while Beyoncé mouths the words behind him, smiling. The last seconds of the video show Beyoncé dancing and it concludes with close-up shots of the singer's face.
