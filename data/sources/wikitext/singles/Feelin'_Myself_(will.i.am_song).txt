"'''Feelin' Myself'''" is a song by American rapper & singer [[will.i.am]] featuring singer [[Miley Cyrus]], rappers [[French Montana]] and [[Wiz Khalifa]], and record producer [[DJ Mustard]]. It was released on November 26, 2013, by [[Interscope Records]] as the [[lead single]] from the re-release of will.i.am's fourth [[studio album]] ''[[Willpower (will.i.am album)|#willpower]]'' (2013). It was written by its performers along with Jean-Baptiste, while production was handled by non-performing guest artist Mustard. An accompanying music video was premiered on November 26, 2013. The songs instrumental was noted for its similarity to "Gettin Tho'd" by rapper [[Paul Wall]], which was also produced by Mustard. The song peaked at number ninety-six on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]]. 

Outside the United States, the song peaked within the top ten of the charts in Belgium, the Republic of Ireland and the United Kingdom. "Feelin' Myself" has been described as a [[nightclub|club]]-oriented track which features its performers trading verses over a "glossy" [[West Coast hip hop]] beat. Despite generally negative reception, critics praised the song's production.<ref name="Digital Spy"/>

==Background==
"Feelin' Myself" marks the last of three collaborations between will.i.am and Cyrus in 2013. She first appeared on his track "[[Fall Down (will.i.am song)|Fall Down]]" for the original version of his fourth studio album, ''[[Willpower (will.i.am album)|#willpower]]'' (2013). will.i.am later appeared as a songwriter and producer on the track "Do My Thang" for Cyrus' fourth studio album, ''[[Bangerz]]'' (2013).

The song was also featured in a commercial for Samsung's Milk Music app, which was later discontinued.

==Critical reception==
Lewis Corner of ''[[Digital Spy]]'' gave the song a moderate review stating: "The final result may be devoid of any musical credibility, but it's annoyingly catchy - and much like will.i.am's strange anti-carb diet shoutout, we've got a feeling this is going to be just as hard to give up in 2014." {{rating|3|5}}<ref>{{cite web|last1=Corner|first1=Lewis|title=will.i.am ft. Miley Cyrus: 'Feelin' Myself' - Single review|url=https://www.digitalspy.co.uk/music/review/a539868/william-ft-miley-cyrus-feelin-myself-single-review.html#~oNdCqLWFIgvNU2|website=[[Digital Spy]]|access-date=August 17, 2014|date=December 21, 2013}}</ref> ''[[Spin (magazine)|Spin]]'' wrote the song features "bleepy, stripped-bare production from ratchet-music man of the hour DJ Mustard".<ref>{{cite web|title=NEXT Miley Cyrus Is 'Feelin' Myself' With French Montana, Wiz Khalifa in Will.i.am Video|url=https://www.spin.com/2013/11/miley-cyrus-feelin-myself-will-i-am-dj-mustard-video/|website=[[Spin (magazine)|Spin]]|access-date=August 17, 2014|date=November 26, 2013}}</ref>
James Shotwell of Under the Gun Review felt that the collaboration with the featured artists "didn't bring anything to the table you haven't heard before, and most of it has been said better on at least a dozen other songs. Will.i.am has gone from being on the forefront of pop music to barely staying on the bandwagon, and it’s getting more disappointing with each release."<ref>{{cite web|last1=Shotwell|first1=James|title=SINGLE REVIEW: will.i.am Featuring Miley Cyrus, Wiz Khalifa, and French Montana – "Feeling Myself"|url=http://www.underthegunreview.net/2013/11/05/single-review-will-i-am-featuring-miley-cyrus-wiz-khalifa-and-french-montana-feeling-myself/|publisher=Under the Gun Review|access-date=August 17, 2014|date=November 5, 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140819082945/http://www.underthegunreview.net/2013/11/05/single-review-will-i-am-featuring-miley-cyrus-wiz-khalifa-and-french-montana-feeling-myself/|archive-date=August 19, 2014|url-status=dead}}</ref>

==Music video==
An accompanying music video for "Feelin' Myself," directed by Michael Jurkovac with graphic designer [[Pasha Shapiro]], was premiered through [[Vevo]] on November 26, 2013.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.rollingstone.com/music/videos/will-i-am-and-miley-cyrus-keep-it-classy-in-feelin-myself-20131126|title=Will.i.am and Miley Cyrus Keep it Classy in 'Feelin' Myself': Wiz Khalifa and French Montana join pop hit-makers for simple, sharp new video|access-date=November 27, 2013|date=November 26, 2013|work=[[Rolling Stone (magazine)|Rolling Stone]]|author=Blistein, John}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/earshot/miley-cyrus-raps-williams-feelin-660079|title=Miley Cyrus Raps for Will.i.am's 'Feelin' Myself' (Video)|access-date=November 27, 2013|date=November 26, 2013|work=[[The Hollywood Reporter]]|author=Lee, Ashley}}</ref> All four artists appear in the video.
