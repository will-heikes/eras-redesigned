"'''Break Free'''" is a song by American singer and songwriter [[Ariana Grande]], featuring German music producer [[Zedd]]. It was released by [[Republic Records]] on July 2, 2014, as the second single from Grande's second studio album, [[My Everything (Ariana Grande album)|''My Everything'']] (2014). It was written by [[Savan Kotecha]] and its producers Zedd and [[Max Martin]]. Musically, the song explores the [[Electronic dance music|EDM]] genre, a musical departure for Grande, whose discography is primarily composed of [[Pop music|pop]] and [[Contemporary R&B|R&B]].

"Break Free" peaked at number four on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]], becoming Grande's third consecutive top-ten from ''My Everything'' after "[[Problem (Ariana Grande song)|Problem]]" and "[[Bang Bang (Jessie J, Ariana Grande and Nicki Minaj song)|Bang Bang]]", and her fourth overall. Similarly, "Break Free" hit number three on the Australian [[ARIA Charts]], and number five on the [[Canadian Hot 100]] and the [[Recorded Music NZ|New Zealand Singles Chart]]. Overall, the song attained top-ten peaks in multiple countries including Austria, Czech Republic, Finland, Ireland, the Netherlands, Norway, Poland, Slovakia, and Sweden, as well as reaching the top twenty in Belgium, Denmark, Germany, Italy, Japan, Poland, Scotland, South Korea, Spain, Switzerland, and the United Kingdom.

The song has been certified [[RIAA certification|quintiple platinum]] by the [[Recording Industry Association of America]] (RIAA) and has sold over 1.9 million copies in the United States as of 2020, making it Grande's seventh single to have sold over a million. Its music video was directed by [[Chris Marrs Piliero]] and pays homage to [[science fiction]] films, and has received over 1.2 billion views on [[YouTube]] as of 2026. "Break Free" also marked a milestone in Grande's career, as it entered the top ten on the ''Billboard'' Hot 100 the same week "Problem" and "Bang Bang" were charting. Consequently, she became the second lead female artist in chart history to simultaneously hold three positions in the top ten on the Hot 100 after [[Adele]].

==Release==
On May 10, 2014, during an interview after her performance at [[Wango Tango]], Grande revealed that she wanted "Break Free" to be her next single after "[[Problem (Ariana Grande song)|Problem]]". She said, "I did a song with Zedd, who I just met for the first time here, and I'm really excited about that song. I really want that to be the second single. My whole team is like, 'Oh it's too early to pick a new single,' but I'm really leaning towards that one. I love that song so much. He's fantastic and the song is really fun."<ref>{{cite web|title=Ariana Grande Backstage at Wango Tango|url=http://www.kiisfm.com/pages/events/wango-tango/videos.php?mid=24752018&q=all&c=1411944471|publisher=kiisfm.com|access-date=May 10, 2014|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151227054949/http://www.kiisfm.com/pages/events/wango-tango/videos.php?mid=24752018&q=all&c=1411944471|archive-date=December 27, 2015|df=mdy-all}}</ref>

Grande began dropping hints about her second single on social media throughout the month of June but did not confirm that it was her Zedd collaboration until June 21, at which point she also revealed the song's name on [[Twitter]]. To build hype, Grande started an online countdown for the ten days leading up to "Break Free's" release. On June 28, Grande uploaded a teaser video with a snippet of the song to her [[Instagram]] account.<ref>{{cite web|last1=Grande|first1=Ariana|title="Break Free" teaser video|url=http://instagram.com/p/pzznhISWVy/ |archive-url=https://ghostarchive.org/iarchive/s/instagram/pzznhISWVy |archive-date=December 26, 2021 |url-access=registration|website=Instagram|date=June 28, 2014}}{{cbignore}}</ref>

==Critical reception==
Lewis Corner from [[Digital Spy]] gave the song four stars out of five and said that the track is "too catchy to be generic".<ref>{{cite web|last1=Corner|first1=Lewis|title=Ariana Grande ft. Zedd: 'Break Free' review – Too catchy to be generic|website=[[Digital Spy]]|url=http://www.digitalspy.co.uk/music/review/a590180/ariana-grande-ft-zedd-break-free-review-too-catchy-to-be-generic.html#~p0WfrKDpzdg2Ih|date=August 13, 2014|access-date=January 10, 2015|archive-date=September 24, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150924164613/http://www.digitalspy.co.uk/music/review/a590180/ariana-grande-ft-zedd-break-free-review-too-catchy-to-be-generic.html#~p0WfrKDpzdg2Ih|url-status=live}}</ref> Larry Fitzmaurice from ''[[Pitchfork (website)|Pitchfork]]'' also liked the song praising both the production part of the song and the vocal saying that, "from the zippy opening synth line to the verse's insistent pounding, 'Break Free' certainly invites comparisons to Swedish pop mastermind [[Robyn]]; as ever, Grande brings her own skyscraping voice in top form, so vocally the comparisons don't stick." He compared the message of the song to [[Kelly Clarkson]]'s "[[Since U Been Gone]]".<ref name="Pitchfork"/>

"Break Free" was nominated at the [[2014 Teen Choice Awards]] in the [[Teen Choice Award for Choice Music - Break-Up Song|Break-Up Song]] category.<ref>{{cite web|last1=Seemayer|first1=Zach|title=The 2014 Teen Choice Awards Reveals Their Very Long List of Nominees|date=July 18, 2014|url=http://www.etonline.com/awards/148577_2014_teen_choice_awards_reveals_their_list_of_nominees/|access-date=July 18, 2014|archive-date=October 25, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141025195444/http://www.etonline.com/awards/148577_2014_teen_choice_awards_reveals_their_list_of_nominees/|url-status=live}}</ref> It was also nominated for Top Dance/Electronic Song at the [[2015 Billboard Music Awards]].<ref>{{cite magazine |url=https://www.billboard.com/articles/events/bbma-2015/6524277/billboard-music-awards-2015-finalists |title=Music Awards 2015: See the Full List of Finalists |magazine=Billboard |access-date=December 26, 2015 |archive-date=April 9, 2015 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150409005544/http://www.billboard.com/articles/events/bbma-2015/6524277/billboard-music-awards-2015-finalists |url-status=live }}</ref>

==Commercial performance==
In the United States, "Break Free" debuted at number 15 on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] with 161,000 digital downloads sold within its first week, entering the [[Digital Songs]] chart at number five, for the issues dated July 10, 2014.<ref>Trust, Gary. On the [[Dance Club Songs]], the track reached a peak of number three, while topping the [[Dance/Electronic Songs]] chart for nine weeks, becoming Grande's and Zedd's first number one in the latter. The song has sold 1.9 million copies in the nation as of June 2020.<ref name="us_sales">{{cite magazine |last1=Trust |first1=Gary |title=Ariana Grande's Career Streams & Sales, From 'The Way' to 'Rain on Me': Ask Billboard |url=https://www.billboard.com/articles/business/chart-beat/9409079/ariana-grande-career-streams-sales-2020-ask-billboard |magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]] |access-date=June 26, 2020 |date=June 26, 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200626154254/https://www.billboard.com/articles/business/chart-beat/9409079/ariana-grande-career-streams-sales-2020-ask-billboard |archive-date=June 26, 2020 |url-status=live}}</ref>

Due to its [[catchiness]] and lyricism, the song went on to become a [[gay anthem]].<ref>{{Cite magazine |last=Harrison |first=Mitchell |date=2017-07-19 |title=8 Times Ariana Grande Was a Strong LGBTQ Ally |url=https://www.billboard.com/photos/ariana-grande-lgbt-ally-list-7864993/ |access-date=2024-04-03 |magazine=Billboard |language=en-US |archive-date=April 3, 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240403183250/https://www.billboard.com/photos/ariana-grande-lgbt-ally-list-7864993/ |url-status=live }}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Galindo |first=Matt Stopera, Brian |date=2021-06-20 |title=69 Songs That Physically Make Gay People Scream |url=https://www.buzzfeed.com/mjs538/iconic-gay-anthems |access-date=2024-04-03 |website=BuzzFeed |language=en |archive-date=June 23, 2021 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210623175026/https://www.buzzfeed.com/mjs538/iconic-gay-anthems |url-status=live }}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=The 50 Best Gay Anthems Of The 2010s |url=https://www.pride.com/gay-music/gay-anthems |access-date=2024-04-03 |website=www.pride.com |language=en |archive-date=April 3, 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240403183250/https://www.pride.com/gay-music/gay-anthems |url-status=live }}</ref>

==Uses in other media==
The song was featured in various video games, like ''[[Just Dance 2015]]'' and ''[[Samba de Amigo: Party Central]]''. The song was also used in ''[[The Voice (franchise)|The Voice]]'' media franchise, including a video game based on the show and the episode, "The Blind Auditions, Part 5".

Other appearances include the series premiere of ''[[The Good Place]]'' "[[Everything Is Fine (The Good Place)|Everything Is Fine]]" and ''[[The Detour (TV series)|The Detour]]'' episode "The Tournament". It was also featured in the [[Glee (season 6)|season six]] of ''[[Glee (TV series)|Glee]]'', "[[Child Star (Glee)|Child Star]]", performed by [[Lea Michele]], [[Josie Totah]], [[Dot-Marie Jones]], [[Jane Lynch]], [[Matthew Morrison]], and [[Chord Overstreet]] as their characters [[Rachel Berry]], [[List of Glee characters#Myron Muskovitz|Myron Muskovitz]], [[Sheldon Beiste]], [[Sue Sylvester]], [[Will Schuester]], and [[Sam Evans]]. A small portion of the lyrics is changed for an unknown reason.

[[Dakota Lotus]], [[Olivia Sanabia]] and [[Ruby Rose Turner]] performs "Break Free" in the ''[[Coop & Cami Ask the World]]'' episode, "Would You Wrather Turn 13?" as their respective roles, Coop Wrather, Charlotte Wrather and Cami Wrather, after Ariana Grande fails to show up at Cami's birthday party. The episode aired during the [[COVID-19 pandemic]]. [[Reese Witherspoon]] and [[Nick Kroll]] performed "Break Free" in the 2021 film, ''[[Sing 2]]'' as their respective roles, Rosita and Gunter.

The song was also featured in the 2019 [[Max Martin]] jukebox musical ''[[& Juliet]]''. The song appears in Act 1, where it is performed by Angelique and Lance. The contestants who reached the final of [[Dream Academy]] performed the song in the penultimate episode.
