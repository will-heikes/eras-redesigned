"'''Same Old Love'''" is a song by American singer [[Selena Gomez]] from her second studio album, ''[[Revival (Selena Gomez album)|Revival]]'' (2015). The song was written by [[Charli XCX]], [[Ross Golan]], and its producers [[Stargate (music producers)|Stargate]] and [[Benny Blanco]]. The song was released as the second single from ''Revival'' on September 10, 2015, to [[contemporary hit radio]]. It was initially composed by Charli XCX with Stargate and later recorded by Gomez without the former in the studio at the time. 

"Same Old Love" is a [[Tempo|midtempo]] [[electropop]] and [[synth-pop]] song backed by a [[Wonky (genre)|wonky]] piano [[Loop (music)|loop]], a finger-click [[Beat (music)|beat]] and [[Italo disco]] synths. In the song, Gomez uses a forceful, emotive tone and a raspy delivery that are both uncharacteristic for her, accompanied by Charli XCX's background adlibs. According to Gomez, the lyrics are a representation of various stages of different relationships and are about getting rid of everything toxic in her life, also inspired by her relationship with her father.

Several remixes were commissioned for its release, including an official remix featuring rapper [[Fetty Wap]]. [[Music journalism|Music critics]] mostly complimented its catchy tune and understated, moody sound. The song peaked at number five on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]], marking Gomez's fourth top ten and second consecutive top five single from the album. "Same Old Love" also reached number one on the US [[Pop Airplay]] chart and the top ten in Canada, the Czech Republic, and Slovakia.

"Same Old Love" was accompanied by a [[music video]] directed by [[Michael Haussman]] and filmed in the [[Broadway Theater District (Los Angeles)|Broadway Theater District]] of [[Los Angeles]]. It premiered as an [[Apple Music]] exclusive on September 22, 2015, and was released on Gomez's [[Vevo]] channel on October 7, 2015. The video was well received by critics who complimented Gomez's styling and the emotional storyline. It was inspired by Gomez's relationships with her loved ones and fans. It featured Gomez witnessing several dramatic vignettes on the streets of Los Angeles and performing at a theater. Gomez promoted "Same Old Love" with several televised performances, including on ''[[The Ellen DeGeneres Show]],'' ''[[The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon]],'' and at the [[American Music Awards of 2015|2015 American Music Awards]].

==Background and release==
[[File:Charli XCX Live MTV Artist To Watch.JPG|thumb|left|upright|The lyrics to "Same Old Love" were written by [[Charli XCX]] (''pictured'') who also contributed with [[Backing vocalist|backing vocals]] during the [[refrain|chorus]].]]
"Same Old Love" was written by British singer Charli XCX and Norwegian production team Stargate. During a recording session for Selena Gomez's 2015 studio album ''[[Revival (Selena Gomez album)|Revival]]'', Stargate presented the track to Gomez, who acceded and subsequently recorded it. ===Background===
The accompanying [[music video]] for "Same Old Love" was directed by [[Michael Haussman]] and filmed in the [[Broadway Theater District (Los Angeles)|Broadway Theater District]] of Los Angeles.<ref name="Entertainment Weekly">{{cite magazine |last=Robinson |first=Will |date=September 22, 2015 |title=Selena Gomez Same Old Love music video |url=http://www.ew.com/article/2015/09/22/selena-gomez-same-old-love-music-video |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151105071130/http://www.ew.com/article/2015/09/22/selena-gomez-same-old-love-music-video |archive-date=November 5, 2015 |access-date=December 21, 2015 |magazine=[[Entertainment Weekly]]}}</ref> Filming took place during the evenings of September 15–16, 2015. According to Gomez, the concept was to portray her interpretation of the song's lyrics; a representation of various stages of different relationships. For this reason, she wanted Haussman to translate emotion and cinematography in a fluid storyline.<ref name="Interview">{{cite interview |last=Gomez |first=Selena |subject-link=Selena Gomez |interviewer=Vevo Show & Tell |title=Selena Gomez – Same Old Love (Vevo Show & Tell) |url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MJ5YmBf9hXs |access-date=December 22, 2015 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151215231158/https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MJ5YmBf9hXs |archive-date=December 15, 2015 |url-status=live |publisher=[[Vevo]] |via=YouTube |location=Los Angeles |date=November 9, 2015}}</ref> Regarding the video's inspiration, Haussman explained, "I think that [Gomez's] inspiration is kind of seeing that and all of humanity and feel like, 'OK, I'm not the only one'".<ref name="MTV News 3" /> He initially planned for Gomez's scenes to be shot entirely in a car, but later felt this would disconnect from her intended interpretation and found inspiration from singer [[Tony Bennett]] who was known for walking to his concerts.<ref name="MTV News 3">{{cite web |last=Lindner |first=Emilee |date=September 23, 2015 |title=7 Facts You Didn't Know About Selena Gomez's 'Same Old Love' Video |url=http://www.mtv.com/news/2280394/selena-gomez-same-old-love-music-video-facts-director-michael-haussman/ |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151210143839/http://www.mtv.com/news/2280394/selena-gomez-same-old-love-music-video-facts-director-michael-haussman/ |archive-date=December 10, 2015 |access-date=December 22, 2015 |publisher=MTV News}}</ref>
