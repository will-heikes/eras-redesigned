"'''Round Here'''" is a song recorded by American [[country music]] duo [[Florida Georgia Line]]. It was released in June 2013 as the third single from their album ''[[Here's to the Good Times]]''.<ref>{{cite news|last=Pacella|first=Megan|title=Florida Georgia Line Share How New Single 'Round Here' Caught Their Attention|url=http://tasteofcountry.com/florida-georgia-line-share-how-new-single-round-here-caught-their-attention/|accessdate=June 11, 2013|newspaper=Taste of Country|date=June 3, 2013}}</ref> It was written by [[Rodney Clawson]], [[Chris Tompkins]], and [[Thomas Rhett]].<ref>{{cite news|last=Conaway|first=Alanna|title=Story Behind The Song: Florida Georgia Line - "Round Here"|url=http://www.roughstock.com/blog/story-behind-the-song-florida-georgia-line-round-here|accessdate=June 11, 2013|newspaper=Roughstock|date=May 20, 2013|url-status=dead|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20131203010453/http://www.roughstock.com/blog/story-behind-the-song-florida-georgia-line-round-here|archivedate=December 3, 2013}}</ref>

==Composition==
According to the sheet music published at musicnotes.com, the song is written in the key of [[D-flat major]].<ref>[https://www.musicnotes.com/sheetmusic/mtd.asp?ppn=MN0125841 Florida Georgia Line "Round Here" Sheet Music]</ref>

==Music video==
The music video was directed by [[Peter Zavadil]] and premiered in June 2013.<ref>{{cite news|last=Papadatos|first=Markos|title=Review: Florida Georgia Line shines on 'Round Here' music video|url=http://www.digitaljournal.com/article/351645|accessdate=June 11, 2013|newspaper=Digital Journal|date=June 5, 2013}}</ref> It was filmed in the duo's hometowns, [[Ormond Beach, Florida]] and [[Monroe, Georgia]].<ref>{{cite news|last=Conaway|first=Alanna|title=Florida Georgia Line Show Fans How They Do Things "Round Here" In New Music Video|url=http://www.roughstock.com/blog/florida-georgia-line-show-fans-how-they-do-things-round-here-in-new-music-video|accessdate=June 11, 2013|newspaper=Roughstock|date=June 6, 2013|url-status=dead|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20130920012745/http://www.roughstock.com/blog/florida-georgia-line-show-fans-how-they-do-things-round-here-in-new-music-video|archivedate=September 20, 2013}}</ref>

==Commercial performance==
"Round Here" debuted at number 54 on the U.S. ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' [[Country Airplay]] chart for the week of June 1, 2013.<ref>{{cite news|last=Morris|first=Edward|title=George Strait Cops Top Album Slot (Again)|url=http://www.cmt.com/news/country-music/1708027/george-strait-cops-top-album-slot-again.jhtml|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130610182806/http://www.cmt.com/news/country-music/1708027/george-strait-cops-top-album-slot-again.jhtml|url-status=dead|archive-date=June 10, 2013|accessdate=June 11, 2013|newspaper=[[Country Music Television]]|date=May 25, 2013}}</ref> It debuted at number 36 on the U.S. ''Billboard'' [[Hot Country Songs]] chart for the week of December 22, 2012. It also debuted at number 89 on the U.S. ''Billboard'' [[Hot 100]] and number 90 on the [[Canadian Hot 100]] for the week of June 29, 2013. As of April 2014, the song has sold 1,143,000 copies in the US.<ref name="USSales">{{cite web|first= Paul |last= Grein |date= April 16, 2014 |title= Chart Watch: "Happy" tops 4M |work= [[Yahoo! Music]] |url= https://music.yahoo.com/blogs/chart-watch/chart-watch-happy-tops-4m-214123406.html |publisher= [[Yahoo!]] }}</ref>  The song was certified double Platinum by the RIAA in 2015 and triple Platinum in 2019.<ref name=RIAA />

The song became the duo's third consecutive number 1 on the Country Airplay chart dated for September 21, 2013, thus making Florida Georgia Line only the second duo after [[Brooks & Dunn]] to send its first three singles to number 1 on that chart.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www1.billboard.biz/bbbiz/photos/pdf/2013/country_update_0909.pdf|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160304090920/http://www1.billboard.biz/bbbiz/photos/pdf/2013/country_update_0909.pdf|url-status=dead|archive-date=March 4, 2016|title=On the Charts: Florida Georgia Line rolls three straight; Thomas Rhett's first Top 10|last=Jessen|first=Wade|date=9 September 2013|work=Billboard Country Update|accessdate=12 September 2013}}</ref> During its first week at the top of the charts, the Top 10 of Country Airplay included five songs co-written by [[Thomas Rhett]] or his father, [[Rhett Akins]], one of which was the former's own "[[It Goes Like This (song)|It Goes Like This]]".<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.roughstock.com/blog/thomas-rhett-and-rhett-akins-make-chart-history-as-family-is-responsible-for-writing-half-of-the-top-10 |title=Thomas Rhett and Rhett Akins Make Chart History as Family is Responsible for Writing Half of the Top 10 &#124; New Country Music, Listen to Songs & Video &#124; Roughstock.com |accessdate=2015-06-02 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20131102064612/http://www.roughstock.com/blog/thomas-rhett-and-rhett-akins-make-chart-history-as-family-is-responsible-for-writing-half-of-the-top-10 |archivedate=2013-11-02 }}</ref> The duo performed the song on the November 18th, 2013 episode of [[WWE Raw]].
