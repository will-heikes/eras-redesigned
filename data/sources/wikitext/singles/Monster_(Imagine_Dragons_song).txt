"'''Monster'''" is a song recorded by American [[Rock music|rock]] band [[Imagine Dragons]] for the soundtrack to the 2013 [[Action role-playing game|action role-playing]] game ''[[Infinity Blade III]]''.<ref>{{cite web|title=11 Sep|url=http://imagine-dragons-official-fans.tumblr.com/post/60992954071/fan-club-exclusive-a-new-single-called-monster|work=Imagine Dragons Official Fan Club|publisher=[[Imagine Dragons]], [[Tumblr]]|accessdate=12 September 2013}}</ref> It is the band's fifth appearance on a soundtrack.<ref>{{cite web | url=http://www.allmusic.com/album/answers-to-nothing-mw0002264306 | title=Answers to Nothing – Original Soundtrack | work=[[AllMusic]] | publisher=[[Rovi Corporation]] | accessdate=September 29, 2012 | author=Ruhlmann, William}}</ref> The song was also included on the band’s second studio album, ''[[Smoke + Mirrors]]''<ref name="single">{{cite web|title=Monster - Single|url=https://itunes.apple.com/us/album/monster-single/id696511366|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130912212818/https://itunes.apple.com/us/album/monster-single/id696511366|url-status=dead|archive-date=September 12, 2013|work=iTunes US|publisher=[[Apple Inc.]]|accessdate=12 September 2013}}</ref>

==Background==
"Monster", like all of Imagine Dragons' appearances on soundtracks, is a brand new song written specifically for the soundtrack, this time in collaboration with [[Chair Entertainment]] for their 2013 title, ''[[Infinity Blade|Infinity Blade III]]''. It is used in-game, for the final cutscene and when the player equips the "Imagine Dragon" weapon.<ref>{{cite web|title=Imagine Dragons' "Monster" Debuts in Infinity Blade III|url=http://epicgames.com/community/2013/09/imagine-dragons-monster-debuts-in-infinity-blade-iii/|publisher=Epic Gamers|accessdate=12 September 2013|url-status=dead|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20130916054606/http://epicgames.com/community/2013/09/imagine-dragons-monster-debuts-in-infinity-blade-iii/|archivedate=16 September 2013}}</ref> Speaking of the song, the band stated: "Chair has always been on the cutting edge of mobile gaming. ''Infinity Blade'' has been massive and we felt the song fit perfectly with the tone and visuals. We're pretty big gamers ourselves, so we couldn't be more excited."<ref>{{cite web|title=Imagine Dragons debuting new song through Infinity Blade III|url=http://au.gamespot.com/news/imagine-dragons-debuting-new-song-through-infinity-blade-iii-6414376|publisher=[[GameSpot]]|accessdate=12 September 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130913225551/http://au.gamespot.com/news/imagine-dragons-debuting-new-song-through-infinity-blade-iii-6414376|archive-date=13 September 2013|url-status=dead}}</ref>
