"'''I Won'''" is a song by American rapper [[Future (rapper)|Future]] featuring fellow American rapper [[Kanye West]]. The song was released on April 8, 2014, as the fifth single from the former's second studio album, ''[[Honest (Future album)|Honest]]'' (2014). The song features Future and West rapping about their respective then-fiancées, [[Ciara]] and [[Kim Kardashian]], whom they see as their "trophy wives". Following its release, the song peaked at number 98 on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] chart.

In August 2014, Ciara broke off her engagement to Future due to [[infidelity]] on his part. Kardashian and West are divorced as of June 2022.

== Background ==
"I Won" by [[Future (rapper)|Future]] was [[Hip hop production|produced]] by [[Metro Boomin]] and features a [[guest appearance]] from fellow American rapper [[Kanye West]]. The song features Future and Kanye West rapping about their respective fiancées, [[Ciara (singer)|Ciara]] and [[Kim Kardashian]].<ref name="consequenceofsound1">{{cite magazine|last=Young |first=Alex |url=https://consequence.net/2014/04/listen-futures-new-song-i-won-featuring-kanye-west/ |title=Listen: Future's new song "I Won" featuring Kanye West |magazine=Consequence of Sound |date=2014-04-07 |access-date=2014-04-28}}</ref> Overall the song is about the rapper's fiancées being their trophy wives.<ref name="stereogum1">{{cite web|url=http://www.stereogum.com/1674259/future-i-won-feat-kanye-west/mp3s/ |title=Future – "I Won" (Feat. Kanye West) |website=Stereogum |date=2014-04-07 |access-date=2014-04-28}}</ref> Future takes control of the song's first part, performing romantic and sexual bars through a massive layer of auto-tune.<ref name="rollingstone1">{{cite magazine|url=https://www.rollingstone.com/music/videos/future-and-kanye-west-romance-beachside-models-in-i-won-video-20140418 |title=Future and Kanye West Romance Beachside Models in 'I Won' Video - Video |magazine=Rolling Stone |date=2014-04-18 |access-date=2014-04-28}}</ref>

West starts his verse by mentioning his daughter North West saying, "You grew up on J. Lo, Timberlands by Manolo now, till one day I put an angel in your ultra sound."<ref name="vanityfair1">{{cite magazine|last=Makarechi |first=Kia |url=http://www.vanityfair.com/vf-hollywood/kanye-west-kim-kardashian-i-won |title="I Won": Kanye's Ode to His "Number One Trophy Wife" |magazine=Vanity Fair |access-date=2014-04-28}}</ref> Later in his verse West cleverly insults Kardashian's exes [[Reggie Bush]] and [[Kris Humphries]] simultaneously with the line, "I made it over [[NBA]], [[NFL]] players so every time I score it's like the Super Bowl".<ref name="consequenceofsound1"/> As ever, West appears fully aware of the reaction he's bound to receive when he says "If people don't hate then it won't be right."<ref name="vanityfair1"/> Kanye West also makes reference to his infamous music video for his single "[[Bound 2]]" and Kardashian's sisters and mother.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://pitchfork.com/news/54689-future-and-kanye-west-share-i-won-video/ |title=Future and Kanye West Share "I Won" Video &#124; News |website=Pitchfork |date=2014-04-17 |access-date=2014-04-28}}</ref> The latter reference closing his part of the song with West saying, "You could look at [[Kylie Jenner|Kylie]], [[Kendall Jenner|Kendall]], [[Kourtney Kardashian|Kourtney]] and [[Khloe Kardashian|Khloe]], All your mama ever made was trophies, right?", praising Kim and all her sisters as beautiful.<ref>{{cite magazine|last=Boardman |first=Madeline |url=http://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-news/news/kanye-west-raps-about-kim-kardashians-butt-in-futures-i-won-201484 |title=Kanye West Raps About Kim Kardashian In Future's "I Won" - Us Weekly |magazine=Usmagazine.com |date=2014-04-08 |access-date=2014-04-28}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|last=Dobbins |first=Amanda |url=http://www.vulture.com/2014/04/future-kanye-i-won.html |title=Listen to Future and Kanye's 'I Won' |publisher=Vulture |date=2014-04-14 |access-date=2014-04-28}}</ref><ref>{{cite news|url=http://www.gigwise.com/videos/90312/watch-kanye-west-and-future-reveal-music-video-for-i-won |title=Watch: Kanye West and Future reveal music video for 'I Won' |work=Gigwise |date=2014-04-18 |access-date=2014-04-28}}</ref>

In an April 2014 interview with [[Pitchfork Media|Pitchfork]], Future spoke of the song saying, ""I Won" is not a love song. It's just me uplifting women in general. I'm giving women the power to treat themselves as a trophy—to show that and know it in yourself and be confident—because when they do that, whoever they're with is winning. I'm not teaching motherfuckers how to love."<ref>{{cite web|url=http://pitchfork.com/features/update/9365-future/ |title=Update: Future &#124; Features |website=Pitchfork |date=2014-04-02 |access-date=2014-04-28}}</ref>

== Promotion ==
To promote the song, Future released an online game to go with the song on May 7, 2014. The concept of the game is to click on either Future or Kanye West to make them throw gold chains at the women walking across the beach in their view.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.complex.com/music/2014/05/future-i-won-video-game|title=Drape Women with Gold in Future's New "I Won" Video Game|website=[[Complex Networks]]}}</ref> If the player hits the women with the chain they become trophies similar to the single cover. Upon its release, the video was criticized by writers for major publications as sexist.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.hiphopdx.com/index/news/id.28679/title.kanye-west-future-criticized-as-sexist-for-i-won-video-game|title = Kanye West & Future Criticized as "Sexist" for "I Won" Video Game|date = 8 May 2014}}</ref> Gaby Whitehill of ''[[Gigwise]]'' stated, "'I Won' saw Future and Kanye boast about how attractive their respective spouses - Ciara and Kim Kardashian - are, calling them "trophies". Now, you too can turn grown adult women into inanimate objects with the 'I Won' video game! To gain points, carelessly toss chains at bikini-clad women as they saunter across the beach, and turn them into a gold cup. Brilliant. Play it below, if you really hate yourself/women that much."<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.gigwise.com/news/90815/future-and-kanye-wests-i-won-turned-into-sexist-video-game|title = Future and Kanye West's 'I Won' turned into sexist video game | date=May 2014 }}</ref> Lauren Duca of ''[[Huffington Post]]'' said, "That warranted a conversation about an intersectional understanding of marriage. The horrifyingly sexist video game that Future released this week as a companion to the song? That really only warrants a conversation about the next available time you can take a shower."<ref>{{cite news| url=http://www.huffingtonpost.com/2014/05/08/future-kanye-video-game_n_5287219.html | work=Huffington Post | title=Future And Kanye's 'I Won' Turned Into Horrifyingly Sexist Video Game | date=2014-05-08}}</ref>

== Music video ==
The music video for "I Won" was filmed on a picturesque California beach and was directed by [[Hype Williams]] in March 2014. Speaking of the video with [[WQHT|Hot 97]] later that month, Future said, "It's incredible. We on the water, we on the beach, and it's beautiful women. We made [them] look like the old Hype Williams video girls, when video girls were stars back then."<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/1725006/kanye-west-gets-his-designer-clothes-wet-during-future-video-shoot/ |archive-url=https://archive.today/20140428092222/http://www.mtv.com/news/1725006/kanye-west-gets-his-designer-clothes-wet-during-future-video-shoot/ |url-status=dead |archive-date=April 28, 2014 |title=Kanye West Gets His Designer Clothes Wet During Future Video Shoot |publisher=MTV |date=2014-03-28 |access-date=2014-04-28}}</ref> The clip was released on April 17, 2014.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.rap-up.com/2014/04/17/video-future-f-kanye-west-i-won/ |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140419012738/http://www.rap-up.com/2014/04/17/video-future-f-kanye-west-i-won/ |url-status=dead |archive-date=April 19, 2014 |title=Video: Future f/ Kanye West – 'I Won' |publisher=Rap-Up.com |date=2014-04-17 |access-date=2014-04-28}}</ref> In the black-and-white video West and Future "serenade a slew of curvaceous models striking sexy poses on a private beach."<ref name="rollingstone1"/><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.spin.com/2014/04/future-kanye-west-i-won-video-honest/ |title=Future and Kanye West Serenade Swimsuit Models in 'I Won' Video &#124; == Critical reception ==
"I Won" was met with generally positive reviews from [[music critic]]s. Chris DeVille of [[Stereogum]] called the song excellent and praised the production.<ref name="stereogum1"/>

== Chart performance ==
"I Won" sold 19,276 copies in its first week in the United States according to [[Nielsen Soundscan]], which ended on April 13, 2014. It would be highest debut on the ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' [[Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs]] chart that week.<ref>{{cite web |last=Baker |first=Soren |url=http://www.hiphopdx.com/index/news/id.28391/title.hip-hop-singles-sales-week-of-04-13-14 |title=Hip Hop Singles Sales: Week Of 04/13/14 &#124; Get The Latest Hip Hop News, Rap News & Hip Hop Album Sales |publisher=HipHop DX |date=2014-04-18 |access-date=2014-04-28 |archive-date=2014-04-24 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140424054207/http://www.hiphopdx.com/index/news/id.28391/title.hip-hop-singles-sales-week-of-04-13-14 |url-status=dead }}</ref> The single peaked at number 98 on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] and spent only a total of 3 weeks on the chart. On December 16, 2015, the song was certified [[Recording Industry Association of America certification#List of certifications|Gold]] by the [[Recording Industry Association of America]] (RIAA) for sales of over 500,000 digital copies in the United States.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.riaa.com/gold-platinum/?tab_active=default-award&se=Future+Kanye+West+I+Won#search_section|title=Gold & Platinum - RIAA|publisher=[[Recording Industry Association of America|RIAA]]|access-date=October 17, 2018}}</ref>
