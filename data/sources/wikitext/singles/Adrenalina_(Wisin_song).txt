"'''Adrenalina'''" ({{Translation|"Adrenaline"}}) is a song recorded by Puerto Rican rapper [[Wisin]], featuring Puerto Rican singer [[Ricky Martin]] and Puerto Rican - American singer [[Jennifer Lopez]] for the former's second studio album, ''[[El Regreso del Sobreviviente]]'' (2014). It was written by Martin, Lopez, Wisin, [[Chris Jedi|Chris Jeday]], Luis Enrique Ortiz Rivera, and José "Gocho" Torres, while the production was handled by Jeday. The song was released for [[Music download|digital download]] by [[Sony Music Latin]] on February 18, 2014, as the second [[single (music)|single]] from the album. A Spanish language [[Latin music|Latin]] [[Dance music|dance]] song with [[Hip hop music|hip hop]] and [[pop rock]] influences, its lyrics are sensual.

"Adrenalina" received widely positive reviews from [[Music journalism|music critics]], who complimented its danceable rhythm and noted its featured artists as two Latin icons. The song was nominated for [[Billboard Latin Music Award for Latin Rhythm Airplay Song of the Year|Latin Rhythm Airplay Song of the Year]] at the [[2015 Latin Billboard Music Awards]], as well as both [[Lo Nuestro Award for Urban Song of the Year|Urban Song of the Year]] and [[Lo Nuestro Award for Collaboration of the Year|Collaboration of the Year]] at the [[Premio Lo Nuestro 2015|2015 Premio Lo Nuestro]]. It was commercially successful, reaching the top five in Mexico and Spain, and on the ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' [[Hot Latin Songs]] chart in the United States. It also reached the summit of the ''Billboard'' [[Latin Airplay]], [[Latin Rhythm Airplay]], and [[Latin Digital Songs|Latin Digital Song Sales]] charts. The song has received several [[Music recording certification|certification]]s, including platinum in Mexico and double platinum in Spain.

An accompanying [[music video]], released on March 3, 2014, was directed by [[Jessy Terrero]] and filmed in [[Miami|Miami, Florida]]. The visual depicts Martin and Lopez dancing sensually together, street chases, the underground atmosphere, and a final clandestine party. It was nominated for [[Lo Nuestro Award for Video of the Year|Video of the Year]] at the Premio Lo Nuestro and has received over 870 million views on [[YouTube]]. To promote "Adrenalina", the singers performed it on several television programs and award shows, including [[2014 Premios Juventud]].

==Background and release==
On October 30, 2012, [[Univision]] revealed that reggaeton duo [[Wisin & Yandel]] had recorded a song entitled "Adrenalina" along with [[Jennifer Lopez]] and [[Ricky Martin]]. The duo had previously collaborated with Martin on "[[Frío (song)|Frío]]" (2011), and with Lopez on "[[Follow the Leader (Wisin & Yandel song)|Follow the Leader]]" (2012). [[Wisin]] explained that "Adrenalina" had originally been recorded for Lopez, but upon hearing the tones and the vibes, they decided to invite Martin: "We told him that it was an explosion of theme. The public needs to see people united, and rather for the music so that it does not die."<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://musica.univision.com/noticias/article/2012-10-30/jlo-sacara-adrenalina-en-trio|title=JLo sacará 'Adrenalina' en trío boricua con Wisin, Yandel y Ricky Martin|publisher=[[Univision]]|language=es|date=October 30, 2012|access-date=February 16, 2022|archive-date=February 21, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140221192558/http://musica.univision.com/noticias/article/2012-10-30/jlo-sacara-adrenalina-en-trio|url-status=dead}}</ref>

On January 26, 2014, Wisin shared the artwork for the [[single (music)|single]], announcing that the song would be released on February 17, 2014. Despite the previous announcements, [[Yandel]] of Wisin & Yandel had not participated in the song.<ref>{{cite tweet|user=wisin|author=[[Wisin]]|number=427243708665569280|date=January 26, 2014|title=El 17 de febrero sale el tema que revolucionara la musica latina 'Adrenalina' Wisin feat @ricky_martin & @JLo|url-status=live|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220216100804/https://twitter.com/wisin/status/427243708665569280|archive-date=February 16, 2022|access-date=February 16, 2022}}</ref> In an interview with ''[[People en Español]]'', Wisin talked about the collaboration:

{{blockquote| I am honored and extremely grateful to have created this partnership between Jennifer, Ricky and myself. It is something very significant that is reaching a worldwide audience. The chemistry between us is magical and I know that people are going to get infected with that great energy, that Adrenaline, from the song.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://peopleenespanol.com/article/estrena-el-video-de-wisin-adrenalina-con-ricky-martin-y-jennifer-lopez-miralo-aqui-video/|title=Estrena 'Adrenalina', el video de Wisin con Ricky Martin y Jennifer López: Míralo aquí (Video)|work=[[People en Español]]|language=es|date=March 4, 2014|access-date=February 16, 2022|archive-date=February 16, 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220216100618/https://peopleenespanol.com/article/estrena-el-video-de-wisin-adrenalina-con-ricky-martin-y-jennifer-lopez-miralo-aqui-video/|url-status=live}}</ref>}}

===Accolades===
In 2022, Cadena Dial ranked "Adrenalina" among the "10 songs to motivate you to do sports".<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.cadenadial.com/2022/10-canciones-para-motivar-deporte-80177.html|title=¡10 canciones para motivarte a hacer deporte!|language=es|publisher=Cadena Dial|date=January 5, 2022|access-date=April 11, 2022|archive-date=April 11, 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220411092958/https://www.cadenadial.com/2022/10-canciones-para-motivar-deporte-80177.html|url-status=live}}</ref> The song has received a number of awards and nominations. It was nominated for [[Billboard Latin Music Award for Latin Rhythm Airplay Song of the Year|Latin Rhythm Airplay Song of the Year]] at the [[2015 Latin Billboard Music Awards]], but lost to "[[6 AM]]" by [[J Balvin]] featuring [[Farruko]].<ref name="BMAs">{{cite news|url=https://www.billboard.com/music/awards/billboard-latin-music-awards-2015-finalists-6465538/|title=Romeo Santos and Enrique Iglesias Lead List of Finalists for ''Billboard'' Latin Music Awards|last=Cobo|first=Leila|magazine=Billboard|date=February 9, 2015|access-date=April 7, 2022|archive-date=April 7, 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220407145659/https://www.billboard.com/music/awards/billboard-latin-music-awards-2015-finalists-6465538/|url-status=live}}</ref><ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.billboard.com/music/awards/billboard-latin-music-awards-2015-winners-list-complete-6539008/|title=''Billboard'' Latin Music Awards 2015: Complete Winners List|magazine=Billboard|date=April 30, 2015|access-date=April 7, 2022|archive-date=April 7, 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220407145658/https://www.billboard.com/music/awards/billboard-latin-music-awards-2015-winners-list-complete-6539008/|url-status=live}}</ref> The track was also nominated for both [[Lo Nuestro Award for Urban Song of the Year|Urban Song of the Year]] and [[Lo Nuestro Award for Collaboration of the Year|Collaboration of the Year]] at the [[Premio Lo Nuestro 2015|27th Premio Lo Nuestro]].<ref name="Lo Nuestro">{{cite news|url=http://www.peopleenespanol.com/article/premio-lo-nuestro-2015-la-lista-completa-de-nominados-univision-enrique-iglesias|title=Premio Lo Nuestro 2015: La lista completa de nominados|work=People en Español|date=December 2, 2014|access-date=December 2, 2014|language=es|archive-date=April 7, 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220407155112/https://peopleenespanol.com/article/premio-lo-nuestro-2015-la-lista-completa-de-nominados-univision-enrique-iglesias/|url-status=live}}</ref>

==Commercial performance==
"Adrenalina" became a big hit, following its release.<ref name="Remezcla"/> The song debuted at number 12 on ''Billboard''{{'}}s [[Hot Latin Songs]] chart on March 15, 2014,<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/charts/latin-songs/2014-03-15/|title=''Billboard'' Hot Latin Songs|magazine=Billboard|date=March 15, 2014|access-date=April 7, 2022|archive-date=April 7, 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220407125042/https://www.billboard.com/charts/latin-songs/2014-03-15/|url-status=live}}</ref> becoming Wisin's 4th entry,<ref name="latinsongs"/> Lopez's 12th,<ref name="JLo latinsongs">{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/artist/jennifer-lopez/chart-history/htl/|title=Jennifer Lopez Chart History (Hot Latin Songs)|magazine=Billboard|access-date=April 7, 2022|archive-date=April 7, 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220407125130/https://www.billboard.com/artist/jennifer-lopez/chart-history/htl/|url-status=live}}</ref> and Martin's 39th.<ref name="RM latinsongs">{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/artist/ricky-martin/chart-history/htl/|title=Ricky Martin Chart History (Hot Latin Songs)|magazine=Billboard|access-date=November 28, 2021|archive-date=November 28, 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20211128114508/https://www.billboard.com/artist/ricky-martin/chart-history/htl/|url-status=live}}</ref> The following week it climbed to its peak at number two, being held off the top spot by "[[Odio (song)|Odio]]" (2014) by [[Romeo Santos]] featuring [[Drake (musician)|Drake]],<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/charts/latin-songs/2014-03-22/|title=''Billboard'' Hot Latin Songs|magazine=Billboard|date=March 22, 2014|access-date=April 7, 2022|archive-date=April 7, 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220407125256/https://www.billboard.com/charts/latin-songs/2014-03-22/|url-status=live}}</ref> while giving Wisin his second, Lopez her fifth, and Martin his 22nd top 10 hit. "Adrenalina" spent a total of 26 weeks on the chart and marked Wisin's highest-charting and longest running single at the time, surpassing "Que Viva La Vida" (2013). It also ties "[[No Me Ames]]" (1999) and "[[On the Floor|Ven a Bailar]]" (2011) as Lopez's longest-charting hits in her career.<ref name="latinsongs"/><ref name="JLo latinsongs"/><ref name="RM latinsongs"/> "Adrenalina" spent 10 weeks atop the US [[Latin Rhythm Airplay]] chart and reached the summit of both the US [[Latin Digital Songs|Latin Digital Song Sales]] and [[Latin Airplay]] charts.<ref name="latinairplay"/><ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/artist/wisin/chart-history/lra/|title=Wisin Chart History (Latin Rhythm Airplay)|magazine=Billboard|access-date=April 7, 2022|archive-date=April 7, 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220407131419/https://www.billboard.com/artist/wisin/chart-history/ldt/|url-status=live}}</ref> On [[Latin Pop Airplay]], it peaked at number two, being held off the top spot by "Odio".<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/charts/latin-pop-airplay/2014-04-26/|title=''Billboard'' Latin Pop Airplay|magazine=Billboard|date=April 26, "Adrenalina" debuted and peaked at number 94 on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] on March 22, 2014, becoming Wisin's first entry, Lopez's 24th, and Martin's 16th.<ref name="hot100"/><ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/artist/jennifer-lopez/chart-history/hsi/|title=Jennifer Lopez Chart History (Hot 100)|magazine=Billboard|access-date=March 31, In Spain, "Adrenalina" debuted at number 28 on March 2, 2014, and subsequently peaked at number three on the chart issue dated March 16, 2014. The song spent 52 weeks on the chart and was certified double platinum by the [[Productores de Música de España]] (PROMUSICAE), for track-equivalent sales of over 80,000 units in the country.<ref name="ES chart"/><ref name="ES cert"/> In Mexico, the track peaked at number four and was certified platinum by the [[Asociación Mexicana de Productores de Fonogramas y Videogramas]] (AMPROFON), for sales of over 60,000 units.<ref name="MX chart"/>

In 2015, on the [[Bailando 2015|tenth season]] of Argentine TV dance competition ''[[Bailando por un Sueño (Argentine TV series)|Bailando por un Sueño]]'', Verónica Ojeda and Nicolás Scillama performed their [[Freestyle dance]] choreography with the song, while Bárbara Vélez and Ignacio Pérez Cortés danced to it as their Latin pop choreography.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.unosantafe.com.ar/veronica-ojeda-elimino-samid-del-bailando-n2069463.html|title=Verónica Ojeda eliminó a Samid del Bailando|language=es|work=Diario Uno Santa Fe|access-date=April 29, 2022|date=August 12, 2015|archive-date=April 29, 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220429123347/https://www.unosantafe.com.ar/veronica-ojeda-elimino-samid-del-bailando-n2069463.html|url-status=live}}</ref><ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.eltrecetv.com.ar/videos/showmatch-2015/adrenalina-y-peligro-barbie-velez-aposto-a-una-coreo-caliente-pero-un_081639|title=¡Adrenalina y peligro! They received a score of 39 out of 40. [[Milo Manheim]] and [[Witney Carson]] danced to "Adrenalina" with [[Riker Lynch]] on the [[Dancing with the Stars (American season 27)|27th season]] of ''[[Dancing with the Stars (American TV series)|Dancing with the Stars]]'' in 2018. Their choreography received acclaim from the judges, who gave them the score of 29 out of 30.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://ew.com/recap/dancing-with-the-stars-season-27-episode-6/|title=Dancing With the Stars recap: 'Trio Night'|last=Fremont|first=Maggie|magazine=[[Entertainment Weekly]]|access-date=April 29, 2022|date=October 15, 2018|archive-date=April 29, 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220429123420/https://ew.com/recap/dancing-with-the-stars-season-27-episode-6/|url-status=live}}</ref> Italian singer [[Mietta]] covered the track as "Farfalla" on the Italian singing competition television series ''[[Il cantante mascherato]]'' in 2020.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.ascoltitv.it/il-cantante-mascherato-2021-farfalla-canta-adrenalina-foto-e-video/|title=Il Cantante Mascherato 2021, Farfalla canta Adrenalina (foto e video)|last=Marighetti|first=Debora|language=it|publisher=Ascolti TV|access-date=April 29, 2022|date=February 12, 2020|archive-date=April 29, 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220429123335/https://www.ascoltitv.it/il-cantante-mascherato-2021-farfalla-canta-adrenalina-foto-e-video/|url-status=live}}</ref> The following year, Chilean singer [[Américo]] delivered a performance of the song on the Chilean television series ''[[¿Quién es la máscara? (Chilean TV series)|¿Quién es la máscara?]]'' as "Piña".<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.chilevision.cl/quien-es-la-mascara/presentaciones/te-pasaste-nos-sorprendiste-pina-subio-la-temperatura-del-publico-y|title='Te pasaste, nos sorprendiste': Piña subió la temperatura del público y los investigadores|language=es|publisher=[[Chilevisión]]|access-date=April 29, 2022|date=November 7, 2021|archive-date=April 29, 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220429123347/https://www.chilevision.cl/quien-es-la-mascara/presentaciones/te-pasaste-nos-sorprendiste-pina-subio-la-temperatura-del-publico-y|url-status=live}}</ref>
