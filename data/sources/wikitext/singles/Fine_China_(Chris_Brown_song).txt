"'''Fine China'''" is a song by the American singer [[Chris Brown]]. It was the [[lead single]] from his sixth studio album, ''[[X (Chris Brown album)|X]]'', and was released on March 29, 2013, by [[RCA Records]]. It was written by Brown, Eric Bellinger, [[RoccStar]] and [[Sevyn Streeter]], and produced by "RoccStar" and G'harah "PK" Degeddingseze. According to Brown and his producers, the song was inspired by the works of [[Michael Jackson]], [[Stevie Wonder]] and [[Sam Cooke]], among others.

The song received mainly positive reviews from music critics, who considered it "infectious and memorable", mainly for its "throwback style", also citing [[Justin Timberlake]], and Jackson's ''[[Off the Wall (album)|Off the Wall]]'' album as clear references in the song. "Fine China" was a moderate success on the charts, reaching the top-40 in Australia, New Zealand, the UK and the US, where it reached a peak of number 31 on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] chart. "Fine China"'s music video was released on April 1, 2013.

In February 2012, Chris Brown won his first career [[Grammy Award for Best R&B Album]] for his fourth studio album ''[[F.A.M.E. (Chris Brown album)|F.A.M.E.]]'' (2011), Eric Bellinger, who co-wrote "Say it With Me" for the album, thought, "Wow, I'm actually doing something here. I was doing something right because so many people get nominated and that's it; but to be a part of a project that actually won was the moment I realized I was in the right business." Bellinger also told ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' that he stayed with Brown in the studio every day for about two months. Then, RoccStar and PK played the beat and he immediately thought about Michael Jackson. So they said, "Go in the booth and do some Michael-type stuff."<ref>{{cite magazine |last=McDermott |first=Tyler K. |title=Eric Bellinger Talks Chris Brown's 'Fine China' & Usher's Upcoming Album |url=https://www.billboard.com/music/rb-hip-hop/eric-bellinger-talks-chris-brown-fine-china-usher-upcoming-album-1563135/ |magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]] |access-date=October 27, 2013 |date=May 22, 2013}}</ref> In another interview, Bellinger said, "[Chris Brown] really, really went back to the days of [[Sam Cooke]], but we were still able to keep it 2013 at the same time." Bellinger also cited Timberlake as another influence for the song and Brown's album ''X''.<ref>{{cite web |last=Smith |first=Trevor |title=Eric Bellinger Talks Writing Chris Brown's "Fine China" |url=https://www.hotnewhiphop.com/14273-eric-bellinger-talks-writing-chris-brown-s-fine-china-news |website=Hot New Hip Hop |access-date=October 27, 2013 |date=March 31, 2013}}</ref>

For one of the producers of the song, Roccstar, he was aiming for authentic music, saying that, "Seeing he's supposed to be the next MJ, it's kind of an automatic influence. I personally carry the Jackson sound a lot because I used to be around them so much. [...] With the bass line, I wasn't really thinking 'Let's make a Michael Jackson song,' I was just like 'let's make something that feels great.' Chris was like, 'We got to do some real shit this album. People need to feel exactly what I'm doing, it needs to be emotional and people need to be emotionally attached to this record,' so we just went in."<ref>{{cite magazine |last=Platon |first=Adelle |title=RoccStar Talks Producing 'Fine China,' Chris Brown's Next Single |url=https://www.vibe.com/article/roccstar-talks-producing-fine-china-chris-browns-next-single |magazine=[[Vibe (magazine)|Vibe]] |access-date=October 27, 2013 |date=April 4, 2013}}</ref>

===Storyline===
The video opens with a beautiful girl at war with her father, who does not wish for her to date Brown. Brown pulls up to her house in a car and she runs out to him, ready for a night on the town. It then diverges into a mix of "[[You Rock My World]]", "[[Smooth Criminal]]" and "[[Bad (Michael Jackson song)|Bad]]," hats, leather, studs, lights, dance into a club, fight with the mafia and all. Brown and his bros dance for his giggling lady love before an Asian gang shows up, first to a martial-arts-dance-battle, and then they're out for blood, with futuristic guns, more cars and the girl's father leading the charge. The mafia is met with Brown's gang outside, everybody's guns locked and stocked, and it ends with a "To Be Continued..." vibe.<ref name="hitfix">{{cite news|last=Hasty|first=Katie|title=Watch: Chris Brown's new song 'Fine China' is Michael Jackson meeting racial conflict: Watch|url=http://www.hitfix.com/immaculate-noise/watch-chris-browns-new-song-fine-china-is-michael-jackson-meeting-racial-conflict-watch|work=[[HitFix]]|access-date=October 28, 2013|date=April 1, 2013|archive-date=April 4, 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130404232338/http://www.hitfix.com/immaculate-noise/watch-chris-browns-new-song-fine-china-is-michael-jackson-meeting-racial-conflict-watch|url-status=dead}}</ref>

==Live performances==
Brown performed "Fine China" at the [[2013 Billboard Music Awards|2013 ''Billboard'' Music Awards]] on May 20, 2013. The performance had [[Eastern world|Eastern]]-inspired design and plot machinations, with a mock fight sequence. Andrew Unterberger of ''Pop Dust'' was favorable to his performance, writing that, "To be fair, if there was one pop star in music right now who was born to deliver an on-stage karate dance performance, it was probably Breezy, and he acquits himself admirably in the mock-combat. And at least with this performance, he saves the super-physical stuff for the end, leaving him free to, y'know, actually sing during his proper performance–something of a novelty for Brown at award-show performances of late."<ref>{{cite web|title=Chris Brown – 'Fine China' (Official Video)|url=http://popdust.com/2013/05/19/instant-billboard-music-awards-review-chris-brown-fine-china/|publisher=Pop Dust|access-date=October 28, 2013|date=May 19, 2013}}</ref> He also performed the song during a medley at the [[BET Awards 2013|2013 BET Awards]] on June 30, 2013, alongside "[[Don't Think They Know]]". The performance started with Brown wearing a black skeleton outfit and flanked by dancers marked with bright yellow "X"s on their otherwise black and white ensembles, while performing "Fine China". Later, he strayed soon and slowed everything down with a candle and blacklit performance of "Don't Think They Know". Later, [[Nicki Minaj]] appeared suspended from the ceiling in a ball. She delivered a spitfire verse before the spotlight returned to Brown, who put his hood up sheepishly at the conclusion of the performance.<ref>{{cite web|title=CHRIS BROWN + NICKI MINAJ OPEN THE 2013 BET AWARDS WITH 'FINE CHINA' + 'DON'T THINK THEY KNOW' MASH-UP [VIDEO]|url=https://popcrush.com/chris-brown-2013-bet-awards-nicki-minaj/|publisher=Pop Crush|access-date=October 28, 2013|date=June 30, 2013}}</ref>

Kyle Anderson of ''[[Entertainment Weekly]]'' called it "a buzzy, bubbly bounce with yet another killer chorus".<ref name="ew">{{cite magazine |last=Anderson |first=Kyle |title=Chris Brown returns with 'Fine China' single and video |url=http://music-mix.ew.com/2013/04/01/chris-brown-fine-china-video/ |magazine=Entertainment Weekly |access-date=October 27, 2013 |date=April 1, 2013}}</ref> Robert Copsey of ''[[Digital Spy]]'' commented on the song's influences, writing that it has "[[Stevie Wonder]]-esque R&B beats and a light-yet-growly vocal not too dissimilar to [[Michael Jackson]]", ultimately calling it, "less a gracious nod and more like a well-polished tribute act".<ref name="ds">{{cite web |last=Copsey |first=Robert |title=Chris Brown: 'Fine China' – Single review |url=http://www.digitalspy.co.uk/music/review/a470509/chris-brown-fine-china-single-review.html |website=Digital Spy |access-date=October 27, 2013 |date=April 4, 2013}}</ref> In a less favorable review, Will Hermes of ''Rolling Stone'' gave the song 2.5 out of 5 stars, calling it "generic, if well-built, R&B praise song to a woman, rocking a squishy, [[Daft Punk]]-y synth line and bright string arrangements, while the chorus centers on Brown declaring, 'I'm not dangerous' – a word choice he probably thought long and hard about, just as listeners will".<ref name="rs2">{{cite magazine |last=Hermes |first=Will |title=Fine China – Song Reviews |url=https://www.rollingstone.com/music/songreviews/fine-china-20130415 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130505003312/http://www.rollingstone.com/music/songreviews/fine-china-20130415 |url-status=dead |archive-date=May 5, 2013 |magazine=Rolling Stone |access-date=October 27, 2013 |date=April 15, 2013}}</ref>

==Chart performance==
After its release on April 2, 2013, "Fine China" entered  on the US ''[[Billboard Hot 100]]''at number 54,<ref name=acharts1>{{cite web |url=https://acharts.us/us_singles_top_100/2013/16 |title=US Singles Top 100 (April 20, 2013) |publisher=Music Charts |access-date=October 28, 2013}}</ref> and rose to the top 40 during the week of May 16, giving Brown his 30th top 40 single on the chart.<ref name=acharts2>{{cite web |url=https://acharts.us/us_singles_top_100/2013/21 |title=US Singles Top 100 (May 25, 2013) |publisher=Music Charts |access-date=October 28, 2013}}</ref><ref name="hot100">{{cite magazine |title=Chris Brown – Hot 100 Billboard |url=https://www.billboard.com/artist/chris-brown/chart-history/hsi/ |magazine=Billboard |access-date=June 14, 2019}}</ref> The song peaked at number 31, ion the week of June 8, 2013n.<ref name="hot100"/> However, it was Brown's lowest charting lead-single since "[[Wall to Wall (song)|Wall to Wall]]" peaked at number 79 in 2007.<ref name="hot100"/> The song performed better on the [[Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs]] chart and on the US [[R&B Songs]], peaking at number ten and three, respectively.<ref name="r&bchart">{{cite magazine|url={{BillboardURLbyName|artist=chris brown|chart=R&B/Hip-Hop Songs}}|title=Chris Brown Album & Song Chart History: R&B/Hip-Hop Songs|magazine=Billboard|publisher=Prometheus Global Media|access-date=July 4, 2012}}</ref><ref name="r&bsongs">{{cite magazine|title=Chris Brown – R&B Songs &#124; Billboard|url=http://www.billboard.com/artist/1486846/chris-brown/chart?f=1230|magazine=[[Billboard Magazine]]|access-date=October 28, 2013}}</ref> On November 10, 2017, the single was certified [[RIAA certification|platinum]] by the [[Recording Industry Association of America]] (RIAA) for combined sales and streaming equivalent units of a million units in the United States.<ref>{{cite web|title=Gold & Platinum – Chris Brown – Fine China|url=https://www.riaa.com/gold-platinum/?tab_active=default-award&ar=Chris+Brown&ti=Fine+China#search_section|work=[[Recording Industry Association of America|Billboard]]|access-date=June 14, 2019}}</ref>

In the United Kingdom, the song reached the top-thirty, peaking at number 23 on the [[UK Singles Chart]].<ref name=ukchart /> On the [[ARIA Charts|ARIA Singles Chart]], the song debuted and peaked at number 26.<ref name=aria /> It has since been certified gold by the [[Australian Recording Industry Association]] (ARIA), denoting sales of 35,000 copies. In New Zealand, the song was a little better, debuting and peaking at number 21 on the [[RIANZ]] chart.<ref name=nz /> In [[Belgium]], the song proved to be more successful, reaching a peak of number ten, giving Brown his sixth top ten in the country.<ref name="Flanders Tip" />
