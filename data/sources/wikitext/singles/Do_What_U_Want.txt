"'''Do What U Want'''" is<!--DO NOT change to "was" when the original version still exists whether available through retailers and streaming services or not--> a song by American singer [[Lady Gaga]] from her third [[Album#Types of album|studio album]], ''[[Artpop]]'' (2013). Featuring [[Guest appearance|guest vocal]]s from American singer [[R. Kelly]], the song was released as the album's second single on October 21, 2013, by Streamline and [[Interscope Records]]. The lyrics involve Gaga's declaring that her thoughts, dreams, and feelings are her own regardless of anything other people say. She wrote and produced "Do What U Want" alongside Paul "[[DJ White Shadow]]" Blair, with additional writing from Kelly, Martin "[[Tchami]]" Bresso and William "[[DJ Snake]]" Grigahcine. Blair first presented Gaga with the song's initial concept two years prior to its release. Production on "Do What U Want" was completed in 2013, with Kelly's vocals added soon after. Its sudden popularity upon premiering led to the song becoming the second single from ''Artpop''.

Musically, "Do What U Want" is a mid-[[tempo]] [[synth-pop]], [[electropop]] and [[Contemporary R&B|R&B]] song featuring [[1980s in music|1980s]]-style [[synthesizer]]s and an [[Electronic music|electronic]] [[instrumental]] track. The lyrics discuss the media's appetite for publishing opinion and critique, with Gaga telling detractors that her thoughts, dreams and feelings are her own, no matter what one does with her body. Upon its release, "Do What U Want" received generally positive reviews from music critics, who praised the song's simplicity and production. The single cover for "Do What U Want", a close-up of Gaga's buttocks in a floral thong, was photographed by American fashion photographer [[Terry Richardson]], who had also directed the song's accompanying music video. The video was planned to be released through [[BitTorrent]] in December 2013 but was cancelled for unknown reasons.

"Do What U Want" was a commercial success, topping the charts in Greece and Hungary while peaking within the top 10 in Austria, Canada, Denmark, France, Ireland, Italy, Lebanon, Norway, Scotland, South Korea, Spain, and the United Kingdom as well as the top 20 in Belgium, the Czech Republic, Germany, New Zealand, Sweden, Switzerland, and the United States. Live performances of the song were televised on the [[2013 American Music Awards]], ''[[Alan Carr: Chatty Man]]'', ''[[Saturday Night Live]]'', ''[[The Voice (American TV series)|The Voice]]'' and ''[[The X Factor (British TV series)|The X Factor]]''. Several [[remix]]es of "Do What U Want" were released, including ones with guest vocals from American singer [[Christina Aguilera]] and American rapper [[Rick Ross]].

On January 10, 2019, Gaga decided to have "Do What U Want" removed from music streaming services after the airing of a [[television documentary]], ''[[Surviving R. Kelly]]'', exposed new allegations of [[sexual misconduct]] and [[Sexual assault|assault]] by Kelly, who had previously been charged regarding [[R. Kelly#Allegations of child pornography and abuse|similar accusations in 2002]] but was [[Acquittal|acquitted]] in 2008. The song was removed from new [[LP record|vinyl]] and [[Compact disc|CD]] pressings of ''Artpop'' in November 2019.

==Writing and development==

Kelly]], Martin "[[Tchami]]" Bresso and William "[[DJ Snake]]" Grigahcine.<ref name="liner"/> It was produced by Blair and Gaga and features vocals by Kelly.<ref name=Brandle>{{cite magazine|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|url=https://www.billboard.com/music/music-news/lady-gagas-do-what-u-want-hits-the-web-listen-5763135/|first=Lars|last=Brandle|access-date=October 21, 2013|title=Lady Gaga's 'Do What U Want' Hits The Web: Listen|date=October 21, 2013|url-status=live|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131021114058/http://www.billboard.com/articles/columns/pop-shop/5763135/lady-gagas-do-what-u-want-hits-the-web-listen|archive-date=October 21, 2013}}</ref><ref name=Danton>{{cite magazine|magazine=[[Rolling Stone]]|access-date=October 21, 2013|url=https://www.rollingstone.com/music/videos/lady-gaga-releases-do-what-u-want-with-r-kelly-20131021|title=Lady Gaga Releases 'Do What U Want' With R. Blair recalled how in 2011 his friend Martin was playing him a particular [[beat (music)|beat]] from his own remix project. Blair liked the music and presented it to Gaga who had begun writing the song's lyrics while on the [[Born This Way Ball]] tour. Blair described its beat as "some space age [[George Jetson]] R&B sound".<ref name="dev">{{cite news|url=https://www.chicagotribune.com/entertainment/celebrity/aboutlastnight/chi-lady-gaga-r-kelly-do-what-u-want-20131021,0,1162457.column?track=rss|title=How R. According to the [[sheet music]] published at Musicnotes.com, "Do What U Want" is set in the [[time signature]] of common time, with a moderate [[tempo (music)|tempo]] of 96 [[beats per minute]]. It is composed in the [[key (music)|key]] of [[F-sharp minor|F{{music|sharp}} minor]] with Gaga's vocals ranging from the notes of E<sub>3</sub> to F{{music|sharp}}<sub>5</sub>. The song follows a basic sequence of D–E–F{{music|sharp}}m–E–D–E as its [[chord progression]].<ref name="sheet">{{cite web|url=http://www.musicnotes.com/sheetmusic/mtdFPE.asp?ppn=MN0127159|title=Lady Gaga feat. R. Kelly – 'Do What U Want' – Sheet Music|date=November 19, 2013 |access-date=January 8, 2014|publisher=Musicnotes.com. [[Universal Music Publishing Group]]|url-status=live|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140108071746/http://www.musicnotes.com/sheetmusic/mtdFPE.asp?ppn=MN0127159|archive-date=January 8, 2014}}</ref> "Do What U Want" was recorded at [[Record Plant Studios]] in Hollywood, California and at [[PatchWerk Recording Studios]], in Atlanta, Georgia. Gaga's vocals were recorded by Dave Russell and Bill Malina, while Kelly's by Abel Garibaldi and Ian Moonness. Russell held primary [[audio mixing (recorded music)|mixing]] of the track at Record Plant, with additional support from Benjamin Rice, Ghazi Hourani, Zane Shoemake, and Dino "SpeedoVee" Zisis. The song's [[instrumentation (music)|instrumentals]] include [[guitars]] by Tim Stewart, [[Programming (music)|programming]] by Rick Pearl, and [[audio mastering]] done by Gene Grimaldi at Oasis Mastering Studios in Burbank, California.<ref name="liner"/>

==Removal==
Gaga initially defended her collaboration with Kelly, saying that they both have had  "sometimes [...] very untrue things written about" them.<ref name="bbckelly">{{cite web|last=Savage|first=Mark|title=Lady Gaga breaks her silence on R Kelly|url=https://www.bbc.com/news/entertainment-arts-46808599|publisher=BBC News|date=January 10, 2019|access-date=May 3, 2026|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260503162018/https://www.bbc.com/news/entertainment-arts-46808599|archive-date=May 3, 2026|url-status=live}}</ref> However, on January 10, 2019, Gaga spoke out after the release of the [[Lifetime (TV Network)|Lifetime]] series, ''[[Surviving R. Kelly]]'', which documented the sexual abuse allegations against Kelly. The singer confessed her regret about working with Kelly, explaining that her thinking was "explicitly twisted" and that she had "poor judgment" at that time. Gaga vowed to support women who had been through abuse and by the next day had the track removed from iTunes and all streaming services.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.telegraph.co.uk/news/2019/01/10/lady-gaga-apologises-working-r-kelly-promises-withdraw-song/|title=Lady Gaga apologises for working with R. Kelly and promises to withdraw song from streaming services|date=January 10, 2019|access-date=January 10, 2019|newspaper=The Daily Telegraph}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://theblast.com/lady-gaga-r-kelly-song-removed-apple-music/|title=Lady Gaga's Collaboration With R. Kelly Removed From Apple Music|last=Vasque|first=Whitney|date=January 11, 2019|access-date=January 11, 2019|work=The Daily Blast}}</ref> Keith Caulfield from ''Billboard'' reported that even the remixes featuring Kelly were not playable on Spotify and other platforms, only the version featuring Aguilera was present. He theorized that since changes to digital music services take time, Gaga's management were working and removing the song from every medium gradually.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/articles/news/8493096/lady-gaga-do-what-u-want-r-kelly-removed|title=Lady Gaga's 'Do What U Want' Featuring R. Kelly Removed from Apple Music, iTunes, Spotify|last=Caulfield|first=Keith|date=January 10, 2019|access-date=January 11, 2019|magazine=Billboard}}</ref> Following Gaga's statement and just hours before the song's removal, sales of "Do What U Want" rose by 13,720% in the US to 2,000 music downloads, according to Nielsen SoundScan.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/lady-gaga-do-what-u-want-sales-surge-statement-r-kelly/|title=Lady Gaga's 'Do What U Want' Sales Surge After Her Statement Against R. Kelly|last=Caulfield|first=Keith|date=January 11, 2019|access-date=January 17, 2019|magazine=Billboard}}</ref>

The song was removed from new vinyl and CD pressings of ''Artpop'' in November 2019.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.billboard.com/articles/business/8533062/lady-gaga-artpop-without-r-kelly-do-what-u-want|title=Lady Gaga Lists New 'ARTPOP' Version Without R. Kelly|last=Cirisano|first= Tatiana|quote=New CD and vinyl versions of Gaga's famed third studio album are now available for preorder online, due out Nov. 11 -- nearly six years to the date of the album's initial Nov. 6, 2013 arrival. Both leave "Do What U Want," which features the controversial singer, off the tracklist.}}</ref>
