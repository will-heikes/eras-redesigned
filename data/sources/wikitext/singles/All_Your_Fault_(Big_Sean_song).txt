"'''All Your Fault'''" is a song recorded by American rapper [[Big Sean]] featuring fellow rap artist [[Kanye West]], from Sean's third studio album ''[[Dark Sky Paradise]]''. It contains samples from [[Ambrosia (band)|Ambrosia]]'s 1978 song "[[How Much I Feel]]" and features additional vocals from [[Travis Scott]].<ref>{{Citation|title = Big Sean – Dark Sky Paradise [Credits]|url = https://genius.com/Big-sean-dark-sky-paradise-credits-lyrics|access-date = 2018-11-11}}</ref> The song was produced by Kanye West, with co-production by OG Webbie, and additional producion by Nashiem Myrick, Mano, and [[Noah Goldstein]].

==Music video==
On June 24, 2015, a music video was released for the track, although it had not been released as a single. A video for the fellow album track “I Know,” featuring [[Jhené Aiko]]  released on the same day.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://pigeonsandplanes.com/news/2015/06/video-big-sean-ft-kanye-west-all-your-fault|title=Video: Big Sean ft. Kanye West - "All Your Fault"|work=PigeonsandPlanes|access-date=October 3, 2018}}</ref> Directed by Mark Mayer and [[Aaron Platt]],<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.stereogum.com/1811315/big-sean-all-your-fault-feat-kanye-west-i-know-feat-jhene-aiko-videos/video/|title=Big Sean – "All Your Fault" (Feat. ==Commercial performance==
Upon the release of ''Dark Sky Paradise'', "All Your Fault" charted at number 80 on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]].<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/artist/big-sean/chart-history/hsi/|title=Big Sean Chart History|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|access-date=October 3, 2018}}</ref> It peaked at number 28 on the US [[Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs]] chart in the same week and spent a total of three weeks on the chart.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/artist/big-sean/chart-history/bsi/|title=Big Sean All Your Fault Chart History|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|access-date=October 3, 2018}}</ref>
