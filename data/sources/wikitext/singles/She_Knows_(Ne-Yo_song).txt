"'''She Knows'''" is a song by American singer [[Ne-Yo]], taken from his sixth studio album, ''[[Non-Fiction (Ne-Yo album)|Non-Fiction]]'' (2015). It features a guest verse by American rapper [[Juicy J]]. The song was released on September 16, 2014 by Compound Entertainment and [[Motown|Motown Records]], and serves as the [[lead single]] from the album following the release of an [[Contemporary R&B|R&B]]-tinged first single, "[[Money Can't Buy (song)|Money Can't Buy]]".<ref name="iTunes">{{cite web |url=https://itunes.apple.com/us/album/she-knows-feat.-juicy-j-single/id917435703 |title=ITunes - Music - She Knows (Feat. Juicy J) - Single by Ne-Yo |website=[[iTunes]] |access-date=30 June 2022 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141112165211/https://itunes.apple.com/us/album/she-knows-feat.-juicy-j-single/id917435703 |archive-date=12 November 2014 |url-status=dead}}</ref> The song was produced by [[Dr. Luke]] and [[Cirkut (record producer)|Cirkut]], with vocal production by Jesse "Corparal" Wilson.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.hotnewhiphop.com/562277-ne-yo-she-knows-feat-juicy-j-new-song|title=Ne-Yo - She Knows Feat. Juicy J|last=Lilah|first=Rose|work=HotNewHipHop.com|date=September 18, 2014|access-date=September 26, 2014}}</ref>

==Critical reception==
[[AllMusic]]'s Andy Kellman put it alongside 'She Said I'm Hood Tho' and 'One More' as being "all tough but finely crafted slow jams."<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.allmusic.com/album/non-fiction-mw0002801550|title=Non-Fiction - Ne-Yo|last=Kellman|first=Andy|publisher=[[AllMusic]]|access-date=November 20, 2017}}</ref> Justin Charity of ''[[Complex (magazine)|Complex]]'' described the song as "the rare, contemporary hit, with obvious appeal to Juicy J and to the rest of us as a clean, big beat swing at urban radio play."<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.complex.com/music/2015/01/album-review-ne-yo-non-fiction|title=Ne-Yo Turns From Thoughtful Lover to Cheap Pickup Artist on His New Album|last=Charity|first=Justin|work=[[Complex (magazine)|Complex]]|date=January 27, 2015|access-date=November 20, 2017}}</ref>

==Remixes==
The official remix, the "R&B Remix" features [[Trey Songz]], [[The-Dream]] and [[T-Pain]]. Another official remix, released on February 20, 2015, features [[Fabolous]], [[French Montana]] and the original guest [[Juicy J]]. The song features vocals from the infamous Crack Kid on [[Vine (software)|Vine]]. The album remix features a second verse from Juicy J in the beginning of the song.

==Music video==
A music video was uploaded on [[YouTube]], October 8, 2014. Everywhere Ne-Yo goes, from the grocery store to the library to the office, he sees pole dancing girls. The pole art version was also uploaded on YouTube, November 10, 2014. The video is directed by [[Emil Nava]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.emilnava.com/portfolio/ne-yo-she-knows-ft-juicy-j/|title=Ne-Yo FT. Juicy J She Knows|website=Emilnava.com|access-date=24 May 2022}}</ref>
