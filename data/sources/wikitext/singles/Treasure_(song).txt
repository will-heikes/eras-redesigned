"'''Treasure'''" is a song by American singer and songwriter [[Bruno Mars]] from his second studio album, ''[[Unorthodox Jukebox]]'' (2012). It was written by Mars, [[Philip Lawrence (songwriter)|Philip Lawrence]], Ari Levine, and Phredley Brown, while production was handled by Mars, Lawrence and Levine under the name of [[the Smeezingtons]]. Due to the similarities with [[Breakbot]]'s "[[Baby I'm Yours (Breakbot song)|Baby I'm Yours]]", new writing credits were added. The song was selected as the third single by [[Atlantic Records]] and was first issued to airplay in Italy on May 10, 2013.

The single became Mars's seventh top ten hit in the United States since his career as an artist began in 2010. It has also reached the top-ten in countries like Australia, Canada, France, Israel, and New Zealand. It reached the top of the charts in Libya, Mexico, and South Africa. "Treasure" received media attention for its funk and disco vibe, which few artists have brought back, comparing it to [[Michael Jackson]]'s music.

The single was certified five-times platinum by the [[Recording Industry Association of America]] (RIAA), eight times platinum by [[Music Canada]] (MC) and four times platinum by [[Recorded Music New Zealand]] (RMNZ). The accompanying music video for the song was shot by [[Cameron Duddy]] and Mars in [[Las Vegas]], Hollywood and premiered on June 14, 2013. The video presents Mars's band, The Hooligans, in a live performance. "Treasure" was performed on all his tours since its release. It was also used at his [[Super Bowl XLVIII halftime show]] [[set list]].

==Background and production==
[[File:Breakbot (cropped) 2.jpg|thumb|right|upright|[[Breakbot]] inspired the song and was later credited as a writer.]]

In an interview with [[Rob LeDonne]], [[Philip Lawrence (songwriter)|Philip Lawrence]] explained the inspiration for the song: "Well, the thing we learned after [[The Doo-Wops & Hooligans Tour|touring with Doo-Wops]] was how it is we like to feel on stage when performing. We're fun, like to dance and party, and we didn't really get to do a lot of that on the first album. Even going to festivals and seeing big bands live, like [[Coldplay]] or [[Bruce Springsteen]], we knew the second time around we wanted something fun. It's the kind of song where the whole band can get up and jam and have this [[Earth, Wind and Fire]] kind of moment."<ref name="Bruno Mars' Right Hand Man">{{cite web |last=LeDonne |first=Rob |date=September 4, 2013 |title=Philip Lawrence: Bruno Mars' Right Hand Man Goes Solo |url=http://americansongwriter.com/2013/09/songwriter-u-philip-lawrence-bruno-mars-right-hand-man-goes-solo/ |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140312083245/http://www.americansongwriter.com/2013/09/songwriter-u-philip-lawrence-bruno-mars-right-hand-man-goes-solo |archive-date=March 12, 2014 |access-date=October 13, 2013 |work=[[American Songwriter]]}}</ref>

"Treasure" is one of the eleven songs composed and produced by [[the Smeezingtons]] for [[Bruno Mars]]'s second studio album, ''[[Unorthodox Jukebox]]'', with additional songwriting by Phredley Brown. It was [[audio mixing (recorded music)|mixed]] at Larrabee Sound Studios in Hollywood by [[Manny Marroquin]]. Ari Levine [[sound recording and reproduction|recorded]] the track at Levcon Studios in California. [[Charles Moniz]] served as the song's additional engineer. It was mastered by David Kutch at The Mastering Place.<ref name="album notes"/>

In December 2012, French electronic artist [[Breakbot]] tweeted Mars regarding the similarity to "Treasure" and his song "[[Baby I'm Yours (Breakbot song)|Baby I'm Yours]]" (2010).<ref>{{cite web |date=December 5, 2015 |title=Breakbot calls out Bruno Mars |url=http://stoneyroads.com/2012/12/breakbot-calls-out-bruno-mars |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160504235205/http://stoneyroads.com/2012/12/breakbot-calls-out-bruno-mars |archive-date=May 4, 2016 |access-date=March 30, 2015}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |date=December 17, 2012 |title=Bruno Mars accusé de plagiat sur "Treasure" par l'artiste Breakbot |url=https://fr.news.yahoo.com/bruno-mars-accus%C3%A9-plagiat-treasure-lartiste-breakbot-123200745.html |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160528194552/https://fr.news.yahoo.com/bruno-mars-accus%C3%A9-plagiat-treasure-lartiste-breakbot-123200745.html |archive-date=May 28, 2016 |access-date=March 30, 2015 |work=Purecharts |language=fr}}</ref> In May 2013, during an interview, Breakbot explained that the boss of Because Music had mentioned, a year previously, that Mars wanted to cover "Baby I'm Yours". As Breakbot was busy finishing the album, "it did not happen." Breakbot called "Treasure" a "rip-off" of his song; however, he was "cool with it"; he explained that he also had many influences on his music "with lots of bits taken from here and there".<ref>{{cite web |last=Dorof |first=Jakob |date=May 2013 |title=Breakbot: Interview |url=http://www.tinymixtapes.com/features/breakbot |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160510052114/http://www.tinymixtapes.com/features/breakbot |archive-date=May 10, 2016 |access-date=September 2, 2015 |work=[[Tiny Mix Tapes]]}}</ref> The song was re-registered with new writing credits, which included [[Breakbot|Thibaut Berland]] and Christopher Khan.<ref name="BBBS">{{cite magazine |date=November 26, 2019 |title=The 60 Greatest Dance Songs of the Decade: Staff List |url=https://www.billboard.com/articles/news/dance/8544613/best-dance-songs-of-the-decade-top-60 |access-date=December 4, 2019 |magazine=Billboard}}</ref>

"Treasure" is composed in the key of [[E-flat major|E{{flat}} major]] with a [[tempo]] of 112 [[beats per minute]].<ref name="musicnotes">{{cite web |title=Bruno Mars – Treasure (Digital Download) |url=http://www.musicnotes.com/sheetmusic/mtdFPE.asp?ppn=MN0113925 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://archive.today/20170114213430/http://www.musicnotes.com/sheetmusic/mtd.asp?ppn=MN0113925 |archive-date=January 14, 2017 |access-date=March 28, 2015 |work=Musicnotes}}</ref> The [[chord progression]] of A{{flat}}M<sub>7</sub>–(Gm<sub>7</sub>)–Fm<sub>7</sub>–Gm<sub>7</sub>–Cm–(B{{flat}}) repeats throughout the song, changing only to end phrases on B{{flat}}<sub>9sus</sub> (A{{flat}}/B{{flat}}), a deceptive [[Cadence (music)|cadence]]. Mars's vocals range from the low note of [[B♭ (musical note)|B{{flat}}<sub>3</sub>]] to the high note of [[E (musical note)|E{{flat}}<sub>5</sub>]].<ref name=musicnotes/> Its composition features punch bass lines, "high synthesizer chimes", call-and-response structure on the backing vocals and "the fill at the end of the chorus" is inspired by "a roller disco".<ref name="NJTM">{{cite news |last=McCall |first=Tris |date=July 14, 2013 |title=Song of the Week: 'Treasure,' Bruno Mars |url=https://www.nj.com/entertainment/music/2013/07/song_of_the_week_treasure_bruno_mars.html |access-date=October 27, 2019 |website=[[The Star-Ledger]]}}</ref> All the vocals on the track are taken from a "playbook of a '70s pop Lothario".<ref name="NJTM"/> It's has been dubbed as a "roller-rink-ready slice of pop-funk with heart-eyed lyrics". It also includes a "clap-along breakdown and a giddy chorus".<ref name="RS26">{{cite magazine |date=February 14, 2026 |title=The 12 Most Romantic Bruno Mars Songs |url=https://www.rollingstone.com/music/music-lists/most-romantic-bruno-mars-songs-1235513513/count-on-me-2010-1235513591/ |accessdate=February 22, 2026 |magazine=[[Rolling Stone]]}}</ref>

===Interactive music video===
After the release of his music video, Mars and his label created a website that integrated fan footage shot using [[Instagram]]'s video function with the official video. This was the first project to be created using Instagram's video functionality, launched in June 2013.<ref name="Interactive" />

When fans visited TreasureDance.com, they saw an old-fashioned-looking TV with a pair of knobs. After pressing play, the official video began to play, interspersed with fan footage that had been [[hashtag|hashtagged]] with #treasuredance. Users could skip footage by clicking on the television knobs; they could also click on Instagram usernames to see those dancers' accounts. Jules LaPlace, the [[Chief Technology Officer]] of OKFocus, who produced the website, and [[Ryder Ripps]], the company's creative director, said that the song's "tempo, temperament and general vibe" made it perfect for the project.<ref name="Interactive">{{cite web |last=Ehrlich |first=Brenna |date=June 22, 2013 |title=Bruno Mars Puts His Fans Center Stage With 'Treasure' Interactive |url=http://mtv.com/news/articles/1711034/bruno-mars-treasure-interactive-instagram.jhtml |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131004232952/http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1711034/bruno-mars-treasure-interactive-instagram.jhtml |archive-date=October 4, 2013 |access-date=September 3, 2015 |work=MTV News}}</ref>
