"'''Love Somebody'''" is a song by American [[pop music|pop rock]] band [[Maroon 5]]. It was sent to US [[contemporary hit radio|contemporary hit]] radio on May 14, 2013, by [[A&M Octone Records]], as the fourth and final single from their fourth studio album ''[[Overexposed (album)|Overexposed]]'' (2012). The song was written by [[Adam Levine]], [[Nathaniel Motte]], [[Ryan Tedder]] and [[Noel Zancanella]]; the latter two are also the producers. "Love Somebody" is a [[dance-pop]] song that lyrically equates love and physical intimacy and explores "salvation on the dance floor".

Upon release, the song was a respectable success on the charts and became the fourth top ten hit from the album on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]]. Despite its success, the single received mixed reviews from music critics who praised its composition, linking it to the works by [[Coldplay]], but criticized its production and Levine's vocals. Following the release of the album, due to strong digital downloads, the song peaked at number eight on the [[Gaon Chart|singles chart]] in South Korea.

==Background and production==
[[File:Ryancanalroomny-2.jpeg|thumb|left|upright|[[OneRepublic]]'s [[Ryan Tedder]] served as a co-writer of "Love Somebody".]]
In mid-2011, Maroon 5 started working on their fourth studio album. One of the members of the band, James Valentine spoke to ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' and revealed their plans to release their fourth studio album in early 2012.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.digitalspy.com/music/news/a335543/maroon-5-we-want-to-release-new-album-soon.html|title=Maroon 5: 'We want to release new album soon'|work=[[Digital Spy]]. [[Nat Mags]]|last=Corner|first=Lewis|date=August 17, 2011|access-date=January 21, 2013}}</ref> On March 26, 2012, the band posted a video on [[YouTube]] featuring numerous scenes from the band in the studio, recording the album.<ref>{{cite web|title=Overexposed - June 26th|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yHe3gC7GsyM&feature=player_embedded|work=Maroon5. [[YouTube]]| date=March 26, 2012 |access-date=April 10, 2012}}</ref> On June 26, 2012, the album was released under the title ''[[Overexposed (album)|Overexposed]]''.<ref name="overexposed">{{cite web|url=https://music.apple.com/us/album/overexposed/536292140|title=Overexposed by Maroon 5|work=[[iTunes Store]] (US). [[Apple Inc|Apple]]|date=January 2012 |access-date=January 21, 2013}}</ref>

The band's lead singer [[Adam Levine]] co-wrote "Love Somebody" with [[3OH!3]] singer [[Nathaniel Motte]], [[Noel Zancanella]] and [[OneRepublic]]'s [[Ryan Tedder]].<ref name="Notes"/><!-- Third-party source only --> The latter two also produced it and provided [[Programming (music)|programming]] and [[Key (music)|keys]].<ref name="Notes"/> Tedder and Zancanella also co-wrote and co-produced another song for ''Overexposed'' titled "[[Lucky Strike (Maroon 5 song)|Lucky Strike]]".<ref name="Notes"/> "Love Somebody" was [[Engineering (music)|engineered]] by Smith Carlson at the Patriot Studios in [[Denver, Colorado|Denver]] and Noah "Mailbox" Passovoy at the [[Conway Studios]] in [[Los Angeles]]; Eric Eylands served as the engineering assistant.<ref name="Notes"/> Serban Ghenea [[Mixing (music production)|mixed]] the song at the Mixstar Studios in [[Virginia Beach]]. John Hanes served as engineer for mix, while Phil Seaford as mixing assistant.<ref name="Notes"/>

==Composition==
"Love Somebody" is a [[dance-pop]] song with a length of three minutes and forty-nine seconds.<ref name="Notes"/><ref name="enstarz">{{cite web|url=https://www.enstarz.com/articles/18369/20130522/maroon-5-love-somebody-music-video-adam-levine-covered-metallic-paint-the-voice-performance-watch.htm|title=Maroon 5 'Love Somebody' Music VIDEO: Adam Levine Covered in Metallic Paint Following 'The Voice' Performance |work=Enstarz|last=Jacobsen|first=Kevin |date=May 22, 2013 |access-date=December 15, 2013}}</ref> It is set in the key of [[D-flat major|D{{flat}} major]] and its instrumentation consists of [[piano]] and [[guitar]].<ref name="musicnotes">{{cite web|url=http://www.musicnotes.com/sheetmusic/mtdFPE.asp?ppn=MN0109521&|title=Maroon 5 - Love Somebody|date=September 25, 2012 |publisher=Musicnotes. [[Universal Music Publishing Group]]|access-date=January 28, 2013}}</ref> Rob Sheffield of ''[[Rolling Stone]]'' described the song's [[Synthesizer|synth]] [[Hook (music)|hook]] as the [[Sly Stone]]-meets-[[Eurythmics]].<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.rollingstone.com/music/albumreviews/overexposed-20120626|title=Overexposed - Album Reviews|magazine=[[Rolling Stone]]|last=Sheffield|first=Rob|date=June 26, 2012|access-date=January 28, 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120626223356/https://www.rollingstone.com/music/albumreviews/overexposed-20120626|archive-date=June 26, 2012}}</ref> According to Chris Payne of ''Billboard'', Tedder and Zancanella's influence is all over the song and inches through the [[Verse (music)|verses]] "before bubbling over just before the [[Refrain (song)|chorus]] kicks in."<ref name="billboard">{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/articles/review/1066943/maroon-5-overexposed-track-by-track-review|title=Maroon 5, 'Overexposed': Track-By-Track Review|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|last=Payne|first=Chris|date=June 26, 2012|access-date=January 28, 2013}}</ref> Adam Markovitz of ''[[Entertainment Weekly]]'' in a review of the album, wrote that the song together with "[[Daylight (Maroon 5 song)|Daylight]]" have "thickly" produced choruses.<ref name="entertainment">{{cite magazine|url=http://www.ew.com/ew/article/0,,20605733,00.html|archive-url=https://archive.today/20121227174030/http://www.ew.com/ew/article/0,,20605733,00.html|url-status=dead|archive-date=December 27, 2012|title=Overexposed Review|magazine=[[Entertainment Weekly]]|last=Markovitz|first=Adam|date=June 27, 2012|access-date=January 29, 2013}}</ref> Lyrically, "Love Somebody" equates love and physical intimacy, with Levine singing: "I really wanna love somebody [...] I really wanna touch somebody".<ref>{{cite news|last1=R. Holz|first1=Adam|title=Overexposed Album Review (2012) &#124; Plugged In|url=http://www.pluggedin.com/music-reviews/album/maroon-5-overexposed|access-date=October 30, 2015|work=[[Plugged In (publication)]]}}</ref> Nate Chinen of ''[[The New York Times]]'' described the lyrical theme of the song as "a plea for salvation on the dance floor or some other horizontal surface".<ref name="nytimes">{{cite news|url=https://www.nytimes.com/2012/07/10/arts/music/albums-from-clare-and-the-reasons-and-maroon-5.html?_r=1&|title=Albums from Clare and the Reasons and Maroon 5|work=[[The New York Times]]|last=Chinen|first=Nate|date=July 9, 2012|access-date=January 29, 2013}}</ref>

==Critical reception==
[[File:Coldplay Viva La Vida Tour in Hannover August 25th 2009.jpg|thumbnail|right|Critics linked "Love Somebody" to the works by British alternative rock band [[Coldplay]].]]
Suzanne Byrne of [[RTÉ.ie]] wrote that "Love Somebody" alongside "Daylight", "The Man Who Never Lied" and "[[Lucky Strike (Maroon 5 song)|Lucky Strike]]" are potential single releases.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.rte.ie/ten/2012/0630/maroon5.html|title=Maroon 5 - Overexposed|publisher=[[RTÉ.ie]]. [[Raidió Teilifís Éireann]]|last=Byrne|first=Suzanne|date=June 30, 2012|access-date=June 23, 2013}}</ref> Brittany Frederick of StarPulse called the song "earnest",<ref name="star pulse">{{cite web|url=http://www.starpulse.com/news/Brittany_Frederick/2012/06/26/album_review_maroon_5_overexposed|title=Album Review: Maroon 5, 'Overexposed'|publisher=StarPulse|last=Frederick|first=Brittany|date=June 26, 2012|access-date=January 25, 2013|archive-date=March 22, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160322184444/http://www.starpulse.com/news/Brittany_Frederick/2012/06/26/album_review_maroon_5_overexposed|url-status=dead}}</ref> while Cameron Adams of ''[[Herald Sun (Australia)|Herald Sun]]'' described it as [[Coldplay]] "trying to be" [[Katy Perry]].<ref>{{cite news|url=http://www.heraldsun.com.au/entertainment/music/album-review-overexposed-by-maroon-5/story-fn7oonq8-1226404138860|title=Album review: Overexposed by Maroon 5|work=[[Herald Sun (Australia)|Herald Sun]]|publisher=[[The Herald and Weekly Times]]|last=Adams|first=Cameron|date=June 20, 2012|access-date=January 24, 2012}}</ref> Similarly as Adams, [[PopMatters]]' Evan Sawdey wrote that "Love Somebody" is the group's "sky-scraping Coldplay moment".<ref>{{cite magazine|url=http://www.popmatters.com/pm/review/160424-maroon-5-overexposed/|title=Maroon 5: Overexposed|magazine=[[PopMatters]]|last=Sawdey|first=Evan|date=July 10, 2012|access-date=January 23, 2013}}</ref> Jeremy Thomas of 411 Mania labeled the song as one of the more emotional tracks on ''Overexposed'' and a "rare moment where the overhanging production tricks ease up just enough to make for an honest-to-God song with emotion and not just a laser scope aimed at the [[Billboard charts|''Billboard'' charts]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.411mania.com/music/album_reviews/243968/Maroon-5---Overexposed-Review.htm|title=Maroon 5 - Overexposed Review|publisher=411Mania|last=Thomas|first=Jeremy|date=June 26, 2012|access-date=January 26, 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130126192551/http://www.411mania.com/music/album_reviews/243968/Maroon-5---Overexposed-Review.htm|archive-date=January 26, 2013|url-status=dead}}</ref>

In a review of the album, Fraser McAlpine of [[BBC Music]] wrote that the band had left "the strutting cockerel heat" of their 2004 single "[[This Love (Maroon 5 song)|This Love]]" for the "saturated rave bliss" of "Love Somebody" or "The Man Who Never Lied". According to him that's an effect of the market they have chosen to contribute on.<ref name="bbc">{{cite web|url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/music/reviews/6rq4|title=BBC - Music - Review of Maroon 5 - Overexposed|publisher=[[BBC Music]]. [[BBC]]|last=McAlpine|first=Fraser|date=June 22, 2012|access-date=January 26, 2013}}</ref> ''Billboard'''s Chris Payne thought that the song fades into the background of the other stronger tracks on ''Overexposed''.<ref name="billboard"/> Adam Markovitz of ''Entertainment Weekly'' was more critical towards Levine's vocals on "Love Somebody" and wrote that the singer's voice is processed in a high disaffected whine — "like a male [[Rihanna]] or an android castrato", which according to him, it's ideal for tracing the contours of a pop hook.<ref name="entertainment"/>

==Chart performance==
Upon the release of ''Overexposed'', due to strong digital downloads, "Love Somebody" debuted on the South Korea [[Gaon Chart|Gaon International Chart]] at number eight on June 24, 2012, with sales of 35,892 digital copies.<ref name="South Korea"/> The next week, it fell to number 15 and sold an additional 16,752 copies.<ref name="South Korea1">{{cite web|url=http://gaonchart.co.kr/main/section/online/download/list.gaon?nationGbn=E |title=South Korea Gaon International Chart (Week, July 1, 2012 to July 7, 2012) |publisher=Gaon Chart |access-date=January 24, 2013 |language=ko |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130316222941/http://gaonchart.co.kr/main/section/online/download/list.gaon?nationGbn=E |archive-date=March 16, 2013 }}</ref> It stayed on the chart's top 100 for total of eight weeks.<ref name="South Korea2">{{cite web|url=http://gaonchart.co.kr/main/section/online/download/list.gaon?nationGbn=E |title=South Korea Gaon International Chart (Week, August 12, 2012 to August 18, 2012) |publisher=Gaon Chart |access-date=January 24, 2013 |language=ko |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130316222941/http://gaonchart.co.kr/main/section/online/download/list.gaon?nationGbn=E |archive-date=March 16, 2013 }}</ref> The song debuted at number 54 on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]], and  peaked at number 10, making ''Overexposed'' Maroon 5's first studio album to produce four Top 10 singles.<ref>{{cite news|last1=Wood|first1=Mikael|title=Maroon 5 takes 'V' to No. 1 on Billboard 200 album chart|url=https://www.latimes.com/entertainment/music/posts/la-et-ms-maroon-5-v-album-billboard-chart-top-20140910-story.html|access-date=October 30, 2015|work=[[Los Angeles Times]]|date=September 10, 2014}}</ref> As of June 2014, the song has sold 1,694,000 copies in the US.<ref name="us sales"/>

[[File:LoveSomebodyVEVO.jpg|thumb|right|Levine and Ratajkowski in the music video]]
The music video for "Love Somebody", was shot in January 2013. It premiered on [[NBC]] on May 20, 2013,<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.maroon5sin.com/news/title/get-ready-for-maroon-5s-music-video-for-love-somebody|title=Get Ready For Maroon 5's Music Video for "Love Somebody"|work=Maroon 5 Sin Club|access-date=July 13, 2020}}</ref> during the television series ''[[The Voice (American TV series)|The Voice]]'' and released on [[Vevo]], the following day.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.facebook.com/maroon5/posts/our-video-for-love-somebody-is-on-vevo-check-it-out-belowhttpwwwyoutubecomwatchv/10151622387838482/|title=Our video for "Love Somebody" is on VEVO! Check it out below!|work=[[Facebook]]|access-date=May 21, 2013}}</ref> Directed by [[Rich Lee]], the video features Levine and [[Emily Ratajkowski]] as his love interest, with the Maroon 5 members are all covered in grey paint. Levine initially paints himself out of a purely white background and then paints Ratajowski in front of him. The last scenes feature the band member's instruments spraying paint when played continually, a girl dancing seductively, and Levine and his love interest passionately embracing - all while covered in grey paint. In the very last scene, Levine reverses what he has done, removing all the paint from the scene, leaving no trace of them behind.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://radio.com/2013/05/21/maroon-5-release-paint-drenched-love-somebody-video/|title=Maroon 5 Releases Paint-Drenched 'Love Somebody' Video|access-date=May 24, 2018|date=May 21, 2013|work=Radio.com|last=E.
