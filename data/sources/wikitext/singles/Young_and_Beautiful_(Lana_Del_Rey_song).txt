"'''Young and Beautiful'''" is a song by American singer and songwriter [[Lana Del Rey]] used for the soundtrack to the drama film ''[[The Great Gatsby (2013 film)|The Great Gatsby]]''.

Contemporary music critics lauded the single, calling it "haunting" and "somber". Lyrically, "Young and Beautiful" follows a young lover's apprehension about whether love can last. A music video, directed by Chris Sweeney and filmed by [[Sophie Muller]], was released on May 10, 2013.

"Young and Beautiful" reached number one in the [[Commonwealth of Independent States]] as well as the top 10 in Australia, Hungary, Israel, and Italy. In May 2013, the song broke into the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]]; it peaked at 22 on the chart, making it Del Rey's second highest peak up to that point. Shortly after, the song also peaked at 3 on the [[Hot Rock Songs]] chart. The song is [[Music recording certification|certified]] nine-times Platinum in Australia, six-times Platinum in the US, and Platinum or Double Platinum in seven additional countries.

Del Rey and Nowels received accolades for their songwriting from various music and film awards, including nominations for [[Grammy Award for Best Song Written for Visual Media|Best Song Written for Visual Media]] at the [[56th Annual Grammy Awards]], and Best Song at the [[19th Critics' Choice Awards]]. They eventually won the [[Satellite Award for Best Original Song|Best Original Song]] at the [[18th Satellite Awards]]. On January 26, 2014, "Young and Beautiful" was announced as number 7 on the annual [[Triple J's Hottest 100]] in Australia.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.abc.net.au/triplej/hottest100/13/countdown/|title=The Countdown|date=September 10, 2013 |publisher=ABC|access-date=February 4, 2016}}</ref>

==Background==
Working with the film's director, co-writer, and co-producer [[Baz Luhrmann]], Del Rey wrote the original song "Young and Beautiful" for the [[The Great Gatsby: Music from Baz Luhrmann's Film|soundtrack]] of the [[The Great Gatsby (2013 film)|2013 film adaptation]] of ''[[The Great Gatsby]]'', from the perspective of [[Daisy Buchanan]].<ref name=latimesgatsby>{{cite web|last=Brown|first=August|title='Gatsby' soundtrack to feature Jay-Z, Lana del Rey, The xx and more|url=http://www.latimes.com/entertainment/music/posts/la-et-ms-gatsby-soundtrack-to-feature-jayz-lana-del-rey-the-xx-and-more-20130404,0,6073681.story|work=[[Los Angeles Times]]|access-date=April 6, 2013|date=April 4, 2013}} (Note: This article asserts Baz Luhrmann co-wrote the song, but he is not credited as such in either the soundtrack's liner notes or the film's credits.)</ref>

In an interview with [[Catalunya Ràdio]], Del Rey said she had three new songs for ''[[Paradise (Lana Del Rey EP)|Paradise]]'': "I Sing the Body Electric", "In the Land of Gods and Monsters" and "Will You Still Love Me When I'm No Longer Young and Beautiful." Del Rey sang the chorus of "Young and Beautiful" during the Catalunya Ràdio broadcast.<ref name=CatalunyaRadio>{{cite news|url=http://www.catradio.cat/audio/646450/Lana-del-Rey-una-de-les-estrelles-del=|archive-url=https://archive.today/20140110103800/http://www.catradio.cat/audio/646450/Lana-del-Rey-una-de-les-estrelles-del=|url-status=dead|archive-date=January 10, 2014|title=Lana del Rey, una de les estrelles del Sónar 2012, entrevistada a 'El matí'|date=June 16, 2012}}</ref>

Baz Luhrmann later gave an interview which contained a [[Skype]] chat with Del Rey, where Luhrmann said "we are very lucky that the song found a film",<ref name="Luhrmann Great Gatsby Interview">{{cite news|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AaQaSNlpWcU|title=Baz Luhrmann talks about working with Lana Del Rey for The Great Gatsby|via=[[YouTube]]|date= June 2013}}</ref> implying the song had already been written before the film.

Luhrmann changed some of the lyrics in the beginning of the second verse from the demo version of "Will You Still Love Me" to fit with the film.<ref name=autogenerated1>{{cite web|last=Brown|first=August|title='Gatsby' soundtrack to feature Jay-Z, Lana del Rey, The xx and more|url=http://www.latimes.com/entertainment/music/posts/la-et-ms-gatsby-soundtrack-to-feature-jayz-lana-del-rey-the-xx-and-more-20130404,0,6073681.story|work=[[Los Angeles Times]]|access-date=April 6, 2013|date=April 4, 2013}}</ref> Clearly, it always was intended for ''[[Paradise (Lana Del Rey EP)|Paradise]]'', which did get picked up by various media.<ref name=theguardian>{{cite web |url=https://www.theguardian.com/music/2013/dec/19/lana-del-rey-oscars-sabotage|work=The Guardian|title=Is Lana Del Rey's Oscar campaign being sabotaged?|date= December 19, 2013}}</ref>

The song was released to [[contemporary hit radio]] as a single and was used as the film's kickoff single.<ref name=rapupgatsby>{{cite web|title=Jay-Z Calls on Beyoncé, André 3000, Fergie, & Lana Del Rey for 'Great Gatsby' Soundtrack|url=http://www.rap-up.com/2013/04/04/jay-z-calls-on-beyonce-andre-3000-fergie-lana-del-rey-for-great-gatsby-soundtrack/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130501170442/http://www.rap-up.com/2013/04/04/jay-z-calls-on-beyonce-andre-3000-fergie-lana-del-rey-for-great-gatsby-soundtrack/|url-status=dead|archive-date=May 1, 2013|work=[[Rap-Up]]|access-date=April 6, 2013}}</ref> A snippet of the track appeared in the official trailer for the film and played during the scene where the characters portrayed by [[Leonardo DiCaprio]] and [[Carey Mulligan]] express their romantic feelings for one another.<ref name=mtvgatsby>{{cite web|last=Markman|first=Rob|title='Great Gatsby' Soundtrack Boasts Big Names Jay-Z, Beyonce And Lana Del Rey: Fourteen-song soundtrack also includes the likes of Florence and the Machine, will.i.am and Gotye.|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1704988/great-gatsby-soundtrack-jay-z-beyonce-lana-del-rey.jhtml|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130406061456/http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1704988/great-gatsby-soundtrack-jay-z-beyonce-lana-del-rey.jhtml|url-status=dead|archive-date=April 6, 2013|work=MTV|access-date=April 6, 2013|date=April 4, 2013}}</ref>

In October 2013, a remix by [[Cedric Gervais]] was commissioned for the record label [[Universal Music Group|Universal Germany]]. It was sent to Italian [[contemporary hit radio]] by Universal Music on January 10, 2014.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.radioairplay.fm/RadioDate/PublicDetails/61598|title=LANA DEL REY - Young and Beautiful (Cedric Gervais Remix) (Polydor) - Radio Date January 10, 2014<!-- Bot generated title -->|access-date=January 9, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140109160052/http://www.radioairplay.fm/RadioDate/PublicDetails/61598|archive-date=January 9, 2014|url-status=dead}}</ref>

==Music video==
The music video for "Young and Beautiful" was set to be released on April 22, 2013,<ref name=ybvideo>{{cite web|last=Makarechi|first=Kia|title='Great Gatsby' Soundtrack Tracklist Includes Jay-Z, Beyonce, Andre 3000, Florence, The XX & More|url=http://www.huffingtonpost.com/2013/04/04/great-gatsby-soundtrack-jay-z-beyonce-andre-3000_n_3014439.html|work=[[The Huffington Post]]|access-date=April 7, 2013|date=April 4, 2013}}</ref> but was officially released on May 10, 2013.<ref name=musicvideo>{{cite web|last=Corner|first=Lewis|title=Lana Del Rey premieres 'Young and Beautiful' music video - watch|url=http://www.digitalspy.com/music/news/a480251/lana-del-rey-premieres-young-and-beautiful-music-video-watch.html|work=[[Digital Spy]]|access-date=May 10, 2013|date=May 10, 2013}}</ref> Directed by Chris Sweeney, the video's production was helmed by Adam Smith and Jacob Swan-Hyam, with [[Sophie Muller]] shooting the footage.<ref name=production>{{cite web|title=Lana Del Rey - Young and Beautiful| date=May 10, 2013 |url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=o_1aF54DO60|via=[[YouTube]]|publisher=LanaDelReyVEVO|access-date=May 10, 2013}}</ref> Flanked by a full-string orchestra, Del Rey sings Dan Heath's Orchestral version of her song in 1920s [[art deco]] fashion.<ref name=musicvideo/> She is shown singing the song in a dark room with glittery diamond-tears on her cheek which look like tattoos.<ref name="billmag">{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/music/music-news/lana-del-rey-is-young-and-beautiful-and-sad-in-new-video-1561338/|title=Lana Del Rey is 'Young and Beautiful' and Sad in New Video|magazine=Billboard|first=Jason|last=Lipshutz|date=May 10, 2013|access-date=May 11, 2013}}</ref><ref name="spinmag"/> Jason Lipshutz of ''Billboard'' magazine described the video as "somber" and the singer's look as "especially demure". Lipshutz concluded that "the clip ends without ever reaching any kind of conclusion -- much like the rhetorical question 'Will you still love me when I'm no longer young and beautiful?' at the heart of the song."<ref name="billmag" /> ''Spin''{{'}}s Marc Hogan described it as "elegantly conceived, but succeed[s] only as much as the music does".<ref name="spinmag">{{cite web|url=https://www.spin.com/2013/05/lana-del-rey-great-gatsby-young-and-beautiful-video/|title=Lana Del Rey Serenades an Orchestra in 'Young and Beautiful' Video|work=[[Spin (magazine)|Spin]] |first=Marc|last=Hogan|date=May 10, 2013|access-date=May 11, 2013}}</ref>

==Reception and legacy==
Critical reception of "Young and Beautiful" has been fairly positive. Hip hop magazine ''[[Rap-Up]]'' called the single "haunting",<ref name=rapupgatsby/> while MTV called it "somber-sounding".<ref name=mtvgatsby/> Jason Lipshutz of ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' called the ballad, "typically lush", paralleling the single with Del Rey's previous hits, "[[Video Games (Lana Del Rey song)|Video Games]]" and "[[Born to Die (Lana Del Rey song)|Born to Die]]".<ref name=billboard/> Canadian journal ''[[National Post]]'' wrote "Young and Beautiful" picks up where "Video Games" left off, stating the song was relevant to the book because of "its obsession with decay and the fleeting nature of the good life certainly resonate."<ref name=nationalpost>{{cite web|last=Berry|first=David|title=First Impressions: Lana Del Rey's Young & Beautiful picks up where Video Games left off|url=https://nationalpost.com/entertainment/music/first-impressions-lana-del-rey-young-beautiful|work=[[National Post]]|access-date=May 7, 2013|date=April 22, 2013|archive-url=https://archive.today/20130615231211/http://arts.nationalpost.com/2013/04/22/first-impressions-lana-del-rey-young-beautiful/|archive-date=June 15, 2013|url-status=live}}</ref> The journal continued to call it an "artifact" of Del Rey's consistently dark tone, adding that by the second play-through "its bald directness becomes its appeal."<ref name=nationalpost/> MTV commented that the track "falls very much in-line with what Lana Del Rey's fans have come to expect" from Del Rey.<ref name=mtv/> Jeff Benjamin of [[Fuse (TV channel)|Fuse]] said the track featured "a big, sweeping ballad with piano, violins and Lana's warbly delivery." He added that "if the single takes off, perhaps the songbird can finally scrub her image clean of that 'Girl that totally {{nowrap|f-cked}}{{sic}} up her [[Saturday Night Live]] debut' tag."<ref>{{cite web|last=Benjamin|first=Jeff|title=Lana Del Rey Releases 'Great Gatsby' Single "Young & Beautiful"|url=http://www.fuse.tv/2013/04/lana-del-rey-young-beautiful-great-gatsby|access-date=May 8, 2013|work=[[Fuse (TV channel)|Fuse]]|date=April 22, 2013}}</ref> ''Rolling Stone''{{'s}} Jody Rosen recognized "Young and Beautiful" as the album's centerpiece, calling it "inert" and "a drag" despite its symmetry with the album's entire theme.<ref name=stonereview>{{cite magazine|title=Review: The Great Gatsby: Music From Baz Luhrmann's Film|url=https://www.rollingstone.com/music/albumreviews/the-great-gatsby-music-from-baz-luhrmann-s-film-20130503|magazine=[[Rolling Stone]]|access-date=July 21, 2013|author=Rosen, Jody|author-link=Jody Rosen|date=May 3, 2013}}</ref> Finding the track restrained in comparison to her earlier work, August Brown, writing for ''The Los Angeles Times'', called the single "clean" and "classy".<ref name=latimesreview>{{cite web|last=Brown|first=August|title=Lana Del Rey cuts video for 'Gatsby's' single 'Young and Beautiful'|url=https://www.latimes.com/entertainment/music/la-xpm-2013-may-10-la-et-ms-lana-del-rey-video-gatsby-young-and-beautiful-20130510-story.html|work=Los Angeles Times|access-date=July 21, 2013|date=May 10, 2013}}</ref> "Young and Beautiful" was given a positive review by ''Stereogum''{{'}}s Tom Breihan, although he disapproved of the vapidity of the line: "make me wanna party".<ref name=stereogumreview>{{cite web|last=Breihan|first=Tom|title=Lana Del Rey – "Young & Beautiful"|url=http://www.stereogum.com/1327672/lana-del-rey-young-beautiful/mp3s/|work=[[Stereogum]]|access-date=July 21, 2013|date=April 22, 2013<!--, 9:10 AM-->}}</ref> Nathan Ellis, writing for ''Far Out Magazine'', praised the track for the "silky vocals" and "dulcet <ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/dulcet|title=Definition of DULCET|website=www.merriam-webster.com|date=April 12, 2025 }}</ref> tones."<ref>{{cite web|url=https://faroutmagazine.co.uk/lana-del-rey-isolated-vocal-young-and-beautiful/|title=Listen to the remarkable audio of Lana Del Rey's isolated vocals on 'Young and Beautiful'|work = Far Out Magazine|date=April 14, 2020 }}</ref>

The song was nominated for [[Grammy Award for Best Song Written for Visual Media|Best Song Written for Visual Media]] at the [[56th Annual Grammy Awards]]. "Young and Beautiful" was also nominated for Best Original Song at the World Soundtrack Awards 2013.<ref name=soundtrack>{{cite web|title=BMI Congratulates the Nominees of the 2013 World Soundtrack Awards|url=http://www.bmi.com/news/entry/bmi_congratulates_the_nominees_of_the_2013_world_soundtrack_awards|work=[[Broadcast Music, Inc.|BMI]]|access-date=August 20, 2013|date=August 20, 2013}}</ref> It won the Best Original Song at the [[18th Satellite Awards]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.pressacademy.com/award_cat/current-nominees/|title=Current Nominees|access-date=February 4, 2016}}</ref>

==Commercial performance==
Debuting at number 82 on the United States' [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]], "Young and Beautiful" instantly became Del Rey's highest-charting single up to that point.<ref name=bbhot100>{{cite magazine|last=Trust|first=Gary|title=Weekly Chart Notes: Steve Martin, Edie Brickell Blast Back; Lana Del Rey Debuts; Mariah Carey, Nicki Minaj Get Hot (100)-Headed|url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/weekly-chart-notes-steve-martin-edie-brickell-blast-back-lana/|magazine=Billboard|access-date=May 7, 2013|date=May 5, 2013}}</ref> With 48,000 units in first-week sales, the song also entered the [[Hot Digital Songs]] [[Billboard charts|chart]] at 36.
