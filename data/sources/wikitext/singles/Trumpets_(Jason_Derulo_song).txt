"'''Trumpets'''" is a song by American singer [[Jason Derulo]], released as the fourth single in Australia, New Zealand and the European market and third single in the UK, from his third studio album, ''[[Tattoos (Jason Derulo album)|Tattoos]]'' (2013). The song was written by Derulo (under his real name Jason Desrouleaux) and [[Jon Bellion]], who also handled the production.

Although the song wasn't released as a single in the United States at the time, it was included on the US version of the album, ''[[Talk Dirty (album)|Talk Dirty]]'' (2014). "Trumpets" was released in the US as the fifth single from ''Talk Dirty''<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.singersroom.com/content/2014-07-10/Jason-Derulo-Ready-For-Fifth-Album-Confirms-Next-Single/|title=Jason Derulo Ready for Fifth Album, Confirms Next Single|date=July 10, 2014}}</ref> and has since peaked at number 14 on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]]. It was sent to US pop and rhythmic radio on August 19, 2014.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.allaccess.com/top40-rhythmic/future-releases|title = Top 40 Rhythmic Future Releases &#124; R&B Song and Hip-Hop Music Release Dates &#124;}}</ref> It was released on August 19, 2014, in the United States.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/1xtra/playlist/ |title=Radio 1Xtra Playlist |date=November 7, 2013 |publisher=BBC Radio |access-date=May 28, 2025 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://archive.today/20131109082311/http://www.bbc.co.uk/1xtra/playlist/ |archive-date=November 9, 2013 }}</ref>

==Background==
Following the release of "[[Marry Me (Jason Derulo song)|Marry Me]]" as the third single from his album ''Tattoos'' in the US and some international markets, "Trumpets" was released as the fourth overall single from the album to the UK and European territories. "Trumpets" was written by [[Jason Derulo|Jason Desrouleaux]] and [[Jon Bellion]] who also handled the production.<ref name="album credits">{{cite AV media notes |title=Tattoos|title-link=Tattoos (Jason Derulo album)|others= |year=2013 |type=Booklet |publisher=[[Beluga Heights Records|Beluga Heights]], [[Warner Bros. Records|Warner Bros]]}}</ref> The song was recorded by Vince Watson at Atlantic Studios in Hollywood, California.<ref name="album credits" /> with additional recordings by Josh Collins and Juan P. Negrete.<ref name="album credits" /> It was mixed by Roberto "Tito" Vazquez at Plush recording Studios in Orlando Florida.<ref name="album credits" /> It was mastered by Tom Coyne at sterling Sound in New York.<ref name="album credits" />

==Composition==
"Trumpets" is a midtempo [[Pop music|pop]] and [[Contemporary R&B|R&B]] song<ref>{{Cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/music/pop/the-two-sides-of-jason-derulo-6553115/|title = The Two Sides of Jason Derulo: Here's How He's Still Winning|magazine = [[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|date = May 2015}}</ref> that runs for a duration of three minutes and thirty seven seconds and was written by [[Jason Derulo]] and [[Jon Bellion]], who also handled the song's production.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://kempiredaily.com/new-music-jason-derulo-trumpets-lyric-video |title=<nowiki>New Music: Jason Derulo – “Trumpets” [Lyric Video</nowiki> |publisher=Kempire Daily |accessdate=February 22, 2014}}</ref> Lyrically the song is about being so in love with and infatuated with someone that every time you're together, trumpets blast through your head, as heard in the lyrics "Every time that you get undressed/ I hear symphonies in my head."<ref name="buzzworthy.mtv.com">{{cite web|author=Jenna Hally Rubenstein |url=http://buzzworthy.mtv.com/2013/11/05/jason-derulo-trumpets-video/ |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131107233934/http://buzzworthy.mtv.com/2013/11/05/jason-derulo-trumpets-video |url-status=dead |archive-date=November 7, 2013 |title=Jason Derulo Hears Actual 'Trumpets,' Oddly, Feels Like Katy Perry Every Time He's About To Get Some In His New Video |publisher=Buzzworthy.mtv.com |date=November 5, 2013 |accessdate=February 22, 2014}}</ref> It also states how various features of Jason's girlfriend remind him of other songs by other artists, including [[Coldplay]] (her eyes— possibly a reference to "[[A Rush of Blood to the Head|Green Eyes]]"), [[Katy Perry]] (her bra— possibly a reference to "[[California Gurls]]", in the video for which Perry dons a distinctive cream-shooting bra) and [[Kanye West]] (her rear— possibly a reference to the [[2 Chainz]] and [[Kanye West]] single "[[Birthday Song (2 Chainz song)|Birthday Song]]", which features many lyrics about "booty").

==Music video==
On October 11, 2013, the official lyric video for the song premiered on Derulo's official YouTube channel, after which he tweeted '#trumpets #tattoos' with a link to the video, to help promote the song's release.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://singersroom.com/content/2013-10-13/Jason-Derulo-Trumpets-Lyric-Video/ |title=Videos &#124; Jason Derulo – Trumpets (Lyric Video) |publisher=Singersroom |date=October 13, 2013 |accessdate=February 22, 2014}}</ref> The music video for "Trumpets" made its world premiere online through Derulo's official YouTube channel on November 3, 2013.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.directlyrics.com/jason-derulo-trumpets-news.html |title=Jason Derulo – "Trumpets" Music Videos |publisher=Directlyrics.com |date=November 4, 2013 |accessdate=February 22, 2014}}</ref> The video depicts Jason attempting to have sex with a young woman. Just as she takes off her clothes, he is bombarded by the sound of instruments and a marching band bursts into his bedroom.<ref name="buzzworthy.mtv.com" /><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.rap-up.com/2013/11/04/video-jason-derulo-trumpets/ |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131106090433/http://www.rap-up.com/2013/11/04/video-jason-derulo-trumpets/ |url-status=dead |archive-date=November 6, 2013 |title=Video: Jason Derulo – 'Trumpets' |publisher=Rap-Up.com |accessdate=February 22, 2014}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|author=|url=http://www.capitalfm.com/artists/jason-derulo/videos/trumpets/ |title=Jason Derulo – 'Trumpets' (Official Video): Music Video |publisher=Capital FM |date=November 5, 2013 |accessdate=February 22, 2014}}</ref>

==Live performances==
Derulo performed "Trumpets" live for the first time in the UK on BBC Radio 1 1Xtra special performance in the Live Lounge.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/p01n4vql |title=BBC Radio 1 – Fearne Cotton, Jason Derulo in the Live Lounge, Jason Derulo – Trumpets in the Live Lounge |publisher=Bbc.co.uk |date=December 10, 2013 |accessdate=February 22, 2014}}</ref> He also performed the song at the [[Capital (radio network)|Capital FM]] [[Jingle Ball|Jingle Bell Ball]] on December 8, 2013, along with his debut single "[[Whatcha Say]]", "The Other Side", "In My Head" and "Talk Dirty".<ref>{{cite web|author=|url=http://www.capitalfm.com/artists/jason-derulo/performances/trumpets-live-jingle-bell-ball-2013/ |title=Jason Derulo – 'Trumpets' (Live Performance, Jingle Bell Ball 2013): pictures, news, songs, tours |publisher=Capital FM |date=December 8, 2013 |accessdate=February 22, 2014}}</ref> Derulo made his first televised performance of the song during a promotional tour in the UK on ''[[The Paul O'Grady Show]]'' on December 10, 2013.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.jasonderulo.com/news/jason-derulo-performs-trumpets-live-uk-paul-o-grady-show-208651 |title=Jason Derulo Performs "Trumpets" Live in the UK on the Paul O'Grady Show! – Jason Derulo Latest News |publisher=Jasonderulo.com |date=December 13, 2013 |accessdate=February 22, 2014}}</ref> Derulo also performed this track on New Years Day for the Dick Clark post show. On July 4, 2014, Derulo performed it on ''[[Good Morning America]]'' as part of its Summer Concert Series.<ref>{{cite web|title=Jason Derulo Rocks NYC With 'Trumpets,' 'The Other Side'|url=https://abcnews.go.com/GMA/video/jason-derulo-rocks-central-park-trumpets-24426990|website=ABC News|accessdate=August 16, 2014|date=July 4, 2014}}</ref> He performed the song during the ''[[MDA Show of Strength]]'' that aired on August 31, 2014, but was taped in May 2014.

==Release and artwork==
The artwork for the single features several of the same trumpet seen in Derulo's CGI neck tattoo on the album cover of ''[[Tattoos (Jason Derulo album)|Tattoos]]''. On November 7, the single was first released in the UK. It was added to the playlist of British [[urban contemporary]] station [[BBC Radio 1Xtra]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/1xtra/playlist/ |title=Radio 1Xtra Playlist – Thursday 7th November |work=[[BBC Radio 1Xtra]] |publisher=BBC |accessdate=November 8, 2013 |archive-url=https://archive.today/20131109082311/http://www.bbc.co.uk/1xtra/playlist/ |archive-date=November 9, 2013}}</ref> It has since been released to many international territories while "[[Talk Dirty (Jason Derulo song)|Talk Dirty]]" was released in the US as the third single.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QXjt0WdgTF4 |title=Jason Derulo 'Talks Dirty' & Jordin Sparks' NSYNC Love! (JINGLE BALL 2013) |publisher=YouTube |date=December 16, 2013 |accessdate=February 22, 2014}}</ref>

==Chart performance==
"Trumpets" first appeared on the [[Dutch Top 40]] in the Netherlands issue dated November 30, 2013, debuting at its current peak position of number 22.<ref>{{cite web |author=<!-- not stated --> |website=Stichting Nederlandse Top 40 |url=http://www.top40.nl/top40/2013/week-48 |title=Top 40-lijst van week 48, 2013 |date=November 30, 2013 |access-date=February 22, 2014}}</ref> In Australia, "Trumpets" debuted at number 22 the [[ARIA Charts|ARIA Singles Chart]] issue dated December 15, 2013: it reached its peak position of number one in its fourth week on the chart.<ref>{{cite web|author=Steffen Hung |url=http://australian-charts.com/showitem.asp?interpret=Jason+Derulo&titel=Trumpets&cat=s |title=Jason Derulo – Trumpets |publisher=australian-charts.com |accessdate=February 22, 2014}}</ref> It has also made an appearance on the [[Recorded Music NZ|New Zealand Top 40 Singles Chart]], where it debuted at number 31, and has since reached its current peak of number 3.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://aotearoamusiccharts.co.nz/archive/singles/2013-12-13 |title=NZ Top 40 Singles Chart &#124; The Official New Zealand Music Chart |publisher=Nztop40.co.nz |date=December 16, 2013 |access-date=February 22, 2014}}</ref>

"Trumpets" made its debut on the UK R&B Singles Chart at number fifteen,<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.officialcharts.com/charts/r-and-b-singles-chart/20131117/114/ |title=2013-11-23 Top 40 R&B Singles Archive |publisher=[[Official Charts Company]] |date=November 23, 2013 |accessdate=February 22, 2014}}</ref> it has since reached its peak position of number one in its fifth week on the chart.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.officialcharts.com/charts/r-and-b-singles-chart/20131215/114/ |title=2013-12-21 Top 40 R&B Singles Archive |publisher=[[Official Charts Company]] |date=December 21, 2013 |accessdate=February 22, 2014}}</ref> It reached number 4 on the UK Singles Chart, becoming his third Top 10 single from the album and his eighth overall.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.officialcharts.com/charts/singles-chart/20131215/7501/ |title=2013-12-21 Top 40 Official Singles Chart UK Archive |publisher=[[Official Charts Company]] |date=December 21, 2013 |accessdate=February 22, 2014}}</ref> "Trumpets" remained at fourth position for four consecutive weeks.

The song reached a peak of No. 14 on the Billboard Hot 100, and has sold over a million copies in the US as of October 2014.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://music.yahoo.com/blogs/chart-watch/chart-watch-fergie-shows-l-a-some-love-171456124.html |title=Chart Watch: Fergie Shows L.A. Some Love |author= Paul Grein |date=October 23, 2014 |work=Yahoo! Music (Chart Watch) }}</ref>
