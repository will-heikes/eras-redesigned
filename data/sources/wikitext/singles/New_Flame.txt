"'''New Flame'''" is a song recorded by American singer [[Chris Brown]]. It was originally released as a promotional single from Brown's album on March 5, 2014. This version only featured rapper [[Rick Ross]] as a guest artist.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.rap-up.com/2014/03/05/new-music-chris-brown-f-rick-ross-new-flame/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140306054550/http://www.rap-up.com/2014/03/05/new-music-chris-brown-f-rick-ross-new-flame/|url-status=dead|archive-date=March 6, 2014|title=New Music: Chris Brown f/ Rick Ross – 'New Flame'|website=Rap-Up|access-date=October 11, 2016}}</ref> A few months later, a day after the [[BET Awards 2014|2014 BET Awards]] were held, the single version of the song featuring Ross and [[Usher (musician)|Usher]] was released on June 30, 2014, as the fifth official single from his sixth studio album ''[[X (Chris Brown album)|X]]''.<ref name="itunes"/> The song peaked at number 27 on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] and number 10 in the United Kingdom.

==Music video==
A music video for the song was released on August 14, 2014.<ref name="Music-video">[https://web.archive.org/web/20140818081626/http://www.rap-up.com/2014/08/14/video-chris-brown-f-usher-rick-ross-new-flame/ Rap-Up.com || Video: Chris Brown f/ Usher & Rick Ross – 'New Flame']</ref> Rapper [[Tyga]] makes a [[cameo appearance]] in the video.<ref name="Music-video"/>

The video starts with Brown standing in a white location wearing white clothing. The video then shows Brown standing in the front fire. Usher appears standing in a high large structure upon a pool with a girl wearing a red colored long dress. When the video comes to rapper Rick Ross, he's shirtless in a white room and sitting in a white chair. The music video ends with Brown  and some dancers are dancing in the front of fire.

==Chart performance==
The single peaked at number 27 on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] chart and spent a total of 22 weeks on the chart. It also peaked at number six on the US [[Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs]] chart and spent 26 weeks on the chart. On November 10, 2017, the single was certified [[RIAA Certification|double platinum]] by the [[Recording Industry Association of America]] (RIAA) for combined sales and [[album-equivalent unit|streaming equivalent unit]]s of over two million copies in the United States.

In the United Kingdom, the song peaked at number ten on the [[UK Singles Chart]] and only spent seven weeks on the chart. The single was certified Silver by the [[British Phonographic Industry]] (BPI) for sales of over 200,000 copies in the UK.
