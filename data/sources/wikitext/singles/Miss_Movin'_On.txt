"'''Miss Movin' On'''" is the debut single by American girl group [[Fifth Harmony]]. It was written by [[Mitch Allan]], [[Jason Evigan]], [[Lindy Robbins]], and [[Julia Michaels]], with production handled by Allan and Evigan. The song was released on July 16, 2013 as the lead single from the group's debut [[extended play]], ''[[Better Together (EP)|Better Together]]'' (2013).<ref>{{cite web|url=https://music.apple.com/us/album/miss-movin-on-single/671017974|title= Better Together (EP) by Fifth Harmony|work=iTunes|date= 16 July 2013}}</ref> Musically, the song is a [[power pop]] track with a synth-backed chorus and soaring hooks. In its lyrics, the group expresses a message of empowerment after the end of a relationship.

"Miss Movin On" became the group's first song to debut on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]], while also entering its [[Mainstream Top 40|pop]] counterpart chart in New Zealand. The song's accompanying music video, directed by [[Hannah Lux Davis]] and shot on-site in [[San Diego]], California at the [[Del Mar Fairgrounds]] features the girls enjoying themselves at an amusement park after getting over the break-ups of their boyfriends. The group performed the song several times, including on televised programs such as ''[[The Today Show]]'', ''[[Fox & Friends]]'', and  ''[[Live! with Kelly and Michael]]''.

==Background and release==
The main producer of Fifth Harmony's debut extended play, Julian Bunetta, stated on Twitter that two singles from the album would be released during June 2013. On June 4, 2013, Fifth Harmony tweeted that there would be an upcoming announcement related to the single and on June 7 the group announced via a Vine video that the debut single is called "Miss Movin' On".<ref>{{cite web|author= |url=http://popdust.com/2013/06/10/listen-to-six-seconds-of-fifth-harmonys-first-official-single-miss-movin-on/|title=Listen to Fifth Harmony's New Song "Miss Movin' On" on Vine|publisher=Popdust|date= |accessdate=September 13, 2015}}</ref> Fifth Harmony released a short preview of the song on Twitter.<ref>{{cite news|url=http://www.huffingtonpost.com/2013/06/14/fifth-harmony-miss-moving-on_n_3444592.html|title=Fifth Harmony Previews Debut Single 'Miss Movin' On' (LISTEN)|work=Huffington Post|date=June 14, 2013|accessdate=September 13, 2015}}</ref>

==Composition==
"Miss Movin' On" is a [[power pop]] song that lasts for three minutes and 14 seconds. Its written in the [[Key (music)|key]] of [[F-sharp major|F{{music|sharp}} major]] and is set in the [[time signature]] of {{music|time|4|4}} [[common time]] with a moderate [[tempo]] of 76 [[beats per minute]]. Songwriting and production is largely based on previous The Suspex productions, most notably [[Demi Lovato]]'s "[[Heart Attack (Demi Lovato song)|Heart Attack]]" which shows similar explosive chorus, huge vocals and soaring hooks.<ref name="muumuse">{{cite web|url=https://www.muumuse.com/2013/10/fifth-harmony-better-together-ep-review.html/|title= Review: Fifth Harmony's Debut EP, 'Better Together', Is Here At Last|publisher= MuuMuse|date= October 26, 2013|access-date= March 26, 2017}}</ref><ref name="sputnik">{{cite web|url=https://www.sputnikmusic.com/review/62409/Fifth-Harmony-Better-Together/|title= Fifth Harmony - Better Together|website= Sputnikmusic|date= May 21, 2014|access-date= March 26, 2017}}</ref>

The song begins with [[guitar chord]]s,<ref name="MTV"/> with [[Camila Cabello]] and [[Lauren Jauregui]] belting out the song's first [[verse (popular music)|verse]] with the [[chord progression]] of F{{music|sharp}}–C{{music|sharp}}–D{{music|sharp}}<sub>m</sub>,<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.musicnotes.com/sheetmusic/mtd.asp?ppn=MN0122617|title= Miss Movin' On by Fifth Harmony - Music Sheet|publisher= musicnotes.com|work= [[Universal Music Publishing Group]]|date= 13 August 2013|access-date= March 27, 2017}}</ref> followed by the explosive [[synth]]-backed [[refrain|chorus]].<ref name="sputnik"/> Jauregui and [[Dinah Jane]] sing the second verse with ad-libs from [[Normani|Normani Kordei]]. Its bridge is sung by Jane, while its last chorus features the first appearance from [[Ally Brooke]], alongside Kordei, Jauregui, and Jane. The bridge also has a beat comparable to English rock band [[Queen (band)|Queen]]'s single, "[[We Will Rock You]]".<ref name="muu"/> Lyrically, the song express a message of [[empowerment]] after the end of a relationship.<ref name="sputnik"/><ref name="MTV"/><ref name="muu"/>

==Reception==
===Critical reception===
Jessica Sager from PopCrush gave the song 4.5 out of 5 stars, praising the group's vocals as having "a hefty dose of soul that many of their Top 40 contemporaries, most of whom are a lot older than they are, lack."<ref>{{cite web|author= |url=http://popcrush.com/fifth-harmony-miss-movin-on-song-review/|title=Fifth Harmony, 'Miss Movin' On' – Song Review|publisher=PopCrush|date= 16 June 2013|accessdate=September 13, 2015}}</ref> Christina Lee, from [[Idolator (website)|Idolator]] called the song "explosive". Lee also noted the equal distribution in vocals, saying the group "exchanged the vocals in a way that each member is heard, but in subtle and unexpected ways".<ref>{{cite web|last1=Lee|first1=Christina|title=Fifth Harmony's "Miss Movin' On": Hear Their Explosive Debut Single|url=http://www.idolator.com/7464204/fifth-harmony-miss-movin-on|website=Idolator|date=16 June 2013 |accessdate=June 10, 2017}}{{dead link|date=August 2025|bot=medic}}{{cbignore|bot=medic}}</ref>

In a positive review, Jenna Rubenstein wrote on the [[MTV]] blog that "we effing loving it" and also complimented the single's "shiny, polished pop beat" and lyrics.<ref name="MTV">{{cite web|author=|url=http://buzzworthy.mtv.com/2013/06/17/fifth-harmony-miss-movin-on/#more-224652|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130621023247/http://buzzworthy.mtv.com/2013/06/17/fifth-harmony-miss-movin-on#more-224652|url-status=dead|archive-date=June 21, 2013|title=New Song: Fifth Harmony, 'Miss Movin' On'|publisher=MTV|date=June 17, 2015|accessdate=September 13, 2015}}</ref> [[Examiner.com|Examiner]] and ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' named the single a "pop kiss-off anthem", with ''Billboard'' writing that the "empowering track fulfills the group's promise of "fun pop".<ref>{{cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/1567344/fifth-harmony-bows-miss-movin-on-lyric-video-watch|title=Fifth Harmony Bows 'Miss Movin' On' Lyric Video: Watch|magazine=Billboard|date=June 18, 2013|accessdate=September 13, 2015}}</ref> Bill Lamb of ''[[About.com|About]]'' shared similar sentiments and noted that the group is trying to create a popularity for "girl groups", and praises the song for containing a powerful ballad that influences the chorus.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://top40.about.com/od/top10newpopsongs/tp/Top-10-New-Pop-Songs-July-16-2013.htm|title=Top 10 New Pop Songs July 16, 2013|publisher=[[About.com|About]]|access-date=March 30, 2016|archive-date=December 4, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141204181432/http://top40.about.com/od/top10newpopsongs/tp/Top-10-New-Pop-Songs-July-16-2013.htm|url-status=dead}}</ref>

Samantha Martin from ''PopDust'' praised the group's "distinct voices", as they "blend seamlessly when singing".<ref>{{cite web|title=Fifth Harmony Tease 30 Seconds of "Miss Movin' On"...And We Want More!|url=http://popdust.com/2013/06/14/fifth-harmony-new-song-single-miss-movin-moving-on-review/|website=PopDust|accessdate=June 10, 2017}}</ref> The Portuguese website Original Tune praised the song for "undoubtedly" making the "most complete of an artist revealed by X-Factor US". He continued his review by complementing the "production" as the producers "took great care" not to make the song sound "too generic".<ref>{{cite web|title=Single Review: Fifth Harmony – 'Miss Movin' On'|url=http://original-tune.com/2013/06/single-review-fifth-harmony-miss-movin-on/|website=Original Tune|accessdate=June 10, 2017}}</ref> Bradley Stern of ''MuuMuse'' gave the song four out of five stars calling the song a "power-pop empowerment anthem". Stern also praised the "huge vocals", "soaring hooks" and the "fists-aloft, fuck-off chorus."<ref name="muu">{{cite web|url=http://www.muumuse.com/2013/06/fifth-harmony-premiere-debut-single-miss-movin-on-single-review.html/|title=Fifth Harmony Premieres Debut Single "Miss Movin 'On" (Single Review)|date=16 June 2013 |accessdate= November 30, 2014}}</ref> [[Sugarscape]] compared the track to [[Demi Lovato]]'s songs.<ref>{{cite web|last=Smith|first=Carl|url=http://www.sugarscape.com/main-topics/music/880878/x-factor-usas-fifth-harmony-unveil-debut-single-miss-movin-listen|title=The X Factor USA's Fifth Harmony unveil debut single 'Miss Movin' On' - Listen|publisher=Sugarscape|date=June 16, 2013|accessdate=September 13, 2015}}</ref>

===Commercial performance===
During the single's first chart tracking week, it sold 37,000 digital downloads and had 489,000 streams in the United States, debuting at number 85 on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]].<ref>{{cite web|author= |url=https://www.sweetyhigh.com/blog/celebrity-news/fifth-harmony-makes-it-on-billboard-hot-100-chart|title=Fifth Harmony Makes It On Billboard Hot 100 Chart!|publisher=Sweety High|date=August 3, 2013|accessdate=September 13, 2015}}</ref> On August 15, the song re-entered the chart, at a new peak of 83.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/biz/articles/news/4750824/chart-moves-finatticz-fly-danielle-bradbery-bows-panic-at-the-disco-lead|title=Chart Moves: Finatticz Fly; Danielle Bradbery Bows; Panic! At The Disco Lead Host Of Rock Debuts|magazine=Billboard|date=July 26, 2013|accessdate=September 13, 2015}}</ref> It then peaked at number 76,<ref name="Fifth Harmony Hot 100">{{cite magazine|title=Fifth Harmony Album & Song Chart History (The Hot 100)|url=http://www.billboard.com/artist/5665538/fifth-harmony/chart?f=379|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|accessdate=3 December 2014}}</ref> and spent eleven weeks on the chart.<ref name="Fifth Harmony Hot 100"/> On the [[Mainstream Top 40]] chart, it peaked at number 27, spending nine weeks there.<ref name="Fifth Harmony Pop Songs">{{cite magazine|title=Fifth Harmony Album & Song Chart History (Pop Songs)|url=http://www.billboard.com/artist/5665538/fifth-harmony/chart?f=381|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|accessdate=3 December 2014}}</ref> The song also charted on the ''Billboard'' [[Heatseekers Songs]] at number four, spending five weeks on the chart.<ref name=HeatseekingSongs>{{cite magazine|title=Billboard - Artists - Fifth Harmony - Heatseeking songs| magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]] |url=http://www.billboard.com/artist/5665538/fifth-harmony/chart?f=887|accessdate=22 March 2014}}</ref>

Outside of the United States, "Miss Movin' On" charted at number 27 in New Zealand, becoming the group's first top 40 entry as part of their debut single.

==Music video==
After releasing the lyric video for the single on June 18, 2013, the music video was posted on Fifth Harmony's official YouTube account on July 15, 2013.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/music/music-news/fifth-harmonys-miss-movin-on-video-watch-the-girls-rock-an-1728823/ |title=Fifth Harmony's 'Miss Movin' On' Video: Watch The Girls Rock An Amusement Park|magazine=Billboard|date=July 15, 2013|access-date=September 13, 2015}}</ref> Directed by [[Hannah Lux Davis]], the video shows the five girls enjoying themselves at an amusement park together, all of them having recently gotten over their break-ups with their boyfriends.  Throughout the video the girls are seen singing into vintage microphones. The music video was filmed in [[Del Mar, California]] at the [[San Diego County Fair]].

The group became the first alumnae from the American version of [[The X Factor (American TV series)|''The X Factor'']] to reach 15 million YouTube views for an original song, when the song "Disarm You" featuring Ilsey video surpassed this feat on October 20, 2013. Since then, the video became the group's fourth [[Vevo]] certification for reaching over 100 million views. As of April 2024, the video had achieved over 180 million views.

==Live performances==
The group performed "Miss Movin' On" for the first time on television on July 18, 2013, on ''[[Today (American TV program)|The Today Show]]'' in New York City.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.idolator.com/7471831/fifth-harmony-today-show-miss-movin-on-me-my-girls|title=Watch Fifth Harmony Perform "Miss Movin' On" & "Me And My Girls" On 'TODAY'|publisher=Idolator|date=July 18, 2013|accessdate=September 13, 2015}}{{dead link|date=August 2025|bot=medic}}{{cbignore|bot=medic}}</ref> While visiting radio stations as part of the single's promotion, the group sung acoustic versions of the song on KIIS FM and [[Radio Disney]]. The song was on the set list for the group's mall tour titled Harmonize America and [[Cher Lloyd]]'s [[I Wish Tour]] throughout September 2013, where Fifth Harmony was the opening act.<ref>{{cite web|author= |url=http://www.ryanseacrest.com/2013/07/26/cher-lloyd-announces-i-wish-tour-with-fifth-harmony/|title=Cher Lloyd Announces 'I Wish' Tour With Fifth Harmony |publisher=Ryan Seacrest|date=July 26, 2013|accessdate=September 13, 2015}}</ref>

Other performances of the song include ''[[Fox & Friends]]'', ''[[The Arsenio Hall Show]]'', and ''[[Live! with Kelly and Michael]]''.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://video.foxnews.com/v/2585359881001/girl-power-fifth-harmony-take-america/?playlist_id=929831930001|title=Girl power: Fifth Harmony take America &#124; On Air Videos &#124; Fox News|publisher=Fox News|date= |accessdate=September 13, 2015}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.fifthharmonyofficial.com/news/5h-on-live-with-kelly-and-michael-tomorrow/|title=5H on LIVE with Kelly and Michael TOMORROW!|work=Fifth Harmony|access-date=2013-10-23|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131112205044/http://www.fifthharmonyofficial.com/news/5h-on-live-with-kelly-and-michael-tomorrow/|archive-date=2013-11-12|url-status=dead}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.idolator.com/7490108/fifth-harmony-belt-out-miss-movin-on-on-the-arsenio-hall-show-watch|archive-url=https://archive.today/20131023151822/http://www.idolator.com/7490108/fifth-harmony-belt-out-miss-movin-on-on-the-arsenio-hall-show-watch|url-status=dead|archive-date=October 23, 2013|title=Fifth Harmony Belt Out "Miss Movin' On" On 'The Arsenio Hall Show': Watch|publisher=Idolator|date= 23 October 2013|accessdate=September 13, 2015}}</ref>  and at the 2014 [[Radio Disney Music Awards]].<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/music/pop/radio-disney-music-awards-2014-selena-gomez-one-direction-becky-g-6070034/|title=Radio Disney Music Awards 2014: Selena Gomez, One Direction, Taylor Swift Among Winners|magazine=Billboard|access-date=June 16, 2014}}</ref>
