"'''I Lived'''" is a song recorded by American [[pop rock]] band [[OneRepublic]]. It was released on September 23, 2014, as the fifth and final single from the band's third album, ''[[Native (album)|Native]]'' (2013). It was originally planned to be released as the fourth single in January 2014 but was shelved in favor of "[[Love Runs Out]]".<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.unrealitytv.co.uk/x-factor/x-factor-results-one-republic-stun-lived-video/|title=X Factor results: One Republic stun with I Lived VIDEO|work=Unreality TV|access-date=March 11, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150310040626/http://www.unrealitytv.co.uk/x-factor/x-factor-results-one-republic-stun-lived-video/|archive-date=March 10, 2015|url-status=dead}}</ref> It was the final song covered in the [[Dreams Come True (Glee)|series finale]] of the hit [[Fox Broadcasting Company|FOX]] musical comedy-drama ''[[Glee (TV series)|Glee]]'' in March 2015.<ref>{{cite tweet |author= Lesley Goldberg |user= Snoodit|number= 570663666439180288 |date= February 25, 2015 |title= "Jane Lynch says #Glee will flash back to 2009 & the pilot before jumping to 2020 w/ the cast covering One Republic's I Lived in the finale." |access-date= March 25, 2015 }}</ref><ref name="Exton">{{cite web|url=http://www.vh1.com/celebrity/2015-02-25/jane-lynch-glee-series-finale-song/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150226135157/http://www.vh1.com/celebrity/2015-02-25/jane-lynch-glee-series-finale-song/|url-status=dead|archive-date=February 26, 2015|title=Jane Lynch Reveals What Song the 'Glee' Cast Will Sing in the Series Finale|last=Exton|first=Emily|date=February 25, 2015|publisher=VH1}}</ref><ref>{{cite tweet |author= OneRepublic |user= OneRepublic|number= 570723567102373889 |date= February 25, 2015 |title= "We're so honored that the cast of @GLEEonFOX will be singing #ILived in the series finale episode on @FOXTV!" |access-date= March 25, 2015 }}</ref>

==Conception==
OneRepublic frontman [[Ryan Tedder]] noted that he wrote the song for his four-year-old son. In an interview with ''[[People (magazine)|People]]'' magazine, he said, "The whole idea, to quote the late great [[Robin Williams]] from ''[[Dead Poets Society]]'', is very much '[[carpe diem]]'." He said that "it's absolutely universal and applicable to everybody." Regarding the lyrics "With every broken bone, I swear I lived", Tedder said, "So for every day that you're on this earth, for every minute that you have, the whole idea is doing nothing less than exactly what you feel you're supposed to do and squeezing every last drop out of life every day, regardless of the difficulties or trials that you face."<ref name="people 20141124">{{cite web |last1=Corinthos |first1=Aurelie |title=OneRepublic's Ryan Tedder Wrote This Song for His Son – And Now the Remix Will Help Fight AIDS |url=https://www.people.com/article/ryan-tedder-onerepublic-song-aids-research |website=People |publisher=Time |access-date=June 4, 2022 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150813221727/https://www.people.com/article/ryan-tedder-onerepublic-song-aids-research |archive-date=August 13, 2015 |date=November 24, 2022 |url-status=dead}}</ref> As with other songs on the ''Native'' album, Tedder drew songwriting inspiration from [[U2]], where he focused on writing songs from the heart and from personal experience.<ref>{{cite news|url=http://radio.com/2013/03/26/how-u2-influenced-onerepublics-personal-new-album/|title=How U2 Influenced OneRepublic's Personal New Album|work=[[Radio.com]] }}</ref>

==Music video==
The music video was released on [[Vevo]] and [[YouTube]] on September 25, 2014. It is directed by Noble Jones. The idea was from [[Sophie Muller]].<ref name="director-of-mv">{{cite web|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=z0rxydSolwU|title=OneRepublic – I Lived – YouTube|work=[[OneRepublic]], [[Vevo]], [[Interscope Records]] and [[Mosley Music Group]]|date=September 25, 2014|access-date=December 3, 2014}}</ref> The video is dedicated to 15-year-old Bryan Warnecke, a fan of the band, who has [[cystic fibrosis]].<ref name="mv-note-1">{{cite web|url=http://popcrush.com/onerepublic-i-lived-music-video/|title=OneRepublic's "I Lived" music video dedicated to fan with cystic fibrosis|last=Chau|first=Thomas|work=Popcrush|date=September 27, 2014|access-date=December 3, 2014}}</ref> Bryan Warnecke talks about living with cystic fibrosis in the music video:
<blockquote>"[[Cystic fibrosis]] is a disease that attacks the [[Human digestive system|digestive system]] and slowly shuts down the [[Lung|lungs]]. The [[mucus]] in everyone else's lungs is more watery, so you guys can clear it easier, but when it comes to my lungs, the mucus is very syrupy. I've been doing my treatments every day of my life, so to me, it's just one of those things that's normal. My name is Bryan Warnecke, and I've been living with cystic fibrosis for 15 years... When I first found out the [[life expectancy]] for the first time, it really scared me. Right now, it's up to about 36. It's just one of those things that really make you appreciate life. It makes you appreciate where you are, as a person..."</blockquote>

In the video, Bryan Warnecke is seen bicycling, skateboarding, playing hockey, and enjoying other leisure activities to make the most of his life. He also shares the fear of living with cystic fibrosis: "It feels like I'm breathing through a straw", says Warnecke. "Whenever I try to breathe hard, it hurts. I want to make the most out of my life and have as much fun as I possibly can and my biggest fear is not being able to do that."  The clip ends with Warnecke and his dad driving to the [[Red Rocks Amphitheatre]] in [[Colorado]] to watch [[Ryan Tedder]] and the band perform live.<ref name="mv-note-2">{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/music/pop/onerepublic-touching-i-lived-video-fan-cystic-fibrosis-6266347/|title=Watch OneRepublic's touching 'I Lived' video, dedicated to a fan with cystic fibrosis|last=Stutz|first=Colin|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|date=September 25, 2014|access-date=December 3, 2014}}</ref> A postscript notes that in 2014, "Bryan and his teammates have ridden over 1200 miles and raised $300,000 for Cystic Fibrosis research", and refers viewers to the website of the [[Cystic Fibrosis Foundation]] (CFF).

==Cover versions and usage in media==

"I Lived" served as the theme song for the [[2015 NHL Playoffs]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.axs.com/onerepublic-s-i-lived-named-theme-of-2015-nhl-stanley-cup-playoffs-49054|title=OneRepublic's 'I Lived' named theme of 2015 NHL Stanley Cup Playoffs|author=Brittany Frederick|date=April 16, 2015|work=AXS|access-date=June 3, 2015}}</ref> It also served as the tribute song for the retirement ceremony of [[Detroit Pistons]] center [[Ben Wallace (basketball)|Ben Wallace]] at [[The Palace of Auburn Hills]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DfES0cN0nlM&t=1s|title=Detroit Pistons – Pistons Hits: Ben Wallace Tribute|website=[[YouTube]] |access-date=January 16, 2016}}</ref> [[ESPN]] has used it on multiple programs, including ''[[SportsCenter]]'' and [[First Take (TV series)|''First Take'']], while [[Jordan Smith (musician)|Jordan Smith]], the winner of [[The Voice (American season 9)|season 9]] of ''[[The Voice (American TV series)|The Voice]]'', sang the song in a promotional music video of NBC for the [[2016 Summer Olympics]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=snYNg6-OnyI|title=Jordan Smith, 2016 Rio Olympics - "I Lived", The Voice|publisher=NBC|access-date=June 1, 2016}}</ref>

The song has been used on the TV shows ''[[90210 (TV series)|90210]]'', ''[[The Michael J. Fox Show]]'' and ''[[Red Band Society]]'', as well as in the 2014 film ''[[The Giver (film)|The Giver]]''.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.musictimes.com/articles/7849/20140722/listen-onerepublic-the-giver-soundtrack-song-ordinary-human-review-audio.htm|title=LISTEN: OneRepublic 'The Giver' Soundtrack Song 'Ordinary Human' Recalls Modern U2|work=Music Times|date=July 22, 2014 |access-date=March 11, 2015}}</ref> "I Lived" was the final song covered in the [[Dreams Come True (Glee)|series finale]] of the musical comedy-drama ''[[Glee (TV series)|Glee]],''<ref>{{cite tweet|number=570663666439180288|user=Snoodit|title="Jane Lynch says #Glee will flash back to 2009 & the pilot before jumping to 2020 w/ the cast covering One Republic's I Lived in the finale."|author=Lesley Goldberg|date=February 25, 2015|access-date=March 25, 2015}}</ref><ref name="Exton"/><ref>{{cite tweet|number=570723567102373889|user=OneRepublic|title="We're so honored that the cast of @GLEEonFOX will be singing #ILived in the series finale episode on @FOXTV!"|author=OneRepublic|date=February 25, 2015|access-date=March 25, 2015}}</ref> and was the first song used by [[Noah Galloway]] and his partner [[Sharna Burgess]] on [[Dancing with the Stars (American season 20)|season 20]] of ''[[Dancing with the Stars (American TV series)|Dancing with the Stars]].''

[[NBC]] used the song in commercials for ''[[The Office (American TV series)|The Office]]'' and ''[[Parks and Recreation]],''<ref name="OFFICETALLY">{{cite web|url=http://www.officetally.com/the-office-livin-the-dream-clips|title=The Office: Livin' the Dream clips|author=The Office Promos|date=April 27, 2013|publisher=OfficeTally|access-date=May 1, 2013}}</ref> while [[American Broadcasting Company|ABC]] featured it in multiple ads for its 2017 drama ''[[The Good Doctor (American TV series)|The Good Doctor]]'' and the [[Modern Family (season 11)|final season]] of ''[[Modern Family]]'' in 2019. The song is also used as the background music for [[Huawei Mate 10]] commercial, with [[Naomi Watts]] and [[Anna Kendrick]].

It was sung by [[Peter Gallagher]] in the Season Two [[series finale]] titled "Zoey's Extraordinary Goodbye," of ''[[Zoey's Extraordinary Playlist]].''

WWE used the song to pay tribute to John Cena at [[Saturday Night's Main Event XLII]].

==Reception==
Marcus Floyd of ''[[Renowned for Sound]]'' gave the single four out of five stars, citing its finger-picking introduction as setting an easy and soothing atmosphere, and calling the overall song a catch and enlightening addition to their singles.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://renownedforsound.com/index.php/single-review-onerepublic-i-lived/|title=Renowned for Sound – Single Review: OneRepublic – 'I Lived'|date=October 6, 2014}}</ref> Camilla Cassidy of The Edge also gave it four out of five stars, describing the song as an anthem for hope and empowerment, with meaningful lyrics and a wistful tone that inspires listeners, however, she found its delivery to be somewhat absent, and "neither somber nor particularly energetic".<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.theedgesusu.co.uk/records/2014/11/14/review-onerepublic-i-lived/|title=Review: OneRepublic – 'I Lived'|author=Camilla Cassidy|work=The Edge|date=November 14, 2014 }}</ref> Amy Sciarretto of PopCrush described the track as having "a funky, lived-in energy, and is looser than many of its counterparts. It also has more fun than any other track on the record."<ref>{{cite web|url=http://popcrush.com/onerepublic-native-album-review/?trackback=tsmclip|title=OneRepublic, 'Native' – Album Review|work=PopCrush|date=March 27, 2013 }}</ref>

Emily Tan of Idolator grouped the song with "Counting Stars" and "Something I Need" as songs that "will keep core fans and AC radio listeners happy".<ref name="idolator">{{cite web|url=http://idolator.com/7448419/one-republic-native-album-review|archive-url=https://archive.today/20130411031358/http://idolator.com/7448419/one-republic-native-album-review|url-status=dead|archive-date=April 11, 2013|title=One Republic's 'Native': Album Review|last=Tan|first=Emily|date=March 26, 2013|publisher=Idolator|access-date=March 26, 2013}}</ref> Haley Black of ''Highlight Magazine'' while not reviewing the song specifically in her ''Native'' review, listed "I Lived" along with "Au Revoir" as a recommended track on the album.<ref name=HM>{{cite web|url=http://highlightmagazine.net/2013/03/21/onerepublic-native/|title=OneRepublic – Native|last=Black|first=Haley|date=March 21, 2013|work=Highlight Magazine|access-date=March 26, 2013}}</ref> Matt Collar of [[AllMusic]] grouped the song among the album's "clearing house for mainstream pop sensibilities".<ref name=AM>{{cite web|url=http://www.allmusic.com/album/mw0002484790|title=Native – OneRepublic – Songs, Reviews, Credits, Awards – AllMusic|author=Matt Collar|work=AllMusic}}</ref>

Colin Stutz of ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' called the music video a touching tribute to Warnecke.<ref name="mv-note-2"/>
