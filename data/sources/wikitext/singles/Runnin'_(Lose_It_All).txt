"'''Runnin' (Lose It All)'''" is a song by British record producer [[Naughty Boy]], featuring American singer [[Beyoncé]] and British singer [[Arrow Benjamin]]. It was released online on 17 September 2015 through Virgin EMI Records. Upon its release, the song received positive reviews from [[music journalism|music critics]], mostly praising the song's arrangements and Beyoncé's vocals.

The accompanying [[music video]] was directed by Charlie Robins received critical acclaim for directing and cinematography, being shortlisted for the [[Brit Award for British Video of the Year]] and winning at the [[Cannes Lions International Festival of Creativity]].

Commercially, the track has reached number one in France and Scotland, the top 10 in the Czech Republic, New Zealand, Poland, Spain, and the United Kingdom, becoming Naughty Boy's fifth, Beyoncé's 18th, and Arrow Benjamin's first top ten song in the UK.

On 16 September 2015, [[Naughty Boy]] announced that he would release a song titled "Runnin' (Lose It All)" featuring [[Beyoncé]] and [[Arrow Benjamin]]. On the same date he shared the artwork for the single, its lyrics, a 15-second sound snippet along with a video through his [[Instagram]] account and began a countdown until its online release.<ref name="ew">{{cite magazine|url=http://www.ew.com/article/2015/09/16/naughty-boy-beyonce-runnin-lose-it-all-teaser|title=Naughty Boy teases new song with Beyoncé|magazine=[[Entertainment Weekly]]|publisher=[[Time Inc.]]|first=Eric|last=Renner Brown|date=16 September 2015|access-date=17 September 2015}}</ref><ref name="bill">{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/music/rb-hip-hop/beyonce-naughty-boy-runnin-lose-it-all-6699687/|title=New Beyonce! Naughty Boy Previews Their Song 'Runnin' (Lose It All)'|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|publisher=[[Prometheus Global Media]]|first=Erin|last=Strecker|date=16 September 2015|access-date=17 September 2015}}</ref> The video consisted of two humans floating alone, submerged underwater.<ref>{{cite news|url=http://www.rap-up.com/2015/09/16/naughty-boy-beyonce-runnin-lose-it-all-teaser/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150919002831/http://www.rap-up.com/2015/09/16/naughty-boy-beyonce-runnin-lose-it-all-teaser/|url-status=dead|archive-date=19 September 2015|title=Preview Beyoncé and Naughty Boy's Collaboration 'Runnin' (Lose It All)'|work=[[Rap-Up]]|publisher=Devin Lazerine|date=16 September 2015|access-date=17 September 2015}}</ref> The following day, on 17 September 2015, "Runnin{{' "}} premiered online. ==Composition==
"Runnin' (Lose It All)" is a [[power ballad]]<ref name="bill" /> featuring elements of [[drum and bass]].<ref name="guard">{{cite news|url=https://www.theguardian.com/music/musicblog/2015/sep/18/beyonce-turns-up-on-new-naughty-boy-track-runnin-lose-it-all-listen|title=Beyoncé turns up on new Naughty Boy track Runnin' (Lose it All) – listen|work=[[The Guardian]]|first=Elle|last=Hunt|date=18 September 2015|access-date=19 September 2015}}</ref> It was noted by Elle Hunt of ''[[The Guardian]]'' to be established in the genre of British [[dance music]].<ref name="guard" /> The song's composition consists of melancholy piano keys.<ref name="ew" /><ref name="bill" /> It opens with Beyoncé singing the first verse over a piano track with lyrics such as "Nothing else matters now; you're not here. So where are you? I've been calling you, [I'm] missing you".<ref name="ew2">{{cite magazine|url=http://www.ew.com/article/2015/09/17/beyonce-naughty-boy-runnin-lose-it-all|title=Beyoncé's new song 'Runnin' (Lose It All)' with Naughty Boy is here|first=Will|last=Robinson|date=17 September 2015|magazine=Entertainment Weekly|publisher=Time Inc.|access-date=19 September 2015}}</ref><ref name="rs">{{cite magazine|url=https://www.rollingstone.com/music/news/hear-beyonce-sing-on-soulful-naughty-boy-produced-runnin-20150917|title=Hear Beyonce Sing on Soulful, Naughty Boy-Produced'|magazine=[[Rolling Stone]]|publisher=[[Jann Wenner|Wenner Media]]|first=Brittany|last=Spanos |date=17 September 2015|access-date=19 September 2015}}</ref> Following the [[Hook (music)|hook]], the song's tempo increases and "Runnin{{' "}} continues with a [[UK garage|garage]] break afterwards.<ref name="guard" /><ref name="ew2" /> During the chorus, Beyoncé sings about how she will stop running from herself as she repeats the lines "Ain't running from myself no more".<ref name="rs" /><ref name="slate" /> The second verse is sung by Arrow Benjamin and the last chorus is sung as a duet between both singers.<ref name="ew2" /> Eric Renner Brown from ''[[Entertainment Weekly]]'' further found "typically soulful Beyoncé lyrics".<ref name="ew" /> Elle Hunt of ''The Guardian'' compared the song with Beyoncé's own "[[Halo (Beyoncé song)|Halo]]" and "[[Haunted (Beyoncé song)|Haunted]]" as well as Naughty Boy's previous collaborations with singer [[Emeli Sandé]].<ref name="guard" />

==Critical reception==
Elle Hunt from ''The Guardian'' praised "Runnin{{' "}} as "a shimmery club classic, the kind we've seen plenty of in the past 20 years".<ref name="guard" /> [[CBS News]] writer Andrea Park dubbed Beyoncé's vocals as "unmistakeable".<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cbsnews.com/news/beyonce-releases-new-single-runnin/|title=Beyonce releases new single "Runnin{{' "}}|publisher=[[CBS News]]. [[CBS]]|first=Andrea|last=Park|date=18 September 2015|access-date=19 September 2015}}</ref> ''[[Exclaim!]]''{{'}}s Alex Hudson also described the singer's voice as "powerful".<ref name="exclaim">{{cite news|url=http://exclaim.ca/music/article/naughty_boy-runnin_lose_it_all_ft_beyonce_arrow_benjamin_video|title=Naughty Boy "Runnin' (Lose It All)" (ft. Beyoncé & Arrow Benjamin) (video)|work=[[Exclaim!]]|first=Alex|last=Hudson|date=17 September 2015|access-date=19 September 2015}}</ref> Laura Bradley of ''[[Slate (magazine)|Slate]]'' magazine noted that Beyoncé opened the song with "the kind of force few other singers can muster".<ref name="slate">{{cite news|url=http://www.slate.com/blogs/browbeat/2015/09/18/beyonc_and_naughty_boy_share_new_song_runnin_lose_it_all_with_new_music.html|title=Beyoncé Is Back: Hear Her New Naughty Boy Collaboration "Runnin' (Lose It All)"|first=Laura|last=Bradley|date=18 September 2015|access-date=19 September 2015}}</ref> She further compared it with the singer's own "[[XO (song)|XO]]", due to their reminiscent "triumphant, love-conquers-all spirit".<ref name="slate"/> Nick Levine of ''[[NME]]'' described the song as "pleasant rather than transcendent", and that it "begins as a minimal piano ballad before Naughty Boy introduces some clattering [[UK garage]] beats and it becomes, well, not a banger exactly, but definitely something you could tap your foot to under your desk."<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.nme.com/blogs/nme-blogs/beyonce-returns-on-startling-naughty-boy-collaboration-runnin-lose-it-all-15378|title = Beyonce Returns on Startling Naughty Boy Collaboration 'Runnin (Lose It All)'|website = [[NME]]|date = 18 September 2015}}</ref>

==Commercial performance==
"Runnin' (Lose It All)" peaked at number four on the [[UK Singles Chart]], becoming Naughty Boy's fifth, Beyoncé's 18th, and Arrow Benjamin's first top ten song in the UK. The song also reached number one on the Scottish Singles Chart and the French Singles Chart. It was certified double platinum by the [[British Phonographic Industry]] (BPI) for sales of 1,200,000 copies.<ref name="BPI"/> It also reached number one the UK [[airplay]] chart.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.hookblast.com/2015/10/16/uk-airplay-analysis-naughty-boys-runnin-sees-13-3-play-increase-to-hit-no-1/ |title=UK Airplay Analysis: Naughty Boy's Runnin' sees 13.3% play increase to hit No.1 |website=www.hookblast.com |url-status=usurped |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151020142707/http://www.hookblast.com/2015/10/16/uk-airplay-analysis-naughty-boys-runnin-sees-13-3-play-increase-to-hit-no-1/ |archive-date=2015-10-20}}</ref> In the United States, the single peaked at number 90 on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] chart issue dated 10 October 2015.<ref name="US100"/>

==Music video==
The [[music video]] for the song was directed by Charlie Robins. It was released on Naughty Boy's official [[Vevo]] account on 17 September 2015. It features freedivers [[Guillaume Néry]] and [[Alice Modolo]] as a man and a woman underwater running towards each other, trying to reunite. The video is inspired by the short film ''Ocean Gravity''. Discussing the conception of the video, Robins said: "In order to achieve the effect we were after we had to film in the middle of a fast, deep and quite dangerous current through a lagoon, this gave us the forward momentum you see in the film. They didn’t use tanks for air so everything you see in the film was done holding their breath, sometimes up to six minutes at a time. I still can’t quite believe how they do it. No CGI was used. Quite something."<ref>{{cite web|title=Watch Naughty Boy's Breathtaking Underwater Video for 'Lose It All'|url=http://radio.com/2015/09/17/naughty-boy-beyonce-lose-it-all-video/|website=[[Radio.com]]|access-date=19 September 2015|date=17 September 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160220062032/http://radio.com/2015/09/17/naughty-boy-beyonce-lose-it-all-video/|archive-date=20 February 2016|url-status=dead}}</ref> The video was filmed at [[Rangiroa]], an atoll northeast of Tahiti in French Polynesia. The video was choreographed by Julie Gaultier, who co-directed ''Ocean Gravity'' with Néry, and was shot over four days.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cosmopolitan.com/entertainment/music/q-and-a/a46561/lose-it-all-video-diver-interview-alice-modolo/|title=What It Was Like to Shoot the Breathtaking Underwater Video for That New Beyoncé Song|work=[[Cosmopolitan (magazine)|Cosmopolitan]]|first=Darla|last=Murray|date=21 September 2015|access-date=24 September 2015}}</ref>

A ''[[Time (magazine)|Time]]'' magazine writer described the clip as "a very serene underwater adventure".<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://time.com/4039791/naughty-boy-beyonce-video/|title=Watch Naughty Boy's Latest Underwater Video for 'Runnin' (Lose It All)' Feat. Beyoncé|magazine=[[Time (magazine)|Time]]|publisher=Time Inc.|date=18 September 2015|access-date=19 September 2015}}</ref> Alex Hudson from ''Exclaim!'' wrote that the visuals depicted a "dramatic underwater romance".<ref name="exclaim" /> Laura Bradley of ''Slate'' interpreted the video as "a surreal take on a classic premise — two people search for one another across great distances, and finally come together in an embrace".<ref name="slate" /> "Runnin' (Lose It All)" won a Bronze Lion award in the Excellence In Music Video category at the [[Cannes Lions International Festival of Creativity]], on 24 June 2016,<ref>{{cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/business/7416509/cannes-lions-beyonce-formation-video-grand-prix|title=Beyonce's 'Formation' Music Video Wins Grand Prix at Inaugural Cannes Lions Entertainment for Music Awards|first=Andrew|last=Hampp|magazine=Billboard|date=24 June 2016|access-date=26 June 2016}}</ref> and won the Bronze Award for Best Music Promo Film at the [[Creative Circle|Creative Circle Awards]].<ref>{{cite web |title=About: Sarah Tognazzi |url=http://www.sarahtognazzi.com/pages/about |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190701124409/http://www.sarahtognazzi.com/pages/about |archive-date=1 July 2019 |access-date=18 May 2021}}</ref> The song was also shortlisted for the [[Brit Award for British Video of the Year]] and was nominated at the [[Los Premios 40 Principales for Best International Video|Los Premios 40 Principales]].

==Usage in media==
The song is featured in the trailer to the 2017 film ''[[Everything, Everything (film)|Everything, Everything]]''. In August 2017, the song was also featured in the background for Chanel's "Gabrielle" television campaigns.<ref>{{cite web|title=Chanel:Gabrielle|url=https://tvadvertsongs.com/chanel-gabrielle/|website=[[TV Commercial Songs]]|date=3 September 2017|access-date=24 September 2017|archive-date=21 January 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210121171454/https://www.tvadvertsongs.com/chanel-gabrielle/|url-status=dead}}</ref>
