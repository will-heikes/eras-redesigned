"'''Loyal'''" is a song by American singer [[Chris Brown]], released as the fourth [[Single (music)|single]] from his sixth [[studio album]] ''[[X (Chris Brown album)|X]]'' (2014). Produced by [[Nic Nac]] and Mark Kragen, the song features American rapper [[Lil Wayne]] and another American rapper, varying by version: [[Tyga]] on the album version, [[French Montana]] on the East Coast version, and [[Too Short|Too $hort]] on the West Coast version. The song was written by Brown, Wayne, [[Bobby Brackins]], [[Ty Dolla Sign|Ty Dolla $ign]], and the producers, as well as different interpolative-credited artists, varying for each respective version.

"Loyal" received praise from critics, that commended its musicality and unapologetic lyrical content. The song became the highest charting song off the ''X'' album, peaking at number nine on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]]. It stayed on the chart for 36 weeks. Among the certifications it gained, it was most notably certified 8× Platinum by the [[Recording Industry Association of America]] (RIAA), 3× Platinum by the [[Australian Recording Industry Association]] (ARIA), and 2× Platinum by the [[British Phonographic Industry]] (BPI). The song was promoted with performances by Brown and Lil Wayne at the [[BET Awards 2014]], [[Summer Jam (festival)|Summer Jam]], [[2014 Soul Train Music Awards]], and ''[[The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon]]''.

== Background and theme ==
Producer [[Nic Nac]] and American R&B singer [[Ty Dolla Sign|Ty Dolla $ign]], started the work of the song, creating the beat and a first version of the hook.<ref>{{cite web | url=https://www.complex.com/music/a/david-drake/interview-nic-nac-producer-chris-brown-loyal | title=Interview: Producer Nic Nac Talks About the Making of the Other Song of the Summer, Chris Brown's "Loyal" | website=[[Complex Networks]] }}</ref> Nic Nac's manager played that unfinished demo for Chris Brown's manager, then Brown heard it and decided to create a song from it. Talking about the making of "Loyal", Brown said: “Nic Nac and Ty Dolla Sign came with a couple concepts for it, and wrote the pre-demo before I finished the record off”.<ref>{{cite web | url=https://m.youtube.com/watch?v=BH71mjrv97s | title=MC 100: Chris Brown on "Loyal" | website=[[YouTube]] | date=29 December 2014 }}</ref>

On December 16, 2013, the audio to the song was premiered online.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.xxlmag.com/rap-music/new-music/2013/12/chris-brown-featuring-lil-wayne-and-french-montana-loyal/ |title=Chris Brown Featuring Lil Wayne And French Montana "Loyal" - XXL |publisher=Xxlmag.com |date=December 16, 2013 |access-date=January 30, 2014}}</ref> The song was originally released in two versions, an East Coast version and a West Coast version. Both versions feature [[Chris Brown]] and [[Lil Wayne]]; the East Coast version also features [[French Montana]], while the West Coast version features [[Too Short]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.complex.com/music/2013/12/chris-brown-f-lil-wayne-french-montana-loyal |title=Chris Brown f/ Lil Wayne & French Montana "Loyal" |publisher=Complex |date=December 16, 2013 |access-date=January 30, 2014}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.rap-up.com/2013/12/16/new-music-chris-brown-f-lil-wayne-french-montana-loyal/ |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131216233834/http://www.rap-up.com/2013/12/16/new-music-chris-brown-f-lil-wayne-french-montana-loyal/ |url-status=dead |archive-date=December 16, 2013 |title=New Music: Chris Brown f/ Lil Wayne & French Montana – 'Loyal' |publisher=Rap-Up.com |date=December 16, 2013 |access-date=January 30, 2014}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|author=B.Dot |url=http://rapradar.com/2013/12/16/new-music-chris-brown-ft-lil-wayne-x-french-montana-loyalty/ |title=New Music: Chris Brown Ft. Lil Wayne x French Montana "Loyalty" |publisher=Rap Radar |date=December 16, 2013 |access-date=January 30, 2014}}</ref> Then on December 19, both versions of the song were officially released as [[Music download|digital downloads]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://itunes.apple.com/us/album/loyal-east-coast-version-feat./id786456294 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140327060229/https://itunes.apple.com/us/album/loyal-east-coast-version-feat./id786456294 |url-status=dead |archive-date=March 27, 2014 |title=<nowiki>iTunes - Music - Loyal (East Coast Version) [feat. Lil Wayne & French Montana</nowiki>&#93; - Single by Chris Brown |publisher=[[iTunes]] |date=December 19, 2013 |access-date=January 30, 2014}}</ref><ref name="apple1">{{cite web|url=https://itunes.apple.com/us/album/loyal-west-coast-version-feat./id786460205 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140419044735/https://itunes.apple.com/us/album/loyal-west-coast-version-feat./id786460205 |url-status=dead |archive-date=April 19, 2014 |title=<nowiki>iTunes - Music - Loyal (West Coast Version) [feat. Lil Wayne & Too $hort</nowiki>&#93; - Single by Chris Brown |publisher=[[iTunes]] |date=December 19, 2013 |access-date=January 30, 2014}}</ref> On March 24, 2014, the album version of "Loyal" was released, featuring Lil Wayne and [[Tyga]], as the fourth single from his sixth studio album ''[[X (Chris Brown album)|X]]''.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.huffingtonpost.com/2013/12/16/chris-brown-loyal-lil-wayne-french-montana_n_4452821.html |title=Chris Brown's 'Loyal' Drops With Lil' Wayne and French Montana |publisher=Huffingtonpost.com |date=December 16, 2013 |access-date=January 30, 2014}}</ref> "Loyal" contains an [[Interpolation (popular music)|interpolation]] of "[[Money Ain't a Thang]]" (1998), performed by [[Jay-Z]] featuring [[Jermaine Dupri]], for which its composers are credited as writers.
It also contains an interpolation of "Shine" (2000), by Lil Wayne.

The overall theme of the song is [[Infidelity|unfaithful women]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.vibe.com/chris-brown-lil-wayne-french-loyal-stream-new-music |title=New Music: Chris Brown Feat. Lil' Wayne & French Montana 'Loyal' |publisher=Vibe |date=December 16, 2013 |access-date=January 30, 2014}}</ref> In a 2014 interview with [[Ebro Darden]], Chris Brown described the song as following the steps of hip hop tracks like 'All About U' (1996) by [[Tupac Shakur]] and [[Nate Dogg]], which depict experiences with groupies who follow celebrities for opportunistic reasons.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2014-10-31 |title=Chris Brown On 'Ebro In The Morning' - Rap Radar |url=https://rapradar.com/2014/10/31/chris-brown-on-ebro-in-the-morning/ |access-date=2024-02-28 |website=rapradar.com |language=en-US}}</ref>

==Composition==
The song is composed in the key of [[F-sharp major]] in a common time, and has a tempo of 99 beats per minute.

==Critical reception==
The song was met with positive reviews. ==Chart performance==
''Loyal'' debuted at number 82 on the US [[Billboard Hot 100]] chart on the week of February 1, 2014. It eventually made its way to the top-ten, peaking at number nine, becoming Brown's 10th top-ten single on the chart. The song spent a total of 36 weeks on the chart. It also peaked at number four on the US [[Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs]] chart and spent 14 weeks on the chart. As of July 2014, the song has sold a million digital copies in the United States.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.vibe.com/2014/07/chris-brown-loyal-platinum/|title=Chris Brown's 'Loyal' Goes Platinum|first=Camille|last=Augustin|publisher=[[Vibe (magazine)|Vibe]]|date=July 31, 2014|access-date=August 5, 2017}}</ref> In 2023, the single was certified [[RIAA certification|6× Platinum]] by the [[Recording Industry Association of America]] (RIAA) for combined sales and streaming equivalent units of over six million units in the United States.
