"'''Levels'''" is a song by American singer [[Nick Jonas]]. It was released on August 21, 2015, by Island, Safehouse and Republic Records. The song was written and produced by Ian Kirkpatrick and The Monsters and the Strangerz, with additional songwriting provided by Sam Martin, Sean Douglas, Talay Riley and Like Mike. The song's accompanying music video was directed by Colin Tilley and was released on August 30, 2015. The song is the first and only single from ''Nick Jonas X2'', the reissue of ''[[Nick Jonas (album)|Nick Jonas]]''. The song was listed by SPIN Magazine as the 11th best song of 2015.<ref name="spin.com">{{cite web|url=http://www.spin.com/2015/11/the-101-best-songs-of-2015/5/|title=The 101 Best Songs of 2015 - SPIN - Page 5|date=30 November 2015|access-date=24 June 2016}}</ref> The dance remixes of "Levels" reached number one on ''Billboard's'' [[Dance Club Songs]] chart in its December 12, 2015 issue.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/artist/312243/Nick%20Jonas/chart?f=359|title=Nick Jonas - Chart history - Billboard|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|access-date=24 June 2016}}</ref>

==Background==
On August 13, 2015, it was revealed that his new single would be called "Levels" and would be released on August 21, 2015. The announcement was made with a 15-second video preview showing him waking up and getting ready for the day in a James Bond fashion.<ref name="vmas">{{cite web|url = http://www.mtv.com/news/2239613/nick-jonas-levels-vma-preshow/|archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20150815204858/http://www.mtv.com/news/2239613/nick-jonas-levels-vma-preshow/|url-status = dead|archive-date = August 15, 2015|title = Nick Jonas Is Going To All New 'Levels' On The VMA Pre-Show|website = MTV.com|date = August 13, 2015|access-date = August 13, 2015}}</ref>
On August 14, Nick said in an interview for MTV, “I’ve been hard at work on the new music trying to push myself lyrically to say some deeper things,” he said. “And I’ve gone through a lot in this past year and a half and I want to talk about it and just push myself as an artist and continue to grow.”
On August 15, Jonas revealed part of the song's lyrics on his personal Twitter account. He tweeted "I know we can get higher... There's levels to your love. Yeah there's levels to your love."
On August 17, 2015, Nick released the cover of the song.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.idolator.com/7604601/nick-jonas-new-single-levels-cover|archive-url=https://archive.today/20150824023531/http://www.idolator.com/7604601/nick-jonas-new-single-levels-cover|url-status=dead|archive-date=August 24, 2015|title=Nick Jonas Unveils The Cover Of New Single "Levels"|work=Music News, Reviews, and Gossip on Idolator.com|date=18 August 2015}}</ref>

==Reception==
''[[Spin (magazine)|Spin]]'' listed "Levels" as the 11th best song of 2015. '“Levels” clocks in at under three minutes, but were Nick Jonas expands it to three hours of immaculately stacked madness, we'd still play it on a loop. “Levels” offered a beat flexible and exuberant enough to challenge him to rise to the upper-floor'.<ref name="spin.com"/> ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' praised the song, calling it 'really, really good'.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/6671073/nick-jonas-levels-new-single-listen|title=Listen to Nick Jonas' Funky New Single, 'Levels'|magazine=Billboard|date=21 August 2015}}</ref> [[MTV]]'s Emilee Lindner wrote about the song: "Levels' fuses funk with a club beat, layering the former Jonas Brother’s vocals with his spiraling falsetto ad-libs. It’s a quick, under-three-minute tune, but it’s enough to inject you with a little party if you need it."<ref name="MTV Levels">{{cite web|last1=Lindeer|first1=Emilee|title=Nick Jonas' New Song 'Levels' Is A Bouncy Banger: Listen|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/2247354/nick-jonas-levels/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150822131321/http://www.mtv.com/news/2247354/nick-jonas-levels/|url-status=dead|archive-date=August 22, 2015|website=[[MTV]]|publisher=[[Viacom International]]|access-date=August 21, 2015|location=United States|date=August 21, 2015}}</ref>

==Music video==
On August 19, 2015, Jonas released a behind-the-scenes picture on his Instagram account of the video shoot.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.idolator.com/7604739/nick-jonas-teases-levels-music-video-behind-scenes-photo|title=Nick Jonas Teases "Levels" Music Video With Behind-The-Scenes Photo|work=Music News, Reviews, and Gossip on Idolator.com|date=19 August 2015}}{{dead link|date=August 2025|bot=medic}}{{cbignore|bot=medic}}</ref> The music video was released on August 30, 2015.<ref name="MTV Levels"/> On August 25, three snippets of the music video were released online showing that the video takes place in an industrial warehouse with Nick surrounded by dancers, cars and cameras.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/2250326/nick-jonas-sexy-levels-music-video-teaser/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150826222958/http://www.mtv.com/news/2250326/nick-jonas-sexy-levels-music-video-teaser/|url-status=dead|archive-date=August 26, 2015|title=Nick Jonas Is Sexier Than Ever In These 'Levels' Music Video Teasers|work=MTV News}}</ref> On August 29, the behind the scenes video was released online where it was revealed that the video was directed by [[Colin Tilley]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/2254915/nick-jonas-levels-set-visit/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150830051857/http://www.mtv.com/news/2254915/nick-jonas-levels-set-visit/|url-status=dead|archive-date=August 30, 2015|title=Nick Jonas Takes Us Behind The Scenes Of His 'Levels' Video: Watch|work=MTV News}}</ref> The video depicts Jonas and multiple women dancing in an abandoned warehouse. In a couple scenes, Nick is shown going up an elevator, in reference towards a lyric in the beginning of the song.
