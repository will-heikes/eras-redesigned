"'''Work Bitch'''" ([[radio edit|edited for radio]] as "'''Work Work'''") is a song recorded by American singer [[Britney Spears]] for her eighth studio album ''[[Britney Jean]]'' (2013). It was written by Spears, [[will.i.am]], [[Otto Knows]], [[Sebastian Ingrosso]], [[Anthony Preston (record producer)|Anthony Preston]] and [[Ruth-Anne Cunningham]]. The song's production was handled by Ingrosso, Jettman and Adams, while vocal production was done by Adams and Preston. "Work Bitch" made its premiere on September 15, 2013, on [[iHeartRadio]] and select [[Clear Channel Communications|Clear Channel]] radio stations and was released as the [[lead single]] from ''Britney Jean'' the following day, by [[RCA Records]].

"Work Bitch" is an [[electronic dance music|EDM]] song containing mostly [[Spoken word music|spoken lyrics]], where Spears repeatedly exhorts "bitches" to "get to work". "Work Bitch" garnered critical acclaim, with praise drawn towards its production and female empowerment lyrics. It received several certifications from multiple countries. "Work Bitch" debuted and peaked at number 12 on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]]. Outside of the United States, "Work Bitch" peaked within the top ten of the charts in 14 countries, including Canada, France, Spain and the United Kingdom.

An accompanying music video for "Work Bitch" was directed by Ben Mor and filmed in [[Malibu, California]], and released in October 2013. It is [[List of most expensive music videos|one of the most expensive music videos of all time]] and has generated acclaim from critics, who praised Spears' dancing and the visual cinematography.

"Work Bitch" was written by Spears with will.i.am and Anthony Preston, and features a beat from DJ [[Otto Knows]].<ref name=hp/> Knows is managed by [[Swedish House Mafia]] member [[Sebastian Ingrosso]], who also received a songwriting credit; however, Ingrosso revealed he did not write the lyrics.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/music/pop/sebastian-ingrosso-on-britney-spears-new-single-i-didnt-write-it-5694976/|title=Sebastian Ingrosso on Britney Spears' New Single: 'I Didn't Write It!'|last=McCarthy|first=Zel|date=September 12, 2013|access-date=September 15, 2013|magazine=Billboard|publisher=Prometheus Global Media}}</ref> "Work Bitch" is an [[electronic dance music|EDM]] song<ref name="Slant"/> written in [[common time]] in the key of [[E minor]],<ref>{{Cite web|last1=Britney|first1=Spears|last2=Otto|first2=Jettman|last3=Anthony|first3=Preston|last4=William|first4=Adams|last5=Sebastian|first5=Ingrosso|last6=Ruth-Anne|first6=Cunningham|last7=Britney|first7=Spears|date=November 7, 2013|title=Work Bitch|url=https://www.musicnotes.com/sheetmusic/mtd.asp?ppn=MN0126814|access-date=June 8, 2021|website=Musicnotes.com}}</ref> at a tempo of 128 [[beats per minute]], wherein Spears, repeatedly exhorts "bitches" to "get to work".<ref name=hp>{{cite news|url=http://www.huffingtonpost.com/2013/09/15/work-bitch-leaks-britney-spears-release_n_3930424.html|title='Work B**ch' Leaks Ahead of Britney Spears' Official Release|last=Boardman|first=Madeline|date=September 15, 2013|access-date=September 15, 2013|work=[[The Huffington Post]]|publisher=[[AOL]]}}</ref> It starts with a basic club beat, with a monotone taunting chorus: "You want a hot body? / You want a [[Bugatti Automobiles|Bugatti]]? / You want a [[Maserati]]? / You better work bitch!"<ref name=bbleak/><ref name=hp/> Spears alludes to the luxuries she has attained due to her strong work ethic: "You want a [[Lamborghini]]? / Sip [[Martini (cocktail)|martinis]]? / Look hot in a bikini? / You better work bitch!" and "You wanna live fancy? / Live in a big mansion? / Party in France?" <ref name=hp/>

According to Miriam Coleman of ''[[Rolling Stone]]'', it "features mostly [[Spoken word|spoken lyrics]], [and] serves as a testament to the fine things a strong work ethic can bring about".<ref name=rs>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.rollingstone.com/music/videos/britney-spears-releases-work-bitch-early-after-track-leaks-20130915|title=Britney Spears Releases 'Work Bitch' Early After Track Leaks|last=Coleman|first=Mirriam|magazine=Rolling Stone|publisher=Wenner Media|date=September 15, 2013|access-date=September 15, 2013}}</ref> ''[[Idolator (website)|Idolator]]'' contributor Christina Lee thought that the song "sounds like the lights-out after-party" to [[RuPaul]]'s 1993 debut single "[[Supermodel (You Better Work)]]".<ref name=idolator>{{cite web|url=http://idolator.com/7484328/britney-spears-work-bitch|archive-url=https://archive.today/20130916020458/http://idolator.com/7484328/britney-spears-work-bitch|url-status=dead|archive-date=September 16, 2013|title=Britney Spears' "Work Bitch" Hits The Web Early: Listen To The Club Banger That Works (Bitch)|last=Lee|first=Christina|date=September 15, 2013|access-date=September 15, 2013|publisher=Idolator (Buzz Media)}}</ref> will.i.am commented that the song "is a reflection of Britney moreso than [''Britney Jean'']. The album is what the album is, but we felt that song needed to come out to keep the foundation on what Britney represents. But it shouldn't reflect the album—an album is a body of work as a collective. If we had to pick a song like, 'Oh, what song fits every color of the record,' you shouldn't do that… We felt that song represents '[[Britney: Piece of Me|Piece of Me]]', that Britney oomph."<ref name=bbwiliam>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/music/music-news/william-work-bitch-reflects-britney-spears-more-than-her-new-album-5755398/|title=Will.i.am: 'Work Bitch' Reflects Britney Spears More Than Her New Album|last=Lipshutz|first=Jason|date=October 11, 2013|access-date=October 25, 2013|magazine=Billboard|publisher=Prometheus Global Media}}</ref> Some critics noted Spears's apparent British accent in her performance, with ''Billboard'' writing: "Britney utilizes a British accent on “Work Bitch.” This is an okay thing!"<ref>{{Cite web |last=Lipshutz |first=Jason |date=2013-09-16 |title=Britney Spears’ ‘Work Bitch’: The 10 Best Things About The New Single |url=https://www.billboard.com/music/music-news/britney-spears-work-bitch-the-10-best-things-about-the-new-single-5695492/ |access-date=2025-07-11 |website=Billboard |language=en-US}}</ref>

==Commercial performance==
The song debuted on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] at number 12, marking Spears' 31st song on the chart and the fifth highest debut of her career on the chart, and her seventh in the top 20. It also marked Spears' 19th top 20 single and overall her 23rd top 40 single. It also entered the top 10 on the Hot Digital Songs chart at number six. "Work Bitch" sold 174,000 copies in its first week,<ref>{{cite web|last=Grein|first=Paul|url=http://music.yahoo.com/blogs/chart-watch/week-ending-sept-22-2013-songs-brit-back-204122521.html|title=Week Ending Sept. 22, 2013. Songs: The Brit Is Back|publisher=Music.yahoo.com|date=September 25, 2013|access-date=September 27, 2013}}</ref> marking Spears' highest first-week sales since her 2011 number-one single "[[Hold It Against Me]]".<ref>{{cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/5725173/jack-johnson-scores-fourth-no-1-album-on-billboard-200|title=Jack Johnson Scores Fourth No. 1 Album on Billboard 200|magazine=Billboard|publisher=Prometheus Global Media|date=September 25, 2013|access-date=September 27, 2013}}</ref> With its debut on the [[Mainstream Top 40]] at number 25, the song marks Spears' 31st chart entry, pushing her past [[Mariah Carey]] (30) for the second-most entries to the chart's October 3, 1992, launch.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/5719511/chart-highlights-britney-spears-bows-katy-perry-lady-gaga-leap-to-no-1|title=Chart Highlights: Britney Spears Bows, Katy Perry, Lady Gaga Leap To No. 1|last=Trust|first=Gary|date=September 23, 2013|access-date=September 27, 2013|magazine=Billboard|publisher=Prometheus Global Media}}</ref> In its second week, the song fell to number 41 on the ''Billboard'' Hot 100. The following week, it climbed back to number 13 after the release of the accompanying music video. As of July 2016, "Work Bitch" has sold 967,000 copies in the United States.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/ask-billboard-britney-spears-career-sales-how-big-a-hit-will/|title=Ask Billboard: Britney Spears' Career Sales & How Big a Hit Will 'Make Me' Be?|last=Trust|first=Gary|date=July 17, 2016|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|access-date=July 17, 2016|archive-date=July 18, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160718143046/http://www.billboard.com/articles/columns/chart-beat/7439077/ask-billboard-britney-spears-career-sales-how-big-a-hit-will|url-status=live}}</ref>

In the United Kingdom, "Work Bitch" debuted and peaked at number seven on the [[UK Singles Chart]] on November 10, 2013 – for the week ending date November 16, 2013 – falling 30 places to number 37 the following week. The song also peaked at number two on the [[UK Dance Chart]].<ref name=bbwiliam/>
