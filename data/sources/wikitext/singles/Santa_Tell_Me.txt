"'''Santa Tell Me'''" is a [[Christmas music|Christmas song]] by American singer-songwriter [[Ariana Grande]]. It was released as a [[Single (music)|single]] by [[Republic Records]] on November 24, 2014, and was included on the Japanese exclusive reissue of Grande's first EP ''[[Christmas Kisses (EP)|Christmas Kisses]]'' (2013), as well as a "naughty version" on Grande's second EP ''[[Christmas & Chill]]'' (2015). She and [[Savan Kotecha]] co-wrote the song with its producer [[Ilya Salmanzadeh]].

"Santa Tell Me" has become a Christmas [[Standard (music)|standard]], with a significant rise in popularity every [[Christmas season]]. It became the first holiday song released in the 21st century to reach the top five in the United States when it peaked at number five on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] in 2024, ten years after its release. It has reached the top ten in numerous other countries worldwide, including Australia, Austria, Canada, Czech Republic, Denmark, Finland, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Italy, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, the Netherlands, New Zealand, Norway, Poland, Portugal, Singapore, Slovakia, South Korea, Sweden, Switzerland, and the United Kingdom, as well as the top 20 in Belgium, Croatia, Ireland, Malaysia and the Philippines. On the [[Billboard Global 200|''Billboard'' Global 200]] chart, "Santa Tell Me" additionally peaked at number five.

[[Chris Marrs Piliero]] directed the track's music video, which was released on Grande's official [[Vevo]] on December 12, 2014. She first performed the song at the 2014 ''A Very Grammy Christmas'' concert in the [[Shrine Auditorium]] in [[Los Angeles]] on November 18, 2014.

==Background==
Grande released her first Christmas EP titled ''[[Christmas Kisses (EP)|Christmas Kisses]]'' on December 17, 2013.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://music.apple.com/us/album/christmas-kisses-ep/776101988|title=Christmas Kisses – EP by Ariana Grande|date=January 2013|publisher=[[iTunes Store]]|access-date=March 15, 2025|archive-date=August 29, 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180829175212/https://itunes.apple.com/us/album/christmas-kisses-ep/id776101988|url-status=live}}</ref> The extended play contains four songs, and included covers of classics, such as "[[Santa Baby]]" and [[Wham!]]'s "[[Last Christmas]]". It also contained two original songs: "Love Is Everything" and "Snow in California".{{citation needed|date=October 2026}}

"Santa Tell Me" was first mentioned by Grande on October 28, 2014, in a livestream through Twitcam.<ref>{{cite web|last1=Hyndman|first1=Jessica|title=Is Ariana Grande Behind That Secret 'Hunger Games' Soundtrack Song?|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/1979171/ariana-grande-hunger-games-mockingjay-soundtrack/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141031202736/http://www.mtv.com/news/1979171/ariana-grande-hunger-games-mockingjay-soundtrack/|url-status=dead|archive-date=October 31, 2014|publisher=MTV|date=October 28, 2014}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Lltouqs4f5g&t=2m21s|title=YouTube|via=YouTube|access-date=December 9, 2018|archive-date=December 15, 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20211215193522/https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Lltouqs4f5g&t=2m21s|url-status=live}}</ref> In the livestream, she said that she did not want to do a Christmas song at first, but she changed her mind. "Santa Tell Me" is a [[Christmas music|Christmas song]] with influences of [[Pop music|pop]] and [[Contemporary R&B|R&B]]. It is written in the key of [[G major]], with a moderate tempo of 96 [[Tempo|beats per minute]] in [[common time]]. Grande's vocals range from the low note of B<sub>3</sub> to the high note of A<sub>5</sub>. Lyrically, the song features the protagonist asking Santa Claus to tell her if her new lover "really cares", and whether he will leave her like men in her past have.{{Citation needed|date=June 2015}}

Songwriters Savan Kotecha and ILYA wanted to write a Christmas song and the idea for "Santa Tell Me" came to Kotecha when he was taking a shower. In an interview with Songwriting Magazine, he said, "I was in the shower in LA and it just came into my head. I have the voice note somewhere. I just stepped out of the shower, got water all over the phone and I recorded it." The pair then swapped ideas before Grande came in and helped finish the song.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.songwritingmagazine.co.uk/how-i-wrote/ariana-grande-santa-tell-me|title=How We Wrote Santa Tell Me|website=Songwriting Magazine|date=December 13, 2022|access-date=May 22, 2023|archive-date=May 22, 2023|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230522141404/https://www.songwritingmagazine.co.uk/how-i-wrote/ariana-grande-santa-tell-me|url-status=live}}</ref>

==Critical reception==
[[Brian Mansfield]] of ''[[USA Today]]'' compared the song unfavorably to [[Mariah Carey]]'s "[[All I Want for Christmas Is You]]", stating that it fell short in its attempt at becoming a "seasonal staple".<ref>{{cite news|last1=Mansfield|first1=Brian|url=https://www.usatoday.com/story/life/music/2014/12/01/ariana-grande-santa-tell-me-listen-up-song-of-the-week/19737323/|title=Song of the week: Ariana Grande's 'Santa Tell Me'|newspaper=[[USA Today]]|date=December 1, 2014|accessdate=December 5, 2014}}</ref> ''Billboard'' placed "Santa Tell Me" at number 13 on their "The 100 Best Christmas Songs of All Time" list. It is the highest-ranking song on the list among those released in the 21st century.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.billboard.com/lists/best-christmas-songs/|title=The 100 Best Christmas Songs of All Time: Staff List|newspaper=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|date=November 30, 2021|archive-date=December 30, 2022|access-date=November 29, 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20221230032803/https://www.billboard.com/lists/best-christmas-songs/|url-status=live}}</ref>

==Commercial performance==
"Santa Tell Me" has recurrently charted in several nations every year since its original release in 2014. In the United States, "Santa Tell Me" debuted at number 65 on the weekly US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] chart dated December 13, 2014, and reached number 42 in its original run.<ref name="hot100"/> Six years later, "Santa Tell Me" re-entered the Hot 100 chart at a new high of number 39 on the chart issue dated December 19, 2020, earning Grande her 35th top 40 entry of her career. As of 2025, it has since peaked at number five, earning Grande her 23rd top ten hit and 17th top five hit on the ''Billboard'' Hot 100.<ref name="hot100"/> "Santa Tell Me" is the most-recently released Christmas song to reach the top ten of the Hot 100{{snd}}one of the two such songs released in the 21st century to achieve this feat, the other being [[Kelly Clarkson]]'s "[[Underneath the Tree]]"{{snd}}and the most recently-released holiday song to reach the top five.

In its original run, "Santa Tell Me" peaked at number seven on the [[Billboard (magazine)|''Billboard'']] [[Adult Contemporary (chart)|Adult Contemporary]] chart for the week ending December 27, 2014. It also topped the ''Billboard'' Holiday 100 chart on the week ending January 10, 2015, becoming only the third song, and second by a female artist, to do so since the chart's inception in 2010. As of 2024, it has been certified 4× Platinum for having sold 4 million units in the United States. On the [[Billboard Global 200|''Billboard'' Global 200]], which tracks the most streamed and digitally sold songs in over 200 territories, "Santa Tell Me" peaked at number five, becoming Grande's third top-five hit on the chart.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/articles/business/chart-beat/9505043/mariah-carey-christmas-rules-global-charts/|title=Mariah Carey's 'All I Want For Christmas Is You' Rules Both Billboard Global Charts|last=Trust|first=Gary|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|date=December 28, 2020|accessdate=December 28, 2020|archive-date=December 28, 2020|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201228190214/https://www.billboard.com/articles/business/chart-beat/9505043/mariah-carey-christmas-rules-global-charts/|url-status=live}}</ref> In Canada, "Santa Tell Me" debuted at number 73 on the [[Canadian Hot 100]], eventually reaching a peak of number 27 in its original chart run. The track re-entered the Canadian Hot 100 chart, and has since attained a new peak of number eight in 2020, becoming Grande's 19th top ten hit in the country.<ref>{{cite magazine|title=Ariana Grande – Chart history: Billboard Canadian Hot 100|url=http://www.billboard.com/artist/1484343/Ariana+Grande/chart?f=793|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|date=December 13, 2014|access-date=February 19, 2015|archive-date=April 26, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150426095754/http://www.billboard.com/artist/1484343/Ariana+Grande/chart?f=793|url-status=live}}</ref>

In the United Kingdom, "Santa Tell Me" entered the [[UK Singles Chart]] at number 79 for the week of December 6, 2014, originally peaking at number 68 three weeks later. In 2015, it failed to re-enter the charts; however, in 2016, the song reached number 90. In 2017, it was among many other holiday songs (such as "[[Driving Home for Christmas]]" and "[[Step into Christmas]]") reaching new chart peaks in the UK by re-entering the UK Singles Chart at number 60, and climbing to an even higher position of number 29 the following week. It eventually reached number 13 on the week of December 28, 2018, attaining the same position once again the following year (in December 2019). In November 2023, "Santa Tell Me" re-entered the UK Singles Chart for an eighth consecutive year, reaching an overall peak position of number 8 in late December. With over 220 million plays, "Santa Tell Me" is also the eighth most played Christmas track in the United Kingdom. It is one of the highest-placed Christmas songs present in the top forty and the most streamed Christmas track in the United Kingdom released since 2010.<ref>{{Cite web|title=The Official Top 40 most-streamed Christmas songs|url=https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/the-official-top-40-most-streamed-christmas-songs__31778/|url-status=live|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200530134911/https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/the-official-top-40-most-streamed-christmas-songs__31778/|archive-date=May 30, 2020|accessdate=December 5, 2020|publisher=[[Official Charts]]}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|title=The Official Top 40 biggest Christmas songs of the 21st century revealed|url=https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/biggest-christmas-songs-21st-century/|url-status=live|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20231207231233/https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/biggest-christmas-songs-21st-century/|archive-date=December 7, 2023|accessdate=December 30, 2023|publisher=[[Official Charts]]}}</ref>

==Music video==
The official video was released on December 12, 2014. It was directed by Alfredo Flores and Jones Crow.<ref name="Vid">{{cite web|url=http://www.vevo.com/watch/ariana-grande/Santa-Tell-Me/USUV71403077|title=Santa Tell Me - Ariana Grande - Vevo|publisher=Vevo|accessdate=April 10, 2016|archive-date=December 12, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161212223824/http://www.vevo.com/watch/ariana-grande/santa-tell-me/USUV71403077|url-status=live}}</ref> The video features Grande with her friends dancing, laughing and giving gifts around her house along with a two-minute [[outtake]] section at the end.<ref>{{cite web|last1=Ishler|first1=Julianne|title=Ariana Grande Releases Adorable 'Santa Tell Me' Music Video|url=http://hollywoodlife.com/2014/12/13/ariana-grande-santa-tell-me-music-video/|website=Hollywood Life|date=December 13, 2014|access-date=December 14, 2014|archive-date=November 20, 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20181120062251/https://hollywoodlife.com/2014/12/13/ariana-grande-santa-tell-me-music-video/|url-status=dead}}</ref> It surpassed 100 million views on November 26, 2016, making it Grande's fourteenth Vevo-certified music video after "[[Let Me Love You (Ariana Grande song)|Let Me Love You]]".<ref>{{cite web|title=Side To Side - Ariana Grande - Vevo|url=http://www.vevo.com/watch/ariana-grande/santa-tell-me/USUV71403077|publisher=[[Vevo]]|accessdate=November 27, 2016|date=November 26, 2016|archive-date=December 12, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161212223824/http://www.vevo.com/watch/ariana-grande/santa-tell-me/USUV71403077|url-status=live}}</ref>
