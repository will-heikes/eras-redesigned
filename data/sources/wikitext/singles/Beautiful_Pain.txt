"'''Beautiful Pain'''" is a song by American rapper [[Eminem]] featuring Australian singer [[Sia]], appearing as a bonus track on his 2013 album ''[[The Marshall Mathers LP 2]]'',<ref>{{Cite news|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/2305860/eminem-sia-beautiful-pain/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161111112048/http://www.mtv.com/news/2305860/eminem-sia-beautiful-pain/|url-status=dead|archive-date=November 11, 2016|title=Eminem Calls On Sia To Heal His 'Beautiful Pain': Listen To 'The Marshall Mathers LP 2' Bonus Track|work=MTV News|access-date=2018-05-23|language=en}}</ref> marking the first collaboration between the two [[musicians]]. They further collaborated on the song "[[Guts Over Fear]]" from Eminem's 2014 compilation album ''[[SHADYXV]]''. ''Billboard'' described the song as an "emo-punched rap ballad", and one of Eminem's best collaborations with a female artist.<ref>{{Cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/lists/eminem-best-collaborations-female-artists/|title=Eminem's Top 9 Collaborations With Female Artists: Beyoncé, Rihanna, P!nk and More (Critic's Picks)|magazine=Billboard|access-date=2018-05-23}}</ref> In 2014, ''Spin'' ranked the song number 264 out of Eminem's 289 songs released to date.<ref>{{Cite news|url=https://www.spin.com/2014/10/eminem-every-song-ranked/|title=All 289 Eminem Songs, Ranked|date=2014-10-27|work=Spin|access-date=2018-05-23}}</ref>
