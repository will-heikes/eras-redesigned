"'''Say Something'''" is a song by American [[Pop music|pop]] duo [[A Great Big World]] from their debut album, ''[[Is There Anybody Out There? (album)|Is There Anybody Out There?]]'' (2014). Written by the duo members—[[Ian Axel]] and Chad King—alongside Mike Campbell, the song was originally recorded by Axel for his solo album ''This Is the New Year'' (2011). It was later released as a single by the duo on September 3, 2013, by [[Epic Records]]. Following its usage on American reality TV show ''[[So You Think You Can Dance (United States)|So You Think You Can Dance]]'', the track gained attention from singer [[Christina Aguilera]], who wanted to collaborate with A Great Big World on the song. Soon afterwards, a re-recorded version of "Say Something" with Aguilera was released on November 4, 2013.

"Say Something" is a slow-tempo [[piano ballad]] that talks about a breakup, where the lover is implored to say something because the singer is giving up on them, with the singers expressing humility, sadness, and regret. In the single version with Aguilera, she plays a ghost of the lover to whom the song is addressed as she traces the steps of the lead vocal. The song was praised by [[music criticism|music critics]] for its lyrics, composition and Aguilera's vocal delivery. At the [[57th Annual Grammy Awards]], the song earned A Great Big World and Aguilera a [[Grammy Award for Best Pop Duo/Group Performance]].

"Say Something" did not sell significantly well until the version with Aguilera was available. It debuted at number 16 on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] chart after A Great Big World and Aguilera performed the song on ''[[The Voice (U.S. season 5)|The Voice]]''. It eventually peaked at number four and has since sold over ten million copies in the United States. It also topped the singles charts in Australia, the [[Flanders]] region of Belgium, and Canada, while reaching top 5 in Austria, Israel, New Zealand, Sweden and the United Kingdom. A music video was released on November 19, 2013, featuring the trio singing as people "act out the heartbreaking lyrics." To further promote "Say Something", A Great Big World and Aguilera performed the track at the [[American Music Awards of 2013]]. Numerous covers of the song were released, including those by [[Luke Evans]] and [[Nicole Kidman]], and [[Tiësto]].

==Background and release==
"Say Something" was originally released on February 8, 2011, on band member Ian Axel's solo album ''This Is the New Year'' with the song featuring harmonies by guest vocalist [[Jenny Owen Youngs]], but the track went largely unnoticed until it received attention after being used on the TV series ''[[So You Think You Can Dance (United States)|So You Think You Can Dance]]'' on [[So You Think You Can Dance (U.S. season 10)#Week 10 (September 3, 2013)|season 10's semi final episode]] contemporary dance routine by the eventual champion Amy Yakima. This sparked a chain reaction that eventually made its way to [[Christina Aguilera]].<ref name="billboardinterview">{{cite news|last=Lipshut|first=Jason|title=A Great Big World Talks 'Say Something' & Answering Christina Aguilera's Call|url=https://www.billboard.com/music/music-news/a-great-big-world-talks-say-something-answering-christina-5778275/|access-date=November 10, 2013|newspaper=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|date=November 6, 2013}}</ref> "'Say Something' was danced to on ''So You Think You Can Dance'' almost two months ago, and so many people responded to it," Axel tells Billboard. "In that whole process, someone on our team played it for someone on Christina's team, and we got a call that Christina wanted to record it, and then, literally a week later, we were in L.A. recording it with her."<ref name="billboardinterview" /> The pair considers Aguilera as having "one of the top voices in the world," says Chad King, and re-recorded "Say Something" with her in two hours. "We look at Christina as this icon who can say anything and make it sound amazing," says King.<ref name="billboardinterview" />

About approaching the duo to re-record the track, Aguilera said, "Somebody sent me the song (...) and it's just the most simplest song that doesn't have to fit a formula to be heard and to be appreciated, so I just heard it and I've never done anything like this before and I was just like, 'You know', I just started to hear a harmony part over it and I was like, 'get in touch with these guys and see if they wanna get in the studio and sit behind the piano and just vibe together and see what happens...' and we did, and they're so humble, so sweet, so down to earth, and I'm all about that and supporting that, and we came together so organically and it was fun."<ref name="access">{{cite news|title=Christina Aguilera Talks Collaborating on Say Something; Blake Shelton on His Mom Putting Him in Beauty Pageants|url=http://watch.accesshollywood.com/video/christina-aguilera-talks-collaborating-on-say-something-blake-shelton-on-his-mom-putting-him-in-beauty-pageants/2820428278001|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131110212316/http://watch.accesshollywood.com/video/christina-aguilera-talks-collaborating-on-say-something-blake-shelton-on-his-mom-putting-him-in-beauty-pageants/2820428278001|url-status=dead|archive-date=November 10, 2013|access-date=November 10, 2013|newspaper=[[Access Hollywood]]}}</ref> Aguilera also added, "It's so quiet and still and steady and in a way pleading," she describes the song. "I'm only taking on projects that feel good to me and represent, as always, a purpose of the here and now in my life."<ref name="idolatorvid">{{cite news|last=Wass|first=Mike|title=Christina Aguilera Teams Up with a Great Big World for a New Version Of "Say Something": Watch|url=http://www.idolator.com/7491626/christina-aguilera-great-big-world-say-something|archive-url=https://archive.today/20131110202506/http://www.idolator.com/7491626/christina-aguilera-great-big-world-say-something|url-status=dead|archive-date=November 10, 2013|access-date=November 10, 2013|newspaper=[[Idolator (website)|Idolator]]|date=November 1, 2013}}</ref>

==Critical response==
[[File:Ian Axel and Chad Vaccarino at Bowery Ballroom May 2011.jpg|thumb|150px|right|The personal lyrics, written by the band members (''pictured''), were highly praised by critics.]]
Rick Florino of ''[[Artist Direct]]'' gave the song five out of five stars, calling it "[[Academy Award|Oscar]]-worthy", with "a cinematic heft to the track that makes it utterly vivid and vibrant." Florino also praised Aguilera's performance, calling it "one of her best ever, fortifying the hook and harmony masterfully."<ref name="artistdirect">{{cite news|last=Florino|first=Rick|title=A Great Big World "Say Something" Featuring Christina Aguilera Song Review&nbsp;– 5 out of 5 stars|url=http://www.artistdirect.com/entertainment-news/article/a-great-big-world-say-something-featuring-christina-aguilera-song-review-5-out-of-5-stars/10856258|access-date=November 10, 2013|newspaper=[[Artist Direct]]|date=November 7, 2013}}</ref> Writing for ''[[The Times-Picayune/The New Orleans Advocate|The New Orleans Advocate]]'', Keith Spera opined that the song is "a forthright, unadorned break-up ballad".<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.nola.com/entertainment_life/festivals/christina-aguilera-played-the-pop-diva-for-better-and-worse-at-the-new-orleans-jazz/article_3dd1b291-c893-57c2-82e5-c2a9b94b98aa.html|title=Christina Aguilera played the pop diva, for better and worse, at the New Orleans Jazz Fest|work=[[The Times-Picayune/The New Orleans Advocate]]|publisher=Georges Media Group|date=May 3, 2014|first=Keith|last=Spera|access-date=September 26, 2023}}</ref> Lewis Corner of ''[[Digital Spy]]'' praised Aguilera for "reminding us why she always sounds infinitely better when heading up a ballad", calling it "a beautifully simplistic ode to heartache that evidently connects."<ref name="digitalspyreview" />

Robert Copsey also of ''Digital Spy'' called it "one of [Aguilera's] most understated outings in recent memory, and it's all the better for it."<ref name="ds">{{cite news|last=Copsey|first=Robert|title=Playlist: 10 tracks you need to hear|url=http://www.digitalspy.co.uk/music/thesound/a528593/playlist-10-tracks-you-need-to-hear.html|access-date=November 11, 2013|newspaper=[[Digital Spy]]|date=November 4, 2013}}</ref>  Jon O'Brien of ''[[Yahoo! ===Australia and Europe===
In [[Australia]], "Say Something" debuted at number 47 on the week of December 29, 2013. Later, it re-entered at number 45, on January 19, 2014. The song went to jump from number 50 to number 9, on the week of February 2, 2014, until it reached the top of the [[ARIA Charts]] on the week February 16, 2014. It became A Great Big World's first number-one single and Aguilera's third number-one single (the last being "Beautiful" in 2003).<ref name="aria" /> The song became Aguilera's 18th Top 10 single in Australia, her last entry was on the Pitbull song "[[Feel This Moment]]" which made it to number six in March 2013.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.take40.com/news/44523/aria-take-40-chart-facts-25th-january-2014---pharrell-williams-%22happy%22-stays-at-number-one|title=ARIA Take 40 Chart Facts 25th January 2014|work=take40.com|publisher=MCM Media|first=Gavin|last=Ryan|date=January 25, 2014|access-date=January 25, 2014|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140202174142/http://www.take40.com/news/44523/aria-take-40-chart-facts-25th-january-2014---pharrell-williams-%22happy%22-stays-at-number-one|archive-date=February 2, 2014|df=mdy-all}}</ref> In [[New Zealand]], the single debuted at number 18, on December 16, 2013, while on February 10, 2014, the song reached a peak of number two; Aguilera's highest charting-single since her collaboration with [[Maroon 5]] in "[[Moves Like Jagger]]" (2011), and her 14th top-five single.<ref name="nz" />

In [[Europe]], the song managed to become a huge success. In [[Austria]], the song peaked at number four on the [[Austrian Singles Chart]],<ref name="austria" /> while in [[Norway]], the song peaked at number eight on the [[Norwegian Singles Chart]]<ref name="norway" /> and in [[Sweden]], "Say Something" became a success, peaking at number four on the [[Swedish Singles Chart]].<ref name="swedish" /> In other countries, "Say Something" was a moderate success, where in [[Spain]], it peaked at number 24,<ref name="spain" /> in [[Switzerland]], the song reached a peak of number 31<ref name="swiss" /> and in [[France]], the song is currently at number 29, as of March 1, 2014.<ref name="french" />
In the [[United Kingdom]], "Say Something" debuted and peaked at number four on the [[UK Singles Chart]], becoming Aguilera's 24th Top 40 hit in Britain and A Great Big World's debut there.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/sam-smith-becomes-first-brits-critics-choice-winner-to-debut-at-number-1-2308/|title=Sam Smith becomes first BRITs Critics Choice winner to debut at Number 1|work=officialcharts.com|publisher=The Official Charts Company|first=Justin|last=Myers|date=February 24, 2014|access-date=February 25, 2014}}</ref>

===Reception===
[[File:xtinasaysomethingvideo.png|thumb|right|220px|The scene where [[Christina Aguilera]] is shown with tears welling in her eyes was praised by critics and fans alike due to her delivery.]]
The video received widespread acclaim from critics. For Jason Lipshut of ''[[Billboard Magazine]]'', "Aguilera and AGBW's Ian Axel look utterly sorrowful as they croon the break-up ballad together, with Axel carefully pounding away at the grand piano and Aguilera appearing on the verge of tears as the song reaches its climax. Meanwhile, various tear-inducing images accompany the majestic track, often in slow motion&nbsp;– most strikingly, an older man climbing into a hospital bed with his peaceful female counterpart and emitting a silent shout."<ref name="billboardvideo">{{cite news|last=Lipshut|first=Jason|title=A Great Big World, Christina Aguilera Shine In 'Say Something' Video: Watch|url=https://www.billboard.com/music/music-news/a-great-big-world-christina-aguilera-shine-in-say-something-video-5793380/|access-date=November 20, 2013|newspaper=Billboard|date=November 20, 2013}}</ref> Peter Gicas of ''[[E! No over-the-top diva theatrics, no wigs&nbsp;— just raw emotion and a genuinely powerful performance."<ref name="muumuse1">{{cite news|last=Stern|first=Bradley|title=The Video for A Great Big World's "Say Something" with Christina Aguilera Will Also Make You Cry|url=http://www.muumuse.com/2013/11/the-video-for-a-great-big-worlds-say-something-with-christina-aguilera-will-also-make-you-cry.html/|access-date=November 20, 2013|newspaper=MuuMuse|date=November 19, 2013}}</ref>

*The song is featured in the movie ''Perdona Si Te Llamo Amor'', released on June 19, 2014.
*The song is heard in the first theatrical trailer for the [[If I Stay (film)|film adaptation]] of [[Gayle Forman]]'s novel, ''[[If I Stay]]''.<ref>{{cite web| url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2agNeovUjr0|title=If I Stay&nbsp;— Official Trailer (2014) Chloë Grace Moretz [HD]|website=YouTube|date=April 15, 2014| access-date=June 1, 2014}}</ref>

*The song is heard in the episode 4 of season one of ''[[Red Band Society]]''.
*The original version of the song, without Christina Aguilera, is used in a [[South Park season 19|season 19]] episode of ''[[South Park]]''; the episode was entitled "[[Tweek x Craig]]".
*The song is featured in a trailer for the movie ''[[The 33]]''.
* The original version of the song is featured at the end of episode 4, titled "Secrets and Lies", from season 8 of ''[[Heartland (Canadian TV series)|Heartland]]''.
*The song is featured at the end of the season five finale of ''[[Republic of Doyle]]'' ("Buried").
*The song was featured in the Chinese drama ''[[Meteor Garden (2018 TV series)|Meteor Garden 2018]]''.
*The instrumental version of the song was featured in [[Google]]'s 2020 [[Super Bowl commercial]] titled "Loretta".
