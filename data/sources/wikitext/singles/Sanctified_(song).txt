"'''Sanctified'''" is a song by American rapper [[Rick Ross]], taken from his sixth studio album ''[[Mastermind (Rick Ross album)|Mastermind]]'' (2014). The song, [[Record production|produced]] by [[Kanye West]], [[Mike Dean (record producer)|Mike Dean]], and [[DJ Mustard]], featuring guest appearances by rappers Kanye West and [[Big Sean]]. It was written by the aforementioned rappers and producers and [[Betty Wright]] sang the hook. 

"Sanctified" garnered critical acclaim from [[music critic]]s, with most praising the production and Kanye West's appearance. It is the highest-charting song on ''Mastermind'' to date, debuting at number 25 on the [[UK R&B Chart]], number 78 on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] and number 99 on the [[Canadian Hot 100]] upon the album's release.

== Background and development ==
[[File:Kanye West Lollapalooza Chile 2011 2.jpg|180px|thumb|right|[[Kanye West]] was the song's primary [[Hip hop production|producer]] and he also makes a guest appearance.]]
In February 2014, a short preview of the song was released featuring the chorus, performed by [[Kanye West]] and [[Big Sean]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://hypetrak.com/2014/02/rick-ross-featuring-kanye-west-big-sean-sanctified-snippet/ |title=Rick Ross featuring Kanye West & Big Sean – Sanctified (Snippet) |website=Hypetrak |date=February 12, 2014 |access-date=March 12, 2014}}</ref> Following the track list reveal for the album, on February 7, 2014, producer [[DJ Mustard]] revealed that he co-[[Hip hop production|produced]] "Sanctified" with [[Kanye West]].<ref>{{cite magazine|last=Tardio |first=Andres |url=http://www.hiphopdx.com/index/news/id.27428/title.kanye-west-dj-mustard-co-produce-sanctified-on-rick-ross-mastermind- |title=Kanye West & DJ Mustard Co-Produce "Sanctified" On Rick Ross' "Mastermind" |magazine=[[HipHopDX]] |publisher=Cheri Media Group |date=February 8, 2014 |access-date=March 2, 2014 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140409164056/http://www.hiphopdx.com/index/news/id.27428/title.kanye-west-dj-mustard-co-produce-sanctified-on-rick-ross-mastermind- |archive-date=April 9, 2014 |url-status=dead}}</ref> The song was also produced by [[Mike Dean (record producer)|Mike Dean]], along with being written by [[Rick Ross|William Roberts]], [[Big Sean|Sean Anderson]], Kanye West, and [[DJ Mustard|Dijon McFarlane]]. Betty Wright lent her vocal talent to the song during a late night studio session she did as a favor to DJ Khaled in order for him to meet a deadline. She recorded the hook in fifteen minutes while sitting on a sofa<ref>{{Cite news|url=https://www.newyorker.com/culture/culture-desk/the-woman-who-put-the-soul-in-sanctified|title=The Woman Who Put the Soul in "Sanctified"|last=Neyfakh|first=Leon|date=April 16, 2014|newspaper=The New Yorker|issn=0028-792X|access-date=April 23, 2016}}</ref> In the song, West raps about how he feels about people playing up his earlier works and dismiss who he is now, before saying that he's not going to worry about it. Then later he says, "Wipe my forehead with a handkerchief and wash my sins in the blood of Jesus, People sayin', 'Ye, we need another ''[[Yeezus]]''." This being a reference to his experimental sixth studio album.<ref name="rollingstone1"/> 

Rick Ross stated that the song was one of his favorites featured on ''Mastermind''. He said, "'Sanctified' was most definitely one of the favorites. It's because how much time—Just watching Kanye put into this because it actually started as a sample. We did the sample maybe 10 different ways and then we ultimately went with a beautiful lady out of Miami, Ms. [[Betty Wright]]. She blessed us. So, just hearing that chorus. Just hearing the way it came out. All the time we put in it. It most definitely was dope."<ref name="hiphopdx1">{{cite magazine|last=Harling |first=Danielle |url=http://www.hiphopdx.com/index/news/id.27773/title.rick-ross-says-the-notorious-b-i-g-sample-on-nobody-was-approved-by-d-roc |title=Rick Ross Says The Notorious B.I.G. Sample On "Nobody" Was Approved By D-Roc |magazine=HipHopDX |publisher=Cheri Media Group |date=March 5, 2014 |access-date=March 12, 2014 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141004093354/http://www.hiphopdx.com/index/news/id.27773/title.rick-ross-says-the-notorious-b-i-g-sample-on-nobody-was-approved-by-d-roc |archive-date=October 4, 2014 |url-status=dead}}</ref> Then after Ross' sixth studio album ''[[Mastermind (Rick Ross album)|Mastermind]]'' was released for free streaming on [[iTunes]], the full version of "Sanctified" was released.<ref name="rollingstone1">{{cite magazine|last=Grow |first=Kory |url=https://www.rollingstone.com/music/news/stream-rick-ross-mastermind-and-kanyes-sanctified-guest-spot-20140226 |title=Stream Rick Ross's 'Mastermind' and Kanye West's Guest Spot on 'Sanctified' &#124; Music News |magazine=[[Rolling Stone]] |publisher=[[Jann Wenner|Wenner Media]] |date=February 26, 2014 |access-date=March 12, 2014}}</ref>

On March 6, 2014, Big Sean released his verse for the song that was cut from the album version of the song.<ref name="autogenerated1">{{cite web|last=Alexis |first=Nadeska |url=http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1723602/big-sean-sanctified-remix.jhtml |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140306215444/http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1723602/big-sean-sanctified-remix.jhtml |url-status=dead |archive-date=March 6, 2014 |title=Big Sean Dubs Himself The 'Black Brad Pitt' On 'Sanctified' Remix: Listen Now |website=[[MTV]] |publisher=[[Viacom (2005–present)|Viacom]] |date=March 6, 2014 |access-date=March 12, 2014}}</ref> Detailing the song's recording process to [[MTV]], Big Sean said, "Me and [Kanye West] was in the studio working on a whole bunch of ideas and [Ross] came in the conversation. And everybody know Rick Ross albums is events. So Ye pulled a track up, and I was telling him I think it's a banger, it's a smash, and he thought we should give it to Rozay. Me and him wrote our verses on the spot and we sent it to [Ross] and he did what he did to it."<ref name="autogenerated1"/>

== Critical reception ==
"Sanctified" was met with critical acclaim from [[music critic]]s, with most of them favoring West's guest appearance over Ross himself. Mikael Wood of ''[[The Los Angeles Times]]'' called it the album's best song.<ref name="latimes1"/> Kevin Ritchie of ''[[Now (newspaper)|Now]]'' named it the album's best song, praised Betty Wright's performance singing the hook and Kanye West's production.<ref>{{cite news|last=Ritchie |first=Kevin |url=http://www.nowtoronto.com/music/story.cfm?content=196932 |title=Rick Ross |newspaper=[[Now (newspaper)|Now]] |publisher=[[Now Communications]] |date=March 6, 2014 |access-date=March 12, 2014}}</ref> Nick Catucci of ''[[Entertainment Weekly]]'' and Michael Madden of ''[[Consequence of Sound]]'' both considered the song one of the album's best.<ref>{{cite magazine|last=Catucci |first=Nick |url=https://ew.com/article/2014/05/12/mastermind/ |title=Mastermind Review |magazine=[[Entertainment Weekly]] |publisher=[[Time Inc.]] |date=February 28, 2014 |access-date=March 12, 2014}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|last=Madden |first=Michael |url=https://consequence.net/2014/03/album-review-rick-ross-mastermind/ |title=Album Review: Rick Ross – Mastermind |website=[[Consequence of Sound]] |date=March 7, 2014 |access-date=March 12, 2014}}</ref> Kellin Miller of [[AllHipHop]] stated that it "has a strong case for an early song of the year nomination."<ref>{{cite web|first=Kellin |last=Miller |url=https://allhiphop.com/2014/02/28/ahh-album-review-rick-ross-mastermind/ |title=AHH Album Review: Rick Ross' Mastermind |website=[[AllHipHop]] |publisher=AHH Holdings LLC |date=February 28, 2014 |access-date=March 12, 2014}}</ref> Dan Rys of ''[[XXL (magazine)|XXL]]'' praised the song's production and called West's appearance as show stealing.<ref>{{cite magazine|last=Rys |first=Dan |url=https://www.xxlmag.com/rap-music/reviews/2014/03/rick-ross-mastermind-album-review/ |title=Rick Ross Delivers A Winning Formula On 'Mastermind' |magazine=[[XXL (magazine)|XXL]] |publisher=[[Townsquare Media]] |date=March 4, 2014 |access-date=March 12, 2014}}</ref> Jesse Cataldo of [[Slant Magazine]] said, "The album revives when Kanye West shows up to produce and guest on "Sanctified," a revival song that pairs religious rebirth with monetary excess in a sort of frenzied ritual."<ref>{{cite magazine|last=Cataldo |first=Jesse |url=http://www.slantmagazine.com/music/review/rick-ross-mastermind |title=Rick Ross: Mastermind &#124; Music Review |magazine=[[Slant Magazine]] |date=March 3, 2014 |access-date=March 12, 2014}}</ref>

Peter Cashmore of ''[[NME]]'' stated that it "is a glorious snail’s-pace gush of braggadocio. In fact, if the album had wrapped up after "Sanctified", rather than dribbling to a close with the weedy "Walkin’ On Air" and "Thug Cry", then ''Mastermind'' would be pretty much wall-to-wall bangers."<ref>{{cite web|last=Cashmore |first=Peter |url=https://www.nme.com/reviews/various-artists/15122 |title=Rick Ross - 'Mastermind' |website=[[NME]]|publisher=[[IPC Media]] |date=March 3, 2014 |access-date=March 12, 2014}}</ref> Craig Jenkins of ''[[Pitchfork (website)|Pitchfork]]'' gave this song the tag of "Best New Track" and stated, "'Sanctified' is an outstanding entry into Ross and Kanye’s catalogue of stunners, but it never feels like Ross' song."<ref>{{cite web|last=Jenkins |first=Craig |url=https://pitchfork.com/reviews/albums/19097-rick-ross-mastermind/ |title=Rick Ross: Mastermind |website=Pitchfork |date=March 6, 2014 |access-date=March 13, 2014}}</ref> Christopher Weingarten of ''[[Rolling Stone]]'' said, the song "shines with guest singer Betty Wright's powerful gospel rasp and Kanye West confessing his sins on a featured verse. Ross, meanwhile, ends up talking designer clothes and fellatio."<ref>{{cite magazine|last=Weingarten |first=Christopher R. |url=https://www.rollingstone.com/music/albumreviews/mastermind-20140305 |title=Rick Ross 'Mastermind' Album Review |magazine=Rolling Stone |publisher=Wenner Media |date=March 5, 2014 |access-date=March 13, 2014}}</ref> Ken Capobianco of the ''[[Boston Globe]]'' said that "Kanye West turns his host into an afterthought on the fine, gospel-influenced "Sanctified".<ref>{{cite news|last=Capobianco|first=Ken |url=https://www.bostonglobe.com/arts/music/2014/03/04/album-review-rick-ross-mastermind/xcYXHEwdF3jvxAqv5RXxML/story.html |title=ALBUM REVIEW: Rick Ross, 'Mastermind' |newspaper=[[Boston Globe]]|publisher=[[John W. Henry]] |date=March 4, 2014 |access-date=March 13, 2014}}</ref>

Sheldon Pearce of ''[[HipHopDX]]'' stated, "While Kanye is transposing himself with [[Muhammad Ali]] and washing his sins in the blood of Jesus and boldly second guessing God’s direct message the most grandiose thing Ross can muster is an ill-fated (and perhaps uninformed) comparison to fallen Waco cult leader [[David Koresh]]. It feels tired and uninspired."<ref>{{cite magazine |last=Pearce |first=Sheldon |url=http://www.hiphopdx.com/index/album-reviews/id.2235/title.rick-ross-mastermind |title=Rick Ross - Mastermind |magazine=HipHopDX |publisher=Cheri Media Group |date=March 4, 2014 |access-date=March 13, 2014 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140307135737/http://www.hiphopdx.com/index/album-reviews/id.2235/title.rick-ross-mastermind |archive-date=March 7, 2014 |url-status=dead }}</ref> Nathan Slavik of DJBooth praised the song's depth and said it featured "a rewind-worthy verse from Kanye and a completely superfluous Big Sean."<ref>{{cite web|last=Slavik |first=Nathan |url=http://www.djbooth.net/index/albums/review/rick-ross-mastermind#review |title=Rick Ross - Mastermind |website=DJBooth |publisher=The DJBooth LLC |date=March 3, 2014 |access-date=March 13, 2014 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161104014259/http://djbooth.net/index/albums/review/rick-ross-mastermind |archive-date=November 4, 2016 |url-status=dead }}</ref> Jesal Padania of ''RapReviews'' called the song riotously enjoyable, but said that Ross' contribution did not add anything to the song.<ref>{{cite web|last=Padania |first=Jesal |url=https://www.rapreviews.com/archive/2014_03F_mastermind.html |title=Feature for March 4, 2014 - Rick Ross' "Mastermind" |website=RapReviews |date=March 4, 2014 |access-date=March 13, 2014}}</ref>

''[[Complex (magazine)|Complex]]'' named it the second best song of the first half of 2014. Writer Justin Charity commented saying, "While hardly their strongest verses of even the past couple years, Ross, Kanye, and Sean all come correct with the quotables here, from Sean's opening mantra to Kanye's aggressive confessional to post-sex grilled cheese. The song's weary gospel build-up makes way for the minimal, rarefied ecstasy of that spaceship hangover melody. An arena confession I need to be shouting at the livest of live shows by year's end: "All I wanted was a hundred million dollars and a bad bitch!" Verdict: We need another ''[[Yeezus]]''."<ref>{{cite magazine|last=Charity |first=Justin |url=https://www.complex.com/music/2014/06/best-songs-of-2014-so-far/rick-ross-sanctified |title=2. Rick Ross f/ Kanye West & Big Sean "Sanctified" — The Best Songs of 2014 (So Far) |magazine=[[Complex (magazine)|Complex]] |publisher=[[Complex Media]] |date=June 30, 2014 |access-date=July 17, 2014}}</ref> [[MySpace]] also named the song one of the 50 best songs of the same time period. A writer for them said, "'Sanctified' is a behemoth of a track, replete with an original vocal line from Bettye LaVette that plays like a gospel sample and an impeccable verse from 'Ye that makes up for Ross rapping, "Fellatio's amazin', make grilled cheese for you, the best." It's simply the best gospel song masquerading as a rap jam this year."<ref>{{cite web|url=https://myspace.com/discover/editorial/2014/06/16/50-best-songs-of-2014-so-far/|title=50 Best Songs of 2014 So Far|website=[[Myspace]]|date=June 17, 2014|access-date=June 18, 2014 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140618112050/https://myspace.com/discover/editorial/2014/06/16/50-best-songs-of-2014-so-far/ |archive-date=June 18, 2014 |url-status=dead}}</ref>
