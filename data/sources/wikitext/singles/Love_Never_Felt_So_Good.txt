"'''Love Never Felt So Good'''" is a song by American singer and songwriter [[Michael Jackson]], released posthumously on May 2, 2014. The song, reworked from a 1980 demo track originally composed by Jackson and Canadian singer-songwriter [[Paul Anka]], was the first single released from Jackson's second posthumous album, ''[[Xscape (album)|Xscape]]''.

Two versions of the single were developed. The first was a solo version produced by American record producer John McClain and Dutch record producer [[Giorgio Tuinfort]]. The second version was a duet featuring fellow American singer [[Justin Timberlake]], produced by American record producers [[Timbaland]] and [[Jerome "J-Roc" Harmon|J-Roc]], which received positive reviews from [[Music journalism|music critics]]. Its accompanying music video premiered on May 14, 2014, on ''[[The Ellen DeGeneres Show]]''. In the video, Timberlake appears with a crowd of young dancers, who reference Jackson's most known dance moves, interspersed with archival footage of the pop singer's many short films. The song is the second collaboration between Jackson and Anka to be released since Jackson's death in 2009—the first being "[[This Is It (Michael Jackson song)|This Is It]]".

The duet version of "Love Never Felt So Good" charted within the top ten in eighteen international charts, reaching the top spot in five countries. Michael Jackson became the first artist in history to have a top 10 single on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] in five different decades when "Love Never Felt So Good" reached number 9 on May 31, 2014 (six decades if his [[Jackson 5]] tenure is included). It spent eight weeks on top of the US Adult R&B Songs. The song also charted at number 8 on the [[UK Singles Chart]].

==Background==
The original demo was conceived and recorded during a two-week collaborative stint between Jackson and Anka in 1980, around the same time as "[[This Is It (Michael Jackson song)|This Is It]]" (originally titled "I Never Heard") and "[[Don't Matter to Me]]".<ref>{{Cite book |last=Shields |first=Damien |url=https://books.google.com/books?id=OdpKuQEACAAJ |title=Michael Jackson: Songs and Stories from the Vault |date=June 22, 2018 |publisher=Modegy LLC |isbn=978-0-9861991-7-2 |language=en}}</ref> The original version only featured Jackson's vocals, finger snaps and a piano, played by Anka. The song was later recorded by [[Johnny Mathis]], with revised lyrics by Anka and [[Kathleen Wakefield]]. The Mathis version was included on his 1984 album, ''[[A Special Part of Me]]''. In 2006, Jackson's demo leaked online.

In 2010, the co-executor of the [[estate of Michael Jackson]], John McClain, and Dutch composer, Giorgio Tuinfort, remixed the track to be considered for ''[[Michael (Michael Jackson album)|Michael]]'', the first posthumous album of Jackson's unreleased material. The track was ultimately not selected for the ''Michael'' album.

In 2014, after being left on the cutting room floor for four years, the McClain/Tuinfort version of the track was selected as the first track on the ''Xscape'' album. Yet another version of the track—co-produced by Timbaland with J-Roc and featuring vocals from Justin Timberlake—was included as a bonus track on the deluxe version of ''Xscape''. The version featuring Timberlake also includes sampled percussion and breaths from Jackson's 1979 song "[[Working Day and Night]]".

==Critical reception==
Upon its release, "Love Never Felt So Good" received positive reviews from critics. Surprised by the quality of the song, Aisha Harris from ''Slate'' magazine wrote that Timberlake's version "sounds like an outtake from ''[[Off the Wall (album)|Off the Wall]]''" and further commented: "If producers are going to put out more MJ music, let's hope it continues to sound this good."<ref name="Slate">{{cite web |date=May 2, 2014 |title=The New Michael Jackson Song With Justin Timberlake is Actually Good |url=http://www.slate.com/blogs/browbeat/2014/05/02/new_michael_jackson_song_with_justin_timberlake_listen_to_love_never_felt.html |access-date=May 2, 2014 |work=Slate}}</ref> Chris Richards of ''[[The Washington Post]]'' called the solo Jackson rendition "terrific. It's airy, sweet, funky and pretty much impossible to dislike," but panned the Timberlake remix, which he commented "surfaces all the grody feelings that came rushing in 2010 with Jackson's first posthumous album, ''[[Michael (Michael Jackson album)|Michael]]''."<ref>{{cite news |date=May 2, 2014 |title=Michael Jackson, "Love Never Felt So Good" - it's trending, but is it good? |url=https://www.washingtonpost.com/blogs/style-blog/wp/2014/05/02/michael-jackson-love-never-felt-so-good-its-trending-but-is-it-good/ |access-date=May 2, 2014 |newspaper=The Washington Post}}</ref> 

Gavin Edwards from ''[[Rolling Stone]]'' said, "even if it isn't on a par with Jackson's best work, it sounded like it could have easily been the sixth single off ''[[Bad (album)|Bad]]'', which is more impressive than it sounds."<ref>{{cite magazine |last=Edwards |first=Gavin |date=May 2, 2014 |title=IHeartRadio Music Awards 2014's 10 Best and Worst Moments |url=https://www.rollingstone.com/music/news/iheartradio-music-awards-2014s-10-best-and-worst-moments-20140502 |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140503113651/https://www.rollingstone.com/music/news/iheartradio-music-awards-2014s-10-best-and-worst-moments-20140502 |archive-date=May 3, 2014 |access-date=May 4, 2014 |magazine=[[Rolling Stone]]}}</ref>
Stephanie Chase from ''Contactmusic'' said, "It's not groundbreaking or an exceptional example of his artistry, but it gives a good indication that ''[[Xscape (album)|Xscape]]'' could serve as a fitting tribute to Jackson and his legacy.<ref>{{cite web |last=Chase |first=Stephanie |date=2014-05-02 |title=Michael Jackson - Love Never Felt So Good Single Review |url=http://www.contactmusic.com/single-review/michael-jackson-love-never-felt-so-good |work=Contactmusic.com}}</ref> Jon O'Brien from [[Yahoo!]] commented: "the classy disco-soul of this single could easily have been lifted from ''Off the Wall'' or ''[[Thriller (Michael Jackson album)|Thriller]]''."<ref>{{cite web |last=OBrien |first=Jon |date=May 5, 2014 |title=Weekly Singles Review: Michael Jackson ft. Justin Timberlake, Foxes & Pitbull |url=https://uk.celebrity.yahoo.com/news/weekly-singles-review-michael-jackson-ft-justin-timberlake-105400349.html |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140505182949/https://uk.celebrity.yahoo.com/news/weekly-singles-review-michael-jackson-ft-justin-timberlake-105400349.html |archive-date=May 5, 2014 |access-date=May 5, 2014 |publisher=Yahoo}}</ref>

"Love Never Felt So Good" also got praised by musician [[DJ Cassidy]], who told the song had a positive impact on him.<ref>{{cite web |title=Michael Jackson – Love Never Felt So Good Lyrics - Genius Lyrics |url=https://genius.com/3233122 |work=Genius}}</ref>

<blockquote>I haven't been a huge fan of albums that come out after an artist dies. You know, it just doesn't have their stamp on it, so it's never going to be what it would have been if the person was alive. But "Love Never Felt So Good" was a great record. It was definitely one of my top five songs from the last five years.</blockquote>

==Commercial performance==

The single debuted on the [[UK Singles Chart]] at No. 27 in the issue dated May 4, 2014, after just 24 hours on sale.<ref name="bbc_debut">{{cite news |date=May 4, 2014 |title=Calvin Harris scores sixth UK number one |url=https://www.bbc.com/news/entertainment_and_arts/ |access-date=May 5, 2014 |publisher=BBC |location=London, England}}</ref><ref name="ukchartreview">{{cite web |last=Lane |first=Daniel |date=May 4, 2014 |title=Calvin Harris scores his sixth UK Number 1 single with Summer |url=http://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/calvin-harris-scores-his-sixth-uk-number-1-single-with-summer-2980/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140509143325/http://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/calvin-harris-scores-his-sixth-uk-number-1-single-with-summer-2980/ |archive-date=May 9, 2014 |access-date=May 5, 2014 |publisher=Official Charts Company}}</ref> It reached number one in South Korea, Denmark, Israel, South Africa, and Germany and Belgium's respective urban charts.

In the United States, the single sold more than 90,000 downloads from the debut date of May 2, 2014, to May 4, 2014, and drew 52 million all-format radio audience impressions since its first plays that Friday. With the [[Clear Channel Communications|Clear Channel]]'s promotional synergy after the premiere of the song on the [[iHeartRadio Music Awards]], the song received hourly plays on May 2, 2014, on the company's pop, adult and R&B/hip-hop stations, greatly contributing to its airplay,<ref name="bbdebut">{{cite magazine |last1=Trust |first1=Gary |last2=Caulfield |first2=Keith |date=May 5, 2014 |title=Michael Jackson, Justin Timberlake's Duet Off To More Than 'Good' Start |url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/6077508/michael-jackson-justin-timberlake-hot-100-love-never-felt-so-good |access-date=May 6, 2014 |magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]}}</ref> which made the song debut on the following ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' radio charts: Adult R&B Songs (No. 1), Adult Contemporary (No. 2), R&B/Hip-Hop Airplay (No. 4) and Adult Pop Songs (No. 21).<ref>{{cite magazine |last=Trust |first=Gary |date=May 5, 2014 |title=Chart Highlights: Beyonce Scores 20th Dance Club Songs No. 1 With 'Partition' |url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/chart-highlights-beyonce-scores-20th-dance-club-songs-no-1-with/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140508084346/http://www.billboard.com/articles/columns/chart-beat/6077518/chart-highlights-beyonce-scores-20th-dance-club-songs-no-1-with |archive-date=May 8, 2014 |access-date=May 6, 2014 |magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]}}</ref> The song made splashy debuts at No. 20 on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]]<ref>{{cite magazine |date=May 21, 2014 |title=Michael Jackson, Coldplay Hit Hot 100 Top 10; Counting Jackson's entire chart career in addition to his solo career, the 2010s is actually his sixth decade with a single charted (not peaking) in the US Top 10, as [[The Jackson 5]] single "[[I Want You Back]]" first reached the Top 10 the week of December 27, 1969, on its way to a number one peak in late January 1970; however, Jackson was not credited on that single as an individual artist.<ref>{{cite magazine |date=September 12, 2008 |title=The Hot 100: Dec. 27, 1969 |url=http://www.billboard.com/charts/1969-12-27/hot-100 |access-date=May 21, 2014 |magazine=Billboard}}</ref>

In 2014, "Love Never Felt So Good" was ranked as the 78th most popular song of the year (2014) on the ''Billboard'' Hot 100.<ref>{{cite magazine |date=January 2, 2013 |title=2014 Year End Charts - Hot 100 Songs |url=http://www.billboard.com/charts/year-end/2014/hot-100-songs |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160201073006/http://www.billboard.com/charts/year-end/2014/hot-100-songs |archive-date=February 1, 2016 |access-date=December 9, 2014 |magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]}}</ref>
