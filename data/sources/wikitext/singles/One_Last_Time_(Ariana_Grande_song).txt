"'''One Last Time'''" is a song by American singer-songwriter [[Ariana Grande]]. It was released digitally by [[Republic Records]] on August 22, 2014, as the second [[promotional single]] from her second studio album, ''[[My Everything (Ariana Grande album)|My Everything]]'' (2014). The song was sent to [[contemporary hit radio]] as the fifth and final single from the album on February 10, 2015. It was written by [[David Guetta]], [[Savan Kotecha]], [[Giorgio Tuinfort]], [[Rami Yacoub]], and [[Carl Falk]]. The song was produced by the latter two, with Tuinfort serving as a co-producer and [[Ilya Salmanzadeh|Ilya]] serving both as a co-producer and vocal producer.

"One Last Time" is a [[dance-pop]] and [[Electronic dance music|EDM]] song. Its instrumentation consists of sampled [[drums]] and a [[synth]] line. The single reached the top-twenty in twelve countries, including Australia, Canada, France, the United Kingdom and the United States, where it became Grande's sixth top-twenty single on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]]. It was later certified sextuple platinum in Australia, and quadruple platinum in Canada, the United Kingdom, and the United States.

The song's accompanying music video was released on February 15, 2015, and was directed by [[Max Landis]]. It is visually presented as a [[Found footage (pseudo-documentary)|found footage]], similar to Landis's previous work. The video faced controversy due to its similarity to the music video for the Australian band [[Safia (band)|SAFIA]]'s single "[[You Are the One (Safia song)|You Are the One]]", with Grande and Landis being accused of [[intellectual property theft]]. Grande promoted the song with a live performance on ''[[The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon]]'', the [[2015 NBA All-Star Game|2015 NBA All-Star Game halftime show]], and during her first world tour, [[The Honeymoon Tour]] (2015).

A French version of the song featuring French singer [[Kendji Girac]], alternatively titled "Attends-moi", was released on February 16, 2015, in France, Belgium and Switzerland. An Italian version of the song featuring Italian rapper [[Fedez]] was released on May 26, 2015, only in Italy. Grande re-released "One Last Time" as a [[charity single]] in June 2017, following the [[Manchester Arena bombing]] at her concert the previous month. It resulted in the song reaching a new peak of number 2 on the [[UK Singles Chart]] following the [[One Love Manchester]] concert, as well as reaching a new peak of number 15 in the [[Irish Singles Chart]] and peaking at number one in Scotland. Grande donated all proceeds from the re-release to the We Love Manchester Emergency Fund to aid the victims of the bombing and their families. Overall, the song reached a top-ten peak in Belgium, the Czech Republic, France, Italy, Lebanon, the Netherlands, Slovakia, the United Kingdom and the top-twenty of Australia, Canada, Denmark, Ireland, Spain, and the United States.

== Background and release ==
[[File:David Guetta 6, 2012.jpg|upright|thumb|left|French DJ [[David Guetta]] (pictured in 2012) co-wrote "One Last Time".]]
"One Last Time" was written by the songwriting team [[Savan Kotecha]], [[Rami Yacoub]], and [[Carl Falk]], with Dutch producer [[Giorgio Tuinfort]] and French [[disc jockey]], [[David Guetta]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.apra.com.au/cms/worksearch/worksearch.srvlt?action=workSearch#|title=Works Search: One Last Time by Ariana Grande|publisher=[[Australasian Performing Right Association]]|access-date=July 17, 2016|archive-date=November 17, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151117020448/https://www.apra.com.au/cms/worksearch/worksearch.srvlt?action=workSearch|url-status=live}}</ref> Kotecha had previously collaborated with Grande when producing the singles from ''My Everything'', "[[Problem (Ariana Grande song)|Problem]]", "[[Break Free (song)|Break Free]]", "[[Bang Bang (Jessie J, Ariana Grande and Nicki Minaj song)|Bang Bang]]", and "[[Love Me Harder]]". Guetta was working on his album ''[[Listen (David Guetta album)|Listen]]'', and while making the album he chose to give "One Last Time" to Grande, since "[he] had like 120 songs and [was] trying to make an album that ma[de] sense because some of the songs can be great, but that doesn't mean that they would fit together. ['One Last Time' is] one example", he claimed.<ref name="idolatordg">{{cite news|last1=Wass|first1=Mike|title=David Guetta Talks New LP ''Listen'', Smash Hit "Hey Mama" & The Future Of Dance Music: Idolator Interview|url=http://www.idolator.com/7593658/david-guetta-interview-listen-hey-mama-future-dance-music|access-date=May 23, 2015|work=[[Idolator (website)|Idolator]]|date=May 20, 2015}}{{dead link|date=August 2025|bot=medic}}{{cbignore|bot=medic}}</ref> "One Last Time" was strongly considered to be ''[[My Everything (Ariana Grande album)|My Everything]]''{{'}}s lead-single, according to [[Republic Records]]'s executive [[vice president]], [[Charlie Walk]]; however, "Problem" was chosen instead, being the song that best "set the tone for the project". Later, Walk claimed that {{"'}}Break Free' felt like summer", while "Bang Bang" helped usher in English singer [[Jessie J]]'s album, ''[[Sweet Talker (Jessie J album)|Sweet Talker]]'', and "fall/winter felt like the best time for 'Love Me Harder', since it's a heavier, darker record", he added.<ref name="billboardvp">{{cite magazine|last1=Trust|first1=Gary|title=Five Hits, One Album: The Strategy Behind Ariana Grande's Singles From 'My Everything'|url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/five-hits-one-album-the-strategy-behind-ariana-grandes-singles/|access-date=May 24, 2015|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|date=April 28, 2015|archive-date=June 1, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150601041748/http://www.billboard.com/articles/columns/chart-beat/6546185/five-hits-one-album-the-strategy-behind-ariana-grandes-singles|url-status=live}}</ref>

"One Last Time" was produced by Falk and Yacoub, co-produced by Tuinfort and [[Ilya Salmanzadeh|Ilya]], with vocal production being done by Ilya, Kotecha and Yacoub. "One Last Time" is a [[dance-pop]] and [[Electronic dance music|EDM]] song,<ref name="officialchartscompany"/><ref name="popmatters"/><ref>{{Cite web |last=Garvey |first=Meaghan |title=Ariana Grande: 'No Tears Left to Cry' |url=https://pitchfork.com/reviews/tracks/ariana-grande-no-tears-left-to-cry/ |access-date=2025-07-10 |website=[[Pitchfork (website)|Pitchfork]] |language=en-US |archive-date=December 3, 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201203104331/https://pitchfork.com/reviews/tracks/ariana-grande-no-tears-left-to-cry/ |url-status=live }}</ref> with a total length of three minutes and seventeen seconds (3:17).<ref name="IT"/> Its instrumentations consist in "pummeling drums and a three-note synth line".<ref name="BillboardReview"/> "One Last Time" is written in the key of [[A-flat major]], with a moderately fast tempo of 125 [[beats per minute]]. Grande's vocals range from [[A-flat (musical note)|A{{music|flat}}<sub>3</sub>]] to [[F (musical note)|F<sub>5</sub>]].<ref name="musicnotes">{{cite web|title=One Last Time by Ariana Grande - Digital Sheet Music|url=https://www.musicnotes.com/sheetmusic/mtdFPE.asp?ppn=MN0147039|publisher=Musicnotes| date=February 19, 2015 |access-date=May 24, 2015}}</ref> Lyrically, the song describes a female protagonist who asks her former lover to spend one last time with her, even though they have already moved on from her and is with someone new.<ref name="fdrmx" /> As noted by Jason Lipshut of ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'', "the downbeat admission of 'I know/that you got everything/But I got nothing here without you' is the pained sound of a narrator racked with guilt, the chorus sets aside that humiliation and scoops up a sense of hope,"<ref name="BillboardReview" /> with the singer pleading, "One last time/I need to be the one who takes you home/One more time/I promise after that, I'll let you go".<ref name="fdrmx">{{cite news|last1=Feraren|first1=John Mychal|title=Ariana Grande: 'One Last Time' Single Review|url=http://fdrmx.com/ariana-grande-one-last-time-single-review/|access-date=May 24, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150210062418/http://fdrmx.com/ariana-grande-one-last-time-single-review/|publisher=FDRMX|date=January 18, 2015|archive-date=February 10, 2015}}</ref> Rob Copsey of [[The Official Charts Company]] noted that the song " reminded him of Swedish's recording artist, [[Loreen (singer)|Loreen]]'s 2012 [[Eurovision Song Contest|Eurovision]] winning song, "[[Euphoria (Loreen song)|Euphoria]]".<ref name="officialchartscompany" />

== Copyright infringement lawsuit ==
In August 2016, [[Alex Greggs]] filed a lawsuit against Grande, [[David Guetta]], [[Rami Yacoub]], [[Carl Falk]], [[Universal Music Group]] and [[Republic Records]] for alleged copyright infringement of the song "Takes All Night" by Skye Stevens, which Greggs wrote. The lawsuit alleged that the "harmonic background remains the same in both songs for the entire sixteen measures of the chorus. ... Although the rhythm of the two compositions may differ ... there is substantial similarity on the most important rhythmic placement of the pitches on strong melodic and harmonic beats (1 and 3)". Greggs sought $150,000 per infringement.<ref>Cavassuto, Maria. [https://variety.com/2016/music/news/ariana-grande-sued-over-one-last-time-song-1201843951 "Ariana Grande Sued for Copyright Infringement Over Song 'One Last Time'"], ''Variety'', August 24, 2016, accessed August 28, 2017</ref> The case was later dismissed after both sides filed a Joint Stipulation for Dismissal.<ref>{{Cite web |url=https://www.leagle.com/decision/infdco20170817790 |title=GREGGS v. === Synopsis ===
The music video displays news anchor reports about the Earth on course to pass through the tail of a comet named Eurydice, as well as displaying a worldwide panic. The rest of the video then is presented through the use of a handheld video camera. Grande is in a [[Fiat]] car as the passenger, with her boyfriend (played by Matt Bennett), who is recording. They are stuck in traffic as they try to leave the city, implied to be [[Los Angeles]], [[California]].<ref name="PPC">{{cite web|last1=Chau|first1=Thomas|title=Ariana Grande Braves the Apocalypse in 'One Last Time' Music Video|url=http://popcrush.com/ariana-grande-one-last-time-music-video/|website=[[PopCrush]]|date=February 16, 2015|access-date=July 19, 2016|archive-date=April 9, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160409104418/http://popcrush.com/ariana-grande-one-last-time-music-video/|url-status=live}}</ref> While everyone around them is panicking, Grande seems to be calm as she wants to get a closer look at the comet. Impatient, she leaves the car while Bennett tries to stop her, and then follows. They make their way through the crowds, but are stopped by the police who are barricading the street. Meanwhile, the comet erupts in the sky and begins to crash to the ground as the song starts. Grande manages to get through but Bennett is unable to, so he goes through an open ambulance and manages to catch up with her in an alleyway with stairs leading up into an apartment complex as Grande notices the comet in the sky. The two enter a home of a frightened family and try to leave through the other door, only for the comet to crash into the building, nearly killing both of them. Frightened, the two continue on and enter a room filled with television screens of the comet and an old man sitting at a desk. As the former two of them try to leave, the guy tries to stop them, but they knock him to the ground, and all the screens change to the same countdown timer. Grande and Bennett eventually make their way up the rooftop after subduing the old man again, who had caught up with them.<ref>{{cite web|author=Chris Coplan|title=Watch Ariana Grande's apocalyptic video for "One Last Time"|url=https://consequence.net/2015/02/watch-ariana-grandes-apocalyptic-video-for-one-last-time/|website=[[Consequence of Sound]]|date=February 17, 2015|access-date=July 24, 2016|archive-date=August 22, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160822043141/http://consequenceofsound.net/2015/02/watch-ariana-grandes-apocalyptic-video-for-one-last-time/|url-status=live}}</ref><ref>{{cite magazine|author=Helen Regan|title=Watch Ariana Grande's Apocalyptic New Video 'One Last Time'|url=https://time.com/3710821/ariana-grande-one-last-time-video/|magazine=[[Time (magazine)|Time]]|date=February 16, 2015|access-date=July 24, 2016|archive-date=August 12, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160812001750/http://time.com/3710821/ariana-grande-one-last-time-video/|url-status=live}}</ref> The camera is then set down, and shows the two of them embracing each other one last time until the comet crashes down.<ref>{{cite web|last1=Grande|first1=Ariana|title=Ariana Grande - One Last Time|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BPgEgaPk62M|website=[[YouTube]]|date=February 15, 2015|access-date=February 16, 2015|archive-date=February 16, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150216072828/https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BPgEgaPk62M&gl=US&hl=en|url-status=live}}</ref>

===Reception and plagiarism allegations===
Ariana Bacle of ''[[Entertainment Weekly]]'' called the music "very ''Chronicle''", comparing the video to Max Landis' previous work.<ref>{{cite web| url=http://www.ew.com/article/2015/02/16/ariana-grande-avoids-comets-one-last-time-video| title=Ariana Grande avoids comets in 'One Last Time' video| publisher=[[Entertainment Weekly]]| author=Ariana Bacle| date=February 16, 2015| access-date=February 16, 2015| archive-date=February 16, 2015| archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150216204738/http://www.ew.com/article/2015/02/16/ariana-grande-avoids-comets-one-last-time-video| url-status=live}}</ref> Rachel Paoletta of ''[[MTV.com|MTV News]]'' describes the music video as "a decidedly darker and more badass video" compared to her "sexy, sultry, and sandy video for '[[Love Me Harder]]{{'"}}. She also stated: "Ariana Grande's new video takes us to the end of the world, and brings us to the edge of our seats".<ref>{{cite web| url=http://www.mtv.com/news/2080938/ariana-grande-one-last-time-video/| title=Ariana Grande's 'One Last Time' Video Shows Us The Apocalypse Is Better When You Have A Hot Dude To Cuddle| publisher=[[MTV]]| first=Rae |last=Paoletta| date=February 16, 2015| access-date=February 16, 2015| archive-date=February 17, 2015| archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150217015245/http://www.mtv.com/news/2080938/ariana-grande-one-last-time-video/| url-status=dead}}</ref> In response to similarities between the Grande video and the music video accompanying [[Australia]]n band [[SAFIA]]'s single "[[You Are the One (Safia song)|You Are the One]]", Grande and Landis were accused of [[intellectual property theft]]. The controversy centered on similarities in the shared apocalyptic themes and stylistic format (a single, tracking shot) as well as parallels in the beginning of both videos (where a female protagonist exits a vehicle despite the protests of the male driver) and final shot (a lover's embrace as the world comes to an end around them). Members of the band described the similarities as an instance of when "big labels and/or big film firms steal ideas from small independent creatives who are trying really hard to make something different for a change". Despite acknowledging similarities between the two videos, Landis denied that his ideas are stolen from them and commented that both music videos had much in common with a third earlier music video by Perth group Injured Ninja.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.news.com.au/entertainment/music/ariana-grande-accused-of-ripping-off-aussie-band-safia-in-new-music-video/news-story/dea97a76471831da7e51095e4fb864f5|title=Ariana Grande accused of ripping off Aussie band SAFIA in new music video|publisher=[[News.com.au]]|first=Kathy |last=McCabe|date=February 17, 2015|access-date=February 17, 2015|archive-date=February 17, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150217062325/http://www.news.com.au/entertainment/music/ariana-grande-accused-of-ripping-off-aussie-band-safia-in-new-music-video/story-e6frfn09-1227222611139|url-status=live}}</ref>

During The Honeymoon Tour in Paris on May 15, 2015, Grande performed the French version of "One Last Time" with Kendji Girac for the first time.<ref name="pb">{{cite web|last=Piton|first=Quentin|url=http://www.purebreak.com/news/kendji-girac-s-invite-sur-scene-au-zenith-de-paris-pour-le-concert-d-ariana-grande/86122|title=Kendji Girac s'invite sur scène au Zénith de Paris pour le concert d'Ariana Grande|work=Purebreak|language=fr|date=May 16, 2015|access-date=May 16, 2015|archive-date=May 18, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150518085012/http://www.purebreak.com/news/kendji-girac-s-invite-sur-scene-au-zenith-de-paris-pour-le-concert-d-ariana-grande/86122|url-status=live}}</ref> On May 27, 2015, she sang the song on the third season finale of ''[[The Voice of Italy]]''.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.idolator.com/7594613/ariana-grande-one-last-time-the-voice-italy?andro=1&chrome=1|first=Mike|last=Wass|title=Ariana Grande Performs "One Last Time" on 'The Voice Italy': Watch|work=idolator|date=May 28, 2015|access-date=December 29, 2021}}{{dead link|date=August 2025|bot=medic}}{{cbignore|bot=medic}}</ref>
