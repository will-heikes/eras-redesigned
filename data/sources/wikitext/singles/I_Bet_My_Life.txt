"'''I Bet My Life'''" is a song by American [[pop rock]] band [[Imagine Dragons]]. The track, written by band members [[Ben McKee]], [[Daniel Platzman]], [[Dan Reynolds (musician)|Dan Reynolds]] and [[Daniel Wayne Sermon|Wayne Sermon]], was released as the lead [[single (music)|single]] from their second studio album ''[[Smoke + Mirrors]]'' (2015) on October 27, 2014. The band's network debut performing the track occurred at the [[American Music Awards]] (2014), where they received the award for [[American Music Award for Favorite Alternative Artist|Favorite Alternative Artist]].<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://time.com/3601633/imagine-dragons-amas-i-bet-my-life/|title=Imagine Dragons "I Bet My Life" American Music Awards|author=Daniel D’Addario|magazine=[[Time (magazine)|Time]]|date=November 23, 2014}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://perezhilton.com/2014-11-23-ama-american-music-awards-2014-imagine-dragons-performance#.VHN4WJPF8tI|title=Imagine Dragons Bet It All On A New Sound At The 2014 AMAs!|access-date=November 24, 2014|archive-date=December 27, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141227015655/http://perezhilton.com/2014-11-23-ama-american-music-awards-2014-imagine-dragons-performance#.VHN4WJPF8tI|url-status=dead}}</ref>

==Composition==

===Themes===
Lead singer [[Dan Reynolds (musician)|Dan Reynolds]] has stated that the song is about his relationship with his parents and explained that while "at times it's been strained and difficult... in the end, 'I Bet My Life' celebrates the bond that we still hold on to."<ref name="Spin">{{cite web |url=https://www.spin.com/2014/10/imagine-dragons-new-album-announcement-i-bet-my-life/ |title=Imagine Dragons Release Bombastic New Song From Upcoming Album |work=[[Spin (magazine)|Spin]] |date=October 27, 2014 |access-date=October 28, 2014 |author=Grebey, James}}</ref>

==Music video==
On December 12, 2014, Imagine Dragons released the official music video for "I Bet My Life".  The video was directed by [[Jonathan Desbiens|Jodeb]] and features actors [[Dane DeHaan]]<ref>{{cite web|url=http://radio.com/2014/12/12/imagine-dragons-i-bet-my-life-video/|title=Imagine Dragons' 'I Bet My Life' Video Sucks a Kid Into a Dam « Radio.com|access-date=December 12, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151208163804/http://radio.com/2014/12/12/imagine-dragons-i-bet-my-life-video/|archive-date=December 8, 2015|url-status=dead}}</ref> and [[Alex Neustaedter]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.imdb.com/name/nm2614237/|title=Alex Neustaedter « IMDb.com|website=[[IMDb]] }}</ref> Footage from the video featured in advertising by American automobile manufacturer Jeep at the American Music Awards, prior to the release of the video.<ref>{{cite web|last1=Pollack|first1=Judann|title=Chrysler Blitzes American Music Awards With Fergie, Imagine Dragons and More|date=November 23, 2014 |url=http://adage.com/article/cmo-strategy/chrysler-blitzes-amas-custom-commercials/295975/|publisher=AdvertisingAge|access-date=12 January 2015}}</ref>

===Synopsis===
The beginning and end of the video take place at the [[Salt River (Arizona)|Salt River]] in Arizona. A car is shown driving to the river. The cast are the members of Imagine Dragons, and their relatives and friends. Somewhere else at the river, a boy in a shirt (DeHaan) and a boy in overalls (Neustaedter) are seen fighting at the waterbed. While walking away, Neustaedter eventually pushes DeHaan into the river and it is implied that DeHaan swims away, while the former stares in shock. DeHaan is pulled towards a dam, in which he is sucked in. He lands in a house, looks out the window, and sees that everything is underwater. He finds a bed and goes to sleep. The next morning, DeHaan wakes up in a sailboat and takes control of it. He tries unsuccessfully to avoid a waterfall, but ends up flying over the land in the boat after he goes over the waterfall. While looking over the boat, he decides to flip it over. He falls into [[Phoenix, Arizona|a city]], where a crowd is shown carrying him. They toss and turn him, until he realizes it was a vision and he was knocked unconscious in the dam. He is pulled back up to the surface by Neustaedter, and survives. The last shot of the video zooms out from the point where DeHaan is picked up, and the video ends.

==Reception==
Carolyn Menyes of Music Times called the song "Another [Imagine Dragons] strong armed rolling anthem."<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.musictimes.com/articles/13551/20141027/imagine-dragons-i-bet-my-life-review-new-single-listen.htm |title=REVIEW: Imagine Dragons Release Fan-Pleasing New Single "I Bet My Life" from New Album 2015 [LISTEN&#93; : Reviews |publisher=Music Times |date=2014-10-27 |access-date=2017-05-05}}</ref> James Grebey of www.spin.com called it "Bombastic"<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.spin.com/2014/10/imagine-dragons-new-album-announcement-i-bet-my-life/ |title=Imagine Dragons Release Bombastic New Song From Upcoming Album |publisher=SPIN |date=2014-10-27 |access-date=2017-05-05}}</ref> and USA Today named it "Song of the week" during October 27-November 4.<ref>{{cite news| url=https://www.usatoday.com/story/life/music/2014/10/27/imagine-dragons-i-bet-my-life-listen-up-song-of-the-week/17998245/ | work=USA Today | first1=Brian | last1=Mansfield | title=Song of the Week: Imagine Dragons' 'I Bet My Life' | date=October 27, 2014}}</ref>
