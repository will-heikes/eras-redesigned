"'''Not a Bad Thing'''" is a song by American singer [[Justin Timberlake]] from his fourth studio album, ''[[The 20/20 Experience – 2 of 2]]'' (2013). It was released as the third single from the album on February 24, 2014, by [[RCA Records]]. It was written and produced by Timberlake, [[Timothy "Timbaland" Mosley]], and [[Jerome "J-Roc" Harmon]], with additional writing from [[James Fauntleroy]]. It is a [[mid-tempo]] [[Pop music|pop]] [[Sentimental ballad|ballad]] about love that uses an [[acoustic guitar]] throughout.

"Not a Bad Thing" received positive reviews from several [[music journalism|contemporary music critics]]. Its music video premiered on ''[[The Ellen DeGeneres Show]]'' on March 20, 2014. It is a mini-documentary that follows two documentary filmmakers searching for a couple who got engaged on January 12, 2014, while heading to New York City on the [[Long Island Rail Road]] train, with the man proposing to his girlfriend to "Not a Bad Thing". The video concludes with the filmmakers being led to several false leads, with a second part of the video to follow. Timberlake performed it on ''[[The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon]]'' and during [[The 20/20 Experience World Tour]]. It reached number 8 on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] and topped the [[Mainstream Top 40]].

== Release and promotion ==
RCA executives reportedly believed that "Not a Bad Thing" could be ''The 20/20 Experience – 2 of 2''{{'}}s largest commercial success. However, they instead opted to release "[[Take Back the Night (song)|Take Back the Night]]" and "[[TKO (song)|TKO]]" first, to represent the album's R&B sound and distinguish "Not a Bad Thing" from the sonically similar "[[Mirrors (Justin Timberlake song)|Mirrors]]".<ref name="Billboard-2014"/> A radio edit of it was [[Music download|digitally]] released on February 24, 2014, in Austria,<ref name="Amazon.com (DE)-2"/> France,<ref name="Amazon.com (FR)"/> Germany,<ref name="Amazon.com (DE)-2"/> Italy,<ref name="Amazon.com (IT)"/> Spain,<ref name="Amazon.com (ES)"/> Switzerland,<ref name="Amazon.com (DE)-2"/> the United Kingdom<ref name="Amazon.com (UK)"/> and the United States.<ref name="Amazon.com"/> The next day, it impacted the [[contemporary hit radio]] in the United States.<ref name="AllAccess-2"/> On March 31, it was sent to US [[Adult Contemporary (radio network)|adult contemporary radio]].<ref name="AllAccess"/> The song was released as a [[CD single]] in Austria, Germany and Switzerland; apart from the radio edit of it, it also featured the Black Friday remix version of "[[TKO (song)|TKO]]".<ref name="Amazon.com (DE)"/> The song impacted the mainstream radio in Italy on June 4.<ref name="Corradini, Paola"/>
