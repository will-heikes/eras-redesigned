"'''Good Kisser'''" is a song by American singer [[Usher (musician)|Usher]]. The single was released on May 5, 2014, by [[RCA Records]]. The accompanying music video debuted the same day. The song was written by [[Pop Wansel|Andrew "Pop" Wansel]], [[Bobby Bloom]], [[Jeff Barry]], [[Ronald Colson|Ronald "Flippa" Colson]], Jameel Roberts, Terry Sneed and [[Oak Felder|Warren "Oak" Felder]], with co-writing done by Usher, produced by Pop Wansel and Co-produced by Ronald "Flippa" Colson, Jproof, Terry "Tru" Sneed, Natural and Oak Felder. Originally intended to be the lead single from his eighth [[studio album]], ''[[Hard II Love]]'', the song was scrapped from the standard edition track listing, with the track only being included on the Japanese edition of the album.

In North America, the song reached number 65 on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]], number one on the [[Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Airplay]] chart, and number 14 on the [[Rhythmic Songs]] chart. It also charted in Australia, Flanders, France, Germany, the Netherlands and South Korea. "Good Kisser" was ranked at number two on ''Billboard''{{'}}s "10 Best R&B Songs on 2014" list.<ref>{{Cite news|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/events/year-in-music-2014/6398482/the-10-best-rb-songs-of-2014|title=The 10 Best R&B Songs of 2014|newspaper=Billboard|access-date=2016-10-12}}</ref> It was nominated for [[Best R&B Song]] and [[Best R&B Performance]] at the [[57th Annual Grammy Awards]].

==Background==
"Good Kisser" was supposed to be a track from his upcoming eighth studio album, ''Flawed'', which Usher announced was in the works on March 19, 2013.<ref name="next album1">{{cite web|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1703993/usher-new-album.jhtml|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130323015401/http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1703993/usher-new-album.jhtml|url-status=dead|archive-date=March 23, 2013|title=Usher's Next Album Is 'Everything You Can Imagine'|publisher=[[MTV News]]. [[Viacom (2005–present)|Viacom]]|access-date=May 5, 2014}}</ref> Talking to ''[[The Fader]]'', he described the album as "everything you can imagine", saying that it's "gonna be freaking out of here".<ref name="next album1"/> In an interview with Eric Bellinger by ''[[Rap-Up]]'', the former explained that he, along with [[Jermaine Dupri]], [[Bryan-Michael Cox]], and [[Brian Alexander Morgan]], were working on Usher's next album.<ref name="next album">{{cite web|url=http://www.rap-up.com/2013/04/09/rap-up-tv-eric-bellinger-talks-new-usher-and-keri-hilson-albums/|title=Rap-Up TV: Eric Bellinger Talks New Usher and Keri Hilson Albums|work=Rap-Up|publisher=SPIN Media Group (a division of [[Spin Media]])|access-date=May 5, 2014|archive-date=October 9, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141009082446/http://www.rap-up.com/2013/04/09/rap-up-tv-eric-bellinger-talks-new-usher-and-keri-hilson-albums/|url-status=dead}}</ref> Bellinger compared the album's music to Usher's ''[[Confessions (Usher album)|Confessions]]'' (2004), saying that it is "more R&B, more urban" than Usher's ''Looking 4 Myself''.<ref name="next album"/> The latter declared that his next album would show that he is "still Usher".<ref name="next album"/> The official remix features an additional verse from [[Rick Ross]].

==Production and composition==
"Good Kisser" was written by [[Pop Wansel|Andrew "Pop" Wansel]], [[Ronald Colson|Ronald "Flippa" Colson]], Jameel Roberts, Terry Sneed and [[Oak Felder|Warren "Oak" Felder]], with co-writing done by Usher. It was produced by Pop Wansel, with co-production handled by Ronald "Flippa" Colson, JProof, Terry "Tru" Sneed, Natural and Oak Felder.<ref name = "Directlyrics">{{cite web|date=May 5, 2014|url=http://www.directlyrics.com/new-music-usher-good-kisser-news.html|title=New Music: Usher – 'Good Kisser' (Full Audio)|publisher=Directlyrics|access-date=May 5, 2014}}</ref><ref name = "RollingStone">{{cite magazine|last=Reed|first=Ryan|date=May 5, 2014|url=https://www.rollingstone.com/music/news/usher-gets-funky-with-new-single-good-kisser-20140505#ixzz30sbuiBpG|title=Usher Gets Funky With New Single 'Good Kisser'|magazine=[[Rolling Stone]]|publisher=[[Jann Wenner|Wenner Media]] [[Limited liability company|LLC]]|access-date=May 5, 2014}}</ref> In an interview, Pop discussed how the song was conceived: {{blockquote|"...a lot of times when I wake up singing something random, it turns into a song. So I woke up one morning just singing 'Don't nobody kiss it like you baby,' and I was just singing it all day long. [...] Later that night, I went to the studio and just put it all down, then forgot about it.<ref name = "vibeinterview">{{cite web|last=Fresh|first=Mikey|date=May 8, 2014|url=http://www.vibe.com/article/interview-pop-wansel-writing-and-producing-ushers-good-kisser-single|title=Interview: Pop Wansel On Writing And Producing Usher's 'Good Kisser' Single|work=[[Vibe (magazine)|Vibe]]|publisher=[[Spin Media]]|access-date=May 9, 2014}}</ref>}}
Pop later went to a Los Angeles studio to show the song to Mark Pitts, who later gave it to Usher for him to perform. Usher made some lyrical changes to the song, including the change of the word "suck" to "kiss" in order to make the song less "X-rated".<ref name = "vibeinterview"/>

"Good Kisser" is an uptempo "stripped-back soul-funk" [[Contemporary R&B|R&B]] song that has a "sensuous, sturdy bassline", "salsa drums", a "dusty vinyl pops", '"[[Rich Harrison]]-recalling" "cowbell-heavy percussion", "jazzy keyboards", "handclaps" and Usher's "loose vocal delivery".<ref name = "RollingStone"/><ref name = "LAtimes">{{cite web|last=Wood|first=Mikael|date=May 5, Kisser'|work=[[The Los Angeles Times]]|publisher=[[Tribune Company]]|access-date=May 5, 2014}}</ref><ref name = "COS">{{cite web|last=Coplan|first=Chris|date=May 5, ==Release and reception==
In most countries, except for the United Kingdom, "Good Kisser" was issued as a single, by [[RCA Records]] on May 5, 2014. RCA issued the single in the UK on June 15.<ref name = "iTunesUK"/> It was Usher's first solo release since his song, "[[Scream (Usher song)|Scream]]" (2012).<ref name = "officialchartsarticle"/> Two teasers of the song and video had been released previously, one on April 29,<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JJ50gHyaD5c|title=Usher – Good Kisser|publisher=Teaser on [[YouTube]]|date=April 29, 2014|access-date=May 5, 2014}}</ref> and another one on May 2.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jU68si_a8Bw|title=Usher – Good Kisser (Teaser 2)|publisher=YouTube|date=May 2, 2014|access-date=May 5, 2014}}</ref> The track was sent to [[Urban adult contemporary]] radio stations in the United States on May 12,<ref name = "usurbanrelease">{{cite web|url=http://frequencynews.com/musiclibrary_detail.cfm?LabelID=133&ArtistID=104&AlbumID=0&SingleID=4066|title=Single: Good Kisser|work=Frequency News|access-date=May 8, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140508222831/http://frequencynews.com/musiclibrary_detail.cfm?LabelID=133&ArtistID=104&AlbumID=0&SingleID=4066|archive-date=May 8, 2014|url-status=dead}}</ref> where on that same day, he also made a performance debut of the song on ''[[The Voice (American TV series)|The Voice]]''.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.broadwayworld.com/bwwtv/article/Usher-to-Debut-New-Single-Good-Kisser-on-THE-VOICE-512-20140509#|title=Usher to Debut New Single 'Good Kisser' on THE VOICE, 5/12|publisher=Broadway World|date=May 9, 2014|access-date=May 9, 2014}}</ref>

On the week that was issued May 24, 2014, "Good Kisser" landed a hot shot debut on the US ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' [[Billboard Hot 100|Hot 100]] at number 70,<ref name = Billboardhot100debut/> and on the [[Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs]] chart at number 18.<ref name = Billboardrandbhiphopdebut>{{cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/biz/charts/2014-05-24/hot-r-and-b-hip-hop-songs|title=Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs: May 24, 2014|magazine=Billboard|access-date=May 15, 2014}}</ref> It also made a debut on the [[Rhythmic Songs]] chart at number 32.<ref name="radiodebut">{{cite news |url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/chart-highlights-trey-songz-tops-rhythmic-songs-with-na-na/ |title=Chart Highlights: Trey Songz Tops Rhythmic Songs With 'Na Na' |first=Gary |last=Trust |magazine=Billboard |date=May 12, 2014 |access-date=May 13, 2014}}</ref> The track was downloaded more than 29,000 times and got 13 million radio plays on the week ending May 11.<ref name = "Billboardinterview">{{cite magazine|last=Wete|first=Brad|url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/usher-sexy-next-album-single-good-kisser/|title=Usher Says He's "Keeping It Sexy' with Next Album and 'Good Kisser' Single|magazine=Billboard|publisher=Prometheus Global Media|date=May 20, 2014|access-date=May 24, 2014}}</ref> While dropping to number 73 on the Hot 100<ref>{{cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/biz/charts/2014-05-31/the-billboard-hot-100|title=The Billboard Hot 100: May 31, 2014|magazine=Billboard|access-date=May 27, 2014}}</ref> and number 20 on the Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs chart the next week,<ref>{{cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/biz/charts/2014-05-31/hot-r-and-b-hip-hop-songs|title=Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs: May 31, 2014|magazine=Billboard|access-date=May 27, 2014}}</ref> it had also made a new peak on the Rhythmic Songs chart of the twenty-fourth place.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/biz/charts/2014-05-31/rhythmic-40|title=Rhythmic: May 31, 2014|magazine=Billboard|access-date=May 27, 2014}}</ref> After a few weeks, the song reached number 65 on the Hot 100 issue dated June 28. In Europe, the song debuted at number 70 on the Belgian Flanders [[Ultratip]] chart, number 27 on the Flanders Urban chart,<ref name = "Flanders_Urban"/> and number 133 in France.<ref name = "France"/> It made its start at number 44 and number seven on the [[ARIA Charts|ARIA]] pop and urban charts respectively,<ref name = "Australia"/><ref name="ARIA Urban debut">{{cite web|url=http://www.ariacharts.com.au/chart/urban-singles |title=Australian Urban Singles Chart |publisher=ARIA Charts |access-date=May 18, 2014 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140518222928/http://www.ariacharts.com.au/chart/urban-singles |archive-date=May 18, 2014 }}</ref> both official record chart of the Oceania country of Australia. In Asia, it debuted at number 36 on the South Korean International chart, with sales of 329,520.<ref name = "southkorea"/>

Reviews for "Good Kisser" have been mixed. ''[[Rolling Stone]]'' writer Jon Dolan, giving it a 2.5 out of 5 star, called it a "half-mast funk-soul jam", say that it was "like watching a guy rev his engine for five minutes and never leave the driveway".<ref name = "Rolling Stone review">{{cite magazine|last=Dolan|first=Jon|date=May 15, 2014|url=https://www.rollingstone.com/music/songreviews/good-kisser-20140515|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140525061205/http://www.rollingstone.com/music/songreviews/good-kisser-20140515|url-status=dead|archive-date=May 25, 2014|title=Usher Good Kisser Song Review|magazine=Rolling Stone|publisher=Wenner Media|access-date=May 16, 2014}}</ref> In July 2014, ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' listed "Good Kisser" as one of the "10 Best Songs of 2014 (so far)" saying that the song "simmers without boiling but leaves listeners fully satisfied".<ref>{{cite magazine|title=10 Best Songs of 2014 (So Far): Critics' Picks|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/list/6150405/10-best-songs-of-2014-so-far-critics-picks|date=July 10, 2014|access-date=July 17, 2014}}</ref>

==Music video==
"Good Kisser"'s accompanying music video, directed by Christopher Sims, was released on May 5, the same day of the release of the single.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1lQtoRFaLsA|title=Usher – Good Kisser|publisher=Music Video on YouTube|date=May 5, 2014|access-date=May 16, 2014}}</ref> An official behind-the-scenes video came out on May 14.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rnrYIIuCOcQ|title=Usher – Good Kisser (Behind The Scenes)|publisher=YouTube|date=May 14, 2014|access-date=May 16, 2014}}</ref> The video shows Usher dancing and drumming shirtless, and features model [[List of America's Next Top Model contestants|Felicia Fo Porter]].<ref name = "officialchartsarticle">{{cite web|last=Liv|first=Moss|date=May 16, 2014|url=http://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/watch-ushers-video-for-new-single-good-kisser-2987/|title=Watch Usher's video for new single, Good Kisser|publisher=[[Official Charts Company]]|access-date=May 15, 2014}}</ref> ''[[USA Today]]''<nowiki>'</nowiki>s Patrick Ryan described the video as "[[Pharrell]]'s "[[Marilyn Monroe (Pharrell Williams song)|Marilyn Monroe]]" video, only with fewer [[Smokey the Bear]] hats".<ref>{{cite web|last=Ryan|first=Patrick|date=May 5, 2014|url=https://www.usatoday.com/story/life/music/2014/05/05/usher-releases-good-kisser-video/8720715/|title=Usher releases 'Good Kisser' video|work=[[USA Today]]|access-date=May 16, 2014}}</ref> As of May 2024 the video has over 65 million views.
