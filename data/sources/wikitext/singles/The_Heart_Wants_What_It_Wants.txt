"'''The Heart Wants What It Wants'''" is a song by American singer [[Selena Gomez]]. It was released on November 6, 2014, through [[Hollywood Records]], as the lead and only single from her compilation album ''[[For You (Selena Gomez album)|For You]]'' (2014). The song was written by Gomez, [[Antonina Armato]], [[David Jost]] and [[Tim James (record producer)|Tim James]].<ref name=Rockmafia>{{cite web|last1=Rockmafia|first1=Page|title=Selena Gomez – The Heart Wants What It Wants (MUSIC VIDEO)|url=http://www.rockmafia.com/news/231853/|website=rockmafia|date=November 7, 2014|archivedate=November 11, 2014|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20141111013226/http://rockmafia.com/news/231853/|url-status=dead|accessdate=June 2, 2018}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|last1=Mackinley|first1=Page|title=Selena Gomez's 'For You' Album Tracklist Leaks: 'Stars Dance' Gets Dubkiller Remix|url=http://www.inquisitr.com/1577001/selena-gomezs-for-you-album-tracklist-leaks-stars-dance-gets-dubkiller-remix/|website=Inquisitr|date=November 3, 2014}}</ref><ref>{{cite book|title="The Heart Wants What it Wants" – Selena Gomez – Sheet Music|date=2014|publisher=Downtown Music Publishing, Good Fellowship Publishing, Downtown DLJ Songs, Antonina Songs, Downtown DMP Songs, Mafia Della Roccia and Akashic Field Music|isbn=978-1-4950-0875-7|url=https://books.google.com/books?id=sEmyBwAAQBAJ&q=the+heart+wants+what+it+wants+sheet+music|accessdate=June 24, 2016}}</ref> Armato and James, who collectively form the production duo [[Rock Mafia]], also produced the song. A mid-tempo [[Pop music|pop]] and [[Contemporary R&B|R&B]] track with a [[Minimal music|minimal]] [[electropop]] beat, its accompanying music video was premiered on November 6, 2014, alongside the premiere of the song itself.<ref>{{cite web|last1=Boardman|first1=Madeline|title=Selena Gomez Cries in Emotional "The Heart Wants What It Wants" Video|url=https://www.usmagazine.com/entertainment/news/selena-gomez-cries-in-emotional-the-heart-wants-what-it-wants-clip-2014611|website=Us Weekly|date=November 6, 2014}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|last1=Byrne|first1=Suzy|title=Selena Gomez Debuts 'The Heart Wants What It Wants,' an Apparent Ode to Justin Bieber|url=https://music.yahoo.com/blogs/music-news/selena-gomez-debuts-the-heart-wants-what-it-wants-an-apparent-ode-to-justin-bieber-153144187.html|website=Yahoo Music|date=November 6, 2014}}</ref> It is Gomez's last single release under the label.<ref>{{cite news|last1=Wass|first1=Mike|title=Selena Gomez's "The Heart Wants What It Wants" Gets A Batch Of Official Dance Remixes: Listen To Them All|url=http://www.idolator.com/7572989/selena-gomez-the-heart-wants-what-it-wants-official-dance-remixes|accessdate=April 7, 2017|work=[[Idolator (website)|Idolator]]|date=December 4, 2014}}{{dead link|date=August 2025|bot=medic}}{{cbignore|bot=medic}}</ref>

Music critics mostly commended the song's lyrical content, production, vocals, and Gomez's new direction. The song reached number six on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]]. It also reached the top ten in Canada, Denmark, Lebanon, and South Africa, as well as the top 40 in thirteen additional countries. It is [[Music recording certification|certified]] Quadruple Platinum in the US and Norway and Silver or higher in six additional countries.

==Background and release==
Gomez said about the song, "And it's also a step for me knowing like, 'Ok, this is what I'm about to say, and I need to say it when I'm ready' [...] And I think after this year, it's the perfect way to end the year, it's the perfect way to end a chapter in a way. It's like, this is what I'll say about every single person that has judged me for every decision that I've made, for every person, [and] heart that is being judged for something they've done, and now I just want to release it," Gomez stated in an interview with [[Ryan Seacrest]] on 102.7 KIIS FM during the world premiere of "The Heart Wants What It Wants."<ref>{{cite web|last1=Inocencio|first1=Marc|title=Selena Gomez Talks New Single and Justin Bieber: 'I'm Upset When He's Upset'|url=http://www.ryanseacrest.com/2014/11/06/selena-gomez-talks-new-single-and-justin-bieber-im-upset-when-hes-upset/|website=RyanSeacrest.com|date=November 6, 2014}}</ref> In its first day on radio, the song had a listening audience of 10.061&nbsp;million.<ref name=Impact/>

A demo of the song was recorded by American singer [[Gwen Stefani]] under the title "My Heart Wants What It Wants", which leaked online in December 2022.<ref>{{cite web |last1=Deville |first1=Chris |title=Hear Gwen Stefani's Unreleased Version of Selena Gomez's "The Heart Wants What It Wants" |url=https://www.stereogum.com/2207720/hear-gwen-stefanis-previously-unreleased-version-of-selena-gomezs-the-heart-wants-what-it-wants/news/ |website=Stereogum |date=December 6, 2022 |access-date=6 December 2022}}</ref>

"The Heart Wants What It Wants" was written by [[Selena Gomez]], [[Antonina Armato]], [[David Jost]], and [[Tim James (record producer)|Tim James]].  It is a midtempo pop and [[contemporary R&B|R&B]] song,<ref name="sound"/><ref>{{cite web|url=http://top40.about.com/od/reviews/fl/Selena-Gomez-The-Heart-Wants-What-It-Wants.htm|title=Selena Gomez – "The Heart Wants What It Wants"|author=Bill Lamb|work=[[About.com]]|accessdate=19 November 2015|archive-date=October 7, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151007223024/http://top40.about.com/od/reviews/fl/Selena-Gomez-The-Heart-Wants-What-It-Wants.htm|url-status=dead}}</ref> that contains a minimal [[electropop]] beat complete with finger snaps, ominous synths and haunting groans.<ref name="fuse">{{cite web|url=http://www.fuse.tv/videos/2014/11/selena-gomez-heart-wants-what-it-wants|title="The Heart Wants What It Wants": Is Selena Gomez Singing, Crying About Justin Bieber in New Single? – Fuse|work=Fuse|accessdate=26 June 2015}}</ref> Several critics noted the reminiscence of the song to the works by singers [[Lorde]] and [[Lana Del Rey]].<ref name="fuse"/><ref name="axs"/>
Lyrically, Gomez details being in love with a bad boy despite knowing he may not be a stand-up guy.<ref name="fuse"/> She sings, "The bed's getting cold and you're not here / The future that we hold is so unclear / But I'm not alive until you call / And I'll bet the odds against it all... / There's a million reasons why I should give you up / But the heart wants what it wants."<ref name="fuse"/>

The title is from a letter by [[Emily Dickinson]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://archive.emilydickinson.org/correspondence/mbowles/l262.html|title=Dickinson/M. Bowles Correspondence spring 1862 (letter 262)}}</ref> The saying was popularized in 1992 when it was quoted by [[Woody Allen]] in an interview about his relationship with [[Soon-Yi Previn]].<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://time.com/archive/6721029/the-heart-wants-what-it-wants/|title=The Heart Wants What It Wants|magazine=Time |date=31 August 1992}}</ref>

According to the sheet music published at Musicnotes.com, "The Heart Wants What It Wants" is written in the key of [[G Minor]] with a tempo of 80-84 beats per minute. It follows a chord progression of Eb-Cm-Gm-Bb.<ref>[https://www.musicnotes.com/sheetmusic/mtd.asp?ppn=MN0143554 Selena Gomez "The Heart Wants What It Wants" Sheet Music]. musicnotes.com</ref>

==Critical reception==
Lucas Villa of ''[[AXS (company)|AXS]]'' favorably compared "The Heart Wants What it Wants" to the music of [[Lana Del Rey]] for Gomez's use of "[[trip hop]] beats, guitar and distorted [[Emile Haynie]]-styled yelps" alongside her "darkest" lyrics yet. He further wrote that the song "stands as a heartbreaking revelation in her five-year-old songbook, one that's vulnerably beautiful and unapologetically honest."<ref name="axs">{{cite web|last1=Villa|first1=Lucas|title=Review: Selena Gomez has a Lana Del Rey moment on 'Heart Wants What it Wants'|url=http://www.axs.com/review-selena-gomez-has-a-lana-del-rey-moment-on-heart-wants-what-it-w-27469|website=AXS|date=November 10, 2014}}</ref> Tim Sendra of [[AllMusic]] highlighted the song and praised it by calling it "very adult and real-sounding" and adding that "she acquits herself well here too."<ref>[https://www.allmusic.com/album/for-you-mw0002767478 Review by AllMusic] Retrieved 20 December 2014</ref> Popology Now called this song "emotionally charged."<ref>[http://www.popologynow.com/2014/11/24/album-review-selena-gomez-for-you/ Review by Popology Now] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141229221333/http://www.popologynow.com/2014/11/24/album-review-selena-gomez-for-you/ |date=December 29, 2014 }} Retrieved 29 December 2014</ref> ''[[The Huffington Post]]''{{'s}} Christopher Rosen deemed the song "a fairly good anthem for those going through heartbreak."<ref>{{cite web|last1=Rosen|first1=Christopher|title=Selena Gomez's Emotional New Video Addresses Justin Bieber Relationship|url=http://www.huffingtonpost.com/2014/11/06/selena-gomez-the-heart-wants-what-it-wants_n_6113890.html|website=[[The Huffington Post]]|accessdate=June 23, 2015|date=November 6, 2014}}</ref>

[[Idolator (website)|Idolator]] praised the song: "The Rock Mafia-produced gem shows the former teen queen in an entirely new light. There’s a vulnerability we haven’t seen before as Selena sings about the dark side of her very own fairytale with palpable honesty and conviction" and the editor called it the selling point of an album and added that if Selena follows this direction, many hits will be coming for her."<ref>{{Deprecated archive|url=https://archive.today/20141130035959/http://www.idolator.com/7571928/selena-gomez-for-you-album-review|title=Review by Idolator}} Retrieved 29 December 2014</ref>  The [[Times of India]] editor Kasmin Fernandes added that this song shows her in an "entirely new light."<ref>[https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/entertainment/english/music/news/Music-Review-For-You/articleshow/45474965.cms Review by The Times Of India] Retrieved 29 December 2014</ref> [[Renowned for Sound]] was also positive: "smooth dose of pop with a second-guessing love storyline."<ref name="sound">[http://renownedforsound.com/index.php/album-review-selena-gomez-for-you/ Review by Renowned for Sound] Retrieved 29 December 2014</ref> When reviewing single, Music Times concluded: "Emotional song channels Justin Bieber drama but is still stunning on its own."<ref>[http://www.musictimes.com/articles/14670/20141106/selena-gomez-the-heart-wants-what-it-wants-review-justin-bieber-listen.htm Review by Music Times] Retrieved 29 December 2014</ref>

==Chart performance==
{{More citations needed section|date=July 2015}}
During its first week, "The Heart Wants What It Wants" sold 103,000 digital copies in the United States, which allowed it to debut at number six of [[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'s [[Digital Songs]]. These sales gave Gomez her second best debut on the list, after "[[Who Says (Selena Gomez & the Scene song)|Who Says]]" with her band [[Selena Gomez & the Scene]], which debuted with 116,000 copies. On the [[Billboard Hot 100]] chart, which combines digital sales with radio and streaming performance, the song debuted at number twenty-five; the best entry of the week. On Billboard's [[Streaming Songs]], it debuted at number thirty-seven for receiving 3.1&nbsp;million streams in a week.<ref>{{cite magazine|last1=Trust|first1=Gary|title=Hot 100 Chart Moves: Selena Gomez's 'Heart' Starts at No. 25|url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/hot-100-chart-moves-selena-gomez-big-sean/|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|publisher=[[Prometheus Global Media]]|access-date=July 20, 2015|date=November 14, 2014}}</ref> After performing the song at the [[American Music Awards]] on November 23, 2014, Gomez received  her second top 10 on the Billboard Hot 100, and it reached number six, the same position obtained by "[[Come & Get It (Selena Gomez song)|Come & Get It]]" in May 2013. The week after its release, the song sold 136,000 copies and reached number five on Digital Songs, since it had a 98% increase in downloads compared to the previous week. Simultaneously, it reached the fifth position in Streaming Songs and debuted at number forty-two on [[Radio Songs (chart)|Radio Songs]]. In the latter, the song became Gomez's second top ten, again behind "Come & Get It".<ref>{{cite magazine|last1=Trust|first1=Gary|title=Taylor Swift No. 1 on Hot 100, Selena Gomez, Mark Ronson Hit Top 10|url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/hot-100-taylor-swift-no-1-selena-gomez-mark-ronson/|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|publisher=[[Prometheus Global Media]]|access-date=July 20, 2015|date=December 3, 2014}}</ref> In the [[Pop Songs]] chart, based on the level of [[airplay]] that songs receive on pop radio stations in the United States, "The Heart Wants What It Wants" became Gomez's third top 10 single in her solo career and her third consecutive single making it to the first ten spots on the chart. In late January 2015, the song debuted on the [[Hot Dance Club Songs]] chart, marking the most popular songs in the dance clubs of the United States. As of May 2017, the single has sold 1.4&nbsp;million copies in the United States.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/ask-billboard-selena-gomezs-career-album-song-sales/|title=Ask Billboard: Selena Gomez's Career Album & Song Sales|last=Trust|first=Gary|magazine=Billboard|date=14 May 2017|access-date=15 May 2017}}</ref>

In the [[Canadian Hot 100]], it also achieved the best debut in its first week, at number nine. This made "The Heart Wants What It Wants" Gomez's third top 10 in the country and her second best charting single after "[[Come & Get It (Selena Gomez song)|Come & Get It]]", which peaked at number six, followed by "[[Love You like a Love Song]]", which reached the tenth position. Weeks later "The Heart Wants What It Wants" equaled the position of "Come & Get It" in the Canadian Hot 100 chart.

The song experienced success in Europe, reaching top ten such as Denmark, Slovakia, Greece and Czech Republic, top twenty in Hungary, Norway and Finland, top thirty in United Kingdom, Spain, Belgium and France and top forty in Romania. The song appeared on the Austrian, German, Swedish, Dutch, Russian and Italian Singles Chart, although it failed to enter into the top forty.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://lescharts.com/showitem.asp?interpret=Selena+Gomez&titel=The+Heart+Wants+What+It+Wants&cat=s|title=les charts - Selena Gomez - The Heart Wants What It Wants|work=lescharts.com|date=November 15, 2014|access-date=September 21, 2025}}</ref>

==Live performance==
Gomez performed the single for the first time at the [[American Music Awards of 2014|2014 American Music Awards]].<ref>{{cite web|title=American Music Awards: Selena Gomez, Garth Brooks, Imagine Dragons to Perform|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/earshot/american-music-awards-selena-gomez-747016|website=Hollywood Reporter|date=November 6, 2014}}</ref> In her presentation, Gomez wore a skin-colored dress, and the background consisted of images as dark lights, thorns, roses, broken glass and wings appeared behind her on the screen. At the end of her performance, the singer added the phrase "I thought you were the one...".

Caitlin White from [[MTV]] described the visuals as "amazing" and said: "It is a more mature song for Selena, but one that almost everyone in the audience could relate, given the intense and emotional response public."<ref>{{cite web|last1=White|first1=Caitlin|title=Selena Gomez's Tearful, Emotional Performance Of 'The Heart Wants What It Wants' Will Leave You Sobbing|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/2007053/selena-gomez-2014-amas-performance/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141127044917/http://www.mtv.com/news/2007053/selena-gomez-2014-amas-performance/|url-status=dead|archive-date=November 27, 2014|website=[[MTV]]|accessdate=June 23, 2015|date=November 23, 2015}}</ref> [[The Wrap]]'s Matt Donnelly wrote that it was "a considerable growth moment" for the singer.<ref>{{cite web|last1=Donnelly|first1=Matt|title=Selena Gomez Delivers Emotional AMA Performance With 'The Heart Wants What It Wants' (Video)|url=https://www.thewrap.com/selena-gomez-delivers-emotional-ama-performance-with-the-heart-wants-what-it-wants-video/|website=The Wrap|accessdate=June 23, 2015|date=November 23, 2014}}</ref> Casey Rackham of [[Zap2it]] called the performance "beautifully and emotionally intense." She commented that the use of wings was "pretty empowering."<ref>{{cite web|last1=Rackham|first1=Casey|title=elena Gomez takes AMAs to an emotional place with 'The Heart Wants What It Wants'|url=http://www.zap2it.com/blogs/american_music_awards_2014_selena_gomez_performance-2014-11|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141126190623/http://www.zap2it.com/blogs/american_music_awards_2014_selena_gomez_performance-2014-11|url-status=dead|archive-date=November 26, 2014|website=[[Zap2it]]|accessdate=June 23, 2015|date=November 23, 2014}}</ref>  After her presentation at the American Music Awards, the song went to number one on the [[Billboard Twitter Real-Time]], position it had occupied in its release.

An instrumental version of the song was used as an interlude on the [[Revival Tour (Selena Gomez tour)|Revival Tour]] in 2016.
