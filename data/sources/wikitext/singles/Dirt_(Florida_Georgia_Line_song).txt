"'''Dirt'''" is a song recorded by American [[country music]] duo [[Florida Georgia Line]]. It is the first single from their second studio album, ''[[Anything Goes (Florida Georgia Line album)|Anything Goes]]'', which was released on October 14, 2014.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.usatoday.com/story/life/music/2014/07/08/song-of-the-week-dirt-florida-georgia-line/12357847/ |title=Song of the week: 'Dirt,' Florida Georgia Line |publisher=Usatoday.com |date=2014-07-08 |accessdate=2014-07-13}}</ref>

==Content==
Written by [[Rodney Clawson]] and [[Chris Tompkins]], the song is a mid-tempo ballad about various life events centering on dirt.<ref>{{cite web|last=Hudak |first=Joseph |url=https://www.rollingstone.com/music/news/florida-georgia-line-dig-a-hit-with-dirt-20140716 |title=Florida Georgia Line Digs a Hit With 'Dirt' |publisher=Rolling Stone |date=2014-07-16 |accessdate=2014-08-22}}</ref> The final lines of the song include the lyrics "You know you came from it / Someday you'll return to it", which the duo initially did not want to include as they felt it was a cliché.<ref name=Dirt>{{cite web|last1=Vinson|first1=Christina|title=Florida Georgia Line, 'Dirt' – Lyrics Uncovered|url=https://tasteofcountry.com/florida-georgia-line-dirt-lyrics/|website=Tasteofcountry|accessdate=August 11, 2013}}</ref> However they eventually decided to include the line.<ref name=Dirt/> Brian Kelley, one-half of the duo, told the ''[[Chicago Sun-Times]]'', "I think a '[[Cruise (song)|Cruise]]'-type song would have been accepted, but just when you think you have FGL figured out, we wanted to go against the grain and change it up with a song like 'Dirt'".<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.suntimes.com/entertainment/29056456-421/florida-georgia-line-finds-life-beyond-cruise.html#.U-QLnIBdVVo |title=Florida Georgia Line finds life beyond 'Cruise' - Chicago Sun-Times |publisher=Suntimes.com |date= |accessdate=2014-08-22 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140808221427/http://www.suntimes.com/entertainment/29056456-421/florida-georgia-line-finds-life-beyond-cruise.html#.U-QLnIBdVVo |archive-date=2014-08-08 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

==Music video==
The music video was directed by [[Nigel Dick]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.etonline.com/music/148242_florida_georgia_line_dirt_video_exclusive_behind_the_scenes/index.html |title=EXCLUSIVE! Go Behind-the-Scenes of Florida Georgia Line's New Video 'Dirt' &#124; Entertainment Tonight |publisher=Etonline.com |date=2014-07-10 |accessdate=2014-08-22}}</ref> It follows the life of a married couple, alternating between the past and present, where the family is gathered for the mother's funeral. The video has a lot of visual references to dirt. Dirt is portrayed as a substance that you can smear in the face of your loved ones one minute, then bury family in it the next.<ref>{{cite journal|last=Ragusa|first=Tammy|date=11 August 2014|title=Reviews|journal=[[Country Weekly]]|volume=21|issue=32|pages=66–67|issn=1074-3235}}</ref> It stars [[JD Souther]] in the leading role and Lindsay Heyser as the young Rosie.<ref>{{cite news|last1=Howell|first1=Coti|title=Florida Georgia Line's 'Dirt' Video Sees a Love Through the Years|url=https://tasteofcountry.com/florida-georgia-line-dirt-video/|accessdate=October 25, 2014|work=Taste of Country|date=July 8, 2014}}</ref>

==Commercial performance==
"Dirt" debuted at number 16 on the [[Country Airplay]] chart for the week of July 26, 2014, becoming the highest-airplay debut by a country duo. "Dirt" also debuted on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] at number 11 and on the [[Canadian Hot 100]] the same week at number 10, being the "Hot Shot Debut" of the week on both charts. Also during the same week, "Dirt" jumped from number 40 to number one on the [[Hot Country Songs|''Billboard'' Hot Country Songs]] chart.<ref>{{cite magazine|last1=Jessen|first1=Wade|title=Florida Georgia Line's 'Dirt' Plows To No. 1 On Hot Country Songs|url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/florida-georgia-lines-dirt-plows-to-no-1-on-hot-country-songs/|access-date=July 18, 2014|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|date=July 16, 2014}}</ref> On its final week on the chart, for the week of January 10, 2015, the song jumped from number 22 to number 11 on the Hot Country Songs chart and was the airplay garner for the week. Afterwards, however, the song dropped off the charts.<ref>{{cite magazine|title=Country Music: Top Country Songs Chart :Billboard|url=https://www.billboard.com/charts/country-songs/2015-01-10|accessdate=November 7, 2015|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|date=January 10, 2015}}</ref>

"Dirt" is the sixth consecutive single of the duo to peak in the top 5 on the ''Billboard'' Hot Country Songs chart. The song reached its million sales mark in the US in October 2014.<ref>{{cite news |url=https://music.yahoo.com/blogs/chart-watch/chart-watch-all-about-that-transatlantic-1-224309687.html |title=Chart Watch: All About That Transatlantic #1|author=Paul Grein |date= October 8, 2014 |publisher=Yahoo! Music |work=Chart Watch }}</ref> As of December 2014, the song has sold 1,272,000 copies in the US.<ref name=sales>{{cite news |url=http://roughstock.com/news/2015/01/32252-country-musics-top-30-digital-singles-week-january-21-2015/ |title=Country Music's Top 30 Digital Singles: Week of January 21, 2015 |author= Matt Bjorke |date=January 21, 2015 |work=Roughstock }} Sales figures given [https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1OQSwu651Nu1PpvWGslphCoewhgaloUOuo5-Ooh6iODM/edit#gid=0 here]</ref> The song was certified double Platinum on April 30, 2015.<ref name=RIAA />

In Canada, the song sold 17,000 downloads in its debut week.<ref>{{cite web|author=Alan Cross |url=http://ajournalofmusicalthings.com/weekly-music-sales-report-analysis-16-july-2014/ |title=A Journal of Musical ThingsWeekly Music Sales Report and Analysis: 16 July 2014 |publisher=A Journal of Musical Things |date=2014-07-18 |accessdate=2014-08-22}}</ref>
