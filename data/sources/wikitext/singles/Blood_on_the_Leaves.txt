"'''Blood on the Leaves'''" is a song by American rapper [[Kanye West]] from his sixth studio album ''[[Yeezus]]'' (2013). In the song, West presents his thoughts on how fame can cause the destruction of relationships, while making comparisons of contemporary times to the [[lynching]] of African Americans in the United States during the pre-[[Civil rights movement|civil rights]] era. West delivers his vocals through an [[Auto-Tune]] processor, like on his 2008 album ''[[808s & Heartbreak]]''. It contains samples of both singer [[Nina Simone]]'s 1965 rendition of [[Billie Holiday]]'s song "[[Strange Fruit]]", and "R U Ready" by the duo [[TNGHT]], who co-produced the track.

The song received universal acclaim from music critics, with many commenting on its lyrical message and praising its samples. It was named one of the best tracks of 2013 by multiple publications. ''[[Pitchfork (website)|Pitchfork]]'' later placed the track on their list of the 200 best songs of the 2010s. Despite not being released as a [[Single (music)|single]], the song managed to enter charts in the [[United States]], [[United Kingdom]], [[France]], and [[Australia]] in 2013. A [[music video]] was set to be directed by American filmmaker [[David Lynch]], but plans were abandoned. Since its release, West has performed the song live on multiple occasions, including at the [[2013 MTV Video Music Awards]] and on the 2016 [[Saint Pablo Tour]].

== Background and composition ==
[[File:TNGHT Mezzanine San Francisco 4-20-2013.jpg|thumb|200px|alt=Musical duo TNGHT performing in 2013|The song features production from musical duo [[TNGHT]].]]
The song features production by the musical duo [[TNGHT]], consisting of Scottish producer [[Hudson Mohawke]] and Canadian producer [[Lunice]].<ref name="liner notes" /> Mohawke had previously worked with West on the 2012 [[GOOD Music]] collaboration "[[Mercy (GOOD Music song)|Mercy]]",<ref>{{cite web | url=https://www.factmag.com/2012/04/06/hudson-mohawke-produces-kanyes-g-o-o-d-music-single/ | title=Hudson Mohawke contributes to Kanye's new G.O.O.D. Music track | website=[[Fact (UK magazine)|FACT Magazine]] | date=April 6, 2012 | access-date=May 8, 2019 | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190508141452/https://www.factmag.com/2012/04/06/hudson-mohawke-produces-kanyes-g-o-o-d-music-single/ | archive-date=May 8, 2019 | url-status=live }}</ref> along with other tracks on the compilation album ''[[Cruel Summer (GOOD Music album)|Cruel Summer]]'' (2012).<ref name="pitchfork" /> After working on ''Yeezus'', Mohawke was introduced to an entirely new approach to songwriting.<ref name="guardian">{{cite web | url=https://www.theguardian.com/music/2015/mar/14/hudson-mohawke-kanye-west-producer | title=Hudson Mohawke: Kanye West's go-to guy is changing the face of pop | website=[[The Guardian]] | date=March 14, 2015 | access-date=May 8, 2019 | author=McQuaid, Ian | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160521083245/http://www.theguardian.com/music/2015/mar/14/hudson-mohawke-kanye-west-producer | archive-date=May 21, 2016 | url-status=live }}</ref> According to Mohawke, the duo's song "R U Ready", which is sampled in "Blood on the Leaves", was one of the first songs the two recorded together and was also one of the first pieces of music the duo sent to West to sample.<ref name="pitchfork"/>

West had previously sampled the work of [[Nina Simone]] on the ''[[808s & Heartbreak]]'' track "Bad News" and the ''[[Watch the Throne]]'' collaborative track "New Day" with [[Jay-Z]].<ref>{{cite web | url=https://tvone.tv/34889/5-times-nina-simone-inspired-kanye/ | title=5 Times Nina Simone Inspired Kanye | publisher=[[TV One (U.S. TV network)|TV One]] | date=June 23, 2016 | access-date=May 8, 2019 | author=Gidarisingh, Ricardo A. | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190508141449/https://tvone.tv/34889/5-times-nina-simone-inspired-kanye/ | archive-date=May 8, 2019 | url-status=live }}</ref> Rapper [[Travis Scott]] viewed West as "a genius" and the only person who would think of combining "[[Strange Fruit]]" with a Hudson Mohawke beat in the  "crazy-ass song".<ref name="pitchfork"/> When he first heard the combination, Travis Scott "was jumping on niggas' backs and shit, like, 'Ahhhh!'".<ref name="pitchfork"/>

==Critical reception==
The song received universal acclaim from music critics, with many commenting on its lyrical message and praising its samples. When reviewing the album for ''[[HipHopDX]]'', Justin Hunte wrote that "Blood on the Leaves" is "[the] one song [from ''Yeezus''] that must be heard immediately."<ref>{{cite web | url=https://hiphopdx.com/reviews/id.2095/title.kanye-west-yeezus | title=Kanye West "Yeezus" Album Review | website=[[HipHopDX]] | date=June 17, 2013 | access-date=May 11, 2019 | author=Hunte, Jusitn | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190511132617/https://hiphopdx.com/reviews/id.2095/title.kanye-west-yeezus | archive-date=May 11, 2019 | url-status=live }}</ref> The staff of ''Popdust'' rated the song three and a half out of five, describing it as "an epic Yeezy tale about a relationship gone sour over issues of money and celebrity and bad timing and possibly even an unwanted child, depending on how you interpret the final verse."<ref>{{cite web | url=https://www.popdust.com/kanye-wests-yeezus-reviewed-blood-on-the-leaves-1889716244.html | title=Kanye West's "Yeezus" Reviewed: "Blood on the Leaves" | website=Popdust | date=June 17, 2013 | access-date=May 11, 2019 | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190511132617/https://www.popdust.com/kanye-wests-yeezus-reviewed-blood-on-the-leaves-1889716244.html | archive-date=May 11, 2019 | url-status=live }}</ref> The song was branded by Jon Dolan of ''[[Rolling Stone]]'' as being "a buzzing, bluesy, static-y track."<ref>{{cite magazine | url=https://www.rollingstone.com/music/music-album-reviews/yeezus-94536/ | title=Kanye West, 'Yeezus' | magazine=[[Rolling Stone]] | date=June 14, 2013 | access-date=May 11, 2019 | author=Dolan, Jon | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20181210202622/https://www.rollingstone.com/music/music-album-reviews/yeezus-94536/ | archive-date=December 10, 2018 | url-status=live }}</ref> The song impressed director [[David Lynch]]: "I think it's one of the most modern pieces and so minimal, so powerful but at the same time so beautiful. Ryan Dombal of ''[[Pitchfork (website)|Pitchfork]]'' called the song the "album pinnacle" and "a nightmarish story of divorce and betrayal,"<ref>{{cite web | {{cite web | url=https://www.hotnewhiphop.com/557282-waka-flocka-blood-on-the-leaves-freestyle-new-song | title=Waka Flocka - Blood On The Leaves (Freestyle) | website=HotNewHipHop | date=May 19, 2014 | access-date=June 9, 2019 | author=Goddard, Kevin | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150913082239/http://www.hotnewhiphop.com/waka-flocka-blood-on-the-leaves-freestyle-new-song.1898925.html | archive-date=September 13, 2015 | url-status=live }}
</ref> A remix of the song was shared by [[Nicolas Jaar]] in February 2016.<ref>{{cite web | url=https://pitchfork.com/news/63470-listen-to-nicolas-jaars-remix-of-kanye-wests-blood-on-the-leaves/ | title=Listen to Nicolas Jaar's Remix of Kanye West's "Blood on the Leaves" | website=[[Pitchfork (website)|Pitchfork]] | date=February 10, 2016 | access-date=January 5, 2019 | author=Strauss, Matthew | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190105201032/https://pitchfork.com/news/63470-listen-to-nicolas-jaars-remix-of-kanye-wests-blood-on-the-leaves/ | archive-date=January 5, 2019 | url-status=live | df=mdy-all }}</ref> American rapper [[Yvngxchris|YvngxChris]] used a similar chop of this sample for his [[Blood on the Leaves (Yvngxchris song)|song of the same name]] as West's song, which blew up on [[TikTok]] and would eventually get a remix with [[SSGKobe]], [[Kashdami]], [[UnoTheActivist]], and [[Ken Carson]].<ref>{{Cite web|title=yvngxchris {{!}} "If I can make it, so can you"|url=https://flaunt.com/content/16-year-old-yvngxchris-if-i-can-make-it-so-can-you|access-date=2021-12-02|website=Flaunt Magazine|language=en-US}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|date=2021-11-20|title=yvngxchris Locks In With ssgkobe, Unotheactivist & More For "Blood On The Leaves" Remix|url=https://www.hotnewhiphop.com/363535-yvngxchris-locks-in-with-ssgkobe-unotheactivist-and-more-for-blood-on-the-leaves-remix-new-song|access-date=2021-12-02|website=HotNewHipHop|language=en}}</ref>
