"'''&num;Selfie'''" (stylized in [[all caps]]) is a [[satirical music|satirical song]] produced by American DJ duo [[the Chainsmokers]]. It was released on January 29, 2014 with [[Dim Mak Records]].

==Background and promotion==
The Chainsmokers, composed of Andrew Taggart and Alex Pall, had noticed how the word ''[[selfie]]'' had become a trend and they wanted to take advantage of it. They made a demo of a song containing a monologue from a female clubber about taking good selfies.<ref name=SelfieBillboard>{{cite magazine|last=Hyman|first=Dan|title='Harlem Shake' 2.0? '#Selfie' Is Going Viral With Slick Marketing|url=http://www.billboard.com/biz/articles/news/digital-and-mobile/5923140/harlem-shake-20-selfie-is-going-viral-with-slick|magazine=Billboard|accessdate=March 15, 2014|date=March 4, 2014}}</ref> Inspiration came from the regular club-going women that the Chainsmokers saw on their nights out in New York City, with one of the goals of the track being to incorporate the line "let me take a selfie." The Chainsmokers described in an interview with ''[[The Phoenix New Times]]'',
<blockquote>
Honestly, "#Selfie" is kind of a phenomenon for us. We made it and thought it was funny and put it out as an edit. Then Dim Mak wanted to buy it, and they bought it from us and put it out officially and we made a video for it. This was all just because we thought it was fun. And then the song just kind of took on a life of its own. Which is honestly really great because it's brought in a new audience of people that listen to music that we've made that we're more passionate about.<ref name="phoenix">{{cite news|last=Savage |first=Amanda |url=http://blogs.phoenixnewtimes.com/uponsun/2014/02/the-chainsmokers-interview-selfie-maya-scottsdale.php |title=The Chainsmokers: 'We Just Try to Mix What's Fun' |work=The Phoenix New Times |date=February 27, 2014 |accessdate=March 10, 2014}}</ref>
</blockquote>
The duo released the track to their [[SoundCloud]] account along with other posts in [[Vine (software)|Vine]] and [[Instagram]].

==Composition==
"#Selfie" includes spoken word verses; the uncredited female voice is Alexis Killacam.<ref name="bubbling"/><ref name="chicago">{{cite news|url=http://www.chicagoreader.com/Bleader/archives/2014/03/11/the-chainsmokers-paint-by-numbers-virality|title=The Chainsmokers' paint-by-numbers virality|last=Raymar|first=Miles|date=March 11, 2014|accessdate=March 18, 2014|work=[[Chicago Reader]]}}</ref> The song details a [[Narcissism|narcissistic]] young woman at a club, presumably speaking to friends, as she is concerned about taking selfies and uploading them to her [[Instagram]] account, while she criticizes other people at the club, including their outfits, in addition to dealing with a guy named Jason with whom she has a love/hate relationship. At the end of each spoken verse, she says "Let me take a selfie".<ref name=huffingtonpost>{{cite web|first=Pollo |last=Del Mar |url=http://www.huffingtonpost.com/pollo-del-mar/the-chainsmokers-selfie-g_b_4916820.html |title=The Chainsmokers' Bring Cheeky Smash "#Selfie" to SF March 8 |work=The Huffington Post |date=March 6, 2014 |accessdate=March 10, 2014}}</ref> The lyrics make several cultural references, including to Instagram and the [[Cedric Gervais]] remix of [[Lana Del Rey]]'s song, "[[Summertime Sadness#Cedric Gervais remix|Summertime Sadness]]".

== Critical reception==
The song has been compared to the viral impact of "[[Harlem Shake (song)|Harlem Shake]]" by [[Baauer]]. The Chainsmokers explained that one of their initial intentions with "#Selfie" was to re-create the viral impact that "Harlem Shake" had, with the general public taking the song and maybe including their own interpretations along with the beat. The structure of it has drawn comparisons to the 1982 hit "[[Valley Girl (song)|Valley Girl]]" by [[Frank Zappa]] and his daughter, [[Moon Unit Zappa]].<ref name="SelfieBillboard2">{{cite magazine|last=Trust|first=Gary|date=March 11, 2014|title=Chart Moves: Avicii, The Chainsmokers Burn Up Dance Charts; Coldplay Makes 'Magic' On Hot 100, Hot Rock Songs; The Pretty Reckless Rules Mainstream Rock|url=http://www.billboard.com/biz/articles/news/chart-alert/5937495/chart-moves-avicii-the-chainsmokers-burn-up-dance-charts|magazine=Billboard|accessdate=March 15, 2014}}</ref><ref name="SelfieYahoo">{{cite web|last=Okano|first=Marisa|title=#SELFIE: The Chainsmokers' 7 Tips For Taking The Perfect One|url=http://music.yahoo.com/blogs/music-news/-selfie--the-chainsmokers--7-tips-for-taking-the-perfect-one-201522035.html|website=Yahoo!|date=March 11, 2014|accessdate=March 15, 2014}}</ref><ref name="hitfix" />

==Commercial performance==
"#Selfie" debuted on the ''Billboard'' [[Dance/Electronic Songs]] chart at number 19, with first week sales of 9,000 digital downloads according to [[Nielsen SoundScan]].<ref name="bubbling"/> The next week, "#Selfie" sold another 53,000 digital downloads and debuted at number 55 on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]], the highest debut of the week.<ref name="hitfix">{{cite web |last=Newman |first=Melinda |url=http://www.hitfix.com/news/5-things-you-need-to-know-right-now-about-the-chainsmokers-selfie |title=5 things you need to know right now about The Chainsmokers' "#Selfie" |work=[[HitFix]] |date=March 6, 2014 |accessdate=March 10, 2014 |archive-date=October 25, 2016 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161025051215/http://www.hitfix.com/news/5-things-you-need-to-know-right-now-about-the-chainsmokers-selfie |url-status=dead }}</ref> The sales drove the song to number six on the Dance/Electronic Songs and number three on the Dance/Electronic Digital Songs charts. "#Selfie" additionally debuted at number seven on [[Dance/Mix Show Airplay]], becoming only the sixth song in the chart's 11-year history to debut as high or above and the first since [[Lady Gaga]] entered at number six with "[[Alejandro (song)|Alejandro]]" in May 2010.<ref name="blasts">{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/chainsmokers-selfie-blasts-up-dance-charts-enters-hot-100/ |title=Chainsmokers' '#SELFIE' Blasts Up Dance Charts, Enters Hot 100 |magazine=Billboard |last=Murray|first=Gordon|date=March 6, 2014 |access-date=March 10, 2014}}</ref> In its third week of release, the song sold another 88,000 downloads (up by 64%) to move into the top ten of the [[Hot Digital Songs]] chart. It also reached a peak of number 27 on the [[Pop Songs]] chart.<ref name="top40w2">{{cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/5930454/rick-ross-rules-with-fifth-no-1-album-on-billboard-200 |title=Rick Ross Rules With Fifth No. 1 Album On Billboard 200 |magazine=Billboard |date=August 18, 2013 |accessdate=March 14, 2014}}</ref> On the Hot 100, the song consecutively jumped to number 28.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.digitalspy.co.uk/music/news/a557444/idina-menzels-frozen-song-let-it-go-enters-us-singles-top-ten.html |title=Idina Menzel's Frozen song 'Let It Go' enters US singles top ten - Music News |work=Digital Spy |date=March 13, 2014 |accessdate=March 14, 2014}}</ref> It also started on the [[Hot Dance Club Songs]] chart at number 33.<ref name="hotdance">{{cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/charts/2014-03-22/dance-club-play-songs?order=gainer |title=Dance Club Songs : Mar 22, 2014 (Biggest Jump) |magazine=Billboard |date=March 11, 2014 |accessdate=March 14, 2014}}</ref> The fourth week saw the song sell a further 111,000 digital downloads and moved to number 18 on the Hot 100.<ref name="hot100">{{cite web|url=http://music.yahoo.com/blogs/music-news/chart-watch-selfie-smash-003624707.html|title=Chart Watch: "#Selfie" Is A #Smash|last=Grein|first=Paul|date=March 19, 2014|accessdate=March 19, 2014|publisher=Yahoo!}}</ref> It reached the top of the Dance/Electronic Songs, and also moved up the other charts like Dance/Electronic Streaming Songs (16–7; 1.4 million US streams, according to Nielsen BDS), Dance/Mix Show Airplay (4–2) and Hot Dance Club Songs (33–19).<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/the-chainsmokers-take-selfie-to-no-1-on-dance-chart/|title=The Chainsmokers Take '#SELFIE' to No. 1 on Dance Chart|last=Murray|first=Gordon|date=March 20, 2014|access-date=March 20, 2014|magazine=Billboard}}</ref> "#Selfie" sold another 101,000 copies next week and moved to number 16 on the Hot 100.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://music.yahoo.com/blogs/music-news/chart-watch-frozen-comes-summer-002850198.html|title=Chart Watch: After 'Frozen', Here Comes 'Summer'|last=Grein|first=Paul|date=March 28, 2014|accessdate=March 28, 2014|publisher=Yahoo!}}</ref> As of June 2014, the song has sold 865,000 copies in the United States.<ref>{{cite magazine |url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/daft-punk-dj-snake-lil-jon-lead-mid-year-soundscan-dance-electronic/ |title=Daft Punk, DJ Snake & Lil Jon Lead Mid-Year SoundScan Dance/Electronic Charts |last=Murray |first=Gordon |magazine=Billboard |date=July 2, 2014 |access-date=July 2, 2014}}</ref>

In the United Kingdom, "#Selfie" debuted at number 115 on the [[UK Singles Chart]] and moved to number 42 the next week.<ref name="ukdebut">{{cite web|url=http://www.officialcharts.com/music-charts/ |title=Official Singles Chart UK Top 100 - 15th March 2014 |website=[[OfficialCharts.com]] |date=March 15, 2013 |archivedate=February 10, 2014 |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20140210100326/http://www.officialcharts.com/music-charts/ |url-status=dead }}</ref> The song finally peaked at number 11 in the UK. In Australia "#Selfie" debuted at number 98 on the [[ARIA Charts]], and moved to a peak of number 3.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.ariacharts.com.au/news/45194/chartifacts---tuesday-4th-march-2014 |title=Chartifacts – Tuesday 4th March 2014|publisher=[[ARIA Charts]]|accessdate=March 11, 2014}}</ref><ref name="aus"/>

In 2015, the Japanese pop trio [[MAX (band)|MAX]] released a cover version of the song, renamed "#SELFIE ~ONNA NOW~." The song was released to commemorate their 20th anniversary.
