"'''Feeling Myself'''" is a song by  Trinidadian rapper [[Nicki Minaj]] featuring American singer [[Beyoncé]] from Minaj's third studio album, ''[[The Pinkprint]]'' (2014). It was written by Minaj, [[SZA (singer)|SZA]], Beyoncé, and [[Hit-Boy]] and produced by Hit-Boy with additional vocal production by Beyoncé.<ref name="credits"/> "Feeling Myself" is a [[Hip hop music|hip hop]] song backed by a [[West Coast of the United States|West Coast]] [[Synthesizer|synth]], driving [[Bass (sound)|bass]] and [[drums]].

==Background and recording==
In May 2014, Minaj's manager [[Gee Roberson]] contacted her to inform her that Beyoncé wanted a remix of "[[Flawless (Beyoncé song)|Flawless]]" on which she would be featured. Following this, Beyoncé sent Minaj a new version of the song she wanted and told Minaj, "I want you to be you. I don't want you to hold back."<ref name="Minaj">{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/music/rb-hip-hop/nicki-minaj-explains-beyonce-flawless-remix-6206968/|title=Nicki Minaj Explains How Beyoncé's 'Flawless' Remix Came to Be|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|publisher=[[Prometheus Global Media]] |first=Erika|last=Ramirez|date=August 4, 2014|access-date=August 5, 2014}}</ref> Minaj agreed to do the song with her and in return Beyoncé would be featured on ''The Pinkprint''.<ref name="Minaj"/> Singer-songwriter [[SZA (singer)|SZA]] was enlisted to co-write parts of the song. She was called and later went to the studio to meet and work with the song's producer, Hit-Boy. During the studio session, SZA wrote the song's lyrics whilst Hit-boy produced it.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.hiphopdx.com/index/news/id.31954/title.sza-discusses-co-writing-nicki-minajs-feeling-myself-updates-on-a-album|work=HipHopDX|last=Mansell|first=Henry|title=SZA Discusses Co-Writing Nicki Minaj's "Feeling Myself" & Updates "A" Album Status|date=January 1, 2015|access-date=January 2, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150103230248/http://www.hiphopdx.com/index/news/id.31954/title.sza-discusses-co-writing-nicki-minajs-feeling-myself-updates-on-a-album|archive-date=January 3, 2015|url-status=dead}}</ref>

According to Minaj's [[audio engineer]], Aubry "Big Juice" Delaine, when Minaj first heard the beat, she was "crazy" about it. The final [[Audio mixing (recorded music)|mixing]] took place around November 2014, right before the album had to be handed in. Delaine recalls, "it was crunch time. Hit-Boy, on the spot, added those hard [[Roland TR-808|808 drums]] in the middle of the song." The song was then sent over to Beyoncé and her engineer [[Stuart White (recording engineer)|Stuart White]] for her full vocals, with White ending up mixing the song. Delaine said when they sent the song back, "that's when Beyoncé put her sauce on it and all of her extra vocals. We listened to that and knew that song was out of here".<ref name="Big Juice">{{Cite web|url=https://www.revolt.tv/2019/11/7/20951462/nicki-minaj-engineer-big-juice-interview|title=Studio Sessions - Big Juice talks being Nicki Minaj's main engineer, her "MotorSport" verse, the rapper being a perfectionist and more|last=Nelson Jr|first=Keith|website=[[Revolt (TV network)|Revolt]]|date=November 7, 2019|access-date=March 29, 2020}}</ref>

==Composition==
The song's [[Beat (music)|beat]] is built over a [[West Coast of the United States|West Coast]] [[Synthesizer|synth]], driving [[Bass (sound)|bass]] and [[drums]] along with [[bell]]s and [[whistles]] incorporated into the [[Production (music)|production]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://exclaim.ca/Music/article/nicki_minaj-feeling_myself_ft_beyonce|title=Nicki Minaj "Feeling Myself" (ft. Beyoncé)|last=Hughes|first=Josiah|date=December 11, 2014|access-date=January 29, 2015|work=[[Exclaim!]]}}</ref> The song contains rough edges, with high winding synths and intense lyrics that revolve around both Minaj and Beyoncé "[[Braggadocio (rap)|bragging]]" about themselves.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.musictimes.com/articles/19680/20141211/nicki-minaj-beyonce-feelin-myself-the-pinkprint-leak-listen-stream.htm|title=Buzz Nicki Minaj, Beyoncé "Feelin' Myself' Leaks Before 'The Pinkprint' Release Date|work=Music Times|date=December 11, 2014|access-date=January 29, 2015|last=Menyes|first=Carolyn}}</ref>

==Music video==
On May 18, 2015, the music video premiered exclusively on music streaming service [[Tidal (service)|Tidal]]. It features the two singers in various scenes at [[Coachella Valley Music and Arts Festival|Coachella]] and several other locations. It also features Beyoncé in a [[Nebraska Cornhuskers]] hoodie.<ref name="stereo">{{cite web|url=https://www.stereogum.com/1802518/nicki-minaj-feelin-myself-feat-beyonce-video/video/|title=Nicki Minaj – "Feeling Myself" (Feat. Beyoncé) Video |publisher=[[Stereogum]]. [[Spin Media]]|first=Caitlin|last=White|date=May 18, 2015|access-date=July 25, 2015}}</ref> In June 2015, ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' named "Feeling Myself" video as one of the 10 best music videos of the first half of 2015.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/music/pop/2015-10-best-music-videos-taylor-swift-maroon-5-sia-6605833/|title=Top 10 Best Music Videos of 2015 (so far)|magazine=Billboard|publisher=Prometheus Global Media|first=Jason|last=Lipshutz|date=June 24, 2015|access-date=July 25, 2015}}</ref>

On February 3, 2015, an audio video for the song was uploaded to Minaj's official [[YouTube]] channel, which as of February 2025, has surpassed 150 million views.{{Citation needed|date=July 2021}}

==Reception==

[[Pitchfork (website)|''Pitchfork'']] named the song "Best New Track" of the day, with Molly Beauchemin commenting that "Nicki Minaj is the only woman who can get Beyoncé to do her bidding. When a woman [[Beyoncé (album)|capable of 'stopping the world']] tells you to 'carry on', there's no choice but to bring it."<ref>{{cite web|url=https://pitchfork.com/reviews/tracks/17259-nicki-minaj-feeling-myself-ft-beyonce/|title=Nicki Minaj "Feeling Myself" [ft. Beyoncé]|date=December 17, 2014|last=Beauchemin|first=Molly|work=[[Pitchfork (website)|Pitchfork]]|access-date=January 29, 2015}}</ref> Additionally, ''Pitchfork'' named "Feeling Myself" the 13th best music video of 2015.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://pitchfork.com/features/staff-lists/9763-the-best-music-videos-of-2015/3/|title=Best Music Videos of 2015|date=December 7, 2015 |publisher=Pitchfork Media|access-date=January 14, 2016}}</ref>

Despite not being sent to radio as an official single from the album, "Feeling Myself" peaked at number 39 on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] chart, staying in the top 100 for 20 weeks.  As of December 2017, the song has sold 474,261 copies in the US.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://hiphopdx.com/news/id.34051/title.hip-hop-singles-sales-wiz-khalifa-fetty-wap-twayne |title=Hip Hop Singles Sales: Wiz Khalifa, Fetty Wap, T'Wayne |last=Balfour |first=Jay |website=[[HipHopDX]] |publisher=Cheri Media Group |date=May 30, 2015 |access-date=May 30, 2015}}</ref>
