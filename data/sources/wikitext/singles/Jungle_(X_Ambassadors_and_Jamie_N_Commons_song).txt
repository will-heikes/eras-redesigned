"'''Jungle'''" is a song by American rock band [[X Ambassadors]] and British blues rock singer [[Jamie N Commons]]. It was released as a single on 18 December 2013 by [[Kidinakorner|KIDinaKORNER]] and [[Interscope Records|Interscope]].<ref name="allmusicsingle" /> The track appeared on X Ambassadors' 2014 EP ''[[The Reason (EP)|The Reason]]'' and their 2015 studio album ''[[VHS (album)|VHS]]''.

== Composition ==
"Jungle" has been described as an "amped up" [[alternative rock]]<ref name="billboardstutz">{{cite magazine|last1=Stutz|first1=Colin|title=Jay-Z Gets Into the Game on 'Jungle' Remix in New Beats Commercial (Video)|url=https://www.billboard.com/music/rb-hip-hop/jay-z-gets-into-the-game-on-jungle-remix-in-new-beats-commercial-6121438/|access-date=7 September 2017|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|date=15 June 2014}}</ref> and [[electronic music|electronic]] song,<ref name="pitchforkgoble">{{cite news|last1=Goble|first1=Corban|title=Mixdown: Don't Tread on This Edition|url=https://pitchfork.com/thepitch/379-mixdown-dont-tread-on-this-edition/|access-date=12 September 2017|work=[[Pitchfork (website)|Pitchfork]]|date=20 June 2014}}</ref> as well as a "rock-heavy, upbeat track".<ref name="mtvalexis">{{cite news|last1=Alexis|first1=Nadeska|title=Dr. Dre Welcomes Jay Z To The 'Jungle' With NBA Finals Remix|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/1846969/jayz-jungle-remix-beats-by-dre/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140619223403/http://www.mtv.com/news/1846969/jayz-jungle-remix-beats-by-dre/|url-status=dead|archive-date=June 19, 2014|access-date=7 September 2017|work=[[MTV]]|date=16 June 2014}}</ref> The song features "a drum-beat reminiscent of [[Queen (band)|Queen]]'s '[[We Will Rock You]]{{' "}}.<ref name="billboardvideo" /> Jeff Benjamin of [[Fuse (TV channel)|Fuse]] compared it to an [[Imagine Dragons]] track, stating it "is full of Alex [da Kid]'s booming electronic-rock percussion, but with soulful humming and bluesy belting in place of Dragons singer [[Dan Reynolds (singer)|Dan Reynolds]]' signature shouty approach."<ref name="fusebenjamin" /> "Jungle" was co-written by X Ambassadors, Jamie N Commons, [[Alex da Kid]] and [[Mike Del Rio]]. Alex da Kid and [[Mike Del Rio]] also produced the song.<ref name="allmusicsingle" />

Speaking about the goal of the song in a ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' interview, [[Alex da Kid]] stated, "It was my goal to write and produce an anthemic song that would resonate with anyone that has grown up in or been around underprivileged surroundings."<ref name="billboardvideo" /> In an interview with ''[[The News & Observer]]'', X Ambassadors member Sam Harris commented on the writing process: "I had written a hook for it, and Alex had the beat. I thought he was gonna put a rapper on it, but then he gave it to Jamie, and Jamie wrote these great verses on it."<ref name="newsobslindsey">{{cite news|last1=Lindsey|first1=Craig D.|title=X Ambassadors: Out of the 'Jungle' and into Chapel Hill|url=http://www.newsobserver.com/entertainment/music-news-reviews/article10096985.html|access-date=6 September 2017|work=[[The News & Observer]]|date=16 October 2014}}</ref> According to Sam Harris, the negative experiences that his brother and bandmate Casey Harris had encountered as a blind person after relocating to [[New York City]] served as an inspiration for the song.<ref name="billboardvideo" />

== Release and reception ==
"Jungle" was made available for digital download on 18 December 2013 by [[Kidinakorner|KIDinaKORNER]] and [[Interscope Records]].<ref name="allmusicsingle" />

Jason Warshof of Ithaca.com described the song as a "thumping, gritted-teeth anthem".<ref name="ithacawarshof">{{cite news|last1=Warshof|first1=Jason|title=X Ithacans: Ambassadors rock the State|url=http://www.ithaca.com/entertainment/music/x-ithacans-ambassadors-rock-the-state/article_a76b3b98-1d0a-11e6-b91f-fb4e48584a14.html|access-date=12 September 2017|work=Ithaca.com|date=18 May 2016}}</ref> Harlew Brown of ''[[Spin (magazine)|Spin]]'' called it "a soulful heavy hitter".<ref name="spinbrown">{{cite news|last1=Brown|first1=Harley|title=Star-Studded Beats Commercial Shows World Cup Game Faces|url=https://www.spin.com/2014/06/star-studded-beats-commercial-shows-world-cup-game-faces/|access-date=13 September 2017|work=[[Spin (magazine)|Spin]]|date=6 June 2014}}</ref> Andy Gensler of ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' regarded it as a "pounding, foot-stomping [[blues]] beat", noting that it sounds like "[[John Bonham]] with a shotgun, holding up a guttural-sounding blues vocal."<ref name="billboardiovine" />

== Music video ==
The official music video for the song was exclusively premiered on 16 June 2014 on the website of ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' magazine.<ref name="billboardvideo">{{cite magazine|last1=Brandle|first1=Lars|title=X Ambassadors & Jamie N Commons 'Jungle' Video: Exclusive Premiere|url=https://www.billboard.com/articles/news/6121460/x-ambassadors-jamie-n-commons-jungle-video-exclusive-premiere-alex-da-kid|access-date=6 September 2017|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|date=16 June 2014}}</ref> It was co-directed by Emile Rafael and [[Alex da Kid]]. The video features members of various subcultures in [[Los Angeles]].<ref name="billboardvideo" /> [[AXS (company)|AXS]] contributor William Pankey described it as "a fly-covered, grubby slice of life centered around the piss-stained, impoverished streets of Los Angeles."<ref name="axspankey">{{cite news|last1=Pankey|first1=William|title=Karma in the Jungle: the five best Jamie N Commons songs|url=https://www.axs.com/news/karma-in-the-jungle-the-five-best-jamie-n-commons-songs-42199|access-date=12 September 2017|work=[[AXS (company)|AXS]]|date=26 February 2015}}</ref>

== Live performances ==
X Ambassadors and Jamie N Commons included the song on the setlist for their Into the Jungle U.S. Tour, which lasted from 30 September to 18 October 2014.<ref>{{cite news|title=X Ambassadors and Jamie N Commons announce fall "Into the Jungle U.S. Tour"|url=https://clizbeats.com/x-ambassadors-jamie-n-commons-announce-fall-jungle-u-s-tour-6276/|access-date=13 September 2017|work=Clizbeats|date=22 July 2014}}</ref> On 31 January 2017, X Ambassadors performed "Jungle" alongside "Collider" at the Opening Night celebration of [[Super Bowl LI]] at [[Minute Maid Park]], Houston.<ref name="ajcsuperbowl">{{cite news|last1=Ruggieri|first1=Melissa|title=X Ambassadors mix rock with sports at Super Bowl 2017 'Opening Night'|url=http://music.blog.ajc.com/2017/01/31/x-ambassadors-mix-rock-with-sports-at-super-bowl-51-opening-night/|access-date=12 September 2017|work=[[The Atlanta Journal-Constitution]]|date=1 February 2017|archive-date=13 September 2017|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170913231249/http://music.blog.ajc.com/2017/01/31/x-ambassadors-mix-rock-with-sports-at-super-bowl-51-opening-night/|url-status=dead}}</ref>

== Remixes ==
=== Jay-Z remix ===
On 14 June 2014, a remix version featuring a rap segment by American rapper [[Jay-Z]] debuted during Game 5 of the [[2015 NBA Finals|NBA Finals]] between [[Miami Heat]] and [[San Antonio Spurs]].<ref name="billboardstutz" /> On the same day, the remix was made available exclusively on [[Beats Music]],<ref name="vergepopper">{{cite news|last1=Popper|first1=Ben|title=Beats Music is using big exclusives to battle Spotify, starting with new Jay-Z remix 'Jungle'|url=https://www.theverge.com/2014/6/16/5814102/beats-music-exclusive-jay-z-jungle|access-date=7 September 2017|work=[[The Verge]]|date=16 June 2014}}</ref><ref name="bikosoff">{{cite news|last1=Kosoff|first1=Maya|title=Beats Music Gets An Exclusive Song Featuring Jay-Z Before Spotify Does|url=https://www.businessinsider.com/beats-music-exclusive-jay-z-song-2014-6|access-date=7 September 2017|work=[[Business Insider]]|date=16 June 2014}}</ref><ref name="billboardgensler">{{cite magazine|last1=Gensler|first1=Andy|title=New Jay Z Remix Available Only on Beats Music|url=https://www.billboard.com/biz/articles/news/branding/6121442/new-jay-z-remix-available-only-on-beats-music|access-date=7 September 2017|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|date=15 June 2014}}</ref> before it was released as a single for digital download on 23 June.<ref name="itunesremix" /> The remix was commissioned as part of an advertising campaign by [[Beats Electronics]] for the [[2014 FIFA World Cup]].<ref name="coscoplan" />

In a [[VH1]] interview, X Ambassadors singer Sam Harris commented on the background of the remix version: "Our producer calls me up out of the blue. He's like, 'Hey, guess what? I got Jay-Z to do a remix of "Jungle"!' Came out of nowhere."<ref name="vh1thorpe">{{cite news|last1=Thorpe|first1=Isha|title=X Ambassadors Reveal The Real Story Behind Jay Z's Remix Of "Jungle"|url=https://www.vh1.com/news/s8d7qd/x-ambassadors-jay-z-jungle-remix-interview|access-date=7 September 2017|work=[[VH1]]|date=22 September 2015}}</ref> One of the interludes on X Ambassadors' album ''[[VHS (album)|VHS]]'', titled "Good News on the Remix", features a recording of the conversation between Sam Harris and the producer discussing Jay-Z's remix.<ref name="vh1thorpe" /> The lyrics of the additional verse contain references to Brazilian [[favela]]s, [[religious persecution]], [[Muhammad Ali]] as well as several films, including ''[[A Bronx Tale]]'' (1993), ''[[Training Day]]'' (2001) and ''[[City of God (2002 film)|City of God]]'' (2002).<ref name="coscoplan">{{cite news|last1=Coplan|first1=Chris|title=Listen: Jay Z jumps on "Jungle" remix|url=https://consequence.net/2014/06/listen-jay-z-jumps-on-jungle-remix/|access-date=7 September 2017|work=[[Consequence of Sound]]|date=16 June 2014}}</ref><ref name="rsgrow">{{cite magazine|last1=Grow|first1=Kory|title=Jay Z Adds Sporty Verse to Beats by Dre Ad Song 'Jungle'|url=https://www.rollingstone.com/music/videos/jay-z-adds-sporty-verse-to-beats-by-dre-ad-song-jungle-20140616|access-date=7 September 2017|magazine=[[Rolling Stone]]|date=16 June 2014}}</ref>

Colin Stutz of ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' stated that while the remix "isn't much different from the original", Jay-Z "gets his point across, going nonstop for more than a minute straight", with "a heavy hitting verse".<ref name="billboardstutz" /> According to Colin Joyce of ''[[Spin (magazine)|Spin]]'', the rapper "manages to transform the bombastic single into [a] stadium-sized banger".<ref name="spinjoyce">{{cite news|last1=Joyce|first1=Colin|title=Hear Jay Z Hop on X Ambassadors' Jubilant 'Jungle' for Beats by Dre|url=https://www.spin.com/2014/06/jay-z-x-ambassadors-jamie-n-connors-jungle-remix/|access-date=12 September 2017|work=[[Spin (magazine)|Spin]]|date=16 June 2014}}</ref>

== Usage in media ==
The song was used as the official theme for [[Battleground (2014)|WWE Battleground]] in 2014 and in the emd credits of [[Hitman: Agent 47]] in 2015.

"Jungle" was used in a five-minute promotional video titled "The Game Before the Game" by [[Beats Electronics]], released in June 2014 for the [[2014 FIFA World Cup|FIFA World Cup]] and intended to promote Beats by Dre's redesigned Solo2 headphones. Directed by Nabil Elderkin, the advertisement features, among others, [[Neymar]], [[Cesc Fàbregas]], [[Luis Suárez (Uruguayan footballer)|Luis Suárez]], [[Nicki Minaj]], [[Lil Wayne]], [[LeBron James]] and [[Serena Williams]].<ref name="billboardiovine">{{cite magazine|last1=Gensler|first1=Andy|title=Jimmy Iovine's 'Secret iPod': How 'Jungle' Became the New Beats Song|url=https://www.billboard.com/articles/news/6113708/jimmy-iovines-secret-ipod-how-jungle-became-the-new-beats-song|access-date=13 September 2017|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|date=5 June 2014}}</ref><ref name="adweeknudd">{{cite news|last1=Nudd|first1=Tim|title=Did Beats by Dre Just Out-Nike Nike With This Incredible World Cup Ad?|url=https://www.adweek.com/creativity/did-beats-dre-just-out-nike-nike-incredible-world-cup-ad-158147/|access-date=13 September 2017|work=[[Adweek]]|date=5 June 2014}}</ref><ref name="rsgrowad">{{cite magazine|last1=Grow|first1=Kory|title=Beats by Dre Pairs Soccer's Finest With Nicki Minaj and Lil Wayne|url=https://www.rollingstone.com/music/videos/beats-by-dre-pairs-soccers-finest-with-nicki-minaj-and-lil-wayne-20140606|access-date=13 September 2017|magazine=[[Rolling Stone]]|date=6 June 2014}}</ref> Speaking about the selection of "Jungle" for the campaign, Beats by Dre's chief marketing officer Omar Johnson told ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' magazine, "We had a really huge track from a really huge artist. == Controversy ==
On 25 November 2016, a copyright lawsuit was filed by Songs Music Publishing, LLC in the [[United States District Court for the Middle District of Tennessee]] against German automobile manufacturer [[Porsche]] and the Cramer-Krasselt advertising agency which alleged that one of the company's advertisements for the sports car [[Porsche Boxster/Cayman|Porsche 718 Cayman]] "copies several quantitatively and qualitatively important portions of 'Jungle{{' "}}. According to the complaint, the defendants copied the original drum rhythms, organ stabs and background vocals from the song, which together "negates any suggestion of independent creation".<ref>{{cite news|last1=Hampton|first1=Chris|title=Did Porsche create a fake song to get out of paying X Ambassadors for theirs?|url=http://www.chartattack.com/news/2016/12/06/porsche-x-ambassadors/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161207162432/http://www.chartattack.com/news/2016/12/06/porsche-x-ambassadors/|url-status=usurped|archive-date=December 7, 2016|access-date=12 September 2017|work=[[Chart Attack]]|date=6 December 2016}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|last1=Fordyce|first1=Niall A.|title=Songs Music Publishing v. Porsche, et al. – M.D. Tenn. (2016)|url=http://mcir.usc.edu/inplay/Pages/SongsMusicv.Porsche.html|website=Music Copyright Infringement Resource|publisher=[[USC Gould School of Law]]|access-date=12 September 2017|archive-date=13 September 2017|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170913183334/http://mcir.usc.edu/inplay/Pages/SongsMusicv.Porsche.html|url-status=dead}}</ref>
