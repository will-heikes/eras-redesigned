"'''Sun Daze'''" is a song recorded by American [[country music]] duo [[Florida Georgia Line]]. It is the second single from their second studio album, ''[[Anything Goes (Florida Georgia Line album)|Anything Goes]]'', which was released on October 14, 2014.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.rollingstone.com/music/news/florida-georgia-line-talk-new-single-sun-daze-make-wwe-cameo-20140923 |title=Florida Georgia Line Talk New Single 'Sun Daze,' Make WWE Cameo |publisher=Rolling Stone |date=2014-09-23 |accessdate=2014-09-24}}</ref> The song was written by the duo's members [[Tyler Hubbard]] and [[Brian Kelley]], along with [[Sarah Buxton]], [[Cary Barlowe]] and [[Jesse Frasure]].

==History and content==
Thematically, the song is about a man who plans to participate in various recreational activities, including [[flip cup]], [[sexual intercourse]], and [[substance intoxication]] of [[cannabis]]. ''[[Country Weekly]]'' describes the song as "[[reggae]]-indebted" and said that "While it retains the party friendly vibe of much of FGL's work, the loose, stripped-down production is a bit of a departure for the duo". The group members told the magazine that “It still kinda bumps and it's still got a cool guitar line and it's got the whistles going and it's still groovy. You can't not shake your head to that song. It's just a simple song, man. We just try to be very transparent in the way we write and the way we live. There's nothing better than just kicking back . . . whatever 'Sun Daze' is for that person—staying home, making a drink, playing basketball, whatever it may be.”<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.countryweekly.com/news/florida-georgia-lines-sun-daze-booted-another-song-anything-goes|title=Florida Georgia Line's "Sun Daze" Booted Another Song Off "Anything Goes"|last=Freeman|first=Jon|date=5 November 2014|work=[[Country Weekly]]|accessdate=19 November 2014}}</ref>

In an article for ''[[The Washington Post]]'', Emily Yahr cited the presence of the term "getting laid" and the [[double entendre]] lyric "''I'll sit you up on a kitchen sink / Stick a pink umbrella in your drink''" as examples of increasingly prominent sexual content in country music in the 2010s.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.washingtonpost.com/blogs/style-blog/wp/2014/10/06/jason-aldean-florida-georgia-line-and-the-evolution-of-sex-songs-in-country-music/|title=Jason Aldean, Florida Georgia Line and the evolution of sex songs in country music|last=Yahr|first=Emily|date=6 October 2014|newspaper=[[The Washington Post]]|accessdate=1 December 2014}}</ref>

==Critical reception==
The song received mixed to negative reviews from several critics. Hannah Smith for ''Vinyl Mag'' says "this song [is] incredibly catchy in the worst way. There's nothing wrong with party songs, but there comes a time when an artist needs to evaluate the direction their career is heading. No one wants to hear middle-aged people singing about getting laid and stoned, which this song addresses multiple times."<ref>{{cite web |url=http://vinylmag.org/2014/09/19/florida-georgia-line-sun-daze/ |title=Florida-Georgia Line "Sun Daze" |publisher=Vinyl Mag |date=2014-09-19 |access-date=2014-09-24 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141014150315/http://vinylmag.org/2014/09/19/florida-georgia-line-sun-daze/ |archive-date=2014-10-14 |url-status=usurped }}</ref> Kevin John Coyne of ''Country Universe'' similarly panned the song, giving it a failing grade and writing "listening to “Sun Daze”, I kept thinking back to those parody music videos that used to pop up on ''[[In Living Color]]'' and ''[[Mad TV|MadTV]]''. A comedy writer couldn't mock Florida Georgia Line better than they're mocking themselves here."<ref>{{cite web|last=Coyne|first=Kevin John|title=Single Review: Florida Georgia Line, "Sun Daze" – Country Universe|url=https://www.countryuniverse.net/2014/10/23/single-review-florida-georgia-line-sun-daze/|website=Country Universe|date=October 23, 2014}}</ref> ''[[Time (magazine)|Time]]'' listed it as one of the worst songs of 2014, writing that the song "sounds like what would happen if Florida Georgia Line made a disastrous attempt at writing a song for Miley Cyrus (and then recorded it after Cyrus rejected it for being even too low brow for her)" and that  "“I sit you up on a kitchen sink / stick the pink umbrella in your drink” wins the I Just Threw Up in My Mouth Award for worst innuendo of 2014."<ref>{{cite web|url=https://time.com/collection-post/3613505/top-10-worst-songs/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20241127145856/https://time.com/collection-post/3613505/top-10-worst-songs/|url-status=dead|archive-date=November 27, 2024|title=Top 10 Worst Songs {{!}} TIME|publisher=[[Time (magazine)|Time]]|accessdate=July 28, 2025}}</ref>

Jen Swirsky of ''Country Music Chat'' gave the song a more positive review, writing "Love it or hate it, 'Sun Daze' is witty. Perhaps witty on subjects that may still be a little too liberal for country music, but witty nonetheless."<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cmchatlive.com/florida-georgia-line-releases-sun-daze/ |title=@FLAGALine Releases Envelope Pushing #SunDaze |publisher=Country Music Chat Live |date=2014-09-22 |accessdate=2014-09-24}}</ref> ''[[Country Weekly]]'' reviewer Tammy Ragusa was more moderate, praising the reggae influences of the instrumentation while criticizing the lyrical content. She said that "their continued attempts to establish themselves as bad boys, gratuitously dropping in overt references to getting 'laid' and 'stoned', are beginning to sound more cartoonish." She also wrote that "This is the kind of thing that sells and garners airplay, and 'Sun Daze' will do well for FGL", ultimately grading the song "C+".<ref>{{cite journal|last=Ragusa|first=Tammy|date=6 December 2014|title=Reviews: Florida Georgia Line - 'Sun Daze'|journal=[[Country Weekly]]|volume=21|issue=49|page=66|issn=1074-3235}}</ref>

==Commercial performance==
The single has been a commercial success for the duo, peaking at No. 3 on the ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' [[Hot Country Songs]] chart and No. 1 on the [[Country Airplay]] chart. The song was certified gold by the [[Recording Industry Association of America|RIAA]] on January 29, 2015, and platinum on September 15, 2015.<ref name=RIAA /> As of April 2015, the single has sold 618,000 copies in the United States.<ref name=sales>{{cite news |url=http://roughstock.com/news/2015/04/34491-country-musics-top-30-digital-singles-week-april-22-2015/ |title=Country Music's Top 30 Digital Singles: Week of April 22, 2015|author= Matt Bjorke |date= April 22, 2015 |work=Roughstock }} Sales figure given [https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1MX4sBuNKC6f3cyxGxlcWn6HkDePh8szm8um2jkV6CVE/edit#gid=0 here]</ref>

==Music video==
The music video was directed by [[Marc Klasfeld]] and premiered in October 2014.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cmt.com/videos/florida-georgia-line/1095138/sun-daze.jhtml|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141030083648/http://www.cmt.com/videos/florida-georgia-line/1095138/sun-daze.jhtml|url-status=dead|archive-date=October 30, 2014|title=CMT : Videos : Florida Georgina Line : Sun Daze|publisher=[[Country Music Television]]|accessdate=October 30, 2014}}</ref> Footage from the 2014 installment of [[WWE]] [[Night of Champions (2014)|Night of Champions]] is shown in the video, an event which Florida Georgia Line served as guest commentators for as a means of cross-promotion.
