"'''Slow Down'''" is a song recorded by American singer [[Selena Gomez]] from her debut solo studio album, ''[[Stars Dance]]'' (2013). It was made available for instant digital download along with the pre-order of the album on June 3, 2013. [[Hollywood Records]] later sent the song to [[contemporary hit radio|mainstream radio]] as the second and final single from the album on August 13, 2013. The song was produced by [[The Cataracs]] and David Kuncio, and infuses [[Electronic dance music|EDM]] elements in its instrumentation. Gomez has been quoted by saying the song speaks of slowing down time by stopping the sun's orbit around Earth and living in one magical moment on the dance floor.

[[Music journalism|Music critics]] gave a favorable reception to the song, with some praising its catchiness and fun production. Slow Down has achieved moderate success, peaking inside the top 20 in The Czech Republic, Russia, and South Korea. In the United States, the song peaked at number 27 on the [[Billboard charts|''Billboard'']] [[Billboard Hot 100|Hot 100]], reached [[List of number-one dance singles of 2013 (U.S.)|number-one]] on ''Billboard''{{'}}s [[Dance Club Songs|Hot Dance Club Songs]] chart, and it has been [[RIAA certification|certified Platinum]] by the [[Recording Industry Association of America]] (RIAA) for selling over 1,000,000 units of the single in the country. On the [[Mainstream Top 40]] airplay chart, it reached the position of number 7.

The accompanying music video for "Slow Down" was directed by [[Philip Andelman]] and filmed in Paris, France. It features Gomez singing along to the track inside of a car, partying in a [[nightclub]], and strutting down the streets of the city. Gomez has performed "Slow Down" in a number of live appearances, including on ''[[Good Morning America]]'', ''[[The Tonight Show with Jay Leno]]'', ''[[Late Show with David Letterman]]'', and UK TV show ''[[Surprise Surprise (British TV series)|Surprise, Surprise]]''. It was also performed as the encore of her [[Stars Dance Tour]].

==Critical reception==
Andrew Hampp of ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' called the song "harmless, frothy fun", while complimenting its "pseudo-[[dubstep]] [[pre-chorus]] [[Breakdown (music term)|breakdown]], stretched-out staccato vocal loops and not-too-naughty-for [[Disney]] lyrics ("You know I'm good with [[mouth-to-mouth resuscitation]]")".<ref name=billboardreview>{{cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/review/4347360/selena-gomez-stars-dance-track-by-track-review|title=Selena Gomez, 'Stars Dance': Track-By-Track Review|last=Hampp|first=Andrew|magazine=Billboard|date=July 23, 2013|access-date=November 8, 2013}}</ref> Caryn Ganz of ''[[Rolling Stone]]'' noted that Gomez's producers "finally [gave] her tighter tunes" like "Slow Down", adding that it is "ripe for remixing."<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.rollingstone.com/music/albumreviews/stars-dance-20130723|title=Selena Gomez Enters Adulthood With Her Unfussy Pop Showcase 'Stars Dance'|date=July 23, 2013|access-date=November 8, 2013|magazine=[[Rolling Stone]]}}</ref> Writing for the ''[[Los Angeles Times]]'', August Brown thought the production "Slow Down" is "rooted in today's pop-EDM default mode, but as that stuff goes, [the song] is pretty capable."<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.latimes.com/entertainment/music/la-xpm-2013-jul-22-la-et-ms-selena-gomez-stars-dance-review-story.html|title=Review: Selena Gomez's 'Stars Dance' a sassy pop-EDM mix|last=Brown|first=August|date=July 22, 2013|access-date=November 8, 2013|work=[[Los Angeles Times]]}}</ref> PopDust contributor Christina Drill was critical towards the song, considering it "adequate-but-forgettable", and further stating: "the only thing that separates it from the faceless EDM masses? Selena cooing, 'You know I'm good with mouth to mouth resuscitation' in a way that's almost believable. A total whatever."<ref name=popdust/> Matthew Horton of [[Virgin Media]] deemed it "charmless".<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.virginmedia.com/music/reviews/selena-gomez-stars-dance.php|title=Selena Gomez: Stars Dance Album Review|last=Horton|first=Matthew|work=[[Virgin Media]]|date=July 22, 2013|access-date=November 8, 2013}}</ref> Joseph Atilano of the [[Philippine Daily Inquirer]] stated that "some of the lyrics are a bit suggestive but there is nothing in them that would turn off any of her loyal fans."<ref>{{cite news|url=http://entertainment.inquirer.net/106185/single-review-slow-down-by-selena-gomez|title=Single Review: "Slow Down" by Selena Gomez|newspaper=[[Philippine Daily Inquirer]]|last=Atilano|first=Joseph|date=August 2, 2013|access-date=November 8, 2013}}</ref>

==Chart performance==
After being released for instant digital download with the pre-order of ''Stars Dance'', "Slow Down" [[Record charts|charted]] in several countries, including Australia, where it peaked at number 91, and in Austria, staying on the country's chart for two weeks before peaking at number 71.<ref name=aus/><ref name=aust/> The song has charted for 32 weeks in France, and has so far peaked at number 31, making the song the third entry for Gomez there, after "[[Love You Like A Love Song]]" and "[[Come & Get It (Selena Gomez song)|Come & Get It]]".<ref name=fr/> In the [[Ultratip]] charts from Belgium, it peaked at number 2 in Flanders and number 13 in Wallonia.<ref name=aust/> On the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] in the United States, "Slow Down" peaked at number 27, and has been certified Platinum by the [[Recording Industry Association of America]] (RIAA).<ref name=hot100/> On the [[Mainstream Top 40 (Pop Songs)|Pop Songs]] component chart, the song entered the top 10 and reached the position of number 7.<ref name=pops/> As of July 2015, "Slow Down" has sold 1 million copies in the United States.<ref name=us_sales>{{cite magazine |url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/ask-billboard-is-there-an-unusually-high-number-of-new-acts-in/ |title=Ask Billboard: Are There an Unusually High Number of New Acts in the Hot 100's Top 10? |last=Trust |first=Gary |magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]] |publisher=[[Prometheus Global Media]] |date=July 19, 2015 |access-date=July 19, 2015}}</ref>
