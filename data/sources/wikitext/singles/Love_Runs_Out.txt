"'''Love Runs Out'''" is a song recorded by American [[pop rock]] band [[OneRepublic]] for the 2014 reissue of their third studio album ''[[Native (album)|Native]]'' (2013). The song was produced by frontman [[Ryan Tedder]], who co-wrote it with bandmates [[Brent Kutzle]], [[Drew Brown (musician)|Drew Brown]], [[Zach Filkins]], and [[Eddie Fisher (drummer)|Eddie Fisher]]. "Love Runs Out" was [[digital distribution|digitally]] released in Australia on April 14, 2014, and was serviced to [[contemporary hit radio]] in the United States on May 6 as the fourth overall single from the album.

==Background and composition==
"Love Runs Out" was originally intended to be the first single from ''Native'', but was eventually passed over in favour of "[[If I Lose Myself]]". Tedder told [[Capital (radio network)|Capital FM]], "We have a new single that's not even on the album that's about to drop. I can't quote the date," and "I wanted ["Love Runs Out"] to be the first single, a few of us in the band did, but I could not finish the chorus. And you can't have a song without a chorus."<ref>{{cite web|last=Rigby|first=Sam|title=OneRepublic's new single 'Love Runs Out' leaks online|url=http://www.digitalspy.com/music/news/a564321/onerepublics-new-single-love-runs-out-leaks-online-listen.html|date=April 13, 2014|work=[[Digital Spy]]|publisher=[[Hearst Magazines UK]]|accessdate=April 25, 2014}}</ref>

Sheet music for "Love Runs Out" shows the key of [[G minor]] with a moderate tempo of 120 beats per minute. The vocals span from D<sub>3</sub> to D<sub>5</sub> in the song.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.musicnotes.com/sheetmusic/mtdFPE.asp?ppn=MN0134437|title=OneRepublic "Love Runs Out" Sheet Music - Download & Print|author=Tedder, Ryan|work=musicnotes.com|date=29 April 2014}}</ref>

==Critical reception==
The song received positive reviews from music critics. [[4Music]] called "Love Runs Out" a "spine-tingling track" with "slick production"<ref name="4music">{{cite episode|title=This Week's Fresh Music|series=This Week's Fresh Music|network=[[4Music]]|airdate=19 July 2014}}</ref> and a "stomper".<ref name="4music2">{{cite episode|title=This Week's Fresh Music|series=This Week's Fresh Music|network=[[4Music]]|airdate=25 July 2014}}</ref>

==Commercial performance==
"Love Runs Out" debuted at number 81 on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] and at number 22 on the [[New Zealand Singles Chart]]. The song eventually reached a peak of number 15 on the Hot 100. It reached its million sales mark in the US in August 2014<ref>{{cite web |url=https://music.yahoo.com/blogs/chart-watch/chart-watch-magic-again-blocks-sam-221248035.html |title= Chart Watch: MAGIC! Again Blocks Sam |author=Paul Grein |date=August 13, 2014 |work=Yahoo! Music }}</ref> and by the end of 2014 had sold 1.4 million copies.<ref name="us_sales">{{cite web|url=http://www.hitsdailydouble.com/news/newsPage.cgi?news10225m01 |archive-url=https://archive.today/20141211001027/http://www.hitsdailydouble.com/news/newsPage.cgi?news10225m01 |url-status=dead |archive-date=December 11, 2014 |title=The Big 9 in 2014: Interscope |website=HITS Daily Double |publisher=HITS Digital Ventures |date=December 10, 2014 |accessdate=December 10, 2014 }}</ref>

The song charted even higher in other countries. It reached the top 5 in Austria, Luxembourg, Canada, Germany and the UK, as well as the top 10 in Spain and South Africa. Its success in Germany was tied to the fact that the TV station [[ZDF]] had selected it as its official anthem for the [[2014 FIFA World Cup]].<ref>{{Cite web |title="Love Runs Out" von OneRepublic ist ZDF-WM-Song für die "Bilder des Tages" |url=https://www.presseportal.de/pm/7840/2732403 |access-date=2022-06-04 |website=presseportal.de |date=9 May 2014 |language=de}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=Hier den Song aus den ZDF-WM-Highlights downloaden: OneRepublic mit "Love Runs Out" |url=https://www.universal-music.de/onerepublic/news/hier-den-song-aus-den-zdf-wm-highlights-downloaden-onerepublic-mit-love-runs-out-228604 |access-date=2022-06-04 |website=www.universal-music.de |language=de}}</ref>

==Music video==
A music video was produced for the song and was directed by [[Sophie Muller]]. It cost the group $180,000. The video has references to [[Sade (band)|Sade]]'s video "[[Soldier of Love (Sade song)|Soldier of Love]]", also under Muller's direction.<ref>{{cite episode|title=This Week's Fresh Music Top 20|series=This Week's Fresh Music|network=[[4Music]]|airdate=26 August 2014}}</ref> As of December 2025, it has over 206 million views on [[YouTube]] and is OneRepublic's fifth most viewed music video.

==In other media==
"Love Runs Out" is one of the official theme songs for [[WWE]]'s [[WrestleMania 35]] event in 2019. It was also included in the video game ''[[NBA 2K15]]'', the soundtrack of which curated by [[Pharrell Williams]].<ref>{{cite web | url=https://www.ign.com/wikis/nba-2k15/Soundtrack | title=Soundtrack - NBA 2K15 Guide | date=22 August 2014 }}</ref>
