"'''Dope'''" is a song recorded by American singer [[Lady Gaga]] for her third [[studio album]], ''[[Artpop]]'' (2013). It was released on November 4, 2013, by [[Interscope Records]] as the second promotional single from the record, following "[[Venus (Lady Gaga song)|Venus]]". It was written by Gaga, [[DJ White Shadow|Paul "DJ White Shadow" Blair]], Dino Zisis, and [[Nick Monson]] and produced by Gaga and [[Rick Rubin]]. Following her hip surgery and cancellation of the [[Born This Way Ball]] tour, Gaga became addicted to drugs, which helped her get relief from the pain of surgery and also to cope with her sabbatical. "Dope" was written about this addiction and evolved from a song she had previously composed for her fans, about her confessions. It was added to ''Artpop'' because Gaga felt the album needed something more autobiographical.

The song received mixed reviews, with some critics complimenting its simple production and vocals, and others labelling the lyrics inept. An [[electronic rock]] [[lament]], in the vein of [[Folk music of Ireland|Irish folk]] [[ballad]] [[The Sprig of Thyme|rue]], "Dope" is a bittersweet song centered on a dark theme. It has a minimal production and is only composed off Gaga's vocals sung in an intoxicated slur. Lyrically it talks about [[substance abuse]] and the yearning for a long lost love. Prior to the release, Gaga uploaded an image of herself on [[Instagram]], wearing a [[snapback (hat)|snapback]] featuring the word DOPE. She also posted the song's lyrics there, triggering the social media website's concern. The artwork featured Gaga with a big blazer, her face covered with a hat and a [[grill (jewelry)|grill]] that made her teeth appear larger. Media outlets found the picture to be "odd" and compared it to different horror films.

Gaga performed the song at the [[2013 YouTube Music Awards]], and later on all dates of her residency, [[Lady Gaga Live at Roseland Ballroom]], and her [[ArtRave: The Artpop Ball]] tour (2014). After its release, "Dope" reached the top of the music charts in Hungary and Spain, while attaining top-ten positions in Belgium, France, Greece, Italy and Luxembourg. The [[streaming media|streams]] from the YouTube Music Awards performance helped the song become Gaga's 13th top-ten on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] reaching number eight, as well as her highest charting promotional single to date.

She subsequently had to be on rest for the next six months. "Dope" was recorded at Shangri-La Studios, Malibu, California, by Jason Lader, Ryan Hewitt and Sean Oakley, with Lader also working on the digital editing of the song and playing [[Keyboard instrument|keyboards]] for it.<ref name="liner"/> Bill Malina completed the additional recording for the track at [[Chicago Recording Company|CRC Recording Studios]], [[Chicago, Illinois]] with Steve Faye. The [[audio mixing (recorded music)|mixing]] for "Dope" was performed by Manny Marroquin at Larrabee Sound Studios, North Hollywood, California, with assistance from Chris Galland and Delbert Bowers, additional mixing being done by [[Andrew Scheps]] at Punker-Pad Mobile Studios.<ref name="liner"/> Other [[instrumentation (music)|instrumentation]] for "Dope" included [[piano]] by Gaga and Adam MacDougall at keyboards. Finally, Gene Grimaldi did the [[audio mastering]] at Oasis Mastering Studios in Burbank, California.<ref name="liner"/> According to the [[sheet music]] published at Musicnotes.com, "Dope" is set in the [[time signature]] of common time, with a moderate [[tempo (music)|tempo]] of 66 [[beats per minute]].<ref name="sheet">{{cite web|url=http://www.musicnotes.com/sheetmusic/mtdFPE.asp?ppn=MN0126948|title=Lady Gaga – 'Dope' – Sheet Music|access-date=January 8, 2014|publisher=Musicnotes.com. [[Universal Music Publishing Group]]|url-status=live|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140108092400/http://www.musicnotes.com/sheetmusic/mtdFPE.asp?ppn=MN0126948|archive-date=January 8, 2014|date=November 8, 2013}}</ref> It is composed in the [[key (music)|key]] of [[E-flat major|E{{music|flat}} major]] with Gaga's vocals spanning the tonal nodes of D<sub>3</sub> to E{{music|flat}}<sub>5</sub>. The chorus of the song follows a basic sequence of E{{music|flat}}–B{{music|flat}}–Cm–A{{music|flat}} as its [[chord progression]].<ref name="sheet"/>

According to Sal Cinquemani from ''[[Slant Magazine]]'', "Dope" is an [[electronic rock]] [[lament]], in the vein of [[Folk music of Ireland|Irish folk]] [[ballad]] [[The Sprig of Thyme|rue]].<ref name="slant"/> ''Rolling Stone'' said that "Dope" reveals a "dark mood" of Gaga as she plays the piano and utters the lyrics.<ref name="rsreview"/> She begins the song with intoxicated, [[Slur (music)|slur]]-like vocals and the lines, "I promise this drink is my last one / I know I fucked up again / Because I lost my only friend".<ref name="slant"/> Amy Sciarretto of ''[[PopCrush]]'' described "Dope" as a dramatic, piano-driven power ballad, comparing its melody to Gaga's "[[Hair (Lady Gaga song)|Hair]]" (2011) because of its "epic and contemplative nature". She also likened its combination of piano, powerful vocals and lyrics to [[Rihanna]]'s "[[Stay (Rihanna song)|Stay]]" (2013).<ref>{{cite web |last=Sciarretto |first=Amy |title=Lady Gaga, 'Dope' – Song Review |url=https://popcrush.com/lady-gaga-dope-song-review/ |website=PopCrush |date=November 3, 2013 |access-date=October 2, 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250225133756/https://popcrush.com/lady-gaga-dope-song-review/ |archive-date=February 25, 2025 |url-status=live}}</ref> There is not much [[Instrumentation (music)|instrumentation]] accompanying the singer's vocals, except for piano sounds and distant synths, making Gaga's singing the focal point of "Dope".<ref name="cragg"/> In order to give the production an intimate feeling and make it emotional, Rubin did not apply any [[pitch (music)|pitch]] correction to Gaga's vocals, as noted by [[Jon Pareles]] from ''[[The New York Times]]''.<ref name="nytimes"/>

The lyrics talk about [[substance abuse]] and the yearning for a long lost love more than drugs.<ref name="slant"/> "Just one last puff and two last regrets/Three spirits and 12 lonely steps", Gaga sings, before uttering the [[Hook (music)|hook]], "I need you more than dope".<ref name="slant"/> "Dope" is one of the most personal songs of Gaga, according to Joe Lynch from [[Fuse (TV channel)|Fuse]]. With lines like "I promise this drink is my last one / I know I fucked up again / Because I lost my only friend", Lynch believed that Gaga was referring to her former assistant Jennifer O'Neil who had months ago moved to court against the singer for unpaid wages.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.fuse.tv/videos/2013/11/lady-gaga-addiction-artpop-song|title=Lady Gaga Addresses Addiction on Latest 'ARTPOP' Song "Dope"|last=Lynch|first=Joe|date=November 4, 2013|access-date=November 4, 2013|publisher=[[Fuse (TV channel)|Fuse]]|url-status=live|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131110031835/http://www.fuse.tv/videos/2013/11/lady-gaga-addiction-artpop-song|archive-date=November 10, 2013}}</ref>

The images raised concern from Instagram who felt that Gaga needed help and decided to reach out to her. ==Commercial performance==
"Dope" debuted at number&nbsp;60 on the US [[Hot Digital Songs]] chart with 31,000&nbsp;downloads for the issue dated November 23, 2013. The song also debuted at number eight on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]], becoming the singer's 13th top ten on the chart, as well as her highest charting promotional single to date.<ref name="hot100">{{cite news|url=https://www.billboard.com/articles/news/5785931/lordes-royals-leads-hot-100-lady-gagas-dope-debuts-at-no-8|title=Lorde's 'Royals' Leads Hot 100, Lady Gaga's 'Dope' Debuts At No. 8|last=Trust|first=Gary|date=November 13, 2013|access-date=November 14, 2013|magazine=Billboard|url-status=live|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131116043437/http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/5785931/lordes-royals-leads-hot-100-lady-gagas-dope-debuts-at-no-8|archive-date=November 16, 2013}}</ref> The low sales of "Dope" were due to [[iTunes Store]]'s "Complete My Album" program, where the customers have the option of buying the full album and hence earlier song purchases are deducted from the song's current week's total.<ref>{{cite news|url=http://music.yahoo.com/blogs/music-news/chart-watch-waiting-gaga-033110534.html|title=Chart Watch: Waiting For Gaga|last=Grein|first=Paul|date=November 14, 2013|access-date=November 14, 2013|publisher=[[Yahoo!]]|url-status=live|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131203130259/http://music.yahoo.com/blogs/music-news/chart-watch-waiting-gaga-033110534.html|archive-date=December 3, 2013}}</ref> Despite the low sales, the high debut was fueled by the heavy [[streaming media|streams]] of the song of about 8.2&nbsp;million, and a number one entry on the ''Billboard'' [[Streaming Songs]] chart, which is one of the [[Billboard Hot 100#History|components]] of the Hot 100.<ref name="hot100"/> About 95% of the online streams came from [[YouTube]] views of the live video of Gaga performing "Dope" at the YouTube Music Awards of 2013. Dating to her first top ten single, "[[Just Dance (song)|Just Dance]]" on the issue of December 6, 2008, Gaga's total of 13 top ten songs in that time frame is preceded by [[Taylor Swift]], with 14 songs to her tally, followed by [[Bruno Mars]] and [[Katy Perry]] with 11 songs each.<ref name="hot100"/> The next week, "Dope" tumbled down the Hot 100 to number&nbsp;71.<ref>{{cite news|url=http://music.yahoo.com/blogs/music-news/chart-watch-lorde-other-top-teens-013711041.html|title=Chart Watch: Lorde & Other Top Teens|last=Grein|first=Paul|date=November 21, 2013|access-date=November 21, 2013|publisher=Yahoo!|url-status=live|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131124030139/http://music.yahoo.com/blogs/music-news/chart-watch-lorde-other-top-teens-013711041.html|archive-date=November 24, 2013}}</ref>

"Dope" debuted at number&nbsp;34 on the [[ARIA Charts|Australian Singles Chart]] for the issue dated November 9, 2013.<ref name="aus"/> In New Zealand, "Dope" debuted at number&nbsp;20 on the [[Recorded Music NZ|New Zealand Singles Chart]], for the week of November 11, 2013, and on the [[Irish Singles Chart]] the song entered at number&nbsp;12.<ref name="nz"/><ref name="ireland"/> Following the release of ''Artpop'', "Dope" also debuted on the [[UK Singles Chart]] at number&nbsp;124 with sales of 1,666 copies.<ref name="uk">{{cite news|url=http://www.musicweek.com/businessanalysis/read/official-charts-analysis-lady-gaga-hits-no-1-with-65k-sales/056769|title=Official Charts Analysis: Lady Gaga hits No.1 with 65k sales|last=Jones|first=Alan|date=November 18, 2013|access-date=November 18, 2013|work=[[Music Week]]|url-access=subscription|url-status=live|archive-url=https://archive.wikiwix.com/cache/20131121000000/http://www.musicweek.com/businessanalysis/read/official-charts-analysis-lady-gaga-hits-no-1-with-65k-sales/056769|archive-date=November 21, 2013}}</ref> It has sold 10,218&nbsp;digital downloads in South Korea, as reported by [[Gaon Chart|Gaon]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://gaonchart.co.kr/digital_chart/download.php?nationGbn=E&current_month=08&current_year=2013&chart_Time=month|title=Online download – 2013 Month End Chart – October/November|publisher=Gaon Chart.
