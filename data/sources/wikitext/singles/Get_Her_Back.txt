"'''Get Her Back'''" is a song by American recording artist [[Robin Thicke]]. The song was released on May 19, 2014, via [[Star Trak Entertainment]] and [[Interscope Records]] as the only single from the album ''[[Paula (album)|Paula]]''.<ref name=Huffington>{{cite web|last1=Goodman|first1=Jessica|title=Robin Thicke's New Album Is Reportedly Called 'Paula' In Attempt To Win Back Paula Patton|date=12 June 2014 |url=http://www.huffingtonpost.com/2014/06/12/robin-thicke-new-album-paula_n_5487690.html|publisher=Huffington Post|accessdate=18 June 2014}}</ref><ref name=BillboardPaula>{{cite magazine|last1=Brandle|first1=Lars|title=Robin Thicke Names His Next Album 'Paula' (Report)|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/6114237/robin-thicke-names-his-next-album-paula-report|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|accessdate=18 June 2014|date=12 June 2014}}</ref> The song was written as an attempt to reconcile with his wife, actress [[Paula Patton]], whom he had separated from in February 2014. Thicke performed and premiered the song live at the [[2014 Billboard Music Awards]], which generated attention before and after the awards ceremony. The single peaked at #82 on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]].

==Background and writing==
After nine years of marriage, Thicke and his wife, American actress [[Paula Patton]], separated in February 2014.<ref>{{cite web|author1=TMZ Staff|title=Robin Thicke's Wife Enough Is Enough ... We're Separating|url=http://www.tmz.com/2014/02/24/robin-thicke-paula-patton-separating/|publisher=TMZ|accessdate=17 June 2014|date=24 February 2014}}</ref> Having been together in a relationship since they were teenagers and then marrying in 2005, Thicke made various public attempts to reconcile with his wife, including dedicating concert performances to her.<ref>{{cite web|author1=TMZ Staff|title=Robin Thick Gushes on Stage...I'm Lost Without Paula|url=http://www.tmz.com/2014/02/27/robin-thicke-concert-paula-patton-dedicates-song-lost-without-u-fairfax/|publisher=TMZ|accessdate=17 June 2014|date=28 February 2014}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|title=Robin Thicke Pleads With Paula Patton During Concert...Again!!|url=http://www.tmz.com/2014/03/08/robin-thicke-paula-patton-concert-plea-boring-video-desperate/|publisher=TMZ|accessdate=17 June 2014|date=8 March 2014}}</ref> In May 2014, it was announced that he would premiere a new song at the [[2014 Billboard Music Awards]] titled "Get Her Back". Days before the awards ceremony, [[TMZ]] obtained a copy of the lyrics that seemingly addressed his separation and his desire to get back with Patton.<ref name=TMZ>{{cite web|author1=TMZ Staff|title=Will Beg for Paula Back At Awards....'I Should've Kissed You Longer'|url=http://www.tmz.com/2014/05/15/robin-thicke-paula-patton-billboard-music-awards-new-song/|publisher=TMZ|accessdate=17 June 2014|date=15 May 2014}}</ref> The song was co-written by guitarist and long-time Thicke collaborator, [[Bobby Keyes (guitarist)|Bobby Keyes]].<ref>{{cite web|title=Song Credits for "Get Her Back" by Robin Thicke, Allmusic.com|website=[[AllMusic]] |url=http://www.allmusic.com/album/get-her-back-mw0002676315/credits}}</ref>

The single "Get Her Back" was released on May 19, 2014,<ref name=allmusic-single>{{cite web | url = http://www.allmusic.com/album/get-her-back-mw0002676315 | title= Get Her Back| publisher=[[AllMusic.com]]| accessdate=9 October 2014}}</ref> and Thicke debuted the song that day at the [[Billboard Music Awards]].<ref name=Billboard>{{cite magazine|last1=Stutz|first1=Colin|title=Robin Thicke Debuts 'Get Her Back' at Billboard Music Awards (Video)|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/events/bbma-2014/6091810/robin-thicke-get-her-back-performance-billboard-music-awards|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|accessdate=17 June 2014|date=19 May 2014}}</ref> The song has been described as a "heartfelt" ballad that is dedicated to his estranged wife, Patton. Lyrically, the protagonist expresses regret and sadness over his part in a failed relationship ("I should've kissed you longer/I should've held you stronger") and how he is willing to reconcile and make amends with his lover ("All I wanna do is make it right...I gotta get her, go get her back/I gotta treat her right/I gotta cherish her for life.")<ref name=TMZ/>

==Release==
"Get Her Back" was released as [[Music download|digital download]] to various music digital retailers on May 19, 2014, one day after he premiered the song at the Billboard Music Awards.

==Music video==
The music video for "Get Her Back", directed by [[Jonas Åkerlund]],  premiered on [[Vevo]] on June 23, 2014.<ref name="Robin Thicke - Get Her Back">{{cite web|title=Robin Thicke - Get Her Back|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bz_EqawkmTg|website=YouTube|date=23 June 2014}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|title=Robin Thicke - Get Her Back (Behind The Scenes)|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=o-RWlQxZZqo|website=YouTube|date=25 June 2014}}</ref> Thicke sings directly into the camera for most of the nearly four-minute video, mainly in close shots from the neck up and bare-chested, in front of a black background and appears with a black eye and bloody nose. Interspersed throughout the video are masked and adorned women who fade in and out and flash across the screen. In a series of shots that appear intermittently and momentarily, Thicke can be seen crying, holding his hands on his head while screaming and shaking in apparent frustration, being caressed by the hands of a woman, and making what seems to be a gun with his finger and pointing it against his head.

The utilization of water reoccurs throughout the video. Thicke is shown immersing his head in water while he shakes and yells soundlessly. A bouquet of roses and a pair of heels are plunged into water in separate shots. A woman is seen through ripples of water, moving  her hand across the water's surface and subsequently is seen submerged in the water, thrashing and ostensibly drowning.

The video simultaneously shows a series of text messages seemingly between Thicke and his estranged wife Paula, some of which include ''You drink too much,'' ''I wrote a whole album about you,'' ''I hate myself,'' ''You embarrassed me,'' and ''You're reckless''.<ref>{{cite web|last1=Rivera|first1=Zayda|title=Robin Thicke's 'Get Her Back' music video features text messages that hint at Paula Patton split |url=http://www.nydailynews.com/entertainment/music-arts/robin-thicke-releases-back-music-video-article-1.1840719|work=[[Daily News (New York)|Daily News]] | location = New York City|date=23 June 2014}}</ref> Whether the texts are taken directly from actual textual exchanges between Paula and Robin, were adapted for the music video based on sentiments expressed to Thicke by Paula, or were created for the video is unknown. When asked about the authenticity of the texts, Thicke remained ambiguous, saying: "I think you should keep a little mystery...I think it's important to make something out of it. As an artist, I have to make art. As an entertainer, I want to entertain. And as a human being, this is all I want to talk about."<ref>{{cite interview |last= Thicke |first= Robin|subject-link= Robin Thicke |interviewer= [[Ryan Seacrest]] |title=Robin Thicke on Recording "Paula" album |work= On Air With Ryan Seacrest |date= June 27, 2014 |url=http://www.ryanseacrest.com/2014/06/27/robin-thicke-listening-to-songs-on-paula-can-be-overwhelming/ }}</ref>

Among the final shots in the video, a human heart in a woman's hand quickly flickers by and a close-up of Thicke's hand reveals him wearing his [[wedding ring]]. The final text of the video, "this is just the beginning" (extraneous to the main series of texts), hints to the viewers that there is more content to follow, and more story to be told, as this is the lead single and video from the album.<ref name="Robin Thicke - Get Her Back"/>
