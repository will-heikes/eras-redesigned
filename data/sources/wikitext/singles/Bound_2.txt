"'''Bound 2'''" is a song by American rapper [[Kanye West]], featured as the final track from his sixth studio album, ''[[Yeezus]]'' (2013). It was [[Hip-hop production|produced]] by West and [[Che Pope]], with additional production being handled by Eric Danchick, Noah Goldstein, [[No I.D.]] and [[Mike Dean (record producer)|Mike Dean]]. The song features vocals from American [[Soul music|soul]] singer [[Charlie Wilson (singer)|Charlie Wilson]] and serves as the album's second single. "Bound 2" incorporates [[sampling (music)|samples]] from "Bound" by [[Ponderosa Twins Plus One]] and the lines "Uh-huh, honey" and "Alright" from [[Brenda Lee]]'s "[[Sweet Nothin's]]". The song also interpolates Wee’s "Aeroplane (Reprise)" for the bridge, sung by Charlie Wilson.

"Bound 2" received universal acclaim from [[music journalism|music critics]], who referred to the song as one of the highlights of the album, comparing its [[Soul music|soul]] influenced, [[Sampler (musical instrument)|sample]]-based production to West's debut studio album, ''[[The College Dropout]]'' (2004). The song peaked at number 55 on the [[UK Singles Chart]] and 12 on the US ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' [[Billboard Hot 100|Hot 100]] chart. The song received two nominations at the [[57th Annual Grammy Awards]] for [[Grammy Award for Best Rap Song|Best Rap Song]] and [[Grammy Award for Best Rap/Sung Performance|Best Rap/Sung Performance]]. West performed the song live on ''[[Later... with Jools Holland]]'' and ''[[The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon]]''.

==Background and composition==

On June 1, 2013, West revealed the [[cover art]] of his sixth studio album ''[[Yeezus]]'' and premiered a 13-second preview of "Bound 2".<ref>{{cite web | url=http://www.stereogum.com/1370362/yeezus-cover-revealed/news/ | title=''Yeezus'' Cover Revealed | publisher=[[Stereogum]]. [[Spin Media]] | date=May 31, 2013 | access-date=August 23, 2013 | author=Lapatine, Scott}}</ref> It was one of three songs that were previewed prior to the album's release, the others being "[[New Slaves]]" and "[[Black Skinhead]]".<ref>{{cite magazine | url=http://www.vibe.com/article/listen-kanye-west-releases-bound-snippet | title=Listen: Kanye West Releases 'Bound' Snippet | magazine=[[Vibe (magazine)|Vibe]] | publisher=Spin Media | date=June 1, 2013 | access-date=August 23, 2013 | last=Rogulewski | first=Charley | archive-date=October 24, 2013 | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131024025508/http://www.vibe.com/article/listen-kanye-west-releases-bound-snippet | url-status=live }}</ref>

The instrumental of "Bound 2" is heavily based around [[soul music|soul]] samples, which critics noted as reminiscent of West's production style in his earlier work.<ref name="A.V.Club"/> The song is predominantly built around a sample taken from "Bound", a song by soul group [[Ponderosa Twins Plus One]] from their 1971 album, ''[[2 + 2 + 1 = Ponderosa Twins Plus One]]''.<ref name="MTV">{{cite web | url=http://www.mtv.com/news/1708420/kanye-west-yeezus-sample-ponderosa-twins/ | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140513022555/http://www.mtv.com/news/1708420/kanye-west-yeezus-sample-ponderosa-twins/ | url-status=dead | archive-date=May 13, 2014 | title=Who's Kanye West Sampling In Retro ''Yeezus'' Preview? | publisher=[[MTV News]]. [[Viacom (2005–present)|Viacom]] | date=June 4, 2013 | access-date=August 23, 2013 | last=Montgomery|first=James}}</ref> Interpolations from "Aeroplane (Reprise)", written by Norman Whiteside and performed by Wee, and the lines "Uh-huh, honey" and "All right" from the song "[[Sweet Nothin's]]", written by [[Ronnie Self]] and performed by country singer [[Brenda Lee]], were also used in the song.<ref name="MTV"/><ref name="Yeezus">{{cite AV media notes | url=http://images.modlife.com.s3.amazonaws.com/custom/kanye/YEEZUS-CREDITS.pdf | chapter=Yeezus Credits | format=PDF | title=Yeezus | others=[[Kanye West]] | year=2013 | type=album notes | publisher=[[Roc-A-Fella Records]] | access-date=August 23, 2013 | archive-date=October 3, 2013 | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131003021031/http://images.modlife.com.s3.amazonaws.com/custom/kanye/YEEZUS-CREDITS.pdf | url-status=live }}</ref> Lee reacted to the sampling of her song on the track, saying:

In an interview with ''[[Rolling Stone]]'', [[Rick Rubin]] explained how the song was constructed:
<blockquote>
The whole song was written without that main sample; it was a last-minute thing. The song had kind of a lot of [[Rhythm and blues|R&B music]] in it. And Kanye gave marching orders of "Take out anything you want, but don't add. Just take away." Like, "OK." And before that, we listened to the sample together and thought, "Hmm, maybe there's a way to integrate this into the song." So we started by getting the sample to work in the song, and then taking out as much stuff as we could, and then in the chorus reducing what was this whole musical thing, just this sort of one ugly, distorted bass note. I was thinking like [[Alan Vega]] and [[Suicide (band)|Suicide]], that kind of noise-synth minimal vibe. So that was the idea behind the chorus: Take it from this sort of R&B thing and turn it into this kind of [[post-punk]], edge thing. And the lyrics he wrote were so good and funny. It just turned into a really good record. The sample is so good.<ref>{{cite news|last1=Grow|first1=Kory|title=Rick Rubin: My Life in 21 Songs|url=https://www.rollingstone.com/music/lists/rick-rubin-my-life-in-21-songs-20160211/t-la-rock-and-jazzy-jay-its-yours-1984-20160209|access-date=11 February 2016|publisher=Rolling Stone|archive-date=February 22, 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180222005936/https://www.rollingstone.com/music/lists/rick-rubin-my-life-in-21-songs-20160211/t-la-rock-and-jazzy-jay-its-yours-1984-20160209|url-status=live}}</ref>
</blockquote>

"Bound 2" was released on June 18, 2013, as the tenth and final track on West's sixth studio album ''[[Yeezus]]''.<ref name="allmusic">{{cite web|last=Jeffries|first=David|url=http://www.allmusic.com/album/yeezus-mw0002554883|title=''Yeezus'' – Kanye West|publisher=[[AllMusic]]|access-date=June 19, 2013|url-status=live|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130621043546/http://www.allmusic.com/album/yeezus-mw0002554883|archive-date=June 21, 2013}}</ref> The song was revealed to be the second single released from ''Yeezus'' in August 2013, following "[[Black Skinhead]]".<ref>{{cite web | url=http://idolator.com/7479497/kanye-west-bound-2-single | title=Kanye West Releases 'Bound 2' As A Single To Trick People Into Thinking 'Yeezus' Sounds Like Old Ye | publisher=[[Idolator (website)|Idolator]]. ===Accolades===

Better weird than boring|url=https://www.theguardian.com/commentisfree/2013/nov/20/kanye-west-bound-2-video-weird|access-date=June 6, 2014|newspaper=[[The Guardian]]|date=November 20, 2013}}</ref> By the time that it had reached seven million views on YouTube, the music video's 13,000 likes were outbalanced by the 50,000 dislikes.<ref>{{cite web|last=Coombes|first=Samantha|title=The internet responds to Kanye West's 'Bound 2' video|url=https://www.gigwise.com/features/86288/|access-date=February 8, 2019|publisher=Gigwise|date=November 21, 2013}}</ref>

In an interview with ''[[The Breakfast Club (radio show)|The Breakfast Club]]'' West explained the concept for the video. 
<blockquote> "I wanted to take white trash T-shirts and make it into a video. I wanted it to look as phony as possible, I wanted the clouds to go one direction, the mountains to go one direction, the horses to go over there, cause I want to show you that this is ''[[The Hunger Games]]''. I want to show you that this is the type of imagery that's been presented to all of us. And the only difference is a black dude in the middle of it."<ref>{{cite web|last1=Takeda|first1=Allison|title=KANYE WEST: 'Bound 2' Music Video Was Supposed To Look 'As Phony As Possible'|url=http://www.businessinsider.com/kanye-west-explains-bound-2-music-video-2013-11?IR=T|website=US Weekly|access-date=29 November 2013}}</ref> </blockquote>

On November 25, 2013, "Bound 3", a parody of the music video was released, starring actors [[James Franco]] and [[Seth Rogen]]. The parody is a [[shot-for-shot]] remake, filmed while they were shooting their movie ''[[The Interview]]''.<ref>{{cite news|first=Christie|last=D'Zurilla |url=http://www.latimes.com/entertainment/gossip/la-et-mg-james-franco-seth-rogen-parody-bound-2-kanye-west-kim-kardashian-20131125-story.html#axzz2liTOBUtt |title=James Franco, Seth Rogen parody Kim and Kanye's 'Bound 2' video|newspaper=[[Los Angeles Times]] |date= November 25, 2013|access-date=November 27, 2013}}</ref> Afterwards, Kim Kardashian revealed through Twitter that she and West found the video to be very funny.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.complex.com/pop-culture/2013/11/kanye-kim-loved-bound-2-spoof-bound-3 |title=Kanye West Kim Kardashian Loved Seth Rogen James Franco Bound 2 Spoof|work=Complex|first= Tanya|last=Ghahremani |date=November 26, 2013 |access-date=November 27, 2013}}</ref> On December 11, 2013, the music video was parodied in the ''[[South Park]]'' episode "[[The Hobbit (South Park)|The Hobbit]]", where West is shown trying to convince the world that Kardashian is not a [[hobbit]].<ref>{{cite web|last=Nicholson|first=Max|title=South Park: 'The Hobbit' Review|url=http://www.ign.com/articles/2013/12/12/south-park-the-hobbit-review|publisher=[[IGN]]. [[Ziff Davis]]|date=December 12, 2013|access-date=December 14, 2013}}</ref> On December 21, 2013, the video was parodied on ''[[Saturday Night Live]]'', with cast members portraying the couple having sex while riding a [[Rudolph the Red-Nosed Reindeer|red-nosed reindeer]].<ref>{{cite magazine |url=http://www.vibe.com/article/snl-spoofs-kanye-west-kim-kardashians-bound-2-video|title='SNL' Spoofs Kanye West And Kim Kardashian's 'Bound 2' Video |magazine=Vibe  |date= December 23, 2013|access-date=December 23, 2013}}</ref>

[[Slant Magazine]] listed it 15th on its list of the best music videos of 2013, commenting; "Wherein Kanye continues to dismantle white American iconography, subverting it by placing himself (and his topless reality star of a honey) in the middle of it all. This is Kanye deconstructing [[Marlon Brando]], [[James Dean]], and [[Elvis Presley|Elvis]] all at once while instilling [[Fear of a Black Planet|fear of a black planet]]."<ref>{{cite web |title=The 25 Best Music Videos of 2013 |url=https://www.slantmagazine.com/music/the-25-best-music-videos-of-2013/3/ |website=Slant Magazine |access-date=January 21, 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200121163845/https://www.slantmagazine.com/music/the-25-best-music-videos-of-2013/3/ |archive-date=January 21, 2020 |date=December 23, 2013 |url-status=live}}</ref>

==Lawsuit==
On December 23, 2013, Ricky Spicer of the [[Ponderosa Twins Plus One]] filed a lawsuit against West over the sample of the group's song, "Bound", alleging that he used Spicer's voice without permission.<ref>{{cite news|url=http://www.latimes.com/entertainment/music/posts/la-et-ms-kanye-west-reportedly-sued-over-sample-in-bound-2-20131224-story.html#axzz2oQZ99dUY |title=Kanye West reportedly sued over sample in 'Bound 2' |newspaper=Los Angeles Times |date=December 24, 2013 |access-date=May 23, 2014}}</ref> Spicer asked that West pay him or cease and desist using his vocals. Roc-A-Fella Records, [[Universal Music Group]], [[Island Def Jam]], and [[Rhino Entertainment]] were all named in the lawsuit along with West.<ref>{{cite web|author=C. Vernon Coleman |url=http://www.xxlmag.com/news/2013/12/kanye-west-sued-bound-2-sample/ |title=Kanye West Sued Over "Bound 2" Sample | work=XXL | publisher=Harris Publications |date=December 24, 2013 |access-date=May 11, 2014}}</ref> The suit was settled in May 2015.<ref>[http://pitchfork.com/news/59730-kanye-west-settles-bound-2-sample-lawsuit/ Pitchfork Entertainment, Kanye West Settles "Bound 2" Sample Lawsuit (05.27.2015)]</ref>

R&B/Hip-Hop Songs]] chart upon the release of ''Yeezus''.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/artist/kanye-west/chart-history/hbu/|title=Kanye West – Bound 2 – Chart magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/charts/r-b-hip-hop-songs/2013-07-06|title=Top Hip-Hop Songs / R&B Songs Chart – July 6, 2013|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|date=January 2, 2013|access-date=February 9, 2019|archive-date=December 10,
