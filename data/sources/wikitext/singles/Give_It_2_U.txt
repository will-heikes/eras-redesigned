"'''Give It 2 U'''" is a song by American singer-songwriter [[Robin Thicke]] from his sixth studio album, ''[[Blurred Lines (album)|Blurred Lines]]'' (2013). It was written and produced by [[Dr. Luke]] and [[Cirkut (record producer)|Cirkut]], with additional writing by Thicke, the featured artist [[Kendrick Lamar]], and [[will.i.am]]. Originally titled "Give It to Me", a [[Demo (music)|demo]] version of the song premiered on the [[Radio program|radio show]] ''Sway in the Morning'' on May 2, 2013. Featuring guest vocals from Lamar, it was released as the third single from the album on August 27, 2013. "Give It 2 U" is an uptempo [[hip hop music|hip hop]] and [[electropop]] song with elements of [[dubstep]]. Lyrically, Thicke sings about sexually pleasing a partner.

The song received mixed reviews, with some [[Music journalism|music critics]] praising it as one of the best tracks from ''Blurred Lines'', while others criticized it for its overt sexual lyrics. However, Lamar's verse in the song was lauded by many critics. "Give It 2 U" achieved moderate success on the charts internationally, receiving more success than the prior single "[[For the Rest of My Life]]". The song became a [[top 40]] hit in Australia, Belgium, Canada, Germany, New Zealand, Scotland, the United Kingdom, and the United States. It peaked at number 45 on both of ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'''s Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs and Rhythmic year-end charts.

An accompanying music video for "Give It 2 U" premiered on August 23, 2013. Directed by [[Diane Martel]], the video was commissioned for a remix of the song, featuring Lamar and an additional verse by [[2 Chainz]]. The video portrays Thicke as a referee, as several [[Dance squad|dance teams]] and [[Float (parade)|floats]] surround him on a football field. The video was met with mixed reception. The song was promoted at the [[2013 MTV Video Music Awards]] alongside Lamar, 2 Chainz, and [[Miley Cyrus]], following a highly controversial performance of "[[Blurred Lines (song)|Blurred Lines]]". It was also regularly performed during Thicke's debut headlining tour, the [[Blurred Lines Tour]] (2014).

The song was written and produced by [[Dr. Luke]], and [[Cirkut]], with additional writing by Thicke, [[Kendrick Lamar]] and [[will.i.am]]. It was written in approximately an hour, with Thicke striving to write a carefree song that is "straight to the dancefloor, straight to the sexy".<ref name="vh1">{{cite magazine |last=Graham |first=Mark |date=July 30, 2013 |title=Robin Thicke Breaks Down Blurred Lines, Track-By-Track (Pt. 2 of 2) |url=https://www.vh1.com/news/36yuoy/robin-thicke-blurred-lines-track-by-track-pt-2 |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20221005145322/https://www.vh1.com/news/36yuoy/robin-thicke-blurred-lines-track-by-track-pt-2 |archive-date=October 5, 2022 |access-date=December 17, 2020 |magazine=[[VH1]]}}</ref> When discussing the song, Thicke described it as "very [[Michael Jackson]], [[Prince (musician)|Prince]] but also pretty hip-hop. It’s a big, shiny pop record. It’s fun".<ref name="inq">{{cite magazine |last=Johnson |first=James |date=June 24, 2013 |title=Robin Thicke On What To Expect From His New Album 'Blurred Lines' |url=https://www.inquisitr.com/811680/robin-thicke-on-what-to-expect-from-his-new-album-blurred-lines/ |access-date=December 17, 2020 |website=[[Inquisitr]]}}</ref> Thicke originally reached out to [[2 Chainz]] to record a verse for the song, although his management had also simultaneously sent the song out to [[Kendrick Lamar]].<ref name="gq">{{cite magazine |last=Phili |first=Stelios |date=May 7, 2013 |title=Robin Thicke on That Banned Video, Collaborating with 2 Chainz and Kendrick Lamar, and His New Film |url=https://www.gq.com/story/robin-thicke-interview-blurred-lines-music-video-collaborating-with-2-chainz-and-kendrick-lamar-mercy |access-date=December 17, 2020 |magazine=[[GQ]]}}</ref>  Both rappers expressed interest in appearing on the song, resulting in a [[Demo (music)|demo]] entitled "Give It to Me".<ref name=gq/><ref name=spin/> On May 2, 2013, Thicke premiered this demo on the radio show ''Sway in the Morning''.<ref name="spin">{{cite magazine |last=Sargent |first=Jordan |date=May 2, 2013 |title=Robin Thicke Goes Dubstep for Will.i.am-Produced 'Give It to Me' |url=https://www.spin.com/2013/05/robin-thicke-give-it-to-me-kendrick-lamar-2-chainz/ |access-date=December 16, 2020 |magazine=[[Spin (magazine)|Spin]]}}</ref> However, this version of the song was later relegated to a remix on the deluxe edition of ''[[Blurred Lines (album)|Blurred Lines]]'' (2013), with the album version only featuring Lamar.

"Give It 2 U" was recorded at Luke's in the Boo, [[Record Plant|Record Plant Studios]], and TDE Red Room Studios in Malibu, Los Angeles, and Carson, respectively.<ref name="EU"/>

==Composition and remixes==
"Give It 2 U" is an [[electropop]] and [[hip hop music|hip hop]] song featuring elements of [[dubstep]].

Lyrically, the song discusses Thicke's desire to sexually please a partner. Speaking on the lyric "Big dick for ya, let me give it to ya", Thicke explained that it was "more a comment of swagger. Like, I'm big-dick swingin'".<ref>{{Cite web |last=Rapkin |first=Mickey |date=October 24, 2013 |title=Robin Thicke Is Red Hot |url=https://www.elle.com/pop-culture/celebrities/robin-thicke-interview |access-date=October 20, 2021 |website=Elle.com}}</ref>

A remix version for the song, featuring an additional verse from [[2 Chainz]], was made available as one of the bonus tracks on the deluxe edition of ''[[Blurred Lines (album)|Blurred Lines]]'' (2013). Another version, the "Norman Doray & Rob Adans Remix", was released in 2013.

==Critical reception==
Rob Tannenbaum of ''[[Rolling Stone]]'' praised the song, citing it as one of the parent album's "cheeky impersonations of dubstep".<ref name="rollingstone">{{cite magazine |last=Tannenbaum |first=Rob |title=Robin Thicke, 'Blurred Lines' |url=https://www.rollingstone.com/music/albumreviews/blurred-lines-20130730 |access-date=July 22, 2013 |magazine=Rolling Stone |publisher=[[Jann Wenner|Wenner Media LLC]] |page=70 |volume=August 1, 2013 |issue=1188 |issn=0035-791X}}</ref> Andrew Chan of ''[[Slant Magazine]]'' criticized the sexual lyrics of the song, citing it as a low point on the album.<ref name="slant">{{cite magazine |last=Chan |first=Andrew |date=July 23, 2013 |title=Robin Thicke: Blurred Lines |url=http://www.slantmagazine.com/music/review/robin-thicke-blurred-lines |access-date=July 24, 2013 |magazine=[[Slant Magazine]]}}</ref> Sophie Schillaci of ''[[The Hollywood Reporter]]'' criticized the song's sexual lyrical content, claiming that "In the wake of Robin Thicke’s “[[Blurred Lines (song)|Blurred Lines]]” controversy (and wild success), the singer is giving his critics some fresh ammo".<ref>{{cite magazine |last=Schillaci |first=Sophie |date=July 2, 2013 |title=Robin Thicke Debuts Controversial New Song in the Wake of 'Blurred Lines' Criticism |url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/earshot/robin-thicke-debuts-controversial-new-578646 |access-date=December 18, 2020 |magazine=[[The Hollywood Reporter]]}}</ref>

==Chart performance==
In the United States, "Give It 2 U" debuted at number 83 on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] chart for the issue dated September 7, 2013.<ref name="hot100"/> The song spent a total of 12 weeks on the chart, peaking at number 25 for the issue dated September 14, 2013.<ref name="hot100"/> On the [[Dance Club Songs]] and [[Mainstream Top 40]] charts, the song peaked at number 21 and 13, respectively.<ref name="club"/><ref name="popsongs"/> The song reached the top ten on both the [[Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs]] and [[Rhythmic (chart)|Rhythmic]] charts, peaking at number 7 and 6, respectively.<ref name="hip"/><ref name="rhythmic"/> It also reached the number 45 position on the year-end versions of both charts.<ref name="hipye"/><ref name="rhythmicye"/>

==Music video==
===Background===
The music video was directed by [[Diane Martel]] in Los Angeles, California and premiered on August 23, 2013, on [[MTV]].<ref name="MTVN">{{cite news |date=August 23, 2013 |title=Robin Thicke Hits the Football Field with Kendrick Lamar for 'Give It 2 U' Video |url=http://www.mtv.com/news/1712906/robin-thicke-give-it-2-u-video-teaser/ |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151114083147/http://www.mtv.com/news/1712906/robin-thicke-give-it-2-u-video-teaser/ |archive-date=November 14, 2015 |access-date=December 15, 2020 |publisher=MTV News}}</ref> Rather than the album version, the video was produced for the remix of "Give It 2 U" featuring both [[Kendrick Lamar]] and [[2 Chainz]]. It features appearances by three models including Alana Greszatai, Jessica Strother, and Mallory Llewellyn, all of whom are termed the "Luxury Girls" in the video.<ref name="en">{{cite news |date=August 28, 2013 |title=Meet Robin Thicke's "Give It 2 U" Video Girls (and Find Out What They Thought of Miley's VMA Performance!) |url=https://www.eonline.com/news/453051/meet-robin-thicke-s-give-it-2-u-video-girls-and-find-out-what-they-thought-of-miley-s-vma-performance |access-date=December 15, 2020 |publisher=E! Online}}</ref> Three American [[Dance squad|dance teams]], including The Passionettes, The Stingettes, and The Baker Girls, also made an appearance in the video.<ref name="en"/> Before its release, [[MTV News]] reported that it was set on a football field, noting that "Thicke and Lamar [are] riding around on mini trucks with women on their laps".<ref name="MTVN"/> In the video, Thicke wore gold-rimmed sunglasses and a golden whistle, while alternating between a black suit jacket and a black-and-white striped suit, with the latter in reference to the referee outfit he wore during his performance at the [[2013 MTV Video Music Awards]].<ref name="nydn">{{cite news |date=August 27, 2013 |title=Robin Thicke debuts 'Give It 2 U' music video after VMAs performance with Miley Cyrus |url=https://www.nydailynews.com/entertainment/music-arts/robin-thicke-debuts-give-2-u-music-video-raunchy-vmas-performance-miley-cyrus-article-1.1438164 |access-date=December 15, 2020 |work=New York Daily News}}</ref> When speaking on working with Martel, Thicke said,
<blockquote>
It's a Diane Martel dream, and I'm just happy to be here. The thing about Diane Martel that makes it so amazing — besides just being a crazy genius — is that she combines all different parts of the world into one soup. She takes the cotton candy, she takes the beautiful football-playing girls and she mixes it all up into one beautiful pot. So we're all very lucky to have her and excited to be working with her again<ref name="MTVN"/>
</blockquote>

===Synopsis and reception===
The video is set on a football field and features a cameo by Thicke's son, Julian Fuego, as well as college dance teams from [[Albany State University]] and [[Alabama State University]]. Several dancers called "Luxury Girls" are shown dressed up like bottles of champagne, rolls of $100 bills and tins of caviar with others holding up [[Paper Mache]] models of lobsters, sushi and other luxurious foods. 2 Chainz enters the field leading a parade float labeled the "Ass Float" while Kendrick enters on a miniature car. The video ends with every girl dancing to the song with Robin Thicke.

Carrie Battan of ''[[Pitchfork (website)|Pitchfork]]'' praised the video, referring to it as "valiant effort to top the mania induced by 'Blurred Lines'".<ref>{{Cite web |date=August 24, 2013 |title=Video: Robin Thicke, Kendrick Lamar, and 2 Chainz: "Give It 2 U" |url=https://pitchfork.com/news/52020-video-robin-thicke-kendrick-lamar-and-2-chainz-give-it-2-u/ |access-date=October 20, 2021 |website=Pitchfork.com}}</ref> Lacey Seidman of [[VH1]] described the video a "delight", commenting that Thicke "unleashes an arsenal of fun foolishness in the Diane Martel-directed video".<ref>{{Cite web |last=Seidman |first=Lacey |date=August 23, 2013 |title=Robin Thicke, 2 Chainz + Kendrick Lamar Delight In A Wacky Pep Rally |url=https://www.vh1.com/news/g7ea35/robin-thicke-give-it-2-u-pep-rally |access-date=October 20, 2021 |publisher=[[VH1]]}}</ref>

==Live performances==
Thicke performed the song at the [[2013 MTV Video Music Awards]] in a [[Medley (music)|medley]] alongside "[[Blurred Lines (song)|Blurred Lines]]" and [[Miley Cyrus]]'s "[[We Can't Stop]]".<ref name="RS2">{{cite news |date=August 22, 2014 |title=Twerk It Out: Miley and Robin's VMA Performance, One Year Later |url=https://www.rollingstone.com/culture/culture-news/twerk-it-out-miley-and-robins-vma-performance-one-year-later-65286/ |access-date=December 18, 2020 |publisher=Rolling Stone}}</ref> [[Kendrick Lamar]] and [[2 Chainz]] accompanied Thicke, performing their verses from the remixed version of the song.<ref name="vmas">{{cite news |date=August 25, 2013 |title=Miley Cyrus Twerks, Gives Robin Thicke Some Tongue at VMAS |url=http://www.mtv.com/news/1713017/miley-cyrus-robin-thicke-vma-twerk/ |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140510081349/http://www.mtv.com/news/1713017/miley-cyrus-robin-thicke-vma-twerk/ |archive-date=May 10, 2014 |access-date=December 18, 2020 |publisher=MTV News}}</ref> Phillip Mlynar of [[MTV News]] described the performance as having a "football referee–themed vibe", as Thicke donned a black-and-white stripped suit with cheerleader-themed dancers surrounding him.<ref name="vmas"/> "Give It 2 U" was regularly performed as the opening number on Thicke's debut headlining tour, the [[Blurred Lines Tour]] (2014).<ref>{{cite news |last1=Stevenson |first1=Jane |date=July 26, 2014 |title=Robin Thicke makes up for lost time at Casino Rama |url=http://www.torontosun.com/2014/07/26/robin-thicke-at-casino-rama |access-date=December 18, 2020 |newspaper=[[Toronto Sun]]}}</ref>
