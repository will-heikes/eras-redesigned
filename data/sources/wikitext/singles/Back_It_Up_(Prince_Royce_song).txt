"'''Back It Up'''" is a 2015 song by American singer [[Prince Royce]], featuring [[Jennifer Lopez]] and [[Pitbull (rapper)|Pitbull]]. The song was released on May 4, 2015, as the second single taken from Royce's fourth studio album, ''[[Double Vision (Prince Royce album)|Double Vision]]''.<ref>{{Cite web | url=https://music.apple.com/au/album/back-it-up-feat-jennifer/992982358 |title = Back It up (Feat. Jennifer Lopez & Pitbull) &#91;Spanish Version&#93; - Single by Prince Royce| website=[[iTunes]] }}</ref>

There are three versions available, a Spanglish version and a Spanish version, with both making chart appearances, and the original 
English version, featuring only Pitbull.<ref>{{Cite web|url= https://music.apple.com/us/album/back-it-up-feat-pitbull-single/989870542 |title=Back It Up (feat. Pitbull) - Single by Prince Royce |publisher=US iTunes Store|date=4 May 2015|access-date=5 August 2015}}</ref>

"Back It Up" marks as the eighth of nine collaborations between Lopez and [[Pitbull (rapper)|Pitbull]], in lined with "[[Fresh Out the Oven]]" in 2009, "[[On the Floor]]" in 2011, "[[Dance Again]]" in 2012, "[[Live It Up (Jennifer Lopez song)|Live It Up]]" in 2013, "[[We Are One (Ole Ola)]]" in 2014 with  [[Claudia Leitte]], "[[Booty (Jennifer Lopez song)|Booty]]" in 2014, a song entitled "Drinks for You (Ladies Anthem)" from Pitbull's ''[[Global Warming (Pitbull album)|Global Warming]]'' album in 2012 and followed by "Sexy Body" off his album ''[[Climate Change (Pitbull album)|Climate Change]]'' released in 2017.

==Music video==
The music video was filmed in [[Miami]] and directed by Colin Tilley. It was released on June 9, 2015<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9w9dXWU5nMI|title=Prince Royce – Back It Up (Official Video) ft. Jennifer Lopez, Pitbull|publisher=YouTube| access-date=July 24, 2015}}</ref> The Spanglish version (included as the Album version) was used for the music video.

==Promotion==
Prince Royce performed the song live on ''[[American Idol (season 14)|American Idol]]'', along with [[Jennifer Lopez]] and [[Pitbull (rapper)|Pitbull]], on 13 May, ''[[Good Morning America]]'' on 27 July and ''[[The View (U.S. TV series)|The View]]'' on 28 July.

==Chart performance==
The Spanish-language version of "Back It Up" debuted on the US Hot Latin Songs chart at number 27 for the week of May 30, 2015. Total sales for the week were 23,000 downloads.<ref>{{Cite web|url= https://www.billboard.com/pro/prince-royce-back-it-up-hot-latin-songs-debut/|title= Prince Royce's 'Back It Up' Debuts on Hot Latin Songs Chart|publisher= www.billboard.com|author= Amaya Mendizabal|date=21 May 2015|access-date=24 July 2015}}</ref>
