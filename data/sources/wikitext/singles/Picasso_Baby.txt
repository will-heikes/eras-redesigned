"'''Picasso Baby'''" is a song by American hip hop artist [[Jay-Z]] from his twelfth studio album ''[[Magna Carta... Holy Grail]]''. It is the second track on the album and features additional vocals by [[The-Dream]] and [[Jean Reno#Personal life|Zofia Borucka Moreno]]. The song was produced by [[Timbaland]] and [[Jerome "J-Roc" Harmon]] and contains a sample of "Sirens" by [[Adrian Younge]]. Following the release of the album, the song peaked at number 91 on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] based on downloads only.

== Background and recording ==
After collaborating on ''[[The Blueprint 3]]'' in 2009, [[Timbaland]] and [[Jay-Z]] parted ways due to creative differences, but reunited again while working with [[Justin Timberlake]] on ''[[The 20/20 Experience]]''. When [[Beyoncé Knowles]] started working on her next studio album in late 2012, Jay-Z invited Timbaland to handle some of the production on it. While both in the studio, Timbaland played Jay the instrumental for "Picasso Baby". At the time, Jay-Z didn't have plans to record a whole album, but after they made the song, the two decided to "go for it". In an interview with [[BBC Radio 1]], Jay-Z stated that "Picasso Baby" "built the sound of" ''[[Magna Carta... Holy Grail]]'' and Timbaland ended up handling most of the production on the album. According to Jay-Z, while the two have recorded many songs in the past, it was not until ''MCHG'' that they got "the chemistry" to collaborate for a whole project.<ref name=bbc>{{cite web|title=Jay-Z. Zane Lowe. Part 1: Magna Carta Holy Grail| date=15 July 2013 |url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fzr6MY664l4|publisher=BBC Radio 1|access-date=2013-07-16}}</ref>

Producer [[Jerome "J-Roc" Harmon|J-Roc]] described "Picasso Baby" as "just New York". He told [[XXL (magazine)|XXL]] that he and Timbaland "were really just feeling the city, the energy that it gives out" and were inspired by the "numerous different cultures and different dialects or sounds."<ref name=xxl-5p>{{cite web|title='Magna Carta Holy Grail' Behind The Scenes: Five Producers Who Helped Craft The Album|date=11 July 2013 |url=http://www.xxlmag.com/news/2013/07/magna-carta-holy-grail-behind-the-scenes-five-producers-who-helped-craft-the-album/|publisher=XXL|access-date=2013-07-16}}</ref>

[[Adrian Younge]], whose song "Sirens" was sampled to create the instrumental, commented that he was "very happy [with how it came out]." Despite usually being critical of how people sample his music, Young thought that "especially for 'Picasso Baby', they literally just looped the top, and it worked. Sometimes you just do a loop and it just works."<ref name="xxl-5p" />

== Composition ==
"Picasso Baby" makes many references to popular artists such as [[Pablo Picasso]], [[Mark Rothko]], [[Jeff Koons]], [[Francis Bacon (artist)|Francis Bacon]], [[Jean-Michel Basquiat]], and [[Andy Warhol]], as well as world-famous painting ''[[Mona Lisa]]''.<ref name="guardian">{{cite news|title=Jay-Z performs Picasso Baby for six hours in New York gallery|url=https://www.theguardian.com/music/2013/jul/11/jay-z-picasso-baby-new-york|newspaper=The Guardian|access-date=2013-07-18|location=London|first=Sean|last=Michaels|date=2013-07-11}}</ref>

==Music video==
The music video for the song, titled "Picasso Baby: A Performance Art Film" was shot on July 10, 2013, at the [[Pace Gallery]] in [[New York City]]. The video, directed by [[Mark Romanek]], was inspired by the work of performance artist [[Marina Abramović]] and in particular by her 2010 installation "The Artist Is Present". In the video, Jay-Z is on a central stage and people queue to get the chance of having him perform the song with them. The shoot was attended by director [[Judd Apatow]], rapper [[Wale (rapper)|Wale]], ''[[Performa]]'' Director and Chief Curator [[RoseLee Goldberg]], as well as artists [[Laurie Simmons]], [[Rashid Johnson]], [[Jerry Saltz]], [[Marilyn Minter]], [[Fred Wilson (artist)|Fred Wilson]], [[Jacolby Satterwhite]], [[Glenn O'Brien]], [[Andres Serrano]], [[Lawrence Weiner]], and Marina Abramović herself, among many other "important figures from the gallery and sales side of fine art."<ref name="guardian" /> On August 2, 2013, the music video premiered on [[HBO]] at 11 p.m. EST.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://rapradar.com/2013/08/02/new-video-jay-z-picasso-baby/ |title=New Video: Jay-Z "Picasso Baby" |publisher=Rap Radar |date=2013-08-02 |access-date=2013-08-03}}</ref>

''Picasso Baby: A Performance Art Film'' was nominated for Best Music Video at the [[56th Annual Grammy Awards]], where it lost to the music video for Jay-Z's collaboration with [[Justin Timberlake]], "[[Suit & Tie]]."<ref>{{cite news|title=56th Annual GRAMMY Awards Nominees|newspaper=Grammy.com |url=http://www.grammy.com/nominees|access-date=7 December 2013}}</ref>

===Controversy with Abramović===
After her involvement in "Picasso Baby: A Performance Art Film", Abramović told in an interview that the rapper had used her. She claimed Jay Z never fulfilled his promise to make a donation to the [[Marina Abramović Institute]] in return of her grant to use "The Artist is Present" concept and her own appearance in the video. Jay Z then replied he had made the donation. In the end, the Institute issued a statement apologizing to both the rapper and Abramović because they never informed the performance artist of the donation.<ref>{{cite news|last1=Gordon|first1=Jeremy|title=Marina Abramovic Says "Cruel" Jay Z "Completely Used" Her for "Picasso Baby" Stunt|url=https://pitchfork.com/news/59629-marina-abramovic-says-cruel-jay-z-completely-used-her-for-picasso-baby-stunt/|access-date=18 October 2017|work=Pitchfork|date=19 May 2015}}</ref><ref>{{cite news|last1=Boucher|first1=Brian|title=Jay Z Defends Himself Against Marina Abramović Accusation with Proof of Donation|url=https://news.artnet.com/art-world/jay-z-defends-marina-abramovic-accusation-proof-donation-300413|access-date=18 October 2017|work=Artnet News|date=20 May 2015}}</ref><ref>{{cite news|last1=Minsker|first1=Evan|title=Marina Abramović Institute Issues Apology to Jay Z|url=https://pitchfork.com/news/59649-marina-abramovic-institute-issues-apology-to-jay-z/|access-date=18 October 2017|work=Pitchfork|date=20 May 2015}}</ref>

== Critical reception ==
''[[Rolling Stone]]'' ranked "Picasso Baby" at number 19, on their list of the 100 Best Songs of 2013, calling it "the standout track from Jay Z's lackluster ''Magna Carta... Holy Grail''".<ref>{{cite magazine|title=Jay Z, "Picasso Baby"|url=https://www.rollingstone.com/music/lists/100-best-songs-of-2013-20131204/jay-z-picasso-baby-19691231|magazine=[[Rolling Stone]]|date=4 December 2013 |access-date=2013-12-07}}</ref> [[NPR]] also listed "Picasso Baby" as one of their 100 favorite songs of 2013, praising in particular the production work of [[Timbaland]].<ref>{{cite web|title=NPR Music's 100 Favorite Songs of 2013|url=https://www.npr.org/blogs/bestmusic2013/2013/12/11/249734685/npr-musics-100-favorite-songs-of-2013|publisher=National Public Radio|access-date=3 February 2014}}</ref>
