"'''The Way'''" is the debut single by American singer-songwriter [[Ariana Grande]] featuring American rapper [[Mac Miller]]. It was released on March 26, 2013 by [[Republic Records]] as the lead single from Grande's debut studio album, [[Yours Truly (Ariana Grande album)|''Yours Truly'']] (2013).<ref>{{cite web | last=Anderson | first=Trevor | title=Ariana Grande's Biggest Billboard Hot 100 Hits | website=Billboard | date=June 26, 2025 | url=https://www.billboard.com/lists/ariana-grande-biggest-hits-hot-100/ | access-date=May 15, 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web | last=Kaufman | first=Gil | title=Ariana Grande Celebrates 10th Anniversary of 'The Way' Collab With Mac Miller: 'I Love You' | website=Billboard | date=March 28, 2023 | url=https://www.billboard.com/music/pop/ariana-grande-celebrates-10th-anniversary-the-way-collab-mac-miller-1235293736/ | access-date=May 15, 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web | last=Lipshutz | first=Jason | title=5 Reasons Why Ariana Grande Became The Queen of the Hot 100 No. 1 Debut | website=Billboard | date=November 9, 2020 | url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/ariana-grande-no-1-debut-hot-100-analysis/ | access-date=May 15, 2026}}</ref> The song was written by [[Sevyn Streeter|Amber Streeter]], [[Al Sherrod Lambert]], [[Jordin Sparks]], [[Brenda Russell]], Miller, and the producer [[Harmony Samuels]]. The song samples [[Big Pun]]'s 1998 song "[[Still Not a Player]]", which itself samples Russell's 1979 song "A Little Bit of Love".

"The Way" received positive reviews from music critics for its 2000's R&B sound and Grande's vocals, which drew comparisons to those of [[Mariah Carey]]. The song became Grande's breakthrough hit and peaked at number nine on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]], earning both Grande and Miller their first top-ten song on the chart. "The Way" has since sold 2.4 million copies in the United States as of 2020 and was certified [[RIAA certification|sextuple platinum]] by the [[Recording Industry Association of America]] (RIAA) for surpassing six million units. An accompanying [[music video]] was directed by Jones Crow and released on March 28, 2013. It depicts Grande and Miller dancing inside a room covered in balloons, as Miller takes several photographs of Grande. A [[Spanglish]] version was also released featuring Miller and Colombian rapper [[J Balvin]].

==Background and development==
[[File:Mac Miller Governors Ball.jpg|left|thumb|upright=0.7|Rapper [[Mac Miller]] was featured on "The Way".]]
On December 12, 2011, Grande released the promotional single "[[Put Your Hearts Up]]", which is a [[bubblegum pop]] song with a vastly different style of music and target demographic than "The Way."<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.huffingtonpost.com/2011/12/12/ariana-grande-debuts-new-_n_1143349.html|title=Ariana Grande Debuts New Single, 'Put Your Hearts Up'|website=[[HuffPost|The Huffington Post]]|date=December 12, 2011|access-date=August 12, 2021|archive-date=June 3, 2019|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190603050225/https://www.huffpost.com/entry/ariana-grande-debuts-new-_n_1143349|url-status=live}}</ref> Whereas the lyrics in "The Way" deal with the subject of a flirty, romantic relationship, some sections with sexual implications, "Put Your Hearts Up" was a song aimed at young audiences, with lyrics about making the world a better place. During a radio interview on KIIS-FM on March 25, 2013 about her debut single, Grande confessed that she had in fact "hated" both the music video for and the song itself, considering it to be "a terrible first impression". She added that she had wanted to "pretend it never happened before it already happened" and that she had felt no enthusiasm to promote the song at the time.<ref>{{cite web|last=Grande|first=Ariana|title=Ariana Grande on KIIS-FM|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CuTS9Wf-PU8|publisher=Youtube|access-date=July 26, 2016|archive-date=December 8, 2019|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20191208220727/https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CuTS9Wf-PU8|url-status=live}}</ref> In a 2013 interview with ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'', Grande implied that her music needed to relate to her maturing personality. Although she insisted on being "there for [her] younger fans" while portraying [[Cat Valentine (Victorious)|Cat Valentine]] on the American [[teen sitcom]] ''[[Sam & Cat]]'' (2013), Grande also wanted to perform music as a result of "growing up".<ref name="Billboard interview March 2013">{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/music/music-news/ariana-grande-talks-breakout-hit-the-way-watch-new-music-video-1554921/|title=Ariana Grande Talks Breakout Hit 'The Way': Watch New Music Video|last=Lipshutz|first=Jason|magazine=Billboard|date=March 28, 2013|access-date=March 25, 2020|archive-date=March 31, 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130331004742/http://www.billboard.com/articles/columns/pop-shop/1554921/ariana-grande-talks-breakout-hit-the-way-watch-new-music-video|url-status=live}}</ref>
[[File:Jordin Sparks at the 2012 USO Gala in Washington D.C., 2012.jpg |thumb|right|250px|"The Way" was initially co-written by [[Jordin Sparks]] (pictured) for her third album, but gave it to Grande.]]

In January 2013, she met with producer [[Harmony Samuels]], at which point he presented her with a demo of the song that featured the vocals of co-writer [[Jordin Sparks]], whom the song was originally intended.<ref>{{cite web|title=Ariana Grande radio interview 3/25/13|url=https://www.youtube.com/CuTS9Wf-PU8?t=6m34s|website=Youtube}}</ref> The beat he had created for the single, samples [[Brenda Russell]]'s "[[Brenda Russell (album)|A Little Bit of Love]]" either directly or indirectly, via [[Big Pun]]'s "[[Still Not a Player]]".<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.spin.com/2013/09/ariana-grandes-yours-truly-a-sweet-sweet-fantasy-of-90s-indebted-hip-hop-and-rb/|title=Ariana Grande's 'Yours Truly': A Sweet, Sweet Fantasy of '90s-Indebted Hip-Hop and R&B|last=Soderberg|first=Brandon|website=[[Spin (magazine)|Spin]]|date=September 13, 2013|access-date=June 7, 2020|archive-date=August 8, 2020|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200808062757/https://www.spin.com/2013/09/ariana-grandes-yours-truly-a-sweet-sweet-fantasy-of-90s-indebted-hip-hop-and-rb/|url-status=live}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1704437/mac-miller-ariana-grande-the-way.jhtml|title=Mac Miller Gives Ariana Grande "Butterflies" On 'The Way'|last=Vena|first=Jocelyn|publisher=[[MTV News]]|date=March 27, 2013|access-date=June 7, 2020|archive-date=December 3, 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131203053716/http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1704437/mac-miller-ariana-grande-the-way.jhtml|url-status=dead}}</ref> Grande immediately connected with the sound and asked [[Mac Miller]] to feature on the song with her, to which he responded, "Sounds like a hit to me."<ref name="Billboard interview March 2013" /> The song was recorded that same month, and caught the attention of [[Republic Records]] VP [[Charlie Walk]] when he heard it being played from Republic co-founder [[Monte Lipman]]'s office. "It was 8:00 one night, and I heard a song coming from Monte's office through my wall. He called me down and I played a video, a DIY of Ariana Grande. We heard the song and I immediately made the decision to set it up [and] put the song out."<ref>{{cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/biz/articles/news/1556439/happening-now-big-things-ahead-for-nickelodeon-star-ariana-grande|title=Happening Now: Big Things Ahead for Nickelodeon Star Ariana Grande|last=Lipshutz|first=Jason|magazine=Billboard|volume=125|number=14|page=46|date=April 13, 2013|access-date=April 3, 2013|url-status=dead|archive-date=October 4, 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131004080607/http://www.billboard.com/biz/articles/news/1556439/happening-now-big-things-ahead-for-nickelodeon-star-ariana-grande}}</ref>

==Critical reception==
[[File:Mariah Carey Neighborhood Ball in downtown Washington 2009 cropped.JPG|upright|thumb|Critics have compared Grande's vocals to that of [[Mariah Carey]] (''pictured'').]]

[[Digital Spy]] gave the song a five out of five star rating comparing the song's sound and Ariana's vocals to [[Mariah Carey]]'s, continuing to say Ariana has a "powerful vocal" whilst the song has "bouncy piano riff" and "throwback R&B beats".<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.digitalspy.co.uk/music/singlesreviews/a469300/ariana-grande-ft-mac-miller-the-way-single-review.html |title=Ariana Grande ft. Mac Miller: 'The Way'&nbsp;– Single review&nbsp;– Music Singles Review |website=[[Digital Spy]] |date=March 18, 2013 |access-date=March 29, 2013 |archive-date=July 28, 2013 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130728224301/http://www.digitalspy.co.uk/music/singlesreviews/a469300/ariana-grande-ft-mac-miller-the-way-single-review.html |url-status=live }}</ref> Sam Lansky of ''[[Idolator (website)|Idolator]]'' gave the song a positive review, calling the vocals "sweet, soulful, and grown-up".<ref>{{cite web|first=Sam|last=Lansky|url=http://idolator.com/7448929/ariana-grande-releases-very-good-single-the-way-goes-to-1-on-itunes|title=Ariana Grande Releases Very Good Single "The Way," Goes To #1 On iTunes|publisher=[[Idolator (website)|Idolator]]|date=March 27, 2013|access-date=May 13, 2013|archive-date=August 12, 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210812114617/https://www.idolator.com/7448929/ariana-grande-releases-very-good-single-the-way-goes-to-1-on-itunes?chrome=1|url-status=dead}}</ref> [[MTV Buzzworthy]]'s Jenna Hally Rubenstein praised the song, advising readers to "keep one eye on Ariana" as "she may very well be the hottest pop newcomer in the game". Rubenstein also commented that the song's "early '00s-influenced R&B vibes" made it sound like an updated version of [[Jennifer Lopez]]'s "[[I'm Real (Jennifer Lopez song)|I'm Real]]" featuring [[Ja Rule]].<ref>{{cite web|last=Rubenstein|first=Jenna Hally|title=New Song: Ariana Grande Featuring Mac Miller, 'The Way'|url=http://buzzworthy.mtv.com/2013/03/27/ariana-grande-mac-miller-the-way/|publisher=[[MTV Buzzworthy]]|access-date=April 7, 2013|date=March 27, 2013|archive-date=April 8, 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130408013037/http://buzzworthy.mtv.com/2013/03/27/ariana-grande-mac-miller-the-way/|url-status=dead}}</ref> Nick Catucci of ''[[Rolling Stone]]'' magazine gave the song 3 and half stars out of five, criticizing Grande's vocals for being "more flirty than freaky", but positively compared her vocals to Carey for being "ecstatic".<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.rollingstone.com/music/songreviews/the-way-20130415|title=Ariana Grande feat. Mac Miller {{!}} 'The Way'|last=Catucci|first=Nick|magazine=[[Rolling Stone]]|date=April 15, 2013|access-date=May 3, 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130430025406/https://www.rollingstone.com/music/songreviews/the-way-20130415|archive-date=April 30, 2013|url-status=dead}}</ref>

As for Jessica Sager, from ''[[PopCrush]]'', the track sounds implausible. According to her, the song deviates from the standards that were expected for the artist, who despite having Victoria Justice and Miranda Cosgrove as closer musical examples, is inspired by "Mariah Carey of the early 2000s". Sager further states that "While Grande has the vocal cords necessary to record any kind of music she desires, her Nickelodeon background will likely make her talent ring false, and it's unclear whether she'll be able to sell herself as a R&B star".  Still according to her, "the track itself is neither good nor bad - its problem is that it is not memorable", since it "gets lost in the middle of the melody" and Miller's rhymes, which is not enough for that it is heard "many times".<ref>{{Cite web |last=Sager |first=Jessica |date=March 25, 2013 |title=Ariana Grande, "The Way" Feat. ==Copyright infringement lawsuit==
On December 11, 2013, Minder Music filed a [[copyright infringement]] lawsuit against Grande, [[Sony/ATV Music Publishing|Sony/ATV]], [[Universal Music Group|UMG Recording]] and other parties. It claimed that the line "What we gotta do right here is go back, back into time", which is spoken in the introduction to "The Way", infringes its copyright in the 1972 single "[[Troglodyte (song)|Troglodyte]]" by [[Jimmy Castor|The Jimmy Castor Bunch]], which contains the spoken lyric, "What we're gonna do right here is go back, way back, back into time".<ref name="The Way lawsuit">{{cite magazine|last=Gardner|first=Eriq|title=Ariana Grande Sued Over Mega-Hit Song 'The Way'|url=http://www.hollywoodreporter.com/thr-esq/ariana-grande-sued-mega-hit-665038|magazine=The Hollywood Reporter|date=December 11, 2013|access-date=December 12, 2013|archive-date=December 15, 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131215050847/http://www.hollywoodreporter.com/thr-esq/ariana-grande-sued-mega-hit-665038|url-status=live}}</ref>

The suit argued that the songs' similarities include "nearly identical lyrics; similar enunciation speed with a fast and consistent pace for "What we gotta do right" and a slightly slower pace for "here is go back," and substantially similar placement" at the beginning of both songs.<ref name="The Way lawsuit"/> Minder Music sought a permanent injunction, statutory damages of $150,000 per infringement, and legal fees.<ref name="The Way lawsuit"/><ref name="vibe">{{cite web|url=http://www.vibe.com/article/hit-single-%E2%80%98-way%E2%80%99-lands-ariana-grande-copyright-infringement-lawsuit|title=Hit Single "The Way" Lands Ariana Grande in a Copyright Infringement Lawsuit|last=Saad|first=Megan|website=Vibe|date=December 12, 2013|access-date=December 12, 2013|archive-date=December 30, 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131230133525/http://www.vibe.com/article/hit-single-%E2%80%98-way%E2%80%99-lands-ariana-grande-copyright-infringement-lawsuit|url-status=live}}</ref> The case was settled outside of court in 2015.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.nydailynews.com/entertainment/music/ariana-grande-settles-70s-funk-star-estate-song-article-1.2126157|title=Did she steal his song? Ariana Grande reaches confidential settlement with '70s funk star Jimmy Castor's estate|last=Farber|first=Jim|website=[[New York Daily News]]|date=February 23, 2015|access-date=August 28, 2017|archive-date=August 18, 2017|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170818215703/http://www.nydailynews.com/entertainment/music/ariana-grande-settles-70s-funk-star-estate-song-article-1.2126157|url-status=live}}</ref>
