"'''Focus'''" is a song by American singer-songwriter [[Ariana Grande]]. It was released on October 30, 2015, by [[Republic Records]]. Originally intended to be the [[lead single]] off of Grande's third studio album, ''[[Dangerous Woman]]'', then named ''Moonlight'', the song was scrapped from the album with its [[Dangerous Woman (song)|title track]] serving as the official lead single. The song appears as a bonus track on the Japan standard and deluxe edition of the album. Written by [[Savan Kotecha]], [[Peter Svensson]], Grande and [[Ilya Salmanzadeh|Ilya]], and produced by the latter with [[Max Martin]], the song features [[horn (instrument)|horns]] with [[Cowbell (instrument)|cowbells]] and [[handclaps]] as [[Percussion instrument|percussion]].

The song received mixed reviews from [[Music journalism|music critics]] who praised Grande's vocals and the song's [[Brass instrument|brassy]] production but criticized its similarity to her 2014 hit "[[Problem (Ariana Grande song)|Problem]]". "Focus" debuted at number seven on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] chart with 113,000 downloads in its first week. It was Grande's sixth top-ten single, and her first unaccompanied by another artist. The song also reached the top ten in Canada, Australia, Austria, Italy, Israel, the Netherlands, and the United Kingdom. By January 2016, "Focus" sold 544,000 copies in the United States and was certified double Platinum by the [[Recording Industry Association of America|RIAA]].

Its music video, directed by [[Hannah Lux Davis]], premiered on October 30, 2015, on Grande's [[YouTube]] channel. Until 2019, it remained the fourth most viewed music video on Grande's YouTube channel. She performed "Focus" at the [[American Music Awards of 2015|2015 American Music Awards]] and at the [[iHeartRadio]] Theater Los Angeles concert, and it was on the set list of the second leg of Grande's 2017 [[Dangerous Woman Tour]].

==Background and release==
"Focus" was written by [[Savan Kotecha]], [[Peter Svensson]], Grande and [[Ilya Salmanzadeh|Ilya]].<ref name="credits">{{cite web|url=http://www.allmusic.com/album/focus-mw0002894390/credits|title=Focus &#91;Single&#93;|publisher=[[AllMusic]]|access-date=July 6, 2017|archive-date=March 19, 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220319033642/https://www.allmusic.com/album/focus-mw0002894390/credits|url-status=live}}</ref> It was produced by [[Max Martin]] and Ilya. [[Serban Ghenea]] mixed the song, assisted by John Hanes.<ref name="Notes">{{cite AV media notes|title=Focus|type=CD liner notes|others=[[Ariana Grande]]|publisher=[[Republic Records]]|year=2015}}</ref> Grande began working on a new material for her third studio album in May 2015, posting details about the project on social media and in conversations with the public. The album was originally entitled ''Moonlight''.<ref>{{cite web|url= http://www.idolator.com/7594806/ariana-grande-moonlight-album-title|title= Ariana Grande Confirms Her New Album Is Titled ''Moonlight''|website= [[Idolator (website)|Idolator]]|date= May 30, 2015|access-date= July 6, 2017|first= Christina|last= Lee|archive-date= May 29, 2020|archive-url= https://web.archive.org/web/20200529220249/https://www.idolator.com/7594806/ariana-grande-moonlight-album-title|url-status= dead}}</ref> The singer posted an unfocused picture of herself on her [[Instagram]] page in July 2015 with the caption "Focus", calling it a "hint". A video released in September 2015 by iHeartMedia Music Summit demonstrated that Grande, [[Republic Records]] [[CEO]] [[Monte Lipman]] and her manager, [[Charlie Walk]], played the song to the approval of the Republic Records staff.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.fuse.tv/2015/08/ariana-grande-new-song-focus-on-me|title=Ariana Grande Teases New Single "Focus," Hear It Now!|work=[[Fuse (magazine)|Fuse]]|date= October 6, 2015|access-date= July 6, 2017|first= Jeff|last= Benjamin|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180304005427/https://www.fuse.tv/2015/08/ariana-grande-new-song-focus-on-me|archive-date= March 4, 2018}}</ref>

Grande announced the single during her September 15, 2015 appearance on ''[[The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon]]'', with a release date of October 30.<ref name=Goodman>{{cite magazine| last=Goodman|first=Jessica|title=Ariana Grande announces new single, 'Focus'|magazine=[[Entertainment Weekly]]|date=September 16, 2015| url=http://www.ew.com/article/2015/09/16/ariana-grande-new-single-focus|access-date=September 18, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180304005526/http://www.ew.com/article/2015/09/16/ariana-grande-new-single-focus/|archive-date= March 4, 2018}}</ref> She began posting snippets of the song's lyrics, video and audio previews and [[Pixelation|pixelated]] versions of the single's cover in early October on Instagram and [[Twitter]], and unveiled "Focus"' cover art on October 14. A week before the song's release date, A 15-second snippet of the track was used in a commercial for Ari by Ariana Grande, the singer's debut fragrance.<ref>{{cite web|title=Ariana Grande Teases 'Focus' In Debut Perfume Ad|url=http://www.fuse.tv/videos/2015/10/ariana-grande-focus-perfume-ad/|website=Fuse|date=October 20, 2015|access-date= March 3, 2018|first= Jeff|last= Benjamin|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180304005836/https://www.fuse.tv/videos/2015/10/ariana-grande-focus-perfume-ad|archive-date= March 4, 2018}}</ref> "Focus" was [[Music download|digitally released]] worldwide on October 30, 2015,<ref name="Di"/> and was added to [[hot adult contemporary]] playlists in the United States on November 2.<ref name="US HAC"/> It was later released to American and Italian [[contemporary hit radio]] stations on November 3 and 6 respectively.<ref name="US CHR"/><ref name="IT CHR"/> "Focus" was released as a [[maxi single]] in Japan on December 4, with regular version containing a mini-calendar and a mini-poster,<ref name="JP-R"/> whilst deluxe edition containing a poster and an A7-sized notebook.<ref name="JP-D"/>

=== Removal from ''Dangerous Woman'' ===
The song was originally intended to be the lead single of Grande's then-named third studio album, ''Moonlight''.<ref>{{cite magazine|first=Reed|last=Ryan|url=https://www.rollingstone.com/music/news/hear-ariana-grandes-brash-brassy-new-song-focus-20151030|title= Hear Ariana Grande's Brash, Brassy New Song 'Focus'|magazine=[[Rolling Stone]] |date= October 30, 2015|access-date= March 3, 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180304010034/https://www.rollingstone.com/music/news/hear-ariana-grandes-brash-brassy-new-song-focus-20151030|archive-date= March 4, 2018}}</ref> Republic Records later told ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' that the track had been removed from the album's standard edition and that the lead single will be changed to "[[Dangerous Woman (song)|Dangerous Woman]]".<ref>{{cite magazine|first=Trust|last=Gary|url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/rihanna-hot-100-no-1-fifth-week-ariana-grande-debuts/|title= Rihanna Rules Hot 100 for Fifth Week, Ariana Grande Debuts at No. 10|magazine= [[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|date= March 21, 2016|access-date= March 3, 2018|url-status=live|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180304010219/https://www.billboard.com/articles/columns/chart-beat/7263992/rihanna-hot-100-no-1-fifth-week-ariana-grande-debuts|archive-date= March 4, 2018}}</ref> The song was moved to the album's Japan edition as a bonus track.<ref name="JPA">{{cite web|url=http://www.universal-music.co.jp/ariana-grande/news/2016/03/23 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160522100345/http://www.universal-music.co.jp/ariana-grande/news/2016/03/23/ |archive-date=2016-05-22 |language=ja |trans-title=Miracle collaboration is seen! ! ==Critical reception==
Amy Davison from [[Digital Spy]] praised "Focus"' "sax-driven" beat;<ref name="digitalspy">{{cite web|url=http://www.digitalspy.com/music/music-videos/news/a676455/ariana-grandes-new-single-focus-has-arrived-with-a-music-video-in-tow/|title= Ariana Grande's new single 'Focus' has arrived with a music video in tow|website= [[Digital Spy]]|first= Amy|last= Davidson|date= October 30, 2017|access-date= March 3, 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180304145833/http://www.digitalspy.com/music/music-videos/news/a676455/ariana-grandes-new-single-focus-has-arrived-with-a-music-video-in-tow/|archive-date= March 4, 2018}}</ref> Kaitlyn Tiffany  of ''[[The Verge]]'' called Grande a "massively talented vocalist", and "Focus" a "very pleasant" listen.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.theverge.com/2015/10/30/9646876/ariana-grande-focus-music-video-samsung-galaxy-note-5|title=Ariana Grande and the Galaxy Note 5 just dropped a new music video|work=[[The Verge]]|first=Kaitlyn|last=Tiffany|date=October 30, 2015|access-date=October 31, 2015|archive-url=https://archive.today/20151105140858/http://www.theverge.com/2015/10/30/9646876/ariana-grande-focus-music-video-samsung-galaxy-note-5|archive-date=November 5, 2015|url-status=live}}</ref> ''[[Time (magazine)|Time]]''{{'s}} Nolan Feeney noted that although it was similar to "Problem", it may not have been a poor single decision.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://time.com/4094111/ariana-grande-focus/|title=Watch the Video for Ariana Grande's New Single 'Focus'|magazine=[[Time (magazine)|Time]]|date=October 30, 2015|access-date=October 31, 2015|first= Nolan|last= Freeney|url-status=live|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180304150043/http://time.com/4094111/ariana-grande-focus/|archive-date= March 4, 2018}}</ref> On ''[[Renowned for Sound]]'', Marcus Floyd called "Focus" predictably "catchy and attention grabbing"; the song ticks "all the boxes of an effective lead single." Giving it four out of five stars, he wrote that "Focus" is one of Grande's "stronger single releases" to date but did not "venture out" of her "comfort zone."<ref name="Renowned">{{cite web|url=http://renownedforsound.com/index.php/single-review-ariana-grande-focus/|title= Single Review: Ariana Grande – 'Focus'|website= Renowned for sound|first= Marcus|last= Floyd|date= November 3, 2015|access-date= March 3, 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180304150150/http://renownedforsound.com/index.php/single-review-ariana-grande-focus/|archive-date= March 4, 2018}}</ref> Steven J. Horowitz of ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' gave the song two-and-a-half out of five stars and wrote that Grande had never released a "less-than-great" single until "Focus", which he considered a "weak" rehash of "Problem".<ref>{{cite magazine |last1= Horowitz|first1= Steven|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/review/track-review/6753991/ariana-grande-grimes-miike-snow-chance-the-rapper-singles-review|title=Does Ariana Grande's 'Focus' Need Sharpening? Plus More of the Week's Best & Worst Singles|magazine=Billboard|date=November 6, 2015 | access-date=November 6, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180304150456/https://www.billboard.com/articles/review/track-review/6753991/ariana-grande-grimes-miike-snow-chance-the-rapper-singles-review|archive-date= March 4, 2018}}</ref>

''Time'' called "Focus" the fifth-worst song of 2015, calling Grande's vocal "dwarfed" by "too-big instrumentals" as it had been on "Problem" and "[[Break Free (song)|Break Free]]"; however, the latter two had a "sustaining energy" which "Focus" lacked. The singer needed a "tightly constructed, elegant hit" after her [[Ariana Grande#Public image|2015 doughnut controversy]], "and this isn't it".<ref name="TimeWorst">{{cite magazine|url=http://time.com/4133060/top-10-worst-songs-2/|title=Worst Songs of 2015|magazine=Time|date= December 3, 2015|access-date=January 14, 2016|author= Time Staff|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180304150643/http://time.com/4133060/top-10-worst-songs-2/|archive-date= March 4, 2018|url-status=live}}</ref> Although Brennan Carley of ''[[Spin (magazine)|Spin]]'' called the song a "catchy slow-burn" and a "horn heavy piece of retro-modern fusion", Grande's vocals lacked "real excitement from a star who's got the restraint and skill to take her instrument places most other [[Pop music|pop]] singers can't anymore." Carley criticized the song's "male howling" for "[getting] grating quickly" and making "Focus" too similar to "Problem".<ref name="Spin"/>

==Commercial performance==
"Focus" debuted at number seven on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] in the United States, selling 113,000 digital downloads in its first full week, becoming Grande's sixth top-ten single and fourth top-ten debut on the chart.<ref name="top10">{{cite web|title=Adele's 'Hello' Tops Hot 100 for Second Week; Ariana Grande, Meghan Trainor Hit Top 10 |url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/adele-hello-hot-100-second-week/|website=[[Billboard.com]]|author=Gary Trust|date=November 9, 2015 |access-date=November 11, 2015|url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180304154230/https://www.billboard.com/articles/columns/chart-beat/6754159/adele-hello-hot-100-second-week|archive-date= March 4, 2018}}</ref> It was her first solo top ten in the country, unaccompanied by another artist.<ref name="top10"/> The song entered the [[Digital Songs]] chart at number five and was number 13 on the Streaming Songs chart, with 13.3 million domestic streams.<ref name="top10"/> It fell to number 13 in its second week on the Hot 100, rebounding to number 12 in its third week following Grande's performance at the [[American Music Awards of 2015|2015 American Music Awards]].<ref>{{cite magazine | url=http://www.billboard.com/charts/hot-100/2015-11-28 | title=Hot 100: The week of November 28, 2015 | magazine=Billboard | access-date=December 9, 2015 | archive-date=February 19, 2018 | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180219210333/https://www.billboard.com/charts/hot-100/2015-11-28 | url-status=live }}</ref> By April 2018, "Focus" sold over 544,000 digital downloads in the US and was certified double Platinum by the [[Recording Industry Association of America|RIAA]].<ref name="us_sales"/> It debuted and peaked at number eight on the [[Canadian Hot 100]], Grande's fifth Canadian top-ten single.

The song debuted at number ten on the [[Official Singles Chart]] in the United Kingdom, Grande's third top ten after the number-one singles "Problem" and "[[Bang Bang (Jessie J, Ariana Grande and Nicki Minaj song)|Bang Bang]]".<ref>{{cite web|last1=Myers|first1=Justin|title=Adele says Hello to another week at Number 1|url=http://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/adele-says-hello-to-another-week-at-number-1__11291|publisher=[[Official Charts Company]]|date=November 6, 2015|access-date= March 3, 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180304154423/http://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/adele-says-hello-to-another-week-at-number-1__11291/|archive-date= March 4, 2018}}</ref> In its second week, the song fell fourteen positions placing at number 24; it spent a total of 14 weeks on the charts. "Focus" also reached the top ten in Australia, Austria, Greece, Italy, the Netherlands and Spain.<ref name="Australiacharts"/><ref name="Austriacharts"/><ref name="Greececharts"/><ref name="Spaincharts"/>

===Background and synopsis===
[[File:Ariana Grande - Focus (Screenshot).png|thumb|right|alt= A screenshot from a music video showing the body of a woman as a silhouette over a dark circle with purple shades.|Screenshot from the "Focus" video; according to Nick Maslow of ''[[People (magazine)|People]]'' magazine, Grande appears in a "world of purple".<ref>{{cite magazine|url=http://www.people.com/article/ariana-grande-focus-single-music-video|title=Ariana Grande Turns Up the Heat in New 'Focus' Music Video|magazine=[[People (magazine)|People]]|first=Nick|last=Maslow|date=October 7, 2015|access-date=November 14, 2015|archive-date=March 4, 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180304142802/http://people.com/celebrity/ariana-grande-debuts-focus-single-and-music-video/|url-status=live}}</ref>]]

The music video for "Focus" was released on [[YouTube]] on October 29, 2015. [[Hannah Lux Davis]], who directed the video, also directed the music videos for Grande's "[[Love Me Harder]]" and the singer's joint single, "[[Bang Bang (Jessie J, Ariana Grande and Nicki Minaj song)|Bang Bang]]".<ref>{{cite magazine|title=Ariana Grande Drops New Single 'Focus'|magazine=Billboard|author= Billboard Staff|date=October 30, 2015|url=https://www.billboard.com/music/pop/ariana-grande-single-focus-6746034/|access-date=October 30, 2015|url-status=live|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180304165930/https://www.billboard.com/articles/columns/pop-shop/6746034/ariana-grande-single-focus|archive-date= March 4, 2018}}</ref> The video [[Product placement|features]] the [[Samsung Galaxy Note 5]].<ref>{{cite news|url=http://www.ibtimes.com/samsung-galaxy-note-5-ariana-grandes-latest-music-video-focus-flaunts-powerful-2168432|title=Samsung Galaxy Note 5: Ariana Grande's Latest Music Video 'Focus' Flaunts The Powerful Phablet|newspaper= [[International Business Times]]|first= Pavithra|last= Rathinavel|date= November 4, 2015|access-date=November 10, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180304170309/http://www.ibtimes.com/samsung-galaxy-note-5-ariana-grandes-latest-music-video-focus-flaunts-powerful-2168432|archive-date= March 4, 2018}}</ref>

The video begins with Grande writing the phrase "focus on me" on a Galaxy Note 5 using its [[S Pen]]. During the first verse, scenes of Grande lip-syncing the song are intercut with silhouettes of the singer dancing in a purple circle. She then performs a choreographed routine with six backing dancers on a purple set. During the second verse, she smiles and poses in a square wearing a black mini dress and high [[Toni Basil]]-style boots. For the song's bridge, the singer (in a star-print leotard, photographed in black-and-white) lies on a neon-lit set, plays a trumpet and performs with her dancers.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=http://www.rap-up.com/2015/10/29/video-ariana-grande-focus/|title= Ariana Grande - 'Focus'|magazine= [[Rap-Up]]|date= October 29, 2015|access-date= March 3, 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180304170534/http://www.rap-up.com/2015/10/29/video-ariana-grande-focus/|archive-date= March 4, 2018}}</ref> The video ends with her right eye illuminated. ''[[Teen Vogue]]''{{'}}s Ella Cerón noted its "slightly throwback" visuals, which match the song.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.teenvogue.com/story/ariana-grande-focus-music-video|title= Ariana France's "Focus" Is Finally Here – And It's the Perfect Song to Sing to Your Haters|magazine= [[Teen Vogue]]|first= Ella|last= Cerón|date= October 30, 2015|access-date= March 3, 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180304170702/https://www.teenvogue.com/story/ariana-grande-focus-music-video|archive-date= March 4, 2018}}</ref>

===Reception===
Sean Fitz-Gerald of [[New York (magazine)#Digital|Vulture]] called the video's geometric look similar to the videos for [[Drake (musician)|Drake]]'s "[[Hotline Bling]]" (2015) and [[Lil Mama]]'s "[[Lip Gloss]]" (2007).<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.vulture.com/2015/10/ariana-grande-focus-music-video.html|title= Here's Ariana Grande's Very Purple 'Focus' Video|work= Vulture|first= Sean|last= Fitz-Gerald|date= October 30, 2015|access-date= March 3, 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180304172400/http://www.vulture.com/2015/10/ariana-grande-focus-music-video.html|archive-date= March 4, 2018}}</ref> ''Billboard''{{'}}s staff ranked the music video for "Focus" as Grande's seventh-best, describing it as "out-of-this-world" with "star-emblazoned jumpsuits and neon wigs"; they compared the clip's aesthetic to the [[List of Disney Channel original films|Disney Channel Original Movie]] ''[[Zenon: Girl of the 21st Century (film)|Zenon: Girl of the 21st Century]]'' (1999) and Grande's dancing to [[Beyoncé]]'s in the video for "[[Single Ladies (Put a Ring on It)]]" (2008).<ref>{{cite magazine|url= https://www.billboard.com/music/pop/ariana-grande-best-music-videos-7318712/|title= Ariana Grande's 10 Best Music Videos|magazine= Billboard|author= Billboard Staff|date= April 1, 2016|access-date= March 3, 2018|archive-date= March 4, 2018|archive-url= https://web.archive.org/web/20180304172506/https://www.billboard.com/articles/columns/pop/7318712/ariana-grande-best-music-videos|url-status= live}}</ref> The music video has received over 1 billion views on [[YouTube]], becoming her eighth most-viewed upload on the site.
