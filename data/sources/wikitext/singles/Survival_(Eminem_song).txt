"'''Survival'''" is a song by American rapper [[Eminem]]. It premiered on August 14, 2013, to promote the [[multiplayer]] trailer for the [[video game]] ''[[Call of Duty: Ghosts]]'',<ref name=EUHNSS>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.rollingstone.com/music/videos/eminem-unveils-hard-hitting-new-song-survival-20130814|title=Eminem Unveils Hard-Hitting New Song, 'Survival': Rapper's eighth album is expected to be released later this year|access-date=August 28, 2013|date=August 14, 2013|author=Reed, Ryan|magazine=[[Rolling Stone]]|archive-date=September 10, 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130910033652/http://www.rollingstone.com/music/videos/eminem-unveils-hard-hitting-new-song-survival-20130814|url-status=dead}}</ref><ref>{{cite Ghosts'|access-date=August 28, 2013|date=August 14, 2013|work=[[Daily News (New York)|New York Daily News]]|author=Rivera, Zayda|archive-date=March 3, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160303173015/http://www.nydailynews.com/entertainment/music-arts/eminem-declares-back-new-single-survival-article-1.1426950|url-status=live}}</ref><ref released as a pre-order bonus when pre-ordering the game.<ref name="nextvg.com">{{cite web|url=http://nextvg.com/2013/09/09/eminems-new-album-discounted-pre-ordering-ghosts/|title=Eminem's new album the second single from ''[[The Marshall Mathers LP 2]]'' on October 8, 2013. It features uncredited vocals in the chorus from Liz Rodrigues of [[the New Royales]]. Upon its single release, the song debuted at number 17 on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]], and upon the album's release, it climbed to a new peak of number 16.

==Release==
In August 2013, following the release of ''Call of Duty: Ghosts'' multiplayer video, Eminem released a video explaining to his fans the new album would be released in the fall, and that a new music video was coming soon.<ref name="rap-up1">{{cite web |url=http://www.rap-up.com/2013/08/14/eminem-premieres-new-song-survival-in-call-of-duty-trailer/ |title=Eminem Premieres New Song 'Survival' in 'Call of Duty' Trailer |publisher=Rap-Up.com |date=August 14, 2013 |access-date=October 9, 2013 |archive-date=September 23, 2015 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150923140338/http://www.rap-up.com/2013/08/14/eminem-premieres-new-song-survival-in-call-of-duty-trailer/ |url-status=dead }}</ref> It was also confirmed that the song would be featured within the game.<ref name="rap-up1"/> This was the third time that an Eminem song was used to promote a ''[[Call of Duty]]'' (''CoD'') game, with "[['Till I Collapse]]" being used in the ''[[Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 2|CoD: Modern Warfare 2]]'' trailer, and "[[Won't Back Down (Eminem song)|Won't Back Down]]" being used in the ''[[Call of Duty: Black Ops|CoD: Black Ops]]'' trailer, in zombies mode as a hidden song and the end credits.<ref name="rap-up1"/> The song is also featured on the soundtrack of ''[[WWE 2K19]]'', being selected by [[AJ Styles]] to be included in the soundtrack.

The song is also heard in the end credits for ''Ghosts''.

==Music video==
The official [[music video]] for "Survival" was released on October 8, 2013.<ref>{{cite web|last=Alexis |first=Nadeska|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1715241/eminem-survival-music-video.jhtml |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131008200734/http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1715241/eminem-survival-music-video.jhtml |url-status=dead |archive-date=October 8, 2013 |title=Eminem's Detroit Childhood Haunts His 'Survival' Video - Music, Celebrity, Artist News |publisher=MTV.com|date=October 8, 2013|access-date=October 9, 2013}}</ref> The music video featured Eminem rapping in front of footage of ''CoD: Ghosts'' featured on a projector, with additional shots featuring elements from the game such as dogs and soldiers in their in-game outfits.<ref>{{cite web |last1=Ortiz |first1=Edwin |title=Video: Eminem "Survival" |url=https://www.complex.com/music/a/edwin-ortiz/eminem-survival-video |website=Complex |access-date=September 6, 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230918172128/https://www.complex.com/music/a/edwin-ortiz/eminem-survival-video |archive-date=September 18, 2023 |date=October 8, 2013 |url-status=live}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |last1=DeVille |first1=Chris |title=Eminem – "Survival" Video |url=https://stereogum.com/1513202/eminem-survival-video/news |website=[[Stereogum]] |access-date=September 6, 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230925063410/https://www.stereogum.com/1513202/eminem-survival-video/news/ |archive-date=September 25, 2023 |date=October 8, 2013 |url-status=live}}</ref> The final scene features Eminem revisiting the house featured on the cover of ''[[The Marshall Mathers LP]]'' and ''[[The Marshall Mathers LP 2]]''. The house was later [[demolished]] by the state of [[Michigan]].<ref>{{cite news|last=Lacy|first=Eric|url=https://www.mlive.com/entertainment/detroit/index.ssf/2013/11/eminems_childhood_home_in_detr.html|title=Eminem's childhood home in Detroit demolished by State of Michigan; 19946 Dresden lot for sale - MLive.com|work=MLive.com|date=November 20, 2013|access-date=January 9, 2019|language=en-US|archive-date=January 9, 2019|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190109205337/https://www.mlive.com/entertainment/detroit/index.ssf/2013/11/eminems_childhood_home_in_detr.html|url-status=live}}</ref>

==Live performances==
Eminem performed the song on ''[[Saturday Night Live]]'' on November 2, 2013, along with "[[Berzerk (song)|Berzerk]]", with [[Skylar Grey]] replacing Liz Rodrigues on backing vocals.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/5778133/eminem-gets-assist-from-rick-rubin-skylar-grey-on-snl-watch|title=Eminem Gets Assist From Rick Rubin, Skylar Grey on 'SNL': Watch|date=November 3, 2013|access-date=January 7, 2015|publisher=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|author=Kevin Rutherford|archive-date=September 30, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140930053825/http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/5778133/eminem-gets-assist-from-rick-rubin-skylar-grey-on-snl-watch|url-status=live}}</ref> He also performed the song on [[BBC Radio 1]] in November 2013, along with "[[Stan (song)|Stan]]" and "[[Not Afraid]]".<ref>{{Cite web |date=2013-12-01 |title=Eminem Performs "Not Afraid" & "Survival" On BBC Radio 1 |url=https://www.xxlmag.com/eminem-performs-afraid-survival-bbc-radio-1/ |access-date=2026-02-18 |website=XXL Mag |language=en |archive-date=October 17, 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20251017062634/https://www.xxlmag.com/eminem-performs-afraid-survival-bbc-radio-1/ |url-status=live }}</ref>

==Critical reception==
The song features rock guitars in a style that makes it similar to works from Eminem's ''[[Recovery (Eminem album)|Recovery]]'', according to Luke Bainbridge of ''[[The Guardian]]''.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.theguardian.com/music/2013/aug/25/eminem-glasgow-review|title=Eminem – review: Bellahouston Park, Glasgow: The 40-year-old rapper's career-spanning set is short on new material but his stage presence is as potent as ever|access-date=August 29, 2013|date=August 24, 2013|work=[[The Guardian]]|author=Bainbridge, Luke|archive-date=August 24, 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130824233649/http://www.theguardian.com/music/2013/aug/25/eminem-glasgow-review|url-status=live}}</ref> ''[[Los Angeles Times]]'' pop music critic Randall Roberts says that the song matches the "voluminous, venom-filled and explosive" levels of the work it accompanies.<ref name=Fi>{{cite web|url=http://www.latimes.com/entertainment/music/posts/la-et-ms-eminem-releases-new-song-survival-via-call-of-duty-trailer-20130814,0,1092201.story|title=First impression: Eminem's new 'Survival,' from Call of Duty|access-date=August 29, 2013|date=August 14, 2013|work=[[Los Angeles Times]]|author=Roberts, Randall|archive-date=August 29, 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130829011056/http://www.latimes.com/entertainment/music/posts/la-et-ms-eminem-releases-new-song-survival-via-call-of-duty-trailer-20130814,0,1092201.story|url-status=live}}</ref> Roberts says the song features an aggressive rapper, "cuss-filled lines...the bombast of distorted electric guitar and a jumbo beat."<ref name=Fi/> He says that the DJ Khalil-produced song, which features the New Royales' Liz Rodrigues, "is a cliché-ridden couplet that sounds like it took as much time to imagine as it did to scribble on a scrap of paper."<ref name=Fi/> ''[[Rolling Stone]]''{{'s}} Ryan Reed describes the song as hard-hitting with background music that classifies as "breakneck, arena-rock guitars and trashy drums."<ref name=EUHNSS/>
