"'''Live It Up'''" is a song recorded by American singer [[Jennifer Lopez]]. It was written by [[RedOne]], [[Alex P]]apaconstantinou, Björn Djupström, Viktor Svensson, [[Pitbull (rapper)|Armando "Pitbull" Perez]] (who is also the featured artist), [[AJ Junior|Achraf Janussi]] and [[Bilal Hajji|Bilal "The Chef" Hajji]]. It was produced by RedOne and Alex P, with co-production from Viktor Svensson and audio production by [[Kuk Harrell]]. The song marks the fourth collaboration between two artists following "[[Fresh Out the Oven]]" (2009), "[[On the Floor]]" (2011) and "[[Dance Again]]" (2012), the latter two of which achieved commercial success. "Live It Up" is a [[dance-pop]] and [[Europop]] song that features Lopez singing inspirational lyrics about [[Free will|living it up]]. It received generally positive reviews from music critics, with several critics predicting that it would be the [[summer hit]] of 2013.

"Live It Up" experienced moderate commercial success worldwide, entering the top-twenty in twelve countries including the United Kingdom, Canada and Australia; however, in the United States the song only managed to reach number 60, thus failing to achieve the same commercial success as Lopez's previous collaborations with Pitbull. Lopez performed the song live for the first time on the [[American Idol (season 12)|season 12]] finale of ''[[American Idol]]'' on May 16, 2013. The following day, the song's [[music video]] was released; set in France, the video is divided into two halves including a chaotic fashion show in [[Paris]], which contrasts the care-free environment of the beach in [[Saint-Tropez]]. The music video was nominated for [[2013 MTV Video Music Awards#Best Choreography|Best Choreography]] at the [[2013 MTV Video Music Awards]].

"Live It Up" is an uptempo [[dance-pop]] and [[Europop]]<ref name="LAT" /><ref name="Idolator Review">{{cite web|last=Lansky|first=Sam|title=Jennifer Lopez & Pitbull's "#LiveItUp": Listen To The Obvious Summer Smash|url=http://idolator.com/7457088/jennifer-lopez-pitbull-live-it-up-listen#comment-14651131|archive-url=https://archive.today/20130629114937/http://idolator.com/7457088/jennifer-lopez-pitbull-live-it-up-listen#comment-14651131|url-status=dead|archive-date=June 29, 2013|publisher=Idolator (SpinMedia)|access-date=May 8, 2013|date=May 8, 2013}}</ref> song with a duration of four minutes and three seconds (4:03). == Critical response ==
The song received generally positive reviews. When the solo version of "Live It Up" was released, Lansky commented that it should be made the "official version" of the song.<ref name="Now Comes In A Nearly Pitbull-Free Mix"/> He wrote that it should be "law" that if "your song features Pitbull, [[will.i.am]] or [[Flo Rida]], you are legally mandated to release a solo version so nobody has to suffer".<ref name="Now Comes In A Nearly Pitbull-Free Mix"/> Jason Lipshutz of ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' called the song a "dance-ready summer anthem", writing that Lopez "seems to be having a blast" in the song, "tossing out inspirational phrases about doing anything we want, living it up, not stopping, and going". He also noted, "Like graham crackers, marshmallows and chocolate, the combination of Jennifer Lopez, Pitbull and producer RedOne has proven to be endlessly winning".<ref name="Billboard review">{{cite magazine|last=Lipshutz|first=Jason|title=Jennifer Lopez, Pitbull 'Live It Up' on New Single: Listen|url=https://www.billboard.com/music/music-news/jennifer-lopez-pitbull-live-it-up-on-new-single-listen-1560819/|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|publisher=[[Prometheus Global Media]]|access-date=May 9, 2013|date=May 8, 2013}}</ref> Gerrick D. Kennedy of the ''[[Los Angeles Times]]'' called the song a "typical Euro-pop jam that RedOne probably weaves in his sleep" that sounds "perfect for summer".<ref name="LAT" /> Writing for She Knows Entertainment, Natasha Shankar thought the chorus was "forced", but however described the song as "catchy" and "vivacious and strong, just like the songstress herself".<ref name="She Knows" />

Amy Sciarretto of PopCrush wrote that while the song will get its "fair share" of remixes and club play, it will also "find a home on the radio and in mall clothing stores".<ref name="PopCrush review"/> According to Sciarretto, the production "makes you think if there ever was a song that sounds like Miami, this is it".<ref name="PopCrush review"/> Sciarretto wrote that while the song is in typical Lopez and Pitbull fashion, but "they've switched it up this time on the production end of things, thanks to the multiple layers of synthy beats".<ref name="PopCrush review"/> She concluded that in September 2013, "Live It Up" will be "one of those songs that defines the three months that came before it".<ref name="PopCrush review"/> ''[[The New York Daily News]]'' felt that "Live It Up" was strikingly similar to "[[Dance Again]]", but stated that it had potential to be a summer hit, praising its "make love, don't fight/let's hook tonight".<ref name="NYDaily review" /> Similarly, [[Digital Spy]]'s Robert Copsey said the song felt "very by-numbers" until [[Günther (singer)|Günther]] announces "Make love, don't fight/ Let's hook tonight", which he said allowed it to become "something we never dreamed possible: a colourful and completely bonkers number that, by comparison, makes [[Nicki Minaj]]'s '[[Starships (song)|Starships]]' sound pedestrian".<ref name="Spy 2">{{cite web|last=Copsey|first=Robert|title=Jennifer Lopez ft. Pitbull: 'Live It Up'&nbsp;– Single review|url=http://www.digitalspy.com.au/music/singlesreviews/a481032/jennifer-lopez-ft-pitbull-live-it-up-single-review.html|publisher=[[Digital Spy]] (Hearst Magazines UK)|access-date=May 14, 2013|date=May 14, 2013}}</ref> Additionally, Copsey noted that there is an "argument that says once you've heard one J Lo/Pitbull/RedOne collaboration then you've heard them all". He, however, disagreed, and wrote that "you won't hear us complaining if it turns into an annual tradition".<ref name="Spy 2" />

Sugey Palomares of ''[[Latina (magazine)|Latina]]'' stated, "This is the third time J.Lo has joined forces with the hit-making producer and Mr. 305, which is no surprise considering the success they garnered in the past. We definitely think third times a charm".<ref>{{cite web|last=Palomares|first=Sugey|title=Jennifer Lopez Premieres Single With Pitbull Live It Up|url=http://www.latina.com/entertainment/music/jlo-pitbull-live-it-up-premiere|work=Latina|publisher=Latina Media Ventures, LLC|access-date=May 9, 2013|date=May 8, 2013}}</ref> Nate Jones of the website PopDust found the fact that Lopez collaborated with Pitbull once again "odd", and wrote that "For artists who aren’t married, and never were in any sort of band together, this is a pretty rare thing". Jones concluded that "If you’re looking for [an] analogue for Pitbull and Jennifer Lopez’s relationship, it’s helpful to think of Pitbull as a nerdy teenage boy and Lopez as his friend’s hot older sister".<ref>{{cite web|last=Jones|first=Nate|title=Just Friends: "Live It Up" and the Odd, Sweet Friendship Between Pitbull and J. Lo|url=http://popdust.com/2013/05/09/just-friends-liveitup-and-the-odd-sweet-friendship-between-pitbull-and-j-lo/|publisher=Pop Dust, Inc.|access-date=May 10, 2013|date=May 9, 2013}}</ref> Katie Hasty of [[HitFix]] was negative, however, and called the song a  "cheap [...] seasonal drug store-bought toy". She wrote that the song fits the "general guidelines" for a "summer jam", but that is disposable and interchangeable.<ref>{{cite news|last=Hasty|first=Katie|title=Jennifer Lopez and Pitbull's new song 'Live It Up': Lab-made, and listen here|url=http://www.hitfix.com/immaculate-noise/jennifer-lopez-and-pitbulls-new-song-live-it-up-lab-made-and-listen-here|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130726065919/http://www.hitfix.com/immaculate-noise/jennifer-lopez-and-pitbulls-new-song-live-it-up-lab-made-and-listen-here|url-status=dead|archive-date=July 26, 2013|work=[[HitFix]]|access-date=May 15, 2013|date=May 8, 2013}}</ref>

== Commercial performance ==
"Live It Up" has experienced moderate success, but not to the extent of "On the Floor" or "Dance Again". For the week of May 13, 2013, it entered the US [[Billboard magazine|''Billboard'']] [[Pop Songs]] chart at number 33 and the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] at number 73, becoming the second highest debut for the week on both charts.<ref name="pop song debut">{{cite magazine|last=Trust|first=Gary|title=Chart Highlights: Mariah Carey, Miguel Blast Onto Radio Rankings With '#Beautiful'|url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/chart-highlights-mariah-carey-miguel-blast-onto-radio-rankings/|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|publisher=[[Prometheus Global Media]]|access-date=May 14, 2013|date=May 13, 2013}}</ref><ref name="73 debut">{{cite web|last=Grein|first=Paul|title=Week Ending May 12, 2013. Songs: #Letdown|url=http://music.yahoo.com/blogs/chart-watch/week-ending-may-12-2013-songs-letdown-205153774.html|publisher=Yahoo! Music: Chart Watch|access-date=May 16, 2013|date=May 16, 2013}}</ref> To date, the song is Lopez's 23rd entry on the Hot 100, excluding featured singles.<ref name="DEC" /> "Live It Up" also charted on the US [[Dance/Electronic Digital Songs]] chart at number eleven,<ref name="DEC">{{cite web|url={{BillboardURLbyName|artist=Jennifer Lopez|chart=Dance/Electronic Digital Songs}}|title=Jennifer Lopez Album & Song Chart History|work=Billboard [[Dance/Electronic Digital Songs]] for Jennifer Lopez|publisher=Prometheus Global Media|access-date=May 16, 2013}}</ref> with first-week sales of 43,000 downloads, which also allowed it enter the [[Hot Digital Songs]] at number 43.<ref>{{cite magazine|last=Trust|first=Gary|title=Weekly Chart Notes|url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/weekly-chart-notes-mariah-carey-adds-to-hot-100-legacy/|magazine=Billboard|publisher=Prometheus Global Media|access-date=May 17, 2013|date=May 16, 2013}}</ref> The next week, sales of the single rose by 53%, bringing its total to 135,000 digital downloads sold in the US.<ref>{{cite web|last=Mansfiel|first=Bryan|title=New 'Idol' downloads lag behind previous winners|url=https://www.usatoday.com/story/idolchatter/2013/05/22/new-idol-download-lag-behind/2351937/|work=USA Today|access-date=May 26, 2013|date=May 22, 2013}}</ref> "Live it Up" peaked at number 60 on the Hot 100.<ref name="BB Hot 100" /> Though performing unsuccessfully on the US ''Billboard'' Hot 100, "Live It Up" managed to top the US ''Billboard'' [[Hot Dance Club Songs]] chart, making it her tenth consecutive number one dance hit, as well as her fourteenth number one overall.<ref name="Dance1" />

"Live It Up" has been more successful in the international music market, having entered the top twenty in eleven countries.
