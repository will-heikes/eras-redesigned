"'''Baby, It's Cold Outside'''" is a [[popular song]] written by [[Frank Loesser]] in 1944 and popularized in the 1949 film ''[[Neptune's Daughter (1949 film)|Neptune's Daughter]]''. While the lyrics make no mention of a holiday, it is commonly regarded as a [[Christmas song]] owing to its winter theme.  The song was released in eight recordings in 1949—including well-known versions by [[Ella Fitzgerald]] and [[Louis Jordan]], [[Hot Lips Page]] and [[Pearl Bailey]], and [[Dean Martin]] and [[Marilyn Maxwell]]—and has been covered hundreds of times since.

==History==
In 1944, Loesser wrote "Baby, It's Cold Outside" to sing with his wife, Lynn Garland, at their [[housewarming party]] in New York City at the Navarro Hotel. They sang the song to indicate to guests that it was time to leave.<ref name="dolls">{{cite book |first=Susan |last=Loesser |url=https://books.google.com/books?id=5Ajnf0SdatsC&dq=%22My+father+had+written+Baby+It's+Cold+Outside+in+1944%22&pg=PA79 |title=A Most Remarkable Fella: Frank Loesser and the Guys and Dolls in His Life; A Portrait by His Daughter |pages=79-81 |publisher=Hal Leonard |year=1993 |isbn=1-55611-364-1 |access-date=March 2, 2026 |oclc=44131813}}</ref> Garland has written that after the first performance, "We became instant parlor room stars. We got invited to all the best parties for years on the basis of 'Baby.' It was our ticket to caviar and truffles. Parties were built around our being the closing act."<ref name="dolls" /> In 1948, after performing the song for years, Loesser sold it to [[Metro-Goldwyn-Mayer|MGM]] for the 1949 romantic comedy ''[[Neptune's Daughter (1949 film)|Neptune's Daughter]]''. Garland was furious: "I felt as betrayed as if I'd caught him in bed with another woman."<ref name="I've Got the Song Right Here!: Inside the Music of Frank Loesser">{{cite web |website=Schimmel Center at Pace University |title=I've Got the Song Right Here!: Inside the Music of Frank Loesser |url=http://schimmelcenter.org/ive-got-the-song-right-here-inside-the-music-of-frank-loesser |date=October 26, 2016 |access-date=December 9, 2018 |archive-date=December 9, 2018 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20181209124448/http://schimmelcenter.org/ive-got-the-song-right-here-inside-the-music-of-frank-loesser |url-status=usurped}}</ref>

According to Esther Williams, the producers of ''Neptune's Daughter'' had planned to use a different Loesser song, "[[On a Slow Boat to China]]", but studio censors thought it was too suggestive and replaced it with "Baby."<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.nytimes.com/1999/09/02/garden/at-home-with-esther-williams-swimming-upstream.html|title=AT HOME WITH: Esther Williams; Swimming Upstream|last=Purdum|first=Todd S.|newspaper=The New York Times|date=September 2, 1999|page=F1|access-date=August 8, 2021}}</ref>

The song won the [[22nd Academy Awards|1950 Academy Award]] for [[Academy Award for Best Original Song|Best Original Song]].<ref name="British Hit Singles & Albums">{{cite book|first=David|last=Roberts|year=2006|title=British Hit Singles & Albums |edition=19th|publisher=Guinness World Records Limited|location=London|isbn=1-904994-10-5|page=134}}</ref><ref name="Inman">{{cite web|last1=Inman|first1=Davis|title=Ray Charles and Betty Carter, "Baby, It's Cold Outside"|url=http://americansongwriter.com/2011/12/ray-charles-and-betty-carter-baby-its-cold-outside/|website=American Songwriter|access-date=February 20, 2018|date=December 19, 2011|archive-date=February 20, 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180220152057/http://americansongwriter.com/2011/12/ray-charles-and-betty-carter-baby-its-cold-outside/|url-status=live}}</ref>

==Lyrics==
The song is a [[Call and response (music)|call and response]] duet between two people: a host and a guest, usually performed by male and female singers respectively. Every line in the song features a statement from the guest followed by a response from the host. The lyrics consist of the host trying to convince the guest that she should stay for a romantic evening because he fears her getting too cold outside, despite the fact that she feels she should return home to her concerned family and neighbors.<ref>{{Cite book|publisher=Yale University Press |isbn=978-0300110517|last=Riis|first=Thomas Laurence|title=Frank Loesser|pages=[https://archive.org/details/frankloesser00riis/page/71 71]–73| date=January 1, 2008 |url=https://archive.org/details/frankloesser00riis |url-access=registration}}</ref>

In the original edition from Loesser's publishing company "Susan Publications, Inc." from 1948, the host's character name is "The Wolf" and the guest "The Mouse." It's unclear of the origin or meaning of these character names; they don't occur in the film version. This edition's tempo is given as "Loesserando", a humorous reference to the composer's name.<ref name="Baby, It's Cold Outside First Edition">{{cite web|url=https://www.sheetmusicsinger.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Baby-Its-Cold-Outside-1948.pdf|title=Baby, It's Cold Outside|publisher=sheetmusicsinger.com|date=October 2021|access-date=December 15, 2024}}</ref><ref name="Fresh Air Celebrates Frank Loesser's 100th Birthday">{{cite web|first=Michael|last=Feinstein|url=http://www.wbur.org/npr/128169934/fresh-air-celebrates-frank-loesser-s-100th-birthday|title=Fresh Air Celebrates Frank Loesser's 100th Birthday43|publisher=Wbur.org|date=June 29, 2010|access-date=April 9, 2018|archive-date=April 10, 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180410072213/http://www.wbur.org/npr/128169934/fresh-air-celebrates-frank-loesser-s-100th-birthday|url-status=live}}</ref>

===Criticism===
While studying in the United States in 1949, the future leader of the [[Muslim Brotherhood]], [[Sayyid Qutb]], reportedly expressed outrage with the song and its perceived decadent use at dance in a church.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.nytimes.com/2018/12/13/arts/music/baby-its-cold-outside-history-facts.html|title=How 'Baby, It's Cold Outside' Went From Parlor Act to Problematic|last=Fortin|first=Jaycn|newspaper=The New York Times|date=December 13, 2018|access-date=November 3, 2023}}</ref>

Since 2009, the song has also faced criticism among some listeners for the alleged implications of its lyrics, with elements such as the line, "Say, what's in this drink?" and the "wolf's" unrelenting pressure for the "mouse" to remain in spite of her repeated suggestions that she should go home being described as suggestive of [[sexual harassment]] or even [[date rape]].<ref name="WaPo">{{cite news|url=https://www.washingtonpost.com/posteverything/wp/2014/12/19/baby-its-cold-outside-was-once-an-anthem-for-progressive-women-what-happened/|title='Baby It's Cold Outside' was once an anthem for progressive women. What happened?|last=Hannun|first=Marya|newspaper=[[The Washington Post]]|date=December 19, 2014|access-date=January 14, 2015|archive-date=December 23, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141223230200/http://www.washingtonpost.com/posteverything/wp/2014/12/19/baby-its-cold-outside-was-once-an-anthem-for-progressive-women-what-happened/|url-status=live}}</ref><ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.wsj.com/articles/baby-its-cold-outside-lands-on-the-culture-wars-bonfire-11544625000|title='Baby, It's Cold Outside' Heats Up Culture Wars|last=Carlton|first=Jim|work=[[The Wall Street Journal]]|date=December 12, 2018|access-date=December 17, 2018|archive-date=December 17, 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20181217004312/https://www.wsj.com/articles/baby-its-cold-outside-lands-on-the-culture-wars-bonfire-11544625000|url-status=live}}</ref>

In 2018, the airing of the song was canceled by a number of radio stations including Canada's [[Canadian Broadcasting Corporation|CBC]] streaming service, after social media criticism and public pressure regarding the song's lyrics. This was referred to by some media outlets as part of a wider [[cancel culture]] at the time of works liable to offend people.<ref name=KG>{{cite news|url=https://www.cbc.ca/radio/q/blog/have-we-hit-peak-cancel-culture-1.4944521|title=Have we hit peak cancel culture?|last=Kinos-Goodin|first=Jesse|date=December 3, 2018|website=www.cbc.ca|publisher=CBC|access-date=March 16, 2019|archive-date=March 11, 2019|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190311125922/https://www.cbc.ca/radio/q/blog/have-we-hit-peak-cancel-culture-1.4944521|url-status=live}}</ref> On November 30, 2018, [[Cleveland]], Ohio, radio station [[WDOK]] ''Star 102'' announced that it had removed the song from its playlist due to its lyric content, based on listener input, amid the [[MeToo movement]].<ref>{{cite news |title=#MeToo movement causes Cleveland radio station to stop playing 'Baby It's Cold Outside' |url=https://www.nbc4i.com/news/state-news/-metoo-movement-causes-cleveland-radio-station-to-stop-playing-baby-its-cold-outside/1629116532 |access-date=December 4, 2018 |agency=NBC4 |publisher=NBC4i |date=November 30, 2018 |archive-date=April 3, 2019 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190403003310/https://www.nbc4i.com/news/state-news/-metoo-movement-causes-cleveland-radio-station-to-stop-playing-baby-its-cold-outside/1629116532 |url-status=live}}</ref> On December 4, 2018, the Canadian radio broadcasters [[Bell Media]], [[CBC Radio]], and [[Rogers Media]] followed suit.
