"'''Body Language'''" is a song by American rapper [[Kid Ink]]. The song was released on September 9, 2014 by Tha Alumni Music Group, 88 Classic and [[RCA Records]], as the [[lead single]] from his third [[studio album]] ''[[Full Speed (album)|Full Speed]]'' (2015).

The song, produced by [[Cashmere Cat]] and melody created by [[Stargate (production team)|Stargate]], features a [[guest appearance]]s from [[RCA Records]] label cohorts, singers [[Usher (musician)|Usher]] and [[Tinashe]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.hotnewhiphop.com/561772-kid-ink-body-language-feat-usher-tinashe-new-song |title=Kid Ink - Body Language Feat. Usher & Tinashe |publisher=Hotnewhiphop.com |date=2014-09-08 |access-date=2022-05-08}}</ref>

==Music video==
The accompanying [[music video]] for the track was directed by Darren Craig and filmed in Atlanta, Georgia.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.mtv.com/news/1961803/kid-ink-usher-tinashe-behind-the-scenes-body-language-video/ |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141016083138/http://www.mtv.com/news/1961803/kid-ink-usher-tinashe-behind-the-scenes-body-language-video/ |url-status=dead |archive-date=October 16, 2014 |title=Kid Ink, Usher And Tinashe Take You Behind-The-Scenes Of 'Body Language' Video |work=[[MTV]] |date=October 13, 2014 |access-date=October 5, 2015}}</ref> It was released on October 27, 2014.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.complex.com/music/2014/10/kid-ink-f-usher-tinashe-body-language-video |title=Watch Kid Ink's "Body Language" Video f/ Usher and Tinashe |work=[[Complex (magazine)|Complex]] |date=October 27, 2014 |access-date=October 5, 2015}}</ref>
