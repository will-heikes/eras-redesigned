"'''Want to Want Me'''" is a song recorded by American singer [[Jason Derulo]] for his fourth studio album, ''[[Everything Is 4]]'' (2015). It was released as the album's [[lead single]] on March 9, 2015.<ref name="iTunes">{{cite web|url=https://itunes.apple.com/album/want-to-want-me/id973769400?i=973769485&v0=9990&ign-mpt=uo%3D1|title=Want to Want Me - Single by Jason Derulo|publisher=[[iTunes Store]]|access-date=April 5, 2015|archive-date=April 2, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150402113717/https://itunes.apple.com/album/want-to-want-me/id973769400?i=973769485&v0=9990&ign-mpt=uo%3D1|url-status=dead}}</ref> The song was written by Derulo, [[Sam Martin (singer)|Sam Martin]], [[Lindy Robbins]], [[Mitch Allan]], and its producer, [[Ian Kirkpatrick (record producer)|Ian Kirkpatrick]].<ref>{{cite news|url=http://www.rap-up.com/2015/03/09/new-music-jason-derulo-want-to-want-me/|title=New Music: Jason Derulo – 'Want To Want Me'|work=[[Rap-Up]]|date=March 9, 2015|access-date=April 5, 2015|archive-date=April 10, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150410230315/http://www.rap-up.com/2015/03/09/new-music-jason-derulo-want-to-want-me/|url-status=dead}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|author=Corner, Lewis|url=http://www.digitalspy.co.uk/music/news/a637313/jason-derulo-gets-steamy-in-the-bedroom-for-want-to-want-me-video.html|title=Jason Derulo gets steamy in the bedroom for 'Want to Want Me' video|website=[[Digital Spy]]|date=March 23, 2015|access-date=April 5, 2015|archive-date=March 24, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150324155837/http://www.digitalspy.co.uk/music/news/a637313/jason-derulo-gets-steamy-in-the-bedroom-for-want-to-want-me-video.html|url-status=live}}</ref>

"Want to Want Me" peaked at number five on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]], becoming Derulo's sixth top ten hit in the United States.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/wiz-khalifa-see-you-again-no-1-hot-100/|magazine=Billboard|title=Wiz Khalifa's 'See You Again' Stays at No. 1 on Hot 100|first=Gary|last=Trust|date=June 10, 2015|access-date=June 10, 2015|archive-date=November 20, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151120124604/http://www.billboard.com/articles/columns/chart-beat/6590883/wiz-khalifa-see-you-again-no-1-hot-100|url-status=live}}</ref> The song topped the [[UK Singles Chart]] with first-week sales of 127,000 units. It marked Derulo's fourth number-one single in the United Kingdom, following "[[In My Head (Jason Derulo song)|In My Head]]" (2010), "[[Don't Wanna Go Home]]" (2011) and "[[Talk Dirty (Jason Derulo song)|Talk Dirty]]" (2013).<ref>{{cite web|author=Finbow, Katy|url=http://www.digitalspy.co.uk/music/news/a650187/jason-derulos-want-to-want-me-debuts-at-number-1-in-uk-singles-chart.html|title=Jason Derulo's 'Want to Want Me' debuts at number 1 in UK singles chart|website=Digital Spy|date=May 31, 2015|access-date=May 31, 2015|archive-date=June 1, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150601011704/http://www.digitalspy.co.uk/music/news/a650187/jason-derulos-want-to-want-me-debuts-at-number-1-in-uk-singles-chart.html|url-status=live}}</ref> "Want to Want Me" remained at number one in the UK for four weeks.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/jason-derulo-scores-fourth-week-at-official-singles-chart-number-1__9877/|title=Jason Derulo scores fourth week at Official Singles Chart Number 1|last=Myers|first=Justin|date=June 21, 2015|publisher=[[Official Charts Company]]|access-date=October 10, 2015|archive-date=September 24, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150924172321/http://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/jason-derulo-scores-fourth-week-at-official-singles-chart-number-1__9877/|url-status=live}}</ref> It also reached number one in Austria, Israel, and South Africa, as well as the top ten in seventeen additional countries. It was the third-bestselling song of 2015 in Israel. It is [[Music recording certification|certified]] Gold or higher in eighteen countries.

Derulo said that the song was the first one recorded for his album ''[[Everything Is 4]]'', and he decided to release it as the lead single after having several friends listen to it.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.sorrisi.com/musica/jason-derulo-everything-is-4-intervista-want-to-want-me/|title=Jason Derulo e "Everything is 4", l'intervista - TV Sorrisi & Canzoni|date=May 25, 2015|access-date=June 15, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170622030007/http://www.sorrisi.com/musica/jason-derulo-everything-is-4-intervista-want-to-want-me|archive-date=June 22, 2017|url-status=dead}}</ref>

The song was originally intended to be on [[Chris Brown]]'s 2014 album ''[[X (Chris Brown album)|X]]'', and was featured on the album's original track listing, titled "Want You to Want Me",<ref>{{cite web | url=https://www.capitalxtra.com/artists/chris-brown/news/x-tracklist/ | title=Chris Brown's 'X' Tracklist Includes Kendrick Lamar, R Kelly and Aaliyah | website=Capital XTRA }}</ref> but the singer eventually decided to replace it with the track "Drunk Texting".<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.daringtodo.com/lang/it/2015/10/26/chris-brown-ha-scartato-la-hit-want-to-want-me-di-jason-derulo/|title=Chris Brown ha scartato la hit Want To Want Me di Jason Derulo - DaringToDo.com|access-date=June 15, 2016|archive-date=March 5, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160305011126/http://www.daringtodo.com/lang/it/2015/10/26/chris-brown-ha-scartato-la-hit-want-to-want-me-di-jason-derulo/|url-status=live}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.rnbjunk.com/want-to-want-me-di-jason-derulo-e-uno-scarto-di-chris-brown-spunta-una-demo/|title=Want To Want Me di Jason Derulo è uno scarto di Chris Brown? Spunta una demo|last=Davide|date=October 25, 2015|access-date=June 15, 2016|archive-date=January 25, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160125222751/http://www.rnbjunk.com/want-to-want-me-di-jason-derulo-e-uno-scarto-di-chris-brown-spunta-una-demo/|url-status=live}}</ref>

According to some news reports, also [[Olly Murs]] recorded a version of "Want to Want Me", wanting to release the song as his single, but he was forced to reject it as his voice wasn't high enough to reach the high notes. Murs later admitted to regretting turning it down after seeing how popular of a smash hit it became.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.femalefirst.co.uk/music/musicnews/olly-murs-gutted-passed-want-want-jason-derulo-879875.html|title=Olly Murs gutted he passed 'Want to Want Me' to Jason Derulo|date=September 29, 2015 |access-date=August 19, 2018|archive-date=August 20, 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180820074605/https://www.femalefirst.co.uk/music/musicnews/olly-murs-gutted-passed-want-want-jason-derulo-879875.html|url-status=live}}</ref>

==Music composition==
The song is written and recorded in E♭ [[Major scale|major]], running at 112-116 beats per minute. It has a main chord progression of E♭-Cm.<ref>[http://www.musicnotes.com/sheetmusic/mtd.asp?ppn=MN0149267 Jason Derulo "Want To Want Me" Sheet Music] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151030065751/http://www.musicnotes.com/sheetmusic/mtd.asp?ppn=MN0149267 |date=October 30, 2015 }} musicnotes</ref>

==Critical reception==
''[[Time (magazine)|Time]]'' named "Want to Want Me" the eighth-best song of 2015.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=http://time.com/4132832/top-10-best-songs-2/|title=Best Songs of 2015|magazine=Time|access-date=January 14, 2016|archive-date=January 14, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160114124818/http://time.com/4132832/top-10-best-songs-2/|url-status=live}}</ref> ''[[Pitchfork (website)|Pitchfork]]'' praised it for being "Derulo's best song, a crisp, feel-good blast of infatuated power-pop that feels warmer and more lived-in than any of his previous singles."<ref>{{Cite web | title=Jason Derulo: Everything Is 4 Album Review {{!}} Pitchfork | url=https://pitchfork.com/reviews/albums/20669-everything-is-4/ | access-date=2026-09-16 | website=pitchfork.com}}</ref> ''[[Stereogum]]'' also praised the track, describing it as "an absolute monster of a pop song".<ref>{{cite web|first= Tom |last= Breihan |title= The Number Ones: Jason Derulo's "Whatcha Say" |website= [[Stereogum]] |date= June 2, 2023 |url= https://www.stereogum.com/2225104/the-number-ones-jason-derulos-whatcha-say/columns/the-number-ones/|quote= ...but ''Everything Is 4'' also had “Want To Want Me,” an absolute monster of a pop song that went all the way to #5.|accessdate= June 2, 2023}}</ref>

==Commercial performance==
"Want to Want Me" debuted at number 45 on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] chart on March 28, 2015. It jumped to number ten on the week of May 9, 2015,<ref>{{Cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/wiz-khalifa-hot-100-jason-derulo/|title=Wiz Khalifa Rules Hot 100, Jason Derulo Hits Top 10|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]] |access-date=August 8, 2018|archive-date=August 8, 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180808050224/https://www.billboard.com/articles/columns/chart-beat/6546364/wiz-khalifa-hot-100-jason-derulo|url-status=live}}</ref> before reaching number five dated June 20.<ref>{{Cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/wiz-khalifa-see-you-again-no-1-hot-100/|title=Wiz Khalifa's 'See You Again' Knocks 'Uptown Funk' off No. 1 on Hot 100|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]] |access-date=August 8, 2018|archive-date=August 8, 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180808043329/https://www.billboard.com/articles/columns/chart-beat/6590883/wiz-khalifa-see-you-again-no-1-hot-100|url-status=live}}</ref>

The song's accompanying [[music video]] was directed by [[Colin Tilley]].
