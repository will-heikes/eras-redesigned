"'''How Many Times'''" is the third single from [[DJ Khaled]]'s eighth studio album ''[[I Changed a Lot]]'' (2015). It features [[Chris Brown]], [[Lil Wayne]] and [[Big Sean]], and was released on May 12, 2015.

The song, produced by [[Lee on the Beats]], Bkorn, and Khaled himself, peaked at number 68 on the ''Billboard'' Hot 100. It was the first single by Khaled released under [[RED Distribution]]/[[Sony Music Entertainment|Sony]] and his own label [[We the Best Music]], after parting ways with [[Cash Money Records|Cash Money]] and [[Republic Records]]. The song was panned by critics, many of whom deemed it [[misogyny|misogynistic]].<ref>{{Cite web| title=The symbolic annihilation of the black woman in rap videos: a content analysis | url=https://repository.arizona.edu/bitstream/handle/10150/624121/azu_etd_15407_sip1_m.pdf?sequence=1&isAllowed=y | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230818210909/https://repository.arizona.edu/bitstream/handle/10150/624121/azu_etd_15407_sip1_m.pdf?sequence=1&isAllowed=y | archive-date=2023-08-18}}</ref>

A [[music video]] for the song was released on May 11, 2015.<ref>{{cite journal |url=http://www.rap-up.com/2015/05/11/video-dj-khaled-chris-brown-lil-wayne-big-sean-how-many-times/ |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150513055600/http://www.rap-up.com/2015/05/11/video-dj-khaled-chris-brown-lil-wayne-big-sean-how-many-times/ |url-status=dead |archive-date=May 13, 2015 |title=VIDEO: DJ KHALED FEAT. [[Ace Hood]] makes a cameo. This quote would then quickly evolve into a [[Internet meme|meme]] and has since then been featured in many of Khaled's later singles, most notably "[[For Free (DJ Khaled song)|For Free]]", which features Canadian rapper [[Drake (musician)|Drake]], and in "[[Do You Mind (DJ Khaled song)|Do You Mind]]", which features [[Nicki Minaj]], [[Chris Brown]], [[August Alsina]], [[Jeremih]], [[Future (rapper)|Future]], and [[Rick Ross]].
