"'''Applause'''" is a song by American singer [[Lady Gaga]] from her third studio album, ''[[Artpop]]'' (2013). It was released as the album's lead single through [[Interscope Records]] on August 12, 2013. Written and produced by Gaga, [[DJ White Shadow]], Dino Zisis, and [[Nick Monson]], additional songwriters included [[Tchami|Martin Bresso]], Nicolas Mercier, Julien Arias and [[DJ Snake|William Grigahcine]]. "Applause" was inspired by the cheering of her fans, which kept her motivated during the months she toured with the [[Born This Way Ball]] in pain, before cancelling it due to a hip injury, and pays respect to the art of performance. It is an [[electropop]], [[dance-pop]] and [[Eurodance]] song built around [[synthesizers]] and [[hi-NRG]] beats, with lyrics addressing how Gaga is dependent upon her fans' adoration and how she lives to perform.

The song received positive reviews for its catchy chorus, likened to Gaga's debut album, ''[[The Fame]]'' (2008). "Applause" topped charts in Greece, Hungary, Lebanon, and Spain and reached the top 10 in numerous countries. It was certified multi-platinum in Canada, Sweden, and the U.S.

An accompanying music video directed by fashion photography duo [[Inez and Vinoodh]] was released on August 19, 2013, and broadcast on jumbotrons across [[Times Square]]. The video received positive reviews from critics, who saw it as a profile of Gaga herself and noted references to [[German Expressionist cinema]] and [[Andy Warhol]]. To promote the record, Gaga opened the [[2013 MTV Video Music Awards]] with a performance of the song in which she represented her career through several on-stage wardrobe changes. She also performed it live on ''[[Good Morning America]]'', ''[[Saturday Night Live]]'', and many of her tours and residencies.

Initial recording sessions for ''Artpop'' coincided with the [[Born This Way Ball]]: up to fifty songs were written and considered for inclusion.<ref name="freealbum">{{cite web|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/1671501/lady-gaga-born-this-way-follow-up/|title=Lady Gaga 'Already Working' On Born This Way Follow-Up|last=Montgomery|first=James|date=September 26, that she intended to make audiences have "a really good time" with ''Artpop'', engineering the album to mirror "a night at the club".<ref name=mtv222>{{cite web|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/1714293/lady-gaga-artpop-kiis-ustream/|title=Lady Gaga's ARTPOP Isn't An Album, But 'A Night At The Club'|last=Vena|first=Jocelyn|date=September 18, A hip surgery in February 2013 caused the singer to take a six-month hiatus, during which she studied literature and music with her creative team, the Haus of Gaga. It also allowed her to review and enhance her musical direction, which she described as a meticulous "gazing process".<ref name="WWD"/> "Applause" was written and produced by Gaga and DJ White Shadow, along with [[Nick Monson]] and Dino Zisis while on the road for her [[Born This Way Ball]] tour in 2012. Other songwriters working on the track included [[Tchami|Martin Bresso]], Nicolas Mercier, Julien Arias and [[DJ Snake|William Grigahcine]].<ref name="WWD">{{cite web|title=Lady Gaga: 'I'm Every Icon'|url=http://www.wwd.com/eye/people/lady-gaga-im-every-icon-7068388|work=[[Women's Wear Daily]]|first=Jessica|last=Iredale|date=July 28, 2013|access-date=August 11, 2013|url-status=live|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130814114412/http://www.wwd.com/eye/people/lady-gaga-im-every-icon-7068388|archive-date=August 14, 2013}}</ref><ref name=ApplauseSnippet>{{cite web|title=Lady Gaga new single 'Applause' snippet leaks|url=http://www.digitalspy.com/music/news/a505740/lady-gaga-new-single-applause-snippet-leaks-listen|first=Sam|last=Rigby|work=Digital Spy|date=August 10, 2013|access-date=August 11, 2013|url-status=live|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160402222452/http://www.digitalspy.com/music/news/a505740/lady-gaga-new-single-applause-snippet-leaks-listen/|archive-date=April 2, 2016}}</ref> Talking to [[Sirius XM Radio]], Gaga explained the inspiration behind the songwriting of "Applause":
<blockquote>
"I realized it was the applause of the fans that really kept me going. Because I would be ready to go onstage and just be crying hysterically not understanding even how I was feeling. I was feeling very dizzy, I had a lot of vertigo, I had pain but it's like fuck if I know what hurts the most because I'd been on tour for a year. But I didn't want to let them down and I just couldn't cancel because the thought of leaving 50,000 kids in the arena just broke my heart. So I went out every night and I played and I played and I played until I couldn't walk one night."<ref name="sirius"/>
</blockquote>

Recording sessions for the song took place at [[Record Plant Studios]], Hollywood, California, and Platinum Sound Recording Studio, [[New York City]] by Dave Russell, with Benjamin Rice and Andrew Robertson working as assistants. Russell also did the [[audio mixing (recorded music)|mixing]] of the track at Record Plant and at Heard It! Studios. Additional mixing was carried out by Bill Malina, with assistance from Rice and Ghazi Hourani. Rick Pearl performed the additional [[programming (music)|programming]] and Gene Grimaldi completed the [[audio mastering]] at Oasis Mastering Studios in Burbank, California.<ref name="liner">{{cite AV media notes|title=Artpop|title-link=Artpop|others=Lady Gaga|year=2013|chapter=Digital booklet|first=Lady|last=Gaga|page=13|type=Liner notes|publisher=[[Interscope Records]]|id=687955814|location=Santa Monica, California}}</ref>

"Applause" is set in the time signature of [[common time]] and has a [[tempo]] of 140 [[beats per minute]]. It is composed in the key of [[G minor]], following the [[chord progression]] of Gm–F–E{{music|flat}}–Cm–F–Gm, with Gaga's vocals spanning from [[F (musical note)|F<sub>3</sub>]] to [[A (musical note)|A<sub>5</sub>]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.musicnotes.com/sheetmusic/mtd.asp?ppn=MN0122709|title=Lady Gaga&nbsp;– 'Applause'&nbsp;– Sheet Music|access-date=September 3, 2015|publisher=Musicnotes.com. [[Universal Music Publishing Group]]|url-status=live|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160306150619/http://www.musicnotes.com/sheetmusic/mtd.asp?ppn=MN0122709|archive-date=March 6, 2016|date=August 14, 2013}}</ref> The record has been pinned to the [[electropop]], [[dance-pop]] and [[Eurodance]] [[music genre]]s,<ref name=WP/><ref name="Slant"/><ref name=huffpost/> and has been cited as returning Gaga to her career roots by mirroring the sounds of her [[The Fame|debut album]].<ref name=huffpost/><ref name=mtvcomp>{{cite web|last=Montgomery|first=James|title=Lady Gaga Rush-Releases 'Applause' Gaga drops her new single a week early ... just hours after Katy Perry releases 'Roar.'|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/1712188/lady-gaga-applause-single-released/|publisher=MTV News|access-date=August 21, 2013|date=August 12, 2013|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141025173933/http://www.mtv.com/news/1712188/lady-gaga-applause-single-released/|archive-date=October 25, 2014}}</ref> Evan Sawdey of ''[[PopMatters]]'' described it as "closer to '[[Just Dance (song)|Just Dance]]' in style over any of her ''[[Born This Way (album)|Born This Way]]''-era hits, but in a much more aggressive fashion, catering to the gradual [[Electronic dance music|EDM]]-stranglehold of pop radio."<ref name="Popmatters"/> Erin Coulehan of ''[[Rolling Stone]]'' concurred, calling it "a throwback to '[[LoveGame]]'-era Gaga".<ref name="RS"/>

==Chart performance==
In the United States, "Applause" did not enter the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] in its first week.<ref name="applause1stweek">{{cite magazine|last=Trust|first=Gary|title=Robin Thicke Leads Hot 100 For 10th Week, Katy Perry's 'Roar' Debuts|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/5650672/robin-thicke-leads-hot-100-for-10th-week-katy-perrys-roar-debuts|magazine=Billboard|access-date=August 14, 2013|date=August 14, 2013|url-status=live|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130816033503/http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/5650672/robin-thicke-leads-hot-100-for-10th-week-katy-perrys-roar-debuts|archive-date=August 16, 2013}}</ref> It also ranked below the top 75 threshold of the US [[Hot 100 Airplay (Radio Songs)|Radio Songs]] chart, with 16 million audience impressions across 210 stations, with the track officially impacting radio stations on August 19, 2013. "Applause" reached number one on the [[Dance/Electronic Songs]] chart,<ref name="applause1stweek"/> and entered the US [[Mainstream Top 40 (Pop Songs)|Pop Songs Chart]] at number 20; the highest debut on that chart, by a solo female artist in 2013. The following week, "Applause" debuted at number six on the ''Billboard'' Hot 100. Following its radio and retail release the track charted on Digital Songs at number three, Streaming Songs at number nine and Radio Songs at number forty.<ref>{{cite magazine|first=Gary|last=Trust|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/5660927/robin-thicke-no-1-katy-perry-no-2-on-hot-100|title=Robin Thicke No. 1, Katy Perry No. 2 On Hot 100|magazine=Billboard|date=August 21, 2013|access-date=March 9, 2016|url-status=live|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130821172152/http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/5660927/robin-thicke-no-1-katy-perry-no-2-on-hot-100|archive-date=August 21, 2013}}</ref>

And she's sort of dragging that leg as a trophy and making it back on stage as a fully-realized, complete new person".<ref name="director">{{cite web|last=Vena|first=Jocelyn|title=Lady Gaga's 'Applause': Director Cracks Open Five 'Hip' Scenes!|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/1712597/lady-gaga-applause-director-scenes/|publisher=MTV News|date=August 19, 2013|access-date=September 6, 2015|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160412161431/http://www.mtv.com/news/1712597/lady-gaga-applause-director-scenes/|archive-date=April 12, 2016}}</ref> [[Jo Ratcliffe]], a [[London]]-based illustrator known for her hand-drawn graphic design, was hired to provide animation effects for the video. ==Live performances and other appearances==
[[File:Lady Gaga Applause MTV VMA 2013 4.jpg|thumb|left|Gaga performing "Applause" at the [[2013 MTV Video Music Awards]], where she portrayed looks from her previous eras|alt=Lady Gaga singing in a sparkling blue and black outfit with a short blonde bob wig, raising one arm while flanked by three dancers in black against a green backdrop.]]

"Applause" was performed live for the first time at the [[2013 MTV Video Music Awards]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/1711264/lady-gaga-2013-vma-performer/|title=Lady Gaga To Return at the 2013 MTV Video Music Awards!|publisher=MTV News|date=July 25, 2013|access-date=August 12, 2013|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140511172940/http://www.mtv.com/news/1711264/lady-gaga-2013-vma-performer/|archive-date=May 11, 2014}}</ref> Gaga began the performance wearing a square-shaped head piece and an all-white cape as pre-recorded boos were quickly replaced by sound of cheers. Throughout the performance she cycled through costumes and wigs she had previously employed throughout her career.<ref name="vmacareer">{{cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/5672589/lady-gaga-opens-2013-mtv-vmas-with-a-round-of-applause|title=Lady Gaga Opens 2013 MTV VMAs With A Round Of 'Applause'|last=Payne|first=Chris|date=August 25, 2013|access-date=September 4, 2015|magazine=Billboard|url-status=live|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150919173805/http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/5672589/lady-gaga-opens-2013-mtv-vmas-with-a-round-of-applause|archive-date=September 19, 2015}}</ref> She danced with a [[Jeff Koons]] blue gazing ball, and had her face painted in the style of the single cover by one of her dancers.
