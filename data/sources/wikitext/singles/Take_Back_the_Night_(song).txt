"'''Take Back the Night'''" is a song by American singer [[Justin Timberlake]] from his fourth studio album, ''[[The 20/20 Experience – 2 of 2]]'' (2013). It was released by [[RCA Records]] as the [[lead single]] from the album on July 12, 2013. "Take Back the Night" was written and produced by Timberlake, [[Timothy "Timbaland" Mosley]] and [[Jerome "J-Roc" Harmon]], with additional writing from [[James Fauntleroy]]. Musically, it is a [[disco]] and [[Contemporary R&B|R&B]] song that addresses a potential love interest.

"Take Back the Night" received mixed-to-positive reviews from music critics, noticing its similarity to [[Michael Jackson]]'s works and "poppier" accessibility compared to Timberlake's previous album, but criticized its length. Commercially, it reached the top forty positions in most countries, peaking at number 29 on the ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' [[Hot 100]], with higher success on the [[Pop Airplay]] charts at number 14 and number eight on the [[Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs]] charts. The song gathered controversy with the [[Take Back the Night Foundation]], an anti-rape organization, which expressed concerns about the similarities between the song's title and the organization's name.

The music video for "Take Back the Night" was directed by Jeff Nicholas, [[Jonathan Craven]], and Darren Craig, and premiered on July 30 on Timberlake's YouTube channel. In the music video, Timberlake walks through [[Chinatown, Manhattan|Chinatown]] and performs on the sidewalk, later riding in a "classic car" during the night. The song was part of the [[set list]] for Timberlake's [[Legends of the Summer Stadium Tour]] (2013) with rapper [[Jay-Z]] and his fifth solo tour, [[The 20/20 Experience World Tour]] (2013–15).

== Production and release ==
"Take Back the Night" was written by Timberlake, [[Timothy "Timbaland" Mosley]], [[Jerome "J-Roc" Harmon]], and [[James Fauntleroy]], and produced by Timbaland, Timberlake, and Harmon. Timberlake arranged and produced his vocals, which were recorded at Jungle City Studios in New York City, New York. Harmon provided keyboards for it, while Elliott Ives played the guitar. Benjamin Wright and The Benjamin Wright Orchestra played the horns, while Terry Santiel played the percussion. [[Chris Godbey]], with assistance from Alejandro Baima, engineered it, Dave Kutch mastered it, and [[Jimmy Douglass]] mixed it with Godbey and Timberlake at Jungle City Studios.<ref name="GER CD single" />

While releasing and promoting ''[[The 20/20 Experience]]'', Timberlake revealed that he only released half of the album, and announced and that the second half would be released in November.<ref name="hollywoodreporter">{{cite news |date=March 18, 2013 |title=Justin Timberlake Confirms Second '20/20 Experience' Album Coming |url=http://www.hollywoodreporter.com/earshot/justin-timberlake-second-20-20-429619 |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131025200134/http://www.hollywoodreporter.com/earshot/justin-timberlake-second-20-20-429619 |archive-date=October 25, 2013 |access-date=December 28, 2013 |newspaper=[[The Hollywood Reporter]]}}</ref> In May 2013, he announced that it would be released in September.<ref>{{cite magazine |last=Letkemann |first=Jessica |date=May 5, 2013 |title=Justin Timberlake Tweets '20/20 Experience' Part 2 Due September 30 |url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/1560683/justin-timberlake-tweets-2020-experience-part-2-due-september-30 |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130604031118/http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/1560683/justin-timberlake-tweets-2020-experience-part-2-due-september-30 |archive-date=June 4, 2013 |access-date=June 6, 2013 |magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]] |publisher=[[Prometheus Global Media]]}}</ref> On July 10, 2013, Timberlake posted a 45-second video on his official YouTube channel. In another scene, he is seen riding in a "classic car" during the night.<ref>{{cite web |author=RTT Staff Writer |date=July 22, 2013 |title=Justin Timberlake Shoots 'Take Back the Night' Video |url=http://www.rttnews.com/2154590/justin-timberlake-shoots-take-back-the-night-video.aspx |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240302092304/https://www.rttnews.com/2154590/justin-timberlake-shoots-take-back-the-night-video.aspx |archive-date=March 2, 2024 |access-date=July 14, 2013 |publisher=RTTNews}}</ref> The "Take Back the Night" video was directed by Jeff Nicholas, [[Jonathan Craven]], and Darren Craig. Beginning on July 30 at 7 am, fans can visit a custom website for a "virtual journey throughout New York City", which will offer fun facts about the music video. The song was a little better on the [[Pop Songs]] chart, debuting at number 20, becoming the greatest gainer of the week,<ref name="billboardpopdebut">{{cite magazine |last=Trust |first=Gary |date=July 22, 2013 |title=Chart Highlights: Jonas Brothers, One Direction Debut On Pop Songs |url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/4218620/chart-highlights-jonas-brothers-one-direction-debut-on-pop-songs |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140221065742/http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/4218620/chart-highlights-jonas-brothers-one-direction-debut-on-pop-songs |archive-date=February 21, 2014 |access-date=December 31, 2013 |magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]}}</ref> before peaking at number 14.<ref name="Pop Airplay" /> On the [[Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs]] chart, it reached the top ten, peaking at number 8, becoming his sixth top ten on the R&B charts.<ref name="billboardr&bdebut">{{cite magazine |last=Ramirez |first=Rauly |date=August 12, 2013 |title=Justin Timberlake's 'Take Back the Night' Hits Top 10 on Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs |url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/justin-timberlakes-take-back-the-night-hits-top-10-on-hot-rbhip/ |access-date=December 31, 2013 |magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]}}</ref> On the ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' airplay charts, it reached number 23 on the [[Adult R&B Songs]] chart,<ref name="Adult R&B Songs" /> number 21 on the [[Adult Pop Airplay]] chart,<ref name="Adult Pop Airplay" /> and at number 10 on the [[Rhythmic Airplay]] chart.<ref name="Rhythmic Airplay" /> In the United Kingdom, the song also failed to reach the top twenty, peaking at number 22, remaining one of his lowest charting solo singles, only losing to "[[Tunnel Vision (Justin Timberlake song)|Tunnel Vision]]".<ref name="uk">{{cite web |title=Justin Timberlake &#124; Artist &#124; Official Charts |url=http://www.officialcharts.com/artist/_/justin%20timberlake/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130307085827/http://www.officialcharts.com/artist/_/justin%20timberlake |archive-date=March 7, 2013 |access-date=December 31, 2013 |publisher=[[Official Charts Company]]}}</ref> Also in the United Kingdom, the song reached number 4 on the UK Airplay chart.<ref name="UK Airplay" />

In Australia, the song missed the top forty, only reaching number 57.<ref name="Australia" /> However, it reached number 11 on the urban chart.<ref name="Australia Urban" /> In Belgium, it reached number 26 in [[Flanders]],<ref name="Flanders" /> and number 6 on its urban chart,<ref name="Flanders 3" /> but missed the top forty in [[Wallonia]], only reaching number 45.<ref name="Wallonia" /> In Canada, it reached number 23.<ref name="Canada" /> In [[Denmark]], it reached number 37.<ref name="Denmark" /> In Ireland, the song missed the top forty, only reaching number 49,<ref name="irish" /> while in Italy, the song only reached number 54.<ref name="Italy" /> In Germany, the song only peaked at number 52.<ref name="german" /> In Japan, the song reached number 15.<ref name="Japan" /> In the [[Netherlands]], the song reached number 23 on the [[Dutch Top 40]],<ref name="Dutch Top 40" /> and at number 38 on [[Single Top 100]].<ref name="Single Top 100" /> In France, the song was his only single to miss the top forty, only peaking at number 85.<ref name="france" /> In Switzerland, "Take Back the Night" became Timberlake's lowest charting solo single, only reaching number 48 - his lowest was 2003's "[[I'm Lovin' It (song)|I'm Lovin' It]]", with a peak of number 47.<ref name="swiss" />
