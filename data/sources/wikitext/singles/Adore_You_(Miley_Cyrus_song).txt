"'''Adore You'''" is a song recorded by American singer [[Miley Cyrus]]. It is the opening track of her fourth studio album ''[[Bangerz]]'' (2013) and was released as its third and final single on December 17, 2013, by [[RCA Records]]. The song was written and produced by [[Oren Yoel]], with additional songwriting provided by [[Stacy Barthe]]. "Adore You" is a [[Pop music|pop]] and [[Contemporary R&B|R&B]] ballad in which Cyrus discusses her affection towards her boyfriend.

"Adore You" received favorable reviews from music critics, who complimented its overall production and Cyrus' vocals; it was further recognized as being among the more conservative offerings from its parent album. The song peaked at number 21 on the [[Billboard Hot 100]]. It performed moderately on national [[record chart]]s throughout Europe and Oceania, with a gold certification in Australia. The [[music video]] for "Adore You" was officially premiered through [[Vevo]] on December 26, 2013. It depicts Cyrus suggestively posing in a bed and a bathtub, and is interspersed with [[night vision]] footage that resembles a [[celebrity sex tape]].

"Adore You" is a pop<ref>{{Cite journal|last=Hogan|first=Marc|url=https://www.spin.com/2013/12/miley-cyrus-adore-you-video/|title=Miley Cyrus Masturbates for Attention in 'Adore You' Video|date=December 26, 2013|journal=[[Spin (magazine)|Spin]]|location=New York|access-date=January 19, 2014}}</ref> and R&B<ref>{{cite web|url=http://popdust.com/2013/10/02/miley-cyrus-bangerz-reviewed-adore-you/|title=Miley Cyrus' "Bangerz" Reviewed: "Adore You"|last=Drill|first=Christina|publisher=Popdust|date=October 2, 2013|access-date=April 12, 2014}}</ref> ballad, which lasts for four minutes and 38 seconds.<ref name="LinerNotes"/> The song performs in [[common time]], at a [[tempo]] of 60 [[beats per minute]]. Played in the key of [[C major]], the chord structure of C—Fmaj7—Am is followed throughout, and Cyrus's vocal range spans from G<sub>3</sub> to A<sub>4</sub>.<ref name=sheetmusic>{{cite web|url=http://www.musicnotes.com/sheetmusic/mtdFPE.asp?ppn=mn0126285|title=Miley Cyrus "Adore You" Sheet Music - Download & Print|date=15 October 2013|publisher=Music Notes|access-date=November 2, 2013}}</ref> Its lyrics discuss "power imbalance in love";<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.theguardian.com/culture/2013/oct/04/miley-cyrus-bangerz-review|title=Miley Cyrus: Bangerz – review|last=Empire|first=Kitty|newspaper=[[The Guardian]]|date=October 4, 2013|access-date=February 15, 2014}}</ref> in the chorus, she states her affection towards her boyfriend through the lines "When you say you need me / Know I need you more / Boy, I adore you", although Kyle Fowle of ''[[Slant Magazine]]'' felt that Cyrus' reliance in her partner is admittedly detrimental to her personal well-being as the song continues.<ref name="Slant"/>

In December 2013, Cyrus announced that "Adore You" would be serviced as the third single from her fourth studio album, ''[[Bangerz]]'' (2013). Its launch followed the tracks "[[We Can't Stop]]" and "[[Wrecking Ball (Miley Cyrus song)|Wrecking Ball]]", which were respectively released as the first and second singles from the record earlier that year.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.entertainmentwise.com/news/136191/No-Wrecking-Ball-Miley-Cyrus-Releases-Demure-Artwork-For-Adore-You-Single|title=No Wrecking Ball? ==Critical reception==
Upon its release, "Adore You" received favorable reviews from music critics, who complimented its overall production and Cyrus' vocals. Heather Phares from [[AllMusic]] felt that opening ''Bangerz'' with an "unabashedly romantic slow song" was "almost as bold a move as the publicity events that preceded the album's release."<ref name="Allmusic">{{cite web|url=http://www.allmusic.com/album/bangerz-mw0002577863|title=Bangerz - Miley Cyrus|last=Phares|first=Heather|publisher=[[AllMusic]]. [[Rovi Corporation]]|date=October 8, 2013|access-date=October 6, 2013}}</ref> Jon Dolan of ''[[Rolling Stone]]'', who had a similar opinion to Phares', called the track a "starkly beautiful album opener".<ref name="RollingStone">{{cite magazine|url=https://www.rollingstone.com/music/albumreviews/bangerz-20131004|title=Miley Cyrus 'Bangerz' Review|last=Dolan|first=Jon|magazine=[[Rolling Stone]]|publisher=[[Jann Wenner|Wenner Media, LLC]]|date=October 4, 2013|access-date=October 4, 2013}}</ref> Nick Catucci of ''[[Entertainment Weekly]]'' complimented the track for "[flaunting] a key facet of her versatile voice: the throaty diva swoon."<ref name="EntertainmentWeeklyReview">{{cite magazine|url=http://www.ew.com/ew/article/0,,20740686,00.html|title=Bangerz - Miley Cyrus|last=Catucci|first=Nick|magazine=[[Entertainment Weekly]]|publisher=[[Time Inc.|Time]]|date=October 1, 2013|access-date=October 1, 2013|archive-date=October 1, 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131001173442/http://www.ew.com/ew/article/0,,20740686,00.html|url-status=dead}}</ref> Kitty Empire from ''[[The Guardian]]'' suggested that "Adore You" was one of the "least showy and most successful cuts" from ''Bangerz''.<ref name="TheGuardian">{{cite web|url=https://www.theguardian.com/culture/2013/oct/03/miley-cyrus-bangerz-review|title=Miley Cyrus: Bangerz – review|last=Sullivan|first=Caroline|newspaper=[[The Guardian]]|date=October 3, 2013|access-date=October 4, 2013}}</ref>  Writing for [[Slant Magazine]], Kyle Fowle appreciated the "careful balance of vulnerability and strength" for "[elevating the] potentially tired themes [of destructive dependence]".<ref name="Slant">{{cite web|url=http://www.slantmagazine.com/music/review/miley-cyrus-bangerz |title=Miley Cyrus: Bangerz: Music Review|last=Fowle|first=Kyle|publisher=[[Slant Magazine]]|date=October 6, 2013|access-date=October 10, 2013}}</ref> Jason Lipshutz of ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' thought that the song was "emotionally mature but not overly provocative" and a "[wise]" choice for opening the album.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/review/5740707/miley-cyrus-bangerz-track-by-track-review|title=Miley Cyrus, 'Bangerz': Track-By-Track Review|last=Lipshutz|first=Jason|magazine=Billboard|publisher=Prometheus Global Media|date=October 1, 2013|access-date=January 3, 2014}}</ref> However, in a more mixed review, Evan Sawdey from [[PopMatters]] felt that the track "[featured] almost zero rising action [and did] little to drive the point home."<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.popmatters.com/review/175706-miley-cyrus-bangerz/|title=Miley Cyrus: Bangerz|last=Sawdey|first=Evan|publisher=[[PopMatters]]|date=October 6, 2013|access-date=October 10, 2013}}</ref>

==Commercial performance==
"Adore You" was the highest-charting non-single from ''Bangerz'' in the United States, having entered at number 42 on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] the week that the record was released in October. In its second week, the track dropped to number 79 before falling off the following week. After its accompanying music video was released in December, "Adore You" re-entered the chart at its new peak position of number 22.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/5855137/eminem-rihanna-rule-hot-100-for-a-fourth-week-miley-cyrus-returns|title=Eminem, Rihanna Rule Hot 100 for a Fourth Week; Miley Cyrus Returns|last=Trust|first=Gary|magazine=Billboard|publisher=Prometheus Global Media|date=January 2, 2014|access-date=February 17, 2014}}</ref>  It has since risen to number 21.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.digitalspy.com/music/news/a552675/katy-perrys-dark-horse-spends-fourth-week-at-no1-in-us.html|title=Katy Perry's 'Dark Horse' spends fourth week at No.1 in US|last=Corner|first=Lewis|publisher=Digital Spy. Hearst Corporation|date=February 20, 2014|access-date=February 20, 2014}}</ref> The track underperformed relative to "[[We Can't Stop]]" and "[[Wrecking Ball (Miley Cyrus song)|Wrecking Ball]]", which respectively peaked at numbers two and one after being serviced as the first and second singles from ''Bangerz''.<ref name="Underperformance">{{cite web|url=http://www.fuse.tv/videos/2014/02/miley-cyrus-cedric-gervais-adore-you-remix|title=Miley Cyrus Recruits Lana Del Rey Hitmaker for "Adore You" Remix|last=Benjamin|first=Jon|work=[[Fuse (TV channel)|Fuse]]|publisher=[[The Madison Square Garden Company]]|date=February 11, 2014|access-date=February 15, 2014}}</ref> However, the track debuted at number 1 on the ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' [[Streaming Songs]] chart the week that its music video was released.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/biz/articles/news/chart-alert/5855161/miley-cyrus-adore-you-video-sends-her-to-no-1-on-social-50|title=Miley Cyrus' 'Adore You' Video Sends Her to No. 1 on Social 50 Chart|last=Gruger|first=William|magazine=Billboard|publisher=Prometheus Global Media|date=January 3, 2014|access-date=April 15, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140407235251/http://www.billboard.com/biz/articles/news/chart-alert/5855161/miley-cyrus-adore-you-video-sends-her-to-no-1-on-social-50|archive-date=April 7, 2014|url-status=dead}}</ref> Elsewhere in North America, the song reached number 36 on the [[Canadian Hot 100]].<ref name="CanadaHot100"/>

"Adore You" charted moderately on national [[record chart]]s throughout Europe. It respectively reached numbers 21 and 27 on the Scottish Singles Chart and the [[UK Singles Chart]], both of which are managed by the [[Official Charts Company]].<ref name="ScottishSinglesChart"/><ref name="UKSinglesChart"/> The song additionally charted at number 24 on the Danish [[Tracklisten]],<ref name="DanishSinglesChart"/> number 44 on the [[Irish Singles Chart]],<ref name="IrishSinglesChart"/> and number 50 on the Spanish [[Productores de Música de España|PROMUSICAE]].<ref name="SpainSinglesChart"/> The track peaked at number 143 on the French [[Syndicat National de l'Édition Phonographique|SNEP]],<ref name="Franceposition"/> and reached numbers 19 and 20 on the Belgian Ultratip charts in Flanders and Wallonia, respectively, which represent the songs that failed to enter the primary [[Ultratop]] chart.<ref name="Flandersposition"/><ref name="Walloniaposition"/> In Oceania, "Adore You" reached number 25 on the Australian [[ARIA Charts]] and number 15 on the [[Recorded Music NZ|Official New Zealand Music Chart]];<ref name="ARIACharts"/><ref name="NZTop40"/> in the former country, the track was certified double platinum in 2023.<ref name=ARIA/>

A remix of "Adore You" produced by [[Cedric Gervais]] was premiered on February 13, 2014, and was officially released on March 3, 2014, by his record label [[Spinnin' Records]]. It replaces the original mid-tempo [[pop music]] elements with an up-tempo [[dance music]] production, while maintaining Cyrus' original vocals. The remix received generally favorable reviews from [[Music journalism|music critics]], who felt that it complimented the album version. An accompanying music video was premiered through Spinnin' Records on February 13, 2014, and is composed of re-edited footage of the clip for the original version of the song.

Cyrus first contacted Gervais over the possibility of remixing "Adore You" in January 2014, shortly after he received the [[Grammy Award for Best Remixed Recording, Non-Classical]] for his version of "[[Summertime Sadness]]" by [[Lana Del Rey]].<ref name="Leak">{{cite web|url=http://www.nydailynews.com/entertainment/music-arts/remix-guru-cedric-gervais-set-work-miley-cyrus-article-1.1591995|title=Grammys 2014: Remix guru Cedric Gervais set to work with Miley Cyrus after winning for Lana Del Rey tune|last=Dillon|first=Nancy|newspaper=Daily News|publisher=Mortimer Zuckerman|date=January 26, 2014|access-date=February 12, 2014}}</ref>

Gervais' version of the track surfaced online on February 11,<ref name="Leak"/> although it was not officially acknowledged by Gervais' record label [[Spinnin' Records]] until the premiere of its music video on February 13.<ref name="RemixVideo">{{cite web|url=http://www.idolator.com/7505751/miley-cyrus-adore-you-cedric-gervais-official-video|title=The Cedric Gervais Remix Of Miley Cyrus' "Adore You" Now Has An Official Video: Watch|last=Wass|first=Mike|publisher=[[Idolator (website)|Idolator]]. [[Spin Media]]|date=February 13, 2014|access-date=February 15, 2014|archive-date=February 15, 2014|archive-url=https://archive.today/20140215170822/http://www.idolator.com/7505751/miley-cyrus-adore-you-cedric-gervais-official-video|url-status=dead}}</ref> It was announced through Gervais' official website that it would be officially released on March 3, 2014.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://cedricgervais.com/2014/02/miley-cyrus-vs-cedric-gervais-adore-you-remix-official-video/ |title=Miley Cyrus vs. Cedric Gervais - Adore You (Remix) [Official Video] |publisher=CedricGervais.com |date=February 13, 2014 |access-date=February 15, 2014 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140221172147/http://cedricgervais.com/2014/02/miley-cyrus-vs-cedric-gervais-adore-you-remix-official-video/ |archive-date=February 21, 2014 }}</ref>

The remixed version of "Adore You" substitutes the original "amorphous rhythms" with prominent elements of [[dance music]], which incorporates a "thumping beat" and progresses into a "dazzling drop" during the chorus.<ref name="BillboardRemix">{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/music/music-news/miley-cyrus-adore-you-gets-cedric-gervais-remix-listen-5901226/|title=Miley Cyrus' 'Adore You' Gets Cedric Gervais Remix: Listen|last=Lipshutz|first=Jason|magazine=Billboard|publisher=Prometheus Global Media|date=February 11, 2014}}</ref> The production maintains the "emotional center of the song" while adding "a cascading signal boost."<ref>{{cite web|url=http://buzzworthy.mtv.com/2014/02/12/miley-cyrus-adore-you-cedric-gervais-remix/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140221211524/http://buzzworthy.mtv.com/2014/02/12/miley-cyrus-adore-you-cedric-gervais-remix/|url-status=dead|archive-date=February 21, 2014|title=Miley Cyrus' 'Adore You' Gets An EDM Makeover From Cedric Gervais|last=O'Neil|first=Luke|publisher=MTV Buzzworthy. Viacom|date=February 12, 2014|access-date=February 15, 2014}}</ref> An accompanying music video for the Cedric Gervais remix of "Adore You" was premiered through [[Spinnin' Records]] on February 13, 2014; it is a re-edited version of the original music video, primarily using scenes where Cyrus is seen lying under a bed sheet dressed in her bra and underwear. Mike Wass from Idolator felt that the remix "works quite well with the existing visual".<ref name="RemixVideo"/>

===Reception and performance===
Gervais' remix of "Adore You" received generally favorable reviews from music critics. Jason Lipshutz from ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' and Lewis Corner from [[Digital Spy]] wrote favorably of the redone production; the former complimented the conversion of the original "yearning ballad" into a "first-pumping anthem",<ref name="BillboardRemix"/> while the latter appreciated its transition into a "club anthem" from the first "romantic ballad".<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.digitalspy.com/music/news/a550541/miley-cyruss-adore-you-gets-cedric-gervais-remix-listen.html|title=Miley Cyrus's 'Adore You' gets Cedric Gervais remix - listen|last=Corner|first=Lewis|publisher=[[Digital Spy]]. [[Hearst Corporation]]|date=February 11, 2014|access-date=February 12, 2014}}</ref> Noting that the original version of the track had not charted as strongly as Cyrus' previous singles "[[We Can't Stop]]" and "[[Wrecking Ball (Miley Cyrus song)|Wrecking Ball]]", Esi Mensah from Entertainmentwise thought that Gervais "rescued the track" after becoming "a fun electronic dance song."<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.entertainmentwise.com/news/140882/DJ-Turns-Miley-Cyrus-Song-Adore-You-Into-Club-Banger-LISTEN#1ABOvPFSRZ0HoVP5.99|title=DJ Turns Miley Cyrus' Song 'Adore You' Into Club Banger|last=Mensah|first=Esi|publisher=Entertainmentwise. [[Gigwise]]|date=February 13, 2014|access-date=February 15, 2014|archive-date=March 2, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140302120726/http://www.entertainmentwise.com/news/140882/DJ-Turns-Miley-Cyrus-Song-Adore-You-Into-Club-Banger-LISTEN#1ABOvPFSRZ0HoVP5.99|url-status=dead}}</ref>

James Shotwell from Under the Gun Review felt that the album version of the track was "a bit of a snoozer", and jokingly said to attempt to "resist" enjoying the remix.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.underthegunreview.net/2014/02/13/required-listening-miley-cyrus-adore-you-cedric-gervais-remix/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140213172925/http://www.underthegunreview.net/2014/02/13/required-listening-miley-cyrus-adore-you-cedric-gervais-remix/|url-status=dead|archive-date=February 13, 2014|title=Required Listening: Miley Cyrus - "Adore You" (Cedric Gervais Remix)|last=Shotwell|first=James|publisher=Under the Gun Review|date=February 13, 2014|access-date=February 16, 2014}}</ref> Vanyaland contributor Michael Marotta agreed that the song benefited from "a much-needed energy injection" that he hoped would "[get] exposed" through Cyrus' Bangerz Tour.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.vanyaland.com/2014/02/13/true-banger-listen-cedric-gervais-club-remix-miley-cyrus-adore/|title=True Banger: Listen to Cedric Gervais' club remix of Miley Cyrus' 'Adore You'|last=Marotta|first=Michael|publisher=Vanyaland|date=February 13, 2014|access-date=February 16, 2014}}</ref> Writing for [[Idolator (website)|Idolator]], Mike Wass commented the track worked better with the original "minimal production" and thought that the remix was "cheesy", although he admitted that he "wouldn't switch channels if it came on the radio."<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.idolator.com/7505334/miley-cyrus-adore-you-cedric-gervais-remix|archive-url=https://archive.today/20140212220536/http://www.idolator.com/7505334/miley-cyrus-adore-you-cedric-gervais-remix|url-status=dead|archive-date=February 12, 2014|title=Miley Cyrus' "Adore You" Gets A Thumping Cedric Gervais Remix: Listen|last=Wass|first=Mike|publisher=Idolator. Spin Media|date=February 11, 2014|access-date=February 12, 2014}}</ref>
