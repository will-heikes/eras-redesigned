"'''The Other Side'''" is a song by American singer [[Jason Derulo]], released as the [[lead single]] from his third studio album, ''[[Tattoos (Jason Derulo album)|Tattoos]]'' (2013), and its US counterpart ''[[Talk Dirty (album)|Talk Dirty]]'' (2014). Derulo co-wrote the song with [[Martin Johnson (musician)|Martin Johnson]] and [[Ammo (musician)|Joshua "Ammo" Coleman]]; the latter is also the producer.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.songfacts.com/detail.php?id=29224 |title=The Other Side song meaning |last= |first= |date= |website=Songfacts.com |access-date=May 26, 2016}}</ref> The song was later featured in a TV spot for the 2017 Disney/Pixar film [[Coco (2017 film)|''Coco'']].<ref>{{Cite web|title=Coco Official Final Trailer|date=2017-11-06|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Rvr68u6k5sI|via=[[YouTube]]|accessdate=2017-12-10}}</ref>

"The Other Side" was met with mixed reviews from critics, some praised its energy and catchiness while others defined it as a generic [[autotune]]d [[dance-pop]] track.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://popcrush.com/jason-derulo-the-other-side/|title = Jason Derulo, 'The Other Side' – Song Review|website=Popcrush.com| date=17 April 2013 }}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.digitalspy.com/music/single-reviews/a486975/jason-derulo-the-other-side-single-review/|title = Jason Derulo: 'The Other Side' review|website=Digitalspy.com|date = 4 June 2013}}</ref>

==Composition==
Courtney E. Smith of [[Radio.com]] wrote that "The Other Side" is about what "men really think about before the first time they have sex with a woman they care about."<ref>{{cite web|url=http://radio.com/2013/04/24/jason-derulo-sex-the-other-side/|title=5 Things We Learned About Jason Derulo's Sex Life From 'The Other Side'|first=Courtney E.|last=Smith|website=Radio.com}}</ref>

"The Other Side" is performed in the key of [[A major]] with a [[tempo]] of 128 beats per minute, with Derulo's vocals ranging from E<sub>3</sub> to C{{music|sharp}}<sub>5</sub>.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Key & BPM for The Other Side by Jason Derulo |url=https://tunebat.com/Info/The-Other-Side-Jason-Derulo/5FljCWR0cys07PQ9277GTz |access-date=June 16, 2023 |website=Tunebat}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last1=Josh |first1=Coleman |last2=Jason |first2=Derulo |last3=Martin |first3=Johnson |date=2013-06-04 |title=The Other Side |url=https://www.musicnotes.com/sheetmusic/mtd.asp?ppn=MN0118537 |access-date=2023-06-16 |website=Musicnotes.com |language=en}}</ref>

==Music video==
The lyric video was uploaded to Jason's YouTube account on April 23, 2013.<ref>{{cite AV media |people=Jason Derulo |year=2013 |title=The Other Side |medium=Music Video |url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9gXP8wcICqQ |via=[[YouTube]]|accessdate=September 18, 2013}}</ref> The official music video was uploaded on May 1 and was directed by [[Colin Tilley]].<ref name="youtube">{{cite AV media |people=Colin Tilley (Director), Jason Derulo |year=2013 |title=The Other Side |medium=Music Video |url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=byp94CCWKSI |accessdate=September 18, 2013}}</ref> It features Derulo along with his girlfriend, played by Erika Marosi,{{Citation needed|date=September 2013}} in many different locations such as in a bedroom and in a club.<ref name="youtube"/> It also features Derulo doing a headstand near the end of the video.<ref name="youtube"/><ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.starpulse.com/news/index.php/2012/01/08/jason_derulo_feels_blessed_to_survive_ |title=Jason Derulo Feels 'Blessed' To Survive Neck Injury |publisher=Starpulse.com |date=January 8, 2012 |accessdate=September 18, 2013}}</ref>

Derulo created the acoustic version of this song with [[Tyler Ward]].
