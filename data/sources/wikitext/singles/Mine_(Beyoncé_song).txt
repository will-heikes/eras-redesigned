"'''Mine'''" is a song recorded by American singer [[Beyoncé]] featuring Canadian rapper [[Drake (musician)|Drake]]. The song was written by [[Noah "40" Shebib]], Drake, Beyoncé, [[Majid Jordan]], [[Omen (record producer)|Sidney "Omen" Brown]] and [[Key Wane]], with production by Shebib, Jordan, Brown and Beyoncé for the latter's self-titled fifth studio album ''[[Beyoncé (album)|Beyoncé]]'' (2013). "Mine" is an [[alternative R&B]] song, that contains [[Trap music (hip hop)|trap]] elements,  [[Music of Africa|African]] beats with muted modern [[Hip hop music|hip hop]] as well as elements of [[Afrobeats]]. Lyrically, Beyoncé reveals her everyday doubts regarding marriage and motherhood.

"Mine" received acclaim from music critics, who praised the song's production, Beyoncé's vocal performance and the song's breakdown. Following the release of the album, "Mine" charted in multiple regions, making appearances on the US [[Billboard Hot 100]] and the [[UK Singles Charts]].

==Background and composition==

In September 2013, [[Drake (musician)|Drake]] revealed that he was working with Beyoncé on her new project.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.rap-up.com/2013/09/24/drake-praises-beyonce-reveals-collaboration/|title=Drake praises Beyoncé, Reveals Collaboration|work=[[Rap-Up]]|date=September 24, 2013|access-date=July 16, 2014|archive-date=December 14, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141214181452/http://www.rap-up.com/2013/09/24/drake-praises-beyonce-reveals-collaboration/|url-status=dead}}</ref> During an interview with ''[[Vibe (magazine)|Vibe]]'', he talked about his collaboration with Beyoncé on "Mine", stating, "I had fun with it. I always wanted to write for her. She never really sings that minimal... We'll see where that goes."<ref name="Vibe">{{cite web|url=http://www.vibe.com/photo-gallery/outtakes-drake-talks-beyonce-trophies-and-next-album|title=Outtakes: Drake Talks Beyoncé, 'Trophies' And Next Album|work=[[Vibe (magazine)|Vibe]]|first=John|last=Kennedy|date=December 30, 2013|access-date=July 16, 2014|archive-date=June 20, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140620225614/http://www.vibe.com/photo-gallery/outtakes-drake-talks-beyonce-trophies-and-next-album|url-status=live}}</ref>
"Mine" was written by Beyoncé, [[Noah Shebib]], [[Drake (musician)|Aubrey Graham]], [[Majid Jordan|Jordan Ullman]], [[Omen (record producer)|Sidney Brown]] and [[Key Wane|Dwane Weir]] while the production was helmed by [[Noah "40" Shebib]], [[Majid Jordan]], Brown and Beyoncé. Brown also handled additional [[Programming (music)|drum programming]], and Beyoncé served as the song's vocal producer. Ramon Rivas [[Audio engineer|engineered the song, and Beyoncé's vocals were recorded by Noah "40" Shebib]], while Key Wane provided intro keys and intro programming. The song was finally [[Audio mixing (recorded music)|mixed]] and [[Audio mastering|mastered]] by Noel "Gadget" Campbell, Tom Coyne and Aya Merrill. "Mine" was recorded in two studios: Jungle City Studios and Oven Studios, both in New York City.<ref name="credits"/>

==Music video==
The accompanying music video for "Mine" was shot by [[Belgium|Belgian]] director Pierre Debusschere at [[Stockton Beach]], [[Australia]].<ref name="credits"/><ref name="huffpo">{{cite web|url=http://www.huffingtonpost.com/2013/12/13/beyonce-new-album-jay-z-blue-ivy-drake_n_4437106.html|title=Beyonce Surprises Fans With New Album Featuring Jay Z, Blue Ivy, Drake|date=December 13, 2013|last=Makarechi|first=Kia|work=[[The Huffington Post]]|access-date=January 4, 2014|archive-date=January 3, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140103092948/http://www.huffingtonpost.com/2013/12/13/beyonce-new-album-jay-z-blue-ivy-drake_n_4437106.html|url-status=live}}</ref> It was shot by Melbourne cinematographer Stefan Duscio. The visual was released on December 13, 20,13, to [[iTunes Store]] on ''Beyoncé'' its,  along with sixteen other music videos for every track on the album.<ref name="pr">{{cite press release|url=http://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/beyonce-available-worldwide-now-235687181.html|title=BEYONCÉ Available Worldwide Now|publisher=[[Parkwood Entertainment]]/Columbia Records via [[PR Newswire]]|date=December 13, 2013|access-date=September 3, 2014|archive-date=November 25, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141125101800/http://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/beyonce-available-worldwide-now-235687181.html|url-status=live}}</ref> On November 24, 2014 it was also uploaded to the singer's [[Vevo]] account.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/6327633/beyonce-platinum-edition-streaming-spotify|title=Beyonce's New 'Platinum Edition' Album Is Streaming on Spotify Now!|magazine=Billboard|first=Joe|last=Lynch|date=November 24, 2014|access-date=December 8, 2014|archive-date=December 27, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141227161816/http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/6327633/beyonce-platinum-edition-streaming-spotify|url-status=live}}</ref> Debusschere, who also directed "Ghost" on the ''Beyoncé'' album and has previously worked with [[Kanye West]] on his musical short film ''[[Runaway (2010 film)|Runaway]]'' (2010), said of his collaboration with Beyoncé: "I think Beyoncé came to me because she wanted something other than the usual clips. We shot two videos in a month in Australia. My closest friends knew, but other than that, I had to keep the secret until the end."<ref name="lavenir">{{cite web|url=http://www.lavenir.net/article/detail.aspx?articleid=DMF20131220_00407249|title=Le Bruxellois Pierre Debusschere clippe Beyonce en Pietà|date=December 20, 2013|publisher=l'avenir.net|language=fr|access-date=January 4, 2014|archive-date=January 4, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140104204315/http://www.lavenir.net/article/detail.aspx?articleid=DMF20131220_00407249|url-status=live}}</ref><ref name="elle belgique">{{cite web |url=http://www.elle.be/fr/19393-avant-premiere-beyonce-par-un-realisateur-belge.html |title=Avant-première: Beyoncé par un réalisateur Belge |last=Clauss |first=Elisabeth |work=[[Elle (magazine)|Elle]] |location=Belgium |language=fr |date=December 13, 2013 |access-date=January 4, 2014 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161220111506/http://www.elle.be/fr/19393-avant-premiere-beyonce-par-un-realisateur-belge.html |archive-date=December 20, 2016 |url-status=dead }}</ref> Picking up on the theme of isolation from the lyrics that begins the song, the video references two works of art: [[Michelangelo]]'s sculpture "[[Pietà]]", and [[René Magritte]]'s painting "The Lovers" (1928). During the first part of the video, Beyoncé is shown wearing a veil and a gown, sitting with a dancer covered in white paint resting their head on her lap, and with a mask on her face, which she puts on, with the statue's peaceful expression. [[René Magritte]]'s painting, like the video, shows a couple kissing with their heads enshrouded by white fabric. "The Lovers" was on display as part of a retrospective at New York's [[Museum of Modern Art]] at the time of the video's release.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://galleristny.com/2013/12/really-liking-this-magritte-quotation-in-beyonces-mine-video/ |title=Really Liking This Magritte Quotation in Beyoncé's 'Mine' Video |publisher=galleristny.com |first=Andrew |last=Russeth |date=December 20, 2013 |access-date=March 9, 2014 |archive-date=January 27, 2014 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140127060142/http://galleristny.com/2013/12/really-liking-this-magritte-quotation-in-beyonces-mine-video/ |url-status=live }}</ref> In the case of the music video, the shirts are inscribed with the words "Yours" and "Mine".
