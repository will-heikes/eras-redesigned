"'''Seen It All'''" is a song by American rapper [[Jeezy]], released as the second single from his seventh studio album ''Seen It All: The Autobiography'' (2014).<ref>{{cite web|url=https://itunes.apple.com/us/album/seen-it-all-feat.-jay-z-single/id893739140 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140701161908/https://itunes.apple.com/us/album/seen-it-all-feat.-jay-z-single/id893739140 |url-status=dead |archive-date=July 1, 2014 |title=iTunes – Music – Seen It All (feat. JAY Z) – Single by Jeezy |publisher=iTunes |date=2014-07-01 |access-date=2014-07-12}}</ref> It is a hip hop song and it features vocals from fellow rapper [[Jay-Z]] and was produced by [[Cardo (record producer)|Cardo]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.xxlmag.com/news/2014/07/cardo-producing-jeezy-seen-it-all-interview/ |title=Cardo Speaks On Producing Jeezy And Jay Z's "Seen It All" |work= XXL Magazine|date=2014-07-02 |access-date=2014-07-12}}</ref>
