"'''Jealous'''" is a song performed by American singer [[Nick Jonas]] for [[Nick Jonas (album)|his eponymous sophomore studio album]]. It was released by [[Island Records]] on September 7, 2014 as the second single from the record. "Jealous" was written by Jonas, [[Sir Nolan]], [[Simon Wilcox]], and was later produced by Sir Nolan.
"Jealous" peaked at No. 7 on the US ''Billboard'' [[Billboard Hot 100|Hot 100]] becoming Jonas' highest-charting single to date. It is also his highest-selling single, with 3 million copies sold in the United States alone to date. "Jealous" also peaked at No. 1 on the US Hot Dance Club Songs chart,<ref>{{cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/charts/2015-01-10/dance-club-play-songs|title=Hot Dance Club Songs.|magazine=Billboard|publisher=Nielsen Business Media, Inc|date=January 10, 2015|access-date=January 1, 2015|archive-date=January 8, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150108150632/http://www.billboard.com/charts/2015-01-10/dance-club-play-songs|url-status=live}}</ref> No. 2 on the US ''Billboard'' [[Mainstream Top 40]] for five weeks, and No. 10 on the ''Billboard'' [[Canadian Hot 100]]. "Jealous" was also a top 20 hit in Australia, reaching No. 15 on the [[ARIA Charts]], and a top 30 hit in Ireland, where it peaked at No. 28. It also peaked at No. 8 in New Zealand. "Jealous" had its biggest success in the United Kingdom, where it peaked at No. 2 on the [[UK Singles Chart]].

The song's [[music video]] was shot by director Peter Tunney, a frequent collaborator of Jonas'. The video received positive reviews and was praised for inspirational quotes in the background. The singer performed "Jealous" on television shows such as ''[[Late Night with Seth Meyers]]'', ''[[Today (U.S. TV program)|Today]]'', ''[[The Kidd Kraddick Morning Show]]'', ''[[VH1]] [[Big Morning Buzz Live]]'', ''VH1 Artist to Watch'', the ''[[2014 MTV Europe Music Awards]]''' kick-off special and ''[[The Ellen DeGeneres Show]]'' and included it on the [[Nick Jonas Live]] Tour [[set list]]. The official remix of "Jealous" featured singer-songwriter [[Tinashe]] and was released on November 5, 2014. The song was also covered by ''[[The Voice (U.S. season 7)|The Voice]]'' season seven contestant [[Chris Jamison]]. The uncensored version was uploaded to his official YouTube account but is not present on the explicit version of the album. The song is also included as a bonus track on the UK deluxe version of ''[[Last Year Was Complicated]]'', along with "[[Chains (Nick Jonas song)|Chains]]" and "[[Levels (Nick Jonas song)|Levels]]".

In an interview with [[E! News]] August 2014, Jonas confirmed that "Jealous" would be the second single of his upcoming album. He revealed the song was about the feeling of threat men experience when another man looks [at] or interacts with their girlfriend.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://uk.eonline.com/news/572205/nick-jonas-admits-next-single-jealous-is-about-real-life-talks-packing-on-muscle-for-tv-and-working-with-dylan-penn|title=Nick Jonas Admits Next Single Jealous Is About Real Life, Talks Packing on Muscle for TV and Working With Dylan Penn - E! Online|date=August 22, 2014|work=E! Online|access-date=October 24, 2014}}</ref> In a later interview with Just Jared, Jonas explained:

"[It's] just that thing of puffing your chest up every once in a while...You feel like you're ready to go." Jonas believes his feelings were elated filming the TV series 'Kingdom', stating, "At the time I wrote and recorded it, I was in the middle of all the training for 'Kingdom,' so I was really sort of hyped up on testosterone."<ref>{{cite web|url=http://popcrush.com/nick-jonas-talks-new-single-jealous-album-title/|title=Nick Jonas Talks New Single 'Jealous' + Reveals Album Title|work=PopCrush|date=August 23, 2014|access-date=October 24, 2014|archive-date=July 29, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160729100722/http://popcrush.com/nick-jonas-talks-new-single-jealous-album-title/|url-status=live}}</ref>

"Jealous" is a pop and R&B song.<ref>{{cite news |author=Patrick Ryan |url=https://www.usatoday.com/story/life/music/2014/11/10/nick-jonas-grows-up-new-album-jealous/18778251/ |title=Nick Jonas engineers R&B reinvention (and gets ripped) |newspaper=[[USA Today]] |date=November 10, 2014 |access-date=August 27, 2015 |archive-date=October 10, 2015 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151010215629/http://www.usatoday.com/story/life/music/2014/11/10/nick-jonas-grows-up-new-album-jealous/18778251/ |url-status=live }}</ref> Jonas told ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' magazine that the song has a "[[Lionel Richie]] vibe."<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.musictimes.com/articles/9435/20140829/nick-jonas-channels-lionel-richie-elvis-balancing-music-acting.htm|title=Nick Jonas Channels Lionel Richie, Elvis While Balancing Music, Acting|work=Music Times|date=August 29, 2014 |access-date=October 24, 2014|archive-date=October 6, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141006105555/http://www.musictimes.com/articles/9435/20140829/nick-jonas-channels-lionel-richie-elvis-balancing-music-acting.htm|url-status=live}}</ref><ref>{{cite magazine|last=Caulfield|first=Keith|url=https://www.billboard.com/music/pop/nick-jonas-new-single-6236492/|title=Nick Jonas Says New Single Is 'New-Agey Lionel Richie'|date=August 29, 2014|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|publisher=[[Prometheus Global Media]]|access-date=October 24, 2014|archive-date=March 4, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160304073334/http://www.billboard.com/articles/columns/pop-shop/6236492/nick-jonas-new-single|url-status=live}}</ref>

Talking about "Jealous"' music video, Jonas said: "The video is basically a trip into the mind of Nick Jonas by way of stream of consciousness. So the director, Peter, he said 'I think if you asked someone right now, 'Who is Nick Jonas?' I think you'd get a million different answers. After having the chance to get to know you, I feel like none of them would really be right because you are so different than the public perception. So let's get inside your mind because this song is very personal even though it's fun. It's kind of a dance song in a way. But let's dig a little deeper and see what it means to you.' So the video is really layered, like his art. You can see he layers a bunch of different news clippings and articles and says something greater over top of it. The video is the same way. The real message is that jealousy is something we all feel from all different walks of life, whether it's about romance or love or family or whatever it is — insecurities. It touches on all that and also has sort of a fun through line with me and Olivia is actually in the video as well."<ref>{{cite web|url=http://popdust.com/2014/08/25/nick-jonas-new-single-jealous-album/|title=Nick Jonas Talks New Single 'Jealous' And Album|date=August 25, 2014|work=Popdust|access-date=October 24, 2014|archive-date=October 28, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141028143128/http://popdust.com/2014/08/25/nick-jonas-new-single-jealous-album/|url-status=live}}</ref>

==Tinashe remix==
A remix of "Jealous" produced by [[Sir Nolan]] was released on November 5, 2014, by Jonas' record label [[Island Records]]. The remix received generally favorable reviews from [[Music journalism|music critics]], who felt that it complimented the album version.

===Reception and performance===
Justin Davis of Complex wrote that Tinashe added her own female touch to the bouncy song, giving the other side of the story for Jonas' ode to being protective over his romantic conquests by sympathizing with her own admission of [[jealousy]]. The song remains just as catchy as the original, with Jonas' macho verses remaining intact, but the addition of Tinashe gives the song new life.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.complex.com/music/2014/11/nick-jonas-tinashe-jealous-remix|title=Listen to Nick Jonas's "Jealous (Remix)" ft. Tinashe|author=Justin Davis|date=November 5, 2014|work=Complex|access-date=November 7, 2014|archive-date=November 5, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141105162129/http://www.complex.com/music/2014/11/nick-jonas-tinashe-jealous-remix|url-status=live}}</ref>

Radio.com columnist Courtney E. Smith wrote that the Hip-hop's up-and-coming it girl puts an extra sexy spin on the former Jonas Brother's angsty single, adding herself to the chorus and turning the music's Prince factor up to 10.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://radio.com/2014/11/05/nick-jonas-tinashe-jealous-remix-listen/|title=Tinashe Hops on Remix of Nick Jonas' 'Jealous': Listen|access-date=November 7, 2014|archive-date=November 7, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141107000008/http://radio.com/2014/11/05/nick-jonas-tinashe-jealous-remix-listen/|url-status=live}}</ref>
