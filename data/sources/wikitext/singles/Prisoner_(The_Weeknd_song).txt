"'''Prisoner'''" is a song by Canadian singer-songwriter [[the Weeknd]], featuring American singer-songwriter [[Lana Del Rey]], from the former's second studio album ''[[Beauty Behind the Madness]]'' (2015). They co-wrote the song with [[Illangelo]], who co-produced it with the Weeknd. It is the first of the four collaborations between the two artists, being followed by "[[Party Monster (song)|Party Monster]]", from the Weeknd's third studio album ''[[Starboy (album)|Starboy]]'' (2016).<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://consequenceofsound.net/2017/04/lana-del-rey-and-the-weeknd-reunite-on-lust-for-life-listen/|title=Lana Del Rey and The Weeknd reunite on "Lust For Life" — listen|magazine=[[Consequence of Sound]]|first=Sarah|last=Davis|date=April 19, 2017|access-date=September 1, 2019}}<br>- {{cite web|url=http://cache.umusic.com/_sites/theweeknd.com/pdf/starboy.pdf |title=The Weeknd Starboy Album Credits|website=Cache.umusic.com|date=November 28, 2016|access-date=September 18, 2019}}</ref>

== Commercial performance ==
"Prisoner" debuted at number 16 on the [[Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs]] chart, with 51,291 copies sold in its first week. The song also accrued 3.7 million US streams.<ref>{{cite magazine|last=Mendizabal|first=Amaya|title=The Weeknd's Entire New Album Has Charted On Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs|url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/weeknd-album-charted-hot-rb-hip-hop-songs/|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|access-date=September 5, 2015|date=September 9, 2015}}</ref>

== Critical reception ==
Alex Kritselis of ''Bustle'' wrote, "The mid-tempo duet sounds exactly how you'd expect it to sound: dark, tortured, and a little bit over-the-top. Depending on how you feel about The Weeknd and Del Rey — two artists who share a flair for the dramatic — this either makes the track absolutely perfect or absolutely unlistenable. Personally, I'm digging it."<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.bustle.com/articles/105760-lana-del-rey-the-weeknds-prisoner-lyrics-are-dark-tortured-exactly-what-youd-expect|title=Lana Del Rey & The Weeknd's "Prisoner" Lyrics Are Dark, Tortured & Exactly What You'd Expect|website=Bustle|date=August 21, 2015 |access-date=December 31, 2021}}</ref> In his review of the album, ''Rolling Stone''{{'s}} Jon Dolan described the song as "a summit of luxuriant sadness" and "quite the slow-burning pity party".<ref>{{Cite magazine|url=https://www.rollingstone.com/music/music-album-reviews/beauty-behind-the-madness-126821/|title=The Weeknd 'Beauty Behind the Madness' Album Review|first=Jon|last=Dolan|magazine=Rolling Stone|date=September 10, 2015|access-date=December 31, 2021}}</ref> Chris DeVille of ''[[Stereogum]]'' called "Prisoner" "the most intriguing part" of ''Beauty Behind the Madness'', and said the song "feels like an old Weeknd mixtape track, but with touches of Del Rey's doomed noir glamor".<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.stereogum.com/1825638/the-weeknd-prisoner-feat-lana-del-rey/news/|title=The Weeknd – "Prisoner" (Feat. Lana Del Rey)|website=Stereogum|date=August 21, 2015|access-date=December 31, 2021}}</ref> ''Vulture''{{'s}} Dee Lockett wrote, "Lana, the tortured lover, and the Weeknd, the addict, sound exactly as you'd expect on this bombastic morbid-pop-meets-lusty-R&B ballad. They croon about their respective sorrows: Lana wonders if Hollywood has poisoned her, while the Weeknd comes to grips with falling in love. For most people, that last one would be a relief, but for someone who previously bragged, "I just fucked two bitches before I saw you," it's like living out a horror film. But they each agree that whatever pain they're in, they brought it on themselves: "I'm addicted to a life that's so empty and so cold / I'm a prisoner to my decisions," they whine in tandem. Of course, it ends with a Lana soliloquy."<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.vulture.com/2015/08/hear-the-weeknd-and-lana-del-reys-new-duet.html|title=The Weeknd and Lana Del Rey Make the Perfect Self-Loathing Pair on New Duet 'Prisoner'|first=Dee|last=Lockett|website=Vulture|date=August 21, 2015 |access-date=December 31, 2021}}</ref>

In 2019, ''Billboard''{{'s}} Kirsten Spruch included "Prisoner" in her list of Del Rey's best collaborations.<ref>{{Cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/music/pop/lana-del-rey-best-collabs-the-weeknd-stevie-nicks-asap-rocky-8519065/|title=Lana Del Rey's Best Collabs: The Weeknd, Stevie Nicks, A$AP Rocky & More|first=Kirsten|last=Spruch|magazine=Billboard|date=July 8, 2019|access-date=December 31, 2021}}</ref> In his 2019 list of "Every Lana Del Rey Song, Ranked", Richard S. He of ''Billboard'' ranked the song number 75.<ref>{{Cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/media/lists/lana-del-rey-best-songs-ranked-8528500/|title=Every Lana Del Rey Song, Ranked: Critic's List|first=Richard S.|last=He|magazine=Billboard|date=March 31, 2021|access-date=December 31, 2021}}</ref>
