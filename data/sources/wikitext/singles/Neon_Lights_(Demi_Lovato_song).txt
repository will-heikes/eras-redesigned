"'''Neon Lights'''" is a song by American singer [[Demi Lovato]] from her fourth studio album, ''[[Demi (album)|Demi]]'' (2013). The song was released as the album's third single on November 19, 2013 by [[Hollywood Records]].<ref name="US release">{{cite web|last=Kitchener|first=Shaun|title=Demi Lovato Announces 'Neon Lights' As New Single (LISTEN)|url=http://www.entertainmentwise.com/news/127761/Demi-Lovato-Announces-New-Single-As-Skyscraper-Continues-Chart-Revival-LISTEN|work=Entertainment Wise|publisher=Giant Digital|access-date=9 October 2013|date=September 27, 2013|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131021223622/http://www.entertainmentwise.com/news/127761/Demi-Lovato-Announces-New-Single-As-Skyscraper-Continues-Chart-Revival-LISTEN|archive-date=October 21, 2013|df=mdy-all}}</ref> [[Ryan Tedder]] and [[Noel Zancanella]] produced and co-wrote the song along with Lovato, Mario Marchetti and Tiffany Vartanyan. On September 29, 2013, Lovato announced the name of her concert tour, the [[Neon Lights Tour]], and soon after that "Neon Lights" would become the third single from the album.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.entertainmentwise.com/news/127761/Demi-Lovato-Announces-New-Single-As-Skyscraper-Continues-Chart-Revival-LISTEN |title=Demi Lovato Announces 'Neon Lights' As New Single (LISTEN) |last=Kitchener |first=Shaun |work=Entertainmentwise |publisher=Entertainmentwise.com |access-date=September 30, 2013 |date=September 27, 2013 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131021223622/http://www.entertainmentwise.com/news/127761/Demi-Lovato-Announces-New-Single-As-Skyscraper-Continues-Chart-Revival-LISTEN |archive-date=October 21, 2013 |df=mdy-all }}</ref><ref name="Tour 1">{{cite web |url=http://www.digitalspy.co.uk/music/news/a519485/demi-lovato-announces-the-neon-lights-tour-for-2014.html |title=Demi Lovato announces 'The Neon Lights' tour for 2014 |last=Harris |first=Jamie |work=[[Digital Spy]] |publisher=Digitalspy.co.uk |access-date=September 30, 2013 |date=September 30, 2013}}</ref><ref name="Tour 2">{{cite web |url=http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1714771/demi-lovato-neon-lights-tour.jhtml |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131001131558/http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1714771/demi-lovato-neon-lights-tour.jhtml |url-status=dead |archive-date=October 1, 2013 |title=Demi Lovato's Neon Lights Tour Kicking Off On Valentine's Day |last=Vena |first=Jocelyn |work=[[MTV]] |publisher=MTV.com |access-date=September 30, 2013 |date=September 30, 2013}}</ref> The song, with its prominent [[Electronic dance music|EDM]] and [[four-on-the-floor]] influences, covered new ground for Lovato, who had been previously known for her pop rock and ballad singles. Its accompanying music video was released on November 21, 2013.<ref name=NeonLightsVideo>{{cite web|last=Lovato|first=Demi|title=Demi Lovato - Neon Lights (Official)|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v9uDwppN5-w|publisher=[[YouTube]]|date=November 21, 2013}}</ref>

The single reached number one on the Israeli TV Airplay chart. In New Zealand, the song peaked at number 12 and was certified gold. It reached number 36 on the Hot 100, peaking at this position for three non-consecutive weeks. The song reached number 2 on the Ukraine Dance Charts. It topped the US [[Hot Dance Club Songs]] chart and became Lovato's third top 10 single at [[Mainstream Top 40]], reaching number 7. The song has since been certified double platinum in the US.

An accompanying music video for "Neon Lights" was directed by Ryan Pallotta, and was premiered through [[Vevo]] on November 21, 2013. Lovato has performed the track on several television programs, and has also included the song on the set list of her [[The Neon Lights Tour|Neon Lights Tour]] (2014). A re-recorded rock version with American rock band [[The Maine (band)|The Maine]] was included on her first remix album ''[[Revamped]]''.<ref>{{cite magazine|last=Paul|first=Larisha|title=Demi Lovato Is Feeling Unstoppable on 'Confident (Rock Version)'|url=https://www.rollingstone.com/music/music-news/demi-lovato-confident-rock-version-from-new-album-revamped-1234808886/|date=August 18, 2023|access-date=August 19, 2023|magazine=Rolling Stone}}</ref>

==Production and composition==
"Neon Lights" is a [[dance music|dance]]<ref name=RyanTedder/> and [[electropop]]<ref name="billboard"/> song with [[Electronic dance music|EDM]],<ref name="idolator">{{cite web|last=Lansky|first=Sam|title=Demi Lovato Teases "Neon Lights" Video, Due September 29|url=http://www.idolator.com/7486504/demi-lovato-neon-lights-video-teaser|archive-url=https://archive.today/20130928140603/http://www.idolator.com/7486504/demi-lovato-neon-lights-video-teaser|url-status=dead|archive-date=September 28, 2013|work=Idolator|date=September 27, 2013|publisher=[[Spin Media]]|access-date=9 October 2013}}</ref> [[dance-pop]] and [[four-on-the-floor]] influences. In October 2013, Tedder explained his idea to produce the dance record, stating, "That record, Neon Lights, we did intentionally because I wanted to. I heard it on [a friend's] demo-reel and the next day I woke up and the melody was in my head, and I couldn't get it out of my head for two days, that's when I knew."<ref name=RyanTedderYouTube>{{cite web|title=Ryan Tedder Talks About Working With Demi Lovato|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1eCg1coN1So|work=[[YouTube]]|publisher=Capital FM|access-date=October 11, 2013}}</ref> Tedder also went on to compliment Lovato's vocals, stating, "She came in and just ripped it. She in pop music has one of the biggest ranges, possibly the highest full voice singer I've ever worked with. She can belt full voice like three octaves above middle C, it's just crazy, and with complete power and complete control. At the beginning she's singing the lowest note she's ever done and by the end she's going as high as she's ever gone." Tedder says the song was a "fun dance record."<ref name=RyanTedder>{{cite web|title=OneRepublic's Ryan Tedder Hails Demi Lovato's Voice And "Incredible" Range|url=http://www.capitalfm.com/artists/onerepublic/news/ryan-tedder-demi-lovato-video/|publisher=Capital FM|access-date=October 12, 2013}}</ref> According to Musicnotes.com, Lovato's vocal range spans from the low note of C{{sharp}}<sub>3</sub> to the high note of D<sub>5</sub>. The song moves at a [[tempo]] of 120 beats per minute in the key of [[F-sharp minor|F{{sharp}} minor]].<ref name=MusicSheet>{{cite web|last=Lovato|first=Demi|title=Demi Lovato "Neon Lights" Sheet Music|date=June 11, 2013 |url=http://www.musicnotes.com/sheetmusic/mtdFPE.asp?ppn=MN0118724|publisher=musicnotes.com}}</ref>

==Release==
Lovato announced that the song would be the third single from ''Demi'' on September 27, 2013, by posting a short teaser video to her [[Facebook]] page featuring the words "Neon Lights" formed out of neon light tubes, with the background music being "Neon Lights". The words "SUNDAY 6 PM EST" appeared on screen just before it ended, suggesting the release of the music video or the new single.<ref>{{cite web|title=Demi Lovato's Facebook Video| website=[[Facebook]] |url=https://www.facebook.com/photo.php?v=10151941392749756|access-date=9 October 2013}}</ref> However, this was actually a reference to a live Facebook chat Lovato had with her fans on September 29, where she confirmed "Neon Lights" as the next single of ''Demi'' and also announced her associated [[Neon Lights Tour|tour of the same name]] in support of the album.<ref>{{cite web|title=Demi Lovato's Facebook Tour Announcement| website=[[Facebook]] |url=https://www.facebook.com/photo.php?fbid=10151946288334756&set=a.54794019755.62263.9208539755&type=1|access-date=9 October 2013}}</ref> Despite the announcement in September, the official release of the single was on November 19, with the music video being released two days later on November 21.

==Critical reception==
Jason Lipshutz of ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' called "Neon Lights" a "misguided dance stunt". He explained that the song "covers well-worn electro-pop territory" and does so very effectively. He also states that the song is well done for "the blinking synthesizers and clomping bass that receive a boost from the singer's unflappable power."<ref name="billboard">{{cite magazine|title=Demi Lovato, 'Demi': Track-By-Track Review|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/review/1561233/demi-lovato-demi-track-by-track-review|magazine=Billboard|publisher=[[Prometheus Global Media]]|access-date=11 October 2013}}</ref>  Sam Lansky of ''[[Idolator (website)|Idolator]]'' also described "Neon Lights" as out of place, and called it a "by-the-numbers..... concession to pop-EDM trend-following" that succeeded to dazzle.<ref>{{cite web|last=Lansky|first=Sam|title=Demi Lovato's 'Demi': Album Review|url=http://www.idolator.com/7457954/demi-lovato-demi-album-review|archive-url=https://archive.today/20131013075356/http://www.idolator.com/7457954/demi-lovato-demi-album-review|url-status=dead|archive-date=October 13, 2013|work=Idolator|date=May 14, 2013|publisher=[[Spin Media]]|access-date=11 October 2013}}</ref>

==Music video==
The music video was released on November 21, 2013.<ref>{{cite web|title=Demi Lovato - Neon Lights (Official)|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v9uDwppN5-w|publisher=Youtube.com|access-date=November 21, 2013|date=November 25, 2013}}</ref> The video was directed by Ryan Pallotta, who previously worked with Lovato, who co-directed for the music video, "[[Made in the USA (song)|Made in the USA]]". The video begins as Lovato emerging from a pool of water. In the next scene, she performs the introduction of the song in a room wired with neon lights and another scene where Lovato is later shown dancing and singing with the backup dancers in a club. During the video, the singer is wearing neon makeup and neon rain starts falling down. The scenes are shown repeatedly throughout the video. Lovato is also shown swimming nude.

"'''Neon Lights (Rock Version)'''" is the [[Re-recording (music)|re-recorded]] version of "Neon Lights" by American singer-songwriter [[Demi Lovato]] with American rock band [[The Maine (band)|the Maine]]. It was released on September 15, 2023, as a track on ''[[Revamped]]'', Lovato's first [[remix album]].
