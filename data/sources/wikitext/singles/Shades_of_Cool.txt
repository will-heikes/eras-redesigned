"'''Shades of Cool'''" is a song by American singer-songwriter [[Lana Del Rey]] from her third studio album, ''[[Ultraviolence (album)|Ultraviolence]]'' (2014). She and [[Rick Nowels]] wrote the track, which was produced by [[Dan Auerbach]]. It was released on May 26, 2014, by [[Interscope Records]] as the second single from ''Ultraviolence''. Lyrically, the single talks about an "unfixable" man.

"Shades of Cool" received general acclaim from [[music journalism|music critics]], who praised its musical style. Commercially, the single peaked at number 79 on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] and entered the top 40 of a few [[record chart]]s in Europe. A music video for "Shades of Cool" was directed by [[Jake Nava]] and was described as a [[film noir]] visual.

[[EMI Music Publishing]]|access-date=December 17, 2014}}</ref> It was described as a [[waltz]] [[musical instrument|instrumented]] by "slow-burn" [[bassline]], guitar, and [[string instruments]]; the track features a guitar solo delivered by Auerbach.<ref name="DailyCal">{{cite web|url=http://www.dailycal.org/2014/06/23/new-shade-cool-lana-del-rey-album/|work=[[The Daily Californian]]|date=June 2014}}</ref><ref name=slate1>{{cite web|title=Lana Del Rey's New Song Is a Beautiful, Brooding Return to "operatic" [[soprano]] vocals blending with "trademark wailing [[vibrato]]" create a ghostly atmosphere on "Shades of Cool".<ref name="DailyCal"/><ref name="RS"/> Chris Coplan from [[Consequence is unbreakable."

== Reception ==
"Shades of Cool" received positive reviews from music critics. Coplan of Consequence of Sound complimented the song for its "grace and sophistication".<ref name="cos1" /> Saran Shetty of ''[[Slate (magazine)|Slate]]'' called "Shades of Cool" "a beautiful, brooding return to form [for Del Rey]", and opined that the single would "fit perfectly" in a ''[[James Bond]]'' theme track.<ref name="slate1" /> Likewise, ''[[Rolling Stone]]''{{'}}s Calyn Ganz wrote that the track "would be perfect for a ''James Bond'' film directed by [[Quentin Tarantino]]."<ref name="RS"/> Lathan Ryan from ''[[PopMatters]]'' positively compared Del Rey's vocals on "Shades of Cool" to those of [[Cocteau Twins]] vocalist [[Elizabeth Fraser]], observing that "Del Rey has never sounded better".<ref>{{cite web|last1=Lathan|first1=Ryan|title=Lana Del Rey: Ultraviolence|url=http://www.popmatters.com/review/182811-lana-del-rey-ultraviolence/|publisher=[[PopMatters]]|access-date=July 15, 2014|date=June 16, 2014}}</ref>

The single peaked at number 79 on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]].<ref name="hot100"/> It managed to enter the top 40 of a few [[record chart]]s: Belgian Wallonia (number 40),<ref name="wall"/> France (number 37),<ref name="france"/> Hungary (number 19),<ref name="hungary"/> and Spain (number 31).<ref name="spain"/> The single peaked at number three on the Greece Digital Songs, a chart operated by ''Billboard'' magazine.<ref name="greece"/>

==Music video==
The music video for "Shades of Cool" was directed by [[Jake Nava]] and was released on June 17, 2014.<ref name="YahooMusic">{{cite web|url=https://www.yahoo.com/music/bp/lana-del-rey-releases-new-film-noir-music-video-for--shades-of-cool-222517920.html|title=Lana Del Rey Releases New Film Noir Music Video for 'Shades of Cool'|publisher=[[Yahoo! Music]]|first=Jeremy|last=Blacklow|date=June 18, 2014|access-date=December 7, 2014}}</ref> The video features tattoo artist [[Mark Mahoney]], who also appeared in the music video for "West Coast", as Del Rey's love interest.<ref name="BBVideo">{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/music/music-news/lana-del-rey-shades-of-cool-music-video-ultraviolence-6121609/|title=Lana Del Rey's 'Shades Of Cool' Video|magazine=Billboard|first=Jason|last=Lipshutz|date=June 18, 2014|access-date=December 7, 2014}}</ref> Filmed in Los Angeles, California, the clip is a "romantic" visual depicting the streets of Los Angeles at night, a 1970s-themed neighborhood, and a pool.<ref name="YahooMusic"/><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.spin.com/2014/06/lana-del-rey-shades-of-cool-video-ultraviolence/|title=Watch Lana Del Rey's Bewitching, Surreal 'Shades of Cool' Video|first=Chris|last=Martins|work=[[Spin (magazine)|Spin]]|date=June 17, 2014|access-date=December 7, 2014}}</ref> In the video, Del Rey dances for her love interest in "flowing" dresses.<ref name="YahooMusic"/> An alternative version of the visual for "Shades of Cool", which was also directed by Nava, was released in July 2014. This version, in which Del Rey is seen drowned in a pool, was described as "darker" than the original video.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/1858928/lana-del-rey-dies-shades-of-cool-video/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140706161926/http://www.mtv.com/news/1858928/lana-del-rey-dies-shades-of-cool-video/|url-status=dead|archive-date=July 6, 2014|title=Lana Del Rey Dies In The New Version Of The 'Shades Of Cool' Video|first=Brenna|last=Erlich|publisher=MTV News|date=July 1, 2014|access-date=December 7, 2014}}</ref>
