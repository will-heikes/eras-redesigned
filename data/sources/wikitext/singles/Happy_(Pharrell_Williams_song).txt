"'''Happy'''" is a song written, produced, and performed by American musician [[Pharrell Williams]], released as the only single from the [[Despicable Me 2 (soundtrack)|soundtrack album]] for the film ''[[Despicable Me 2]]'' (2013).{{Efn|The song "[[Just a Cloud Away]]", also from the soundtrack, was released as a single on March 24, 2022, after a resurgence in popularity due to [[TikTok]].}} The song was first released on July 2, 2013 with the soundtrack, and it was released as a single on November 21, 2013, alongside a 24-hour-long [[music video]]. The song was reissued on December 16, 2013, by [[Back Lot Music]] under exclusive license to [[Columbia Records]], a division of [[Sony Music Entertainment|Sony Music]].<ref name="USrelease"/> The song also served as the lead single from Williams's second studio album, ''[[Girl (Pharrell Williams album)|Girl]]'' (2014). An official remix features American rapper [[Sky Blu (rapper)|Sky Blu]] of [[LMFAO]].

"Happy" is an uptempo [[Soul music|soul]] and [[neo soul]] song on which Williams's [[falsetto]] voice has been compared to [[Curtis Mayfield]]'s by critics. The song has been highly successful, peaking at No. 1 in the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, [[Ireland]], New Zealand, and 19 other countries. It was the best-selling song of 2014 in the United States with 6.45 million copies sold for the year,<ref name="US Sales">{{cite web|first= Paul |last= Grein |title= It's Official: Pharrell Has 2014's Best-Selling Song |publisher= [[Yahoo! Music]] |date= December 31, 2014 |access-date= April 22, 2015 |url= https://www.yahoo.com/music/its-official-pharrell-has-2014s-best-selling-106748836876.html}}</ref> as well as in the United Kingdom with 1.5 million copies sold for the year.<ref name="Top 40">{{cite web|first= Rob |last= Copsey |title= The Official Top 40 Biggest Selling Singles of 2014 |publisher= [[Official Charts Company]] |date= January 1, 2015 |access-date= January 1, 2015 |url= http://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/the-official-top-40-biggest-selling-singles-of-2014-3389/}}</ref> It reached No. 1 in the UK on a record-setting three occasions and became the most downloaded song of all time in the UK in September 2014;<ref>{{cite web|first= Rob |last= Copsey |title= Pharrell's Happy now the UK's most downloaded track of all time |publisher= Official Charts Company |date= September 9, 2014 |access-date= April 22, 2015 |url= http://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/pharrells-happy-now-the-uks-most-downloaded-track-of-all-time-3108/}}</ref> it is the [[List of best-selling singles in the United Kingdom|ninth-highest-selling single of all time in the country]].<ref name="uk sales"/> It was nominated for an [[Academy Award for Best Original Song]]. A live rendition of the song won the [[Grammy Award for Best Pop Solo Performance]] at the [[57th Annual Grammy Awards]].

The music video for "Happy" was nominated for [[MTV Video Music Award for Best Male Video|Best Male Video]] and [[MTV Video Music Award for Video of the Year|Video of the Year]] at the [[2014 MTV Video Music Awards]]. It also won the [[Grammy Award for Best Music Video]] at the 57th Annual Grammy Awards.<ref name="MTV2"/><ref name="MTV"/> The song was ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]''{{'}}s number-one single for 2014. "Happy" was the most successful song of 2014, with 13.9 million units (sales plus equivalent streams) worldwide.<ref>{{cite web|title= IFPI publishes Digital Music Report 2015 |publisher= [[International Federation of the Phonographic Industry]] |date= 14 April 2015 |page= 12 |access-date= 15 April 2015 |url= http://www.ifpi.org/news/Global-digital-music-revenues-match-physical-format-sales-for-first-time |archive-date= 14 April 2015 |archive-url= https://web.archive.org/web/20150414194629/http://www.ifpi.org/news/Global-digital-music-revenues-match-physical-format-sales-for-first-time |url-status= dead}}</ref> In 2024, the song appeared on the [[Piece by Piece (soundtrack)|soundtrack]] to the LEGO animated biography of Williams' life, ''[[Piece by Piece (2024 film)|Piece by Piece]]''.

==Background==
Williams provided vocals for French duo [[Daft Punk]]'s 2013 album ''[[Random Access Memories]]'' on the songs "[[Lose Yourself to Dance]]" and "[[Get Lucky (Daft Punk song)|Get Lucky]]". After returning from the recording sessions in [[Paris]], he attended a meeting with record label managers who said that the results were "spectacular" and that "Get Lucky" would be Daft Punk's next single. They also made Williams an offer to record his own album, to which he agreed quickly, "overwhelmed that someone wanted to know what's in my heart".<ref>{{cite magazine|first= Angus |last= Batey |title= We Heard Pharrell's New Album & This Is What It Sounds Like |magazine= [[NME]] |date= February 21, 2014 |access-date= March 20, 2014 |url= https://www.nme.com/blogs/nme-blogs/we-heard-pharrells-new-album-this-is-what-it-sounds-like}}</ref>

On December 17, 2013, it was announced that Williams had signed to [[Columbia Records]] and would release his second studio album in 2014.<ref name="Vibe">{{cite magazine|title= Pharrell Williams Signs to Columbia Records |magazine= [[Vibe (magazine)|Vibe]] |date= December 17, 2013 |access-date= March 20, 2014 |url= http://www.vibe.com/article/pharrell-williams-signs-columbia-records}}</ref> Williams originally wrote the song for [[CeeLo Green]] and felt Green's recording of the song was better, but [[Elektra Records]], Green's record label, decided against it since he was on the verge of releasing his Christmas album, ''[[Cee Lo's Magic Moment]]''.<ref>{{cite web|first= Bang |last= Showbiz |title= Pharrell Williams – Pharrell Williams: Happy was for Cee Lo Green |publisher= [[Contactmusic.com]] |date= May 1, 2014 |access-date= May 1, 2014 |url= http://www.contactmusic.com/story/pharrell-williams-happy-was-for-cee-lo-green_4175044}}</ref><ref>{{cite magazine|first= Ryan |last= Reed |title= Pharrell Says 'Happy' Originally Recorded by Cee Lo Green |magazine= [[Rolling Stone]] |date= April 30, 2014 |access-date= March 23, 2019 |url= https://www.rollingstone.com/music/music-news/pharrell-says-happy-originally-recorded-by-cee-lo-green-247481/}}</ref><ref>{{cite news|first= Sean |last= Michaels |title= Pharrell Williams' Happy was originally recorded by Cee Lo Green |newspaper= [[The Guardian]] |date= April 30, 2014 |access-date= March 23, 2019 |url= https://www.theguardian.com/music/2014/apr/30/pharrell-williams-happy-was-originally-recorded-by-cee-lo-green}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|first= Jimmy |last= Blake |title= Pharrell's Happy originally recorded by Cee Lo Green |work= [[Newsbeat]] |date= May 1, 2014 |access-date= March 23, 2019 |url= https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/newsbeat-27234842}}</ref>

"Happy" was written and produced by Williams, with backing vocals by Rhea Dummett, Trevon Henderson, Ashley L. Lee, Shamika Hightower, Jasmine Murray and Terrence Rolle.<ref name="24h">{{cite web|title= Pharrell Williams – Happy – The World's First 24 Hour Music Video |access-date= March 20, 2014 |url= http://24hoursofhappy.com/ |archive-url= https://web.archive.org/web/20140323012659/http://24hoursofhappy.com/ |archive-date= March 23, 2014 |url-status= dead}}</ref> The track was digitally edited and arranged by Andrew Coleman and Mike Larson, who also recorded it with assistance from Matthew Desrameaux at Circle House Studios in [[Miami|Miami, Florida]]. The song was later mixed by Leslie Brathwaite at Music Box Studios in [[Atlanta|Atlanta, Georgia]].<ref name="24h"/>

"Happy" plays for 3 minutes and 53 seconds<ref name="USrelease"/> in [[common time]] and at a [[tempo]] of 160 [[beats per minute]]. The song is written in the key of [[F minor]]. Williams sings the upper notes in falsetto; his vocal range spans from F<sub>3</sub> to C<sub>5</sub>.<ref name="Sheet">{{cite web |title='Happy' by Pharrell Williams – Digital Sheet Music |url=http://www.musicnotes.com/sheetmusic/mtdFPE.asp?ppn=MN0119660 |access-date=January 26, 2013 |publisher=[[EMI Music Publishing]] |via=Musicnotes}}</ref> His singing on the track has been compared to that of [[Curtis Mayfield]].<ref name="RS"/><ref>{{cite news|first= Mikael |last= Wood |title= Pharrell Williams, a 'Happy' and busy guy |newspaper= [[Los Angeles Times]] |date= February 20, 2014 |access-date= March 3, 2013 |url= https://www.latimes.com/entertainment/music/posts/la-et-ms-pharrell-williams-20140220,0,6981329.story}}</ref>

"Happy" is an up-tempo [[Soul music|soul]] and [[neo soul]] song.<ref name="Tingen">{{cite magazine|first= Paul |last= Tingen |author-link= Paul Tingen |title= Inside Track: Happy |magazine= [[Sound on Sound]] |location= Cambridge |date= May 2014 |access-date= October 23, 2014 |url= http://www.soundonsound.com/sos/may14/articles/inside-track-0514.htm}}</ref><ref>{{cite news|first= Ali |last= Ryland |title= Wireless Festival 2014: From OutKast to Pharrell, the top ten acts to watch |newspaper= [[Metro (British newspaper)|Metro]] |date= July 1, 2014 |access-date= May 25, 2015 |url= http://metro.co.uk/2014/07/01/wireless-festival-2014-from-outkast-to-pharrell-the-top-ten-acts-to-watch-4766164/}}</ref> According to music journalist [[Paul Tingen]], "Happy" is "a mid-tempo ... song in a faux-[[Motown]] style, with an arrangement that is, by modern standards, very sparse: programmed drums, one bass and one keyboard part, and handclaps both programmed and played, all topped off by Williams's lead vocals and a whole posse of backing vocals".<ref name="Tingen"/> [[Jody Rosen]] viewed the song as a "standout" with a "sprightly [[neo soul|neosoul]] [[funk]] [[Groove (music)|groove]]".<ref name="RS">{{cite magazine|first= Jody |last= Rosen |title= Pharrell – Happy |magazine= [[Rolling Stone]] |date= June 7, 2013 |access-date= November 23, 2013 |url= https://www.rollingstone.com/music/songreviews/happy-20130607 |archive-url= https://web.archive.org/web/20130613052247/http://www.rollingstone.com/music/songreviews/happy-20130607 |url-status= dead |archive-date= June 13, 2013}}</ref>

"Happy" was met with very positive reviews. 'Because I'm happy, clap along if you feel like happiness is the truth' sings Williams as the jaunty rhythm gets your head nodding."<ref name="RfS">{{cite web|first= Huw |last= Woodward |title= Single kind of song that makes you want to dance and sing along".<ref name="CM">{{cite web|first= Holly |last= Williams |title= Pharrell Williams – Happy Single Review |date= 6 January 2014 |publisher= "Contagiously peppy and brilliantly simple".<ref>{{cite magazine|first= Reggie |last= Ugwu |title=Pharrell, 'G I R L': Track-By-Track Review |date= 25 February 2014 |magazine= [[Billboard ==Chart performance==
===Netherlands===
In October 2013, a month prior to its single release, "Happy" catapulted to No. 1 on the [[Dutch Top 40]] chart in the Netherlands, partially as a result of radio station [[3FM]] airplay and subsequent online downloads, as well as featuring in a [[Transavia.com|Transavia]] commercial.<ref>{{cite news|first= Pierre |last= Oitmann |title= Pharrell Williams scoort eerste solo-nummer 1-hit |work= [[NU.nl]] |publisher= [[Sanoma]] |date= October 11, 2013 |access-date= November 24, 2013 |url= http://www.nu.nl/muziek/3599159/pharrell-williams-scoort-eerste-solo-nummer-1-hit.html |language= nl}}</ref> It was released worldwide on November 21, 2013. In 2014, after 35 weeks on the charts and then still in the top 15, "Happy" became the most successful song in the Dutch Top 40 of all time.<ref>{{cite web|title= Happy van Pharrell Williams grootste Top 40-hit ooit |publisher= [[Dutch Top 40]] |url= http://www.top40.nl/nieuws/happy-van-pharrell-williams-grootste-top-40-hit-ooit |archive-date= April 12, 2014 |archive-url= https://web.archive.org/web/20140412041753/http://www.top40.nl/nieuws/happy-van-pharrell-williams-grootste-top-40-hit-ooit |language= nl}}</ref><ref name="DutchTop40">{{cite web|title= Top 2500 |publisher= [[Dutch Top 40]] |access-date= April 15, 2014 |url= http://www.top40.nl/bijzondere-lijsten/top-2500 |language= nl}}</ref> It went on to finish number five on the year-end Single Top 40 list, giving Williams three songs in the top five, along with "Blurred Lines" and "Get Lucky", both of which feature him.

===United Kingdom===
In the United Kingdom, "Happy" debuted at No. 71 on the [[UK Singles Chart]] on December 1, 2013.<ref>{{cite web|title= Official Singles Chart Top 100: 01 December 2013 – 07 December 2013 |publisher= Official Charts Company |date= December 7, 2013 |access-date= April 10, 2015 |url= http://www.officialcharts.com/charts/singles-chart/20131201/7501}}</ref> It then spent four weeks climbing before finally reaching No. 1 on December 29, becoming the final UK number one single of 2013.<ref name="UKC"/> It became Williams' third No. 1 song in Britain, counting "Get Lucky" and "Blurred Lines", where he appeared as a featured artist.<ref>{{cite news|first= Amy |last= Duncan |title= X Factor winner Sam Bailey's reign lasts just one week as she is knocked off No.1 spot by Pharrell |date= December 29, 2013 |newspaper= [[Metro (British newspaper)|Metro]] |access-date= March 3, 2014 |url= https://metro.co.uk/2013/12/29/x-factor-winner-sam-baileys-reign-lasts-just-one-week-as-she-is-knocked-off-no-1-spot-by-pharrell-4244740/}}</ref> It has since spent four non-consecutive weeks at No. 1, reaching the peak on three separate occasions. In doing so, Williams became only the third artist ever to achieve this, and the first since [[Guy Mitchell]] in 1957 with "[[Singing the Blues]]".<ref name="UK"/> Following the single's third week at No. 1, it spent five weeks in the top three before returning to the summit, boosted by his performance at the [[2014 Brit Awards]].<ref name="UK"/> In the same week that "Happy" returned to No. 1 for the third time, it sold its millionth copy in the UK, Williams' third in under a year, having previously done so with Daft Punk's "Get Lucky" in June 2013, and Thicke's "Blurred Lines" in July 2013. This made Williams only the second artist in UK chart history (the first being [[the Beatles]]) to have three singles sell 1 million copies in under a year,<ref name="UK"/> as well as only the third artist ever to have three million-sellers, along with the Beatles (who have six) and [[Rihanna]] (who has four).<ref name="UK">{{cite web|first= Justin |last= Myers |title= Unstoppable Pharrell scores chart record hat-trick as Happy smashes a million |publisher= Official Charts Company |date= March 2, 2014 |access-date= March 3, 2014 |url= http://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/unstoppable-pharrell-scores-chart-record-hat-trick-as-happy-smashes-a-million-2790/}}</ref>

"Happy" was the best-selling single of UK in 2014, with 1.5 million copies sold for the year,<ref name="Top 40"/> and became the most downloaded song of all time in the UK by September 2014, with over 1.62 million sold.<ref name="Top 40 2015">{{cite web|first= Rob |last= Copsey |title= The Official Top 40 Biggest Selling Singles of the Millennium so far revealed |publisher= Official Charts Company |date= June 11, 2015 |access-date= June 12, 2015 |url= http://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/the-official-top-40-biggest-selling-singles-of-the-millennium-so-far-revealed__9816/}}</ref> It had also been streamed over 25 million times and was the first song released in the 2010s to go 3× Platinum.<ref>{{cite news|title= Pharrell's Happy achieves rare triple platinum sales |work= [[Newsbeat]] |publisher= [[BBC Online]] |date= July 24, 2014 |access-date= April 22, 2015 |url= https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/newsbeat-28476358}}</ref> With the inclusion of streaming, "Happy" has achieved combined sales of over 2 million.<ref>{{cite web |last=Jones |first=Alan |date=November 3, 2014 |title=Official Charts Analysis: Taylor Swift tops albums with 90,336 sales of 1989 |url=http://mediaor.com/post/101668321624/official-charts-analysis-taylor-swift-tops-albums |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150413042110/http://mediaor.com/post/101668321624/official-charts-analysis-taylor-swift-tops-albums |archive-date=2015-04-13 |access-date=May 25, 2015 |work=[[Music Week]] |via=Mediaor.com}}</ref> Additionally, "Happy" and [[Idina Menzel]]'s "[[Let It Go (Disney song)|Let It Go]]" became the first singles ever to spend an entire calendar year on the official UK top 75 chart. On June 12, it was announced that "Happy" had overtaken "[[Evergreen (Westlife song)#Will Young version|Evergreen]]" by [[Will Young]] as the [[List of best-selling singles of the 2000s (century) in the United Kingdom|UK's best-selling single of the 21st century]], with sales of 1.81 million.<ref name="Top 40 2015"/> "Happy" spent 92 weeks in the UK top 100.<ref>{{cite web |title=Pharrell Williams |url=http://www.officialcharts.com/artist/18922/pharrell%20williams/ |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150616061726/http://www.officialcharts.com/artist/18922/pharrell%20williams/ |archive-date=2015-06-16 |publisher=[[Official Charts Company]]}}</ref> {{as of|2020|February}}, the song has sold 1,950,000 copies.<ref name="uk sales">{{cite web|first= Justin |last= Myers |title= The best-selling singles of all time on the Official UK Chart |publisher= [[Official Charts Company]] |date= February 14, 2020 |access-date= December 4, 2021 |url= https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/the-best-selling-singles-of-all-time-on-the-official-uk-chart__21298/}}</ref>

===New Zealand===
{{More citations needed section|date=June 2015}}
On the [[Official New Zealand Music Chart|New Zealand Singles Chart]], "Happy" debuted at No. 11 on December 23, 2013. The next week it moved to No. 2. Then on January 6, 2014, "Happy" began a 12-week consecutive reign in the No. 1 position, right up until March 24, 2014. It then dropped to No. 2 for one week, then spent a further three weeks at No. 1, bringing the total number of weeks at No. 1 to fifteen, breaking the 36-year-long record for most weeks spent at No. 1, previously held by [[Boney M.]]'s "[[Rivers of Babylon]]".<ref name="NZ"/><ref>{{cite news|first= Brendan |last= Manning |title= Pharrell's happy chart-buster |newspaper= [[The New Zealand Herald]] |date= April 28, 2014 |access-date= April 28, 2014 |url= https://www.nzherald.co.nz/entertainment/pharrells-happy-chart-buster/MTPMLDSXERY67L5RU2FYYUQQME/}}</ref> {{as of|2015|February|22|df=US}}, "Happy" had spent 62 straight weeks in the New Zealand Top 40 since its debut, before finally dropping out of the chart the following week. The song has been certified 7× Platinum in New Zealand.<ref name="NZ"/><ref name="RMNZ"/>

===United States===
"Happy" reached the top of the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] for the week ending March 8, 2014, giving Williams his fourth No. 1 single on the chart, and his first as lead artist. It took twelve years and a week (as the issue date is March 8, 2014) to achieve his first Hot 100 No. 1 hit as a lead artist.<ref>{{cite magazine|first= Gary |last= Trust |title= Pharrell Williams' 'Happy' Hits No. 1 on Hot 100 |magazine= Billboard |date= February 26, 2014 |access-date= February 26, 2014 |url= https://www.billboard.com/articles/news/5915783/pharrell-williams-happy-hits-no-1-on-hot-100}}</ref> The song remained at No. 1 the following week, when it topped the [[Hot 100 Airplay (Radio Songs)|Hot 100 Airplay]] chart.<ref>{{cite magazine|first= Gary |last= Trust |title= Pharrell's 'Happy' Holds at No. 1 On Hot 100 |magazine= Billboard |date= March 5, 2014 |access-date= March 6, 2014 |url= https://www.billboard.com/articles/news/5923189/pharrell-williams-happy-holds-at-no-1-on-hot-100-chart}}</ref> The song remained at the No. 1 position on the Hot 100 longer than any other song in the calendar year. On the week of May 17, 2014, after spending ten weeks at the summit, "Happy" was finally knocked off number one by [[John Legend]]'s "[[All of Me (John Legend song)|All of Me]]" down to the No. 2 spot.<ref>{{cite magazine|first= Gary |last= Trust |title= John Legend's 'All of Me' Tops Hot 100, Ariana Grande Debuts at No. 3 |magazine= Billboard |date= May 7, 2014 |access-date= May 10, 2014 |url= https://www.billboard.com/articles/news/6077635/hot-100-john-legend-all-of-me-ariana-grande-iggy-azalea}}</ref> "Happy" became the 28th song in the history of the chart to reach this milestone.<ref>{{cite magazine|first= Gary |last= Trust |title= Pharrell Williams Tops Hot 100 for 10th Week, Iggy Azalea Vaults Into Top 10 |magazine= Billboard |date= April 30, 2014 |access-date= April 30, 2014 |url= https://www.billboard.com/articles/news/6070358/pharrell-williams-tops-hot-100-chart-10-weeks-iggy-azalea-top-10}}</ref> It spent a total of 22 weeks in the top 10, entering on the week-ending February 15, 2014 and departing on July 19, 2014. 

Topping the airplay charts for ten weeks,<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.billboard.com/artist/pharrell-williams/chart-history/HSB/|title=Pharrell Williams Chart History (Radio Songs)|work=Billboard|access-date=January 25, 2026}}</ref> the song also holds the record for the second-highest audience peak for a week on the Hot 100 Airplay with 225.9 million impressions, while [[Robin Thicke]]'s "[[Blurred Lines]]", a song in which Pharrell was featured as well as produced and co-wrote, holds the all-time record.<ref>{{cite magazine|first= Gary |last= Trust |title= Pharrell Williams' 'Happy' Holds Atop Hot 100; DJ Snake & Lil Jon Hit Top 10 |magazine= Billboard |date= April 2, 2014 |access-date= April 3, 2014 |url= https://www.billboard.com/articles/news/6032600/pharrell-williams-happy-hot-100-dj-snake-lil-jon-hit-top-10}}</ref> The single was also the strongest in sales. It was number one on the [[Digital Song Sales]] for 11 weeks,<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.billboard.com/artist/pharrell-williams/chart-history/HDS/|title=Pharrell Williams Chart History (Digital Song Sales)|work=Billboard|access-date=January 25, 2026}}</ref>  and passed its 4 million sales mark in April 2014, the first song of 2014 to do so.<ref>{{cite news|first= Paul |last= Grein |date= March 26, 2014 |title= Chart Watch: "Happy" tops 4M |work= Chart Watch |publisher= Yahoo! Music |access-date= March 30, 2014 |url= https://music.yahoo.com/blogs/chart-watch/chart-watch-happy-tops-4m-214123406.html}}</ref> The song sold 5,633,000 copies in the US in the first six months of 2014, the most ever of any song in the first six months of any calendar year.<ref>{{cite web|first= Paul |last= Grein |title= Chart Watch Extra: 12 Facts About 2014's First Six Months in Music |date= July 3, 2014 |work= Chart Watch |publisher= Yahoo! Music |url= https://music.yahoo.com/blogs/chart-watch/chart-watch-extra-12-facts-about-the-first-six-months-235342239.html |archive-date= July 14, 2014 |archive-url= https://web.archive.org/web/20140714183816/https://music.yahoo.com/blogs/chart-watch/chart-watch-extra-12-facts-about-the-first-six-months-235342239.html}}</ref> It was also the best-selling song of 2014 in the US with 6.45 million copies sold for the year,<ref name="US Sales"/> and has sold over 6.9 million total copies in the US {{as of|2015|April|lc=y}}.<ref name="USSales">{{cite magazine|title= The Rise of Uptown Funk: Could It Become the All-Time #1 Seller? |magazine= [[Hits (magazine)|Hits]] |date= April 9, 2015 |access-date= April 9, 2015 |url= http://hitsdailydouble.com/news&id=295457}}</ref> Relatively, the song performed the least on streaming, staying at number-one on the [[Streaming Songs|streaming chart]] for four weeks.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.billboard.com/artist/pharrell-williams/chart-history/stm/|title=Pharrell Williams Chart History (Streaming Songs)|work=Billboard|access-date=January 25, 2026}}</ref>

===Other charts===
{{More citations needed section|date=June 2015}}
The song also topped the charts of 22 other countries, including Australia, Austria, Canada, France, Germany, Ireland, Italy, Poland and Spain. It spent twelve weeks (including seven consecutive) at No. 1 in Ireland,<ref>{{cite web |last=Corner |first=Lewis |date=February 14, 2014 |title=Pharrell Williams holds on to Irish No.1 with 'Happy' |url=http://www.digitalspy.co.uk/music/news/a551294/pharrell-williams-holds-on-to-irish-no1-with-happy.html |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160806132011/http://www.digitalspy.com/music/irish-singles-chart/news/a551294/pharrell-williams-holds-on-to-irish-no1-with-happy/ |archive-date=2016-08-06 |access-date=February 15, 2014 |publisher=[[Digital Spy]]}}</ref> making it the third song in less than a year to spend more than seven weeks at No. 1, after Robin Thicke's "Blurred Lines" and [[Avicii]]'s "[[Wake Me Up (Avicii song)|Wake Me Up!]]". In France, "Happy" spent 22 weeks at No. 1, the longest reign at the top of the [[Syndicat National de l'Edition Phonographique]] (until 2026 with "[[Melodrama (Disiz and Theodora song)|Melodrama]]"), beating [[Lou Bega]]{{'}}s "[[Mambo No. 5]]", which spent 20 weeks at No. 1 in 1999.

==Music video==
To coincide with the single release, the website 24hoursofhappy.com was launched, featuring a visual presentation of "Happy" advertised as "the world's first 24-hour music video". It was directed by the French directing team We Are from LA<ref>{{cite web|title= 57th Annual GRAMMY Awards Winners & Nominees |website= [[Grammy Award|Grammy.com]] |url= http://www.grammy.com/nominees?genre=18 |archive-url= https://web.archive.org/web/20150210235347/http://www.grammy.com/nominees?genre=18 |archive-date= February 10, 2015}}</ref> with creative director [[Yoann Lemoine]],<ref>{{cite web|first= Luke |last= Tierney |title= Pharrell Williams 'Happy' by We Are from LA |website= Promonews.tv |date= November 27, 2013 |access-date= March 8, 2014 |url= http://www.promonews.tv/videos/2013/11/27/pharrell-williams-happy-we-are-la}}</ref> and shot by [[steadicam]] operator Jon Beattie.<ref>{{cite web|first= Sebastian |last= Wöber |title= How did Jon Beattie shoot Pharrell's "24 Hours of Happy"? |publisher= cinema5D |date= November 28, 2013 |access-date= May 17, 2014 |url= http://www.cinema5d.com/how-did-jon-beattie-shoot-pharrells-24-hours-of-happy/}}</ref> It consists of the four-minute song repeated multiple times, with various people dancing around [[Los Angeles]] and miming along. The website allows users to navigate to various points in the 24-hour timeframe, including all 360 four-minute segments.<ref>{{cite web|first= Ikechi |last= Awazie |title= Be an Inspiration: 24 Hours of Happy Music |publisher= Be a Light to the World |date= February 27, 2014 |access-date= May 13, 2014 |url= http://www.awazieikechi.com/inspiration-24-hours-happy-music/}}</ref> The video featured appearances by [[Jimmy Kimmel]], [[Tyler, the Creator]], [[Jamie Foxx]], [[Magic Johnson]], [[Issa Rae]], [[Earl Sweatshirt]], [[Kelly Osbourne]], [[JoJo (singer)|JoJo]], [[Sérgio Mendes]], [[Ryan Heffington]], [[Whit Hertford]], [[Leah LaBelle]], [[Jasper Dolphin]], and ''Despicable Me 2'' stars [[Steve Carell]] and [[Miranda Cosgrove]]. It held the [[Guinness World Records|Guinness World Record]] for the longest music video until 2020, when it was surpassed by [[Twenty One Pilots]]'s 178-day long live stream of fan clips for their single "[[Level of Concern]]".

A four-minute edit of the video was also released onto the [[I Am Other]] and Williams's own [[YouTube]] channel on November 21, 2013, and January 8, 2014 respectively; both uploads of the video had received over a billion views each as of April 2024.<ref>{{cite web |date=November 21, 2013 |title=Pharrell Williams – Happy (Official Music Video) |url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y6Sxv-sUYtM |access-date=March 4, 2018 |publisher=[[i Am Other]] |via=[[YouTube]]}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |date=January 8, 2014 |title=Pharrell Williams - Happy (Video) |url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZbZSe6N_BXs |access-date=January 2, 2023 |website=[[YouTube]] |publisher=[[Pharrell Williams]]}}</ref> It was nominated for [[MTV Video Music Award for Best Male Video|Best Male Video]] and [[MTV Video Music Award for Video of the Year|Video of the Year]] at the [[2014 MTV Video Music Awards]].<ref name="MTV2">{{cite news|title= Best Male Video 2014 MTV Video Awards |publisher= [[MTV]] |date= August 24, 2014 |access-date= August 24, 2014 |url= http://www.mtv.com/ontv/vma/2014/best-male-video/|archive-url= https://web.archive.org/web/20140719232809/http://www.mtv.com/ontv/vma/2014/best-male-video/|url-status= dead|archive-date= July 19, 2014}}</ref><ref name="MTV">{{cite news|title= Video of the Year 2014 MTV Video Music Awards |publisher= [[MTV]] |date= August 24, 2014 |access-date= August 24, 2014 |url= http://www.mtv.com/ontv/vma/2014/video-of-the-year/|archive-url= https://web.archive.org/web/20140717233233/http://www.mtv.com/ontv/vma/2014/video-of-the-year/|url-status= dead|archive-date= July 17, 2014}}</ref> The 24-hour version of the music video for "Happy" was also projected at the [[Buenos Aires International Festival of Independent Cinema]] in April 2014.<ref>{{cite web|title= BAFICI |publisher= Government of Buenos Aires |access-date= May 18, 2016 |url= http://festivales.buenosaires.gob.ar/descargas/bafici/Grilla%5B16%5DBAFICI.pdf |language= es}}</ref>

==="Happy" tribute videos===
The original video spawned many cover videos on YouTube in which people from different cities throughout the world dance to the song. Those videos are usually called "Pharrell Williams – Happy – We Are from [name of the city]".<ref>{{cite web|title= Search for happy pharrell williams we are from – Overview |publisher= [[YouTube]] |access-date= February 18, 2014 |url= https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=happy+pharrell+williams+we+are+from&sm=3}}</ref> {{as of|2014|May}}, more than 1,500 videos have been created. Inspired by this global phenomenon, a French couple launched a website wearehappyfrom.com to showcase the remakes.<ref>{{cite news|title= #BBCtrending: Pharrell's hit video inspires 1,000 re-makes |publisher= [[BBC News Online]] |date= April 4, 2014 |access-date= April 6, 2014 |url= https://www.bbc.com/news/blogs-trending-26889726}}</ref> In April 2014, the [[Embassy of the United States, Yerevan]], [[Armenia]] released a video titled "Happy Yerevan", directed by Artyom Abovyan featuring US ambassador [[John A. Heffern]] and several Armenian celebrities, such as singers [[André (singer)|André]], [[Emmy (Armenian singer)|Emmy]] and [[Aram Mp3]].<ref>{{cite web|title= Happy Yerevan |publisher= U.S. Embassy in Armenia |access-date= April 9, 2014 |date= April 8, 2014 |url= https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rbZZttoWJn0}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|title= Happy Yerevan (Aram MP 3, US Ambassador, Andre & Others) |website= [[Tert.am]] |date= April 9, 2014 |access-date= April 9, 2014 |url= http://www.tert.am/en/video/rbZZttoWJn0/ |url-status= dead |archive-date= October 12, 2014 |archive-url= https://web.archive.org/web/20141012230427/http://www.tert.am/en/video/rbZZttoWJn0/}}</ref> The same month ''artsmedia Albania'' produced a music video for the track featuring inhabitants of [[Tirana]]. The video soon became popular in [[Albania]] and caused controversy over the usage of images of Albanian First Secretary [[Enver Hoxha]].<ref>{{cite web|title= We Are Happy from Tirana – Pharrell Williams #HAPPYFROMTIRANA |publisher= artsmedia Albania |via= YouTube |date= April 18, 2014 |access-date= April 26, 2014 |url= https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=itpUjfSu4M8}}</ref>

====Arrests in Iran for tribute====
In May 2014, a group of Iranian fans who created a tribute to "Happy" were arrested.<ref>{{cite web|first= Hayden |last= Cooper |title= Iranians arrested after posting their own video of Pharrell Williams's song Happy on YouTube |publisher= [[ABC Online]] |date= May 21, 2014 |access-date= May 21, 2014 |url= http://www.abc.net.au/news/2014-05-21/iranians-arrested-after-posting-video-of-happy-on-internet/5466580}}</ref><ref>{{cite news|first1= Josie |last1= Ensor |first2= Ahmad |last2= Vahdat |title= Young Iranians arrested for 'vulgar' Pharrell Williams' Happy dance |newspaper= [[The Daily Telegraph]] |date= May 21, 2014 |access-date= May 21, 2014 |url= https://www.telegraph.co.uk/news/worldnews/middleeast/iran/10845573/Young-Iranians-arrested-for-vulgar-Pharrell-Williams-Happy-dance.html |url-access= subscription |url-status= live |archive-date= January 12, 2022 |archive-url= https://ghostarchive.org/archive/20220112/https://www.telegraph.co.uk/news/worldnews/middleeast/iran/10845573/Young-Iranians-arrested-for-vulgar-Pharrell-Williams-Happy-dance.html}}{{cbignore}}</ref><ref>{{cite news|first= Yasmine |last= Hafiz |title= Young Iranians arrested for 'vulgar' Pharrell Williams' Happy dance |work= The Huffington Post |date= May 20, 2014 |access-date= May 21, 2014 |url= https://www.huffingtonpost.com/2014/05/20/iranians-arrested-dancing-happy-pharrell_n_5360711.html}}</ref><ref>{{cite news|title= Iranian Pharrell fans arrested for Happy tribute video |publisher= BBC News Online |date= May 21, 2014 |access-date= May 21, 2014 |url= https://www.bbc.com/news/entertainment-arts-27499642}}</ref> According to a police chief, the song represented vulgarity and also hurt public chastity. ==="Weird Al" Yankovic parody===
{{main|Tacky (song)}}
In 2014, American musician [["Weird Al" Yankovic]] parodied "Happy" as "[[Tacky (song)|Tacky]]" on his fourteenth studio album ''[[Mandatory Fun]]''. The song mocks questionable style in fashion as well as activities considered gauche. Yankovic recorded the song as one of the last on ''Mandatory Fun'', and received Williams' approval directly, through email. He remarked he was "honored" to have his work spoofed by Yankovic.<ref>{{cite magazine|first= Gary |last= Graff |title= 'Weird Al' Yankovic on Getting Pharrell's Permission: 'He Could Not Have Been Nicer' |magazine= Billboard |date= July 15, 2014 |access-date= June 4, 2021 |url= http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/6157636/weird-al-yankovic-interview-pharrell-mandatory-fun}}</ref> The song's one-shot music video parodies "Happy", and was the first in a series of eight videos released over eight days in promotion of ''Mandatory Fun''. It features cameo appearances by [[Aisha Tyler]], [[Margaret Cho]], [[Eric Stonestreet]], [[Kristen Schaal]], and [[Jack Black]], and was produced by Nerdist Industries.<ref>{{cite magazine|first= Jason |last= Lipshutz |title= 'Weird Al' Yankovic Spoofs Pharrell's 'Happy' with 'Tacky': Watch |magazine= Billboard |date= July 14, 2014 |access-date= June 4, 2021 |url= https://www.billboard.com/music/music-news/weird-al-yankovic-tacky-pharrell-happy-mandatory-fun-6157562/}}</ref><ref>{{cite magazine|first= Claire |last= Suddath |title= The Internet Should Have Killed Weird Al. It Only Made Him Stronger |magazine= [[Bloomberg Businessweek]] |date= July 21, 2014 |access-date= June 4, 2021 |url= http://www.businessweek.com/articles/2014-07-21/weird-al-yankovics-mandatory-fun-owns-the-internet|archive-url= https://web.archive.org/web/20140722183805/http://www.businessweek.com/articles/2014-07-21/weird-al-yankovics-mandatory-fun-owns-the-internet|url-status= dead|archive-date= July 22, 2014}}</ref>
