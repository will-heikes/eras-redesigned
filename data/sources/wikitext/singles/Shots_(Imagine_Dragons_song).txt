"'''Shots'''" is a song by American [[Rock music|rock]] band [[Imagine Dragons]]. It was written by band members [[Ben McKee]], [[Daniel Platzman]], [[Dan Reynolds (musician)|Dan Reynolds]] and [[Daniel Wayne Sermon|Wayne Sermon]], and released as the third and final single from their second studio album ''[[Smoke + Mirrors]]'' (2015).

==Composition==
The song shows influence of [[industrial music]] with heavy use of [[synthesizer]]s. Reynolds' vocals are light, despite the dark lyrical content, recalling [[1980s in music|music from the 1980s]]. Sermon's guitar textures add complexity.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.musictimes.com/articles/26247/20150127/imagine-dragons-shots-review-new-song-smoke-mirrors-listen.htm|title=Music Times - Imagine Dragons, new song 'Shots'|date=10 February 2015}}</ref>

== Promotion ==
Imagine Dragons performed the track during a first ever live commercial (for Target) during the Grammy Awards (2015).<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.cnbc.com/2015/02/09/target-hits-bulls-eye-with-8m-grammy-commercial.html|title=Target hits bull's-eye with $8M Grammy commercial|website=[[CNBC]] |date=February 9, 2015|access-date=May 4, 2015}}</ref> The advertising was filmed on Las Vegas' [[Fremont Street]] under the direction of [[Jonas Åkerlund]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.reviewjournal.com/columns-blogs/norm-clarke/imagine-dragons-put-fremont-street-back-map|title=Imagine Dragons put Fremont Street back on the map|author=NORM CLARKE LAS VEGAS REVIEW-JOURNAL|work=Las Vegas Review-Journal|access-date=February 16, 2015}}</ref> The advertising promoted an exclusive edition of the album available at Target which includes four bonus tracks. It was estimated to cost $8 million and charted at number 2 on the ''Billboard'' Trending 140.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/events/grammys-2015/6465640/target-imagine-dragons-commercial-break-8-million-grammys-2015|title=Grammys 2015: Target's Imagine Dragons Commercial Break Cost About $8 Million|magazine=Billboard|access-date=February 16, 2015}}</ref>

"Shots" was performed on ''[[The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon]]'' (February 18, 2015),<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.nbc.com/the-tonight-show/segments/112871|title=Imagine Dragons: Shots|access-date=May 4, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150508020642/http://www.nbc.com/the-tonight-show/segments/112871|archive-date=May 8, 2015|url-status=dead}}</ref> ''[[The Howard Stern Show]]'' (March 24, 2015),<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.howardstern.com/news/yesterday-on-the-howard-stern-show-imagine-dragons|title=Imagine Dragons: YESTERDAY ON THE HOWARD STERN SHOW - featuring special performances of "radioactive" & their new hit single "shots"!|access-date=May 4, 2015|archive-date=April 28, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150428155116/http://www.howardstern.com/news/yesterday-on-the-howard-stern-show-imagine-dragons|url-status=dead}}</ref> ''[[The Ellen DeGeneres Show]]'' (2015),<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vVxEYl1KQj0 |title=Imagine Dragons - Shots (Live on Ellen) |publisher=YouTube |date=2015-06-03 |access-date=2017-05-05}}</ref> and ''[[Today (U.S. TV program)|Today]]'' (2015).<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.today.com/video/imagine-dragons-are-radioactive-on-the-today-plaza-471727171619 |title=Imagine Dragons are 'Radioactive' on the TODAY plaza |publisher=TODAY.com |date=2015-06-26 |access-date=2017-05-05}}</ref>

==Music video==
On February 12, 2015, Imagine Dragons released the official music video for "Shots".  The video was directed by [[Robert Hales (director)|Robert Hales]] and was inspired by the artwork created by San Diego–based painter [[Tim Cantor]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/imagine-dragons-gearing-up-for-release-of-new-album----smoke--mirrors----next-tuesday-february-17th-300035301.html|title=Imagine Dragons Gearing Up For Release Of New Album -- Smoke... -- SANTA MONICA, Calif., Feb. 12, 2015 /PRNewswire/ --|author=KIDinaKORNER/Interscope Records|date=February 12, 2015|work=prnewswire.com|access-date=February 16, 2015}}</ref> The same artwork also serves as the cover art for all of the singles for the album. Cantor himself appears as the live depiction of one of his paintings.

==Broiler remix==
In April 2015, Norwegian DJ and electronic music duo [[Broiler (DJs)|Broiler]] (Mikkel Christiansen and Simen Auke) released a remix of "Shots". It was a hit in its own right on [[VG-lista]], the official Norwegian Singles Chart, reaching number 4 on the chart. A music video for the remix was released about 2 months later, on June 4.
