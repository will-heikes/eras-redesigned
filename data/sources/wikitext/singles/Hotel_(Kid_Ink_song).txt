"'''Hotel'''" is a song by American rapper [[Kid Ink]]. The song was released as an instant-grat on January 9, 2015 by Tha Alumni Music Group, 88 Classic and [[RCA Records]], later becoming the second single from his third [[studio album]] ''[[Full Speed (album)|Full Speed]]'' (2015).<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.kidinkmusic.com/kid-ink-hotel-ft-chris-brown|title=Kid Ink - Hotel ft. Chris Brown|publisher=Kid Ink Music|access-date=2015-05-01|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150211063344/http://kidinkmusic.com/kid-ink-hotel-ft-chris-brown/|archive-date=2015-02-11|url-status=dead}}</ref> It was sent to US [[urban adult contemporary]] radio on January 27, 2015.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.allaccess.com/urban/future-releases |title=Impacting Urban Singles - January 27th 2015 |publisher=All Access Music Group |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150121121203/http://www.allaccess.com/urban/future-releases |archive-date=January 21, 2015 }}</ref>

== Background ==
After the commercial success of their previous single "[[Show Me (Kid Ink song)|Show Me]]", Kid Ink linked up with American singer [[Chris Brown]] to work on a song with songwriting and production team [[The Featherstones]]. At first the song was displayed as "track 5" (while the rest of the tracks were titled) on the [[iTunes Store]] album pre-order before it was released commercially as a single.

== Music video ==
An animated music video was uploaded to [[Vevo]] on June 9, 2015.
