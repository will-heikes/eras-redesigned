"'''Holy Grail'''" is a song by American rapper [[Jay-Z]] from his twelfth studio album ''[[Magna Carta Holy Grail]]''. It features vocals from American singer [[Justin Timberlake]] and serves as the album's [[lead single]].<ref>{{cite web |date=July 4, 2013 |title=[FIRST LISTEN&#93; Jay Z Drops "Holy Grail" In Most Innovative Campaign Which Turned Out To Be A Flop&#124;JAY Z&#124; News &#124; MTV European |url=http://www.mtv.tv/news/jay-z-magna-carta-holy-grail-justin-timberlake |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140226055336/http://www.mtv.tv/news/jay-z-magna-carta-holy-grail-justin-timberlake/ |archive-date=February 26, 2014 |access-date=August 4, 2013 |publisher=MTV}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |date=July 4, 2013 |title=Stream Jay-Z's 'Magna Carta Holy Grail' On Hot 97&nbsp;– XXL |url=http://www.xxlmag.com/news/2013/07/stream-jay-zs-magna-carta-holy-grail-on-hot-97/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141025192100/http://www.xxlmag.com/news/2013/07/stream-jay-zs-magna-carta-holy-grail-on-hot-97/ |archive-date=October 25, 2014 |access-date=August 4, 2013 |publisher=Xxlmag.com}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |date=July 4, 2013 |title=Jay-Z Justin Timberlake Holy Grail |url=http://www.complex.com/music/2013/07/jay-z-justin-timberlake-holy-grail |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141025142441/http://www.complex.com/music/2013/07/jay-z-justin-timberlake-holy-grail |archive-date=October 25, 2014 |access-date=August 4, 2013 |publisher=Complex}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |date=July 5, 2013 |title=Listen: Jay-Z's "Holy Grail" ft. Justin Timberlake&nbsp;– Fuse |url=http://www.fuse.tv/2013/07/listen-jay-z-holy-grail-justin-timberlake |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141031040206/http://www.fuse.tv/2013/07/listen-jay-z-holy-grail-justin-timberlake |archive-date=October 31, 2014 |access-date=August 4, 2013 |publisher=Fuse.tv}}</ref> It was produced by [[The-Dream]], [[Timbaland]], and [[Jerome "J-Roc" Harmon|J-Roc]], with additional production from [[No I.D.]] and contains elements of [[Nirvana (band)|Nirvana]]'s 1991 single "[[Smells Like Teen Spirit]]". Lyrically, "Holy Grail" finds both performers discussing the hardships of fame.

Following the release of the album, the song debuted at number eight on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]], where it peaked at number four the following week. In other regions, it peaked at number seven on the [[UK Singles Chart]]. "Holy Grail" had sold over 3.4&nbsp;million copies in the United States as of 2018.<ref name="us sales" /> It won [[Grammy Award for Best Rap/Sung Collaboration|Best Rap/Sung Collaboration]] at the [[56th Annual Grammy Awards]]. In 2014, ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' placed it at number 25 on their list of Top 100 [[Hot Rap Songs]] of all time.<ref>{{cite magazine |title=Hot Rap Songs Chart 25th Anniversary: Top 100 Songs |url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/list/5923200/hot-rap-songs-chart-25th-anniversary-top-100-songs?list_page=7 |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140310153212/http://www.billboard.com/articles/list/5923200/hot-rap-songs-chart-25th-anniversary-top-100-songs?list_page=7 |archive-date=March 10, 2014 |access-date=March 6, 2014 |magazine=Billboard}}</ref>

== Recording and composition ==
"Holy Grail", along with another track from ''Magna Carta Holy Grail'' called "[[Oceans (Jay-Z song)|Oceans]]", was recorded in 2011 during the recording sessions for Jay-Z's collaborative album with [[Kanye West]], ''[[Watch the Throne]]''. West wanted the two songs to appear on ''Watch the Throne,'' but Jay-Z chose to keep them for his upcoming studio album and they were replaced with "[[No Church in the Wild]]" and "[[Made in America (Jay-Z and Kanye West song)|Made in America]]".<ref name="bbc">{{cite web |date=July 15, 2013 |title=Jay-Z. Zane Lowe. Part 1: Magna Carta Holy Grail |url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fzr6MY664l4 |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130717030406/http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fzr6MY664l4 |archive-date=July 17, 2013 |access-date=July 16, 2013 |publisher=BBC Radio 1}}</ref> The early version of the instrumental was created in 2011 by [[The-Dream]]. When the recording sessions for ''MCHG'' started, [[Timbaland]], together with fellow producer [[Jerome "J-Roc" Harmon|J-Roc]], rearranged the instrumental to create the final track.<ref name="revolt">{{cite web |date=July 3, 2013 |title=Unlocking the Holy Grail: Timbaland |url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OTaOtTScD2Q |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130705200710/http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OTaOtTScD2Q&gl=US&hl=en |archive-date=July 5, 2013 |access-date=July 17, 2013 |publisher=Revolt}}</ref>

"Holy Grail" is composed in a [[D minor]] key, and is in [[common time]] at a [[tempo]] of 72 [[beats per minute]]. The vocals range from the note of G<sub>3</sub> to C<sub>6</sub>.<ref name="sheet music">[http://www.musicnotes.com/sheetmusic/mtdFPE.asp?ppn=MN0121777&ref=google Jay-Z "Holy Grail" Sheet Music&nbsp;– Download & Print] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131011185620/http://www.musicnotes.com/sheetmusic/mtdFPE.asp?ppn=MN0121777&ref=google |date=October 11, 2013 }}. musicnotes.com. Retrieved July 28, 2013.</ref> Jay-Z lyrically sampled rock group [[Nirvana (band)|Nirvana]]'s single "[[Smells Like Teen Spirit]]" after receiving permission from [[Courtney Love]], who owns the rights to the original song.<ref>{{cite news |last=Chattman |first=Jon |date=June 19, 2013 |title=Free Love: "A-Sides" Interview with Queen Courtney On New Music, Her Autobiography, A Reality TV Show, and Jay-Z Covering Nirvana? |url=http://www.huffingtonpost.com/jon-chattman/free-emloveem-a-sides-int_b_3468219.html?ncid=edlinkusaolp00000003 |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130626234106/http://www.huffingtonpost.com/jon-chattman/free-emloveem-a-sides-int_b_3468219.html?ncid=edlinkusaolp00000003 |archive-date=June 26, 2013 |access-date=July 17, 2013 |work=HuffPost}}</ref> In an interview with [[BBC Radio 1]], Jay-Z stated that "Holy Grail" was "the map for the album" and that it set the tone for the rest of ''MCHG''. When the song was first recorded for ''Watch the Throne'', Jay-Z thought it would get lost on the album and decided to keep it as the "center piece" for his next album.<ref>{{cite web |date=July 16, 2013 |title=Jay-Z. Zane Lowe. Part 2: Fame |url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2XKRykuZH-I |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130717030342/http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2XKRykuZH-I&feature=share |archive-date=July 17, 2013 |access-date=July 17, 2013 |publisher=BBC Radio 1}}</ref> The song's lyrics can be interpreted as Jay-Z's and Timberlake's [[love-hate relationship]] with fame.

== Music video ==
On August 2, 2013, Jay-Z revealed to [[KPWR|Power 106]] that he and fellow tour mate [[Justin Timberlake]] had begun shooting the music video for "Holy Grail".<ref>{{cite web |last=Tardio |first=Andres |title=Jay-Z & Justin Timberlake Are Filming "Holy Grail" Video |url=http://www.hiphopdx.com/index/news/id.24902/title.jay-z-justin-timberlake-are-filming-holy-grail-video- |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130805001156/http://www.hiphopdx.com/index/news/id.24902/title.jay-z-justin-timberlake-are-filming-holy-grail-video-/ |archive-date=August 5, 2013 |access-date=August 4, 2013 |publisher=HipHop DX}}</ref> The music video was directed by [[Anthony Mandler]] and was released on August 29, 2013, on [[Facebook]], making social media history as the first time a clip from major artists debuted exclusively on the site.<ref>{{cite web |date=October 18, 2010 |title=Video: Jay Z f/ Justin Timberlake – 'Holy Grail' |url=http://www.rap-up.com/2013/08/29/video-jay-z-f-justin-timberlake-holy-grail/ |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141025142351/http://www.rap-up.com/2013/08/29/video-jay-z-f-justin-timberlake-holy-grail/ |archive-date=October 25, 2014 |access-date=August 29, 2013 |publisher=Rap-Up.com}}</ref> Dubbed a "visual" rather than a "music video", the piece is a remixed version of the original album version, featuring pitch shifted vocals and a change in the song structure.

The visual was critically acclaimed for both its imagery and remixed vocals. It was photographed by cinematographer David Devlin. Kyle Anderson from ''[[Entertainment Weekly]]'' praised the piece's imagery, describing its nightmarish sequences as "arresting", and complimented the remixed video version of the song as "miles beyond the album version".<ref>{{cite magazine |title=Jay Z drops 'Holy Grail' video featuring Justin Timberlake |url=http://music-mix.ew.com/2013/08/29/jay-z-holy-grail-video-justin-timberlake-facebook/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140217062402/http://music-mix.ew.com/2013/08/29/jay-z-holy-grail-video-justin-timberlake-facebook/ |archive-date=February 17, 2014 |access-date=September 14, 2013 |magazine=Entertainment Weekly}}</ref>

== Reception ==
Andy Gill of ''[[The Independent]]'' described the song as "smooth and mellifluous,"<ref name="independent">{{cite news |last=Gill |first=Andy |date=July 5, 2013 |title=Jay-Z, Magna Carta Holy Grail&nbsp;– review |url=https://www.independent.co.uk/arts-entertainment/music/reviews/jayz-magna-carta-holy-grail--review-8688306.html |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130707140014/http://www.independent.co.uk/arts-entertainment/music/reviews/jayz-magna-carta-holy-grail--review-8688306.html |archive-date=July 7, 2013 |access-date=July 17, 2013 |work=[[The Independent]] |location=London}}</ref> while ''[[Rolling Stone]]''{{'}}s Simon Vozick called it a "surprisingly moody opener."<ref name="vozick">{{cite magazine |date=July 10, 2013 |title=Magna Carta . . . Holy Grail |url=https://www.rollingstone.com/music/albumreviews/magna-carta-holy-grail-20130710 |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170921234554/https://www.rollingstone.com/music/albumreviews/magna-carta-holy-grail-20130710 |archive-date=September 21, 2017 |access-date=July 11, 2013 |magazine=Rolling Stone}}</ref> DJ Booth praised the track as an "appropriately bombastic intro to an epic LP."<ref name="djbooth">{{cite web |date=July 8, 2013 |title=Jay-Z&nbsp;– Holy Grail |url=http://www.djbooth.net/index/tracks/review/jay-z-holy-grail |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130716131439/http://www.djbooth.net/index/tracks/review/jay-z-holy-grail/ |archive-date=July 16, 2013 |access-date=July 17, 2013 |publisher=DJ Booth}}</ref> In ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'', Adelle Platon noted the song "features Timberlake's vocals in raw, heart-wrenching form as he sings of the pitfalls of fame."<ref>{{Cite magazine |last=Platon |first=Adelle |date=December 27, 2016 |title=Justin Timberlake's 14 Best Hip-Hop/R&B Collaborations |url=https://www.billboard.com/music/rb-hip-hop/justin-timberlake-best-hip-hop-rb-collaborations-7633393/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220128004342/https://www.billboard.com/music/rb-hip-hop/justin-timberlake-best-hip-hop-rb-collaborations-7633393/ |archive-date=January 28, 2022 |access-date=June 21, 2017 |magazine=Billboard}}</ref> Criticizing the song as "laughably overblown", Ian Cohen of ''[[Pitchfork Media]]'' writes that Timberlake "cycles through every tortured artist cliché short of a crucifixion metaphor."<ref name="Pitchfork">{{cite web |author=Cohen, Ian |date=July 8, 2013 |title=Jay-Z: Magna Carta Holy Grail |url=http://pitchfork.com/reviews/albums/18247-jay-z-magna-carta-holy-grail/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130813045026/http://pitchfork.com/reviews/albums/18247-jay-z-magna-carta-holy-grail/? |archive-date=August 13, 2013 |access-date=August 12, 2013 |publisher=[[Pitchfork Media]]}}</ref> Cohen further stated that the "Smells Like Teen Spirit" interpolation "becomes just another forgettable status symbol" and commented: "Where [[Kurt Cobain]] felt compromised by his fame, Jay and Timberlake are doing everything in their power not to offend the money people—whether it's [[Samsung]], [[Target Corporation|Target]], or someone dropping $250 to see them at the [[Rose Bowl (stadium)|Rose Bowl]]."<ref name="Pitchfork" />

"Holy Grail" had a hot-shot debut on many charts upon the release of the album, despite not being released as an official single. It debuted at number eight on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]], being at the time the second-highest debut of the year 2013 on that chart.<ref>{{cite web |last=Grein |first=Paul |date=July 16, 2013 |title=Week Ending July 14, 2013. Albums: Jay-Z Back On The Throne &#124; Chart Watch&nbsp;– Yahoo! Music |url=http://music.yahoo.com/blogs/chart-watch/week-ending-july-14-2013-albums-jay-z-030009697.html |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141025165110/https://music.yahoo.com/blogs/chart-watch/week-ending-july-14-2013-albums-jay-z-030009697.html |archive-date=October 25, 2014 |access-date=August 4, 2013 |publisher=Yahoo Music}}</ref> It peaked at number four and remained in the top 10 for 16 weeks. It has sold over 3.4&nbsp;million copies in the US as of 2018.<ref name="us sales">{{cite magazine |last=Trust |first=Gary |date=February 4, 2018 |title=Ask Billboard: Justin Timberlake & *NSYNC's Career Album & Song Sales |url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/justin-timberlake-nsync-career-album-song-sales-ask-billboard/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20211218184449/https://www.billboard.com/pro/justin-timberlake-nsync-career-album-song-sales-ask-billboard/ |archive-date=December 18, 2021 |access-date=February 4, 2018 |magazine=Billboard |publisher=Billboard-Hollywood Media Group}}</ref> After entering at number 24, "Holy Grail" peaked at number seven on the [[UK Singles Chart]].<ref name="uk">{{cite web |title=Official UK Singles Top 100&nbsp;– 20th July 2013 |url=http://www.officialcharts.com/music-charts/ |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130812074956/http://www.officialcharts.com/music-charts/ |archive-date=August 12, 2013 |access-date=July 16, 2013 |publisher=[[UK Singles Chart]]. [[Official Charts Company]]}}</ref> In 2013, "Holy Grail" was ranked as the 22nd most popular song of the year on the ''Billboard'' Hot 100.<ref>{{cite magazine |title=Best of 2013 – Hot 100 Songs |url=http://www.billboard.com/charts/year-end/2013/hot-100-songs?page=1 |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151127085123/http://www.billboard.com/charts/year-end/2013/hot-100-songs?page=1 |archive-date=November 27, 2015 |access-date=December 13, 2013 |magazine=Billboard}}</ref>

== Live performances ==
Justin Timberlake and Jay-Z performed "Holy Grail" together during their co-headlining tour [[Legends of the Summer Stadium Tour|Legends of the Summer]]. They also performed the song live at the [[Wireless Festival]] in [[London]] on July 14, 2013.<ref name="rs">{{cite magazine |date=July 14, 2013 |title=Jay-Z and Justin Timberlake Perform 'Holy Grail' at Wireless Festival |url=https://www.rollingstone.com/music/videos/jay-z-and-justin-timberlake-perform-holy-grail-at-wireless-festival-20130714 |access-date=July 17, 2013 |magazine=Rolling Stone}}</ref> During the concert at [[Barclays Center]] as part of Timberlake's [[The 20/20 Experience World Tour]] on December 14, 2014, Jay-Z joined him on stage to perform the song.<ref>{{cite magazine |last=Stecker |first=Erin |date=December 15, 2014 |title=Beyonce & Taylor Swift Dance Together at Justin Timberlake Show |url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/6406296/beyonce-taylor-swift-beyonce-justin-timberlake-concert |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150103223534/http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/6406296/beyonce-taylor-swift-beyonce-justin-timberlake-concert |archive-date=January 3, 2015 |access-date=December 15, 2014 |magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]}}</ref>

"Holy Grail" was also performed during the [[On the Run Tour (Beyoncé and Jay-Z)|On the Run Tour]] (2014) and as the opening number on [[On the Run II Tour]] (2018), a co-headlining tour by [[Beyoncé]] and Jay-Z. During the performance of the song, Beyoncé sang Justin Timberlake's parts. ''[[Consequence (publication)|Consequence]]''{{'s}} Alex Young chose the song as one of the particular highlights of the show.<ref name="cos">{{cite web |last=Young |first=Alex |date=June 26, 2014 |title=Jay Z and Beyoncé kick off On the Run Tour with massive 42-song setlist |url=https://consequence.net/2014/06/jay-z-and-beyonce-kick-off-on-the-run-tour-with-massive-42-song-setlist/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140628053955/http://consequenceofsound.net/2014/06/jay-z-and-beyonce-kick-off-on-the-run-tour-with-massive-42-song-setlist/ |archive-date=June 28, 2014 |access-date=June 26, 2014 |work=[[Consequence (publication)|Consequence]]}}</ref>
