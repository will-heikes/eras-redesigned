"'''My Songs Know What You Did in the Dark (Light Em Up)'''" is a song by American [[Rock music|rock]] band [[Fall Out Boy]], released as the [[lead single]] for the band's fifth studio album, ''[[Save Rock and Roll]]''.<ref>{{cite magazine|last=Hogan|first=Mark|title=Fall Out Boy Return to 'Save Rock and Roll' With New Single, Album, and Tour|url=https://www.spin.com/2013/02/fall-out-boy-save-rock-and-roll-2013-tour-dates-my-songs-know-what-you-did-in-the-dark/|magazine=[[Spin (magazine)|Spin]]|access-date=11 February 2013}}</ref> It serves as the band's first single following the group's three-year hiatus and regrouping in early 2013.<ref>{{cite magazine|title=Fall Out Boy to 'Save Rock and Roll' in May|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/1537641/fall-out-boy-to-save-rock-and-roll-in-may|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|access-date=11 February 2013}}</ref> The track and its music video were released on February 4, 2013, worldwide and February 5, 2013, in North America,<ref name="iTunes US"/> to coincide with the official news of the band's reformation. The song impacted radio on February 19, 2013.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.allaccess.com/mail/archive/eweekly/alternative/2013-2-12.html|title=AllAccess.com Alternative eWeekly|work=AllAccess|date=February 12, 2013|access-date=July 3, 2016}}</ref> The band members felt that the song best represented their album at its core.

The song's title and principal lyric originate from an unrelated 2004 demo by the band.<ref>[https://twitter.com/PatrickStump/status/341731432613433344 Patrick Stump tweet on 2004 demo] Twitter.com. Retrieved July 17, 2013.</ref><ref>[https://twitter.com/PatrickStump/status/357304806945210368 Patrick Stump tweet on demo]. Twitter.com. Retrieved July 17, 2013.</ref> Fall Out Boy performed "My Songs Know What You Did in the Dark (Light Em Up)" on ''[[Jimmy Kimmel Live!]]'' for the group's first post-hiatus TV performance on February 13 as well as on various TV shows throughout the entire year, and have extensively played it in acoustic version at radio interviews. Lead vocalist and guitarist [[Patrick Stump]] performed the song as a guest on singer-songwriter [[Taylor Swift]]'s [[The Red Tour|Red Tour]] on July 13, 2013, and Swift guested in turn with Fall Out Boy on [[Victoria's Secret Fashion Show 2013]] on November 13, 2013. The song became a staple at several sports-broadcasting soundtracks that year.<ref>[http://popdust.com/2013/07/03/the-top-50-songs-from-the-first-half-of-2013/ The Top 50 Songs From the First Half of 2013] popdust.com. Retrieved May 3, 2015.</ref><ref>[https://www.rollingstone.com/music/features/how-fall-out-boy-became-jock-jam-all-stars-20150127 How Fall Out Boy Became Jock Jam All-Stars]. ''Rolling Stone''. Retrieved March 3, 2015.</ref>

The music video is part one of ''[[The Young Blood Chronicles]]'', a series in which a video was eventually recorded for every song on ''Save Rock and Roll'' as part of a narrative. "My Songs Know What You Did in the Dark (Light Em Up)" was a comeback success, reaching the two million digital downloads mark in July 2013, being the fourth song by Fall Out Boy to do so, and was certified 3× Platinum by the end of the year. It was named by [[BBC Radio 1]] as one of the "Top 100 Tracks of the Past 5 Years"<ref>[https://web.archive.org/web/20130906012039/http://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/b039bblr/features/radio1salist Radio 1's A List] BBC. Retrieved September 15, 2013</ref> and No. 1 on [[Sirius XM Hits 1]]'s best of 2013 countdown.<ref>[http://blog.siriusxm.com/2014/01/02/best-of-2013-the-best-of-siriusxm-hits-1-countdown/ The Hits 1 Best of 2013 Countdown]. [[SiriusXM Hits 1]]. Retrieved January 3, 2014.</ref> It reached the top 10 on both [[Pop Songs]] and [[Alternative Songs]] with heavy airplay, and peaked at No. 13 on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] with twelve consecutive weeks in the top 20.<ref>[http://acharts.us/song/75799 Fall Out Boy - My Songs Know What You Did In The Dark (light Em Up)]. acharts.us. Retrieved March 20, 2015.</ref>

==Composition==
Sheet music for the song "My Songs Know What You Did in the Dark (Light Em Up)" shows the key of [[D minor]] and tempo range of 76 to 80 beats per minute.<ref>[https://www.musicnotes.com/sheetmusic/mtd.asp?ppn=MN0116004 My Songs Know What You Did in the Dark (Light Em Up)]</ref>

==Music video==
The music video for the song is the first of the ''Young Blood Chronicles'', an ongoing series with the band filming a video for every song on ''Save Rock and Roll''. It begins late at night, with a figure – later revealed to be rapper [[2 Chainz]] – walking away from a black van parked in a forest clearing. He starts a bonfire by pouring gasoline over a wood pile and lighting it up with a match. Two women then exit the van, carrying various musical instruments belonging to Fall Out Boy, among them the band's guitars, a bass, drumsticks and various drums and cymbals, before tossing the items into the fire. The women also throw in amplifiers and Fall Out Boy records such as ''Take This To Your Grave'', ''From Under the Cork Tree'' and ''Live in Phoenix'', along with a Fall Out Boy wristband and [[Fall Out Toy Works]] comics. 2 Chainz then uses a flamethrower to hasten the incineration process, while the women return to the van and open its rear doors, revealing four hooded, bound men implied to be Fall Out Boy themselves. The camera then closes in on 2 Chainz's face as he holds up a match and stares at the band menacingly, ending the video.<ref>[http://top40.about.com/od/singles/gr/Fall-Out-Boy-my-Songs-Know-What-You-Did-In-The-Dark-light-Em-Up.htm Fall Out Boy - "My Songs Know What You Did In the Dark (Light Em Up)"] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131115005429/http://top40.about.com/od/singles/gr/Fall-Out-Boy-my-Songs-Know-What-You-Did-In-The-Dark-light-Em-Up.htm |date=2013-11-15 }}. [[About.com]]. Retrieved November 16, 2013.</ref>

Fall Out Boy's capture is shown in the music video for the following single, "The Phoenix", released on March 25, 2013.

==Critical reception==
Jon Dolan of ''[[Rolling Stone]]'' called the song "a big, goofy, stomp-along [[pop metal|pop-metal]] anthem".<ref name="Rolling Stone article 1">{{cite magazine|last1=Dolan|first1=Jon |title=Fall Out Boy: "My Songs Know What You Did in the Dark (Light Em Up)"|url=https://www.rollingstone.com/music/songreviews/my-songs-know-what-you-did-in-the-dark-light-em-up-20130212|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130218121249/http://www.rollingstone.com/music/songreviews/my-songs-know-what-you-did-in-the-dark-light-em-up-20130212|url-status=dead|archive-date=February 18, 2013|access-date=August 18, 2016|magazine=[[Rolling Stone]]|date=February 12, 2013}}</ref> Jason Lipshutz of ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' described the song as "a natural evolution of the Fall Out Boy sound," adding also that the song is "muscular in scope and jittery in practice, with rolling chants cresting above Stump's nervous energy."<ref name="billboard4">{{cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/review/1558321/fall-out-boy-save-rock-and-roll-track-by-track-review|title=Fall Out Boy, 'Save Rock and Roll': Track-By-Track Review|last=Lipshutz|first=Jason|date=April 16, 2013|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|access-date=August 18, 2016}}</ref> Cassie Whitt of ''[[Loudwire]]'' called the song a "[[Pop music|pop]] banger".<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://loudwire.com/emo-holy-trinity-albums-ranked-my-chemical-romance-fall-out-boy-panic-at-the-disco/|title=Every Emo Holy Trinity Album Ranked From Worst to Best (My Chemical Romance, Fall Out Boy, Panic! ==Commercial performance==

Commercially, "My Songs Know What You Did in the Dark (Light Em Up)" debuted on charts worldwide with success, reaching in the top five in the United Kingdom, charts the band has previously received success from with singles like "[[This Ain't a Scene, It's an Arms Race]]" and "[[Thnks fr th Mmrs]]".

On the U.S. [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]], the song debuted at number twenty-six and number eight on the U.S. ''Billboard'' [[Rock Songs]] chart; it reached number two on [[iTunes]] upon its release with 162,000 first-week sales. In its nineteenth week on the chart, it reached a peak of number thirteen on the Hot 100 and number two on the Rock Songs chart (upon ''Save Rock And Roll'''s release). The song has sold over 5 million downloads in the United States as of August 2015, the first song by Fall Out Boy to reach this level.<ref name="Headline Planet">{{cite news |url=http://headlineplanet.com/home/2015/08/10/fall-out-boys-my-songs-know-goes-5x-platinum-uma-thurman-now-platinum/ |title=Fall Out Boy's "My Songs Know What You Did in the Dark" and "Uma Thurman" earn new RIAA certifications. |last=Cantor |first=Brian |date=August 10, 2015 |work=Headline Planet }}</ref> "My Songs Know What You Did in the Dark" has sold over 1,200,000 copies in the UK earning a double platinum certification by the BPI as of 2026.
