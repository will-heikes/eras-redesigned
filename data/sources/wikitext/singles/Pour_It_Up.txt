"'''Pour It Up'''" is a song by the Barbadian singer [[Rihanna]] from her seventh album, ''[[Unapologetic]]'' (2012). It was serviced to [[urban contemporary|urban]] radio stations in the United States on January 8, 2013, as the second US single, and third overall single from the album. It was later also sent to [[contemporary hit radio]] radios in the country. Written by [[R. City|Rock City]] with Rihanna and producers [[Mike Will Made It]] and JBo, it is a [[Club music|club]], [[Trap music|trap]], and [[Contemporary R&B|R&B]] song with a minimal [[hip-hop]] beat. Rihanna brags about her wealth on the song which serves as both a [[strip club]] anthem and a declaration of independence.

"Pour It Up" received a mixed response from critics, some of whom cited it as a highlight on ''Unapologetic'', whilst others felt it was out of place. Upon its release as a single in the US, "Pour It Up" debuted at number 90 on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] chart, on which it ultimately peaked at number 19. It also became Rihanna's second number one single on the [[Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Airplay]] chart and reached number six on the [[Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs]] chart.

Rihanna performed the song on her fifth and sixth headlining tour, the [[Diamonds World Tour]] and [[Anti World Tour]]. The song's official remix was released on March 20, 2013, and features additional rap verses from [[Jeezy|Young Jeezy]], [[T.I.]], [[Rick Ross]] and [[Juicy J]]. Rihanna took pole-dancing lessons from Nicole Williams before the shooting of the music video.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.huffingtonpost.com/2013/10/02/rihanna-pole-dancing-photos-pour-it-up-video_n_4029113.html |title=Rihanna's Pole Dancing Photos Tease Her 'Pour It Up' Video |work=The Huffington Post |date=October 2, 2013 |access-date=May 19, 2014}}</ref>

==Background and release==
"Pour It Up" was written by Michael Williams, Justin Garner, Theron Thomas and Timothy Thomas ([[Planet VI]]).<ref name="Album notes">{{cite AV media notes |title=[[Unapologetic]]|others=[[Rihanna]] |year=2012|type=CD liner notes |publisher=[[Def Jam Recordings]], SRP Records|id=B0017811-02}}</ref> Rihanna is an additional cowriter of the song. It was produced by Williams under his stage name [[Mike Will Made It]], while being co-produced by JBo of Eardrummer Ent.<ref name="Album notes"/> Williams stated in an interview with [[MTV News]] that he had produced multiple records for Rihanna for ''Unapologetic''. He submitted three tracks in total, with "Pour It Up" making the track list.<ref name=mtv1>{{cite web | url=http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1698303/rihanna-mike-will-unapologetic-pour-it-up-single.jhtml | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121205021528/http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1698303/rihanna-mike-will-unapologetic-pour-it-up-single.jhtml | url-status=dead | archive-date=December 5, 2012 | title=Rihanna Came To Mike Will For 'That Turn-Up' | work=[[MTV|MTV Music]]| date=December 3, 2012 | access-date=January 10, 2013 | author=Markman, Rob}}</ref> "Pour It Up" was released to urban radio in the United States on January 8, 2013, as the second single in the country following ''Unapologetic'''s lead single, "[[Diamonds (Rihanna song)|Diamonds]]".<ref name="UrbanRadio">{{cite web | url=http://top40-charts.com/chart.php?cid=27 | title=Urban/UAC Future Releases | publisher=All Access Music Group | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130111123929/http://top40-charts.com/chart.php?cid=27 | archive-date=January 11, 2013 | url-status=live | access-date=January 10, 2013}}</ref> Over three months later, following the success of the song on the format, it was sent to contemporary hit radio on April 9.<ref name="PopRadio">{{cite web | url=http://www.allaccess.com/top40-mainstream/future-releases| title=Top 40/M Future Releases| work=All Access| publisher=All Access Music Group | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130430152925/http://www.allaccess.com/top40-mainstream/future-releases | archive-date=April 30, 2013 | url-status=live| access-date=August 26, 2013}}</ref>

== Critical reception ==
"Pour It Up" received mixed reviews from music critics. Andy Kellman of [[AllMusic]] called it "convincing", writing that "she's at her best when she's flaunting".<ref name="Kellman">{{cite web|last=Kellman|first=Andy|url=http://www.allmusic.com/album/unapologetic-mw0002439379|title=Unapologetic – Rihanna : Songs, Reviews, Credits, Awards|publisher=[[AllMusic]]. [[Rovi Corporation]]|access-date=November 18, 2012}}</ref> Kellman also noted that "Pour It Up" is "a characteristically chilly and booming Mike Will collaboration", also praising "Rihanna's trash talk", writing that "it's something else".<ref name="Kellman"/> Jon Caramanica of ''[[The New York Times]]'' commented that, "it sounds like a track the ambient-goth outfit Salem might make for a strip club."<ref name="Caramanica">{{cite news|last=Caramanica|first=Jon|date=November 21, 2012|url=https://www.nytimes.com/2012/11/21/arts/music/rihannas-album-unapologetic-makes-most-of-her-talent.html|title=Rihanna, Icy Hot and Steely-Strong|newspaper=[[The New York Times]]|page=C1|access-date=November 21, 2012|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121121124116/https://www.nytimes.com/2012/11/21/arts/music/rihannas-album-unapologetic-makes-most-of-her-talent.html|archive-date=November 21, 2012|url-status=dead}}</ref>  Alex Macpherson of ''[[Fact (United Kingdom magazine)|Fact]]'' wrote that on 'Pour It Up', "Rihanna goes through mere scornful contempt and terrifies with her blank, relentless focus."<ref name=fact>{{cite news|last=Macpherson|first=Alex|title=Unapologetic - FACT Magazine|url=http://www.factmag.com/2012/11/23/rihanna-unapologetic/|access-date=January 17, 2013|newspaper=[[Fact (United Kingdom magazine)|Fact]]|date=November 23, 2012}}</ref> Caryn Ganz of [[Spin (magazine)|''Spin'']] called it "moody and murky".<ref name="Ganz">{{cite journal|last=Ganz|first=Caryn|date=November 28, 2012|url=https://www.spin.com/2012/11/rihanna-unapologetic-def-jam/|title=Rihanna, 'Unapologetic' (Def Jam)|journal=[[Spin (magazine)|Spin]]|location=New York|access-date=November 28, 2012}}</ref> Andrew Hampp of ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' named it "an irresistible, Mike WiLL-produced banger for the ladies."<ref name="Billboard review"/>

Jessica Hopper of ''[[Pitchfork Media]]'' was also mixed, writing that on 'Pour It Up,' "she sounds alternately robotic and narcotized."<ref name="Hopper">{{cite web|last=Hopper|first=Jessica|date=November 26, 2012|url=http://pitchfork.com/reviews/albums/17369-unapologetic/|title=Rihanna: Unapologetic|work=[[Pitchfork Media]]|access-date=November 26, 2012}}</ref> Dan Martin of ''[[NME]]'' criticized the song for "killing the mood", calling it an "[[In da Club|In Da Club]] guff".<ref name="Martin">{{cite journal|last=Martin|first=Dan|date=November 24, 2012|url=http://www.nme.com/reviews/rihanna/13899|title=Rihanna - 'Unapologetic'|journal=[[NME]]|location=London|access-date=November 21, 2012}}</ref> Philip Matusavage of ''[[MusicOMH]]'' was negative, commenting that the "sense of emotional trauma displays itself in a different way on the album", calling it "obnoxious",<ref name=musicomh>{{cite news|last=Matusavage|first=Philip|title=Rihanna - Unapologetic &#124; musicOMH|url=http://www.musicomh.com/albums/rihanna-4_1112.htm|access-date=January 17, 2013|newspaper=[[MusicOMH]]|date=November 19, 2012|archive-date=January 22, 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130122001050/http://www.musicomh.com/albums/rihanna-4_1112.htm|url-status=dead}}</ref> while Randall Roberts of the ''[[Los Angeles Times]]'' named it "a nausea-inducing track".<ref name="Roberts">{{cite news|last=Roberts|first=Randall|date=November 16, 2012|url=http://www.latimes.com/entertainment/music/posts/la-et-ms-review-rihannas-unapologetic-shines-light-on-past-drama-20121116,0,7267834.story|title=Review: Rihanna's 'Unapologetic' shines light on past drama|newspaper=[[Los Angeles Times]]|access-date=November 16, 2012}}</ref> Sarah H. Grant of ''[[Consequence of Sound]]'' reflected that, "the effervescence of her breakthrough ''[[Good Girl Gone Bad (Rihanna album)|Good Girl Gone Bad]]'' is lost to some hopeless place", citing "Pour It Up" as an example.<ref name=consequence>{{cite news|last=H. Grant|first=Sarah|title=Album Review: Rihanna – Unapologetic|url=https://consequence.net/2012/11/album-review-rihanna-unapologetic/|access-date=January 17, 2013|newspaper=[[Consequence of Sound]]|date=November 26, 2012}}</ref>

Cultural critic [[Camille Paglia]] praised the song in an August 2013 interview: "in the past year, the only things that sparked my enthusiasm and gave me hope for an artistic revival were in pop music... [one being] Rihanna’s eerie “Pour It Up,” which uses a strip club as a hallucinatory metaphor for an identity crisis about sex and materialism."<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.salon.com/2013/08/21/camille_paglia_it_remains_baffling_how_anyone_would_think_that_hillary_clinton_is_our_partys_best_chance/ |title=Camille Paglia: "It remains baffling how anyone would think that Hillary Clinton is our party's best chance" |work=[[Salon (website)|Salon]] |date=August 21, 2013 |archive-date=August 16, 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250816113236/https://www.salon.com/2013/08/21/camille_paglia_it_remains_baffling_how_anyone_would_think_that_hillary_clinton_is_our_partys_best_chance/ |url-status=live |access-date=May 19, 2014}}</ref>

==Commercial performance==
Upon the release of ''Unapologetic'', "Pour It Up" debuted at number 191 on the [[Syndicat National de l'Édition Phonographique|French Singles Chart]]. Over three months later, the song re-entered the chart at 199 for the date issued March 23, 2013, climbed to a new peak of number 102 the following week.<ref name="France"/> It debuted at number 90 on the ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' [[Billboard Hot 100|Hot 100]] for the issue dated January 19, 2013, since reaching a Top 20 peak of number 19.<ref name="Hot 100"/> For the issue dated March 2, 2013, "Pour It Up" reached number one on the US [[Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Airplay]] chart, becoming Rihanna's second number one on the chart from 30 entries.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/charts/2013-03-02/hot-r-and-b-hip-hop-airplay|title=R&B/Hip-Hop Airplay|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|access-date=March 2, 2013}}</ref><ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/chart-juice-drake-earns-30th-top-10-single-on-rbhip-hop-songs/|title=Chart Juice: Drake Earns 30th Top 10 Single On R&B/Hip-Hop Songs Chart|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|access-date=February 21, 2013}}</ref> It has remained atop the chart for five consecutive weeks.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/chart-highlights-blake-sheltons-cool-crowns-country-airplay/|title=Chart Highlights: Blake Shelton's 'Cool' Crowns Country Airplay|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|access-date=March 18, 2013}}</ref> It has subsequently charted at number six on the US [[Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs]] chart.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/charts/2013-03-23/r-b-hip-hop-songs|title=Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|access-date=March 23, 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130323220934/https://www.billboard.com/charts/2013-03-23/r-b-hip-hop-songs|archive-date=March 23, 2013}}</ref>
As of May 2013, the song had sold more than one million copies in the United States.<ref name="Week Ending May 5, 2013. Songs: Macklemore Pulls A Gaga">{{cite news|url=http://music.yahoo.com/blogs/chart-watch/week-ending-may-5-2013-songs-macklemore-pulls-193602948.html|title=Week Ending May 5, 2013. Songs: Macklemore Pulls A Gaga|publisher=[[Yahoo! Music]]|access-date=May 5, 2013}}</ref> In its ninth week on the chart, "Pour It Up" entered the [[UK R&B Chart]] at number 40 for the issue dated March 30, 2013.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.officialcharts.com/charts/r-and-b-singles-chart/20130324/114/|title=Archive Chart|publisher=[[Official Charts Company]]|access-date=March 24, 2013}}</ref> It climbed to number 22 the following week.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.officialcharts.com/charts/r-and-b-singles-chart/20130331/114/|title=Archive Chart|publisher=[[Official Charts Company]]|access-date=March 31, 2013}}</ref>

Whatever your issue is leave my fans out of it". That same month, Rihanna posted new behind the scenes pictures from the shoot and stated that the video would be released in October 2013.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.rap-up.com/2013/09/29/rihanna-reveals-pour-it-up-video-pics/ |title=Rihanna Reveals 'Pour It Up' Video Pics |publisher=Rap-Up.com |date=September 29, 2013 |access-date=May 19, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130929172550/http://www.rap-up.com/2013/09/29/rihanna-reveals-pour-it-up-video-pics/|archive-date=September 29, 2013|url-status=dead}}</ref> The video was released on her official YouTube channel October 2, 2013, but became unlisted 30 minutes after release.<ref>{{YouTube|TnMMu0809_w}}</ref> It was later uploaded on Rihanna's official VEVO account. The visual features Rihanna starring as a stripper writhing around and grinding on a giant golden throne, scantily clad in a [[diamond]] bra and a denim thong while [[stripper|strippers]] slide down poles positioned, amid a giant pool of water behind her. It also features the singer chucking a load of money around.

''[[Rolling Stone]]'' said the video which is not of "high art certainly matches the lavish strip-club exploits referenced in the song."<ref>{{cite magazine|last=Reed|first=Ryan|title=Rihanna Twerks Dangerously in 'Pour It Up'|url=https://www.rollingstone.com/music/videos/rihanna-twerks-dangerously-in-pour-it-up-20131002|magazine=[[Rolling Stone]]|access-date=October 5, 2013|date=October 2, 2013|archive-date=October 5, 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131005082921/http://www.rollingstone.com/music/videos/rihanna-twerks-dangerously-in-pour-it-up-20131002|url-status=dead}}</ref> [[HitFix]] gave the video a D grade, "it' s a dimly lit video equivalent of a ''[[Playboy]]'' photo spread that is meant to serve the very same purpose for the boys and men who view it. On January 24, 2013, a freestyle cover of "Pour It Up" was released by rapper [[Trina]].<ref>{{cite journal |date=January 24, 2013 |title=New Music: Trina – 'Pour It Up (Freestyle)' |url=http://www.rap-up.com/2013/01/24/new-music-trina-rihanna-pour-it-up-freestyle/#more-148099 |journal=[[Rap-Up]] |access-date=March 15, 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130515112234/http://www.rap-up.com/2013/01/24/new-music-trina-rihanna-pour-it-up-freestyle/#more-148099|archive-date=May 15, 2013|url-status=dead}}</ref> On February 4, 2013, American rapper [[Lil' Kim]] covered "Pour it Up" featuring her boyfriend "Mr. Papers".<ref>{{cite journal |date=February 4, 2013 |title=New Music: Lil' Kim f/ Mr. Papers – 'Pour It Up (Remix)' |url=http://www.rap-up.com/2013/02/04/new-music-lil-kim-f-mr-papers-pour-it-up-remix/#more-149076 |journal=Rap-Up |access-date=March 15, 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130502074711/http://www.rap-up.com/2013/02/04/new-music-lil-kim-f-mr-papers-pour-it-up-remix/#more-149076|archive-date=May 2, 2013|url-status=dead}}</ref> On March 6, 2013, rappers [[French Montana]] and [[Chinx|Chinx Drugz]] released a cover of the single.<ref>{{cite journal |date=March 21, 2013 |title=French Montana ft. Chinx Drugz - "Pour It Up (Remix)" [LISTEN/DOWNLOAD] |url=http://hiphopwired.com/2013/03/06/french-montana-ft-chinx-drugz-pour-it-up-remix-listendownload/ |journal=Hip Hop Wired |access-date=March 6, 2013}}</ref> [[Redlight (musician)|Redlight]] and Seb Chew also remixed the song in the form of a bootleg titled "Pour It Zero's".<ref>{{cite web |date=April 4, 2013 |title=REDLIGHT & SEB CHEW . > POUR IT ZERO's by Redlight UK |url=https://soundbutt.com/redlight-uk/redlight-seb-chew-pour-it |publisher=SoundButt}}{{Dead link|date=May 2020|bot=InternetArchiveBot|fix-attempted=yes}}</ref> Nigerian Hiphop/Pop duo Young Paperboyz also released their remix of "Pour It Up".<ref>{{Cite web |title=MP3 DOWNLOAD: Young Paperboyz – Pour It Up &#91;Remix&#93; Feat Rihanna |url=http://www.naijamayor.com/mp3-download-young-paperboyz-pour-it-up-remix-feat-rihanna/ |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130705141212/http://naijamayor.com/mp3-download-young-paperboyz-pour-it-up-remix-feat-rihanna/ |archive-date=July 5, 2013 |access-date=August 11, 2013}}</ref> Italian rapper [[Jesto]] featuring [[Briga (singer)|Briga]] and Killa Cali remixed the song making the Italian version called ''Fatti l'uno x l'altra''.<ref>[http://www.hiphoprec.com/notizie-hip-hop/rap-italiano/10306-jesto-ft-briga-e-killa-cali-fatti-l-uno-x-l-altra.html Fatti l'uno x l'altra, "Pour it up" italian remix]</ref>

On March 4, 2013, it was reported that Rihanna was enlisting various rappers for an official remix of "Pour It Up". The reports speculated guest appearances from American rappers [[Juicy J]], [[2 Chainz]], [[T.I.]], [[Rick Ross]] and [[Jeezy|Young Jeezy]].<ref>{{cite journal|date=March 21, 2013|url=http://theversed.com/2013/03/04/rihanna-recruiting-star-studded-lineup-for-pour-it-up-remix-including-both-young-jeezy-and-rick-ross/|title=Rihanna Recruiting Star Studded Lineup For 'Pour It Up' Remix Including Both Young Jeezy and Rick Ross?|journal=The Versed|access-date=March 4, 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131029191132/http://theversed.com/2013/03/04/rihanna-recruiting-star-studded-lineup-for-pour-it-up-remix-including-both-young-jeezy-and-rick-ross/|archive-date=October 29, 2013|url-status=dead}}</ref> The official remix was released on March 20, 2013, featuring all but 2 Chainz, but sometime later, another unmastered version of the remix was released, with 2 Chainz rapping a verse after T.I.. With Rihanna's verses remaining the same, Jeezy begins rapping about "throwing hundreds" and "listening to [[Kendrick Lamar]]". Rick Ross then continues, "Dollar after dollar / Bottle after Bottle / Late for you haters even though my plane chartered", and rapping about "sexy ladies". This is followed by a verse from Juicy J promising "I'll make it rain, shawty / You might need a raincoat", and finally a verse from T.I.<ref>{{cite journal|date=March 20, 2013|url=http://www.rap-up.com/2013/03/20/new-music-rihanna-f-young-jeezy-rick-ross-juicy-j-ti-pour-it-up-remix/#more-153067|title=New Music: Rihanna f/ Young Jeezy, Rick Ross, Juicy J, & T.I. – 'Pour It Up (Remix)'|journal=Rap-Up|access-date=March 20, 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130501170552/http://www.rap-up.com/2013/03/20/new-music-rihanna-f-young-jeezy-rick-ross-juicy-j-ti-pour-it-up-remix/#more-153067|archive-date=May 1, 2013}}</ref><ref name="MTV">{{cite journal|date=March 20, 2013|url=http://buzzworthy.mtv.com/2013/03/20/rihanna-young-jeezy-rick-ross-juicy-j-t-i-pour-it-up-remix/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130327081353/http://buzzworthy.mtv.com/2013/03/20/rihanna-young-jeezy-rick-ross-juicy-j-t-i-pour-it-up-remix/|url-status=dead|archive-date=March 27, 2013|title=Rihanna Featuring Young Jeezy, Rick Ross, Juicy J, And T.I., 'Pour It Up (Remix)' (NSFW) |journal=[[MTV]]|access-date=March 20, 2013}}</ref>

Michael Depland of [[MTV]] said, "While we may never get a proper "Pour It Up" video, we DO (''sic'') have the next best thing: a brand-new remix featuring Young Jeezy, Rick Ross, Juicy J, and T.I. Can you imagine if all those dudes rolled through a strip club together? Every dancer in the place could probably retire that night! With all these hip-hop titans on this naughty track, the only rapper we can figure that's missing is [[Drake (musician)|Drake]]. He did recently drop $50,000 in a strip club, but we understand why he may have been left out of this particular remix. When a dude has legal issues with your boyfriend, you probably wouldn't invite him to collabo."<ref name="MTV"/> Joe Lynch of ''Fuse'' noted the addition of both Rick Ross and Young Jeezy saying, "Even though Rick Ross said he "tried to finger" Young Jeezy at the [[BET Hip Hop Awards]] last September, the two rappers have put aside their backstage beef for a higher power: Rihanna. Well, Rihanna and/or money."<ref>{{cite journal|date=March 20, 2013|url=http://www.fuse.tv/2013/03/rihanna-pour-it-up-remix-beefing-rick-ross-young-jeezy|title=Rihanna 'Pour It Up' Remix Reunites Beefing Rick Ross & Young Jeezy|journal=Fuse|access-date=March 20, 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130725231038/http://www.fuse.tv/2013/03/rihanna-pour-it-up-remix-beefing-rick-ross-young-jeezy|archive-date=July 25, 2013|url-status=dead}}</ref>
