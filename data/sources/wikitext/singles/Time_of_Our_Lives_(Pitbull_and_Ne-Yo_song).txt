"'''Time of Our Lives'''" is a song by American rapper [[Pitbull (rapper)|Pitbull]] and American singer [[Ne-Yo]], from the former's eighth studio album ''[[Globalization (album)|Globalization]]'' and the latter's sixth studio album ''[[Non-Fiction (Ne-Yo album)|Non-Fiction]]''. It was released on November 17, 2014, as the third single from the album by [[RCA Records]]. The single was produced by [[Dr. Luke]], [[Cirkut (record producer)|Cirkut]] and Lifted.

The song marks the fourth joint production for co-producers Dr. Luke and Cirkut with Pitbull (after "[[Timber (Pitbull song)|Timber]]", "[[Wild Wild Love]]", and "[[We Are One (Ole Ola)]]") and third for Ne-Yo (after "[[She Knows (Ne-Yo song)|She Knows]]" and [[T-Pain]]'s "[[Turn All the Lights On]]"). It became Pitbull's most successful single from ''Globalization'', reaching number nine on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]]. "Time of Our Lives" also went to number one on the US Rhythmic chart.

==Music video==
The music video was first released onto Pitbull's official [[Vevo]] channel on December 25, 2014. It was directed by [[Gil Green (director)|Gil Green]]. On January 20, 2015, a behind the scenes video was also released on the channel.<ref>{{cite AV media|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wu0yuIEvLZY|title=Pitbull, Ne-Yo – Time of Our Lives (BTS)|date=20 January 2015|work=YouTube}}</ref> The video has received over 200 million views, making it 2x [[Vevo]] certified.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bTXJQ5ql5Fw|title=Pitbull – Time of Our Lives ft. Ne-Yo|date=25 December 2014 |publisher=[[YouTube]]}}</ref>

The video shows Pitbull and Ne-Yo saving up their spare money to save up for a [[Millennium celebrations|New Year's Eve]] party in 1999. Pitbull appears in an old school [[Varsity team|Varsity]] jacket dancing while Ne-Yo sings along in the crowd of women while sporting a fitted hat and Aviators. American singer and actress [[Fergie (singer)|Fergie]] makes a cameo at the party.

== In the media  ==
The song is featured in the pilot episode of ''[[Cooper Barrett's Guide to Surviving Life]]'', and the 2016 films ''[[The 5th Wave (film)|The 5th Wave]]'' and ''[[Office Christmas Party]]''.

==Chart performance==
The single debuted at number 94 on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] chart, on the week of December 13, 2014.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/charts/hot-100/2014-12-13|title=Hot 100 - 2014-12-13|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|date=2 January 2013|access-date=March 1, 2020}}</ref> The following week, the song fell off the chart. On the week of January 3, 2015, the song re-entered the chart at number 87.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/charts/hot-100/2015-01-03|title=Hot 100 - 2015-01-03|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|access-date=March 1, 2020}}</ref> After climbing the chart for three months, the single reached its peak at number nine on the chart.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/charts/hot-100/2015-03-21|title=Hot 100 - 2015-03-21|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|access-date=March 1, 2020}}</ref> The song is Pitbull's last US top ten hit to date. On October 16, 2020, the single was certified [[RIAA Certification|quadruple platinum]] by the [[Recording Industry Association of America]] (RIAA) for combined sales and streaming equivalent units of over four million units in the United States.<ref name="RIAA"/>
