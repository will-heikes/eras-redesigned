"'''Sorry'''" is a song by American [[Hip hop music|hip hop]] recording artist [[Rick Ross]], featuring vocals from American singer [[Chris Brown]]. This was released on October 9, 2015, as the first [[single (music)|single]] from his  eighth studio album ''[[Black Market (Rick Ross album)|Black Market]]''.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/columns/the-juice/6721879/rick-ross-chris-brown-sorry-listen|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151009200854/http://www.billboard.com/articles/columns/the-juice/6721879/rick-ross-chris-brown-sorry-listen|url-status=dead|archive-date=October 9, 2015|title=Rick Ross Releases New Track 'Sorry' With Chris Brown: Listen|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|access-date=November 20, 2015}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://itunes.apple.com/us/album/sorry-feat.-chris-brown-single/id1046136436|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151121201718/https://itunes.apple.com/us/album/sorry-feat.-chris-brown-single/id1046136436|url-status=dead|archive-date=November 21, 2015|title=iTunes - Music - Sorry by (feat. Chris Brown) - Single by Rick Ross|publisher=[[iTunes Store]]. [[Apple Inc]]|access-date=November 20, 2015}}</ref> The song was produced by [[Scott Storch]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.hotnewhiphop.com/576080-rick-ross-sorry-cdq-feat-chris-brown-prod-by-scott-storch-new-song|title=Rick Ross benefits greatly from a Chris Brown feature and vintage Scott Storch production on new single "Sorry."|date=October 7, 2015 |publisher=hotnewhiphop|access-date=November 20, 2015}}</ref>

==Background and composition==
"Sorry" has been described as a "vulnerable" track, where Ross and Brown apologize to their respective girlfriends following their unfaithfulness.<ref>{{cite web | url=https://www.complex.com/music/a/zach-frydenlund/rick-ross-new-song-sorry-f-chris-brown | title=Rick Ross Grabs Chris Brown for "Sorry" | website=[[Complex Networks]] }}</ref> Brown's apology in the song is addressed to [[Karrueche Tran]], his ex-girlfriend who broke up with him after discovering that the singer unwittingly fathered a child with another woman.<ref>{{cite web | url=https://urbanislandz.com/2015/10/13/chris-brown-confirmed-sorry-karrueche-not-rihanna/ | title=CHRIS BROWN Says I Wrote "Sorry" for Karrueche Not Rihanna | date=October 13, 2015 }}</ref> Speaking of the song, Ross described it as a "personal" track "who speaks for itself",<ref>{{cite web | url=https://www.hotnewhiphop.com/34322-rick-ross-announces-new-single-sorry-featuring-chris-brown-news | title=Rick Ross Announces New Single "Sorry" Featuring Chris Brown | website=HNHH | date=October 5, 2015 }}</ref> while Brown said: {{cquote|text=One of the most honest records I’ve done in a while. I appreciate Ross for allowing me to be on [the] record and speak from the heart.<ref>{{cite web|title=Chris Brown talks about "Sorry"|url=https://www.capitalxtra.com/artists/rick-ross/music/chris-brown-collaboration-sorry/|website=Capital Xtra}}</ref>}}
