"'''Get Ugly'''" is a song by American singer [[Jason Derulo]] for his fourth studio album ''[[Everything Is 4]]'' (2015). It was released as the album's fourth single (third in US markets) on December 15, 2015.<ref>{{cite web | url=http://www.allaccess.com/top40-mainstream/future-releases | title=top40-mainstream future-releases | work=AllAccess | access-date=December 5, 2015}}</ref> Derulo co-wrote the song with [[Jason Evigan]], [[Sean Douglas (songwriter)|Sean Douglas]], and [[Ricky Reed]]; the latter is also the producer. The track samples "We Got Our Own Thang" by [[Heavy D|Heavy D & the Boyz]] from their second studio album ''[[Big Tyme]]''.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.dailymotion.com/video/x3lldqo|title = Jason Derulo has GET UGLY... But here's to where the original sample came from..... - video Dailymotion|date = January 9, 2016}}</ref>

==Critical reception==
''[[Pitchfork (website)|Pitchfork]]'' criticized "Get Ugly", calling it: "a brutal attempt to reprise the ubiquitous "[[Wiggle (Jason Derulo song)|Wiggle]]," though the result is more like "[[Sexyback]]" re-imagined under the influence of a half dozen [[Jägerbomb|Jaeger bomb]]s."<ref>https://pitchfork.com/reviews/albums/20669-everything-is-4/</ref>

==Music video==
The [[music video]] for the track, directed by Syndrome, premiered via Derulo's [[YouTube]] channel on December 14, 2015.<ref>{{cite web|title=Jason Derulo's 'Get Ugly' Video Is, In Fact, Very Appealing To The Eyes|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/2687651/jason-derulo-get-ugly-video/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151215222611/http://www.mtv.com/news/2687651/jason-derulo-get-ugly-video/|url-status=dead|archive-date=December 15, 2015|publisher=[[MTV]]|date=December 14, 2015|access-date=December 15, 2015}}</ref> Allison Bowsher of [[Much (TV channel)|Much]] TV channel called it a "good old fashioned dance video" and wrote that it "showcase his talent as a dancer".<ref>{{cite web|title=Jason Derulo Gets Down In Video For 'Get Ugly'|url=http://www.much.com/jason-derulo-gets-down-in-video-for-get-ugly/|publisher=[[Much (TV channel)|Much]]|access-date=October 2, 2016}}</ref> Colin Joyce from ''[[Spin (magazine)|Spin]]'' wrote that the video "plays like a re-imagining of [[Lionel Richie]]'s "[[All Night Long (All Night)|All Night Long]]" video".<ref>{{cite web|title=Jason Derulo Leads a Pastel-Hued Dance Crew in His 'Get Ugly' Video|url=http://www.spin.com/2015/12/jason-derulo-get-ugly-video-everything-is-4-watch/|publisher=[[Spin (magazine)|Spin]]|date=December 14, 2015|access-date=October 2, 2016}}</ref> Radio.com's Amanda Wicks said that Derulo "shows off some fresh moves by way of slick choreography" throughout the video.<ref>{{cite web|title=Watch Jason Derulo Get Colorful in Cartoonish Video for 'Get Ugly'|url=http://radio.com/2015/12/14/watch-jason-derulo-get-colorful-in-cartoonish-video-for-get-ugly/|publisher=Radio.com|date=December 14, 2015|access-date=October 2, 2016}}</ref>
