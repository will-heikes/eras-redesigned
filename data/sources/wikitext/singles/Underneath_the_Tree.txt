"'''Underneath the Tree'''" is a [[Christmas music|Christmas]] song by American singer [[Kelly Clarkson]] from her sixth studio album and debut [[Christmas album]], ''[[Wrapped in Red]]'' (2013). She co-wrote the track with its producer [[Greg Kurstin]]. Accompanied by various instrumental sounds, the song is given a [[Wall of Sound]] treatment, along with [[jingle bell|sleigh bells]] and [[chime (bell instrument)|bell chimes]] to create a Christmas atmosphere. "Underneath the Tree" was first released to [[Adult Contemporary]] radio stations on November 5, 2013, by [[RCA Records]] as the album's lead single.

"Underneath the Tree" has been praised by music critics, who considered it the main highlight of ''Wrapped in Red'' and recognized its potential to be a Christmas classic. They favorably compared it to [[Mariah Carey]]'s 1994 Christmas song "[[All I Want for Christmas Is You]]". The track reached number seven on the [[Billboard Global 200|''Billboard'' Global 200]] in addition to the top ten in Australia, Austria, Canada, Germany, Lithuania, the Netherlands, Switzerland, the United Kingdom, and the United States. It also charted within the top 20 in Belgium, the Czech Republic, Finland, Hungary, Ireland, Latvia, Luxembourg, Portugal, Singapore, and Slovakia. In 2021, "Underneath the Tree" was named [[ASCAP]]'s most popular Christmas song released in the 21st century.<ref>{{cite magazine|title=What Are the Biggest Christmas Songs Released This Century? ASCAP Has a List and Checked It Twice|first=Chris|last=Willman|magazine=Variety|date=December 9, 2021|access-date=July 21, 2022|url=https://variety.com/2021/music/news/christmas-songs-most-popular-justin-bieber-ariana-grande-1235130396/}}</ref>

Directed by English director [[Hamish Hamilton (director)|Hamish Hamilton]], the music video for the song has a live performance from the television special ''[[Kelly Clarkson's Cautionary Christmas Music Tale]]'' at [[The Venetian Las Vegas]]. Apart from the television special, Clarkson has also performed it in various televised performances, such as ''[[Late Night with Jimmy Fallon]]'' and the American singing competition ''[[The Voice (American TV series)|The Voice]]''.

After recording tracks for her greatest hits compilation, ''[[Greatest Hits – Chapter One (Kelly Clarkson album)|Greatest Hits – Chapter One]]'', Clarkson began discussing with her record label [[RCA Records]] about plans for her sixth studio album being a [[Christmas album|Christmas release]] in late 2012, during which she also began to write original Christmas material.<ref name="Billboard 1" /> [[Greg Kurstin]], whom she had previously collaborated with, was approached by Clarkson to produce the album; and together, they co-wrote the tracks "Underneath the Tree" and "4 Carats".<ref name="Liner notes – Wrapped in Red" /> "Underneath the Tree" was produced by Kurstin, who also played most of the instruments, including a [[mellotron]] and a [[Chamberlin]], to evoke a [[Phil Spector]]'s [[Wall of Sound]] treatment—a sound that ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' and [[HitFix]] noticed upon hearing the song.<ref name="Billboard 1" /><ref name="HitFix 1" /> He also had Clarkson provide all the voices in it, including all its backing vocals. She recalled the experience as new, stating, "I've never had to do anything like this before—doing all my backup vocals, essentially being my own choir."<ref name="Billboard 1" />

"Underneath the Tree" is an uptempo [[Christmas music|Christmas]]<ref name="Taste of Country 1" /> pop<ref name="Music Notes" /> song written by Clarkson and Kurstin.<ref name="Music Notes" /> It is one of the five original songs recorded for ''Wrapped in Red''. According to the sheet notes at Musicnotes published by [[EMI Music Publishing]], the song is written in the key of [[E-flat major|E{{music|flat}} major]], in which the [[vocal range]] spans from [[E♭ (musical note)|E{{music|flat}}<sub>4</sub>]] to [[G (musical note)|G<sub>5</sub>]].<ref name="Music Notes" /> Apart from the Wall of Sound, various other sounds, such as [[Jingle bell|sleigh bells]] and [[chime (bell instrument)|bell chimes]], are also prominently heard on the song, which features a [[bari saxophone]] solo on the bridge.<ref name="Idolator 1" />

==Release==
On October 18, 2013, Clarkson premiered "Underneath the Tree" on her [[Vevo]] channel.<ref name="Digital Spy 1" /> On November 5, 2013, RCA Records released it as a single on [[Adult Contemporary (chart)|Adult Contemporary]] radio stations in the United States,<ref name="Radio and Records 1" /> while also planning to release it on [[Mainstream Top 40 (Pop Songs)|Mainstream Pop]] stations.<ref name="Billboard 1" /> It was then released as a digital download in the United Kingdom on November 22, 2013, and in the United States on December 9, 2013.<ref name="Digital download - single" /> A digital EP containing remixes of the song was released internationally on December 17, 2013.<ref name="Digital download - Remixes EP" />

==Critical response==
[[File:Mariah Carey Neighborhood Ball in downtown Washington 2009 cropped.JPG|left |thumb|250px|Critics approvingly compared "Underneath the Tree" to the 1994 Christmas track "[[All I Want for Christmas Is You]]", originally sung by [[Mariah Carey]] (pictured).]]
"Underneath the Tree" has received critical acclaim from various music critics on its release, who praised its catchiness and festive arrangement.<ref name=":0">{{Cite news |last=Eames |first=Tom |date=June 4, 2024 |title=Kelly Clarkson's 10 best songs, ranked |url=https://www.smoothradio.com/artists/kelly-clarkson/songs-best-ranked/ |access-date=May 30, 2025 |work=[[Smooth Radio (2014)|Smooth Radio]] |quote=The song was critically acclaimed for its catchy melody and festive arrangement, drawing comparisons to Mariah Carey's 'All I Want for Christmas Is You'.}}</ref> ''Billboard''{{'}}s Marisa Fox called it the album's "biggest surprise",<ref name="Billboard 1" /> and described it as an optimistic tune that has Clarkson powering through [[Darlene Love]]-style vocals.<ref name="Billboard 1" /> Mikael Wood of the ''[[Los Angeles Times]]'' described it as a remarkably cheery single.<ref name="Los Angeles Times 1" /> In her review, Christina Vinson of [[Taste of Country]] remarked that the track "has everything a Christmas song could want: a jazzy, big band sound, saxophone and bells ringing, all bottled up in an optimistic and catchy holiday track" and said that Clarkson "shines", as her "strong vocals are needed to offset the snazzy production and chorus."<ref name="Taste of Country 1" /> In his review, [[Idolator (website)|Idolator]]'s Carl Williott wrote that the song "sounds instantly familiar—sleigh bells, laments about a lonely Christmas day, a sax solo—but with a tightly coiled hook and Clarkson's transcendent voice, the whole thing is kind of invigorating."<ref name="Idolator 1" /> Sarah Rodman of ''[[The Boston Globe]]'' called it a bouncy, cooing number tailor-made to soundtrack sleigh rides, and named it the best track on the album.<ref name="The Boston Globe 1" />

Critics also approvingly compared it to [[Mariah Carey]]'s "[[All I Want for Christmas Is You]]" (1994),<ref name=":0" /> a track that RCA chief executive [[Peter Edge]] described as the song's inspiration.<ref name="Billboard 2" /> Williot added that "Underneath the Tree" was "already practically a Christmas standard" one week after being released.<ref name="Idolator 1" /> Jenna Hally Rubenstein of [[MTV News]] remarked, "if we may be so bold, it is definitely on par with other impeccable Christmas originals—namely Carey's "All I Want For Christmas Is You"."<ref name="MTV 1" /> Sal Cinquemani of ''[[Slant Magazine]]'' noted the similar themes of both songs, and that "Underneath the Tree" is likely to become Clarkson's very own contemporary standard.<ref name="Slant Magazine" /> Melinda Newman of HitFix wrote, "It's a finger-popping, bell-ringing pleaser that could achieve that rare feat of becoming a new Christmas standard. That hasn't happened since Carey's "All I Want For Christmas" in 1994."<ref name="HitFix 1" /> ''[[The Independent]]''{{'}}s Hugh Montgomery called "Underneath The Tree" a "winner on all fronts" for having "sass, jingle, and a sax solo".<ref name="The Independent 1" />

==Commercial performance==
"Underneath the Tree" has recurrently charted annually since its 2013 release. It first entered the ''Billboard'' [[Holiday Digital Song Sales|Holiday Digital Songs]] chart at number four on November 16, 2013, as part of ''Wrapped in Red''. After its release as a single, it reached number three on December 28, 2013.<ref name="Billboard 3" /> The song also debuted at number 21 on the ''Billboard'' [[Adult Contemporary (chart)|Adult Contemporary]] chart, reaching number one after three weeks, becoming Kelly Clarkson's third chart-topper in that category.<ref name="Billboard 4" /><ref name="Billboard 5" /> Additionally, it entered the ''Billboard'' [[Billboard Christmas Holiday charts|Holiday 100 chart]] at number 34 and later reached the top ten.<ref name="The Hollywood Reporter 1" /><ref name="Billboard 6" /> On the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]], it debuted at number 92 in December 2013, rising to number 78 by January 2014.<ref name="Chart Performance - US" /><ref name="Chart Performance - US 2" /> "Underneath the Tree" re-entered the Hot 100 multiple times, climbing to number 44 in 2018, number 31 in 2019, number 30 in 2020, number nine in 2024 (becoming Clarkson 12th top-ten song), and finally number seven in December 2025.<ref name="Chart Performance - US 8"/> With this, Clarkson earned her 12th Hot 100 hit and her first since "[[Piece by Piece (song)|Piece by Piece]]" in 2016, while the song also became the second-newest holiday track to reach the Hot 100's top ten (after [[Ariana Grande]]'s "[[Santa Tell Me]]").<ref name="Chart Performance - US 5" />

Internationally, "Underneath the Tree" charted in various countries, including Austria, Canada, Germany, Ireland, the Netherlands, and the United Kingdom. In Canada, it debuted at number 41 on the ''Billboard'' [[Canadian Hot 100]] in December 2013, before charting at number seven in December 2024.<ref>{{Cite magazine |title=Kelly Clarkson |url=https://www.billboard.com/artist/kelly-clarkson/ |access-date=November 18, 2024 |magazine=Billboard}}</ref> In the UK, it entered the [[UK singles chart]] at number 79 for the week ending December 14, 2013, before re-charting in 2022, 2023, and 2024, rising at number ten in 2024, becoming Clarkson tenth top-ten song.<ref name="auto" /> The song has sold over 318,000 units and has been streamed over 26 million times in the UK as of 2019.<ref name="Chart Performance - UK 3" /> In Ireland the song entered at number 37 in January 2020, before eventually charting at number 15 in December 2021.<ref name="auto" /> The song also debuted at number 54 on the Australian [[ARIA Singles Chart]] in December 2019, before eventually reaching number nine in January 2024.<ref>{{Cite web |title=ARIA Top 50 Singles Chart |url=https://www.aria.com.au/charts/singles-chart/2024-01-01 |access-date=November 18, 2024 |publisher=Australian Recording Industry Association}}</ref>

In Austria, it debuted at number 68 on the [[Ö3 Austria Top 40]] in 2014 and later peaked at number eight.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Hung |first=Steffen |title=Kelly Clarkson - Underneath The Tree |url=https://austriancharts.at/showitem.asp?interpret=Kelly+Clarkson&titel=Underneath+The+Tree&cat=s |access-date=November 18, 2024 |publisher=Austriancharts.at}}</ref> In the Netherlands, it entered the Dutch Top 40 chat at number 26 before rising to number 25 the following week in January 2013.<ref>{{Cite web |author=<!-- not stated --> |title=Underneath The Tree: Kelly Clarkson |url=https://www.top40.nl/kelly-clarkson-3/underneath-the-tree-24559 |date=2013 |access-date=August 14, 2025 |website=Top 40 NL |language=nl}}</ref> In Japan, "Underneath the Tree" debuted on the ''Billboard'' [[Japan Hot 100]] chart at number 84 for the week ending January 4, 2014, and spent two weeks on the chart.<ref>{{Cite magazine |title=Kelly Clarkson |url=https://www.billboard.com/artist/kelly-clarkson/chart-history/jpn/ |access-date=November 18, 2024 |magazine=Billboard}}</ref> In Germany, it opened at number 43 in December 2018, before eventually ascending to number five in December 2024. In Switzerland, it debuted at number 73 in December 2017, before reaching climbing to number ten in December 2023.

==Music video==
On the eve of ''Wrapped in Red''{{'}}s release, RCA Records released an animated lyric video for "Underneath the Tree", featuring shots of ice skating, a snow-covered town, and copious Christmas trees.<ref name="Huff Post 1" /> The lyric video was released to positive reviews, with Idolator's Sam Lansky describing it as "a nice visual accompaniment to the song that just lends an extra high-octane Yuletide cheer to its already-effervescent spirit".<ref name="Huff Post 1" /><ref name="Idolator 2" /> [[Hamish Hamilton (director)|Hamish Hamilton]] directed the official music video, which premiered on December 3, 2013, on [[Vevo]] and featured a live performance of the song from the stage of Clarkson's [[NBC]] television special, ''[[Kelly Clarkson's Cautionary Christmas Music Tale]]''.<ref name="Kelly Clarkson's Cautionary Christmas Music Tale" /><ref name="Idolator 3" />

==Live performances==
Clarkson has performed "Underneath the Tree" in various televised performances. She first performed "Underneath the Tree" in a live televised performance on ''[[Today (American TV program)|The Today Show]]'' on November 26, 2013.<ref name="NBC 1" /> Apart from being performed from the set of ''Kelly Clarkson's Cautionary Christmas Music Tale'' featured in the song's accompanying music video, a different live performance was aired during the television special itself, in which Clarkson was accompanied by children while singing it.<ref name="The A.V. Club" /> She also performed the song on the [[The Voice (American season 5)|fifth season]] of the American singing competition ''[[The Voice (American TV series)|The Voice]]'' on December 3, 2013.<ref name="TV by the Numbers 1" /><ref name="The Wrap 1" /> On December 5, 2013, she then performed it on ''[[The Ellen DeGeneres Show]]'' and on ''[[Late Night with Jimmy Fallon]]''.<ref name="Idolator 4" /> In 2016 Clarkson performed the track on ''[[The Wonderful World of Disney|The Wonderful World of Disney: Magical Holiday Celebration]]'' television special at the [[Walt Disney World|Walt Disney World resort]],<ref name="ABC News 1" /> and the recording was used on the holiday comedy film ''[[Office Christmas Party]]''.<ref name="IMDb 1" /> In December 2019, Clarkson recorded a performance of the song on her talk show, ''[[The Kelly Clarkson Show]]'', as part of its "Kellyoke" segment.<ref name="Vulture 1" />

==Impact==
[[Brian Mansfield]] of ''[[USA Today]]'' reported that "Underneath the Tree" was American radio's most-played new holiday song of 2013.<ref name="USA Today 1" /> In a report published by [[ASCAP]], "Underneath the Tree" was listed as the most-performed new and original holiday song in their repertoire in the years 2015, 2018, and 2019.<ref name="Chart Performance - US 3" /><ref name="Chart Performance - US 6" /><ref name="Chart Performance - US 7" /> Marketing curator [[PlayNetwork]] also reported that the track was one of the only newer original songs heard by holiday shoppers in 2015.<ref name="Chart Performance - US 4" /> In a 2017 [[op-ed]] piece by ''[[Pitchfork (website)|Pitchfork]]'' contributor Grayson Haver Currin on guitarist [[John Fahey (musician)|John Fahey]]'s Christmas music, he sampled the song as a "new Christmas classic" along with "All I Want for Christmas Is You".<ref name="Pitchfork 1" /> In an interview on ''[[Vogue (magazine)|Vogue]]'', [[Genius (website)|Genius]] content director Elizabeth Milch referred to the song as "the most recent thing to make a play for the "All I Want" crown",<ref name="Vogue 1" /> while ''[[The Washington Post]]'' writer Allison Stewart also described it as a close cousin to "All I Want for Christmas is You".<ref name="Washington Post 1" /> In a 2019 [[Mediabase]] report by ''[[The Atlanta Journal-Constitution]]'', "Underneath the Tree" is the only new song from the past 20 years in their most played top 50 Christmas songs, as most of the tracks that are being played are from the 1940s to 1960s.<ref name="Atlanta Journal-Constitution 1" />

In a retrospective listing of the best holiday songs from each year since 1967, ''Billboard'' editors chose "Underneath the Tree" as the best holiday song of 2013.<ref name="Billboard 12" /> Announcing the return of their 2019 Holiday 100 chart, ''Billboard'' reported that the track is the only one that was released in the 21st century to appear inside the top twenty.<ref name="Billboard 13" /> In a 2019 data analysis by ''[[Quartz (publication)|Quartz]]'' reporter Dan Kopf, he remarked that the song might be a successor to "All I Want for Christmas is You" based on its chart performance.<ref name="Quartz 1" /> Various publications also promulgated "Underneath the Tree" in their best Christmas songs lists throughout the years:
