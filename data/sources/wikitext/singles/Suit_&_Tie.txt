"'''Suit & Tie'''" is a song by American singer [[Justin Timberlake]] featuring American rapper [[Jay-Z]] from the former's third studio album, ''[[The 20/20 Experience]]'' (2013). Written and produced by Timberlake, [[Timbaland]], and [[J-Roc]], with additional writing from [[James Fauntleroy]] and Jay-Z, it contains a portion of the 1972 song "Sho' Nuff" by [[Sly, Slick and Wicked]], whose members are credited as co-writers as a result. A mid-tempo [[Contemporary R&B|R&B]] and [[soul music|soul]] song, it incorporates a [[drawl]], slowed-down synths, and "slightly out of time" drum claps, similar to the chopped and screwed remixing style. Lyrically, it is an ode to the joys of "being handsome and well-dressed".

Released on January 14, 2013, as the [[lead single]] from ''The 20/20 Experience'', "Suit & Tie" received mixed-to-positive reviews from music critics, who praised its production and Timberlake's vocal performance but were divided on its impact as a comeback single. It became a commercial success, selling 315,000 first-week downloads in the US and surpassing the 250,000 debut of "[[SexyBack]]" in 2006, becoming Timberlake's highest-sales week for a download at the time until the release of "[[Can't Stop the Feeling!]]" in 2016. It peaked at number three on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]], the [[UK singles chart]], and the [[Canadian Hot 100]]. By 2018, it had sold 3.3&nbsp;million downloads in the US.

The accompanying music video, directed by [[David Fincher]], was shot on January 25, 2013, and released on Timberlake's Vevo page on Valentine's Day 2013. Timberlake and Jay-Z promoted "Suit & Tie" with performances at the DirecTV Super Saturday Night, the [[55th Annual Grammy Awards]], ''[[Saturday Night Live]]'', the [[SXSW]] MySpace Secret Show, and the [[2013 MTV Video Music Awards]]. Additionally, it was included on the [[set list]]s for four of the former's concert tours: the [[Legends of the Summer Stadium Tour]] (2013), [[the 20/20 Experience World Tour]] (2013–2015), [[the Man of the Woods Tour]] (2018–2019), and [[the Forget Tomorrow World Tour]] (2024–2025).

==Background==
In September 2006, Timberlake released his second album, ''[[FutureSex/LoveSounds]]'',<ref>{{cite news |last=Derschowitz |first=Jessica |date=June 20, 2006 |title=Timberlake To Release His 'Lovesounds' |url=https://www.cbsnews.com/news/timberlake-to-release-his-lovesounds/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140115022122/https://www.cbsnews.com/news/timberlake-to-release-his-lovesounds/ |archive-date=January 15, 2014 |access-date=June 15, 2026 |agency=[[CBS News]]}}</ref> which was a commercial success<ref>{{cite web |title=The Evolution Of: Justin Timberlake |url=http://www.mtv.com/photos/the-evolution-of-justin-timberlake/1667344/6382862/photo.jhtml |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121108011230/http://www.mtv.com/photos/the-evolution-of-justin-timberlake/1667344/6382862/photo.jhtml |archive-date=November 8, 2012 |access-date=September 14, 2011 |publisher=[[MTV]]}}</ref> and received generally positive reviews from music critics.<ref>{{cite Metacritic |id=futuresex-lovesounds/justin-timberlake |type=music |title=FutureSex / LoveSounds |access-date=July 8, 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230213013353/https://www.metacritic.com/music/futuresex-lovesounds/justin-timberlake |archive-date=February 13, 2023 |url-status=live}}</ref> The album spawned six singles, including the US number-one singles "[[SexyBack]]", "[[My Love (Justin Timberlake song)|My Love]]", and "[[What Goes Around... Comes Around]]".<ref>{{cite magazine |title=Justin Timberlake Chart History (Hot 100) |url=https://www.billboard.com/artist/justin-timberlake/chart-history/hsi/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240209121953/https://www.billboard.com/artist/justin-timberlake/chart-history/hsi/ |archive-date=February 9, 2024 |access-date=February 6, 2024 |magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]}}</ref> After wrapping up a [[FutureSex/LoveShow|worldwide concert tour]] to support the album in 2007, Timberlake took a break from his music career to focus on acting,<ref>{{cite web |last=Douglas |first=Ana |date=May 24, 2012 |title=After A 6 Year Hiatus, Justin Timberlake Is Going Back to Music |url=http://www.businessinsider.com/after-a-6-year-hiatus-justin-timberlake-is-getting-back-in-the-music-bizvia-films-2012-5 |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130426153325/http://www.businessinsider.com/after-a-6-year-hiatus-justin-timberlake-is-getting-back-in-the-music-bizvia-films-2012-5 |archive-date=April 26, 2013 |access-date=January 14, 2013 |website=[[Business Insider]]}}</ref><ref name="Anderson-2012">{{cite magazine |last=Anderson |first=Kyle |date=August 7, 2012 |title=Justin Timberlake Totally Not Working on 'Crazy' New Album, Despite What a Dude Named Jim Beanz Says |url=http://music-mix.ew.com/2012/08/07/justin-timberlake-new-album-not-happening/ |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120809204103/http://music-mix.ew.com/2012/08/07/justin-timberlake-new-album-not-happening/ |archive-date=August 9, 2012 |access-date=January 14, 2013 |magazine=[[Entertainment Weekly]]}}</ref> with occasional guest appearances on several singles by [[Madonna]], [[T.I.]], [[Jamie Foxx]], [[Timbaland]], and [[Esmée Denters]].<ref name="Anderson-2012" />

Timberlake's music manager [[Johnny Wright (music manager)|Johnny Wright]] approached him in 2010, discussing possibility of going back to his music career and the difficulties of releasing his future material, because according to Wright, "a lot of the physical record sellers were gone, by the time they've got music again they needed to think about different ways to deliver it". Wright proposed a promotion based on an [[application software|application]] or releasing a new song every month. Timberlake, however, was not interested in returning to music; instead, he continued to focus on his film career. Around the "late part of May, first week in June" 2012, Timberlake invited Wright to dinner and revealed that he had spent the last couple of nights in the studio with Timbaland working on new material. Wright was shocked by the revelation, and the two immediately began planning how to promote and release the album. Ultimately, they agreed on a shorter period, seven or eight weeks, between the singles and the album. Wright told ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' that "such a short window" demanded "a big impact".<ref name="Aswad-2013">{{cite magazine |last=Aswad |first=Jem |date=March 19, 2013 |title=Justin Timberlake Manager Johnny Wright on Secret Plans Around '20/20 Experience,' Touring, Myspace, More |url=https://www.billboard.com/biz/articles/news/branding/1552474/justin-timberlake-manager-johnny-wright-on-secret-plans-around?page=0%2C1 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130324002255/http://www.billboard.com/biz/articles/news/branding/1552474/justin-timberlake-manager-johnny-wright-on-secret-plans-around?page=0%2C1 |archive-date=March 24, 2013 |access-date=March 22, 2013 |magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]] |pages=1-2}}</ref>

In August 2012, producer [[Jim Beanz]] reported that Timberlake had started work on his new music project.<ref>{{cite web |last=Corner |first=Lewis |date=August 7, 2012 |title=Exclusive: Justin Timberlake Working on "Crazy" New Album, Says Jim Beanz |url=http://www.digitalspy.com/music/news/a397907/justin-timberlake-working-on-crazy-new-album-says-jim-beanz.html |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120810233147/http://www.digitalspy.com/music/news/a397907/justin-timberlake-working-on-crazy-new-album-says-jim-beanz.html |archive-date=August 10, 2012 |access-date=May 10, 2026 |publisher=[[Digital Spy]]}}</ref> Shortly after the announcement, Timberlake's publicist denied plans for a new album, stating Timberlake was instead working on Timbaland's upcoming project ''Shock Value III''.<ref name="Anderson-2012" /> Wright stated that although the project involved artists who are primarily Timberlake's friends, it was tough to keep it a secret, so they used codenames. Originally planned for release in October 2012, Timberlake's project was postponed because of his wedding to actress [[Jessica Biel]].<ref name="Aswad-2013" />

==Release==
In early January 2013, via his official Twitter account, Timberlake posted, "I think I'M READY" and later tweeted a link to a YouTube video explaining his absence from music.<ref name="O'Donnell-2013">{{cite magazine |last=O'Donnell |first=Kevin |date=January 10, 2013 |title=Justin Timberlake: 'I'm Ready' to Release New Music |url=http://www.people.com/people/article/0,,20663389,00.html |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130112232512/http://www.people.com/people/article/0,,20663389,00.html |archive-date=January 12, 2013 |access-date=January 10, 2013 |magazine=[[People (magazine)|People]]}}</ref> In the minute-long video, a camera follows Timberlake as he walks through a recording studio, while his voice-over explains it has been so long since his last album because he is not interested in releasing anything he does not love. The video closes with Timberlake entering a recording booth, putting on headphones, and saying, "I'm ready". Subsequently, on the singer's official site, a countdown leading up to Monday, January 14, at 12&nbsp;am. ET appeared, prompting speculation about the release of a new single and album to break Timberlake's hiatus. That timing coincided with a tweet that [[Power 105.1]] radio personality Charlamagne posted, saying, "Justin, Jay-Z Timbaland. New record Monday".<ref>{{cite magazine |last=Schneider |first=Marc |date=January 10, 2013 |title=Justin Timberlake: 'I'm Ready' to Make Music Again |url=https://www.billboard.com/music/music-news/justin-timberlake-im-ready-to-make-music-again-1484236/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130226033510/http://www.billboard.com/biz/articles/news/1510433/justin-timberlake-ready-to-return-to-music |archive-date=February 26, 2013 |access-date=January 13, 2013 |magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]}}</ref>

"Suit & Tie" was released by [[RCA Records]] on January 14, 2013,<ref name="iTunes Store (US)-2013">{{cite web |last=Timberlake |first=Justin |author-link=Justin Timberlake |date=January 14, 2013 |title=iTunes – Music – Suit & Tie (feat. JAY Z) – Single by Justin Timberlake |url=https://itunes.apple.com/us/album/suit-tie-feat.-jay-z-single/id592303884 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130117093431/https://itunes.apple.com/us/album/suit-tie-feat.-jay-z-single/id592303884 |archive-date=January 17, 2013 |access-date=January 13, 2026 |publisher=[[iTunes Store]] (US)}}</ref> and was promoted as the lead single from Timberlake's third studio album, ''[[The 20/20 Experience]]'', released on March 15, 2013.<ref>{{cite web |last=Timberlake |first=Justin |author-link=Justin Timberlake |date=March 15, 2013 |title=iTunes – Musik – The 20/20 Experience (Deluxe Version) von Justin Timberlake |url=https://itunes.apple.com/de/album/20-20-experience-deluxe-version/id598462389 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130309183244/https://itunes.apple.com/de/album/20-20-experience-deluxe-version/id598462389 |archive-date=March 9, 2013 |access-date=March 28, 2013 |publisher=[[iTunes Store]] (DE) |language=German}}</ref> Additionally, Timberlake collaborated with social media platform [[Myspace]] for the site's relaunch on January 15. The site's front page featured an image of Timberlake in a suit and tie, and Myspace offered to stream or download "Suit & Tie" to those who signed up for or logged in to Myspace.<ref>{{cite news |last=Kaufman |first=Gil |date=January 15, 2013 |title=Justin Timberlake's 'Suit' & Tie' Dresses Up Myspace Relaunch |url=http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1700217/new-myspace-justin-timberlake-suit-and-tie.jhtml |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130117044055/http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1700217/new-myspace-justin-timberlake-suit-and-tie.jhtml |archive-date=January 17, 2013 |access-date=January 16, 2013 |publisher=[[MTV News]]}}</ref> Eliot Van Buskirk of Evolver.fm wrote that by doing this, Timberlake  "appears to be sending a message to other artists about what the new Myspace is for: posting music for free, and trying to upsell it to downloads".<ref>{{cite web |last=Van Buskirk |first=Eliot |date=January 15, 2013 |title=Myspace Relaunches With New Justin Timberlake Single 'Suit & Tie' |url=https://www.wired.com/2013/01/justin-timberlake-myspace-single/ |url-access=subscription |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140415144914/https://www.wired.com/2013/01/justin-timberlake-myspace-single/ |archive-date=April 15, 2014 |access-date=December 18, 2025 |publisher=Evolver.fm |via=[[Wired (magazine)|Wired]]}}</ref>

==Critical reception==
[[File:Jay-Z @ Shawn 'Jay-Z' Carter Foundation Carnival (crop 2).jpg|thumb|upright=0.7|[[Jay-Z]]'s contribution on "Suit & Tie" received mixed reviews.|alt=Jay-Z in 2011.]]
Upon release, "Suit & Tie" received mixed-to-positive reviews from music critics. Andy Kellman of AllMusic gave it three out of five stars, stating it "serves the same flirty dancefloor purpose" as "Rock Your Body".<ref name="Kellman" /> Andrew Halverson of ''[[Beats Per Minute (website)|Beats Per Minute]]'' said the song "seems to point to the notion that JT still knows what he's doing".<ref>{{cite web |last=Halverson |first=Andrew |date=January 16, 2013 |title=Track Review: Justin Timberlake – 'Suit & Tie (feat. Jay Z)' |url=https://beatsperminute.com/track-review-justin-timberlake-suit-tie-feat-jay-z/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250114195615/https://beatsperminute.com/track-review-justin-timberlake-suit-tie-feat-jay-z/ |archive-date=January 14, 2025 |access-date=December 18, 2025 |website=[[Beats Per Minute (website)|Beats Per Minute]]}}</ref> Deb Doing Dallas of the ''[[Dallas Observer]]'' called it "just one song off an album that is entirely capable of making up for this sleepy release".<ref>{{cite news |author=Deb Doing Dallas |date=January 15, 2013 |title=Justin Timberlake 'Suit & Tie' is a Boring Rehash |url=https://www.dallasobserver.com/music/justin-timberlake-suit-and-tie-is-a-boring-rehash-7074272/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20191113053944/https://www.dallasobserver.com/music/justin-timberlake-suit-and-tie-is-a-boring-rehash-7074272/ |archive-date=November 13, 2019 |access-date=April 3, 2026 |newspaper=[[Dallas Observer]]}}</ref> A reviewer from ''[[The Independent]]'' wrote that Timberlake's voice appears to have "gone up an octave" since he last released new music. The reviewer also compared "Suit & Tie" to "[[Rock Your Body]]" and "[[SexyBack]]".<ref name="The Independent-2013" /> August Brown of the ''[[Los Angeles Times]]'' called it "a radiant, ramshackle song that's less of a coherent single and more of a coronation event".<ref>{{cite news |last=Brown |first=August |date=January 14, 2013 |title=Single Review: Justin Timberlake's 'Suit & Tie' |url=https://www.latimes.com/entertainment/music/la-xpm-2013-jan-14-la-et-ms-single-review-justin-timberlake-suit-tie-20130114-story.html |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20221203070748/https://www.latimes.com/entertainment/music/la-xpm-2013-jan-14-la-et-ms-single-review-justin-timberlake-suit-tie-20130114-story.html |archive-date=December 3, 2022 |access-date=December 19, 2025 |newspaper=[[Los Angeles Times]]}}</ref> Melinda Newman of HitFix said to "leave it to Timberlake" to represent R&B in a way that "few contemporary artists are today", noting [[Bruno Mars]] and [[Miguel (singer)|Miguel]]  as exceptions.<ref name="Newman-2013" />

Rebecca Twomey of ''[[Marie Claire]]'' said: "It appears it's been worth the wait too as Justin is posed to shake-up the charts with his new sound".<ref>{{cite magazine |last=Twomey |first=Rebecca |date=January 14, 2013 |title=Justin Timberlake Unveils New Track, Suit and Tie, Featuring Jay-Z |url=https://www.marieclaire.co.uk/news/celebrity-news/listen-justin-timberlake-unveils-new-track-suit-and-tie-featuring-jay-z-131884 |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190923075910/https://www.marieclaire.co.uk/news/celebrity-news/listen-justin-timberlake-unveils-new-track-suit-and-tie-featuring-jay-z-131884 |archive-date=September 23, 2019 |access-date=April 3, 2026 |magazine=[[Marie Claire]]}}</ref> [[Stephen Deusner]] of ''[[Pitchfork (website)|Pitchfork]]'' awarded "Suit & Tie" the website's Best New Track tag, praising Timberlake's vocal performance and Timbaland's production by saying: "Timbaland creates a smooth beat out of a marimba roll and harp glissando that Marvin Gaye must have left on the cutting-room floor, and Timberlake rides it with that fluid, effortless falsetto". Deusner, however, called the feature verse from Jay-Z "another in a string of uninspired cameos", but said it could not "sink [the song's] slinky vibe".<ref>{{cite magazine |last=Deusner |first=Stephen M. |author-link=Stephen Deusner |date=January 14, 2013 |title=Justin Timberlake: 'Suit & Tie' [ft. Jay-Z] |url=https://pitchfork.com/reviews/tracks/14762-suit-tie/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130115101654/https://pitchfork.com/reviews/tracks/14762-suit-tie/ |archive-date=January 15, 2013 |access-date=December 1, 2025 |magazine=[[Pitchfork (website)|Pitchfork]]}}</ref> [[Jody Rosen]] of ''[[Rolling Stone]]'' gave it three-and-a-half out of five stars, and said it "is natty—as expertly tailored and crisply pressed as a high-end tux".<ref>{{cite magazine |last=Rosen |first=Jody |author-link=Jody Rosen |date=January 14, 2013 |title=Justin Timberlake Feat. He stated it is not a "sound-redefining, statement-making, globe-conquering comeback single" like "SexyBack", but "more of a midway point" between his previous singles "[[Señorita (Justin Timberlake song)|Señorita]]" and "[[Summer Love (Justin Timberlake song)|Summer Love]]".<ref name="Cragg-2013" /> Nate Jones of ''Popdust'' gave it three-and-a-half out of five, and said to "think of it as an homage to Jay-Z's wife, as a thank you for Jay guesting on a song about Justin's own better half".<ref>{{cite web |last=Jones |first=Nate |date=March 12, 2013 |title=Justin Timberlake's "20/20 Experience" Reviewed: "Suit & Tie" |url=http://popdust.com/2013/03/12/justin-timberlakes-2020-experience-reviewed-suit-and-tie/ |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130314194847/http://popdust.com/2013/03/12/justin-timberlakes-2020-experience-reviewed-suit-and-tie/ |archive-date=March 14, 2013 |access-date=May 15, 2026 |website=Popdust}}</ref>
