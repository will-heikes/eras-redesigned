"'''You & I (Nobody in the World)'''" is a song by American singer [[John Legend]]. Legend co-wrote the song with James Ryan Ho, [[Dave Tozer]] and [[Dan Wilson (musician)|Dan Wilson]], with [[Record production|production]] by [[Malay (producer)|Malay]], Tozer and Legend. It impacted [[urban adult contemporary]] radio in the United States on April 29, 2014, as the fourth single from his fourth studio album ''[[Love in the Future]]'' (2013).<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.allaccess.com/urban/future-releases |title=Urban/UAC Future Releases - R&B, Hip Hop, Release Schedule and Street Dates - ... |work=All Access |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140225142501/http://www.allaccess.com/urban/future-releases |archive-date=February 25, 2014 }}</ref>

== Music video ==
The music video for "You & I (Nobody in the World)" was released on July 10, 2014. In the video, Legend "showcases his appreciation for the true beauty of all women" and sings about his love for his wife [[Chrissy Teigen]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.complex.com/music/2014/07/watch-john-legends-video-for-you-and-i-nobody-in-the-world|title=Watch John Legend's "You & I (Nobody In the World)" Video|author=Zach Frydenlund|date=July 10, 2014|work=Complex}}</ref> The video includes appearances by actress [[Laverne Cox]], [[Tig Notaro]] and [[Tatyana Ali]].<ref>{{cite web|last=Bendix |first=Trish |url=http://www.afterellen.com/morning-brew-jodie-foster-is-back-on-the-set-of-orange-is-the-new-black/07/2014/ |title=Morning Brew - Jodie Foster is back on the set of "Orange is the New Black" |publisher=AfterEllen.com |date=July 11, 2014 |access-date=July 19, 2014}}</ref>
