"'''First Love'''" is a song recorded by American singer [[Jennifer Lopez]] for her eighth studio album, ''[[A.K.A. (album)|A.K.A.]]'' (2014). It was written by [[Max Martin]], [[Savan Kotecha]] and [[Ilya Salmanzadeh]], and produced by Martin and Ilya, with vocal production being handled by Martin, Peter Carlsson, and Ilya. It was released by [[Capitol Records]] as the album's second single on May 1, 2014. The song was first developed when Lopez was talking to Martin, Kotecha and [[Cory Rooney]] about relationships during a break from the album recording. A day later, they appeared with the lyrics of the track, and Lopez recorded it.

"First Love" is a [[pop music|pop]], [[electropop]] and [[contemporary R&B|R&B]] song with synth beats, "hard-hitting percussion" and "big drums" as the song's instrumentation, with critics also noting influences from her previous material. Lyrically, "First Love" talks about finding true love and wishing you would have met the person you're with now years ago, so they can be your first love. The song received positive reviews from most [[music journalism|music critics]], who commended the track for lifting the bar for high-quality pop music, while also praising its "fun and free feel".

Commercially, "First Love" received minor success on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''US'' Billboard Hot 100]] chart, but managed to receive moderate airplay on the [[Pop Songs|''US'' Pop Songs]] and topped the [[Hot Dance Club Songs|''US'' Hot Dance Club Songs]]. It also reached number two in Hong Kong, the top-twenty in [[Spain]] and the top-fifty in [[Italy]]. The accompanying music video for "First Love" was released on May 29, 2014, and was directed by [[Anthony Mandler]]. The black-and-white music video features British model [[David Gandy]] and Lopez during love scenes in a [[desert]]. Lopez promoted the track with live performances on the [[2014 Billboard Music Awards|2014 ''Billboard'' Music Awards]], ''[[American Idol]]'', ''[[Late Night with Jimmy Fallon]]'', as well as on her concerts throughout 2014 - blending the song with elements of "[[Ain't No Mountain High Enough]]" as well as a dance-break.

== Background and release ==
While working on her then-upcoming album, Lopez expressed the interest on working with Swedish [[record producer]] and [[songwriter]] [[Max Martin]]. According to the singer, "I had really wanted to work with Max for many, many years, and it just never happened," she explained. She further commented: "For [A.K.A.] I was like, 'I want to work with you.' I met with him in the summertime, and he was like, 'I'm just going off to do [[Adele]]'s album!' or somebody he works with all the time. I was like, 'When are you back?', he was like, 'October', I was like, 'I'll wait for you'."<ref name=fuse>{{cite news|last1=Hughes|first1=Hilary|title=Jennifer Lopez Tells the Story Behind "First Love" - Interview - Fuse|url=http://www.fuse.tv/videos/2014/06/jennifer-lopez-first-love-interview|access-date=December 8, 2014|work=[[Fuse (TV channel)|Fuse]]|date=June 19, 2014}}</ref> About Martin, Lopez added: "He's a super producer, maker of great pop hits. I've always wanted to work with him but our paths [hadn't] crossed. I sought him out."<ref name="BB Listening Party">{{cite magazine|first=Erika |last=Ramirez |title=Jennifer Lopez Previews New Album in Malibu: First Listen|url=https://www.billboard.com/music/rb-hip-hop/jennifer-lopez-previews-new-album-in-malibu-first-listen-6070019/|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|date=April 25, 2014|access-date=April 30, 2014}}</ref> Later, when he got back, they started trying to find the right song to record and Martin eventually called his songwriter partner, American songwriter [[Savan Kotecha]], to work with them on what would become "First Love".<ref name=fuse/>

The song was first previewed during a six-song listening party of Lopez's then-upcoming eighth studio album on April 25, 2014. During the event, she also revealed that the song would serve as the album's second single."<ref name="BB Listening Party"/> The artwork for the single was unveiled on April 27 and it features Lopez standing behind a chain-link fence.<ref>{{cite magazine|title=Single Cover: Jennifer Lopez&nbsp;– 'First Love'|url=http://www.rap-up.com/2014/04/27/single-cover-jlo-jennifer-lopez-first-love|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140428091738/http://www.rap-up.com/2014/04/27/single-cover-jlo-jennifer-lopez-first-love/|url-status=dead|archive-date=April 28, 2014|magazine=[[Rap-Up]]|date=April 27, 2014|access-date=April 30, 2014}}</ref><ref name=idolatorcover>{{cite web|title=Jennifer Lopez Unveils Flawless "First Love" Cover Art, Confirms The Pop Anthem As Next Single|first=Mike|last=Wass|url=http://www.idolator.com/7515996/jennifer-lopez-first-love-cover-art|archive-url=https://archive.today/20140501011317/http://www.idolator.com/7515996/jennifer-lopez-first-love-cover-art|url-status=dead|archive-date=May 1, 2014|publisher=[[Idolator (website)|Idolator]]|date=April 28, 2014|access-date=April 30, 2014}}</ref> "First Love" was leaked online on April 30, 2014<ref>{{cite web|title=Jennifer Lopez's new single 'First Love' emerges online&nbsp;— listen|url=https://www.digitalspy.com/music/news/a567767/jennifer-lopezs-new-single-first-love-emerges-online-listen.html|first=Amy|last=Davidson|website=[[Digital Spy]]|date=April 30, 2014|access-date=April 30, 2014}}</ref> and [[Capitol Records]] released to [[Music download|digital download]] a day later.<ref name=itunesus/> It was also sent to [[contemporary hit radio|contemporary radio]] on May 20, 2014.<ref name=r&r/> In the [[United Kingdom]], the song was available to digital download on June 8, 2014.<ref name="Amazon.co.uk"/>

While taking a break in between recording sessions for ''A.K.A.'', Lopez had a discussion with [[Cory Rooney]], [[Max Martin]], and [[Savan Kotecha]] about relationships. They talked about "how relationships can be difficult and you have to work at them. And how, when you finally do find that person you wish you had found them years ago."<ref>{{cite news|title=Jennifer Lopez - J Lo Speaks: First Love|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kzB97fcVeRU|access-date=December 8, 2014|work=[[VEVO|Jennifer Lopez Vevo]]|publisher=[[YouTube]]|date=June 19, 2014}}</ref> A couple of days later, Martin and Kotecha called Lopez and told her that they came up with a song based on the conversation they had. According to Lopez: "I was on the set of [American] [[American Idol|Idol]], doing the Hollywood Week, and they wanted to come over and play it for me, they were so excited. They came over at lunch time, to my small dressing room backstage at Idol to play this song and I just loved it. It captured this great feeling in a simple way. I feel like the best songs are like that. It was pretty exciting, we recorded it in the next couple of days."<ref name=radio.com>{{cite news|title=Interview: With 'A.K.A.,' Jennifer Lopez Is More Herself Than Ever|url=http://radio.com/2014/05/07/jennifer-lopez-interview-aka-first-love/|work=[[Radio.com]]|date=May 7, 2014}}</ref>

"First Love" was written by Martin, Kotecha and Swedish songwriter [[Ilya Salmanzadeh]], with production being handled by Martin and Ilya<!-- As written on the album's booklet -->. It was recorded at Conway Studios, Los Angeles, California, and at Wolf Cousin Studios, Stockholm, Sweden. Jeanette Olsson provided [[background vocals]], while Martin and Ilya were also responsible for [[programming (music)|programming]] and vocal production, with Peter Carlsson serving as the latter's. Serban Ghenea mixed the song at MixStar Studios, Virginia Beach, and John Hanes engineered the track.<ref name="Album Credits">{{cite AV media notes |title=A.K.A.|others=[[Jennifer Lopez]] |year=2014 |type=Booklet |publisher=[[Capitol Records]], a division of [[Universal Music Group|UMG Recordings]] |location=Los Angeles, [[California|CA]]}}</ref>

== Commercial performance ==
"First Love" received minor impact on the charts, especially on the [[Billboard charts|''Billboard'' charts]], where it managed to chart on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] at number 87, its debut peak.<ref>{{cite magazine|last1=Mendizabal|first1=Amaya|title=J.Lo Tops R&B/Hip Hop Albums, T.I. & Iggy Team Up for a Top 10 Hit|url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/jlo-tops-rbhip-hop-albums-ti-iggy-team-up-for-a-top-10-hit/|access-date=December 9, 2014|magazine=Billboard|date=June 26, 2014}}</ref> However, the song fared better on [[radio]] and in the [[Nightclub|clubs]], where it reached a peak of number 21 on the [[Mainstream Top 40]] chart<ref name=popsongs/> and topped the [[Hot Dance Club Songs]], becoming her 15th number-one there. With that feat, she tied with [[Donna Summer]] for the seventh-most toppers in the chart's 38-year history.<ref>{{cite magazine|last1=Murray|first1=Gordon|title=Basement Jaxx Bounces Back On Dance Charts|url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/basement-jaxx-clean-bandit-jennifer-lopez/|access-date=December 9, 2014|magazine=Billboard|date=September 5, 2014}}</ref> In the [[UK]], the song only managed to debut at number 63; her lowest charting-single since "[[Papi (Jennifer Lopez song)|Papi]]" (2011).<ref>{{cite web|title=Jennifer Lopez > The Official Charts|url=http://www.officialcharts.com/artist/_/Jennifer%20Lopez|website=[[The Official Charts Company]]|access-date=June 17, 2014}}</ref> In [[Ireland]], it debuted at number 79.<ref name=irish/> The song fared somewhat better in [[Italy]] and [[Spain]], where it reached a peak of numbers 41 and 15, respectively.<ref name=italy/><ref name=spain>{{cite web|title=spanishcharts.com&nbsp;– Jennifer Lopez&nbsp;– Love?|url=http://spanishcharts.com/showitem.asp?interpret=Jennifer+Lopez&titel=Love%3F&cat=a|work=[[Productores de Música de España|PROMUSICAE]]|publisher=Hung Medien|access-date=May 17, 2011}}</ref> In [[South Korea]], it managed to debut at number 15, with first-week sales of 7,582 copies.<ref name=gaon/>

=== Background and shooting ===
{{Quote box|width=30%|align=right|quote="I've been asked if I'd take part in music videos before and I've always said no, but I jumped at the chance of working with a music icon such as Jennifer. A [[music video]] for the song was shot in the [[Deserts of California|California desert]] outside of [[Los Angeles]]  in early May, with [[Anthony Mandler]] being the director. Lopez claimed that the video shoot was the hardest she's done in her career. "It was like the apocalypse," she said on [[iHeartRadio]]. "It was like purgatory. I was waiting for frogs to fall out of the sky. It was a wind storm. Literally, it felt like we were in the middle of a tornado. There was this much dust on my face. We looked like we were covered up on soot. And it was freezing. And of course, you know, in the video I'm not wearing all that much."<ref name=idolatorvid1>{{cite news|last1=Lee|first1=Christina|title=Jennifer Lopez Teases Steamy "First Love" Video|url=http://www.idolator.com/7519352/jennifer-lopez-first-love-video-teaser|archive-url=https://archive.today/20140602041907/http://www.idolator.com/7519352/jennifer-lopez-first-love-video-teaser|url-status=dead|archive-date=June 2, 2014|access-date=June 1, 2014|work=[[Idolator (website)|Idolator]]|agency=[[Spin Media]]|date=May 21, 2014}}</ref> Regarding the video's concept, she elaborated:

On May 27, 2014, Lopez teased the video on her [[Instagram]] account, uploading a sneak peek of it, also announcing that it would be fully released the same day on [[Entertainment Tonight]].<ref>{{cite news|last1=Wass|first1=Mike|title=Jennifer Lopez Teases Her Super Sexy "First Love" Video On Instagram|url=http://www.idolator.com/7519952/jennifer-lopez-sexy-first-love-video-preview|archive-url=https://archive.today/20140602041842/http://www.idolator.com/7519952/jennifer-lopez-sexy-first-love-video-preview|url-status=dead|archive-date=June 2, 2014|access-date=June 1, 2014|work=[[Idolator (website)|Idolator]]|publisher=[[Spin Media]]|date=May 27, 2014}}</ref> Lopez also revealed to ''[[People (magazine)|People]]'', "We talked a lot about whether we would do a little bit of color or pops of color. But Anthony [the director] felt strongly that it be all in black and white. It's a sweet and sexy video, I think," she said. "It definitely captures the feeling of the song."<ref name=people>{{cite news|last1=Gomez|first1=Patrick|title=See Jennifer Lopez in Her Steamy New 'First Love' Video|url=http://www.people.com/article/jennifer-lopez-first-love-music-video|access-date=June 1, 2014|work=[[People Magazine]]|date=May 30, 2014}}</ref>

=== Synopsis and reception ===
[[File:David Gandy for GQ Japan by Arnaldo Anaya-Lucca (2009)-a substOP.jpg|right|135px|thumb|British model [[David Gandy]] (''pictured'') is Lopez's love interest in the video.]]

The [[black-and-white]] [[music video]] starts with Lopez exclaiming, "I knew I had to leave the world behind me to find out who I was," as she wanders the Californian desert in "her finest couture gown," as noted by Mike Wass from ''[[Idolator (website)|Idolator]]''. "I knew that love would never search for me, that I would have to search for it," she continues.<ref name=idolatorvideo>{{cite news|last1=Wass|first1=Mike|title=Jennifer Lopez Heats Up The Desert In Sultry "First Love" Video: Watch|url=http://www.idolator.com/7520363/jennifer-lopez-first-love-video|archive-url=https://archive.today/20140602041846/http://www.idolator.com/7520363/jennifer-lopez-first-love-video|url-status=dead|archive-date=June 2, 2014|access-date=June 1, 2014|work=[[Idolator (website)|Idolator]]|publisher=[[Spin Media]]|date=May 29, 2014}}</ref> It then cuts to the singer in an Issa De'mar plunging black bathing suit, as she writhes around on the ground in the [[desert]], while a mysterious biker arrives in the desert in full motorcycle gear. Later, the video sees Lopez wearing a variety of outfits before encountering British model [[David Gandy]].

The music video was received with positive response from most critics. Mike Wass of ''[[Idolator (website)|Idolator]]'' called it her "sexiest visual since 2011's breathtaking '[[I'm Into You]]', also noting that "it's a fairly simple set-up but, in director Anthony Mandler's capable hands, it turns out to be an absolute feast for the eyes. The black-and-white photography is gorgeous, J.Lo has never looked better and her co-star isn't exactly hard on the eyes either."<ref name=idolatorvideo/> Rachel McRady of ''[[US Weekly]]'' praised Lopez for looking "more fit and fabulous than ever after more than two decades in the business."<ref>{{cite news|last1=McRady|first1=Rachel|title=Jennifer Lopez Rolls Around the Desert in Swimsuit in New "First Love" Music Video: Watch Here|url=https://www.usmagazine.com/entertainment/news/jennifer-lopez-first-love-music-video-2014295|access-date=June 1, 2014|work=[[US Magazine]]|date=May 30, 2014}}</ref> Rebecca Macatee of ''[[E! News]]'' wrote that the singer "flaunts insanely sexy body" in the video,<ref name=e!news/> while Patrick Gomez of ''[[People (magazine)|People]]'' noted that she "continues to look half her age in the sensual music video."<ref name=people/> Lucas Villa of ''[[AXS (ticket merchant)|AXS]]'' found the video to be reminiscent of late director [[Herb Ritts]]' classic clips.<ref>{{cite news|last1=Villa|first1=Lucas|title=Jennifer Lopez and model David Gandy burn up the desert in 'First Love' video|url=http://www.axs.com/news/jennifer-lopez-and-model-david-gandy-burn-up-the-desert-in-first-love--11370|access-date=December 10, 2014|work=AXS|date=May 30, 2014}}</ref> Carolyn Menyes of ''Music Times'' felt that the video "looks like a fancy old school perfume ad."<ref>{{cite news|last1=Menyes|first1=Carolyn|title=WATCH: Jennifer Lopez 'First Love' Music Video Recalls a Perfume Ad with Black & White Love|url=https://www.musictimes.com/articles/6451/20140529/watch-jennifer-lopez-first-love-music-video-recalls-perfume-ad.htm|access-date=December 10, 2014|work=Music Times|date=May 29, 2014}}</ref>

== Live performances ==
Lopez first performed "First Love" during the [[2014 Billboard Music Awards]], where she was awarded with the 2014 Icon Award.<ref>{{cite news|last1=Ramirez|first1=Erika|title=Jennifer Lopez Receives Icon Award, Performs 'First Love' at the 2014 Billboard Music Awards|url=https://www.billboard.com/articles/events/bbma-2014/6092051/jennifer-lopez-icon-award-billboard-music-awards|access-date=June 2, 2014|work=[[Billboard Magazine]]|date=May 18, 2014}}</ref> During the performance, Lopez blended "First Love" with Motown classic '[[Ain't No Mountain High Enough]].'<ref>{{cite news|last1=LongMire|first1=Becca|title=Billboard Music Awards 2014: Jennifer Lopez Recreates Grammys 2000 Look As She Sizzles In Red Chiffon|url=http://www.entertainmentwise.com/news/149563/Billboard-Music-Awards-2014-Jennifer-Lopez-Recreates-Grammys-2000-Look-As-She-Sizzles-In-Red-Chiffon|access-date=June 2, 2014|work=Entertainment Wise}}</ref> Michelle McGahan of ''Pop Crush'' praised Lopez for "not only look[ing] jaw-dropping in a one-shoulder, sparkly black dress, but she belted out the tune with such fervor and showed the world just how well she can really move. (We're talking, rivaling [[Beyoncé]]. That's how good her performance was!) The singer looked so fierce and she so totally nailed the number."<ref>{{cite news|last1=McGahan|first1=Michelle|title=Jennifer Lopez Sizzles in 'First Love' Performance at 2014 Billboard Music Awards [VIDEO]|url=https://popcrush.com/jennifer-lopez-first-love-2014-billboard-music-awards-video/|access-date=December 10, 2014|work=Pop Crush|date=May 19, 2014}}</ref> She also performed the same medley during her free concert in the [[Bronx]], on June 4, 2014.<ref>{{cite magazine|last1=Hampp|first1=Andrew|title=Jennifer Lopez Brings 15 Years of Hits Home to The Bronx (Review)|url=https://www.billboard.com/articles/events/live/6113697/jennifer-lopez-bronx-concert-review-setlist-video-pelham-bay-park|access-date=December 10, 2014|magazine=Billboard|date=June 5, 2014}}</ref> Lopez also performed the track during the finale of the [[American Idol (season 13)|thirteenth season]] of the [[American Idol]], wearing a unique blue one-piece.<ref name=hollywoodlife>{{cite news|title=Jennifer Lopez's Sexy 'First Love' Performance On 'American Idol'|url=http://hollywoodlife.com/2014/05/21/jennifer-lopez-performance-american-idol-finale-season-13/|access-date=June 2, 2014|work=[[Hollywood Life]]|date=May 21, 2014}}</ref> Halfway through "First Love," Jennifer shouted that she was ready to dance — and started to run off the stage and jumped onto the judge's table where she showed some sexy moves.
