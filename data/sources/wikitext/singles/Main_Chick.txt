"'''Main Chick'''" is a song by American rapper [[Kid Ink]] from his second studio album ''[[My Own Lane]]'' (2014). The song was serviced to [[rhythmic contemporary]] radio in the U.S. on March 11, 2014, as the album's third single. The song features a guest appearance by [[Chris Brown]], with [[Record production|production]] provided by [[Mustard (record producer)|DJ Mustard]].

"Main Chick" was originally included on Chris Brown's ''X Files'' mixtape, and after the positive response, it was included on ''My Own Lane''. It debuted at number 69 on the [[UK singles chart|UK Singles Chart]] upon the album's release and peaked at number 60 on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] chart.

== Background and recording==
"Main Chick" was [[Record production|produced]] by [[Mustard (record producer)|DJ Mustard]] and was recorded by Chris Brown and Kid Ink, during the same session as their hit single "[[Show Me (Kid Ink song)|Show Me]]".<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6cH_qK2olUI |title=Kid Ink Speaks On New Album "MY OWN LANE" |publisher=YouTube |date=2014-01-08 |access-date=2014-01-16}}</ref> The song was originally not going to be featured on the album, so it was included on Chris Brown's mixtape ''X-Files''.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.complex.com/music/2013/11/chris-brown-x-files-ep |title=Chris Brown "X Files" EP |publisher=Complex |date=2013-11-19 |access-date=2014-01-16}}</ref> It ended up receiving an extremely positive response from fans, making him reconsider the decision to include it on the album.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.xxlmag.com/news/2014/01/kid-ink-almost-included-pharrell-on-his-album/2/ |title=Kid Ink Almost Included Pharrell On His Album - Page 2 of 3 - XXL |publisher=Xxlmag.com |date=2013-01-09 |access-date=2014-01-16}}</ref> Following the release of the track list for ''[[My Own Lane]]'', it was confirmed that the song would be featured on Ink's second studio album after all.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://theboombox.com/kid-ink-my-own-lane-tracklist/ |title=Kid Ink Reveals 'My Own Lane' Album Tracklist |publisher=Theboombox.com |date=2013-12-13 |access-date=2014-01-16}}</ref> However, Kid Ink made slight adjustments to the album version of the song.<ref>{{cite web|author=Emmanuel C.M. |url=http://www.xxlmag.com/news/2014/01/kid-ink-was-surprised-how-big-show-me-was/ |title=Kid Ink Says He Smokes An Ounce A Day - XXL |publisher=Xxlmag.com |date=2014-01-14 |access-date=2014-02-12}}</ref> The Alumni Music Group, 88 Classic and [[RCA Records]] serviced "Main Chick" to [[rhythmic contemporary]] radio in the United States on March 11, 2014,<ref name="RCUSrelease">{{cite web|title=Rhythmic Future Releases|url=http://www.allaccess.com/top40-rhythmic/future-releases |publisher=All Access Music Group |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140628004841/http://www.allaccess.com/top40-rhythmic/future-releases|url-status=live|archive-date=2014-06-28}}</ref> and to [[urban contemporary]] radio in the US on March 25, 2014.<ref name="UCUSrelease">{{cite web|title=Urban/UAC Future Releases|url=http://www.allaccess.com/urban/future-releases  |publisher=All Access Music Group |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140304123847/http://www.allaccess.com/urban/future-releases|url-status=live|archive-date=2014-03-04}}</ref>

== Critical reception ==
"Main Chick" was met with generally positive reviews from [[music critic]]s. Edwin Ortiz of [[HipHopDX]] said, the song displays "early and often Ink's penchant for tailoring melodies for the masses."<ref>{{cite web|last=Ortiz |first=Edwin |url=http://www.hiphopdx.com/index/album-reviews/id.2212/title.kid-ink-my-own-lane |title=Kid Ink - My Own Lane &#124; Read Hip Hop Reviews, Rap Reviews & Hip Hop Album Reviews |date=14 January 2014 |publisher=HipHop DX |access-date=2014-01-16}}</ref> Sheldon Pearce of ''[[XXL (magazine)|XXL]]'' called it a certified hit in any time zone.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.xxlmag.com/rap-music/reviews/2014/01/kid-ink-my-own-lane-album-review/ |title=Kid Ink Is Unpredictable And Entertaining On 'My Own Lane' - XXL |publisher=Xxlmag.com |date=2014-01-07 |access-date=2014-01-16}}</ref> Grant Jones of RapReviews praised the song's production and called it one of the album's best songs.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.rapreviews.com/archive/2014_01_myownlane.html |title=Kid Ink :: My Own Lane :: RCA Records |publisher=Rapreviews.com |date=2014-01-21 |access-date=2014-02-12}}</ref> David Jeffries of [[AllMusic]] called it a light, airy and attractive track.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.allmusic.com/album/my-own-lane-mw0002599704|title=My Own Lane - Kid Ink &#124; Songs, Reviews, Credits &#124; AllMusic|website=[[AllMusic]]}}</ref> Brent Faulkner of Starpulse said, the song was good, but not elite.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.starpulse.com/news/Brent_Faulkner/2014/01/10/review_kid_ink_my_own_lane |title=Review: Kid Ink, My Own Lane |publisher=Starpulse.com |access-date=2014-01-16 |archive-date=2014-01-23 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140123072023/http://www.starpulse.com/news/Brent_Faulkner/2014/01/10/review_kid_ink_my_own_lane |url-status=dead }}</ref>

== Live performances ==
On May 6, 2014, Kid Ink performed "Main Chick" and "Show Me" on ''[[Jimmy Kimmel Live!]]'' with [[Travis Barker]] and [[Eric Bellinger]] substituting for Chris Brown.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.xxlmag.com/news/2014/05/kid-ink-main-chick-jimmy-kimmel-live/|title=Kid Ink Performs "Main Chick" and "Show Me" on 'Jimmy Kimmel Live' - XXL|date=7 May 2014 }}</ref>

== Remixes ==
The official remix of the song features [[Chris Brown]], [[French Montana]], [[Yo Gotti]], [[Tyga]], [[Lil Bibby]] and a new verse by Kid Ink. It was released on July 7, 2014.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://soundcloud.com/kidinkofficial/kid-ink-main-chick-remix-feat-chris-brown-tyga-french-montana-yo-gotti-lil-bibby|title = Main Chick (Feat. Chris Brown, French Montana, Yo Gotti, Tyga & Lil Bibby)}}</ref>

Rapper Tyga, a frequent collaborator of Kid Ink, released a remix to the song on January 30, 2014.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.complex.com/music/2014/01/tyga-kid-ink-chris-brown-main-chick-remix |title=Kid Ink f/ Tyga & Chris Brown "Main Chick" |publisher=Complex |date=2014-01-30 |access-date=2014-02-12}}</ref> On February 10, 2014, comedian [[Kevin Hart]] requested LL Cool J's "[[I Need Love]]" on [[DJ Whoo Kid]]'s radio show. Whoo Kidd then dared LL Cool J to send him a verse ASAP if he could. Thirty to forty minutes later, LL Cool J sent him the verse over Kid Ink's "Main Chick".<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.complex.com/music/2014/02/kid-ink-ll-cool-j-main-chick-remix-chris-brown-tyga |title=Kid Ink LL Cool J Chris Brown Tyga "Main Chick" Remix |publisher=Complex |access-date=2014-02-12}}</ref>
