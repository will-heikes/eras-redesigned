"'''The Matrimony'''" is a song by American [[Hip hop music|hip hop]] recording artist [[Wale (rapper)|Wale]]. It was released on March 2, 2015, as the second single from his fourth studio album ''[[The Album About Nothing]]'' (2015). The song, [[hip hop production|produced]] by [[Jake One]], features a guest appearance from [[Usher (entertainer)|Usher]]. The song begins with a monologue from [[Jerry Seinfeld]]. The song samples artist Daniel "Danny Keyz" Tannenbaum.

==Chart performance==
"The Matrimony" peaked at number 70 on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] chart. On July 10, 2018, the song was certified [[RIAA Certification|double platinum]] by the [[Recording Industry Association of America]] (RIAA) for sales of over two million digital copies in the United States.<ref name="RIAA"/>
