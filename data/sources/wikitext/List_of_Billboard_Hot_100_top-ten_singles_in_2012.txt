This is a list of singles that charted in the top ten of the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]], an all-genre singles chart, in 2012.

Sixty-two singles charted in the top ten during the year. Fifty acts scored a top-ten hit, with seventeen achieving their first hits as lead or featured artists. Rapper [[Nicki Minaj]] garnered five top-ten singles during the year, the most of any artist, while [[Gotye]]'s "[[Somebody That I Used to Know]]" was the longest-running top-ten single of the year, spending twenty-four weeks in the tier.

Following her death on February 11, 2012, singer [[Whitney Houston]] placed three re-entries in the top fifty of the Hot 100 on the week ending February 25, 2012, led by "[[I Will Always Love You (Whitney Houston song)|I Will Always Love You]]" at number seven.<ref name="feb252012">{{Cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/505143/whitney-houston-returns-to-hot-100s-top-10-with-i-will-always-love-you|magazine=Billboard|title=Whitney Houston Returns to Hot 100's Top 10|publisher=Prometheus Global Media|first=Gary|last=Trust|date=2012-02-15|accessdate=2012-02-15}}</ref> Such re-entries of catalog songs are rare as [[Billboard (magazine)|''Billboard'']] maintains a policy deeming songs ineligible to chart due to moving to the recurrent list after twenty weeks if ranking below number fifty.<ref name="feb252012" /> After Houston's death, however, an adjustment in this policy allowed catalog songs to re-enter the chart's upper half if sales and airplay activity merit inclusion, as was the case with "I Will Always Love You", which surged after the singer's death in digital sales by 6,723% and in airplay by 915% from the previous week.<ref name="feb252012" />

[[Phillip Phillips]]'s "[[Home (Phillip Phillips song)|Home]]" became the first song to enter the top ten in separate chart runs in a single calendar year when it re-entered on the weeks ending August 18, 2012 and December 8, 2012 .<ref name="askbbaug182012">{{Cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/chartbeat/480451/ask-billboard-phillip-phillips-goes-home-to-hot-100s-top-10|magazine=Billboard|title=Ask Billboard: Phillip Phillips Goes 'Home' To Hot 100's Top 10|publisher=Prometheus Global Media|first=Gary|last=Trust|date=2012-08-14|accessdate=2012-08-14}}</ref> The song debuted at number ten on the week ending June 9, 2012 and spent a sole week in the top ten before falling off the chart three weeks later.<ref name="askbbaug182012" />
