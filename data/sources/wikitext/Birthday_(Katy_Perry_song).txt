"'''Birthday'''" is a song by American singer [[Katy Perry]] from her fourth studio album, ''[[Prism (Katy Perry album)|Prism]]'' (2013). She co-wrote the song with [[Bonnie McKee]] and its producers [[Dr. Luke]], [[Max Martin]], and [[Cirkut (record producer)|Cirkut]]. The song was recorded in Studios across [[California]], alongside [[MXM Studios]], based in [[Stockholm, Sweden]]. Inspired by [[Mariah Carey]]'s first album, critics compared "Birthday" to the work of other musicians like [[Prince (musician)|Prince]] and [[Daft Punk]]. Through double entendres in the lyrics of "Birthday", Perry makes sexual references while celebrating a partner's birthday. [[Capitol Records]] sent the track to [[Contemporary hit radio|mainstream]] and [[Rhythmic contemporary|rhythmic]] radio on April 21, 2014 as the album's fourth single. 

Following the release of ''Prism'', the song entered the single charts of [[South Korea]] and [[France]]. After being released as an official single, it reached number one on the US ''Billboard'' [[Dance Club Songs]] chart and the Israeli TV Airplay chart, number three in South Africa, number seven in Canada, number 10 in South Korea, number 17 on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] chart, and the top 40 in nine additional countries. It would later be certified 3× Platinum by [[Australian Recording Industry Association]] (ARIA), 2× Platinum in [[Music Canada]] (MC), and Platinum in New Zealand and the United States.

A [[music video]] for the track was released on April 24, 2014. Primarily recorded with hidden cameras, it features Perry disguised as five different characters in birthday parties and other celebrations. The makeup effects for Perry were designed and created by [[Tony Gardner (designer)|Tony Gardner]]. Perry's portrayal of a Jewish character in the video sparked criticism. The song was also performed at Perry's [[Prismatic World Tour]]. [[Jess Glynne]] covered the song on [[BBC Radio 1]]'s [[Live Lounge]], as did [[Halsey (singer)|Halsey]] on [[SiriusXM]].<ref>{{cite web|title=Jess Glynne cover's Katy Perry's Birthday in the Live Lounge|url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/music/showcase/clips/p022ftsj|work=BBC|access-date=September 12, 2014|date=July 8, 2014}}</ref>

==Production and release==
[[File:BonnieMcKeePerforming.png|thumb|right|[[Bonnie McKee]] (pictured) co-wrote "Birthday".]]
"Birthday" was written by Perry, [[Bonnie McKee]], [[Cirkut (record producer)|Cirkut]], [[Dr. Luke]], and [[Max Martin]]. The latter three produced the song and contributed to the instrumentation and programmed their respective instruments. The drums were played by [[Steven Wolf]], while the horns were played by the Saturday Night Live Band, arranged by [[Lenny Pickett]] and engineered by Dave O'Donnell. Its audio engineering was done by Peter Carlsson, Clint Gibbs, Sam Holland, and Michael Illbert. The track was finally mixed by Serban Ghenea at the MixStar Studios in [[Virginia Beach, Virginia]], accompanied by the mixing engineer, John Hanes. The song itself was recorded at various studios, including Luke's in the Boo in [[Malibu, California]], Conway Recording Studios in [[Hollywood, California]], Playback Recording Studio in [[Santa Barbara, California]], [[MXM Studios]] in [[Stockholm]], Sweden and Secret Garden Studios in [[Montecito, California]].<ref name="Notes" />

The song leaked online on October 16, 2013, two days prior to the official release of ''[[Prism (Katy Perry album)|Prism]]''.<ref>{{cite magazine|author=Lipshutz, Jason |title=Katy Perry's Vibrant 'Birthday' Song Leaks Online |url=https://www.billboard.com/music/music-news/katy-perrys-vibrant-birthday-song-leaks-online-5755778/ |magazine=Billboard |access-date=December 7, 2013 |date=October 16, 2013}}</ref> On April 3, 2014, the singer announced on her [[Twitter]] account that "Birthday" would be released as the album's fourth single, posting its cover art as well. The art is based on a photograph, taken during a birthday party circa 1990, in which a blonde Perry and her sister Angela Hudson smile toward the camera; "puffy" letter balloons were "[[Adobe Photoshop|photoshopped]]" onto the cover to spell out Perry's name. The song title appears in candles over a floral cake.<ref name="mtv">{{cite news|publisher=[[MTV News]]|access-date=April 4, 2014|date=April 3, 2014|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1725395/katy-perry-birthday-single-art.jhtml|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140405031711/http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1725395/katy-perry-birthday-single-art.jhtml|url-status=dead|archive-date=April 5, 2014|title=Katy Perry Reveals 'Birthday' Single Art With Serious Little Kid Attitude|last=Lindner|first=Emilee}}</ref><ref name="capital">{{cite web|publisher=[[Capital (radio network)|Capital]]. It depicts a variety of cakes and sweets—among other confections—decorated with the song's lyrics and concludes with Perry lighting the last candle of a cake.<ref>{{cite news|last=Ehrlich|first=Brenna|title=Katy Perry's 'Birthday' Lyric Video Will Put You in a Sugar Coma|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1725859/katy-perry-birthday-lyric-video.jhtml|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140411221832/http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1725859/katy-perry-birthday-lyric-video.jhtml|url-status=dead|archive-date=April 11, 2014|publisher=MTV News|access-date=April 10, 2014|date=April 10, 2014}}</ref>

==Composition==

[[File:MariahGMA cropped.jpg|thumb|left|[[Mariah Carey]] (''pictured'') inspired "Birthday".]]
At the length of three minutes and thirty-five seconds (3:35),<ref name="iTunes">{{cite web |url=https://music.apple.com/us/album/prism-deluxe-version/690928033 |title=Katy Perry — PRISM |publisher=[[iTunes Store]]. [[Apple Inc]].|date=September 30, 2013|access-date=September 30, 2013}}</ref> "Birthday" is primarily styled in the genre of [[disco]].<ref name="LA">{{cite web |url=http://www.latimes.com/entertainment/music/posts/la-et-ms-katy-perry-prism-review-20131022,0,6156766.story |title=Review: Hits pack Katy Perry's 'Prism' |first=Randall |last=Roberts |work=[[Los Angeles Times]]  |date=October 22, 2013 |access-date=November 25, 2013 }}</ref> According to the sheet music published at Musicnotes.com by [[Kobalt Music Group|Kobalt Music Publishing America, Inc.]], "Birthday" has a tempo of 126 beats per minute. Written in the key of [[B major]], it follows a progression of E<small>maj9</small>–C{{music|sharp}}m<small>7</small>–E<small>maj9</small>–C{{music|sharp}}m<small>7</small>–B. Perry's vocals range from B<sub>3</sub> to F{{music|sharp}}<sub>5</sub>.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.musicnotes.com/sheetmusic/mtdFPE.asp?ppn=MN0128825|publisher=Musicnotes|access-date=April 25, 2014|title=Katy Perry "Birthday" Sheet Music in B Major|date=January 7, 2014}}</ref>
During a preview event for ''[[Prism (Katy Perry album)|Prism]]'', held in [[New York City]], Perry described "Birthday" as her "attempt at writing" a song that American singer [[Mariah Carey]] would have included in her [[Mariah Carey (album)|eponymous debut album]].<ref name="BB2">{{cite magazine|last=Lipshutz|first=Jason|title=Katy Perry's 'Prism' Album Preview: 10 Things You Need To Know|url=https://www.billboard.com/music/music-news/katy-perrys-prism-album-preview-10-things-you-need-to-know-5687099/|magazine=Billboard|date=September 6, 2013| access-date=October 27, 2013}}</ref> Since its release, its musical composition has been strongly compared to that of other works of [[Prince (musician)|Prince]] and duo [[Wendy and Lisa]].<ref name="USA">{{cite web |url=https://www.usatoday.com/story/life/music/2013/09/13/katy-perry-previews-prism-album-in-los-angeles/2812283/ |title=Katy Perry sheds light on 'Prism' at L.A. preview event |first=Edna |last=Gunderson |work=[[USA Today]]  |date=September 13, 2013 |access-date=November 25, 2013 }}</ref><ref name="spin">{{cite web |last=Harvilla |first=Rob |title=Katy Perry Grows Up and Bums Us Out on Her Shrewd, Tepid 'Prism' |url=https://www.spin.com/2013/10/katy-perry-prism-capitol/ |work=[[Spin (magazine)|Spin]]  |access-date=December 8, 2013 |date=October 22, 2013}}</ref>

"Birthday" contains elements of funk-pop, [[synthpop]], and [[disco house]].<ref name="USA" /><ref name="spin" /> The song is backed by a "busy but buoyant" instrumentation featuring a "deeply rhythmic impulse".<ref name="BB2" /> Randall Roberts of the ''[[Los Angeles Times]]'' felt "Birthday" was a musically updated version of disco, achieved by replacing "cheesy [[Strings (music)|strings]]" with "jerky breaks and synth washes".<ref name="LA" /> Its "joyful"<ref name="BB2" /> lyrics present the commemoration of her partner's birthday as a metaphor for [[sexual intercourse]].<ref name="billboard2">{{cite magazine |author=Lipshutz, Jason |title=Katy Perry's 'PRISM': Track-By-Track Review |url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/review/5763162/katy-perrys-prism-track-by-track-review |magazine=Billboard |access-date=December 8, 2013 |date=October 21, 2013}}</ref> The bridge section of "Birthday", which sees Perry singing "Let me get you in your birthday suit / It's time to bring out the big balloons", was found by Ben Rattliff to resemble French duo [[Daft Punk]]'s works.<ref>{{cite news|last=Rattliff|first=Ben |title=This Bubble Gum, Too, Shall Pop |url=https://www.nytimes.com/2013/10/20/arts/music/this-bubble-gum-too-shall-pop.html |newspaper=[[The New York Times]]|access-date=December 8, 2013 |date=October 18, 2013}}</ref>{{Clear}}

==Chart performance==
Upon the release of ''Prism'', due to strong digital download sales, "Birthday" charted on the singles charts in [[France]] and [[South Korea]]. It debuted on the [[Syndicat National de l'Édition Phonographique]] singles chart at number 160 where it stayed for one week.<ref name="fra"/> In South Korea, it peaked at number 51 on the [[Gaon Chart|Gaon International Chart]] with digital downloads of 3,089 copies.<ref name="South Korea">{{cite web|url=http://gaonchart.co.kr/digital_chart/download.php?nationGbn=E&current_week=44&current_year=2013&chart_Time=week|title=South Korea Gaon International Chart|publisher=[[Gaon Chart]]|access-date=October 3, 2013|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131103073904/http://gaonchart.co.kr/digital_chart/download.php?nationGbn=E&current_week=44&current_year=2013&chart_Time=week|archive-date=November 3, 2013}}</ref>

Before being released as a single, the song debuted on the ''Billboard'' Hot 100 chart of April 16, 2014, at number 91.<ref name=BillboardDebut>{{cite magazine |url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/6055524/pharrell-williams-tops-hot-100-ed-sheeran-blasts-in-at-no-15|title=Pharrell Williams Tops Hot 100; Ed Sheeran Blasts In at No. 15|access-date=April 16, 2014|date=April 16, 2014|first=Gary|last=Trust|magazine=Billboard}}</ref> One week later, the song jumped to number 83. The following week, it went from 83 to 37.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=http://billboard.com/articles/news/6070358/pharrell-williams-tops-hot-100-chart-10-weeks-iggy-azalea-top-10|magazine=Billboard|date=April 30, 2014|access-date=April 30, 2014|title=Pharrell Williams Tops Hot 100 For 10th Week, Iggy Azalea Vaults Into Top 10|first=Gary|last=Trust}}</ref> It peaked at 17 on the Hot 100 becoming her 15th entry to reach the top 20 of the chart.<ref name=UShot100/> The song has peaked at the top of [[Hot Dance Club Songs]] chart extending her record for most consecutive dance club no.1 songs to 14. The song has sold more than 1,000,000 copies in the United States; consequently it was certified Platinum by the [[Recording Industry Association of America]] (RIAA).<ref name=RIAA/>

===Synopsis===
[[File:Katy Perry as Yosef in Birthday video.png|thumb|upright|Perry as Yosef Shulem, an alter-ego that was criticized by music critics.]]
The music video begins with short introductions to Perry's alter-egos Goldie, Yosef, Kriss, Ace, and Mandee. Perry is then shown entering all of the parties as "five of the world’s worst birthday entertainers" in disguise.<ref name="inpe"/> At a man's 90th birthday, Goldie emerges from a large birthday cake and begins to give the man a lapdance. At a child's birthday party, Kriss the Clown attempts to create balloon animals, while Ace the Animal Trainer introduces a group of children to a box of mice before appearing to eat one, which makes a child cry. At another child's party, Princess Mandee paints a girl's face incompetently; Goldie begins to suffer from respiratory problems, and Yosef does [[The worm (dance move)|the worm]] at a boy's [[Bar and Bat Mitzvah|Bar Mitzvah]]. Goldie pulls a man's prosthetic leg off and proceeds to play air guitar with it, and Kriss crashes into a table before drinking alcohol behind a tree.

At the elderly man's party, Goldie collapses and a woman tries to use a [[Defibrillation|defibrillator]] on her. While attempting to hit a [[piñata]], Kriss walks onto the road to cause a car crash which shocks the party attendees. At the petting cage, Ace lets a goat run wild which defecates and urinates on the ground; meanwhile Goldie drops a birthday cake on the 90-year-old man, Yosef is then shown beatboxing at the Bar Mitzvah. Princess Mandee takes off her hat, wig and mask and reveals herself to be Perry, to which the children scream in pleasure. Goldie eats cake with the elderly man, before straddling him on his wheelchair. At the end of the song, Princess Mandee and the children say "happy birthday" to the camera. During the credits, Perry's five alter-egos dance and lip-sync to a remixed version of the song.

===Reception===

==Live performances==
"Birthday" was performed as the first encore at the [[Prismatic World Tour]], Perry's third concert tour. The performance of the song at the Newcastle upon Tyne date was broadcast live during [[2014 Billboard Music Awards|the 2014 Billboard Music Awards]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Lipshutz |first=Jason |date=May 18, 2014 |title=Katy Perry Throws 'Birthday' Bash at 2014 Billboard Music Awards |url=https://www.billboard.com/articles/events/bbma-2014/6092038/katy-perry-birthday-performance-2014-billboard-music-awards-prism |website=Billboard.com}}</ref> On May 25, 2014, Perry performed the song at [[BBC Radio 1's Big Weekend]] in Glasgow.<ref>{{Cite web |title=2014 |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/events/ep3g9r |access-date=2023-06-12 |website=BBC Music Events |language=en}}</ref>
