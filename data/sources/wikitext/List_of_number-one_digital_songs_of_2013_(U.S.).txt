{{Short description|2013 highest-selling digital singles in the United States}}

{{Use mdy dates|date=May 2015}}
The highest-selling [[music download|digital singles]] in the United States are ranked in the [[Hot Digital Songs]] chart, published by ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' magazine. The data are compiled by [[Nielsen SoundScan]] based on each single's weekly digital sales, which combines sales of different versions of a single for a summarized figure.

==Chart history==
[[File:Katy Perry at the 2011 Logie Awards.jpg|thumb|upright| [[Katy Perry]]'s "[[Roar (song)|Roar]]" had the second highest debut for 2013 with 557,000 downloads.]]
[[File:YouTube Presents Taylor Swift.jpg|thumb|right|[[Taylor Swift]]'s "[[I Knew You Were Trouble]]" posted the largest weekly download total in 2013, selling 582,000 downloads in the week of January 12 and had the highest debut for 2013.]]
[[File:Robin Thicke performing.jpg|thumb|right|[[Robin Thicke]]'s "[[Blurred Lines]]" tied with [[Macklemore]] & [[Ryan Lewis]]'s "[[Thrift Shop (song)|Thrift Shop]]" for the longest command on the chart in 2013. The two songs topped the chart for 10 weeks each.]]
