'''''Teenage Dream: The Complete Confection''''' is a [[reissue]] of American singer-songwriter [[Katy Perry]]'s third studio album, ''[[Teenage Dream]]'' (2010). It was released on March 23, 2012, by [[Capitol Records]], nearly two years after the original album. Perry collaborated with producers including [[Tricky Stewart]] to refine leftover material from the recording sessions at Playback Recording Studio for ''Teenage Dream''. The final product features three newly recorded songs, which incorporate [[Pop music|pop]] styles previously seen in the original album, an acoustic version of "[[The One That Got Away (Katy Perry song)|The One That Got Away]]" and three additional official remixes featuring [[Missy Elliott]], [[Kanye West]], and [[Tommie Sunshine]].

Two singles were released from ''Teenage Dream: The Complete Confection''. The lead single, "[[Part of Me (Katy Perry song)|Part of Me]]", debuted at number one on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]], while the second single, "[[Wide Awake (Katy Perry song)|Wide Awake]]", peaked at number two in the country. Both songs were eventually certified [[RIAA certification|quintuple-platinum]] by the [[Recording Industry Association of America]] (RIAA). The Kanye West remix of "[[E.T. (song)|E.T.]]" was previously released as a single and peaked at the top of the US ''Billboard'' Hot 100. The record was further promoted with live performances during the [[54th Annual Grammy Awards]] and the [[2012 Billboard Music Awards|2012 ''Billboard'' Music Awards]], in addition to the autobiographical documentary film ''[[Katy Perry: Part of Me]]'' (2012).

Upon its release, ''Teenage Dream: The Complete Confection'' was met with generally favorable reviews from music critics, who were ambivalent towards the new songs' production but questioned the decision to reissue ''Teenage Dream''. As an independent release, the reissue charted within the top 10 in Australia, New Zealand, the United Kingdom, and the United States as well as the top 20 in Belgium (Flanders), Finland, and France. It sold one million copies globally by the end of 2012 and received multi-Platinum certifications in Australia, Denmark, and New Zealand.

In August 2010, Perry released her third studio album ''[[Teenage Dream]]''.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://itunes.apple.com/us/album/teenage-dream/id385406210|title=iTunes - Music - Teenage Dream by Katy Perry|publisher=[[iTunes Store]] (US). {{cite AV media notes|title=[[Teenage Dream]]|others=[[Katy Perry]]|year=2010|publisher=[[Capitol Records]]}}</ref> Following its release, ''Teenage Dream'' became a worldwide commercial success; it debuted at number one on both the U.S. [[Billboard 200|''Billboard'' 200]]<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.usatoday.com/story/life/music/2013/10/21/katy-perry-prism-album-holds-nothing-but-hits/3090225/|title=Katy Perry's 'Prism' alights with 'nothing but hits'|author=Edna Gunderson|newspaper=[[USA Today]]|publisher=[[Gannett Company]]|date=October 21, 2013|access-date=October 25, 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131024055109/http://www.usatoday.com/story/life/music/2013/10/21/katy-perry-prism-album-holds-nothing-but-hits/3090225/|archive-date=October 24, 2013|url-status=live}}</ref> and the [[UK Albums Chart]],<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/katy-perrys-teenage-dream-breaks-1-million-sales-barrier-1331/|title=Katy Perry's Teenage Dream breaks 1 million sales barrier|publisher=[[Official Charts Company]]|date=October 4, 2012|access-date=October 25, 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130313143244/http://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/katy-perrys-teenage-dream-breaks-1-million-sales-barrier-1331/|archive-date=March 13, 2013|url-status=live}}</ref> and charted highly in other international territories.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.ultratop.be/nl/album/203fc/Katy-Perry-Teenage-Dream|title=Katy Perry - Teenage Dream|publisher=[[Ultratop]]. (song)|E.T.]]", and "[[Last Friday Night (T.G.I.F.)]]" each reached the top of the U.S. [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]], ''Teenage Dream'' became the second album in history to produce five number-one singles on the chart after [[Michael Jackson]]'s ''[[Bad (album)|Bad]]''.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/467879/katy-perry-makes-hot-100-history-ties-michael-jacksons-record|title=Katy Perry Makes Hot 100 History: Ties Michael Jackson's Record|author=Gary Trust|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|publisher=[[Prometheus Global Media]]|date=August 17, 2011|access-date=October 25, 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130330234333/http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/467879/katy-perry-makes-hot-100-history-ties-michael-jacksons-record|archive-date=March 30, 2013|url-status=live}}</ref>

In October 2011, producer [[Tricky Stewart]] confirmed that he was working with Perry to refine leftover material from ''Teenage Dream'' recording sessions for "something special she has going on".<ref name="FresheningUp">{{cite web|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/1671985/katy-perry-secret-project-album/|title=Katy Perry 'Freshening Up' Unreleased Songs|author=Jocelyn Vena|publisher=[[MTV News]]. "It was an incredible honor to tie [Jackson's] ''Billboard'' Hot 100 record, but I'm moving forward and had a few things left to get off my chest," she said.<ref name="Announcement"/>

==New material==
[[File:Katy Perry Part of Me premier.jpg|thumb|upright|Perry performing "[[Part of Me (Katy Perry song)|Part of Me]]" at the premiere of ''[[Katy Perry: Part of Me]]'']]
Tricky Stewart stated that he and Perry "always knew that the records [they] created were special [and] at the time it was more contractual obligation [that they did not make the album]". He elaborated that they were originally excluded from the project to balance the original record with tracks from other producers, in addition to Perry not needing more songs at the time.<ref name="FresheningUp"/> The expanded album begins with the twelve tracks included on the standard version of ''Teenage Dream''.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.katyperry.com/albums/teenage-dream-2/|title=Albums|publisher=KatyPerry.com|date=August 24, 2010|access-date=October 26, 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131103014557/http://www.katyperry.com/albums/teenage-dream-2/|archive-date=November 3, 2013|url-status=live}}</ref> The newly recorded material for the reissue, which is made just for the album, begins with an [[Acoustic music|acoustic]] rendition of "[[The One That Got Away (Katy Perry song)|The One That Got Away]]" as the thirteenth track.<ref name="TCCTracks">{{cite web|url=http://www.katyperry.com/albums/teenage-dream-complete-confection/|title=Albums|publisher=KatyPerry.com|date=March 23, 2012|access-date=October 26, 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131103202131/http://www.katyperry.com/albums/teenage-dream-complete-confection/|archive-date=November 3, 2013|url-status=live}}</ref> "[[Part of Me (Katy Perry song)|Part of Me]]" is a [[power pop]] song<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.foxnews.com/entertainment/writer-naomi-wolf-urges-boycott-of-katy-perry-video-calls-it-military-propaganda/|title=Writer Naomi Wolf urges boycott of Katy Perry video, calls it military propaganda|author=Hollie McKay|publisher=[[Fox News Channel]]. [[21st Century Fox]]|date=March 29, 2012|access-date=October 26, 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131224112913/http://www.foxnews.com/entertainment/2012/03/29/writer-naomi-wolf-urges-boycott-katy-perry-video-calls-it-military-propaganda/|archive-date=December 24, 2013|url-status=live}}</ref> that lyrically acts as an "emotional breakup anthem".<ref name="MTVPartOfMe">{{cite web|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/1681371/katy-perry-part-of-me-video-explained/|title=Katy Perry's 'Part Of Me': What's It All About?|author=John Mitchell|publisher=MTV News. Viacom|date=March 20, 2012|access-date=October 26, 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141117084727/http://www.mtv.com/news/1681371/katy-perry-part-of-me-video-explained/|archive-date=November 17, 2014|url-status=dead}}</ref> It was speculated that its concept was inspired by Perry's relationship with her ex-husband [[Russell Brand]].<ref name="MTVPartOfMe"/>

"[[Wide Awake (Katy Perry song)|Wide Awake]]" is a mid-tempo [[pop ballad]] that sees inspiration from [[Electronic music|electronic]] and [[dance-pop]]; it lyrically discusses the end of a relationship, and was also reportedly influenced by Perry's experience with Brand.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.rollingstone.com/music/songreviews/wide-awake-20120327|title=Wide Awake|author=Jody Rosen|magazine=[[Rolling Stone]]|publisher=[[Jann Wenner|Wenner Media, LLC]]|date=March 27, 2012|access-date=October 26, 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130314031729/http://www.rollingstone.com/music/songreviews/wide-awake-20120327|archive-date=March 14, 2013|url-status=dead}}</ref> "[[Dressin' Up]]" is an uptempo, [[dance-rock]] and [[techno]] song that sees the inclusion of "over-sexualized" lyrical content.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://live.hollywoodjesus.com/?p=9878|title=Teenage Dream: The Complete Confection|author=Nate Watts|publisher=Hollywood Jesus|date=March 28, 2012|access-date=October 26, 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130409224021/http://live.hollywoodjesus.com/?p=9878|archive-date=April 9, 2013|url-status=live}}</ref> The seventeenth track is a remix of "[[E.T. ==Commercial performance==
In the United States, ''Teenage Dream: The Complete Confection'' boosted sales of the original ''Teenage Dream'', which consequently re-entered the top-ten of the [[Billboard 200|''Billboard'' 200]] at number seven with first-week sales of 33,000 copies. This resulted in a 190%-increase from the previous tracking week, in which the original album stood at number 31.<ref name="USChart">{{cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/497208/shinedown-has-its-best-week-ever-on-madonna-led-billboard-200|title=Shinedown Has Its Best Week Ever on Madonna-led ''Billboard'' 200|last=Caulfield|first=Keith|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|date=April 4, 2012|access-date=October 25, 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130531001853/http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/497208/shinedown-has-its-best-week-ever-on-madonna-led-billboard-200|archive-date=May 31, 2013|url-status=live}}</ref> A similar situation occurred on the [[UK Albums Chart]], where ''The Complete Confection'' helped ''Teenage Dream'' rise from number 34 to number six after the former was released.<ref name="UKChart">{{cite web|url=https://www.officialcharts.com/charts/albums-chart/20120401/7502/|title=2012-04-07 Top 40 Official Albums Chart UK Archive|publisher=[[Official Charts Company]]|date=April 7, 2012|access-date=October 26, 2013}}</ref>

In Australia, the ''Complete Confection'' peaked at number five and spent a total of 233 weeks on the [[ARIA Albums Chart]].<ref name="australianchart"/> The record ranked on the ARIA year-end album charts, specifically from 2012 to 2014,<ref name=ARIA12/><ref name=ARIA13/><ref name=ARIA14/> then from 2020 to 2025.<ref name=ARIA20/><ref name=ARIA21/><ref name=ARIA22/><ref name=ARIA23/><ref name=ARIA24/><ref name=ARIA25/> In 2023, the album was certified seven-times Platinum by the [[Australian Recording Industry Association]] (ARIA) for sales exceeding 490,000 units.<ref name=ARIA/> Elsewhere, the ''Complete Confection charted'' within the top five in [[Official Aotearoa Music Charts|New Zealand]],<ref name=NZ/> while reaching the top 20 in [[Ultratop|Belgium (Flanders)]],<ref name=Flanders/> [[Official Finnish Charts|Finland]],<ref name="finnishchart"/> and [[SNEP|France]].<ref name="frenchchart"/> The album has been certified two-times Platinum in [[IFPI Danmark|Denmark]] and four-times Platinum in [[Recorded Music NZ|New Zealand]].<ref name=IFPIDen/><ref name=RMNZ/> According to the [[International Federation of the Phonographic Industry]] (IFPI), ''The Complete Confection'' was the 50th highest-selling album of 2012 with one million copies sold.<ref name=wwsales/>
