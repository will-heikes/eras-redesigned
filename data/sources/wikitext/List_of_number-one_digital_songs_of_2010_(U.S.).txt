{{Short description|2010 highest-selling digital singles in the United States}}

The highest-selling [[music download|digital single]]s in the United States are ranked in the [[Hot Digital Songs]] chart, published by ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' magazine. The data are compiled by [[Nielsen SoundScan]] based on each single's weekly digital sales, which combines sales of different versions of a single for a summarized figure.
