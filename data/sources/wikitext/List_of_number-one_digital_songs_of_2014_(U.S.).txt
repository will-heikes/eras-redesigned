{{Short description|2014 highest-selling digital singles in the United States}}

The highest-selling [[music download|digital singles]] in the United States are ranked in the [[Hot Digital Songs]] chart, published by ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' magazine. The data are compiled by [[Nielsen SoundScan]] based on each single's weekly digital sales, which combines sales of different versions of a single for a summarized figure.

==Chart history==
[[File:N.E.R.D @ Pori Jazz 2010 - Pharrell Williams 1.jpg|thumb|upright|[[Pharrell Williams]]' "[[Happy (Pharrell Williams song)|Happy]]" spent the longest amount of time atop Digital Songs of any song in 2014, reigning the chart for 11 weeks.]]
[[File:Taylor Swift 084 (18118732900).jpg|thumb|upright|[[Taylor Swift]]'s "[[Shake It Off]]" had the highest digital sales week of 2014, when it debuted with 544,000 downloads sold.]]
