Barbadian singer [[Rihanna]] has released 53 [[single (music)|singles]] as lead artist, 17 singles as a featured artist, 2 [[Charity record|charity singles]], and 5 [[promotional recording#Single|promotional singles]]. Rihanna topped the ''Billboard'' Hot 100 with three singles as a featured artist: "[[Live Your Life (T.I. song)|Live Your Life]]" with [[T.I.]], and "[[Love the Way You Lie]]" and "[[The Monster (song)|The Monster]]" with [[Eminem]].<ref name="billboard number ones"/> Her string of number-one singles marked [[Billboard Hot 100 chart achievements and milestones|milestones]]. With "S&M" topping the chart in April 2011, she registered the shortest time frame to have ten number-one singles (from "SOS" in May 2006) and became the youngest artist (at 23 years) to have ten number-one songs.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/articles/news/471954/rihannas-sm-reigns-on-hot-100-lady-gagas-judas-debuts|title=Rihanna's 'S&M' Reigns on Hot 100, Lady Gaga's 'Judas' Debuts|first=Gary|last=Trust|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|date=April 20, 2011|access-date=September 16, 2021|archive-date=April 16, 2011|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110416044749/http://www.billboard.com/|url-status=live}}</ref> With "We Found Love" reaching the top ten in October 2011, Rihanna scored the fastest time span to score 20 US top-ten singles (from "Pon de Replay" in June 2005).<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/articles/news/466768/rihanna-becomes-fastest-solo-artist-to-20-hot-100-top-10s|title=Rihanna Becomes Fastest Solo Artist To 20 Hot 100 Top 10s|first=Gary|last=Trust|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|date=October 5, 2011|access-date=September 16, 2021|archive-date=August 25, 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180825094238/https://www.billboard.com/articles/news/466768/rihanna-becomes-fastest-solo-artist-to-20-hot-100-top-10s|url-status=live}}</ref> In the United Kingdom, she is the first female solo artist to have number-one singles in five consecutive years, with "Umbrella", "Take a Bow", "[[Run This Town]]" (as a featured artist on [[Jay-Z]]'s single), "Only Girl (in the World)", and "What's My Name?", from 2007 to 2011.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.bbc.com/news/entertainment-arts-12145918|title=Rihanna makes history in UK chart|date=January 9, 2011|publisher=[[BBC News]]|access-date=September 16, 2021|archive-date=September 28, 2020|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200928091446/https://www.bbc.com/news/entertainment-arts-12145918|url-status=live}}</ref>
