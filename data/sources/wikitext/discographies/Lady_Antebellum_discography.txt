[[Lady A]] (formerly known as Lady Antebellum<ref name="namechange">{{cite web |last1=Willman |first1=Chris |title=Country Group Lady Antebellum Drops 'Antebellum' from Name |url=https://variety.com/2020/music/news/lady-antebellum-drops-antebellum-from-name-lady-a-1234631601/ |website=Variety |date=11 June 2020}}</ref>) are an American [[country music]] group composed of [[Hillary Scott]], [[Charles Kelley]] and [[Dave Haywood]]. They have released nine [[studio album]]s (which includes one Christmas album), two [[extended play]]s, two [[box set]]s, and 23 [[Single (music)|singles]], not counting guest appearances or digital-only releases. The lead singers are Charles Kelly and Hillary Scott. All ten of their full-length releases have debuted in the top ten on the [[Top Country Albums]] chart, including five number-ones.<ref>{{cite magazine |last1=Asker |first1=Jim |title=Lady Antebellum Sails Onto Top Country Albums; Keith Urban, Dan + Shay & Justin Bieber Add Top 10s |url=https://www.billboard.com/articles/business/chart-beat/8544841/lady-antebellum-ocean-no-2-top-country-albums |access-date=January 14, 2020 |magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]] |date=November 26, 2019 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20191128014331/https://www.billboard.com/articles/business/chart-beat/8544841/lady-antebellum-ocean-no-2-top-country-albums |archive-date=November 28, 2019}}</ref> They have sold 10.2 million albums in the US as of February 2016.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Asker |first1=Jim |title=Granger Smith Takes 'Backroad' to No. 1; Charles Kelley Makes Solo Bow |url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/granger-smith-backroad-charles-kelley-solo-debut/ |access-date=January 14, 2020 |date=February 17, 2016 |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160218094934/https://www.billboard.com/articles/columns/chart-beat/6882045/granger-smith-backroad-charles-kelley-solo-debut |archive-date=February 18, 2016}}</ref>

The trio's [[Lady Antebellum (album)|debut album]] was released in 2008 via [[Capitol Records]] Nashville. It included the singles "[[Love Don't Live Here (Lady Antebellum song)|Love Don't Live Here]]", "[[Lookin' for a Good Time]]" and "[[I Run to You]]", with the latter becoming the group's first number one on the US ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' [[Hot Country Songs]] chart as well as the number one country music song of the year according to [[Billboard Year-End|''Billboard'' Year-End]] chart. It was followed by "[[Need You Now (Lady Antebellum song)|Need You Now]]", the title track to the group's [[Need You Now (Lady Antebellum album)|second album]]. This song reached number-one on the country, [[Adult Contemporary (chart)|Adult Contemporary]], and [[Adult Pop Songs]] charts, as well as number 2 on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] and [[Canadian Hot 100]]. After it came the number-one country hits "[[American Honey]]" and "[[Our Kind of Love]]", followed by the top 10 "[[Hello World (Lady Antebellum song)|Hello World]]".

The band's third studio album, ''[[Own the Night]]'', was released on September 13, 2011. Its first two singles, "[[Just a Kiss (song)|Just a Kiss]]" and "[[We Owned the Night]]", both reached number one. Their fifth studio album, ''[[Golden (Lady Antebellum album)|Golden]]'', was released on May 7, 2013.<ref name=Golden>{{cite web|last=Scott|first=Hillary|title=Golden comes out May 7!!!|url=https://twitter.com/HillaryScottLA/status/303872691381882880|access-date=February 19, 2013}}</ref> ''[[747 (album)|747]]'', their sixth studio album, was released on September 30, 2014.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/entry/view/id/94098|title=Lady Antebellum's '747' Album Will Take Off On...|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]] }}</ref>
