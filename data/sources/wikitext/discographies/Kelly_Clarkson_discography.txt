American singer-songwriter [[Kelly Clarkson]] has released ten [[Album|studio albums]], eight [[extended play]]s, one [[compilation album]], one [[remix album]] and 56 [[Single (music)|singles]] (including eight as a featured artist). In 2002, she won the [[American Idol season 1|inaugural season]] of the television competition ''[[American Idol]]'' and was immediately signed to a recording deal with [[19 Recordings]], and [[RCA Records]].{{efn-ua|Prior to the selection of the first ''[[American Idol]]'' winner in 2002, Clarkson signed a recording contract with [[19 Recordings]], which will reserve the phonographic rights to the master recordings commissioned throughout the indenture. 19 then entered into a "master agreement" with [[Simon Cowell]]'s [[S Records]] (trading as Ronagold Limited) to appoint its affiliate [[RCA Records]] the exclusive license to distribute these recordings. An amendment in 2005 extended S to participate as 19's partner for five years, after which the license were directly given to RCA for the rest of the deal's duration.<ref name="Trevor Clawson 1" /><ref name="19 v Sony 1" /><ref name="SEC 1" />}} She made her chart debut in September 2002 with the [[A-side and B-side|double A-side]] single "[[Before Your Love]]"/"[[A Moment Like This]]", latter of which topped the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] chart{{efn-ua|name=FN1|Following a revision of its methodology of gauging the ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' [[Billboard Hot 100|Hot 100]] chart in 1999, sales of singles with more than one current song will be given to the track which currently receives the most radio airplay. In the case of "Before Your Love"/"A Moment Like This", the latter received the most radio airplay detection and was credited on the ''Billboard'' Hot 100 chart, though "Before Your Love" was also deemed eligible to enter independently had it gained enough radio audience at the time.<ref name="Billboard 1" />}} and eventually became the year's best-selling single in the United States.<ref name="Billboard 2" /> Her debut album, ''[[Thankful (Kelly Clarkson album)|Thankful]]'', was released in April 2003 and entered the US [[Billboard 200|''Billboard'' 200]] chart at number one.<ref name="Billboard 3" /><ref name="Yahoo 1" /> ''Thankful''  produced the hit [[lead single]] "[[Miss Independent (Kelly Clarkson song)|Miss Independent]]" and was [[Music certification|certified]] [[RIAA certification|double-platinum]] by the [[Recording Industry Association of America]] (RIAA).<ref name="RIAA – Kelly Clarkson" />

Released in 2004, Clarkson's second studio album ''[[Breakaway (Kelly Clarkson album)|Breakaway]]'' expanded her audience to international markets and currently remains as her most successful album to date with sales of 12 million copies worldwide.{{sfn|Davis|2013|p=509|ref=Davis 2013}} Aided by the commercial successes of its worldwide hit singles "[[Since U Been Gone]]", "[[Behind These Hazel Eyes]]", "[[Because of You (Kelly Clarkson song)|Because of You]]", "[[Walk Away (Kelly Clarkson song)|Walk Away]]", and "[[Breakaway (Kelly Clarkson song)|Breakaway]]", ''Breakaway'' topped the [[Irish Albums Chart]] and the [[Dutch Album Top 100]] chart and became the world's seventh best-selling album of 2005, according to the [[International Federation of the Phonographic Industry]] (IFPI).<ref name="BBC 1" /> Clarkson released her third studio album ''[[My December]]'' in 2007 amidst a highly publicized dispute with music mogul [[Clive Davis]].{{sfn|Davis|2013|p=512|ref=Davis 2013}} Though its commercial performance paled in comparison to its predecessor's,<ref name="Billboard 5" /> it spawned the hit single "[[Never Again (Kelly Clarkson song)|Never Again]]" and was eventually certified platinum by the RIAA.{{sfn|Davis|2013|p=514|ref=Davis 2013}} In 2009, she released her fourth studio album ''[[All I Ever Wanted (album)|All I Ever Wanted]]'', which became her second number one entry on ''Billboard'' 200 chart.<ref name="Billboard 5" /> Its lead single "[[My Life Would Suck Without You]]" currently maintains the record of the [[List of Billboard Hot 100 chart achievements and milestones#Biggest jump to number one|biggest jump to number one]] in the history of the ''Billboard'' Hot 100 chart,<ref name="Billboard 6" /> and was followed by the hit singles "[[I Do Not Hook Up]]" and "[[Already Gone (Kelly Clarkson song)|Already Gone]]".<ref name="Davis1">{{harvp|Davis|2013|p=515|ref=Davis 2013}}</ref>

In 2011, Clarkson released her fifth studio album ''[[Stronger (Kelly Clarkson album)|Stronger]]'', accompanied by the hit singles "[[Mr. Know It All (Kelly Clarkson song)|Mr. Know It All]]" and "[[Stronger (What Doesn't Kill You)]]".<ref name="Davis1" /> ''Stronger'' was certified platinum by the RIAA, fueled by the success of "Stronger (What Doesn't Kill You)", which currently stands as her most successful single with number one peak positions on sixteen ''Billboard'' charts, including the ''Billboard'' Hot 100 chart.<ref name="Davis1" /><ref name="Hollywood Reporter 1" /> In 2012, Clarkson commemorated her tenth career anniversary with her first [[Greatest hits album|greatest hits compilation]], ''[[Greatest Hits – Chapter One (Kelly Clarkson album)|Greatest Hits – Chapter One]]''.<ref name="USA Today 1" /> Accompanied by its hit lead single "[[Catch My Breath]]", ''Chapter One'' was certified gold by the RIAA.<ref name="RIAA – Kelly Clarkson" /> Released in 2013, her sixth studio album and first Christmas record ''[[Wrapped in Red]]'' became that year's [[List of best-selling Christmas albums in the United States|best-selling holiday album in the United States]].<ref name="Yahoo 2" /> Its lead single "[[Underneath the Tree]]" recurrently charts as a popular Christmas hit song every holiday season since its release.<ref name="Quartz 1" />

Clarkson completed her recording contract with 19 and RCA with the release of her seventh studio album ''[[Piece by Piece (Kelly Clarkson album)|Piece by Piece]]'' in 2015.<ref name="Billboard 7" /> Certified gold by the RIAA, it became her third number one album on ''Billboard'' 200 chart and produced the hit singles "[[Heartbeat Song (Kelly Clarkson song)|Heartbeat Song]]" and "[[Piece by Piece (Kelly Clarkson song)|Piece by Piece]]", the latter of which celebrated her milestone 100th number one listing on the ''Billboard'' charts.<ref name="Fuse 1" /><ref name="Billboard 8" /> A year later, she signed a long-term recording contract with [[Atlantic Records]]. Her first record on the label and her eighth studio album ''[[Meaning of Life (album)|Meaning of Life]]'' was released in 2017 and produced the hit single "[[Love So Soft]]".<ref name="PR Newswire 1" /> This was followed by her second Christmas album, ''[[When Christmas Comes Around...]]'' in 2021, accompanied by the single "[[Christmas Isn't Canceled (Just You)]]".<ref name="Official Charts Company" /> In 2023, Clarkson released her tenth studio album, ''[[Chemistry (Kelly Clarkson album)|Chemistry]]'', which included the lead singles "[[Mine (Kelly Clarkson song)|Mine]]" and "[[Me (Kelly Clarkson song)|Me]]".<ref>{{cite magazine|last1=Gomez|first1=Patrick|title=Kelly Clarkson releases powerful post-divorce songs — and teases Chemistry song with Steve Martin|url=https://ew.com/music/kelly-clarkson-me-mine-new-songs-lyrics-chemistry-track-list-steve-martin/|magazine=Entertainment Weekly|date=April 14, 2023|access-date=April 22, 2023|archive-date=May 26, 2023|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230526225326/https://ew.com/music/kelly-clarkson-me-mine-new-songs-lyrics-chemistry-track-list-steve-martin/|url-status=live}}</ref> To date, Clarkson has sold over 35 million albums and 110 million singles worldwide<ref name="Brathwaite_9/19/2022">{{cite magazine | title=Kelly Clarkson's Walk of Fame induction was an American Idol reunion | magazine=[[Entertainment Weekly]]| date=September 19, 2022|
url=https://ew.com/celebrity/kelly-clarkson-walk-of-fame-american-idol-reunion-paula-abdul-simon-cowell-randy-jackson/ | access-date=December 24, 2024}}</ref> and has sold over 18.6 million [[album-equivalent units]] (including 14.6 million in traditional album sales),<ref name="Billboard Music Awards 2" /> 35 million digital tracks,<ref name="Billboard 4" /> 61 billion in cumulative radio audience, and 2.8 billion on-demand streams.<ref name="Billboard Music Awards 1" />
