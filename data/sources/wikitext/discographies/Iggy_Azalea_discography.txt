Australian rapper [[Iggy Azalea]] has released three [[studio album]]s, five [[extended play]]s (EPs), two [[mixtape]]s and seventeen [[single (music)|singles]] (including four as a featured artist). In September 2011, Azalea released her first mixtape, ''[[Ignorant Art]]''. Following the release, Azalea signed a recording contract with [[T.I.]]'s record label [[Grand Hustle Records|Grand Hustle]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/1678427/iggy-azalea-ti-debut-album/|title=T.I. Will Executive Produce Iggy Azalea's Debut Album|publisher=[[MTV News]]|first=Nadeska|last=Alexis|date=2 February 2012|access-date=17 July 2014|archive-date=25 July 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140725222511/http://www.mtv.com/news/1678427/iggy-azalea-ti-debut-album/|url-status=dead}}</ref> In July 2012, she released a free EP entitled ''[[Glory (EP)|Glory]]'', and in October of that year, Azalea released her second mixtape, ''[[TrapGold]]''.

In April 2014, Azalea released her debut studio album, ''[[The New Classic]]'', which peaked inside the top five on the record charts of countries including the United States, Australia and the United Kingdom. The first three [[single (music)|single]]s from the album, "[[Work (Iggy Azalea song)|Work]]", "[[Bounce (Iggy Azalea song)|Bounce]]" and "[[Change Your Life (Iggy Azalea song)|Change Your Life]]" experienced moderate success. The fourth single, "[[Fancy (Iggy Azalea song)|Fancy]]" featuring [[Charli XCX]], reached number one on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]], reigning for seven consecutive weeks, and peaked within the top five charts of Australia and the United Kingdom. The song was one of the best-selling singles worldwide in 2014.<ref>{{Cite web |last=IFPI |title=IFPI Digital Music Report 2015: Charting the Path to Sustainable Growth |url=https://www.musikindustrie.de/fileadmin/bvmi/upload/06_Publikationen/DMR/ifpi_digital-music-report-2015.pdf }}</ref> Azalea’s newfound success was followed with "[[Black Widow (Iggy Azalea song)|Black Widow]]" featuring [[Rita Ora]], the fifth single from ''The New Classic'', which became a top five hit in the US and the UK. During this time, Azalea was also featured on [[Ariana Grande]]'s single "[[Problem (Ariana Grande song)|Problem]]", which peaked at number two in the United States and, likewise to ”Fancy”, became one of the top-selling singles of 2014. In November 2014, Azalea released a reissue of her debut studio album titled ''[[Reclassified]]'', which included the singles "[[Beg for It (song)|Beg for It]]" and "[[Trouble (Iggy Azalea song)|Trouble]]". Azalea achieved three top ten hits simultaneously on the ''Billboard'' Hot 100 in 2014.<ref>{{cite magazine|last=Trust|first=Gary|title=Ariana Grande, Iggy Azalea Triple Up In Hot 100's Top 10, MAGIC! Still No. 1|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/6221978/hot-100-ariana-grande-iggy-azalea-top-10-magic|magazine=Billboard|date=20 August 2014|access-date=7 September 2014|archive-date=18 February 2017|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170218135539/http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/6221978/hot-100-ariana-grande-iggy-azalea-top-10-magic|url-status=live}}</ref> Her second album ''Digital Distortion'' was set to be released in the summer of 2016, but was shelved.<ref>{{cite web | url=http://www.rap-up.com/2015/10/07/iggy-azalea-announces-sophomore-album-digital-distortion/ | title=Iggy Azalea Announces Sophomore Album "Digital Distortion" | publisher=Rap-up.com | date=7 October 2015 | access-date=15 October 2015 | archive-date=10 October 2015 | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151010100442/http://www.rap-up.com/2015/10/07/iggy-azalea-announces-sophomore-album-digital-distortion/ | url-status=dead }}</ref> The album had been promoted with the singles "[[Team (Iggy Azalea song)|Team]]", "[[Mo Bounce]]", and "[[Switch (Iggy Azalea song)|Switch]]".

In late 2017, Azalea announced she had left Def Jam Recordings and would be releasing a new project titled ''Surviving <!-- STOP capitalising the "t" -->t<!-- Read [[MOS:CT]]. Definite articles are not capitalised; it's standard title case. -->he Summer'' under a new record label in the following year, without any of the material originally intended for ''Digital Distortion''.<ref>{{cite web | url=http://www.rap-up.com/2017/11/07/iggy-azalea-drops-4-new-songs/ | title=Iggy Azalea Drops 4 New Songs | publisher=Rap-up.com | date=7 November 2017 | access-date=26 November 2017 | archive-date=1 December 2017 | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20171201033008/http://www.rap-up.com/2017/11/07/iggy-azalea-drops-4-new-songs/ | url-status=dead }}</ref> Azalea then released a new single, "[[Savior (Iggy Azalea song)|Savior]]" featuring [[Quavo]], on 1 February through Island Records. In July 2018, it was announced that the EP ''[[Survive the Summer]]'' would be released on August 3, and feature the single "[[Kream (song)|Kream]]".<ref>{{cite web | url=http://www.rap-up.com/2018/07/05/new-music-iggy-azalea-tokyo-snow-trip-kream-tyga/ | title=Iggy Azalea Drops 'Tokyo Snow Trip' & 'Kream' With Tyga | publisher=Rap-up.com | date=5 July 2018 | access-date=13 July 2018 | archive-date=9 July 2018 | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180709230855/http://www.rap-up.com/2018/07/05/new-music-iggy-azalea-tokyo-snow-trip-kream-tyga/ | url-status=dead }}</ref> On March 1, 2019, she announced that "[[Sally Walker (song)|Sally Walker]]" would be the lead single for her second album, ''[[In My Defense]]'', which was released on July 19, 2019.

In the summer of 2020, Azalea announced her third studio album, ''[[The End of an Era (album)|The End of an Era]]''. On 20 August 2020, Azalea released the original lead single, "[[Dance Like Nobody's Watching (song)|Dance Like Nobody's Watching]]", a collaboration with [[Tinashe]].<ref>{{Cite magazine|title=Iggy Azalea and Tinashe Want You to 'Dance Like Nobody's Watching': Stream It Now|url=https://www.billboard.com/amp/articles/news/9437959/iggy-azalea-tinashe-dance-like-nobodys-watching-dlnw-stream|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|access-date=21 August 2020|archive-date=23 January 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210123142122/https://www.billboard.com/amp/articles/news/9437959/iggy-azalea-tinashe-dance-like-nobodys-watching-dlnw-stream|url-status=live}}</ref> Following its underperformance, Azalea scrapped the song from ''The End of an Era'' and released the album's new lead single "[[Sip It (song)|Sip It]]" with [[Tyga]] in April 2021.<ref>{{cite web|title=Iggy Azalea and Tyga Team Up For Colorful "Sip It" Video|website=[[Complex (website)|Complex]]|first=Joe|last=Price|date=2 April 2021|url=https://www.complex.com/music/iggy-azalea-tyga-sip-it|access-date=3 April 2021|archive-date=2 June 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210602214943/https://www.complex.com/music/iggy-azalea-tyga-sip-it|url-status=live}}</ref> In June 2021, Azalea tweeted that ''The End of an Era'' would be released in August of the same year.<ref>{{cite web|title=Iggy Azalea Finally Announces The Release Date Of Her 'End Of An Era' Album|website=From the Stage|first=Berk|last=Uykucuoglu|date=14 June 2021|url=https://fromthestage.net/iggy-azalea-finally-announces-the-release-date-of-her-end-of-an-era-album/|access-date=6 July 2021|archive-date=9 July 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210709184620/https://fromthestage.net/iggy-azalea-finally-announces-the-release-date-of-her-end-of-an-era-album/|url-status=live}}</ref>
On August 25, 2023, Azalea released her new single "Money Come", her first solo release in two years.
