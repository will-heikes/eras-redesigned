The American singer-songwriter [[Taylor Swift]] has released 68 [[single (music)|singles]] as a lead artist, 9 singles as a featured artist, and 42 [[promotional single]]s. {{as of|2025|5||df=US}}, Swift had achieved 137.5&nbsp;million [[RIAA certification|certified]] digital single units based on sales and on-demand [[Music streaming|streaming]] by the [[Recording Industry Association of America]] (RIAA).<ref>{{cite web |title=Top Artists (Digital Singles) |url=https://www.riaa.com/gold-platinum/?tab_active=top_tallies&ttt=TAS#search_section |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230902091448/https://www.riaa.com/gold-platinum/?tab_active=top_tallies&ttt=TAS#search_section |archive-date=September 2, 2023 |access-date=June 17, 2025 |publisher=[[Recording Industry Association of America]]}}</ref> On the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]], {{as of|October 2026|lc=y}}, she has garnered 16 number-one songs. She is the female musician with the most charted songs (281), most top-40 songs (182), most top-20 songs (117), most top-10 songs (74), most top-10 debuts (63), most top-five songs (46), and most top-five debuts (35). Additionally, she ties with [[Drake (musician)|Drake]] for the most number-one debuts (10).<ref name="US-singles" /><ref>{{Cite magazine |last=Zellner |first=Xander |date=October 7, 2026 |title=Here Are All the Hot 100 & ''Billboard'' 200 Chart Records That Taylor Swift Has Broken |url=https://www.billboard.com/lists/taylor-swift-hot-100-billboard-200-chart-records-broken/ |access-date=October 9, 2026 |magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]] |archive-date=October 9, 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20261009004843/https://www.billboard.com/lists/taylor-swift-hot-100-billboard-200-chart-records-broken/ |url-status=live}}</ref><ref name="Reference-1">{{cite magazine |last=Trust |first=Gary |date=October 5, 2026 |title=Taylor Swift's 'Patient Zero' Debuts at No. 1 on ''Billboard'' Hot 100 |url=https://www.billboard.com/lists/taylor-swift-patient-zero-hot-100-number-one-debut/ |access-date=October 5, 2026 |magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]] |archive-date=October 5, 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20261005175737/https://www.billboard.com/lists/taylor-swift-patient-zero-hot-100-number-one-debut/ |url-status=live}}</ref>

Swift is the first artist to monopolize the top 10 of the ''Billboard'' Hot 100 with ''Midnights'' and the top 14 with ''The Tortured Poets Department'',<ref>{{Cite news |last1=Coscarelli |first1=Joe |last2=Cox |first2=Courtney |last3=Bierman |first3=Fred |date=May 17, 2024 |title=How Big Is Taylor Swift? Besides material for her albums, Swift has recorded songs for film soundtracks including her first number-one Canadian single "[[Today Was a Fairytale]]" for ''[[Valentine's Day (soundtrack)|Valentine's Day]]'' (2010),<ref name="CAN-singles" /> the ''Billboard'' Hot 100 top-30 entries "[[Safe & Sound (Taylor Swift song)|Safe & Sound]]" featuring [[the Civil Wars]] and "[[Eyes Open (song)|Eyes Open]]" for [[The Hunger Games: Songs from District 12 and Beyond|''The Hunger Games'']] (2012),<ref>{{cite magazine |url=https://www.billboard.com/music/music-news/weekly-chart-notes-hunger-games-taylor-swift-adele-498712/ |title=Weekly Chart Notes: ''Hunger Games'', Taylor Swift, Adele |last=Trust |first=Gary |date=March 29, 2012 |magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]] |access-date=June 27, 2022 |archive-date=June 27, 2022 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220627031627/https://www.billboard.com/music/music-news/weekly-chart-notes-hunger-games-taylor-swift-adele-498712/ |url-status=live }}</ref>, the international top-five single "[[I Don't Wanna Live Forever]]" with [[Zayn Malik|Zayn]] for ''[[Fifty Shades Darker: Original Motion Picture Soundtrack|Fifty Shades Darker]]'' (2017), and the ''Billboard'' Hot 100 number-one single "[[I Knew It, I Knew You]]" for ''[[Toy Story 5 (soundtrack)|Toy Story 5]]'' (2026).<ref name="Trust-2026">{{Cite magazine |last=Trust |first=Gary |date=June 15, 2026 |title=Taylor Swift's 'I Knew It, I Knew You' Debuts at No. 1 on ''Billboard'' Hot 100 |url=https://www.billboard.com/lists/taylor-swift-i-knew-it-i-knew-you-number-1-hot-100-debut/ |access-date=June 16, 2026 |magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]] |archive-date=June 15, 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260615194427/https://www.billboard.com/lists/taylor-swift-i-knew-it-i-knew-you-number-1-hot-100-debut/ |url-status=live }}</ref>
