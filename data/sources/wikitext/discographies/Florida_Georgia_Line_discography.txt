American [[country music]] duo [[Florida Georgia Line]] has released five [[studio album]]s, two [[compilation album]]s, four [[extended play]]s, 19 [[single (music)|singles]] (as a lead artist), four featured singles, 27 other charted songs, and 29 [[music video]]s. Eighteen of their singles have reached number one on the US ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' [[Hot Country Songs]], [[Country Airplay]], or [[Canada Country]] charts.
