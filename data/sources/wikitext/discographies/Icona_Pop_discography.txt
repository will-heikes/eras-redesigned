The Swedish duo [[Icona Pop]] has released three [[studio album]]s, four [[extended play]]s and twenty two [[Single (music)|single]]s. In 2012, they released an extended play ''Iconic'' in the US and the album ''[[Icona Pop (album)|Icona Pop]]'' in Sweden. Their song. "[[I Love It (Icona Pop song)|I Love It]]", a pre-release of their self-titled album ''[[Icona Pop (album)|Icona Pop]]'' charted on [[Sverigetopplistan]], the official Swedish singles chart. On 20 September 2013, Icona Pop released their second studio album, a revised edition of ''Icona Pop'', ''[[This Is... Icona Pop]]''.
