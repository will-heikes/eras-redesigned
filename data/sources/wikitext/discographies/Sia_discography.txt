Australian singer-songwriter [[Sia]] has released 10 [[studio album]]s, six [[live album]]s, 68 [[Single (music)|single]]s (including 22 as a featured artist), and 45 [[music video]]s. In 1997, she released her debut studio album entitled ''OnlySee''.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://nla.gov.au/nla.cs-ma-an13682423 |title=Music Australia – Onlysee |publisher=[[National Library of Australia]] |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110629144859/http://nla.gov.au/nla.cs-ma-an13682423 |url-status=live |archive-date=29 June 2011}}</ref> It was commercially unsuccessful, and none of its songs were released as a single. Sia released her second album, ''[[Healing Is Difficult]]'', in 2001.<ref name="HID">{{cite web |url=https://music.apple.com/kr/album/healing-is-difficult-10th-anniversary-edition-deluxe/851277422 |title=''Healing Is Difficult'' - Album by Sia |publisher=[[Apple Music]] |date=9 July 2001 |access-date=3 November 2025}}</ref> The album yielded three singles: "[[Taken for Granted]]",<ref>{{cite magazine |url=https://worldradiohistory.com/UK/Music-Week/2000/Music-Week-2000-05-20.pdf |title=New Releases – For Week Starting May 22, 2000: Singles |magazine=[[Music Week]] |page=29 |date=20 May 2000 |access-date=2 August 2021}}</ref> "[[Little Man (Sia song)|Little Man]]"<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.amazon.co.uk/Little-Man-CD-1-Sia/dp/B00004YRXV |title=Little Man (CD 1) by Sia |publisher=Amazon UK |access-date=11 January 2015}}</ref> and "[[Drink to Get Drunk]]".<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.amazon.com/SIA-DRINK-GET-DRUNK/dp/B004GCWRM2 |title=Sia: Drink to Get Drunk: Vinyl (Different Gear) |publisher=Amazon |access-date=11 January 2015}}</ref> The lead single, "Taken for Granted", peaked at number 10 on the [[UK Singles Chart]].<ref name="UK"/>

In 2004, Sia released her third studio album, ''[[Colour the Small One]]''.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://music.apple.com/au/album/colour-the-small-one/17504060 |title=iTunes – Music – Colour The Small One by Sia |date=January 2004 |publisher=[[iTunes Store]] |location=Australia |access-date=11 November 2011}}</ref> Its singles were "[[Don't Bring Me Down (Sia song)|Don't Bring Me Down]]",<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.amazon.co.uk/Dont-Bring-Me-Down-Sia/dp/B000189WN2 |title=Don't Bring Me Down by Sia: CD Maxi |publisher=Amazon UK |access-date=11 January 2015}}</ref> "[[Breathe Me]]",<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.amazon.de/Breathe-Me-Vinyl-Single-Sia/dp/B0001XQ7OY |title=Breathe Me: Vinyl |publisher=Amazon |language=de |access-date=10 January 2015}}</ref> "[[Where I Belong (Sia song)|Where I Belong]]"<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.amazon.de/Where-I-Belong-Sia/dp/B0002JU5JA |title=Where I Belong: CD maxi |publisher=Amazon Germany |language=de |access-date=11 January 2015}}</ref> and "Numb".<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.amazon.de/Numb-Vinyl-Single-Sia/dp/B00064WYL8 |title=Numb: Vinyl Single |publisher=Amazon Germany |language=de |access-date=11 January 2015}}</ref> "Breathe Me" was the most successful single from ''Colour the Small One'', peaking at number 19 in Denmark,<ref>{{cite web |url=https://danishcharts.dk/showitem.asp?interpret=Sia&titel=Breathe+Me&cat=s |title=Sia – Breathe Me |publisher=[[Tracklisten]] |access-date=3 November 2025}}</ref> number 71 in the United Kingdom<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.officialcharts.com/Artist/9062/SIA/ |title=Sia |publisher=[[Official Charts Company]] |access-date=3 November 2025}}</ref> and number 81 in France.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://lescharts.com/showitem.asp?interpret=Sia&titel=Breathe+Me&cat=s |title=Sia – Breathe Me |publisher=[[SNEP|Le classement de singles]] |language=fr |access-date=3 November 2025}}</ref> In 2008, Sia released her fourth studio album, ''[[Some People Have Real Problems]]''.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0010DJ1VA |title=Some People Have Real Problems: Sia: Music |publisher=Amazon |access-date=11 November 2011}}</ref> The album was certified [[List of music recording certifications|gold]] by the [[Australian Recording Industry Association]] (ARIA),<ref name="ARIA2011"/> and spawned five singles: "Day Too Soon",<ref>{{cite web |title=iTunes Music – Day Too Soon |date=6 November 2007 |publisher=[[iTunes Store]] |location=United States |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150112170913/https://itunes.apple.com/us/album/day-too-soon-ep/id264089354 |archive-date=12 January 2015 |url-status=live |url=https://music.apple.com/us/album/day-too-soon-ep/264089354}}</ref> "[[The Girl You Lost to Cocaine]]", "[[Soon We'll Be Found]]", "[[Buttons (Sia song)|Buttons]]"<ref>{{cite web |url=https://itunes.apple.com/nz/album/buttons-single/id374983244 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150215141936/https://itunes.apple.com/nz/album/buttons-single/id374983244 |title=iTunes Music – Buttons – Single by Sia |publisher=[[iTunes Store]] |location=New Zealand |url-status=dead |archive-date=15 February 2015}}</ref> and "Academia". The follow-up album ''[[We Are Born]]'' was made available in 2010,<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.sanity.com.au/products/2154478/We_Are_Born |title=We Are Born Sia, Alternative, CD |publisher=[[Sanity (music store)|Sanity]] |access-date=11 November 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140808060300/http://www.sanity.com.au/products/2154478/We_Are_Born |archive-date=8 August 2014 |url-status=dead}}</ref> certified gold by the ARIA.<ref name="ARIA2011"/> It generated three singles: "[[You've Changed (Sia song)|You've Changed]]", "[[Clap Your Hands (Sia song)|Clap Your Hands]]" and "[[Bring Night]]".<ref>{{cite web |url=https://itunes.apple.com/nz/album/bring-night-single/id390129290 |title=iTunes Music – Bring Night – Single by Sia |publisher=[[iTunes Store]] |location=New Zealand |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150215144606/https://itunes.apple.com/nz/album/bring-night-single/id390129290 |url-status=dead |archive-date=15 February 2015}}</ref>

In 2011, Sia was featured on the top-ten singles "[[Titanium (David Guetta song)|Titanium]]" by [[David Guetta]] and "[[Wild Ones (Flo Rida song)|Wild Ones]]" by [[Flo Rida]]. In 2013, Sia contributed the song "[[Elastic Heart]]" to [[The Hunger Games: Catching Fire – Original Motion Picture Soundtrack|the soundtrack]] to the 2013 American film ''[[The Hunger Games: Catching Fire]]''. A year later, she released her sixth studio album, ''[[1000 Forms of Fear]]''.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.sanity.com.au/products/2269736/1000_Forms_Of_Fear_BONUS_OFFER |title=1000 Forms of Fear Sia |publisher=Sanity |access-date=8 January 2015 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141218150112/http://www.sanity.com.au/products/2269736/1000_Forms_Of_Fear_BONUS_OFFER |archive-date=18 December 2014 |url-status=dead}}</ref> It became Sia's most successful release, peaking atop the record charts of Australia, Canada and the United States. It was soon certified platinum in Australia and France.<ref name="1000AUS">{{cite web |url=http://www.ariacharts.com.au/chart/albums/78231 |title=ARIA Australian Top 50 Albums |date=27 April 2015 |publisher=Australian Recording Industry Association |access-date=25 April 2015 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150710032348/http://www.ariacharts.com.au/chart/albums/78231 |archive-date=10 July 2015}}</ref><ref name="Infodisc"/> As of January 2016, the album had sold 1 million copies worldwide.<ref name="wwsales">{{cite web |url=https://www.news.com.au/entertainment/music/sia-reclaims-the-songs-adele-rihanna-and-beyonce-rejected-to-rewrite-the-pop-blueprint/news-story/08460609cb95cfa5464a978b0682a215 |title=Sia reclaims the songs Adele, Rihanna and Beyonce rejected to rewrite the pop blueprint |publisher=News Corp Australia Network |date=29 January 2016 |access-date=1 October 2016}}</ref> Its lead single, "[[Chandelier (Sia song)|Chandelier]]", became Sia's first hit single as lead artist, peaking within the top ten of charts in various countries. ''1000 Forms of Fear'' was further promoted by the singles "[[Big Girls Cry]]", Sia's solo version of "[[Elastic Heart#Sia solo version|Elastic Heart]]" and "[[Fire Meet Gasoline]]".

Sia's seventh album, ''[[This Is Acting]]'', was released in 2016.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://music.apple.com/br/album/this-is-acting/1055074478 |title=This Is Acting by Sia |publisher=[[iTunes]] |access-date=4 November 2015}}</ref> It became her second consecutive chart-topping album in Australia and peaked within the top ten in various countries, including Canada, the United Kingdom and the United States. Its two top ten singles in her native country were "[[Alive (Sia song)|Alive]]" and "[[Cheap Thrills (song)|Cheap Thrills]]" (solo or featuring singer/rapper [[Sean Paul]]). The latter became her most successful song as a lead artist, reaching the top five in many European countries and peaking at number one on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]], where it became Sia's first song to do so. In 2017, Sia signed with [[Atlantic Records]] and released [[Everyday Is Christmas (album)|''Everyday Is Christmas'']],<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.stereoboard.com/content/view/211677/9 |title=Sia Lines Up November Release For New Album 'Everyday Is Christmas' |work=Stereoboard |date=16 August 2017 |access-date=16 August 2017 |last=Johnson |first=Laura}}</ref> her first [[Christmas album]] which featured 10 original songs, co-written and produced by [[Greg Kurstin]].<ref>{{Cite news |url=https://www.axs.com/sia-to-release-christmas-album-121568 |title=Sia to release Christmas album |publisher=AXS |access-date=10 March 2020 |language=en-US}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |url=https://www.mtv.co.uk/news/pf9ijp/sia-is-recording-a-christmas-album |title=Sia Has Signed A New Record Deal And Is Releasing A Christmas Album |publisher=MTV |language=en |access-date=10 March 2020}}</ref> "[[Santa's Coming for Us]]" served as the only single from the album.
