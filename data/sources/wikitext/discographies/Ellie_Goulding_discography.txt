The English singer-songwriter [[Ellie Goulding]] has released six [[Album#Studio|studio albums]], two [[remix album]]s, ninth [[extended play]]s (EPs), 54 [[Single (music)|singles]] (including 11 as a featured artist), 13 [[Promotional recording#Single|promotional singles]] and 60 [[music video]]s. She had sold over 2.6 million albums and over 15 million singles (including collaborations) in the United Kingdom alone as of August 2024.<ref name="BPI" /> According to [[Recording Industry Association of America]] (RIAA), Goulding has sold 28.5 million digital singles and 4.5 million albums in the United States.<ref name="RIAA" /> After signing to [[Polydor Records]] in July 2009,<ref>{{cite web |last=Ferguson |first=Paul |url=http://www.herefordtimes.com/news/4581912.Herefordshire_singer__Ellie_Goulding__signs_recording_deal_with_Polydor/ |title=Herefordshire singer, Ellie Goulding, signs recording deal with Polydor |work=[[Hereford Times]] |date=4 September 2009 |access-date=25 November 2014}}</ref> Goulding released her debut extended play, ''An Introduction to Ellie Goulding'', in December of that year. It was followed by the release of her debut studio album, ''[[Lights (Ellie Goulding album)|Lights]]'', in February 2010. The album debuted at number one on the [[UK Albums Chart]] and subsequently earned a double platinum certification from the [[British Phonographic Industry]] (BPI).<ref name="UK"/><ref name="BPI"/> It spawned four singles: "[[Under the Sheets]]", "[[Starry Eyed (Ellie Goulding song)|Starry Eyed]]", "[[Guns and Horses]]" and "[[The Writer (song)|The Writer]]", which reached numbers 53, four, 26 and 19 on the [[UK singles chart]], respectively.<ref name="UK"/> In November 2010, the album was re-released as ''Bright Lights''; it featured seven new songs, including a [[cover version]] of [[Elton John]]'s "[[Your Song#Ellie Goulding version|Your Song]]", which peaked at number two on the UK singles chart.<ref name="UK"/> The sixth single to be lifted from the album, "[[Lights (Ellie Goulding song)|Lights]]", peaked at No. 49 in the United Kingdom,<ref name="UK"/> while becoming Goulding's highest-charting single to date in the United States and Canada, where it reached numbers two and seven, respectively.<ref name="US-singles"/><ref name="CAN-singles"/>

Goulding's second studio album, ''[[Halcyon (Ellie Goulding album)|Halcyon]]'', was released in October 2012, peaking at number one in the UK and reaching the top 10 in Canada, Germany and the US.<ref name="UK"/><ref name="CAN-albums"/><ref name="GER"/><ref name="US-albums"/> It was later certified triple platinum by the BPI and gold by the [[Recording Industry Association of America]] (RIAA).<ref name="BPI"/><ref name="RIAA"/> The album's lead single, "[[Anything Could Happen]]", earned Goulding her third top-five song on the UK Singles Chart when it peaked at number five.<ref name="UK"/> The track also became her first charting single in Australia (number 20) and second appearance on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] (number 47).<ref name="US-singles"/><ref name="AUS"/> Subsequent singles "[[Figure 8 (song)|Figure 8]]" and "[[Explosions (song)|Explosions]]" peaked at numbers 33 and 13 on the UK chart, respectively.<ref name="UK"/> In 2013, Goulding was featured on [[Calvin Harris]]'s single "[[I Need Your Love (Calvin Harris song)|I Need Your Love]]", which reached number four in the UK.<ref name="UK"/> That same year, her album ''Halcyon'' was reissued as ''[[Halcyon Days (Ellie Goulding album)|Halcyon Days]]'', containing 10 additional tracks. It was preceded by the lead single "[[Burn (Ellie Goulding song)|Burn]]", which gave Goulding her first UK number-one single.<ref name="UK"/> The song also became an international success, charting inside the top 10 in several countries.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.ultratop.be/nl/song/11ca83/Ellie-Goulding-Burn |title=Ellie Goulding – Burn |language=nl |publisher=[[Ultratop]] |access-date=25 November 2014}}</ref> ''Halcyon Days'' yielded two more singles: a cover of "[[How Long Will I Love You?#Ellie Goulding version|How Long Will I Love You]]" by [[the Waterboys]] and "[[Goodness Gracious]]". In 2014, Goulding recorded the song "[[Beating Heart (Ellie Goulding song)|Beating Heart]]" for the [[Divergent (soundtrack)|soundtrack]] to ''[[Divergent (film)|Divergent]]'', and she also released her second collaboration with Calvin Harris, titled "[[Outside (Calvin Harris song)|Outside]]". The following year, she contributed the song "[[Love Me like You Do]]" to the [[Fifty Shades of Grey (soundtrack)|soundtrack]] to ''[[Fifty Shades of Grey (film)|Fifty Shades of Grey]]''. "Love Me like You Do" became a commercial success worldwide, earning Goulding her second UK number one and topping the charts in several other countries.<ref name="UK"/><ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.ultratop.be/nl/song/14f827/Ellie-Goulding-Love-Me-Like-You-Do |title=Ellie Goulding – Love Me Like You Do |language=nl |publisher=Ultratop |access-date=10 June 2015}}</ref>

Goulding's third studio album, ''[[Delirium (Ellie Goulding album)|Delirium]]'', was released in November 2015 and peaked at number three in the United Kingdom, the United States and Australia,<ref name="UK"/><ref name="US-albums"/><ref name="AUS"/> while reaching the top five in several other countries.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.ultratop.be/nl/album/456d2/Ellie-Goulding-Delirium |title=Ellie Goulding – Delirium |language=nl |publisher=Ultratop |access-date=13 August 2016}}</ref> The album spawned three singles: "[[On My Mind (Ellie Goulding song)|On My Mind]]", "[[Army (Ellie Goulding song)|Army]]" and "[[Something in the Way You Move]]". In 2016, Goulding recorded the song "[[Still Falling for You]]" for the [[Bridget Jones's Baby: Original Motion Picture Soundtrack|soundtrack]] to ''[[Bridget Jones's Baby|Bridgit Jones' Baby]]''.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Ellie Goulding releases new song from 'Bridget Jones's Baby' soundtrack |url=https://ew.com/article/2016/08/19/ellie-goulding-still-falling-bridget-jones-baby/ |access-date=2024-04-19 |website=EW.com |language=en}}</ref> On the same year, Goulding announced that she was going on indefinite hiatus.<ref>{{Cite news |last=Reporters |first=Telegraph |date=2016-03-10 |title=Ellie Goulding takes a break from music and boyfriend Dougie Poynter: 'I need time off' |url=https://www.telegraph.co.uk/music/news/ellie-goulding-takes-a-break-from-music-and-boyfriend-dougie-poy/ |access-date=2024-04-19 |work=The Telegraph |language=en-GB |issn=0307-1235}}</ref> During the hiatus, to keep in touch with her fans, Goulding released several stand-alone singles: "[[First Time (Kygo and Ellie Goulding song)|First Time]]" with the Norwegian DJ and record producer [[Kygo]], "Return to Love" with the  Italian tenor [[Andrea Bocelli]], and the cover versions of [[Don McLean]]'s "[[Vincent (Don McLean song)|Vincent]]", the Christmas carol "[[O Holy Night]]", and her rendition of [[Joni Mitchell]]'s "[[River (Joni Mitchell song)|River]]", which became Goulding's third number-one in her homecountry and the last number-one single of the 2010s in the UK.  After a five-year gap, Goulding made her long-awaited return with her fourth studio album, ''[[Brightest Blue]]'', which peaked at number one in the UK, her third album to do so. The record was split into two sides;<ref>{{Cite web |date=2020-05-27 |title=Ellie Goulding shares details of new double album Brightest Blue |url=https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/ellie-goulding-shares-details-of-new-double-album-brightest-blue__29854/ |access-date=2024-04-20 |website=Official Charts |language=en}}</ref> the first side ''Brightest Blue'', marked the return of Goulding introspective sound and it spanned the singles: "[[Flux (Ellie Goulding song)|Flux]]", "[[Power (Ellie Goulding song)|Power]]" and "[[Love I'm Given]]", while the second side of the record, titled ''[[EG.0]]'', was a more commercial sound, and described by Goulding herself as the songs that her record label ([[Polydor Records|Polydor]]) wants her to do. It spawned several singles, including: "[[Close to Me (Ellie Goulding, Diplo and Swae Lee song)|Close to Me]]", "[[Hate Me (Ellie Goulding and Juice Wrld song)|Hate Me]]", "[[Sixteen (Ellie Goulding song)|Sixteen]]", "[[Slow Grenade]]" and "[[Worry About Me]]", while the first three saw international commercial success.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2020-05-27 |title=Ellie Goulding Details New Album Brightest Blue |url=https://pitchfork.com/news/ellie-goulding-details-new-album-brightest-blue/ |access-date=2024-04-20 |website=Pitchfork |language=en-US}}</ref>

In 2023, Goulding and Harris released their third collaboration, titled "[[Miracle (Calvin Harris and Ellie Goulding song)|Miracle]]". The track was a commercial success in Europe, specially in the United Kingdom where it became Goulding's 35 chart entry in the [[UK singles chart|UK Official Singles Charts]], the most entries for any British female soloist in history,<ref>{{Cite web |date=2023-04-07 |title=Ellie Goulding on Her Exhilarating Return to the Dance Floor With 'Higher Than Heaven' |url=https://www.vogue.com/article/ellie-goulding-higher-than-heaven-interview |access-date=2024-04-21 |website=Vogue |language=en-US}}</ref> and spent eight non-consecutive weeks at number one, becoming Goulding's fourth UK number-one, and her longest running number one single to date.<ref>{{Cite magazine |last=Brandle |first=Lars |date=2023-06-05 |title=Calvin Harris And Ellie Goulding's 'Miracle' Streak Continues In U.K. |url=https://www.billboard.com/music/chart-beat/calvin-harris-ellie-goulding-miracle-streak-continues-uk-chart-1235344644/ |access-date=2024-04-21 |magazine=Billboard |language=en-US}}</ref> In its second week at number one, Goulding's fifth studio album, ''[[Higher Than Heaven]]'' was released and debuted atop the [[UK Albums Chart]], marking the first time Goulding [[List of artists who have achieved simultaneous number-one UK single and album|simultaneously had a number-one single and album]] in her career.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2023-04-14 |title=Ellie Goulding's Higher Than Heaven and Calvin Harris collab Miracle are Number 1: Official Chart Double |url=https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/ellie-gouldings-higher-than-heaven-and-calvin-harris-collab-miracle-are-number-1-official-chart-double-__39042/ |access-date=2024-04-21 |website=Official Charts |language=en}}</ref> The record also became her fourth UK number-one album, tying with [[Adele]], as the British female artists with the most number ones albums in UK history. The album spawned the singles: "[[Easy Lover (Ellie Goulding song)|Easy Lover]]", "[[All by Myself (Alok, Sigala and Ellie Goulding song)|All by Myself]]", "[[Let It Die (Ellie Goulding song)|Let It Die]]", "[[Like a Saviour]]" and "[[By the End of the Night]]". In September 2023, Goulding collaborated with music producer [[TSHA (musician)|TSHA]], and singer-songwriter [[Gregory Porter]], on the single "[[Somebody (TSHA, Ellie Goulding and Gregory Porter song)|Somebody]]".<ref>{{Cite web |date=2023-09-14 |title=TSHA, Ellie Goulding and Gregory Porter link up on new single, 'Somebody': Listen |url=https://djmag.com/news/tsha-ellie-goulding-and-gregory-porter-link-new-single-somebody-listen |access-date=2024-04-20 |website=DJ Mag |language=en}}</ref> During 2024, Goulding released two remixes: "Brightest Blue" (with Nature), and "[[I Adore You (Hugel, Topic and Arash song)#J Balvin and Ellie Goulding version|I Adore You]]" (alongside [[J Balvin]]) and three acclaimed [[Electronic dance music|electronic dance]] collaborations: "[[Leave Me Slowly]]" (with [[Starsmith]]), "[[Free (Calvin Harris and Ellie Goulding song)|Free]]" (with [[Calvin Harris]]), and "[[In My Dreams (Four Tet and Ellie Goulding song)|In My Dreams]]" (with [[Four Tet]]).<ref>{{Cite web |last=Murray |first=Robin |date=2024-07-26 |title=Calvin Harris, Ellie Goulding Re-Unite On 'Free' {{!}} News |url=https://www.clashmusic.com/news/calvin-harris-ellie-goulding-re-unite-on-free/ |access-date=2025-01-31 |website=Clash Magazine Music News, Reviews & Interviews |language=en-GB}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=UK |first=OCULATE |date=2024-09-11 |title=Four Tet And Ellie Goulding Reunite On Hypnotic New Track 'In My Dreams' |url=https://www.oculate.uk/archives/fourtet-elliegoulding-inmydreams |access-date=2025-01-31 |website=Oculate UK |language=en}}</ref> She also appeared as a featured artist on "My Life/Happiness (Skit)" from [[Big Sean]]'s ''[[Better Me Than You]]'' (2024). Goulding achieved further acclaim with the release of "[[Hypnotized (Anyma and Ellie Goulding song)|Hypnotized]]" (with [[Anyma]]) in early 2025.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2025-01-10 |title=Ellie Goulding & Anyma Team Up For The Mesmerizing New Track "Hypnotized" {{!}} Hit Channel |url=https://hit-channel.com/ellie-goulding-anyma-hypnotized/ |access-date=2025-01-31 |language=en-US}}</ref> She followed this with two more singles later that year: "[[Save My Love (song)|Save My Love]]" (with [[Marshmello]] and Avaion) and "[[Destiny (Ellie Goulding song)|Destiny]]", which was released as the ending them for the Japanese [[manga]] series ''[[Clevatess]]''. In 2026, Goulding appeared as a featured artist on two records: "[[Don't Want Your Love]]" from [[Illenium]]'s [[Odyssey (Illenium album)|''Odyssey'']] and "[[Hush (Muse song)|Hush]]" from [[Muse (band)|Muse]]'s [[The Wow! Signal (album)|''The Wow! Signal'']]. Goulding's sixth studio album ''[[I Know Too Much]]'' was released on 4 September 2026, spawning four singles: "[[Black Prada Dress]]", "[[4 Seasons (song)|4 Seasons]]", "[[Ravers (song)|Ravers]]" and "[[For Your Entertainment (Ellie Goulding song)|For Your Entertainment]]".
