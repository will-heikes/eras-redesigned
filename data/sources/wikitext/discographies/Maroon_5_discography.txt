American [[pop rock]] band [[Maroon 5]] has released eight [[studio album]]s, three [[live album]]s, three [[compilation album]]s, one [[remix album]], seven [[extended play]]s (EPs), 39 [[single (music)|single]]s, 10 [[promotional single]]s, and 48 [[music video]]s. The group originally formed in 1994 as [[Kara's Flowers]] while they were still attending high school. With a line-up of [[Adam Levine]], [[Jesse Carmichael]], [[Michael Madden (musician)|Mickey Madden]] and [[Ryan Dusick]], they released their independent album, ''...We Like Digging?'' in 1994. In 1997, they signed to [[Reprise Records]] and released an album, ''[[The Fourth World (album)|The Fourth World]]''. After a tepid response to the album, the band parted with their record label and attended college. In 2001, the band regrouped and added [[James Valentine (musician)|James Valentine]] to the lineup, and pursued a new direction under the name Maroon 5.<ref>{{cite web | author=Leahey, Andrew | url=http://www.allmusic.com/artist/karas-flowers-mn0000855690 | title=Kara's Flowers | work=[[AllMusic]] | access-date=July 31, 2012}}</ref>

Maroon 5 signed with [[A&M Octone Records|Octone Records]] and recorded their debut album in 2002. The album ''[[Songs About Jane]]'', was released in June 2002 and topped the charts in Australia, France, Ireland, New Zealand, and the United Kingdom. Its lead single "[[Harder to Breathe]]" received heavy airplay, which helped propel the album to number 6 on the US [[Billboard 200|''Billboard'' 200]].<ref name="US-albums"/> The album's second and third singles, "[[This Love (Maroon 5 song)|This Love]]" and "[[She Will Be Loved]]", were worldwide hits in 2004. Five singles were released from the album. For the next few years, the band toured extensively worldwide in support of ''Songs About Jane'' and issued two live recordings: ''[[1.22.03.Acoustic]]'' (2004) and ''[[Live – Friday the 13th]]'' (2005).<ref>{{cite web | author=Leahey, Andrew | url=http://www.allmusic.com/artist/maroon-5-mn0000285232/ | title=Maroon 5 | work=[[AllMusic]] | access-date=July 31, 2012}}</ref>

In 2006, drummer Ryan Dusick departed the band and was replaced by [[Matt Flynn (musician)|Matt Flynn]] and the revised band recorded their second album ''[[It Won't Be Soon Before Long]]'' in early 2007.<ref>{{cite web | author=Moss, Corey | url=http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1553993/20070306/maroon_5.jhtml | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070309123237/http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1553993/20070306/maroon_5.jhtml | url-status=dead | archive-date=March 9, 2007 | title=Maroon 5 Back With 'Harder' Album After Adam Levine Gets Sick Of Partying | work=[[MTV]] | publisher=[[Viacom Media Networks]] | date=March 6, 2007 | access-date=July 25, 2007}}</ref> The album was released in May and peaked at number 1 on the ''Billboard'' 200.<ref name="US-albums"/> Five singles were released from the album. The album's first single, "[[Makes Me Wonder]]", became the band's first number-one single on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]].<ref name="US-singles"/> ''[[Hands All Over (album)|Hands All Over]]'', the band's third studio album, was released in September 2010, peaking at number 2 on the ''Billboard'' 200.<ref name="US-albums"/> The album's lead single "[[Misery (Maroon 5 song)|Misery]]" was a top 15 hit on the ''Billboard'' Hot 100 while the album's fourth single, "[[Moves Like Jagger]]", became the band's second single to top the Hot 100.<ref name="US-singles"/> The band released their fourth studio album ''[[Overexposed (album)|Overexposed]]'' in June 2012. The album peaked at number 2 on the ''Billboard'' 200.<ref name="US-albums"/> Its first two singles, "[[Payphone (song)|Payphone]]" and "[[One More Night (Maroon 5 song)|One More Night]]", both peaked in the top two of the ''Billboard'' Hot 100 and became international hits. The latter single topped the Hot 100, giving the band their third number-one hit on the chart.<ref name="US-singles"/> The album's third single, "Daylight", became a top ten hit in the US and Canada, as well as peaking in the top forty of other multiple countries worldwide.

In September 2014, the fifth studio album ''[[V (Maroon 5 album)|V]]'' (pronounced: "five"),<!--WARNING!, Do not remove this sentence--> was released and topped the ''Billboard'' 200. The lead single "[[Maps (Maroon 5 song)|Maps]]" marked number six on the Hot 100, the second single "[[Animals (Maroon 5 song)|Animals]]" peaked at number three on the same chart, and the third single "[[Sugar (Maroon 5 song)|Sugar]]", number two. In November 2017, the band released their sixth studio album ''[[Red Pill Blues]]'' and included the top ten singles "[[Don't Wanna Know]]" featuring [[Kendrick Lamar]], and "[[What Lovers Do]]" featuring [[SZA]]. The remix version of "[[Girls Like You]]" featuring [[Cardi B]] reached number one on the Hot 100, giving the band their fourth number one single in the US.
