American singer [[Lady Gaga]] has released six solo [[studio album]]s, two collaborative studio albums, four [[film soundtrack]]s, three [[remix album]]s, two [[box set]]s, four [[extended plays]] (EPs), three live albums, 42 [[Single (music)|singles]] (including four as a featured artist), and 16 [[promotional single]]s. Gaga made her debut in August 2008 with the studio album ''[[The Fame]]'', which peaked at number two in the [[United States]], where it was subsequently [[List of music recording certifications|certified]] triple Platinum, while topping the charts in [[Austria]], [[Canada]], [[Germany]], [[Switzerland]], and the [[United Kingdom]]. Its first two singles, "[[Just Dance (song)|Just Dance]]" and "[[Poker Face (song)|Poker Face]]", reached number one in [[Australia]], Canada, the United Kingdom, and the United States, and for the latter, becoming the [[sleeper hit|world's biggest single of the 2009 calendar year]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://americansongwriter.com/ifpi-tallies-global-music-sales-lady-gaga-rules-the-planet/|title=IFPI Tallies Global Music Sales: Lady Gaga Rules The Planet|magazine=American Songwriter|last=Richards|first=Kevin|date=January 21, 2010 |access-date=January 6, 2023|archive-date=January 6, 2023|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230106184112/https://americansongwriter.com/ifpi-tallies-global-music-sales-lady-gaga-rules-the-planet/|url-status=live}}</ref> The album spawned three more singles: "[[Eh, Eh (Nothing Else I Can Say)]]", "[[LoveGame]]" and "[[Paparazzi (Lady Gaga song)|Paparazzi]]". The latter reached the top ten in many countries worldwide, and number one in Germany.

Gaga later released ''[[The Fame Monster]]'' in November 2009, as a deluxe edition or [[reissue]] of ''The Fame'', which was ultimately released also as a standalone EP.<ref name="Birchmeier">{{cite web|url=https://www.allmusic.com/artist/lady-gaga-p1055684|title=Lady Gaga {{!}} Biography|last=Birchmeier|first=Jason|website=[[AllMusic]]|access-date=September 3, 2010|archive-date=October 21, 2010|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20101021072619/http://www.allmusic.com/artist/lady-gaga-p1055684|url-status=live}}</ref> It reached number one in Australia, [[New Zealand]], and the United Kingdom, as well as number five in the United States, where it was certified Platinum. Its lead single, "[[Bad Romance]]", became an international hit and reached number one in twelve countries while topping  at number two in the United States. Subsequent singles, "[[Telephone (song)|Telephone]]" and "[[Alejandro (song)|Alejandro]]", were top ten hits in many countries. Consisting of various remixes of songs from both ''The Fame'' and ''The Fame Monster'', Gaga's first compilation album ''[[The Remix (Lady Gaga album)|The Remix]]'' was released in 2010. It peaked at number six in the United States while reaching the top five in Canada and the United Kingdom. The remix album has sold 500,000 copies worldwide, therefore making it one of the [[List of best-selling remix albums worldwide|best-selling remix albums of all time]].

The singer's second studio album, ''[[Born This Way (album)|Born This Way]]'', was released in May 2011 and reached the number-one spot in the United States along with twenty other countries. Its [[Born This Way (song)|eponymous lead single]] was an international success, peaking at number one in nineteen countries including the United States, where it became her third number-one single. The other singles from the album were "[[Judas (Lady Gaga song)|Judas]]", "[[The Edge of Glory]]", "[[You and I (Lady Gaga song)|You and I]]", "[[Marry the Night]]", and "[[Bloody Mary (song)|Bloody Mary]]". Gaga's third studio album, ''[[Artpop]]'', was released in November 2013 and includes the singles "[[Applause (Lady Gaga song)|Applause]]" and "[[Do What U Want]]". In September 2014, Gaga and [[Tony Bennett]] released the collaborative album ''[[Cheek to Cheek (album)|Cheek to Cheek]]'', which debuted at number one in the US, becoming Gaga's third consecutive number one album.<ref name="usc2c">{{cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/biz/articles/6266770/tony-bennett-lady-gagas-cheek-to-cheek-debuts-at-no-1|title=Tony Bennett & Lady Gaga's 'Cheek To Cheek' Debuts at No. 1|last=Caulfield|first=Keith|date=October 1, 2014|access-date=October 1, 2014|magazine=Billboard|url-status=live|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141003135447/http://www.billboard.com/biz/articles/6266770/tony-bennett-lady-gagas-cheek-to-cheek-debuts-at-no-1|archive-date=October 3, 2014}}</ref> Her fifth album, ''[[Joanne (album)|Joanne]]'', was released in October 2016. Its lead single, "[[Perfect Illusion]]", debuted at number one in France,<ref name=Francedebut>{{cite web|url=http://www.snepmusique.com/tops-semaine/top-singles-telecharges/?ye=2016&we=37|title=Le Top de la semaine : Top Singles Téléchargés – SNEP (Week 37, 2016)|work=SNEP |date=January 25, 2014 |trans-title=Top of the week: Top Downloaded Singles - SNEP (Week 37, 2016)|publisher=[[Syndicat National de l'Édition Phonographique]]|language=fr|access-date=September 17, 2016|url-status=live|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160919140946/http://www.snepmusique.com/tops-semaine/top-singles-telecharges/?ye=2016&we=37|archive-date=September 19, 2016}}</ref> while second single "[[Million Reasons]]" reached number four in the United States. When ''Joanne'' reached number one in the United States, Gaga became the first woman to have four number one albums in the nation during the 2010s.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/lady-gaga-scores-her-fourth-no-1-album-on-billboard-200-chart-joanne/|title=Lady Gaga Scores Her Fourth No. 1 Album on Billboard 200 Chart With 'Joanne'|magazine=Billboard|last=Caulfield|first=Keith|date=October 30, 2016|access-date=October 31, 2016|url-status=live|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161030194903/http://www.billboard.com/articles/columns/chart-beat/7557828/lady-gaga-scores-her-fourth-no-1-album-on-billboard-200-chart-joanne|archive-date=October 30, 2016}}</ref> 

She extended the record in 2018 with the release of the [[A Star Is Born (2018 soundtrack)|soundtrack]] to ''[[A Star Is Born (2018 film)|A Star Is Born]]''.<ref name="asibdebut">{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/a-star-is-born-soundtrack-no-1-debut-billboard-200/|title=Lady Gaga & Bradley Cooper's 'A Star Is Born' Soundtrack Debuts at No. 1 on Billboard 200 Albums Chart|magazine=Billboard|last=Caulfield|first=Keith|date=October 14, 2018|access-date=October 15, 2018|archive-date=October 14, 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20181014231442/https://www.billboard.com/articles/columns/chart-beat/8479831/a-star-is-born-soundtrack-no-1-debut-billboard-200|url-status=live}}</ref> The soundtrack and its lead single, "[[Shallow (Lady Gaga and Bradley Cooper song)|Shallow]]" (with [[Bradley Cooper]]), were international hits, reaching number one in the United States, Australia, New Zealand, Switzerland, and the United Kingdom. Her sixth studio album, ''[[Chromatica]]'' (2020), similarly topped the charts in numerous countries around the world, and also featured the single, "[[Rain on Me (Lady Gaga and Ariana Grande song)|Rain on Me]]" (with [[Ariana Grande]]), which topped the charts in the US, Canada, the UK and several other territories. In September 2021, she released another collaborative album with Bennett titled ''[[Love for Sale (Tony Bennett and Lady Gaga album)|Love for Sale]]''. Gaga subsequently recorded "[[Hold My Hand (Lady Gaga song)|Hold My Hand]]" for the 2022 film ''[[Top Gun: Maverick]]'' and composed its [[Top Gun: Maverick (soundtrack)|score]] alongside [[Lorne Balfe]], [[Harold Faltermeyer]], and [[Hans Zimmer]]. She also recorded "[[Die with a Smile]]" (2024) with [[Bruno Mars]], which topped both the [[Billboard Global 200|''Billboard'' Global 200]] for seventeen weeks and other charts in more than twenty countries, became the [[List of Spotify streaming records|longest-reigning daily hit in Spotify history]], and made Gaga the first artist to have multiple number one songs in the United States in the 2000s, 2010s and 2020s.<ref>{{cite web|last=Stéphanie|first=Bois|url=https://www.nrj.be/article/27790/lady-gaga-et-bruno-mars-battent-un-record-historique-spotify|title=Lady Gaga et Bruno Mars battent un record historique sur Spotify|publisher=[[NRJ (Belgium)|NRJ Belgium]]|language=fr|date=February 12, 2025|access-date=February 15, 2025|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250215174312/https://www.nrj.be/article/27790/lady-gaga-et-bruno-mars-battent-un-record-historique-spotify|archive-date=February 15, 2025|url-status=live}}</ref><ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/music/chart-beat/lady-gaga-bruno-mars-die-with-a-smile-global-200-number-one-eighth-week-1235807210/|title=Lady Gaga & Bruno Mars' 'Die With a Smile' Tops Global 200 for Eighth Week, the Most of 2024|last=Trust|first=Gary|magazine=Billboard|date=October 21, 2024|access-date=October 21, 2024|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20241113003144/https://www.billboard.com/music/chart-beat/lady-gaga-bruno-mars-die-with-a-smile-global-200-number-one-eighth-week-1235807210/|archive-date=November 13, 2024|url-status=live}}</ref> The track along with the singles "[[Disease (Lady Gaga song)|Disease]]" and "[[Abracadabra (Lady Gaga song)|Abracadabra]]" were included on Gaga's album, ''[[Mayhem (Lady Gaga album)|Mayhem]]'', released on March 7, 2025.
