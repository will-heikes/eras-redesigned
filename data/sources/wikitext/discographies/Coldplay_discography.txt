British [[Rock music|rock]] band [[Coldplay]] have released 10&nbsp;[[studio album]]s, 18&nbsp;[[extended play]]s, 6&nbsp;[[live album]]s, 13&nbsp;[[compilation album]]s, 43&nbsp;[[Single (music)|singles]], 14&nbsp;[[promotional single]]s and 5&nbsp;[[charity single]]s. They are considered the most successful group of the 21st century and one of the [[List of best-selling music artists|best-selling acts of all time]],<ref>{{Cite web |date=25 June 2024 |title=Glastonbury 2024: The Reason Coldplay Are the 21st Century's Defining Band |url=https://www.bbc.com/culture/article/20240624-the-reason-coldplay-are-the-21st-centurys-most-loved-band |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240728050702/https://www.bbc.com/culture/article/20240624-the-reason-coldplay-are-the-21st-centurys-most-loved-band |archive-date=28 July 2024 |access-date=28 July 2024 |publisher=BBC News}}</ref> with estimated sales of 170 million records.<ref>{{Cite web |date=11 October 2026 |title=The 30 Best Songs of the 2000s, Ranked |url=https://www.telegraph.co.uk/music/news/best-songs-of-the-2000s-ranked/ |url-access=subscription |url-status=live |archive-url=https://ghostarchive.org/archive/7IB6x |archive-date=12 October 2026 |access-date=11 October 2026 |website=The Telegraph}}</ref> According to [[Luminate (company)|Luminate]], they have shifted 18.2 million albums and 33.6 million songs in the United States, based on physical and digital copies.<ref>{{Cite magazine |date=20 February 2015 |title=Is Coldplay's Planned 2016 Stadium Trek a Farewell Tour? |url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/6480012/coldplay-2016-stadium-farewell-tour-chris-martin-plans |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161120135606/http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/6480012/coldplay-2016-stadium-farewell-tour-chris-martin-plans |archive-date=20 November 2016 |access-date=7 January 2022 |magazine=Billboard}}</ref> Moreover, the [[British Phonographic Industry]] (BPI) claimed that their international success contributed to British music export rates several times.<ref>{{Cite web |date=7 September 2017 |title=British Music Exports Up 11% in 2016 |url=https://www.musicweek.com/labels/read/british-music-exports-up-11-in-2016/069710 |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20251112170440/https://www.musicweek.com/labels/read/british-music-exports-up-11-in-2016/069710 |archive-date=12 November 2025 |access-date=12 November 2025 |website=Music Week}}
* {{Cite web |date=16 June 2021 |title=British Music Exports Reach a Record High |url=https://www.bbc.com/news/entertainment-arts-57486272 |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20251112170443/https://www.bbc.com/news/entertainment-arts-57486272 |archive-date=12 November 2025 |access-date=12 November 2025 |publisher=BBC News}}</ref> Other milestones for the band include more than 40 billion streams on [[Spotify]] and 20 billion views on [[YouTube]].<ref>{{Cite web |date=8 July 2025 |title=25 Jahre Coldplay |trans-title=25 Years of Coldplay |url=https://rp-online.de/kultur/musik/25-jahre-coldplay-ich-bin-nicht-wuetend-nur-enttaeuscht-v1_aid-129916677 |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250729184852/https://rp-online.de/kultur/musik/25-jahre-coldplay-ich-bin-nicht-wuetend-nur-enttaeuscht-v1_aid-129916677 |archive-date=29 July 2025 |access-date=25 July 2025 |website=Rheinische Post |language=de |url-access=subscription}}</ref>

After launching their extended plays ''[[Safety (EP)|Safety]]'' (1998) and ''[[The Blue Room (EP)|The Blue Room]]'' (1999), Coldplay entered the [[UK Singles Chart]] for the first time with "[[Brothers & Sisters (song)|Brothers & Sisters]]", at number 92.<ref>{{Cite web |date=1 May 1999 |title=Brothers & Sisters by Coldplay |url=https://www.officialcharts.com/songs/coldplay-brothers-sisters/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230707164023/https://www.officialcharts.com/songs/coldplay-brothers-sisters |archive-date=7 July 2023 |access-date=3 March 2024 |publisher=Official Charts Company}}</ref> It was followed by "[[Shiver (Coldplay song)|Shiver]]" and "[[Yellow (Coldplay song)|Yellow]]", which became their first Top 40 and Top 10 entries, respectively. With the release of ''[[Parachutes (Coldplay album)|Parachutes]]'' (2000), ''[[A Rush of Blood to the Head]]'' (2002), and ''[[X&Y]]'' (2005), they scored three of the 50 [[List of best-selling albums in the United Kingdom|best-selling albums in the United Kingdom]].<ref>{{Cite web |date=22 July 2026 |title=The Biggest Albums of All Time on the Official UK Chart |url=https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/biggest-albums-all-time-uk/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260722224451/https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/biggest-albums-all-time-uk/ |archive-date=22 July 2026 |access-date=23 July 2026 |publisher=Official Charts Company}}</ref> The former included Top 10 singles such as "[[In My Place]]", "[[Clocks (song)|Clocks]]" and "[[The Scientist (song)|The Scientist]]", while the latter achieved one of the [[List of fastest-selling albums#United Kingdom|largest sales week in the country]].<ref>{{Cite web |date=27 April 2011 |title=50 Fastest-Selling Albums Ever |url=https://www.nme.com/photos/50-fastest-selling-albums-ever/213617/1/1#48 |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121021140850/http://www.nme.com/photos/50-fastest-selling-albums-ever/213617/1/1#48 |archive-date=21 October 2012 |access-date=28 December 2021 |work=NME}}</ref> Coldplay were also the first British act to spend three weeks atop [[Billboard 200|''Billboard'' 200]] since [[the Beatles]] in 2000 and 2001.<ref>{{Cite news |date=7 July 2005 |title=Chart Beat: Fred Bronson Reports On Chart Activity Related To Jessica Simpson, Green Day, Coldplay & Kelly Clarkson |url=https://www.billboard.com/music/music-news/chart-beat-62164/ |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220108173249/https://www.billboard.com/music/music-news/chart-beat-62164/ |archive-date=8 January 2022 |access-date=8 January 2022 |magazine=Billboard}}</ref>
