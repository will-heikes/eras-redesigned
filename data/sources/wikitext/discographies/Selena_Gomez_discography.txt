American singer [[Selena Gomez]] has released three solo [[Album#Studio album|studio albums]], one [[Album#Studio album|collaborative studio album]], three [[compilation album]]s, four [[extended play]]s (EPs), 37 [[Single (music)|singles]] (including five as a featured artist), nine [[Promotional recording|promotional single]]s, and two [[Charity record|charity single]]s. Gomez's first solo studio album, ''[[Stars Dance]]'' (2013), debuted at number one in both the United States and Canada.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/biz/articles/news/chart-alert/5317207/selena-gomez-knocks-jay-z-from-top-of-billboard-200-with-first|title=Selena Gomez Knocks Jay Z From Top of Billboard 200 With First No. 1 Album|date=July 31, 2013|access-date=October 19, 2014|first=Keith|last=Caulfield|magazine=Billboard}}</ref><ref name=canalbums>{{cite magazine|url= https://www.billboard.com/artist/selena-gomez/chart-history/cna/|title=Selena Gomez – Chart History: Canadian Albums|magazine=Billboard|access-date=April 1, 2025}}</ref> Its lead single, "[[Come & Get It (Selena Gomez song)|Come & Get It]]", became a top-ten hit in the US, Canada, and the United Kingdom.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/artist/selena-gomez/chart-history/hsi/|title=Selena Gomez – Chart History: The Hot 100|magazine=Billboard|access-date=October 9, 2022}}<br>{{cite web|url=https://www.billboard.com/artist/selena-gomez/chart-history/can/|title=Selena Gomez – Chart History: Billboard Canadian Hot 100|magazine=Billboard|access-date=October 9, 2022}}<br>{{cite web | url=http://www.officialcharts.com/artist/5875/selena-gomez/ | title=Selena Gomez | publisher=[[Official Charts Company]] | access-date=October 9, 2022 | format=select "Albums" or "Singles"}}</ref> Having spent seven years with [[Hollywood Records]]—including her time with her former band  [[Selena Gomez & the Scene]]—the singer signed a recording contract with [[Interscope Records]] in 2014.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/6406139/selena-gomez-officially-signs-to-interscope-records|title=Selena Gomez Officially Signs With Interscope|magazine=Billboard|date=December 14, 2014|access-date=July 2, 2016|first=Mitchell|last=Peters}}</ref> To officially end her contract with Hollywood Records, she released the compilation album ''[[For You (Selena Gomez album)|For You]]'' (2014), which featured the US and Canada top-ten single "[[The Heart Wants What It Wants]]".<ref name=hot100/>

The singer's second studio album, ''[[Revival (Selena Gomez album)|Revival]]'' (2015), debuted atop the US [[Billboard 200|''Billboard'' 200]], and charted within the top ten of many territories.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/selena-gomez-scores-her-second-no-1-album-on-billboard-200-chart-revival/|title=Selena Gomez Scores Her Second No. 1 Album on Billboard 200 Chart|magazine=Billboard|date=October 18, 2015|access-date=July 6, 2016|first=Keith|last=Caulfield}}</ref> It spawned three top-ten hits in the US—"[[Good for You (Selena Gomez song)|Good for You]]", "[[Same Old Love]]", and "[[Hands to Myself]]"—all of which topped the [[Pop Airplay]] chart, making Gomez the sixth woman to score at least three number-one singles on that chart from a single set.<ref name=hot100/><ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/selena-gomez-third-pop-songs-no-1-hands-to-myself/|title=Selena Gomez Scores Third Pop Songs No. 1 With 'Hands to Myself' & Releases New Single From 'Revival'|magazine=Billboard|date=April 4, 2016|access-date=July 28, 2017|first=Trust|last=Gary}}</ref> Between 2016 and 2019, Gomez released a series of successful standalone singles and collaborations, notably featured on [[Charlie Puth]]'s global hit "[[We Don't Talk Anymore (Charlie Puth song)|We Don't Talk Anymore]]", which topped the charts in Italy and reached the top ten in the US, Australia, and France.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/artist/selena-gomez/chart-history/hsi/|title=Selena Gomez – Chart History: The Hot 100|magazine=Billboard|access-date=October 27, 2021}}<br>{{cite web|url=http://australian-charts.com/showinterpret.asp?interpret=Selena+Gomez|title=Discography Selena Gomez|publisher=ARIA Charts Portal.
