American singer [[Jennifer Lopez]] has released nine [[studio album]]s, one [[remix album]], three [[compilation album]]s, two [[soundtrack]], one [[extended play]], 67 [[single (music)|singles]] (including 14 as a featured artist), five [[charity record|charity singles]] and 13 [[promotional recording|promotional singles]]. As of 2022, the singer has sold more than 80 million records with 15 billion streams worldwide.<ref>{{cite web |last1=Andrews-Dyer |first1=Helena |title=Jennifer Lopez is an icon. In 'Halftime,' she still has something to prove |url=https://www.washingtonpost.com/arts-entertainment/2022/06/16/jennifer-lopez-halftime-documentary/ |website=[[CNN]] |access-date=June 16, 2022 |date=June 16, 2022 |archive-date=June 16, 2022 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220616132708/https://www.washingtonpost.com/arts-entertainment/2022/06/16/jennifer-lopez-halftime-documentary/ |url-status=live }}</ref> She made her chart debut in May 1999 with "[[If You Had My Love]]", which topped the charts in six countries, including the United States. It was followed by the release of her debut studio album, ''[[On the 6]]'' (1999), which reached the top five in several countries and produced four additional singles, including the international hit "[[Waiting for Tonight]]". The simultaneous release of her second studio album, ''[[J.Lo (album)|J.Lo]]'', and the film ''[[The Wedding Planner]]'' in January 2001 made Lopez the first entertainer to have a number-one film and album in the United States in the same week.<ref>{{cite web|last=vanHorn|first=Teri|title=Jennifer Lopez Tops With Moviegoers, Music Buyers|url=http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1438890/jennifer-lopez-tops-with-moviegoers-music-buyers.jhtml|publisher=MTV News|date=January 30, 2001|access-date=April 13, 2013|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140103064208/http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1438890/jennifer-lopez-tops-with-moviegoers-music-buyers.jhtml|archive-date=January 3, 2014}}</ref><ref name="timeTyrangiel">{{cite magazine|url=http://www.time.com/time/nation/article/0,8599,1093638,00.html |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090301064251/http://www.time.com/time/nation/article/0%2C8599%2C1093638%2C00.html |archive-date=March 1, 2009 |title=Jennifer Lopez |author=Josh Tyrangiel |magazine=Time |date=August 13, 2005 |access-date=January 13, 2007 |url-status=dead |author-link=Josh Tyrangiel }}</ref> The album was certified [[Recording Industry Association of America|four-times platinum in the United States]] and yielded four hit singles, including "[[Love Don't Cost a Thing (song)|Love Don't Cost a Thing]]" and "[[I'm Real (Jennifer Lopez song)|I'm Real]]", which peaked at number one on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]]. The following year saw the release of Lopez's remix album, ''[[J to tha L–O! The Remixes]]'' (2002), which became the first remix album in history to debut at number one on the [[Billboard 200|''Billboard'' 200]], and went on to become one of the best selling remix albums of all time.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://news.bbc.co.uk/1/hi/entertainment/3186761.stm |title=Diaz tops actress pay list |access-date=April 18, 2009 |date=August 24, 2004 |work=[[BBC News]] |archive-date=September 4, 2010 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100904060343/http://news.bbc.co.uk/2/hi/entertainment/3186761.stm |url-status=live }}</ref> Three singles were released from the album, including "[[Ain't It Funny (Murder Remix)]]", which topped the ''Billboard'' Hot 100 for six consecutive weeks.

Lopez's third studio album, ''[[This Is Me... Then]]'' (2002), reached number two on the ''Billboard'' 200, and earned double-platinum status in the United States. Four singles were released from the album, including the hits "[[Jenny from the Block]]" and "[[All I Have (song)|All I Have]]", which topped the charts in the United States and New Zealand. Her fourth studio album, ''[[Rebirth (Jennifer Lopez album)|Rebirth]]'' (2005), charted moderately in the United States, eventually earning a [[Recording Industry Association of America|platinum certification]]. It produced two singles, including its lead single, "[[Get Right]]", which went number one in five countries. Lopez's first full-length Spanish album, ''[[Como Ama una Mujer]]'', was released in March 2007 and peaked at number ten on the US ''Billboard'' 200. Two singles were released from the album, most notably "[[Qué Hiciste]]", which was an international success and was certified [[Productores de Música de España|eight-times platinum in Spain]]. A little over six months later, the singer released her sixth studio album, ''[[Brave (Jennifer Lopez album)|Brave]]'' (2007), which became her first album to miss the top ten on the ''Billboard'' 200. Just like the album, its lead single, "[[Do It Well]]", was only a moderate success.

Following a move to [[Island Records]] from [[Sony Music]], Lopez released her seventh studio album, ''[[Love?]]'', in May 2011, which was a moderately successful, but brief, comeback for her. Three singles were released from the album, including "[[On the Floor]]", which became the singer's most successful single in her career. The song topped more than eighteen national charts and sold more than 8.4 million digital copies globally, making it the best-selling single of 2011 by a female artist.<ref>{{cite web |title=Bruno Mars tops 2011 worldwide digital music chart |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/newsbeat-16687378 |website=[[Newsbeat]] |access-date=October 23, 2019 |date=January 24, 2012 |archive-date=October 23, 2019 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20191023233459/http://www.bbc.co.uk/newsbeat/article/16687378/bruno-mars-tops-2011-worldwide-digital-music-chart |url-status=live }}</ref> Lopez's first greatest hits album, ''[[Dance Again... the Hits]]'' (2012), followed a year later and produced the international hit "[[Dance Again]]". Her eighth studio album, ''[[A.K.A. (album)|A.K.A.]]'', was released in June 2014 by [[Capitol Records]] and became her lowest-selling album in the US. Three singles were released from the album, including "[[Booty (Jennifer Lopez song)|Booty]]", which reached the top 20 in Canada and the United States. After ''A.K.A.'s'' underperformance, Lopez then departed from the label and returned to [[Epic Records]]. Instead of releasing studio albums, she released several standalone singles such as "[[Ain't Your Mama]]", "[[Dinero (Jennifer Lopez song)|Dinero]]" and "[[Pa' Ti + Lonely|Pa' Ti]]". In 2022, in collaboration with [[Maluma]], she released a [[Marry Me (soundtrack)|movie soundtrack]]. In 2024, she released a sequel album to ''This Is Me... Then'' entitled ''[[This Is Me... Now]]'', her first studio album in a decade.
