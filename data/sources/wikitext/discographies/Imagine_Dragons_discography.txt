The American [[pop rock]] band [[Imagine Dragons]] has released six [[studio album]]s, five [[live album]]s, one [[compilation album]], twelve [[extended play]]s, forty-three [[Single (music)|singles]], eight [[promotional single]]s and fifty [[music video]]s. According to [[Recording Industry Association of America]], they have sold 111.5 million digital singles and 16 million albums in the US.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Searchable Database - RIAA|url=https://www.riaa.com/gold-platinum/|access-date=October 17, 2021|website=RIAA|language=en-US}}</ref> Imagine Dragons has sold 46 million albums and 55 million singles worldwide, including 74 billion global career streams, making them among the [[List of best-selling music artists|best-selling rock bands in history]].<ref>{{Cite web|last=Center|first=STAPLES|title=Multi Platinum, Grammy Award Winning Band Imagine Dragons Announce 2022 Mercury Tour {{!}} Staples Center|url=https://www.staplescenter.com/news/detail/multi-platinum-grammy-award-winning-band-imagine-dragons-announce-2022-mercury-tour|access-date=October 17, 2021|website=www.staplescenter.com|language=en}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|date=September 3, 2021|title=Imagine Dragons' Dan Reynolds on Mercury - Act 1 and Embracing Vulnerability|url=https://www.pastemagazine.com/article/imagine-dragons-mercuryact-1-interview|access-date=October 17, 2021|website=Paste|language=en}}</ref> ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' named them as the 29th Greatest Adult Alternative artist of all time,<ref>{{Cite magazine|title=Imagine Dragons|url=https://www.billboard.com/music/imagine-dragons|access-date=October 17, 2021|magazine=Billboard}}</ref> as well as the 13th Top Artist of 2010s in the United States.<ref>{{Cite magazine|title=Top Artists - Decade-End|url=https://www.billboard.com/charts/decade-end/top-artists|access-date=October 17, 2021|magazine=Billboard}}</ref>

Imagine Dragons released their first extended play, ''Speak to Me'' in February 2009,<ref>{{Cite web |title=Velour Calendar |url=https://www.velourlive.com/calendar/view_entry.php?id=1016&date=20090221 |access-date=2025-10-19 |website=www.velourlive.com|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230630190805/https://www.velourlive.com/calendar/month.php?date=20090201|archive-date=30 June 2023}}</ref> only to break up shortly after. Following a lineup change, the band reformed and a few months later would release their [[Imagine Dragons (EP)|self-titled EP]] in August 2009. After teasing new songs periodically on MySpace, their third EP ''[[Hell and Silence]]'' released in March 2010. While touring ''Hell and Silence'', the band wrote new material on the road and would debut new songs at live shows with the mentality of prioritizing songs that received the most positive feedback. Their fourth EP ''[[It's Time (EP)|It's Time]]'' released in March 2011, which drew the attention of [[Alex da Kid|Alex Da Kid]], who liked the band's material and offered to produce and sign them to his label [[Kidinakorner]]. Under their new label, the band found commercial success with the release of their fifth extended play, ''[[Continued Silence]]'', in February 2012. "[[It's Time (Imagine Dragons song)|It's Time]]" was released as the lead single from the EP, peaking at number fifteen on the United States [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]].<ref name="US-singles" /> It has since sold over 6 million copies in the United States. ''Continued Silence'' went on to peak at number 40 on the [[Billboard 200|''Billboard'' 200]] in the United States.<ref name="US-albums" /> As of August 31, 2021, Imagine Dragons are also one of the highest certified digital singles acts in the United States by the [[Recording Industry Association of America]] (RIAA), having been certified for 114.5 million digital singles in the country alone.<ref>{{Cite web|date=August 31, 2021|title=Gold & Platinum|url=https://www.riaa.com/gold-platinum/?advance_search=1&tab_active=awards_by_artist&format_option=singles&type=all#search_section|access-date=September 1, 2021|website=RIAA|publisher=[[Recording Industry Association of America]]|language=en-US}}</ref>

''[[Night Visions]]'', the band's debut studio album, was released in September 2012; it peaked at number two on the ''Billboard'' 200 and topped the ''Billboard'' [[Billboard charts|Top Alternative Albums]] and ''Billboard'' [[Billboard charts|Top Rock Albums]] charts.<ref name="US-albums"/><ref name="US-Alternative-albums"/> In addition to "It's Time", the album also contains the singles "[[Radioactive (Imagine Dragons song)|Radioactive]]" and "[[Demons (Imagine Dragons song)|Demons]]". The former single peaked at number three on the ''Billboard'' Hot 100 and has since sold over 8.6 million copies in the United States.<ref name="RUSSales">{{cite magazine |url=https://www.billboard.com/music/rock/imagine-dragons-radioactive-riaa-certified-diamond-6620412/ |title=Imagine Dragons' 'Radioactive' is now RIAA Certified Diamond |magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]] |date=July 8, 2015 |access-date=August 6, 2015}}</ref> It also became a top–ten hit in numerous countries, such as Australia, Germany and Sweden.<ref name="AUS"/><ref name="GER"/><ref name="SWE">{{cite web |url=http://swedishcharts.com/showinterpret.asp?interpret=Imagine+Dragons |title=Discography Imagine Dragons |work=swedishcharts.com |publisher=Hung Medien |access-date=January 18, 2013}}</ref> "Demons" has also been certified Diamond by the RIAA and peaked at number six on the ''Billboard'' Hot 100.<ref name="US-singles"/><ref name="USSales">{{cite web|url=https://music.yahoo.com/photos/top-selling-rock-songs-slideshow/ |title=The 15 Most Downloaded Songs in Rock History |author=Christopher Polk |publisher=[[Yahoo! Music]] |date=January 31, 2015 |access-date=August 6, 2015 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150204045408/https://music.yahoo.com/photos/top-selling-rock-songs-slideshow/ |archive-date=February 4, 2015}}</ref>

The band's second studio album, ''[[Smoke + Mirrors]]'', was released in February 2015, debuting at the top of the ''Billboard'' 200. The album was preceded by 3 singles: "[[I Bet My Life]]", which peaked in the top 40 of the ''Billboard'' Hot 100 at number 28, "[[Gold (Imagine Dragons song)|Gold]]", and "[[Shots (Imagine Dragons song)|Shots]]". "I Bet My Life" and "Shots" both peaked within the top 10 on ''Billboard''{{'}}s Hot Rock and Alternative chart.

The band followed this with their third studio album, ''[[Evolve (Imagine Dragons album)|Evolve]]'', in June 2017. The record was preceded by two singles, "[[Believer (Imagine Dragons song)|Believer]]" and [[Thunder (Imagine Dragons song)|Thunder]]", which both went on to peak at number four on the ''Billboard'' Hot 100. "Believer" was later certified diamond by the RIAA. They were then followed by a third single, "[[Whatever It Takes (Imagine Dragons song)|Whatever It Takes]]", which reached number 12. All three songs have peaked at number one on the [[Hot Rock Songs]] chart. After the ''Evolve'' tour was completed, they released their fourth studio album ''[[Origins (Imagine Dragons album)|Origins]]'' on November 9, 2018. Five singles were released from the album: "[[Natural (Imagine Dragons song)|Natural]]", "[[Zero (Imagine Dragons song)|Zero]]", "[[Machine (Imagine Dragons song)|Machine]]", "[[Bad Liar (Imagine Dragons song)|Bad Liar]]", and "[[Birds (Imagine Dragons song)|Birds]]" featuring [[Elisa (Italian singer)|Elisa]].

After taking a break of more than two years, the band announced their fifth studio album, ''[[Mercury – Act 1]]'', which was released on September 3, 2021. The album was preceded by three singles: "[[Follow You (Imagine Dragons song)|Follow You]]", "[[Cutthroat (song)|Cutthroat]]", and "Wrecked". "Follow You" peaked at number 7 on the Hot Rock and Alternative chart and number one on Rock and Alternative Airplay, and also received an alternate version titled "Follow You (Summer '21)".
