The [[discography]] of [[Bastille (band)|Bastille]], a British [[indie pop]] band, consists of five [[studio album]]s, one [[live album]], twelve [[extended play]]s, thirty-three [[Single (music)|singles]] and thirty-one [[music video]]s. Originally a solo project by Dan Smith, Bastille was formed in 2010.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.allmusic.com/artist/mn0002825814|title=Bastille – Biography|last=Buchanan|first=John D.|publisher=[[AllMusic]]|accessdate=31 March 2013}}</ref> The band released its debut single "Flaws" / "Icarus" in July 2011 on the [[independent record label]] Young and Lost Club. An extended play titled ''Laura Palmer'' followed later that year. The critical success of the EP and extensive touring brought the group to the attention of [[Virgin Records]], which signed them in December 2011.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.emimusic.com/blog/2011/virgin-records-welcomes-new-signings-bastille-and-eric-prydz/# |title=Virgin Records welcomes new signings Bastille and Eric Prydz |publisher=[[Virgin Records]]. [[EMI]] |date=1 December 2011 |accessdate=31 March 2013 |archivedate=29 April 2013 |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20130429173649/http://www.emimusic.com/blog/2011/virgin-records-welcomes-new-signings-bastille-and-eric-prydz/ |url-status=dead }}</ref>

Bastille released their debut studio album, ''[[Bad Blood (Bastille album)|Bad Blood]]'', in March 2013. The album reached number one on the [[UK Albums Chart|United Kingdom]] albums chart and the top ten of the [[Irish Albums Chart|Irish]] albums chart.<ref name="UK-albums"/><ref name="IRL"/> It has been [[List of music recording certifications|certified]] triple platinum by the [[British Phonographic Industry]] (BPI) and platinum by the Recording Industry Association of America (RIAA).<ref name="BPI"/><ref name="RIAA"/> The band released six singles from the album: "[[Overjoyed (Bastille song)|Overjoyed]]", "[[Bad Blood (Bastille song)|Bad Blood]]", "[[Flaws (song)|Flaws]]", "[[Pompeii (Bastille song)|Pompeii]]", "[[Laura Palmer (song)|Laura Palmer]]", and "[[Things We Lost in the Fire (song)|Things We Lost in the Fire]]", with "Pompeii" peaking at number two on the [[UK Singles Chart]] and topping the charts in Ireland and Scotland.<ref name="IRL"/><ref name="UK-singles"/> "Pompeii" reached number five on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] and has been certified 6× Platinum by the [[Recording Industry Association of America|RIAA]].<ref name="RIAA"/>

In November 2013, Bastille released a re-issue album ''All This Bad Blood'', which features songs from the extended edition of ''Bad Blood'' and their mixtapes, as well as two new songs. The band released two singles from the album: "[[Oblivion (Bastille song)|Oblivion]]" and "[[Of the Night]]", a mash-up of the songs "[[The Rhythm of the Night (song)|The Rhythm of the Night]]" by [[Corona (group)|Corona]] and "[[Rhythm Is a Dancer]]" by [[Snap!]].

On 8 December 2014, Bastille released their third mixtape, ''[[VS. (Other People's Heartache, Pt. III)]]'', which included collaborations with [[Haim (band)|Haim]] ("Bite Down"), [[Angel Haze]] ("Weapon"), [[MNEK]] ("bad_news"), [[Grades (record producer)|Grades]] ("Torn Apart"), and [[Rag'n'Bone Man]] and [[Skunk Anansie]] ("Remains").<ref>{{cite web|url=https://genius.com/albums/Bastille/Vs-other-peoples-heartache-pt-iii|title=VS (Other People's Heartache, pt. III) Bastille|website=genius.com|access-date=8 December 2014}}</ref>

On 16 June 2016 "[[Good Grief (song)|Good Grief]]" was released and went on to reach number 13 on the UK Singles Chart and is certified Platinum.<ref name="BPI"/> The album, ''[[Wild World (Bastille album)|Wild World]]'', was released on 9 September and reached number one on the UK Albums Chart for two consecutive weeks.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/bastille-are-kings-of-the-official-albums-chart-for-a-second-week-with-wild-world__16434/|title=Bastille are kings of the Official Albums Chart for a second week with Wild World|publisher=Official Charts Company|accessdate=23 September 2016}}</ref> It reached number two in Belgium and Ireland, and top 5 in Austria, Switzerland and US.<ref name="BEL-FL"/><ref name="IRL"/><ref name="AUT"/><ref name="SWI"/><ref name="US-albums"/> Three more singles were released from the album: "[[Send Them Off!]]", "[[Blame (Bastille song)|Blame]]" and "[[Glory (Bastille song)|Glory]]".

"[[I Know You (Craig David song)|I Know You]]", [[Craig David]] featuring [[Bastille (band)|Bastille]], was released as the second single from David's seventh album, ''[[The Time Is Now (album)|The Time Is Now]]'' on 23 November 2017. It reached number 5 on the UK Singles Chart and number 2 in Scotland.<ref name="UK-singles"/><ref name="SCO-singles"/>

"[[Happier (Marshmello and Bastille song)|Happier]]", a collaboration with [[Marshmello]], was released on 17 August 2018. It reached number two on the UK Singles Chart, tying with "Pompeii" and "Of the Night", and number two in the US, eclipsing "Pompeii".<ref name="UK-singles"/><ref name="US-singles"/> It reached the top 5 in Australia, Ireland and Sweden amongst others.<ref name="AUS"/><ref name="IRL"/><ref name="SWE"/> It is certified double platinum by the [[British Phonographic Industry|BPI]], and six-times platinum by the [[Recording Industry Association of America|RIAA]] and the [[Australian Recording Industry Association]] (ARIA).<ref name="BPI"/><ref name="RIAA"/><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.aria.com.au/pages/httpwww.aria.com.aupagesSinglesAccreds2019.htm|title=ARIA Charts - Accreditations - 2019 Singles|website=ARIA.com|accessdate=27 July 2020}}</ref>

On 7 December 2018, Bastille released their fourth mixtape, ''[[Other People's Heartache#Part four|Other People's Heartache, Pt. 4]]'', which included collaborations with: Kianja ("[[Wild World (song)|Wild World]] (Intro)"); Kianja, [[Craig David]] and [[S-X (producer)|S-X]] ("[[Would I Lie to You? (Charles & Eddie song)|Would I Lie to You?]]"); [[Seeb (music producers)|Seeb]] ("[[Grip (song)|Grip]]"); Kianja, [[Craig David]] and Swarmz ("[[Don't Let Go (Love)]]"); [[Rationale (musician)|Rationale]], [[James Arthur]] ("[[Flowers (Sweet Female Attitude song)|Flowers]]"); Lily Moore, Moss Kena and [[Jacob Banks]] ("The Descent"); and Moss Kena ("Warmth (Outro)").<ref>{{cite web|url=https://genius.com/albums/Bastille/Other-people-s-heartache-pt-4|title=Other People's Heartache pt 4|website=genius.com|accessdate=27 July 2020}}</ref>

The band's third studio album, ''[[Doom Days]]'', was released on 14 June 2019, preceded by four singles: "[[Quarter Past Midnight]]", "[[Doom Days (song)|Doom Days]]", "[[Joy (Bastille song)|Joy]]" and "[[Those Nights]]". The album reached top 5 in both the UK and USA.<ref name="UK-albums"/><ref name="US-albums"/> The album was re-issued as an extended album, ''Doom Days (This Got Out of Hand)'', on 6 December 2019.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.iheart.com/content/2019-12-05-bastille-is-releasing-a-deluxe-version-of-doom-days-with-11-bonus-tracks/|title=Bastille Is Releasing A Deluxe Version Of 'Doom Days' With 11 Bonus Tracks|website=[[iHeartRadio]]|accessdate=5 December 2019}}</ref> Two singles have been released since, a duet with Canadian singer [[Alessia Cara]] of their song "[[Another Place (Bastille and Alessia Cara song)|Another Place]]" and "[[Can't Fight This Feeling]]", a cover of the [[REO Speedwagon]] song, with the latter becoming the band's ninth UK top 40 single.<ref name="UK-singles"/>

In July 2024, Bastille announced a new four-part project title ''[["&"]]''. The first part of the project was released in full on 24 October 2024.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Richards |first=Will |date=2024-07-26 |title=Bastille announce new four-part album '&', share Part One |url=https://www.rollingstone.co.uk/music/bastille-announce-new-four-part-album-share-part-one-41832/ |access-date=2024-11-13 |website=Rolling Stone UK |language=en-GB}}</ref>
