The discography of American [[indie pop]] band [[Fun (band)|Fun]] consists of two [[studio album]]s, seven [[extended play]]s, 11 [[Single (music)|single]]s and seven [[music video]]s. Following the split of his previous band [[The Format]], lead singer [[Nate Ruess]] formed Fun in 2008 with musicians [[Andrew Dost]] and [[Jack Antonoff]], both of whom had previously toured with The Format.<ref>{{cite journal |url=https://www.spin.com/2008/08/members-format-steel-train-anathallo-unite-fun// |title=Members of the Format, Steel Train, Anathallo Unite as Fun |journal=[[Spin (magazine)|Spin]] |publisher=[[Spin Media]] |date=August 20, 2008 |accessdate=May 16, 2014 |author=Kiser, Matt}}</ref> Fun began recording sessions for their debut album in September 2008 and embarked on a North American tour the following month.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.jambase.com/Articles/14910/Steel-Train-Tour.New-Band |title=Members Of The Format, Anathallo & Steel Train Form New Band, Fun |publisher=JamBase |accessdate=May 16, 2014}}</ref> In May 2009, they issued their debut single, "At Least I'm Not as Sad (As I Used to Be)".<ref name="AtLeast"/> ''[[Aim and Ignite]]'', the band's first full-length album, was released by [[Nettwerk|Nettwerk Records]] on August 25, 2009, and peaked at number 71 on the United States [[Billboard 200|''Billboard'' 200]] chart.<ref name="AimAndIgnite"/><ref name="US-albums"/> The album produced an additional two singles, "All the Pretty Girls" and "Walking the Dog", which were then followed by the release of the single "C'mon", a collaboration with American [[alternative rock]] band [[Panic! at the Disco]].

The band's single "[[We Are Young]]", featuring American R&B singer [[Janelle Monáe]], was released in September 2011, beginning a steady rise in popularity after gaining attention on online media and being covered on the television series ''[[Glee (TV series)|Glee]]''.<ref name="Cover">{{cite magazine |url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/502945/fun-the-billboard-cover-story |title=Fun.: The Billboard Cover Story |magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]] |publisher=[[Prometheus Global Media]] |date=March 2, 2012 |accessdate=May 16, 2014 |author=Zemler, Emily}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.musicradar.com/news/guitars/interview-fun-s-nate-ruess-on-writing-and-recording-we-are-young-536774/ |title=Interview: Fun.'s Nate Ruess on writing and recording We Are Young |publisher=[[MusicRadar]] |date=March 27, 2012 |accessdate=May 16, 2014 |author=Bosso, Joe}}</ref> It debuted on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] in December, becoming the band's first single to enter a national record chart.<ref name="Chevy">{{cite magazine |url=http://www.billboard.com/biz/articles/news/branding/1099054/how-funs-we-are-young-scored-chevys-stunt-anthem-super-bowl-spot |title=How fun.'s 'We Are Young' Scored Chevy's 'Stunt Anthem' Super Bowl Spot |magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]] |publisher=[[Prometheus Global Media]] |date=February 5, 2012 |accessdate=May 16, 2014 |author=Hampp, Andrew}}</ref> The song's use in a [[Chevrolet Sonic (2012)|Chevrolet Sonic]] [[Super Bowl]] commercial further increased sales of the song,<ref name="Chevy"/> and it eventually topped the Hot 100 and hit the top ten in multiple other countries,<ref name="US-singles"/> including Canada,<ref name="CAN-singles"/> Ireland,<ref name="IRL-singles"/> and the United Kingdom.<ref name="UK-singles"/> It has become one of the [[List of best-selling singles|best-selling singles of all-time]], with sales of over 9.3 million copies.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.ifpi.org/content/library/DMR2013.pdf |title=Digital Music Report 2013 |publisher=[[International Federation of the Phonographic Industry]] |date=February 26, 2013 |accessdate=February 26, 2013 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130927190016/http://www.ifpi.org/content/library/dmr2013.pdf |archive-date=September 27, 2013 |url-status=dead }}</ref> The band's second studio album, ''[[Some Nights (album)|Some Nights]]'', was released by [[Fueled by Ramen]] on February 21, 2012; it debuted at number three on the ''Billboard'' 200 and received a platinum certification from the [[Recording Industry Association of America]] (RIAA).<ref name="US-albums"/><ref name="RIAA"/> [[Some Nights (song)|The album's title track]] was released as its second single and peaked at number three on the Hot 100, while also reaching the top ten on other national singles charts.<ref name="US-singles"/> "[[Carry On (Fun song)|Carry On]]", "Why Am I the One", and "All Alone" followed as the next three singles from ''Some Nights''.
