The English–Irish [[boy band]] [[One Direction]] have released five [[studio album]]s, ten [[extended play]]s, seventeen [[Single (music)|single]]s (including two [[charity single]]s), two [[video album]]s, and seventeen [[music video]]s. They signed with [[Simon Cowell]]'s record label [[Syco Music|Syco Records]] after being formed and finishing third in the [[The X Factor (British TV series) series 7|seventh series]] of British television singing competition ''[[The X Factor (British TV series)|The X Factor]]'' in 2010.<ref name="Nash">{{cite news|author=Victoria Nash|url=https://www.expressandstar.com/news/2010/12/13/x-factors-one-direction-sign-1m-deal/|title=X Factor's One Direction sign £1m deal|work=[[Express & Star]]|date=13 December 2010|access-date=20 October 2012|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131029195440/http://www.expressandstar.com/news/2010/12/13/x-factors-one-direction-sign-1m-deal/|archive-date=29 October 2013|url-status=live}}</ref> They subsequently signed in North America with [[Columbia Records]].<ref>{{cite news|author=James C. McKinley Jr|url=https://www.nytimes.com/2012/03/24/arts/music/one-direction-and-the-wanted-boy-bands-return.html?_r=2|title=Boy Bands Are Back, Wholesome or Sexy|work=[[The New York Times]]|date=23 March 2012|access-date=3 October 2012|location=United States|archive-date=22 May 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130522205414/http://www.nytimes.com/2012/03/24/arts/music/one-direction-and-the-wanted-boy-bands-return.html?_r=2|url-status=live}}</ref> ''[[One Direction: This Is Us]]'', a 3D [[Documentary film|documentary]] [[concert film]] was released on 29 August 2013 in the United Kingdom and 30 August 2013 in the United States.<ref>One Direction: This Is Us, release date: 29–30 August 2013. Released by Syco Entertainment and directed by [[Morgan Spurlock]]</ref> The film captures the band on the road during the [[Take Me Home Tour (One Direction)|Take Me Home Tour]] and documents their origins and rise to fame.

The group's debut studio album ''[[Up All Night (One Direction album)|Up All Night]]'' was released on 18 November 2011. It topped the charts in sixteen countries. The lead single, "[[What Makes You Beautiful]]", was an international hit, reaching number one on the [[UK Singles Chart]] and number four on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]]; it has since been certified four and six times platinum in the US and Australia, respectively. Subsequent singles, "[[Gotta Be You (One Direction song)|Gotta Be You]]" and "[[One Thing (One Direction song)|One Thing]]", became top ten hits in the UK. The band's second studio album ''[[Take Me Home (One Direction album)|Take Me Home]]'' was released 9 November 2012.<ref name="TMH">{{cite One Direction's third studio album, ''[[Midnight Memories]]'', was released on 25 November 2013. The album was preceded by its lead single "[[Best Song Ever (song)|Best Song Ever]]" and its critically acclaimed second single "[[Story of My Life (One Direction song)|Story of My Life]]". The album was a commercial success, debuting at number one on the UK Albums Chart and the ''Billboard'' 200, making One Direction the first band in history to have their first three albums all debut atop the ''Billboard'' 200. With their fourth album, ''[[Four (One Direction album)|Four]]'', released on 17 November 2014, they extended the record to four consecutive number-one albums. Their next album, ''[[Made in the A.M.]] was'' released on 13 November 2015 & was preceded by its lead single "[[Drag Me Down]]".<ref>{{cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/6649246/one-direction-shares-first-track-without-zayn-malik-drag-me-down|title=One Direction Shares First Track Without Zayn Malik and Barak bii 'Drag Me Down'|magazine=Billboard|date=31 July 2015|access-date=31 July 2015|location=U.S.|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150801044819/http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/6649246/one-direction-shares-first-track-without-zayn-malik-drag-me-down|archive-date=1 August 2015|url-status=live}}</ref> In October 2015, the band announced the second single from the album, "[[Perfect (One Direction song)|Perfect]]". As of May 2022, the band has sold more than 70 million<!--please don't use a record label or managemenet as source--> records worldwide and over 21 million in their home country.<ref>{{Cite news|url=https://www.bbc.com/news/entertainment-arts-52283104|title=One Direction are speaking about anniversary reunion, Liam Payne says|last=Savage|first=Mark|publisher=[[BBC News]]|date=14 April 2020|access-date=30 April 2020|archive-date=9 March 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210309141113/https://www.bbc.com/news/entertainment-arts-52283104|url-status=live}}</ref><ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/newsround/53447792|title=One Direction: Ten years of 1D, but is a reunion on the way?|date=23 July 2020|access-date=25 July 2020|archive-date=1 October 2020|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201001160423/https://www.bbc.co.uk/newsround/53447792|url-status=live}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.musicweek.com/analysis/read/charts-analysis-harry-styles-matches-one-direction-s-no-1-performance/085720|title=Charts analysis: Harry Styles matches One Direction's No.1 performance|website=[[MusicWeek]]|last=Jones|first=Alan|date=29 April 2022|access-date=11 May 2022|archive-date=11 May 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220511162837/https://www.musicweek.com/analysis/read/charts-analysis-harry-styles-matches-one-direction-s-no-1-performance/085720|url-status=live}}</ref> According to [[Recording Industry Association of America]], One Direction has sold 13.5 million digital singles and 12 million albums in the United States.
