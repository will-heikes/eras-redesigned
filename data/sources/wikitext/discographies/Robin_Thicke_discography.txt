The discography of American singer [[Robin Thicke]] consists of 8 [[studio album]]s, 30 [[Single (music)|singles]] and 23 [[music videos]]. Thicke signed his first recording contract with [[Interscope Records]] as a teenager and found success as a songwriter, before he began concentrating on his career as a performer.<ref name="AllMusic-biography">{{cite web |url=https://www.allmusic.com/artist/robin-thicke-mn0000238686/biography |title=Robin Thicke – Biography |work=[[AllMusic]] |access-date=September 25, 2011 |author=Kellman, Andy}}</ref> In 2000, Thicke started recording his debut album, ''[[A Beautiful World]]'', which was released in October 2002. The album debuted at number 152 on the US [[Billboard 200|''Billboard'' 200]],<ref name="US-albums"/> selling 119,000 copies.<ref name="ThickeSoul"/> The record also peaked at number 36 on the [[MegaCharts|Mega Album Top 100]] in the Netherlands.<ref name="NLD"/> Two singles were released from the album; the first, "[[When I Get You Alone]]" became a top ten hit in the Netherlands and New Zealand.<ref name="NLD"/><ref name="NZ"/> That single also peaked at number 17 in Australia and was [[Music recording sales certification|certified]] gold by the [[Australian Recording Industry Association]] (ARIA).<ref name="AUS"/><ref name="ARIA-2003-singles"/> In 2006, Thicke released his second studio album ''[[The Evolution of Robin Thicke]]''. The record debuted at number 5 on the US ''Billboard'' 200,<ref name="US-albums"/> topped it on the [[Billboard (magazine)|''Billboard'']]{{'s}} [[Top R&B/Hip-Hop Albums]],<ref name="US-R&B-albums"/> and became a [[RIAA certification|certified platinum]] by the [[Recording Industry Association of America]] (RIAA).<ref name="RIAA"/> The album also charted in France,<ref name="FRA"/> the Netherlands,<ref name="NLD"/> and the United Kingdom.<ref name="UK-albums"/> ''The Evolution of Robin Thicke'' produced four singles, all of which charted on the ''Billboard''{{'s}} [[Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs]].<ref name="US-R&B-singles">{{cite magazine |url={{BillboardURLbyName|artist=robin thicke|chart=R&B/Hip-Hop Songs}} |title=Robin Thicke – Chart History: R&B/Hip-Hop Songs |magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]] |access-date=January 3, 2014}}</ref> "[[Lost Without U]]" became one of his biggest hits, peaking at number 14 on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] and spending 11 weeks atop the ''Billboard''{{'s}} Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs.<ref name="US-singles"/><ref>{{cite magazine |url=https://www.billboard.com/music/rb-hip-hop/robin-thicke-reveals-blurred-lines-album-release-date-track-list-1567020/ |title=Robin Thicke Reveals 'Blurred Lines' Album Release Date & Track List |magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]] |date=June 14, 2013 |access-date=January 3, 2013 |author=Ramirez, Erika}}</ref>

Thicke's third studio album, ''[[Something Else (Robin Thicke album)|Something Else]]'' was released in September 2008. The album peaked at number 3 on both the US ''Billboard'' 200, and on the ''Billboard''{{'s}} Top R&B/Hip-Hop Albums charts.<ref name="US-albums"/><ref name="US-R&B-albums"/> The album's first two singles, "[[Magic (Robin Thicke song)|Magic]]" and "[[The Sweetest Love]]", both became top 20 hits on the ''Billboard''{{'s}} Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs.<ref name="US-R&B-singles"/> ''[[Sex Therapy: The Session]]'' was released in December 2009, peaking at numbers 9 and 2 on the US ''Billboard'' 200, and on the ''Billboard''{{'s}} Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Albums, respectively.<ref name="US-albums"/> Its [[Sex Therapy (song)|title track]], the first of four singles from the album, became Thicke's second number-one R&B hit.<ref name="US-singles"/> His fifth studio album, ''[[Love After War]]'' was released in December 2011, peaking at number 22 on the US ''Billboard'' 200, while producing two singles: "Love After War" and "[[Pretty Lil' Heart]]".<ref name="US-albums"/>

Thicke has attained his biggest commercial success to date with the releases of his sixth studio album ''[[Blurred Lines (album)|Blurred Lines]]'' and its respective singles. The album's [[Blurred Lines (song)|eponymous lead single]] was a worldwide hit, becoming Thicke's first ''Billboard'' Hot 100 number-one single,<ref name="US-singles"/> and also topping the charts in countries such as Australia, Canada, New Zealand and the United Kingdom.<ref name="NZ"/><ref name="AUS"/><ref name="CAN-singles"/><ref name="UK-singles"/> Aided by the single's success, the album debuted at number one on the ''Billboard'' 200 and produced the follow-up singles "[[Give It 2 U]]", "[[For the Rest of My Life]]" and "[[Feel Good (Robin Thicke song)|Feel Good]]".<ref name="US-albums"/>
