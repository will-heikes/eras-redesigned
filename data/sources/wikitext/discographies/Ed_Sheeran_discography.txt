The discography of English singer-songwriter [[Ed Sheeran]] consists of eight [[studio album]]s, two [[compilation album]]s, 17 [[extended play]]s, one [[video album]], 65 [[single (music)|singles]] (including 28 as [[guest appearance|a featured artist]]), eight [[promotional single]]s, one [[box set]], and 71 [[music video]]s. Sheeran has sold in excess of 200 million records worldwide, making him one of the [[list of best-selling music artists|best-selling music artists in history]].<ref>{{Cite web |date=1 December 2024 |title=Ed Sheeran announces tour of 6 Indian cities in 2025 |url=https://www.cnbctv18.com/travel/lifestyle/ed-sheeran-announces-tour-of-6-indian-cities-in-2025-19517542.htm |access-date=28 June 2026 |work=[[CNBC]]}}</ref> According to [[Recording Industry Association of America|RIAA]], Sheeran is the 15th best-selling digital singles artist in the United States with certified sales of 103 million.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Gold & Platinum |url=https://www.riaa.com/gold-platinum/ |access-date=7 July 2022 |publisher=[[Recording Industry Association of America]] |archive-date=10 November 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201110084738/https://www.riaa.com/gold-platinum/ |url-status=live}}</ref>

Originally an [[unsigned artist|indie artist]] selling music independently on his [[independent record label|own label]] beginning in 2005, Sheeran released nine EPs, steadily gaining public and critical acclaim, resulting in his signing to [[Atlantic Records]] in January 2011. Five months later, Sheeran released his debut single, "[[The A Team (Ed Sheeran song)|The A Team]]", on 12 June 2011. It debuted at No. 3 on the [[UK Singles Chart]] with first-week sales of 57,607 copies. The track attained international chart success, reaching the top ten in numerous countries including Australia and New Zealand, where it peaked at No. 2 and No. 3 respectively. The release was succeeded by a second single, "[[You Need Me, I Don't Need You]]", in August 2011, which peaked at No. 4 in the United Kingdom. Sheeran released his major label debut studio album, ''[[+ (album)|+]]'', on 9 September 2011. In its first week, ''+'' debuted at No. 1 on the [[UK Albums Chart]], selling over 100,000 copies in its first week and was certified 7× Platinum by the [[British Phonographic Industry]] for sales of 2.1 million copies. The album spawned a further three singles, including "[[Lego House]]", which reached the top five in Australia, New Zealand and the UK. "[[Drunk (Ed Sheeran song)|Drunk]]" was released in February 2012, becoming Sheeran's fourth consecutive top ten single when it peaked at No. 9. "[[Small Bump]]" was released in May 2012, peaking at No. 25 in the UK. The final single from ''+'', "[[Give Me Love (Ed Sheeran song)|Give Me Love]]", was released on 21 November 2012 and peaked at No. 18 in the UK.

In 2014, Sheeran released his second studio album, ''[[x (Ed Sheeran album)|×]]'' (Multiply), which peaked at No. 1 in the UK, Ireland, the US, Australia, New Zealand, Canada, Germany, Finland, Denmark, Switzerland, and Norway. It was the fastest-selling album of 2014 in the UK, shifting 180,000 copies in its first week of release.<ref>{{cite news |url=http://www.latimes.com/entertainment/music/posts/la-et-ms-ed-sheeran-x-is-the-fastest-selling-album-of-2014-20140630-story.html |title=Ed Sheeran's 'x' is the fastest-selling album of 2014 in UK |last=Easterhouse |first=Jim |date=30 June 2014 |work=[[Los Angeles Times]] |access-date=14 November 2015 |archive-date=14 November 2015 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151114011242/http://www.latimes.com/entertainment/music/posts/la-et-ms-ed-sheeran-x-is-the-fastest-selling-album-of-2014-20140630-story.html |url-status=live}}</ref> Five singles were released from the album: "[[Sing (Ed Sheeran song)|Sing]]", "[[Don't (Ed Sheeran song)|Don't]]", "[[Thinking Out Loud]]", "[[Bloodstream (Ed Sheeran song)#Ed Sheeran and Rudimental version|Bloodstream]]" (a collaboration with [[Rudimental]]), and "[[Photograph (Ed Sheeran song)|Photograph]]". ''×'' was also the best-selling album of the year in the UK, with almost 1.7 million copies sold.

Sheeran released two singles, "[[Shape of You]]", "[[Castle on the Hill]]", his first solo material in two years, on 6 January 2017. His third studio album, ''[[÷ (album)|÷]]'', followed on 3 March 2017. It debuted at No. 1 in 14 countries, including the UK, where it sold 672,000 units in its first week to become the fastest-selling album by a male solo artist there and the third-highest opening ever (behind [[Adele]]'s ''[[25 (Adele album)|25]]'' and [[Oasis (band)|Oasis]]' ''[[Be Here Now (album)|Be Here Now]]''). It also topped the charts in the United States, Canada, and Australia. All the tracks on the album reached the top 20 of the UK Singles Chart in the week of the album's release, due mainly to heavy streaming.<ref name="UKweek1">{{cite web |url=http://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/ed-sheeran-smashes-official-chart-records-as-is-the-fastest-selling-album-by-a-male-artist-ever__18447/ |title=Ed Sheeran's ÷ is the fastest-selling album by a male artist ever |publisher=[[Official Charts Company]] |last=Copsey |first=Rob |date=10 March 2017 |access-date=13 April 2017 |archive-date=28 April 2017 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170428075832/http://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/ed-sheeran-smashes-official-chart-records-as-is-the-fastest-selling-album-by-a-male-artist-ever__18447/ |url-status=live}}</ref> Sheeran also surpassed [[Calvin Harris]]' record of top 10 hits from one album.<ref>{{cite magazine |url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/7718220/calvin-harris-congratulates-ed-sheeran-uk-chart-record |title=Calvin Harris Congratulates Ed Sheeran for Breaking His UK Chart Record: 'I Love You But Also Fuck You' |magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]] |last=Frankel |first=Jillian |date=10 March 2017 |access-date=13 April 2017 |archive-date=22 April 2017 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170422000443/http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/7718220/calvin-harris-congratulates-ed-sheeran-uk-chart-record |url-status=live}}</ref> The next two singles, "[[Galway Girl (Ed Sheeran song)|Galway Girl]]" and "[[Perfect (Ed Sheeran song)|Perfect]]", both reached the summit in Ireland.

On 23 May 2019, Sheeran announced his fourth studio album, ''[[No.6 Collaborations Project]]'' through an Instagram post. The lead single, a collaboration with [[Justin Bieber]] titled "[[I Don't Care (Ed Sheeran and Justin Bieber song)|I Don't Care]]", was released on 10 May 2019. The album was released on 12 July 2019.

On 6 May 2021, it was announced that Sheeran became the new sponsor of the football shirt of Ipswich Town Football Club, and simultaneously revealed the title of his fifth studio album, ''[[= (album)|=]]''.<ref>{{Cite news |date=6 May 2021 |title=Ed Sheeran to sponsor Ipswich Town shirts |work=BBC News |url=https://www.bbc.com/news/uk-england-suffolk-57009557 |access-date=6 May 2021 |archive-date=6 May 2021 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210506120736/https://www.bbc.com/news/uk-england-suffolk-57009557 |url-status=live}}</ref> His sixth studio album, ''[[− (album)|−]]'', was highly anticipated and released in May 2023. This was followed by his seventh studio album, ''[[Autumn Variations]]'', in September 2023.

In August 2024, it was announced that Sheeran and [[Snow Patrol]] member [[Johnny McDaid]] would write and produce "[[Under the Tree (Ed Sheeran song)|Under the Tree]]", a song for the 2024 [[Netflix]] and [[Locksmith Animation]] film ''[[That Christmas]]''.

His eighth studio album, ''[[Play (Ed Sheeran album)|Play]]'', was released on 12 September 2025. It will be supported by the singles "[[Azizam (song)|Azizam]]", "[[Old Phone]]", and "[[Sapphire (song)|Sapphire]]". He contributed the [[F1 the Album|soundtrack]] of the 2025 sports drama film ''[[F1 (film)|F1]]'' with the single "[[Drive (Ed Sheeran song)|Drive]]".
