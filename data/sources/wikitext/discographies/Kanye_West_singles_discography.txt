{{Kanye West sidebar}}
American rapper [[Kanye West]] has released 140 [[single (music)|singles]] as a lead artist, 85 as a featured artist, and six promotional singles and charted with 65 other songs. As of July 2022, West is one of only seven artists in the United States to be certified for over 100 million digital singles by the [[Recording Industry Association of America]], having sold 133 million certified digital units as a solo artist.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Gold & Platinum|url=https://www.riaa.com/gold-platinum/|access-date=December 13, 2024|publisher=RIAA|language=en-US}}</ref> He is the second [[List of highest-certified music artists in the United States|highest certified American digital singles artist in the United States]], with 84 of his singles going platinum. His [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] history reads; 177 entries, 56 top 40s, 21 top 10s, and five number-one singles.<ref>{{cite magazine |title=Chart History - Kanye West - Hot 100 |url=https://www.billboard.com/artist/kanye-west/chart-history/hsi/ |magazine=Billboard |access-date=April 9, 2020}}</ref><ref>{{cite magazine |author1=Xander Zellner |title=Rihanna Ends Her Longest Break From the Hot 100 With PartyNextDoor's 'Believe It' |url=https://www.billboard.com/articles/business/chart-beat/9352607/rihanna-longest-break-hot-100-partynextdoor-believe-it |magazine=Billboard |access-date=April 9, 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200407135203/https://www.billboard.com/articles/business/chart-beat/9352607/rihanna-longest-break-hot-100-partynextdoor-believe-it |archive-date=April 7, 2020 |date=April 6, 2020}}</ref> Elsewhere, West has 22 [[List of artists with the most UK top-ten singles|top-ten]], and three number-one singles in the UK,<ref>{{cite web |title=Kanye West - Chart History |url=https://www.officialcharts.com/artist/14958/kanye-west/ |website=Official Charts |access-date=April 9, 2020}}</ref> as well as, six number ones in [[Official New Zealand Music Chart#Artists with the most number-one hits|New Zealand]]. West ended the 2000s decade with the most [[List of Billboard number-one rap singles of the 2000s|number-one songs]] on the [[Hot Rap Songs]] chart, most digital song certifications by a male solo artist in the United States,<ref>{{cite web |title=RIAA Tallies The Decade's Top Gold & Platinum Award Winners |url=http://www.riaa.com/newsitem.php?id=C91C40E1-A65A-0F81-EBB3-8FE3B7C0ECEA |website=RIAA |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100221175541/http://www.riaa.com/newsitem.php?id=C91C40E1-A65A-0F81-EBB3-8FE3B7C0ECEA |archive-date=February 21, 2010 |date=February 17, 2010 |url-status=dead}}</ref> and was tenth on ''Billboard''{{'}}s Top Hot 100 Artists of the 2000s chart.<ref>{{cite magazine |title=Hot 100 Artists - Decade End Charts |url=http://www.billboard.biz/bbbiz/charts/decadeendcharts/2009/hot-100-artists |magazine=Billboard |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121008004835/http://www.billboard.biz/bbbiz/charts/decadeendcharts/2009/hot-100-artists |archive-date=October 8, 2012 |date=December 29, 2009}}</ref> As of September 2012, he had the second-most 3 million+ selling digital songs.<ref>{{cite web |author1=Paul Grien |title=Week Ending Sept. 23, 2012. Songs: The Song That Is Sweeping The Globe |url=http://music.yahoo.com/blogs/chart-watch/week-ending-sept-23-2012-songs-song-sweeping-015634675.html |website=Yahoo |access-date=April 26, 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121025115345/http://music.yahoo.com/blogs/chart-watch/week-ending-sept-23-2012-songs-song-sweeping-015634675.html |archive-date=October 25, 2012 |date=September 26, 2012}}</ref>

In 2003, West collaborated with rapper [[Twista]] and singer [[Jamie Foxx]] on the song "[[Slow Jamz]]", which became West's first single to top the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]].<ref name="US-singles">{{cite magazine |url=https://www.billboard.com/artist/kanye-west/chart-history/hsi/ |title=Kanye West – Chart History: Hot 100 |magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]] |access-date=April 7, 2026}}</ref> In 2004, West's debut album ''[[The College Dropout]]'' produced four [[Single (music)|single]]s, including the Hot 100 top ten single "[[All Falls Down]]" and the [[Grammy Award]]-winner "[[Jesus Walks (Kanye West song)|Jesus Walks]]". In 2005, West released his second studio album ''[[Late Registration]]'', which featured five singles, including "[[Touch the Sky (Kanye West song)|Touch the Sky]]", "[[Heard 'Em Say]]" and "[[Gold Digger (Kanye West song)|Gold Digger]]", the latter spent ten weeks at number one on the ''Billboard'' Hot 100, broke digital download records, and was the ninth biggest Hot 100 hit of the 2000s decade.<ref>{{cite magazine |title=West Lives Larges |url=https://books.google.com/books?id=nxMEAAAAMBAJ&q=gold+digger+digital+downloads+2005&pg=PA45 |magazine=Billboard |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200426030217/https://books.google.co.uk/books?id=nxMEAAAAMBAJ&pg=PA45&lpg=PA45&dq=gold+digger+digital+downloads+2005&source=bl&ots=fnOiCMLsdQ&sig=ACfU3U0dzYPIAOK_7J3hNWi5eKf7nXMe7w&hl=en&sa=X&ved=2ahUKEwjdm8DXjIXpAhUPesAKHa5FBPU4ChDoATAFegQIChAB |archive-date=April 26, 2020 |date=September 17, 2005}}</ref><ref>{{cite magazine |author1=Ellias Leight |title=The Top 20 Billboard Hot 100 Hits of the 2000s |url=https://www.billboard.com/articles/news/6304098/billboard-hot-100-hits-2000s |magazine=Billboard |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150324040754/https://www.billboard.com/articles/news/6304098/billboard-hot-100-hits-2000s |archive-date=March 24, 2015 |date=October 31, 2014}}</ref>

In 2007, West's third studio album ''[[Graduation (album)|Graduation]]'' was released and was supported by five singles, including the ''Billboard'' Hot 100 number one single "[[Stronger (Kanye West song)|Stronger]]" and the top ten single "[[Good Life (Kanye West song)|Good Life]]".<ref name="US-singles"/> In 2008, West's fourth album ''[[808s & Heartbreak]]'' was released and featured two top three singles, "[[Love Lockdown]]" and "[[Heartless (Kanye West song)|Heartless]]". The former became just the second song by a non-American Idol artist to debut in the top three of the Hot 100 of the decade.<ref>{{cite magazine |author1=Fred Bronson |title=Love is in the Air |url=http://www.billboard.com/bbcom/chart_beat/bonus_display.jsp?vnu_content_id=1003855128 |magazine=Billboard |access-date=April 26, 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080926105007/http://www.billboard.com/bbcom/chart_beat/bonus_display.jsp?vnu_content_id=1003855128 |archive-date=September 26, 2008 |date=September 25, 2008}}</ref> From 2008 to 2009, West collaborated with other recording artists on multiple Hot 100 top ten singles, including "[[American Boy]]" by singer [[Estelle (musician)|Estelle]], "[[Swagga Like Us]]" by rappers [[Jay-Z]], [[Lil Wayne]] and [[T.I.]], "[[Knock You Down]]" by singer [[Keri Hilson]], "[[Run This Town]]" by Jay-Z and singer [[Rihanna]], and "[[Forever (Drake song)|Forever]]" with rappers [[Drake (musician)|Drake]], Lil Wayne and [[Eminem]].

In 2010, West released his fifth studio album ''[[My Beautiful Dark Twisted Fantasy]]''; four singles were released from the album, all of which reached the top 25 on the ''Billboard'' Hot 100: "[[Power (Kanye West song)|Power]]", "[[Monster (Kanye West song)|Monster]]", "[[Runaway (Kanye West song)|Runaway]]" and "[[All of the Lights]]".<ref name="US-singles"/> The following year, West collaborated with American singer [[Katy Perry]] on a remix of her song "[[E.T. (song)|E.T.]]" which hit number one on the ''Billboard'' Hot 100, marking West's fourth number one single on the chart.<ref name="US-singles"/> ''[[Watch the Throne]]'', a collaboration with Jay-Z, produced seven singles, including the ''Billboard'' Hot 100 top 25 singles "[[H•A•M]]", "[[Otis (song)|Otis]]" and "[[Niggas in Paris]]".<ref name="US-singles"/> West's sixth studio album ''[[Yeezus]]'', released in 2013, featured the singles "[[Black Skinhead]]" and "[[Bound 2]]".

"[[FourFiveSeconds]]", a 2015 collaboration with Rihanna and musician [[Paul McCartney]], became a top five hit on the Hot 100 and a top ten hit in several other countries.<ref name="US-singles"/> The singles "[[Famous (Kanye West song)|Famous]]", "[[Father Stretch My Hands]]", which was released as a two-part single, and "[[Fade (Kanye West song)|Fade]]" were released in support of West's seventh studio album ''[[The Life of Pablo]]'', released in 2016 with singer [[Post Malone]]. "[[Yikes (Kanye West song)|Yikes]]" was released as the first single from Kanye's eighth studio album ''[[Ye (album)|Ye]]'' in 2018 and performed best in [[Canada]] by peaking at number 6 on the [[Canadian Hot 100]] and "[[All Mine (Kanye West song)|All Mine]]" was the second single to be released from the album, which performed best in [[New Zealand]] by reaching number 5 on the [[Official New Zealand Music Chart|NZ Top 40]] chart.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.billboard.com/artist/kanye-west/chart-history/can/|title=Kanye West Chart History|publisher=Billboard|access-date=June 16, 2018}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://aotearoamusiccharts.co.nz/archive/singles/2018-06-08|title=NZ Top 40 Singles Chart|publisher=Recorded Music NZ|access-date=October 14, 2018}}</ref>
