American [[pop rock]] band [[OneRepublic]] has released six [[studio album]]s, one [[live album]], two [[compilation album]]s, twelve [[extended play]]s, 52 [[single (music)|single]]s, ten [[promotional single]]s, and 38 [[music video]]s. Formed in 2002, OneRepublic first found commercial recognition when their song "[[Apologize (OneRepublic song)|Apologize]]" was released onto [[Myspace]] in 2006 and they subsequently became the biggest act on that site. It was remixed by American record producer [[Timbaland]], appearing on his album ''[[Shock Value (Timbaland album)|Shock Value]]''.  Released as a single in 2007, the song topped the charts in multiple countries, including Australia and Canada, while also reaching the top three in the United States and the United Kingdom.<ref name="AUS-singles"/><ref name="CAN-singles"/><ref name="US-singles"/><ref name="UK"/> It has since sold over 5.8 million digital downloads in the United States.<ref name="sales_us">{{cite news|url=http://music.yahoo.com/blogs/music-news/chart-watch-dark-horse-holds-off-happy-230021537.html|title=Chart Watch: 'Dark Horse' Holds Off 'Happy'|last=Grein|first=Paul|work=[[Yahoo! Music]]|publisher=Yahoo! News|date=February 19, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230409215932/https://news.yahoo.com/blogs/music-news/chart-watch-dark-horse-holds-off-happy-230021537.html|archive-date=April 9, 2023|access-date=March 20, 2026|url-status=dead}}</ref> The band's debut studio album, ''[[Dreaming Out Loud]]'', was released in November 2007. It hit the top ten on the albums charts of countries such as Australia, Canada and the United Kingdom.<ref name="AUS-albums"/><ref name="CAN-albums"/><ref name="UK"/> It also peaked at number 14 on the US [[Billboard 200|''Billboard'' 200]], earning a triple Platinum certification from the [[Recording Industry Association of America]] (RIAA).<ref name="US-albums"/><ref name="RIAA"/> The album's second single, "[[Stop and Stare]]", peaked at number 12 on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] while also reaching the top five in Austria and the United Kingdom.<ref name="US-singles"/><ref name="UK"/><ref name="AUT"/> The album spawned three more singles: "[[Say (All I Need)]]", "Mercy", and "[[Come Home (OneRepublic song)|Come Home]]".

''[[Waking Up (OneRepublic album)|Waking Up]]'', the band's second studio album, was released in November 2009. It peaked at number 46 on the ''Billboard'' 200, earning a double Platinum certification from the RIAA.<ref name="US-albums"/><ref name="RIAA"/> The album's lead single, "[[All the Right Moves (OneRepublic song)|All the Right Moves]]", became a top ten hit in multiple countries, including Ireland and Switzerland.<ref name="IRL"/><ref name="SWI"/> It also peaked at number 18 on the ''Billboard'' Hot 100.<ref name="US-singles"/> "[[Secrets (OneRepublic song)|Secrets]]", the album's second single, peaked at number 21 on the Hot 100.<ref name="US-singles"/> "[[Marchin On]]" was released as the album's third single in Europe, becoming a top ten hit in Austria and Germany.<ref name="AUT"/><ref name="GER"/> "[[Good Life (OneRepublic song)|Good Life]]", the album's fourth single, became the band's second top ten hit on the ''Billboard'' Hot 100, where it peaked at number 8.<ref name="US-singles"/> It also peaked at number 5 on the [[Canadian Hot 100]].<ref name="CAN-singles"/>

''[[Native (album)|Native]]'', the band's third album, was released in March 2013. The album peaked at number 4 on the ''Billboard'' 200, earning a four-times Platinum and becoming the band's highest-charting album to date. The album contains the Diamond-certified worldwide hit "[[Counting Stars]]", which topped the charts in Canada and the United Kingdom and reached number 2 in the United States and several other countries, as well as the moderately successful singles "[[Feel Again]]", "[[Something I Need]]" and "[[If I Lose Myself]]". In 2014, there was a reissue of that album, which contained a new single, "[[Love Runs Out]]", which peaked at number 15 on the ''Billboard'' Hot 100 chart, included a dance remix of the song "If I Lose Myself" by [[Alesso]].

''[[Oh My My (album)|Oh My My]]'', OneRepublic's fourth album, was released on October 7, 2016. The first single from the album, "[[Wherever I Go (OneRepublic song)|Wherever I Go]]", reached number 55 on the ''Billboard'' Hot 100. The second single, "[[Kids (OneRepublic song)|Kids]]", reached number 96 on the Hot 100. The band released their fifth album, ''[[Human (OneRepublic album)|Human]]'', on August 27, 2021.
