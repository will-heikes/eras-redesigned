American actress and singer [[Hailee Steinfeld]] has released two [[extended plays]], twenty [[single (music)|singles]] (including nine as a featured artist), three [[promotional single]]s and has made other album appearances. Steinfeld gained recognition for her music after performing "[[Flashlight (Jessie J song)|Flashlight]]" in ''[[Pitch Perfect 2]]'' (2015).

Steinfeld's debut [[extended play]], ''[[Haiz (EP)|Haiz]]'', was released on November 13, 2015, by [[Republic Records]]. The [[lead single]], "[[Love Myself (Hailee Steinfeld song)|Love Myself]]" found commercial success peaking at number 30 on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] and number 15 on the ''Billboard'' [[Mainstream Top 40|Pop Songs]] chart, marking the highest debut for a solo female artist on the chart in 17 years since [[Natalie Imbruglia]]'s "[[Torn (Ednaswap song)#Natalie Imbruglia version|Torn]]" in 1998.{{NoteTag|As attributed by sources published by ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]''.<ref>{{cite magazine|last1=Anderson|first1=Trevor|title=Hailee Steinfeld Finds 'Love' on Billboard + Twitter Top Tracks|url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/hailee-steinfeld-love-myself-twitter-top-tracks/|access-date=November 28, 2015|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|publisher=[[Prometheus Global Media]]|date=August 20, 2015}}</ref><ref>{{cite magazine|url={{BillboardURLbyName|artist=Hailee Steinfeld|chart=Pop Songs B}}|title=Hailee Steinfeld – Chart history (Pop Songs)|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|publisher=[[Prometheus Global Media]]|access-date=November 28, 2015}}</ref>}} ''Haiz'' was re-released twice in 2016. With the first re-release, a new version of "[[Rock Bottom (Hailee Steinfeld song)|Rock Bottom]]" with [[DNCE]] was released as the second single and reached number 33 on the Pop Songs chart. With the second re-release, Steinfeld also added "[[Starving (song)|Starving]]" which found commercial and critical success and reached number 12 on the Hot 100.

Following 2016, Steinfeld made appearances on multiple soundtracks including "[[Capital Letters (song)|Capital Letters]]" from ''[[Fifty Shades Freed (film)|Fifty Shades Freed]]'' (2018), "[[Back to Life (Hailee Steinfeld song)|Back to Life]]" from ''[[Bumblebee (film)|Bumblebee]]'' (2018) and "[[Afterlife (Hailee Steinfeld song)|Afterlife]]" from ''[[Dickinson (TV series)|Dickinson]]'' (2019). She also released a string of successful singles in 2017 which included "[[Most Girls (Hailee Steinfeld song)|Most Girls]]" and "[[Let Me Go (Hailee Steinfeld and Alesso song)|Let Me Go]]" reaching number 58 and 40 on the Hot 100 respectively. In 2020, Steinfeld released her second extended play, ''[[Half Written Story]]'' which was supported by the singles "[[Wrong Direction (song)|Wrong Direction]]" and "[[I Love You's]]". This was followed by various standalone singles including "[[Coast (song)|Coast]]".
