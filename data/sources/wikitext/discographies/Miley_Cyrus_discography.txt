American singer [[Miley Cyrus]] has released ten [[Album|studio albums]], three [[Album|live albums]], four [[Extended play|extended plays]] and 47 [[Single (music)|singles]]. Popularly referred as the original "Teen Queen",<ref>{{Cite web|last=Parade|date=April 6, 2009|title=Miley Cyrus: Teen Queen|url=https://parade.com/75655/parade/miley-cyrus-3/|access-date=January 11, 2022|website=Parade|language=en}}</ref><ref>{{Cite magazine|author=The Hollywood Reporter|date=January 19, 2011|title='Hannah Montana' Finale Sets Disney Ratings Record|url=https://www.billboard.com/music/music-news/hannah-montana-finale-sets-disney-ratings-record-473581/|access-date=January 11, 2022|magazine=Billboard|language=en-US}}</ref> Cyrus has sold 55 million singles and 20 million albums worldwide.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Seven child music stars who made it big|url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/teach/bring-the-noise/articles/zk66jhv|access-date=January 11, 2022|website=BBC Bring The Noise|language=en}}</ref> According to [[Recording Industry Association of America]], she has sold 32 million singles and 10 million albums in the US.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Searchable Database — RIAA|url=https://www.riaa.com/gold-platinum/|access-date=January 11, 2022|website=RIAA|language=en-US}}</ref> ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' ranked Cyrus as the ninth greatest [[Billboard 200|''Billboard'' 200]] female music artist of all time<ref>{{Cite magazine|date=November 30, 2017|title=Greatest of All Time Billboard 200 Women Artists|url=https://www.billboard.com/charts/greatest-billboard-200-women-artists/|access-date=January 11, 2022|magazine=Billboard|language=en-US}}</ref> and the 62nd greatest artist of all time.<ref>{{Cite magazine|date=November 14, 2019|title=Greatest of All Time Artists|url=https://www.billboard.com/charts/greatest-of-all-time-artists/|access-date=January 11, 2022|magazine=Billboard|language=en-US}}</ref>

Cyrus' first musical effort credited to the [[Miley Stewart#Hannah Montana|Hannah Montana]] character was on March 1, 2006, with the show's [[Hannah Montana (soundtrack)|first soundtrack]], which debuted at number one in the United States, and peaked in the top ten on the UK Compilations Chart. In June 2007, the series' second soundtrack and Cyrus' debut studio album were jointly released by [[Walt Disney Records|Walt Disney]] and [[Hollywood Records]], as the [[double album]] ''[[Hannah Montana 2: Meet Miley Cyrus]]'', after Cyrus signed a four-album deal with the latter.<ref>{{cite web|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180804110348/http://www.alloy.com/entertainment/miley-cyrus-spotlight/|archive-date=August 4, 2018|url-status=dead|url=http://www.crushable.com/2007/11/04/entertainment/miley-cyrus-spotlight/|title=Miley Cyrus – Spotlight|publisher=Defy Media LLC|access-date=July 31, 2016}}</ref> The project reached number one in the United States. Its latter disc included the single "[[See You Again (Miley Cyrus song)|See You Again]]", which became Cyrus' first track to impact the top-ten in the United States, Australia, and Canada. On her debut live album ''[[Best of Both Worlds Concert (soundtrack)|Best of Both Worlds Concert]]'', released in March 2008, Cyrus performed seven songs as herself and seven tracks as her title character Montana. The [[Hannah Montana: The Movie (soundtrack)|''Hannah Montana: The Movie'' soundtrack]] peaked at number one in Austria, Canada, New Zealand, Spain, the U.S., and Turkey, and ''[[Hannah Montana 3]]'' topped the ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' Kid Albums and Soundtracks charts.<ref name="US Kid albums">{{Cite magazine|title=Miley Cyrus|url={{BillboardURLbyName|artist=miley-cyrus|chart=kid/}}|url-status=live|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20211121094925/https://www.billboard.com/artist/miley-cyrus/chart-history/kid/|archive-date=November 21, 2021}}</ref><ref>{{Cite magazine|title=Hannah Montana|url={{BillboardURLbyName|artist=hannah-montana|chart=stx/}}|url-status=live|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20211117212802/https://www.billboard.com/artist/hannah-montana/chart-history/stx/|archive-date=November 17, 2021}}</ref> The [[Hannah Montana Forever (soundtrack)|fifth]] and final soundtrack, was released on October 19, 2010, peaked at number 11 on the ''Billboard'' 200.

The singer's second studio album ''[[Breakout (Miley Cyrus album)|Breakout]]'' was released in July 2008, topping the United States, Australian, and Canadian charts. Its lead single "[[7 Things]]" reached the top 10 in the United States, Australia, and Norway. Cyrus' first extended play ''[[The Time of Our Lives (EP)|The Time of Our Lives]]'' followed in August 2009, and its track "[[Party in the U.S.A.]]" made the top five in the United States, Canada, Ireland, and New Zealand. In that same year she released a live album titled ''iTunes Live from London''. She released her third studio album ''[[Can't Be Tamed]]'' in June 2010, peaking within the top five in the United States, Australia, Austria, Canada, Germany, Ireland, Italy, and New Zealand. While the [[Can't Be Tamed (song)|title track]] reached the top 10 in the United States, Canada, Ireland, and New Zealand, the album was less commercially successful than her past efforts.

Cyrus' fourth studio album ''[[Bangerz]]'' was released through [[RCA Records]] in October 2013, topping the United States, Australia, Canada, Ireland, Norway, and the United Kingdom charts. Its songs "[[We Can't Stop]]" and "[[Wrecking Ball (Miley Cyrus song)|Wrecking Ball]]" both reached the summit in the United Kingdom while former also topped the charts in New Zealand and the latter went number one in the United States and Canada. Her fifth album, ''[[Miley Cyrus & Her Dead Petz]]'', was self-released via streaming in August 2015 and did not chart. She released her sixth album ''[[Younger Now]]'' in September 2017, which charted within the top five in the United States, Australia, Canada, Ireland, and New Zealand. Its lead single "[[Malibu (Miley Cyrus song)|Malibu]]" reached the top five in Australia, Canada, Norway, and New Zealand. In 2019, Cyrus released her fourth EP titled ''[[She Is Coming]]''. In 2020, Cyrus released her seventh studio album ''[[Plastic Hearts]]'', which spawned the top-twenty single "[[Midnight Sky]]". In 2023, she released ''[[Endless Summer Vacation]]'', which featured her second number-one single "[[Flowers (Miley Cyrus song)|Flowers]]".
