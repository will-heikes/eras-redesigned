British singer-songwriter [[Jessie Ware]] has released six [[studio album]]s (and one reissue), three [[compilation album]]s, thirty-five [[Single (music)|singles]], four [[extended plays]] and thirty-one [[music video]]s.

Ware released her debut studio album ''[[Devotion (Jessie Ware album)|Devotion]]'' in 2012, charting at number five on the [[UK Albums Chart]]. The album included the singles "[[Running (Jessie Ware song)|Running]]", "[[110% (song)|110%]]" and "[[Wildest Moments]]". The album was later certified Gold by the [[British Phonographic Industry]] for sales over 100,000 copies. The singer's second album ''[[Tough Love (Jessie Ware album)|Tough Love]]'' was released in 2014, scoring Ware her second consecutive top-ten album. It includes the [[Tough Love (Jessie Ware song)|title track]] and the [[Ed Sheeran]]-penned "[[Say You Love Me (Jessie Ware song)|Say You Love Me]]" as singles. Both attained Ware's first top-forty positions on the [[UK Singles Chart]]. Her third album, ''[[Glasshouse (album)|Glasshouse]]'' was released in 2017 and again achieved a top-ten position in the UK. It includes the singles "[[Midnight (Jessie Ware song)|Midnight]]", "[[Selfish Love (Jessie Ware song)|Selfish Love]]" and "[[Alone (Jessie Ware song)|Alone]]". In 2020, she released ''[[What's Your Pleasure?]]'', her fourth album, it includes singles such as "[[Spotlight (Jessie Ware song)|Spotlight]]" and "[[Save a Kiss]]", the album peaked at number three in the UK, being her highest appearance on the chart. Her first two compilation albums were released in 2025.
