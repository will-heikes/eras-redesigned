American singer [[Christina Aguilera]] has released nine [[studio album]]s, five [[extended play]]s (EPs), four [[compilation album]]s, two [[soundtrack album]]s, 54 [[single (music)|singles]] (including eight as featured artist), and 17 [[promotional recording|promotional singles]]. After gaining recognition for her solo musical debut with "[[Reflection (Mulan song)|Reflection]]", the theme song for the [[Walt Disney Company|Disney]] animated film ''[[Mulan (1998 film)|Mulan]]'' (1998), Aguilera signed a multi-album contract with [[RCA Records]] in 1998.

She released her [[Christina Aguilera (album)|self-titled debut album]] in 1999 which debuted at number one on the US [[Billboard 200|''Billboard'' 200]] gaining the singles "[[Genie in a Bottle]]", "[[What a Girl Wants (Christina Aguilera song)|What a Girl Wants]]" and "[[Come On Over Baby (All I Want Is You)]]" which all topped the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]]. "Genie in a Bottle" became Aguilera's early signature song, peaking atop the chart for five weeks. She followed up this success with [[Spanish language]] and [[Christmas music|Christmas]] albums, ''[[Mi Reflejo]]'' (2000) and ''[[My Kind of Christmas (Christina Aguilera album)|My Kind of Christmas]]'' (2000) respectively, garnering success with various songs including "[[Ven Conmigo (Solamente Tú)]]" which topped the US [[Hot Latin Songs]] chart and "[[The Christmas Song]]" which reached top 20 on the Hot 100, marking the second highest charting position of the song on the chart in 55 years. In 2001, she recorded two collaborations, "[[Nobody Wants to Be Lonely]]" with [[Ricky Martin]] and "[[Lady Marmalade#Moulin Rouge! version|Lady Marmalade]]" with [[Mýa]], [[Lil' Kim]] and [[Pink (singer)|Pink]]. The latter found international success, peaking atop various charts including the Hot 100 for five consecutive weeks.

Aguilera released her fourth studio album, ''[[Stripped (Christina Aguilera album)|Stripped]]'' (2002), which debuted at number two on the US ''Billboard'' 200 and spawned the single "[[Beautiful (Christina Aguilera song)|Beautiful]]", which peaked at number two on the Hot 100 and number one in the UK. The album has gone on to become one of the [[List of best-selling albums of the 21st century|best-selling albums of the 21st century]]. She followed this up with her [[double album|dual disc]] fifth studio album, ''[[Back to Basics (Christina Aguilera album)|Back to Basics]]'' (2006), which found commercial success and debuted atop the ''Billboard'' 200. The album garnered the top-ten Hot 100 single "[[Ain't No Other Man]]" and top-30 singles "[[Hurt (Christina Aguilera song)|Hurt]]" and "[[Candyman (Christina Aguilera song)|Candyman]]". Aguilera released her first [[greatest hits album]], ''[[Keeps Gettin' Better: A Decade of Hits]]'' (2008) alongside the single "[[Keeps Gettin' Better]]," which debuted at number seven on the Hot 100, marking her highest debut on the chart. During these years, she also released the singles "[[Car Wash (song)#Christina Aguilera and Missy Elliott version|Car Wash]]", "[[Tilt Ya Head Back]]" and "[[Tell Me (Diddy song)|Tell Me]]".

In 2010, Aguilera released her sixth studio album ''[[Bionic (Christina Aguilera album)|Bionic]]'' (2010), which debuted at number three in the US. Its lead single, "[[Not Myself Tonight]]", topped the US [[Dance Club Songs]] chart and reached number 23 on the Hot 100. Aguilera's seventh studio album, ''[[Lotus (Christina Aguilera album)|Lotus]]'' (2012) also stalled on the chart, debuting at number seven, marking her lowest charting studio album at the time. Its lead single "[[Your Body (Christina Aguilera song)|Your Body]]" fared averagely on the charts, also topping the US Dance Club Songs chart. Aguilera later found commercial success after featuring on the songs "[[Moves like Jagger]]", "[[Feel This Moment]]" and "[[Say Something (A Great Big World song)|Say Something]]". All three songs reached the top ten on the Hot 100, with "Moves Like Jagger" topping the Hot 100, making Aguilera one of the few artists to top the chart in three consecutive decades (1990s, 2000s, and 2010s). Aguilera took a semi-hiatus from music during her time on ''[[The Voice (American TV series)|The Voice]]'' (2011–2016). From 2013 to 2016, she released a string of standalone singles including "[[We Remain]]", "[[Change (Christina Aguilera song)|Change]]" and "[[Telepathy (song)|Telepathy]]".

Aguilera returned to music with her eighth studio album ''[[Liberation (Christina Aguilera album)|Liberation]]'' (2018). The album debuted at number six in the US and was preceded by various singles including "[[Accelerate (Christina Aguilera song)|Accelerate]]" and "[[Fall in Line]]". In 2020, Aguilera re-recorded "Reflection" for the [[Mulan (2020 film)|2020 live adaptation of ''Mulan'']] alongside a new song, "[[Loyal Brave True]]", which was shortlisted for an [[Academy Award for Best Original Song]]. In 2022, she released her second Spanish language and ninth studio album, ''[[Aguilera (album)|Aguilera]]'' (2022), which was released in three [[extended plays]], ''[[La Fuerza]]'', ''[[La Tormenta (EP)|La Tormenta]]'' and ''[[La Luz (EP)|La Luz]]''. In 2024 and 2025, she released a [[The 25th Anniversary of Christina Aguilera (Spotify Anniversaries Live)|live EP]] and [[Christmas in Paris#Soundtrack|soundtrack album]] for the 25th anniversary of both her debut and Christmas album, respectively. Throughout her career, Aguilera has also lent her vocals to various soundtracks, including that for ''[[Moulin Rouge! Music from Baz Luhrmann's Film|Moulin Rouge!]]'' (2001), ''[[Shark Tale]]'' (2004), ''[[The Hunger Games: Catching Fire (soundtrack)|The Hunger Games: Catching Fire]]'' (2013) and ''[[The Addams Family (2019 film)|The Addams Family]]'' (2019).
