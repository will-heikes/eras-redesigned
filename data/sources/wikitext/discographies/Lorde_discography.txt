New Zealand singer-songwriter [[Lorde]] has released four [[studio albums]], four [[extended play]]s, one [[soundtrack album]], 16 [[Single (music)|single]]s (including one as a featured artist), 12 [[promotional singles]] and 16 [[music video]]s. At the age of 13, she was signed to [[Universal Music Group]] (UMG) and started to write music.<ref>{{cite news| url= http://www.stuff.co.nz/dominion-post/culture/performance/8654143/Lorde-NZs-newest-pop-star| title= Lorde: NZ's newest pop star| first= Tom| last= Cardy |work=[[The Dominion Post (Wellington)|The Dominion Post]] | date= 5 October 2013 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131204145411/http://www.stuff.co.nz/dominion-post/culture/performance/8654143/Lorde-NZs-newest-pop-star |archive-date=4 December 2013 |url-status=live}}</ref> In November 2012, when she was 16 years old,<ref>{{cite news| url= http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1715048/lorde-number-1-reaction.jhtml| archive-url= https://web.archive.org/web/20131006045223/http://www.mtv.com/news/articles/1715048/lorde-number-1-reaction.jhtml| url-status= dead| archive-date= 6 October 2013| title= Lorde's 'Royals' May Be #1, But She's Still 'Just A Disgusting Person'| first= James| last= Montgomery| work= [[MTV News]] |publisher=[[Viacom (2005–present)|Viacom]] | date= 3 October 2013| access-date= 5 January 2014}}</ref> she [[Self-publishing|self-released]] ''[[The Love Club EP]]'' via [[SoundCloud]].<ref>{{cite magazine| url= https://www.billboard.com/biz/articles/news/legal-and-management/5819663/new-zealands-broods-signs-to-capitol-in-us-preps| title=New Zealand's Broods Signs To Capitol In U.S., Preps Debut Album with Lorde's Producer|first=Jason|last=Lipshutz| magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]] |access-date=5 January 2014 |date=5 December 2013}}</ref> It was released for sale by UMG in March 2013;<ref>{{cite news |url=http://www.nzherald.co.nz/entertainment/news/article.cfm?c_id=1501119&objectid=10872495| title= Lorde, The Love Club EP |work=[[The New Zealand Herald]] |first=Chris |last=Schulz |date=21 March 2013 |access-date=5 January 2014}}</ref> a song from the EP, "[[Royals (Lorde song)|Royals]]", topped numerous single charts internationally, including the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]]. The track sold over 10 million units worldwide, making it one of the [[List of best-selling singles|best-selling singles]] of all time.<ref name="NZHerald">{{cite news |url=http://www.nzherald.co.nz/entertainment/news/article.cfm?c_id=1501119&objectid=11354524 |title=Birthday girl Lorde's earnings estimated at $11m-plus |first=Matt |last=Nippert |work=The New Zealand Herald |date=7 November 2014 |access-date=7 November 2014}}</ref>

Later that year, Lorde released her debut studio album ''[[Pure Heroine]]'', which included "Royals". It charted at number one in New Zealand and Australia, and achieved [[List of music recording certifications|certifications]] in several countries. As of June 2021, the album has sold over six million copies worldwide.<ref name="Pure Heroine 2021">{{cite magazine |last1=Eggertsen |first1=Chris |title=Joel Little, Lorde and Taylor Swift Hitmaker, Sells Songwriter Catalog to Hipgnosis |url=https://www.billboard.com/articles/news/9582561/hipgnnosis-joel-little-catalog-acquisition-taylor-swift-lorde |access-date=4 June 2021 |magazine=Billboard |date=4 June 2021}}</ref> It was preceded by three additional singles: "[[Tennis Court (song)|Tennis Court]]", "[[Team (Lorde song)|Team]]", and "[[Glory and Gore]]". "Team" was a top-ten hit in several single charts, including Canada and the United States. The following year, Lorde recorded four songs for the [[The Hunger Games: Mockingjay, Part 1 (soundtrack)|soundtrack album]] of the 2014 film ''[[The Hunger Games: Mockingjay – Part 1|The Hunger Games: Mockingjay, Part 1]]'', including the single "[[Yellow Flicker Beat]]".

Lorde's second studio album, ''[[Melodrama (Lorde album)|Melodrama]]'', was released in June 2017 and topped the charts in four countries, including the United States. It yielded three singles: "[[Green Light (Lorde song)|Green Light]]", "[[Perfect Places]]", and a remix of "[[Homemade Dynamite]]" (featuring [[Khalid (American singer)|Khalid]], [[SZA (singer)|SZA]], and [[Post Malone]]); the former reached the top-ten in Australia and Canada. Her third studio album, ''[[Solar Power (album)|Solar Power]]'' (2021), topped the charts in New Zealand and Australia. It featured four singles, including the [[Solar Power (song)|titular track]], which peaked at number two on New Zealand's singles chart. Five songs from the album were then reworked into [[Māori language|Māori]] for her EP ''[[Te Ao Mārama (EP)|Te Ao Mārama]]'' (2021). In June 2025, Lorde released her fourth studio album, ''[[Virgin (Lorde album)|Virgin]]'', which was preceded by the singles "[[What Was That]]" and "[[Man of the Year (Lorde song)|Man of the Year]]".
