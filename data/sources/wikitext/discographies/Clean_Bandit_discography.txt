English [[electronic music]] group [[Clean Bandit]] have released two [[studio album]]s, two [[extended play]]s, 35 [[single (music)|singles]] (including four as a featured artist), 22 [[music video]]s and 13 [[remix]]es. In December 2012, the group released their debut single "[[A+E (song)|A+E]]", which peaked at number 100 on the [[UK Singles Chart]]. The song is the lead single from their debut album, ''[[New Eyes]]'', which was released in May 2014. The album's second single, "[[Mozart's House]]", charted at number seventeen on the UK Singles Chart, becoming Clean Bandit's first top twenty single on the chart. "[[Dust Clears]]" was released as the third single from the album, reaching number forty-three on the UK chart. The album's fourth single, "[[Rather Be]]", features [[Jess Glynne]] and topped the UK Singles Chart, the group's first number one on the chart.

Clean Bandit's 2016 single "[[Rockabye (song)|Rockabye]]", which features rapper [[Sean Paul]] and singer [[Anne-Marie (singer)|Anne-Marie]], became their second number-one hit in the UK, becoming the [[List of UK Singles Chart Christmas number ones|Christmas number one single for 2016]] in its seventh consecutive week at number one. The follow-up to "Rockabye", "[[Symphony (Clean Bandit song)|Symphony]]", featured [[Zara Larsson]] and became their third UK number-one single. Their second album, ''[[What Is Love? (Clean Bandit album)|What Is Love?]]'', followed on 30 November 2018.<ref>{{cite news|url=http://www.completemusicupdate.com/article/clean-bandit-announced-loved-up-new-album/|title=Clean Bandit announce loved up new album|last=Malt|first=Andy|date=28 September 2018|publisher=Complete Music Update|accessdate=29 September 2018}}</ref>
