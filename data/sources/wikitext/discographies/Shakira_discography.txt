Colombian singer-songwriter [[Shakira]] has released 12 [[studio album]]s, one [[reissue]], five [[live album]]s, four [[compilation album]]s, 76 [[Single (music)|single]]s (including 7 as a featured artist), 13 [[promotional singles]], and 62 music videos (see [[Shakira videography]]). Shakira's musical career started at the age of 13 when she signed with [[Sony Music Entertainment|Sony Music]]. Her first two studio albums, ''[[Magia (Shakira album)|Magia]]'' and ''[[Peligro (Shakira album)|Peligro]]'', were released in Colombia in 1991 and 1993, respectively. They performed poorly and had low sales, with the former selling fewer than 1000 copies.<ref name="fullofgraceximenabook">{{cite book|last1=Diego|first1=Ximena|title=Shakira: Woman Full of Grace|date=10 January 2002|publisher=[[Simon & Schuster]]|isbn=9780743216685}}</ref> Fueled by the success of its [[lead single]] "[[Estoy Aquí]]", her next album ''[[Pies Descalzos]]'' (1995) became a success all across Latin America,<ref name=allmusicbio>{{cite web|last1=Huey|first1=Steve|title=Shakira, Biography|url=http://www.allmusic.com/artist/shakira-mn0000790797/biography|publisher=[[AllMusic]]. [[All Media Group]]|access-date=12 July 2014}}</ref> receiving a diamond certification in Colombia.<ref name="piesdiamond">{{cite web|title=Shakira|url=http://www.eltiempo.com/archivo/documento/MAM-475382|website=[[El Tiempo (Colombia)|El Tiempo]]|publisher=Casa Editorial El Tiempo S.A|access-date=7 July 2014|language=Spanish|date=16 August 1996}}</ref> Her success in Latin America was consolidated by ''[[Dónde Están los Ladrones?]]'' (1998), which peaked atop the US ''Billboard'' [[Top Latin Albums]] chart. Like ''Pies Descalzos'', it spawned numerous singles, including the popular "[[Ciega, Sordomuda]]".<ref name=allmusicbio /> The album also spawned the top-ten hit "[[Ojos Así]]", which performed successfully in several European and Latin American countries.<ref name=allmusicbio /> ''Dónde Están los Ladrones?'' is the ninth [[List of best-selling Latin albums in the United States|best-selling Latin album in the United States]].<ref>{{cite magazine|last1=Estevez|first1=Marjua|title=The Top 25 Biggest Selling Latin Albums of the Last 25 Years: Selena, Shakira & More|url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/selena-quintanilla-top-selling-latin-albums-ranking-25-years/|magazine=Billboard|publisher=Prometheus Global Media|access-date=18 October 2017|date=17 October 2017|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20171017153546/http://www.billboard.com/articles/columns/latin/8005603/selena-quintanilla-top-selling-latin-albums-ranking-25-years|archive-date=17 October 2017|url-status=live|url-access=subscription}}</ref>

Motivated by [[Gloria Estefan]], Shakira successfully [[Crossover (music)|crossed over]] into the English-language [[Pop music|pop]] music scene with the release of the multi-platinum selling ''[[Laundry Service (album)|Laundry Service]]'' (2001) and its worldwide chart-toppers "[[Whenever, Wherever]]" and "[[Underneath Your Clothes]]".<ref name=allmusicbio /><ref name=gloriainterviewlatina>{{cite web|last=Romero|first=Angie|title=Gloria Estefan Week: Will There Ever Be Another Shakira? Gloria Thinks So!|url=http://www.latina.com/entertainment/music/gloria-estefan-week-will-there-ever-be-another-shakira-gloria-thinks-so|work=[[Latina (magazine)|Latina]]|access-date=10 November 2013|date=29 September 2011|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131210013522/http://www.latina.com/entertainment/music/gloria-estefan-week-will-there-ever-be-another-shakira-gloria-thinks-so|archive-date=10 December 2013}}</ref><ref name=laundrybillboad>{{cite magazine|last=Cobo|first=Leila|title=Shakira|magazine=Billboard|date=10 November 2001|volume=113|issue=45|page=94|issn=0006-2510}}</ref> The album sold three million copies in six months of its release in the United States.<ref name=laundryuscertbillboard>{{cite magazine|title=Shakira, Pink Notch Triple Platinum|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/75542/shakira-pink-notch-triple-platinum|magazine=Billboard|access-date=23 November 2013}}</ref> By 2002, ''Laundry Service'' had sold more than four million copies across Europe<ref name=laundryeurocert>{{cite web|title=IFPI Platinum Europe Awards - 2002|url=http://www.ifpi.org/content/section_news/plat2002.html|publisher=IFPI|access-date=21 February 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131016055449/http://www.ifpi.org/content/section_news/plat2002.html|archive-date=16 October 2013}}</ref> and was declared the seventh best-selling album in the world in the same year by the [[International Federation of the Phonographic Industry]] (IFPI).<ref name=laundryseventhbillboard>{{cite magazine|last=Cobo|first=Leila|title=Shakira Returns With New Album, Steamy Rihanna Video, Country Duet: Cover Story|url=https://www.billboard.com/music/latin/shakira-cover-story-album-voice-5930246/|magazine=Billboard|access-date=8 March 2014|date=7 March 2014}}</ref> Out of a total of 60 songs, Shakira selected 20 songs and divided them into two albums, the Spanish ''[[Fijación Oral, Vol. 1]]'' and the English ''[[Oral Fixation, Vol. 2]]''; both were released in 2005.<ref name=60songsoralnyt>{{cite news|last1=Pareles|first1=Jon|authorlink1=Jon Pareles|title=The Shakira Dialectic|url=https://www.nytimes.com/2005/11/13/arts/music/13pare.html?pagewanted=all&_r=0|website=[[The New York Times]]|access-date=13 July 2014|date=13 November 2005}}</ref> The former opened at number four on the US ''Billboard'' [[Billboard 200|200]] with first-week sales of 157,000 units—the highest debut of a full-length Spanish-language album in the country.<ref name=fijacionhighdebutpr>{{cite web|last1=Najarian|first1=Lois|title=International Superstar Shakira Makes History with Biggest Spanish Language Album Debut Ever|url=http://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/international-superstar-shakira-makes-history-with-biggest-spanish-language-album-debut-ever-54691102.html|publisher=[[PR Newswire]]. [[UBM plc]]|access-date=13 July 2014|date=15 June 2005}}</ref> It finished as the second best-selling Latin album of the decade and is the eighth best-selling Latin album in the United States.<ref name=fijaciondecadebillboard>{{cite magazine|title=Best of the 2000s: Latin Albums|url=http://www.billboard.com/charts-decade-end/latin-albums?year=2009|magazine=Billboard|access-date=13 July 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20091226084922/http://www.billboard.com/charts-decade-end/latin-albums?year=2009#/web/20091226084922/http://www.billboard.com/charts-decade-end/latin-albums?year=2009|archive-date=26 December 2009}}</ref> Its lead single "[[La Tortura]]" spent 25 non-consecutive weeks at number one on the US ''Billboard'' [[Hot Latin Songs]] chart. "La Tortura" held the record for longest-running number one single on the chart by nearly a decade, until it was succeeded by [[Enrique Iglesias]]'s song "[[Bailando (Enrique Iglesias song)|Bailando]]" in October 2014.<ref name=latorturarecordbreak>{{cite magazine|last1=Mendizabal|first1=Amaya|title=Exclusive: Enrique Iglesias Talks Record-Breaking No. 1 Run for 'Bailando'
|url=http://www.billboard.com/biz/articles/news/chart-alert/6297028/exclusive-enrique-iglesias-talks-record-breaking-no-1-run-for|magazine=Billboard|access-date=30 October 2014|date=29 October 2014}}</ref> ''Oral Fixation, Vol. 2'' debuted within the top ten in most countries, although its sales began to decline in early 2006.<ref name=allmusicbio /> Shakira's label [[Epic Records|Epic]] then reissued the album, adding its second single "Hips Don't Lie" to the track list; the song topped charts in 55 countries,<ref name=harvardhonor>{{cite web|title=Shakira named Artist of the Year|url=http://news.harvard.edu/gazette/story/2011/02/shakira-named-artist-of-the-year/|website=[[Harvard Gazette]]|publisher=[[Harvard University]]|access-date=13 July 2014|date=16 February 2011}}</ref> including the UK Singles chart<ref name="UK" /> and the US ''Billboard'' Hot 100.<ref name="US" /> One of the best-selling singles of the 21st century,<ref name=harvardhonor /> "Hips Don't Lie" successfully revitalised sales of its parent album.<ref name=allmusicbio /> ''Fijación Oral, Vol. 1'' and ''Oral Fixation, Vol. 2'' sold combined copies of over 12 million worldwide.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.huffingtonpost.es/author/shakira/|title=Shakira|website=El Huffington Post|language=es|access-date=23 March 2020}}{{Dead link|date=February 2026 |bot=InternetArchiveBot }}</ref>
