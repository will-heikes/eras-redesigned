The discography of American singer [[John Legend]] consists of eleven full-length [[studio album]]s, six [[live album]]s, two [[video album]]s, three [[extended play]]s, 56 [[Single (music)|singles]] and two [[promotional single]]s.

Prior to the release of Legend's debut album, his career gained momentum through his collaborations with multiple artists; these include [[Slum Village]]'s "[[Selfish (Slum Village song)|Selfish]]", [[Dilated Peoples]]' "[[This Way (Dilated Peoples song)|This Way]]", [[Jay-Z]]'s "[[Encore (Jay-Z song)|Encore]]", [[Alicia Keys]]' "[[You Don't Know My Name]]", the Kanye West remix of [[Britney Spears]]' "[[Me Against the Music]]", and [[Fort Minor]]'s "High Road". Additionally, he played piano on [[Lauryn Hill]]'s "[[Everything Is Everything (Lauryn Hill song)|Everything Is Everything]]". He peaked the ''Billboard'' Hot 100 with his 2013 single "[[All of Me (John Legend song)|All of Me]]". He has sold over 10 million albums in the United States.
