American singer, songwriter, and actress [[Ariana Grande]] has released 8 [[studio album]]s, 2 [[soundtrack album]]s, 2 [[live album|live albums]], 1 [[compilation album]], 1 [[remix album]], 3 [[extended play]]s (EPs), 63 [[single (music)|singles]] (including 17 as a featured artist), and 20 [[Promotional recording#Promotional single|promotional singles]]. After signing with [[Republic Records]], Grande released her debut single, "[[Put Your Hearts Up]]", in December 2011. In April 2013, she made her chart debut with "[[The Way (Ariana Grande song)|The Way]]" featuring [[Mac Miller]], which peaked at number nine on the Hot 100.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/1555888/macklemore-ryan-lewis-top-hot-100-imagine-dragons-ariana-grande-hit-top-10|title=Macklemore & Ryan Lewis Top Hot 100; Imagine Dragons, Ariana Grande Hit Top 10|last=Trust|first=Gary|date=April 3, 2013|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|access-date=May 5, 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170214212901/http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/1555888/macklemore-ryan-lewis-top-hot-100-imagine-dragons-ariana-grande-hit-top-10|archive-date=February 14, 2017}}</ref> Her debut studio album, ''[[Yours Truly (Ariana Grande album)|Yours Truly]]'' (2013), reached the top 10 in Australia, Canada, Denmark, and the United Kingdom, while debuting at number one on the US [[Billboard 200|''Billboard'' 200]], and was subsequently certified platinum by the RIAA.<ref>{{cite magazine |author=<!--Not stated-->|url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/ladies-first-quiz-ariana-grande-15-no-1-female-debut-albums/|title=Ladies First Quiz: Ariana Grande & 15 No. 1 Female Debut Albums|date=September 11, 2013|magazine=Billboard|access-date=September 11, 2013|url-status=live|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170106092349/http://www.billboard.com/articles/columns/pop-shop/5687385/ladies-first-quiz-ariana-grande-15-no-1-female-debut-albums|archive-date=January 6, 2017}}</ref> Her first extended play (EP), ''[[Christmas Kisses (EP)|Christmas Kisses]]'' (2013), in which was re-released in December 2014 in Japan with the bonus track, "[[Santa Tell Me]]". The single is now considered a modern [[Standard (music)|Christmas standard]] for its popularity during the Christmas season throughout the years; it peaked at number five on the Hot 100 and the [[Billboard Global 200|''Billboard'' Global 200]].<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.songwritingmagazine.co.uk/interviews/ariana-grande-santa-tell-me|title=How we wrote Ariana Grande's 'Santa Tell Me' by Savan Kotecha|last=Herenda|first=Devin|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|date=December 13, 2022|access-date=May 6, 2026|archive-date=March 9, 2026|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260309121658/https://www.songwritingmagazine.co.uk/interviews/ariana-grande-santa-tell-me|url-status=live}}</ref> Her second studio album, ''[[My Everything (Ariana Grande album)|My Everything]]'' (2014), became her second consecutive number-one record in the US, and has been certified four-times Platinum by the RIAA.<ref>{{cite magazine|last=Caulfield|first=Keith|title=Ariana Grande Nabs Second No. 1 Album In Less Than A Year|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/6236678/ariana-grande-no-1-album-brad-paisley-kem|magazine=Billboard|date=September 2, 2014|access-date=September 3, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170708070723/http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/6236678/ariana-grande-no-1-album-brad-paisley-kem|archive-date=July 8, 2017}}</ref> Three singles—"[[Problem (Ariana Grande song)|Problem]]" (featuring [[Iggy Azalea]]), "[[Break Free (song)|Break Free]]" (featuring [[Zedd]]), and "[[Bang Bang (Jessie J, Ariana Grande and Nicki Minaj song)|Bang Bang]]" (with [[Jessie J]] and [[Nicki Minaj]])—peaked at number two, four, and three respectively on Hot 100 and charted within the top 10 simultaneously, making Grande the second lead female artist to achieve the feat.<ref>{{cite magazine|last=Trust|first=Gary|title=Ariana Grande, Iggy Azalea Triple Up In Hot 100's Top 10, MAGIC! Still No. 1|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/6221978/hot-100-ariana-grande-iggy-azalea-top-10-magic|magazine=Billboard|date=August 20, 2014|access-date=August 20, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170218135539/http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/6221978/hot-100-ariana-grande-iggy-azalea-top-10-magic|archive-date=February 18, 2017}}</ref> "Problem" and "Bang Bang" also became her first two number-one singles on the [[UK Singles Chart]].<ref>{{cite web|last=Lane|first=Daniel|title=Ariana Grande earns a place in Official Chart history with Problem|url=http://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/ariana-grande-earns-a-place-in-official-chart-history-with-problem-3146/|publisher=[[Official Charts Company]]|date=June 7, 2014|access-date=July 7, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170601175201/http://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/ariana-grande-earns-a-place-in-official-chart-history-with-problem__4302/|archive-date=June 1, 2017}}</ref> With its fourth single being Grande and [[the Weeknd]]'s collaboration "[[Love Me Harder]]", also peaking at number seven, Grande became the only artist to score four top-10 songs on the Hot 100 in 2014.<ref>{{cite web|author=Stern, Bradley|title=Ariana Grande and the Weeknd's "Love Me Harder" Breaks Into the Top 10 of The Billboard Hot 100|url=http://www.idolator.com/7570257/ariana-grande-the-weeknd-love-me-harder-top-10-billboard-hot-100-taylor-swift|work=[[Idolator (website)|Idolator]]|date=November 12, 2014|access-date=January 16, 2015|archive-url=https://archive.today/20150118194252/http://www.idolator.com/7570257/ariana-grande-the-weeknd-love-me-harder-top-10-billboard-hot-100-taylor-swift|archive-date=January 18, 2015|url-status=dead}}</ref>

Her third studio album, ''[[Dangerous Woman]]'' (2016), topped the charts in Italy, Norway, New Zealand, and the UK. It also peaked at number two in the US and received a three-times platinum certification by RIAA. It sold more than 20 million units worldwide, becoming Grande's highest-selling album to date.<ref>{{cite magazine |last=Kaufman |first=Gil |title=
Ariana Grande Celebrating 10th Anniversary of 'Dangerous Woman' LP With Expanded Edition Featuring Unreleased Fan Favorite|url=https://www.billboard.com/music/pop/ariana-grande-10th-anniversary-dangerous-woman-unreleased-song-1236252491/|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]] |date=May 20, 2026|access-date=July 22, 2026}}</ref> Its [[Dangerous Woman (song)|lead single of the same name]] peaked at number eight on the Hot 100, making her the first and only artist in the chart's history to debut in the top 10 with the lead single from each of her first three albums.<ref name="top10">{{cite magazine|title=Rihanna Rules Hot 100 for Fifth Week, Ariana Grande Debuts at No. 10 |url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/rihanna-hot-100-no-1-fifth-week-ariana-grande-debuts/|magazine=Billboard|author=Trust, Gary|date=March 21, 2016|access-date=March 21, 2016|url-status=live| archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160424165135/http://www.billboard.com/articles/columns/chart-beat/7263992/rihanna-hot-100-no-1-fifth-week-ariana-grande-debuts|archive-date=April 24, 2016}}</ref> Its third single, "[[Side to Side]]" featuring Minaj, reached the top five in the US, Canada, New Zealand, and the UK. Her fourth studio album, ''[[Sweetener (album)|Sweetener]]'' (2018), reached at number one in the US, Australia, Italy, and Norway. It was later certified two-times Platinum by the RIAA. Its lead single, "[[No Tears Left to Cry]]", reached the top five in the US, Australia, Canada, Germany, Italy, the Netherlands, New Zealand, and the UK. Her fifth studio album, ''[[Thank U, Next]]'' (2019), landed atop the charts in the US, Australia, Canada, Denmark, Norway, New Zealand, the UK. The album was certified two-times Platinum by the RIAA, and broke numerous chart and streaming records, including the largest streaming week for a pop album and for a female album in the US.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/ariana-grande-thank-u-next-debuts-at-no-1-on-billboard-200-chart-album/|title=Ariana Grande's 'Thank U, Next' Debuts at No. 1 on Billboard 200 Chart With Biggest Streaming Week Ever for a Pop Album|magazine=Billboard|last=Caulfield|first=Keith|date=February 17, 2019|access-date=February 18, 2019|archive-date=January 5, 2020|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200105210938/https://www.billboard.com/articles/columns/chart-beat/8498762/ariana-grande-thank-u-next-debuts-at-no-1-on-billboard-200-chart-album|url-status=live}}</ref> Its [[Thank U, Next (song)|lead single of the same name]] and the second single, "[[7 Rings]]", with both peaked at number one in the US, Australia, Canada, New Zealand, and the UK. Its third single, "[[Break Up with Your Girlfriend, I'm Bored]]", peaked at number two in the US and topped the singles chart in the UK. With these singles, Grande became the first solo artist ever and only the second artist after [[the Beatles]] to occupy the top three spots on the Hot 100 simultaneously.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/ariana-grande-top-3-spots-hot-100/|title=Ariana Grande Claims Nos. 1, 2 & 3 on Billboard Hot 100, Is First Act to Achieve the Feat Since The Beatles in 1964|last=Trust|first=Gary|magazine=Billboard|date=February 19, 2019|access-date=February 19, 2019|archive-date=April 5, 2019|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190405153906/https://www.billboard.com/articles/columns/chart-beat/8498841/ariana-grande-top-3-spots-hot-100|url-status=live}}</ref> She broke the record for the most number-one debuts on the Hot 100 with the 2020 collaborations "[[Stuck with U]]" (with [[Justin Bieber]]) and "[[Rain on Me (Lady Gaga and Ariana Grande song)|Rain on Me]]" (with [[Lady Gaga]]).<ref>{{Cite magazine|title=Lady Gaga & Ariana Grande's 'Rain on Me' Debuts at No. 1 on Billboard Hot 100|url=https://www.billboard.com/articles/business/chart-beat/9394719/rain-on-me-debuts-atop-hot-100-lady-gaga-ariana-grande|magazine=Billboard|date=June 2020|access-date=June 1, 2020|archive-date=June 3, 2020|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200603055218/https://www.billboard.com/articles/business/chart-beat/9394719/rain-on-me-debuts-atop-hot-100-lady-gaga-ariana-grande|url-status=live}}</ref>

Her sixth studio album, ''[[Positions (album)|Positions]]'' (2020), had both the album and its [[Positions (song)|lead single of the same name]] debuted at number one in the US, Canada, and the UK. With this, she became the first artist to accumulate three number-one debuts in a calendar year, the first artist to have five number-one debuts on the Hot 100 and the first to have their first five number-ones debut at the top.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/articles/business/chart-beat/9477041/ariana-positions-luke-combs-hot-100-number-one|title=Ariana Grande's 'Positions' Debuts at No. 1 on Hot 100, Luke Combs' 'Forever After All' Launches at No. 2|last=Trust|first=Gary|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|date=November 2, 2020|access-date=November 2, 2020|archive-date=November 8, 2020|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201108154842/https://www.billboard.com/articles/business/chart-beat/9477041/ariana-positions-luke-combs-hot-100-number-one|url-status=live}}</ref><ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/lists/ariana-grande-chart-records-broken-hot-100/fastest-accumulation-of-two-no-1-debuts/|title=Here Are All the Hot 100 Records That Ariana Grande Has Broken|last=Zellner|first=Xander|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|date=August 11, 2026|access-date=August 12, 2026|archive-date=August 12, 2026|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260812152444/https://www.billboard.com/lists/ariana-grande-chart-records-broken-hot-100/fastest-accumulation-of-two-no-1-debuts/|url-status=live}}</ref> Its second single, "[[34+35]]", peaked at number two in the US, following a remix with [[Doja Cat]] and [[Megan Thee Stallion]]. Grande garnered her sixth and seventh number-one singles on the Hot 100 with remixes of the Weeknd's "[[Save Your Tears#Ariana Grande remix|Save Your Tears]]" in 2021 and "[[Die for You (The Weeknd song)#Ariana Grande remix|Die for You]]" in 2023. Her seventh studio album, ''[[Eternal Sunshine (album)|Eternal Sunshine]]'' (2024), debuted atop the US ''Billboard'' 200. She garnered her eighth and ninth Hot 100 chart-toppers with the album's singles "[[Yes, And?]]" and "[[We Can't Be Friends (Wait for Your Love)]]", becoming her sixth and seventh to debut on that chart's pole position respectively. The soundtrack albums—''[[Wicked: The Soundtrack]]'' and ''[[Wicked: For Good – The Soundtrack]]''—for the [[Wicked (2024 film)|2024 film adaptation]] and [[Wicked: For Good|its 2025 sequel]] of the musical ''[[Wicked (musical)|Wicked]]'', in which Grande recorded most of the songs, both peaked at number two in the US. Her eighth studio album, ''[[Petal (album)|Petal]]'' (2026), topped the charts in 20 countries, including the US, the UK, France, Canada, and Australia. The lead single, "[[Hate That I Made You Love Me]]", became her tenth number-one on the Hot 100 and topped the UK Singles Chart.
