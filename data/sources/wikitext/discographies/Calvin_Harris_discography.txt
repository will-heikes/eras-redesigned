Scottish DJ [[Calvin Harris]] has released seven [[studio album]]s, one [[DJ mix|mix album]], nine [[extended play]]s, 62 [[Single (music)|singles]] (including four as a featured artist), 12 [[promotional single]]s and 48 [[music video]]s. As of November 2014, Harris had sold 8,176,180 singles and tracks in the United Kingdom.<ref name="MW-Nov2014"/> Harris's debut studio album, ''[[I Created Disco]]'', was released in June 2007. The album peaked at number eight on the [[UK Albums Chart]] and was certified gold by the [[British Phonographic Industry]] (BPI).<ref name="UK"/><ref name="BPI"/> Its first two singles, "[[Acceptable in the 80s]]" and "[[The Girls (Calvin Harris song)|The Girls]]", peaked at numbers 10 and three, respectively, on the [[UK Singles Chart]], while the third single, "[[Merrymaking at My Place]]", reached number 43.<ref name="UK"/>

In August 2009, Harris released his second studio album, ''[[Ready for the Weekend (album)|Ready for the Weekend]]'', which debuted at number one on the UK Albums Chart and earned a gold certification from the BPI within two months of release.<ref name="UK"/><ref name="BPI"/> Its lead single, "[[I'm Not Alone (song)|I'm Not Alone]]", became Harris's first number one on the UK Singles Chart as a lead artist.<ref name="UK"/> "[[Ready for the Weekend (song)|Ready for the Weekend]]" was released as the album's second single, peaking at number three in the United Kingdom.<ref name="UK"/> Two more singles were released from the album, "[[Flashback (Calvin Harris song)|Flashback]]" and "[[You Used to Hold Me (Calvin Harris song)|You Used to Hold Me]]", which reached numbers 18 and 27 on the UK chart, respectively.<ref name="UK"/>

His third studio album, ''[[18 Months]]'', was released in October 2012. The album sees Harris stepping away from vocals, instead focusing on the music production. It earned Harris his second consecutive number-one album in his home country.<ref name="UK"/> ''18 Months'' also became his first album to chart on the [[Billboard 200|''Billboard'' 200]] in the United States, peaking at number 19,<ref name="US-albums"/> while reaching number two in Ireland, number five in Australia and number eight in Canada.<ref name="IRE"/><ref name="AUS"/><ref name="CAN-albums"/> The album's lead single, "[[Bounce (Calvin Harris song)|Bounce]]" featuring [[Kelis]], peaked at number two on the UK Singles Chart and gave Harris his first top-10 entry in Australia, peaking at number seven.<ref name="UK"/><ref name="AUS"/> The second single, "[[Feel So Close]]", also peaked at number two in the UK.<ref name="UK"/> The song brought Harris to international prominence, reaching number five in New Zealand and number seven in Australia,<ref name="AUS"/><ref name="NZ"/> as well as becoming his first chart entry as a lead performer on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]], where it charted at number 12.<ref name="US-singles"/>

In 2011, Harris wrote, produced and was featured on [[Rihanna]]'s single "[[We Found Love]]", which topped the charts in 25 countries, including the United Kingdom, the United States, Ireland and Canada.<ref name="UK"/><ref name="IRE"/><ref name="US-singles"/><ref name="CAN-singles"/> The third and fourth singles from ''18 Months'', "[[Let's Go (Calvin Harris song)|Let's Go]]" (featuring [[Ne-Yo]]) and "[[We'll Be Coming Back]]" (featuring [[Example (musician)|Example]]), both reached number two on the UK chart,<ref name="UK"/> with the former also peaking at number 17 on the ''Billboard'' Hot 100.<ref name="US-singles"/> The album's fifth single, "[[Sweet Nothing (Calvin Harris song)|Sweet Nothing]]" featuring [[Florence Welch]], became Harris's fourth number-one single in the UK and first top-10 single in the US.<ref name="UK"/><ref name="US-singles"/> "[[Drinking from the Bottle]]" (featuring [[Tinie Tempah]]) and "[[I Need Your Love (Calvin Harris song)|I Need Your Love]]" (featuring [[Ellie Goulding]]) served as the album's sixth and seventh singles. Having both charted inside the UK top 10 by April 2013, Harris made chart history by becoming the first artist to attain eight top-10 singles from one studio album, overtaking the record previously set by [[Michael Jackson]].<ref>{{cite web |last=Lane |first=Daniel |url=https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/is-calvin-harris-the-new-king-of-pop__2975/ |title=Is Calvin Harris the new King Of Pop? |publisher=Official Charts Company |date=22 April 2013 |access-date=6 August 2015}}</ref> The eighth and final single, "[[Thinking About You (Calvin Harris song)|Thinking About You]]" featuring [[Ayah Marar]], peaked at number eight on the UK Singles Chart.<ref name="UK"/>

Harris's fourth studio album, ''[[Motion (Calvin Harris album)|Motion]]'', was released in October 2014. It peaked at number two on the UK Albums Chart and number five on the US ''Billboard'' 200, his highest-peaking album on the latter chart.<ref name="UK"/><ref name="US-albums"/> The album includes the UK number-one singles "[[Under Control (Calvin Harris and Alesso song)|Under Control]]", "[[Summer (Calvin Harris song)|Summer]]" and "[[Blame (Calvin Harris song)|Blame]]", as well as the top-10 single "[[Outside (Calvin Harris song)|Outside]]".<ref name="UK"/> In July 2015, Harris released the [[Disciples (production team)|Disciples]]-assisted single "[[How Deep Is Your Love (Calvin Harris and Disciples song)|How Deep Is Your Love]]". Throughout 2016, he released a string of singles—"[[This Is What You Came For]]", "[[Hype (Dizzee Rascal and Calvin Harris song)|Hype]]" and "[[My Way (Calvin Harris song)|My Way]]", all becoming commercially successful.

Harris released his fifth studio album, ''[[Funk Wav Bounces Vol. 1]]'', in June 2017. The album was preceded by the singles "[[Slide (Calvin Harris song)|Slide]]", "[[Heatstroke (song)|Heatstroke]]", "[[Rollin (Calvin Harris song)|Rollin]]" and "[[Feels (song)|Feels]]". His sixth studio album, ''[[Funk Wav Bounces Vol. 2]]'', followed in August 2022. The album was supported by "[[Potion (Calvin Harris song)|Potion]]", "New Money" and "[[Stay with Me (Calvin Harris, Justin Timberlake, Halsey and Pharrell Williams song)|Stay with Me]]". Harris's seventh studio and first compilation album, ''[[96 Months]]'', was surprise released in August 2024, which included most non-album singles released from July 2015 to "[[Free (Calvin Harris and Ellie Goulding song)|Free]]" in July 2024. The album also featured songs released under Harris's side project Love Regenerator.
