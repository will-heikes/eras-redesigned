American singer [[Justin Timberlake]] has released six studio albums, two compilation albums, three extended plays, and 52 singles (including 20 as a featured artist). Timberlake started his music career in 1995, as a member of boy band [[NSYNC]].<ref>{{cite web |title=*Nsync - New Music and Songs |url=http://mtv.com/artists/nsync/ |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141111125523/http://www.mtv.com/artists/nsync/ |archive-date=November 11, 2014 |access-date=November 9, 2014 |publisher=[[MTV]]}}</ref> Following the group's hiatus in 2002,<ref>{{cite news |last=Butler |first=Bethonie |date=August 26, 2013 |title=Justin Timberlake and 'N Sync reunite at 2013 VMAs |url=https://www.washingtonpost.com/entertainment/music/justin-timberlake-and-n-sync-reunite-at-2013-vmas/2013/08/26/0de921e0-0e5a-11e3-bdf6-e4fc677d94a1_story.html |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141111030639/http://www.washingtonpost.com/entertainment/music/justin-timberlake-and-n-sync-reunite-at-2013-vmas/2013/08/26/0de921e0-0e5a-11e3-bdf6-e4fc677d94a1_story.html |archive-date=November 11, 2014 |access-date=November 9, 2014 |newspaper=[[Washington Post]]}}</ref> he released his solo debut studio album, ''[[Justified (album)|Justified]]'', in November that same year.<ref name="justifiedrelease">{{cite web |title=Justified by Justin Timberlake |url=https://www.amazon.co.uk/Justified-Justin-Timberlake/dp/B00006RY20/ref=sr_1_1?ie=UTF8&qid=1415558996&sr=8-1&keywords=justified+justin+timberlake |access-date=November 3, 2014 |publisher=[[Amazon.com]] (UK)}}</ref> The album was a commercial success and peaked at number two on the US [[Billboard 200|''Billboard'' 200]] chart<ref name="US-albums" /> and additionally topped the charts in Ireland<ref name="IRE" /> and the United Kingdom.<ref name="UK-albums" /> ''Justified'' earned multiple multi-platinum certifications, including a triple platinum certification from the [[Recording Industry Association of America]] (RIAA)<ref name="RIAA" /> and a sextuple platinum certification from the [[British Phonographic Industry]] (BPI).<ref name="BPI" /> It produced four singles: "[[Like I Love You]]", "[[Cry Me a River (Justin Timberlake song)|Cry Me a River]]", "[[Rock Your Body]]" and "[[Señorita (Justin Timberlake song)|Señorita]]"; all performed well commercially, with two of them becoming top 5 hits on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] chart<ref name="US-singles" /> and top two hits on the [[UK Singles Chart]].<ref name="UK-singles" /> "Rock Your Body" also reached number one in Australia.

''[[FutureSex/LoveSounds]]'', Timberlake's second studio album, was released in September 2006.<ref name="fslvrelease">{{cite web |title=Future Sex / Love Sounds |url=https://www.amazon.co.uk/Future-Love-Sounds-Justin-Timberlake/dp/B000GG46NU/ref=sr_1_1?ie=UTF8&qid=1415559028&sr=8-1&keywords=future+sex+%2F+love+sounds+justin+timberlake |access-date=November 3, 2014 |publisher=Amazon.com (UK)}}</ref> Like its predecessor, the album achieved commercial success internationally and topped the ''Billboard'' 200 chart; it also reached number one in countries such as Australia,<ref name="AUS" /> Canada<ref name="CAN-albums" /> and the United Kingdom.<ref name="UK-albums" /> It was later certified seven times platinum by the RIAA<ref name="RIAA" /> and six times platinum by the [[Australian Recording Industry Association]] (ARIA);<ref name="aria 2014 albums" /> it sold over 10 million copies worldwide.<ref name="fslswwsales">{{cite web |title=The Evolution of: Justin Timberlake |url=http://mtv.com/photos/the-evolution-of-justin-timberlake/1667344/6382862/photo/ |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141111131055/http://www.mtv.com/photos/the-evolution-of-justin-timberlake/1667344/6382862/photo/ |archive-date=November 11, 2014 |access-date=November 4, 2014 |publisher=MTV}}</ref> Six singles were released from ''FutureSex/LoveSounds'', including the ''Billboard'' Hot 100 chart number-one hits "[[SexyBack]]", "[[My Love (Justin Timberlake song)|My Love]]" and "[[What Goes Around... Comes Around]]".<ref name="US-singles" /> Throughout the late 2000s, Timberlake collaborated with several artists on multiple Hot 100 top ten singles, including "[[Give It to Me (Timbaland song)|Give It to Me]]" by [[Timbaland]], "[[Ayo Technology]]" by [[50 Cent]], "[[4 Minutes (Madonna song)|4 Minutes]]" by [[Madonna (entertainer)|Madonna]], "[[Dead and Gone]]" by [[T.I.]] and "[[Love Sex Magic]]" by [[Ciara]].<ref name="US-singles" />

In March 2013,<ref name="20/20release">{{cite web |title=The 20/20 Experience 1 of 2 |url=https://www.amazon.de/20-Experience-Justin-Timberlake/dp/B00BCZU8WM |url-status=live |publisher=Amazon.com (DE)}}</ref> after a six-year hiatus from his solo music career, Timberlake released his third studio album, ''[[The 20/20 Experience]]''&nbsp;– it topped the charts in various countries and set a digital sales record for being the fastest-selling album on the [[iTunes Store]].<ref>{{cite web |last=Corner |first=Lewis |date=March 28, 2013 |title=Justin Timberlake breaks |archive-date=April 14, 2014 |access-date=May 15, 2014 |publisher=[[Nielsen SoundScan]] |page=12}}</ref> The album spawned three singles including the international hits "[[Suit & Tie]]" and "[[Mirrors (Justin Timberlake song)|Mirrors]]"; the latter reached number two on the Hot 100 chart<ref name="US-singles" /> and topped the UK Singles Chart.<ref name="UK-singles" /> In September 2013, Timberlake released the second half of the project, ''[[The 20/20 Experience – 2 of 2]]''.<ref name="20/20part2release">{{cite web |title=The 20/20 Experience - 2 of 2 (Standard Edition) |url=https://www.amazon.de/dp/B00DZS9XSY |access-date=November 3, 2014 |publisher=Amazon.com (DE)}}</ref> It produced three singles including "[[Not a Bad Thing]]", which reached number eight on the Hot 100 chart.<ref name="US-singles" /> In 2016, Timberlake serves as the executive music producer for the soundtrack to [[DreamWorks Animation]]'s ''[[Trolls (film)|Trolls]]'', accompanied by the release of "[[Can't Stop the Feeling!]]", his fifth chart-topping single on the Hot 100.<ref name="cstf">{{cite magazine |author=Trust, Gary |date=May 16, 2016 |title=Justin Timberlake Debuts at No. 1 certified Diamond in France<ref>{{cite web |title=Les Certifications |url=http://www.snepmusique.com/les-disques-dor/?awards_cat=64&awards_artist=Justin+Timberlake&awards_title=Can%27t+Stop+the+Feeling |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161104093953/http://www.snepmusique.com/les-disques-dor/?awards_cat=64&awards_artist=Justin+Timberlake&awards_title=Can%27t+Stop+the+Feeling |archive-date=November 4, 2016 |access-date=December 31, 2016 |publisher=SNEP |language=fr}}</ref> and Poland.<ref>{{cite web |title=ZPAV :: Bestsellery i wyróżnienia - Wyróżnienia - Diamentowe płyty CD |url=http://bestsellery.zpav.pl/wyroznienia/diamentoweplyty/cd.php |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://archive.today/20191123165820/http://bestsellery.zpav.pl/wyroznienia/diamentoweplyty/cd.php |archive-date=November 23, 2019 |access-date=March 1, 2017 |publisher=[[Polish Society of the Phonographic Industry]] |language=pl}}</ref>

On February 2, 2018, Timberlake released his fifth studio album, ''[[Man of the Woods]]''. It topped the ''Billboard'' 200 with the biggest first week sales of the year at the time, selling 293,000 total units.<ref name="Forbes">{{cite magazine |date=February 11, 2018 |title=Justin Timberlake's 'Man Of The Woods' Opens At No. 1 With 2018's Biggest Week |url=https://www.forbes.com/sites/hughmcintyre/2018/02/11/justin-timberlakes-man-of-the-woods-opens-at-no-1-with-2018s-biggest-week/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190606083014/https://www.forbes.com/sites/hughmcintyre/2018/02/11/justin-timberlakes-man-of-the-woods-opens-at-no-1-with-2018s-biggest-week/ |archive-date=June 6, 2019 |access-date=February 22, 2018 |magazine=Forbes}}</ref> The album was supported by the two top-ten singles, "[[Filthy (song)|Filthy]]" and "[[Say Something (Justin Timberlake song)|Say Something]]". ''Man of the Woods'' also marks Timberlake's fourth consecutive No. 1 album and has since been certified [[Music recording sales certification|Platinum]] by the Recording Industry Association of America (RIAA).<ref name="RIAA" /> ''Man of the Woods'' concluded 2018 as the sixth best-selling album of the year.<ref name="autogenerated1">{{cite magazine |last1=Caulfield |first1=Keith |date=January 8, 2019 |title=Drake's 'Scorpion' Is Nielsen Music's Top Album Of 2018 in U.S., 'God's Plan' Most-Streamed Song |url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/nielsen-music-top-album-2018-drake-scorpion/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190202234015/https://www.billboard.com/articles/columns/chart-beat/8492663/nielsen-music-top-album-2018-drake-scorpion |archive-date=February 2, 2019 |access-date=January 14, 2019 |magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]}}</ref>
