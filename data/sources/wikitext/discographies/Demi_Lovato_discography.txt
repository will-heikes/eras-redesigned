American singer [[Demi Lovato]] has released nine [[studio albums]], two [[soundtrack albums]], one [[remix album]], five [[extended play]]s (EPs), 57 [[single (music)|singles]] (including 15 as a featured artist), and 16 [[promotional singles]], and one [[Charity record|charity single]]s. According to [[Recording Industry Association of America]] (RIAA), Lovato has achieved 51 million certified units.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Searchable Database|website=[[Recording Industry Association of America]] |url=https://www.riaa.com/gold-platinum/|access-date=October 23, 2021|agency=Recording Industry Association of America}}</ref> As of August 2023, she has sold 9.7 million album units and 23.9 million song downloads and her songs have registered 7.7 billion on-demand streams in the United States.<ref name="us sales">{{cite magazine |last1=Mims |first1=Taylor |url=https://www.billboard.com/pro/demi-lovato-signs-caa-revamped/ |title=Demi Lovato Signs With CAA Ahead of ''Revamped'' Album Debut |magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]] |date=August 28, 2023 |access-date=August 28, 2023 }}</ref> Each of Lovato's studio albums debuted in the top-ten on the US [[Billboard 200|''Billboard'' 200]],<ref name=bb200/> and four of which have received platinum or higher [[RIAA certification|certifications]] and two gold certifications in the U.S.<ref name=RIAAalbum/> She has a total of 36 chart entries on the US [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]], including four top-ten songs.<ref name=hot100/> Globally, Lovato has sold more than 30 million records.<ref>{{cite news|date=May 24, 2025|title=Esta artista de Camp Rock conquistou um patrimônio incrível|url=https://www.correiobraziliense.com.br/cbradar/esta-artista-de-camp-rock-conquistou-um-patrimonio-incrivel/|access-date=February 13, 2026|newspaper=[[Correio Braziliense]]|language=pt-br|trans-title=This Camp Rock artist has earned an incredible net worth|last=Pereira|first=Maura}}</ref>

After signing with [[Hollywood Records]],<ref>{{cite news|url=http://www.victoriaadvocate.com/news/2009/jun/22/bc-mus-demilovatoaa-_-entertainment/?entertainment&national-entertainment|title=Disney magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/267911/demi-lovato-debuts-at-no-1-on-billboard-200-but-michael-jackson-still-reigns|title=Demi Lovato Debuts At No. 1 On Billboard 200, But Michael making Lovato one of 11 solo artists to score the US ''Billboard'' 200 number-one album before turning 18.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/photos/6546259/number-1-album-under-18-solo-artists|title=These 11 Solo Artists Scored a No. 1 Album Before Turning 18|author =Lynch, Joe|magazine=[[Billboard name="Unbroken debut">{{cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/467230/tony-bennett-85-achieves-first-no-1-album-on-billboard-200|title=Tony Bennett, 85, Achieves First No. 1 Album on The former debuted in the top-ten in the US making her highest career debut, and reached top-ten in the [[UK Singles Chart|United Kingdom]],<ref name=hot100/><ref name="uk"/> while the latter was named by ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' magazine as one of the 500 Greatest Pop Songs Of All Time.<ref>{{Cite magazine |last1=Werthman |first1=Christine |last2=Aniftos |first2=Rania |last3=Atkinson |first3=Katie |last4=Bain |first4=Katie |last5=Chan |first5=Anna |last6=Christman |first6=Ed |last7=Dailey |first7=Hannah |last8=Daw |first8=Stephen |last9=Denis |first9=Kyle |date=October 18, 2023 |title=The 500 Best Pop Songs (Nos. 500-101): Staff List |url=https://www.billboard.com/lists/best-pop-songs-all-time-hits/ |access-date=December 8, 2023 |magazine=Billboard |language=en-US}}</ref> In May 2013, Lovato released her fourth studio album ''[[Demi (album)|Demi]]'', which topped the chart in Canada and also reached number three in the US.<ref>{{cite magazine|url={{BillboardURLbyName|artist=demi lovato|chart=Canadian Albums}}|title=Demi Lovato - Chart History - Canadian Albums|magazine=Billboard|access-date=May 24, 2013}}<br />{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/artist/demi-lovato/|title=Demi Lovato – Chart History|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|access-date=March 17, 2020}}</ref> The album's lead single "[[Heart Attack (Demi Lovato song)|Heart Attack]]" reached the top ten in the US, Canada, New Zealand and reached number three in UK and Ireland.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/artist/demi-lovato/chart-history/hsi/|title=Demi Lovato Chart History – Hot 100|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|access-date=March 17, 2020}}<br />{{cite web|url=https://charts.nz/showinterpret.asp?interpret=Demi+Lovato|title=Discography Demi Lovato|publisher=charts.nz. Lovato's fifth studio album, ''[[Confident (album)|Confident]]'', was released in October 2015, her first release after signing with [[Island Records]].<ref name="IslandRecords">{{cite magazine | url =https://www.billboard.com/articles/business/6576003/demi-lovato-nick-jonas-manager-phil-mcintyre-launch-joint-venture-with | title = Demi Lovato, Nick Jonas, Manager Phil McIntyre Launch Joint Venture with Island Records (Exclusive) | author =Halperin, Shirley|magazine=Billboard|date = May 26, 2015|access-date= June 7, 2020}}</ref> It debuted at number two on the [[Billboard 200|''Billboard'' 200]] and is certified Platinum by the RIAA.<ref name="RIAA"/> The lead single "[[Sorry Not Sorry (Demi Lovato song)|Sorry Not Sorry]]" from Lovato's sixth studio album ''[[Tell Me You Love Me (album)|Tell Me You Love Me]]'' (2017), was a commercial and critical success, the song reached number six in the [[Billboard Hot 100|U.S. ''Billboard'' Hot 100]], and also reached the top ten in Australia, New Zealand and the UK, becoming her most certified song having received a 7× platinum certificate in the RIAA.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.billboard.com/artist/demi-lovato/chart-history/hsi/|title=Demi Lovato Chart History – Hot 100|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|access-date=March 17, 2020}}<br />{{cite web|url=https://charts.nz/showinterpret.asp?interpret=Demi+Lovato|title=Discography Demi Lovato|publisher=charts.nz. Hung Medien|access-date=April 24, 2011}}</ref><ref name="RIAA"/> In November of that year, her collaboration with [[Luis Fonsi]] "[[Échame la Culpa]]" was released and went on to reach number one in seventeen countries. In 2018, Lovato topped the international charts with "[[Solo (Clean Bandit song)|Solo]]", her collaboration with group [[Clean Bandit]], which became her first number-one song in the UK, Germany and Ireland.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.officialcharts.com/artist/5202/demi-lovato/|title=Demi Lovato|publisher=Official Charts Company|access-date=October 8, 2017}}<br />{{cite web|url=http://musicline.de/de/chartverfolgung_summary/artist/Lovato%2CDemi/single|title=Demi Lovato – Chartverfolgung – Single|publisher=Media Control Charts. Hung Medien|access-date=April 24, 2011}}</ref> In 2021, Lovato released her seventh studio album ''[[Dancing with the Devil... the Art of Starting Over]]'', which reached number two on the [[Billboard 200|''Billboard'' 200]] and UK chart.<ref>{{cite magazine|url={{BillboardURLbyName|artist=demi lovato|chart=Billboard 200}}|title=Demi Lovato Album & Song Chart History – Billboard 200|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|publisher=Prometheus Global Media|access-date=April 24, 2011}}<br />{{cite web|url=http://www.officialcharts.com/artist/5202/demi-lovato/|title=Demi Lovato|publisher=Official Charts Company|access-date=October 8, 2017}}</ref> In August 2022, Lovato released her eighth studio album ''[[Holy Fvck]]''. In October 2025, she released her ninth studio album ''[[It's Not That Deep|It's Not that Deep]].''
