The discography of American singer [[Ne-Yo]] consists of ten [[studio album]]s, forty-one [[Single (music)|singles]], several guest appearances with other artists, as well as production and songwriting for other artists.

In 2006, Ne-Yo's debut album, ''[[In My Own Words]]'', debuted at number one on the [[Billboard 200|''Billboard'' 200]] in the United States. His second single, "[[So Sick]]", reached number one on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]]; it was also Ne-Yo's first UK number-one single.<ref name=allmusic>{{cite web| url={{AllMusic|class=artist|id=p737022|pure_url=yes}}| title=Ne-Yo Billboard Albums Charts & Awards| website=[[AllMusic]]| access-date=September 18, 2009}}</ref><ref name=uk>{{cite web|url=https://www.officialcharts.com/artists/|title=The Official Charts Company – Ne-Yo|work=[[Official Charts Company]]|access-date=September 18, 2009}}</ref> The album was certified platinum by the [[Recording Industry Association of America]] in the US and gold by the [[British Phonographic Industry]] in the UK.<ref name=riaa>{{cite web|url=http://riaa.com/goldandplatinumdata.php?resultpage=1&table=SEARCH_RESULTS&action=&artist=ne-yo&format=ALBUM&startYear=1958&endYear=2008&sort=Artist&perPage=25|title=RIAA - Gold & Platinum Search|publisher=[[Recording Industry Association of America]]|access-date=November 28, 2008|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150924151606/http://www.riaa.com/goldandplatinumdata.php?resultpage=1|archive-date=September 24, 2015}}</ref><ref name=bpi>{{cite web|url=http://www.bpi.co.uk/certifiedawards/search.aspx |title=BPI > Certified Awards Search |publisher=[[British Phonographic Industry]] |access-date=August 27, 2009 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090924015932/http://www.bpi.co.uk/certifiedawards/search.aspx |archive-date=September 24, 2009}}</ref> Ne-Yo's second album, ''[[Because of You (Ne-Yo album)|Because of You]]'', was released in May 2007; it debuted at number one in the US and number six in the UK.<ref name=allmusic/><ref name=uk/> The first single, "[[Because of You (Ne-Yo song)|Because of You]]", peaked at number two in the US and number four in the UK.<ref name=allmusic/><ref name=uk/> The album was certified platinum in the US and silver in the UK.<ref name=riaa/><ref name=bpi/>

Ne-Yo's third album, ''[[Year of the Gentleman]]'', was released in September 2008 and debuted at number two in both the US and the UK.<ref name=allmusic/><ref name=uk/> The first single, "[[Closer (Ne-Yo song)|Closer]]", became Ne-Yo's second UK number-one single and peaked at number seven in the US.<ref name=allmusic/><ref name=uk/> ''Year of the Gentleman'' was certified platinum in the US.<ref name=riaa/> In September 2009, Ne-Yo released his first greatest hits album, ''[[Ne-Yo: The Collection]]'', in Japan.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cdjapan.co.jp/detailview.html?KEY=UICD-9061 |title=NE-YO: The Collection <nowiki>[</nowiki>Limited Edition<nowiki>]</nowiki>|website=Cdjapan.co.jp |access-date=September 12, 2009}}</ref>
