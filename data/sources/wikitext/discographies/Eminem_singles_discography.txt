American rapper [[Eminem]] has released 63 [[Single (music)|singles]] as a lead artist and 19 [[promotional recording|promotional singles]]. He has also featured in 21 singles as a guest artist, while entering the charts with over 125 additional songs.

Eminem's debut single, "[[Just Don't Give a Fuck]]", was released in 1998 and was included in ''[[Slim Shady EP]]'', as well as in his second studio album, ''[[The Slim Shady LP]]'', which featured a re-worked version of the song. Eminem's first ever song to enter the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]] was "[[My Name Is]]", which was released in 1999 and became the most successful single from ''The Slim Shady LP''. Along with "[[Guilty Conscience (song)|Guilty Conscience]]", it reached the top 5 in the UK.

The [[lead single]] from Eminem's third studio album, ''[[The Marshall Mathers LP]]'', was the song "[[The Real Slim Shady]]", which became his first entry in the top ten of the ''Billboard'' Hot 100. The third single from the album, "[[Stan (song)|Stan]]", featured vocals from British singer [[Dido (singer)|Dido]], sampled from her song "[[Thank You (Dido song)|Thank You]]". "[[Stan (song)|Stan]]" was the album's most successful single outside of the United States, while it failed to reach the top 50 in Eminem's home country.

In 2002, Eminem released the singles "[[Without Me (Eminem song)|Without Me]]" and "[[Cleanin' Out My Closet]]" from his album ''The Eminem Show'', as well as the single "[[Lose Yourself]]" from the [[8 Mile (soundtrack)|''8 Mile'' soundtrack]], which became his first number one song on the Hot 100 and remained on the top for 12 weeks.<ref>{{cite magazine|url=http://www.billboard.com/bbcom/specials/hot100/charts/most-weeks-no1.shtml|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080830232034/http://www.billboard.com/bbcom/specials/hot100/charts/most-weeks-no1.shtml|url-status=dead|archive-date=August 30, 2008|title=Songs With the Most Weeks at No. 1|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|access-date=March 22, 2009}}</ref> The song also reached the top of various national charts worldwide.

In 2009, the song "[[Crack a Bottle]]", a collaboration with label-mates [[Dr. Dre]] and [[50 Cent]], became Eminem's second number one on the Hot 100 and broke the record for opening week download sales in the US, with 418,000 copies sold in the first week.<ref name="record">{{cite news|date=December 5, 2008|url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/news/269427/eminems-bottle-breaks-digital-record|title=Eminem's 'Bottle' Breaks Digital Record|magazine=Billboard|access-date=February 11, 2009}}</ref>

''Recovery''{{'}}s singles "[[Not Afraid]]" and "[[Love the Way You Lie]]" featuring [[Rihanna]], both released in 2010, became Eminem's third and fourth number one songs on the Hot 100; the latter also reached the top of various national charts worldwide. In June 2014, his song "Not Afraid" was diamond certified by the [[Recording Industry Association of America|RIAA]], thus making Eminem the first artist with two digital diamond awards by RIAA.<ref name=":1">{{cite web|last1=Baker|first1=Soren|title=Eminem First Artist With Two Digital Diamond Awards|url=http://www.hiphopdx.com/index/news/id.29180/title.eminem-first-artist-with-two-digital-diamond-awards|website=HipHopDX|access-date=June 10, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140613090121/http://www.hiphopdx.com/index/news/id.29180/title.eminem-first-artist-with-two-digital-diamond-awards|archive-date=June 13, 2014|url-status=dead}}</ref>

In August 2013, Eminem released the single "[[Berzerk (song)|Berzerk]]" which debuted number 3 on the ''Billboard'' Hot 100 and preceded his eighth studio album ''[[The Marshall Mathers LP 2]]''. The album spawned the singles "[[Survival (Eminem song)|Survival]]", which was showcased during the ''[[Call of Duty: Ghosts]]'' reveal trailer,<ref>{{cite news|date=October 22, 2013 |url=https://news.yahoo.com/call-duty-ghosts-launch-trailer-dogs-destruction-eminem-110913587.html |title='Call of Duty: Ghosts' launch trailer: dogs, destruction, Eminem |publisher=[[Yahoo News]] |access-date=October 22, 2013 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131022152108/http://news.yahoo.com/call-duty-ghosts-launch-trailer-dogs-destruction-eminem-110913587.html |archive-date=October 22, 2013}}</ref> "[[Rap God]]", and "[[The Monster (song)|The Monster]]" featuring Rihanna. "The Monster" became Eminem's fifth number 1 single on the Hot 100, tying him with rappers Ludacris and [[Sean Combs|P. Diddy]] for rappers with the most number 1 singles in the US. In May 2014, Eminem was announced as the most-streamed artist of all time on [[Spotify]].<ref>{{cite web|author=Patrick Ryan | date=May 21, 2014|title=Eminem, Wake me up lead the spotify streams.|url=https://www.usatoday.com/story/life/music/2014/05/21/eminem-wake-me-up-lead-spotifys-most-streamed/9352133/|work=USA Today}}</ref>

From 2017 to 2020, Eminem released various hit singles, including "[[River (Eminem song)|River]]" featuring [[Ed Sheeran]], "[[Lucky You (Eminem song)|Lucky You]]" featuring [[Joyner Lucas]], "[[Killshot (song)|Killshot]]", and "[[Godzilla (Eminem song)|Godzilla]]" featuring [[Juice Wrld]]. The latter two debuted at number 3 on the ''Billboard'' Hot 100.<ref name="US100"/> In 2024, Eminem released "[[Houdini (Eminem song)|Houdini]]" as the lead single to his twelfth studio album ''[[The Death of Slim Shady (Coup de Grâce)]]''. It topped the charts in many countries, as well as debuting at number 1 on the [[Billboard Global 200|''Billboard'' Global 200]] and number 2 on the Hot 100.<ref>{{cite magazine|url= https://www.billboard.com/music/chart-beat/eminem-houdini-number-one-debut-global-charts-1235706120/|title= Eminem's 'Houdini' Unlocks No. 1 Debut on ''Billboard'' Global Charts|last=Trust|first=Gary|magazine=[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]|date=June 10, 2024|access-date=June 10, 2024}}</ref><ref name="US100"/>
