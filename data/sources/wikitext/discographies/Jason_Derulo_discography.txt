American singer and songwriter [[Jason Derulo]] has released five [[studio album]]s, one [[re-issue]]d album, two [[compilation album]]s, six [[extended play]]s, 59 [[Single (music)|singles]] (including 14 as a featured artist), six [[promotional single]]s, and 37 [[music video]]s (including six as a featured artist). Before Derulo established himself as a solo artist, he wrote songs for many artists, including [[Lil Wayne]], [[Pitbull (rapper)|Pitbull]], [[Pleasure P]] and [[Cassie Ventura|Cassie]].<ref name="allmusicbio">{{cite web|url=http://www.allmusic.com/artist/jason-derlo-p1008977/biography|title=Jason Derulo: Biography |website=[[AllMusic]]|access-date=June 10, 2011}}</ref><ref name="reutersbio">{{cite news|author=DGoodman |url=https://www.reuters.com/article/us-derulo-idUSTRE59405N20091005 |title=Heartbreak tale pays off for singer Jason Derulo |work=Reuters |date=October 4, 2009 |access-date=June 10, 2011}}</ref> He also wrote "Bossy" for rapper [[Birdman (rapper)|Birdman]], and made a guest appearance on the song.<ref name="allmusicbio"/>

After signing with [[Beluga Heights Records]], Derulo released his debut single "[[Whatcha Say]]" in May 2009. It reached number one on the [[Billboard Hot 100|''Billboard'' Hot 100]]<ref name="allmusicbio"/> and was [[Music recording sales certification|certified]] triple platinum by the [[Recording Industry Association of America]] (RIAA).<ref name="RIAA"/> The song served as the [[lead single]] from Derulo's [[Jason Derulo (album)|self-titled]] debut studio album, which was released on March 2, 2010, and reached number 11 on the
[[Billboard 200|''Billboard'' 200]].<ref name="US200"/> "[[In My Head (Jason Derülo song)|In My Head]]" and "[[Ridin' Solo]]" were released as the album's second and third singles respectively. While both attained peaks within the top 10 in multiple countries, "In My Head" became Derulo's first number-one single in the likes of Australia and the United Kingdom. A further two singles were released from the album: "[[What If (Jason Derülo song)|What If]]" and "[[The Sky's the Limit (song)|The Sky's the Limit]]".

Derulo's second studio album ''[[Future History (album)|Future History]]'' was released on September 16, 2011. Lead single "[[Don't Wanna Go Home]]" preceded its release in May 2011; where it became the artist's second number-one single in the United Kingdom.<ref name="UK"/> the second single taken from the album, "[[It Girl (Jason Derulo song)|It Girl]]" also achieved success, reaching the top five in Australia, New Zealand and the United Kingdom. "[[Breathing (Jason Derülo song)|Breathing]]" and "[[Fight for You (Jason Derulo song)|Fight for You]]" were also released from the album in 2011; with both attaining top 10 peaks in Australia. In May 2012, Derulo released "[[Undefeated (Jason Derulo song)|Undefeated]]" as the first and only single from the re-release of ''Future History''. Despite managing to reach number 14 in Australia, "Undefeated" performed poorly on the ''Billboard'' Hot 100, reaching number 90 and failing to chart altogether in the United Kingdom.

In April 2013, Derulo unveiled the lead single from his third studio album; ''[[Tattoos (Jason Derulo album)|Tattoos]]'', "[[The Other Side (Jason Derulo song)|The Other Side]]" saw Derulo return to the top five in Canada for the first time since 2009, reaching number 17 in the United States and the top five in Australia and the United Kingdom. Derulo's third studio album in the US, ''[[Talk Dirty (album)|Talk Dirty]]'', outsold his previous best debut ''Jason Derulo'' and included two top 10 platinum singles: "[[Talk Dirty (Jason Derulo song)|Talk Dirty]]" and "[[Wiggle (song)|Wiggle]]". As of 2015, Derulo has sold 28 million albums and songs combined in the US.<ref>{{cite magazine|last=Caulfield|first=Keith |url=http://www.billboard.com/articles/events/hot-100-festival/6649210/hot-100-festival-acts-charts-numbers|title=Billboard's Hot 100 Music Festival Acts On the Charts and By the Numbers|magazine=Billboard|date=July 30, 2015|access-date=July 30, 2015}}</ref>

Derulo released his fourth album ''[[Everything Is 4]]'' in May 2015. It produced the worldwide hit single "[[Want to Want Me]]", which peaked at number 5 on the ''Billboard'' Hot 100 and topped the [[UK Singles Chart]] for four weeks.

In 2020, Derulo's single "[[Savage Love (Laxed – Siren Beat)]]" peaked at number one on the ''Billboard'' Hot 100, marking his first top 10 hit in five years.
