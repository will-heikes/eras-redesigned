English singer and songwriter [[Rita Ora]] has released three [[studio album]]s, two [[extended play]]s, 31 [[Single (music)|singles]] (including eight as a featured artist) and ten [[promotional recording|promotional singles]]. Ora signed a record deal with [[Roc Nation]] in 2008. She featured on [[DJ Fresh]]'s single, "[[Hot Right Now]]", in February 2012, which reached number one in the United Kingdom. Her debut studio album, ''[[Ora (Rita Ora album)|Ora]]'', was released in August 2012.<ref name="Ora album"/><ref name="Ora album 2"/> The album debuted at [[List of UK Albums Chart number ones of the 2010s|number one]] on the [[UK Albums Chart]] and was subsequently [[Music recording certification|certified]] platinum by the [[British Phonographic Industry]] (BPI). ''Ora'' spawned four singles; the first two, "[[How We Do (Party)]]" and "[[R.I.P. (Rita Ora song)|R.I.P.]]", both reached [[List of UK Singles Chart number ones of the 2010s|number one]] on the [[UK Singles Chart]], while "[[Shine Ya Light]]"  and "[[Radioactive (Rita Ora song)|Radioactive]]" reached numbers ten and eighteen in the United Kingdom, respectively.

Ora's second studio album, ''[[Phoenix (Rita Ora album)|Phoenix]]'', was released by [[Atlantic Records]] in November 2018.<ref name="Phoenix"/> The album peaked at number eleven on the UK Albums Chart and was later certified platinum in the United Kingdom. Elsewhere, it entered the album charts, among others, in Australia, Canada, Germany, Netherlands and the United States. Five singles preceded the record, including "[[Your Song (Rita Ora song)|Your Song]]", "[[Anywhere (Rita Ora song)|Anywhere]]", "[[For You (Liam Payne and Rita Ora song)|For You]]" and "[[Let You Love Me]]", with four of them reaching the top 10 on the UK Singles Chart. In 2025, Ora discussed her fourth studio album, although she did not reveal the title, and released the singles "Heat" and "[[All Natural]]".
