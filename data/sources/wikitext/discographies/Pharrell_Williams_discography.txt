The discography of American musician [[Pharrell Williams]] consists of two [[studio album]]s, two [[extended play]]s (EPs), three [[mixtape]]s, 30 [[single (music)|singles]] (50 as a featured artist), one [[Music video#Video album|video album]] and 62 [[Music video|music videos]].

On September 9, 2005, Williams performed the opening single from his first solo album ''[[In My Mind (Pharrell Williams album)|In My Mind]]'', "[[Can I Have It Like That]]"; featuring [[Gwen Stefani]]. The single did poorly in the US, reaching only number 48. It fared better in the UK, reaching number three. Between then and the July 25, 2006 release of "In My Mind", he released "[[Angel (Pharrell song)|Angel]]" (in Europe only), and later "[[Number One (Pharrell Williams song)|Number One]]" (featuring [[Kanye West]]). "[[That Girl (Pharrell song)|That Girl]]" (featuring [[Snoop Dogg]]) was the final single.

In 2013, Williams featured on [[Daft Punk]]'s fourth studio album ''[[Random Access Memories]]'' on the songs "[[Get Lucky (Daft Punk song)|Get Lucky]]" and "[[Lose Yourself to Dance]]". In addition, Williams contributed to [[Azealia Banks]]' debut studio album ''[[Broke with Expensive Taste]]'', featuring on the song "[[ATM Jam]]", though it was taken out of the album prior to release. Williams penned three new original songs, included alongside composer Heitor Pereira's score, for the sequel ''[[Despicable Me 2]]''. These were "[[Just a Cloud Away]]", "[[Happy (Pharrell Williams song)|Happy]]", and "Scream" (featuring [[CeeLo Green]]). His two original songs from the first film were also reprised on the soundtrack - "Despicable Me" and "Fun, Fun, Fun". In March 2013, Williams appeared alongside [[T.I.]] on [[Robin Thicke]]'s hit single "[[Blurred Lines]]". The song has been a worldwide hit, has peaked at number one on the ''Billboard'' Hot 100, and has also reached number one in 13 more countries including the United Kingdom and Germany, making it Williams' third ''Billboard'' Hot 100 number one single. On June 29, 2013, he became the 12th artist in the chart's history to simultaneously hold the number one and two positions (with "Blurred Lines" and "Get Lucky", respectively). By July 2013, only 137 singles in UK chart history had achieved one million sales in the UK - that month Williams scored two million-sellers with "Get Lucky" and "Blurred Lines".<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/blurred-lines-becomes-uks-latest-million-selling-single-2361/|title=Blurred Lines Becomes UK's Latest Million Selling Single|last=Lane|first=Daniel|date=July 17, 2013|publisher=[[Official Charts Company]]|access-date=October 15, 2014}}</ref>

In November 2013, Williams released the first 24-hour music video to his ''Despicable Me 2'' collaboration song, "Happy". Guest appearances included [[Magic Johnson]], [[Steve Carell]], [[Jimmy Kimmel]], [[Jamie Foxx]], [[Steve Martin]], [[Odd Future]], [[Miranda Cosgrove]], [[Janelle Monáe]], and many others. In December 2013, a press release from [[Columbia Records]] announced that Williams had signed a contract with the label and would be releasing an album in 2014, featuring the single "Happy" from the ''Despicable Me 2'' soundtrack. "Happy" has since become one of the [[List of best-selling singles|best-selling singles of all time]], having sold more than 10 million copies worldwide.<ref>{{cite news|url=http://blackamericaweb.com/2014/06/27/happy-sells-over-10-million/|title='Happy' Sells Over 10 Million|last=Franklin|first=Krystal|date=June 27, 2014|publisher=Black America Web|access-date=October 15, 2014}}</ref> It is also the [[List of best-selling music downloads in the United Kingdom|most downloaded song in the United Kingdom]], with over 1.65 million copies sold.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/the-official-top-40-biggest-selling-singles-of-2014-so-far-3163/|title=The Official Top 40 Biggest Selling Singles of 2014 so far!|last=Copsey|first=Rob|date=October 1, 2014|publisher=[[Official Charts Company]]|access-date=October 15, 2014}}</ref> On March 4, 2014, Williams released his second studio album ''[[Girl (Pharrell Williams album)|Girl]]'', which includes the singles "Happy", "[[Marilyn Monroe (Pharrell Williams song)|Marilyn Monroe]]", "[[Come Get It Bae]]" and "[[Gust of Wind]]".<ref>{{cite magazine|url=https://www.rollingstone.com/music/news/pharrell-announces-new-album-g-i-r-l-20140219|title=Pharrell Williams Announces New Album 'G I R L' - Rolling Stone|magazine=Rolling Stone|date=19 February 2014 }}</ref>

On April 5, 2024, Williams quietly released the 10-song project ''Black Yacht Rock, Vol. 1: City of Limitless Access'' exclusively through BlackYachtRock.com.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Strauss |first=Matthew |date=2024-04-05 |title=Pharrell Williams Quietly Releases New Album Black Yacht Rock, Vol. 1: City of Limitless Access |url=https://pitchfork.com/news/pharrell-williams-quietly-releases-new-album-black-yacht-rock-vol-1-city-of-limitless-access/ |access-date=2026-10-15 |website=Pitchfork |language=en-US}}</ref>
