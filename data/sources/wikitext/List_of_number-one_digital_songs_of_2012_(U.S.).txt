{{Short description|2012 highest-selling digital singles in the United States}}

{{Use mdy dates|date=May 2015}}
The highest-selling [[music download|digital singles]] in the United States are ranked in the [[Hot Digital Songs]] chart, published by ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' magazine. The data are compiled by [[Nielsen SoundScan]] based on each single's weekly digital sales, which combines sales of different versions of a single for a summarized figure.
