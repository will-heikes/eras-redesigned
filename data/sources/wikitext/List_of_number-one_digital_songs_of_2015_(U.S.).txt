{{Short description|2015 highest-selling digital singles in the United States}}
{{Use mdy dates|date=November 2022}}

The '''List of number-one digital songs of 2015 in the United States''' are based upon the highest-selling [[music download|digital single]]s ranked in the [[Hot Digital Songs]] chart, published by ''[[Billboard (magazine)|Billboard]]'' magazine. The data are compiled by [[Nielsen SoundScan]] based on each single's weekly digital sales, which combines sales of different versions of a single for a summarized figure.
